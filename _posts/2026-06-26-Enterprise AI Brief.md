### 2. **Anthropic Accuses Alibaba of Massive Fraudulent Campaign Against Claude AI**
- **The Wall Street Journal** · 2026-06-25
- **Summary**: Anthropic has formally accused operators linked to Alibaba’s Qwen AI lab of running the largest known attempt to distill its Claude models. The campaign allegedly used nearly 25,000 fraudulent accounts to generate over 28.8 million interactions with Claude between April and June 2026 [[95]]. Anthropic claims this was an illicit effort to extract model capabilities and training data [[101]].
- **Why It Matters**: This escalation highlights the growing geopolitical and competitive tensions in AI development, particularly around model security and intellectual property. Enterprises using third-party LLMs must now consider robust fraud detection and usage monitoring to protect their own proprietary data and prevent abuse.
- **URL**: [Read more](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/economy/anthropic-accuses-alibaba-of-illicitly-accessing-claude-ai-model-report/3977635)
