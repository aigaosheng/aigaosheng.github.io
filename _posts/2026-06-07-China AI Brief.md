### 3. Shanghai Tightens Regulations on AI-Generated Short Films
- **Vietnam.vn** · 2026-06-06
- **Summary**: Shanghai has issued a new set of eight policy measures to manage and promote the rapidly growing AI short film industry. The new rules require all AI-generated animations and short films to complete registration and licensing before release, with unregistered works to be removed from all platforms. The regulations also include a "2+5" expedited review mechanism and a multi-tiered copyright protection system.
- **Why It Matters**: This marks a significant shift from spontaneous growth to a quality-controlled development model for China's AI content industry. While regulating content, Shanghai is also offering financial support (up to 3 million yuan per project) to maintain competitiveness, showing a dual strategy of control and promotion to build AI into a digital content infrastructure and cultural export tool.
- **URL**: [China tightens regulations on AI short film production.](https://www.vietnam.vn/en/trung-quoc-xiet-chat-quy-trinh-san-xuat-phim-ngan-ai)
