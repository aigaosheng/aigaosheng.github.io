# Payments Brief: OCBC, Standard Chartered Complete Tokenised Deposit Trial

**Today:** A Singapore bank trial connects separate tokenised deposit systems, while a European launch brings stablecoin exchange into an embedded finance platform.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🌐 **OCBC, Standard Chartered Test Tokenised Deposit Payments**

*The Edge Singapore · November 20, 2026*

**Bottom line:** OCBC and Standard Chartered completed a trial of US dollar payments between their separate tokenised deposit systems, settling transfers within minutes outside normal banking hours.

The trial involved two transfers in opposite directions and was conducted with StraitsX, which provided the connecting infrastructure and coordinated payment instructions and transaction information. The Edge reported the trial on November 20; the banks discussed it at the Singapore FinTech Festival on November 19.

**Why it matters:** The test points to a way banks could connect tokenised deposit systems for after-hours payments while reducing the need for bilateral prefunding. It was conducted under MAS’s Project BLOOM, which is exploring digital money and payment infrastructure.

### 2. 💳 **HOPPA Launches Stablecoin Exchange In Europe**

*GlobeNewswire · November 19, 2026*

**Bottom line:** HOPPA has launched a European stablecoin exchange service with regulated exchange services from ZBX and technology from BoomFi.

The service supports USDC on Ethereum, Polygon and Solana, with USDC/EUR trading. GlobeNewswire timestamped the announcement at 11:02 p.m. Eastern Time on November 19, which was 12:02 p.m. Singapore time on November 20; the rollout is staged for retail and business customers.

**Why it matters:** The arrangement shows how fintech platforms can add stablecoin exchange through specialist regulated and technology partners, without building every part of the service themselves. Its European focus makes the rollout a useful signal for how embedded stablecoin services may develop across regulated markets.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/20/3407234/0/en/mica-licensed-zbx-and-boomfi-power-hoppa-s-stablecoin-exchange-launch.html)

## Key Takeaways

- The OCBC and Standard Chartered trial tested interoperability between bank tokenised deposit systems, including payments outside standard banking hours.
- StraitsX provided a connection between the banks’ systems, highlighting the infrastructure needed to make tokenised money usable across platforms.
- HOPPA’s service combines a customer-facing financial platform with regulated exchange and payment technology partners.

## What to Watch

Watch for further details on commercial use of cross-bank tokenised deposit payments in Singapore and how HOPPA expands its stablecoin exchange offering across Europe.
