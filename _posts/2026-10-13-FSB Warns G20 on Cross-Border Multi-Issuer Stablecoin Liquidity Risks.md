# Payments Brief: FSB Warns G20 on Cross-Border Multi-Issuer Stablecoin Liquidity Risks

**Today:** Global standard-setters and payment giants are advancing cross-border payment infrastructure and stablecoin integration while tightening oversight on multi-issuer reserve frictions and liquidity risks.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🏦 **FSB Warns G20 Ministers on Cross-Border Stablecoin Liquidity Mismatches**

*Regulation Tomorrow · October 13, 2026*

**Bottom line:** The Financial Stability Board has highlighted critical financial stability risks in cross-border global stablecoin arrangements ahead of the G20 Finance Ministers meeting.

In a formal letter from FSB Chair Andrew Bailey to G20 Finance Ministers and Central Bank Governors, the FSB detailed its ongoing evaluation of global stablecoins. The report notes that while stablecoins hold potential benefits for the real economy, multi-issuer models where affiliated issuers share liability for a single asset face severe reserve rebalancing frictions during market stress. The FSB recommended developing unified metrics and enhancing cross-border information sharing to close data gaps.

**Why it matters:** For financial institutions and payment service providers in Asia, regulatory focus is pivoting toward structural redemption risks and cross-border reserve liquidity, signaling stricter operational compliance for stablecoin issuers across jurisdictions.

### 2. 💳 **Tether Invests in EFY Finance to Accelerate LATAM Institutional Stablecoin Rails**

*Tether · October 13, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Tether has invested in Latin American fintech EFY Finance's seed round to expand institutional settlement rails for USD₮ and USA₮.

The investment aims to integrate fiat-to-stablecoin infrastructure directly with local banking systems and traditional financial platforms across Latin America. According to Tether, the partnership focuses on bridging the gap between digital dollars and daily enterprise payments in high-inflation markets, leveraging regulated infrastructure to ensure seamless settlement.

**Why it matters:** Institutional stablecoin usage is expanding rapidly outside North America and Europe, providing a blueprint for cross-border B2B payments and trade settlement in emerging markets across Asia-Pacific.

### 3. 💳 **Bakkt Selects Thredd to Power Credit Card-as-a-Service Program**

*Financial Times · October 13, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Digital asset infrastructure firm Bakkt has partnered with global payment processor Thredd to launch its Credit Card-as-a-Service solution.

Bakkt is leveraging Thredd’s card issuing platform to support regulated trading, stablecoin settlement, and cross-border payment operations. The partnership allows institutional clients to bridge card rails with digital asset liquidity, expanding payment options for the tokenized economy.

**Why it matters:** Integrating next-generation card processing with stablecoin settlement architectures provides financial institutions with turnkey solutions to offer hybrid crypto-fiat corporate card products.

### 4. 🌐 **Visa Wins Approval to Prepare for Domestic China Payments Business**

*Morningstar · October 13, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Visa has secured regulatory approval to advance preparations for establishing domestic card payment operations in China.

The clearance permits Visa to take formal concrete steps toward clearing domestic yuan transactions within Mainland China. This marks a significant regulatory milestone in opening China's vast domestic payments market to international card networks.

**Why it matters:** Access to mainland domestic clearing offers cross-border payment processors and regional banks deeper integration into China's consumer and B2B trade payment flows.
