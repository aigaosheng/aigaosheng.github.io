# Strategic Takeaways

* **Compute remains the core battlefield**: The US maintains advantages in frontier AI companies and semiconductor ecosystems, while China continues strengthening domestic AI infrastructure and open-model adoption.
* **Open-source AI is becoming a geopolitical factor**: Chinese open-weight models are challenging traditional assumptions that closed, proprietary systems will dominate global AI deployment.
* **AI hardware independence is critical**: Both US and Chinese companies are increasingly investing in custom chips and alternative computing architectures to secure long-term supply chains.
* **The next phase of competition will be ecosystem-driven**: Model quality alone is insufficient; developer adoption, infrastructure availability, semiconductor access, and global partnerships will determine AI leadership.
