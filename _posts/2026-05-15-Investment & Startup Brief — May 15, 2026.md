### Investment & Startup Brief — May 15, 2026

### Top Stories

- **TDB Begins Disbursal of $12 Billion RDI Fund to Deeptech Startups**
- **Source** · The Economic Times · May 14, 2026
- **Summary** · India’s Technology Development Board (TDB) has started releasing the first tranche of the Rs 1 lakh crore ($12 billion) Research and Development Initiative (RDI) fund. The initial disbursements are targeting deeptech startups in space, drones, and biotech, with 22 projects selected so far.
- **Why It Matters** · This marks a significant injection of patient capital into India’s deeptech ecosystem, allowing startups in capital-intensive sectors (like space and biotech) to scale without the pressure of immediate returns. Ticket sizes range from Rs 3 crore to Rs 150 crore.
- **URL** · https://m.economictimes.com/tech/startups/tdb-kicks-off-rdi-fund-disbursal-backs-startups-across-space-drones-biotech/amp_articleshow/131101803.cms

- **Ex-Peak XV Trio Launches Mettle Capital Targeting $400 Million Fund**
- **Source** · The Economic Times · May 14, 2026
- **Summary** · Former Peak XV Partners managing directors Ashish Agrawal, Ishaan Mittal, and Tejeshwi Sharma have launched Mettle Capital. The new VC fund aims to raise $350-400 million to back Indian startups at Series A and B stages, focusing on enterprise AI, deeptech, and consumer internet.
- **Why It Matters** · The launch of Mettle Capital signals a maturation of the Indian VC landscape, with top-tier talent branching out to fill specific funding gaps. The fund aims to close within the quarter.
- **URL** · https://m.economictimes.com/tech/funding/ex-peak-xv-trio-launches-mettle-capital-targets-350400-million-fund-to-back-indian-startups/amp_articleshow/131073404.cms

- **Cerebras Systems Raises $5.5 Billion in Largest US IPO of 2026**
- **Source** · BSS/AFP · May 14, 2026
- **Summary** · AI chip startup Cerebras Systems priced its Nasdaq IPO at $185 per share, raising approximately $5.5 billion and valuing the company at over $55 billion. The listing comes after OpenAI committed to a $10 billion purchasing contract in January.
- **Why It Matters** · This is a critical test of public market appetite for pure-play AI infrastructure hardware. Its performance will influence the valuation and timing of other AI giants waiting in the wings, such as SpaceX and Anthropic.
- **URL** · https://www.bssnews.net/business/386871

- **Profitability Becomes Mandatory as Indian Startups Evolve Toward IPOs**
- **Source** · Business Standard · May 14, 2026
- **Summary** · As Indian startups like Zepto, Lenskart, and Flipkart prepare for public markets, investors are enforcing stricter discipline on profitability and unit economics. Unlike the 2021 boom, today’s IPOs prioritize offer-for-sale (OFS) for mature investors rather than raising fresh growth capital.
- **Why It Matters** · The market has reset its "diligence bar." Startups that cannot demonstrate a clear path to EBITDA positivity face valuation compression. However, the rise of domestic institutional capital provides a stable landing pad for those who pass the filter.
- **URL** · https://www.business-standard.com/amp/companies/start-ups/startup-funding-ipo-exits-profitability-discipline-investors-public-markets-126051401087_1.html

- **Adfin Raises $18M Series A for AI-Powered Finance Automation**
- **Source** · Startbase · May 15, 2026
- **Summary** · London-based fintech Adfin has raised $18 million in a Series A led by Index Ventures. The platform is evolving from payment management into an "agentic finance" platform that automates entire financial processes using AI.
- **Why It Matters** · "Agentic AI" is emerging as the next major frontier in B2B SaaS, moving beyond dashboards to autonomous task execution. Adfin’s round indicates strong investor appetite for AI that solves specific back-office operational pain points.
- **URL** · https://www.startbase.com/news/startupnews-zum-wochenende-20-26
