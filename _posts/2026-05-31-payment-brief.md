## Top Stories

### 1. Visa outlines €500 million Europe investment as payments sovereignty debate intensifies
- **Finextra** · 2026-05-29
- **Summary**: Visa said it will invest €500 million in Europe, including a new local data processing centre, as policymakers and market participants push for greater regional payments sovereignty. The move is a direct response to concern in Europe about dependence on non-European payment infrastructure and positions Visa as a long-term local operator rather than just an external network provider.
- **Why It Matters**: Payments sovereignty is moving from policy debate to competitive reality. A large infrastructure commitment from Visa signals that global card networks are adapting their operating models to defend share in strategically sensitive markets.

### 2. TrueLayer acquires In3 to combine pay-by-bank with consumer credit at checkout
- **Finextra** · 2026-05-29
- **Summary**: TrueLayer announced the acquisition of Dutch fintech In3, which specializes in consumer credit via bank-payment flows. The deal expands TrueLayer’s pay-by-bank network beyond pure account-to-account initiation toward hybrid checkout experiences that blend open banking with financing.
- **Why It Matters**: This is strategically important because it pushes open-banking payments closer to card-like checkout flexibility. If successful, it could improve merchant adoption by pairing lower-cost bank rails with embedded credit options that consumers already expect.
