**AI-Powered Holiday Shopping: A 520% Surge in 2025**

The 2025 U.S. holiday shopping season is poised for a technological transformation. Adobe predicts that AI-assisted online shopping will experience a staggering 520% growth compared to the previous year, with peak activity expected in the ten days leading up to Thanksgiving. This surge follows a 1,300% increase in AI-driven retail traffic in 2024, highlighting a significant shift in consumer behavior towards AI-powered shopping tools.

---

### 📱 Mobile Shopping Dominates

Mobile devices are set to account for a record 56.1% of online shopping transactions this holiday season, surpassing desktop usage. This trend reflects the growing consumer preference for shopping on-the-go, facilitated by larger smartphone screens and improved mobile shopping experiences.

---

### 🛍️ Glossary

* **Generative AI**: A type of artificial intelligence that can generate new content, such as text, images, or recommendations, based on learned patterns.

* **Cyber Monday**: The Monday following Thanksgiving in the U.S., known for significant online shopping deals.

* **Black Friday**: The Friday after Thanksgiving, marking the beginning of the U.S. holiday shopping season with major sales.
