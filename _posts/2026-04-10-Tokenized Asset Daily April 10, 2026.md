**Source:** FameEX | **Published:** April 10, 2026
**Summary:** A joint report from Keyrock and Securitize projects the tokenized real-world asset (RWA) market will grow from ~$29B today to $400B by 2030. **Why It Matters:** This reinforces RWAs as one of the fastest-growing segments in digital assets, shifting tokenization from experimentation to institutional-scale adoption.
