### 7. Hotel Restrictions Lifted for Upper Circular Road and Beach Road
- **Source**: The Straits Times · 2026-06-21
- **Summary**: The Urban Redevelopment Authority (URA) will lift restrictions on new hotels, backpacker hostels, and serviced apartments in the Upper Circular Road and Beach Road areas, effective for two years until May 31, 2028. The restrictions, in place since 2014 to avoid a glut in Outram, Rochor, Downtown Core, and Singapore River areas, are being eased in response to industry feedback. National Development Minister Chee Hong Tat cited the heritage appeal of both districts as showcasing "how heritage can meet modernity" .
- **Why It Matters**: The policy shift could spur new hospitality developments and revitalize these historic districts, balancing the need for vibrant urban spaces against potential oversaturation. Developers and hospitality operators may see opportunities in these areas, while the two-year window creates a test case for broader relaxation .
