### 1. DeepSeek launches V4 model optimized for Huawei chips, signaling full China AI stack shift

**Source + Publish Date:** Reuters / AsiaOne / WSJ coverage, 2026-04-24

**Summary:**

**Source + Publish Date:** Reuters, 2026-04-24
