# AI for Science Brief: Anthropic Acquires Mithril and Reports Claude Drug Results

**Today:** AI labs are moving from supplying tools to scientists to running discovery programmes and producing machine-checked results themselves, which changes how buyers should judge vendors' scientific claims.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🔬 **Anthropic Acquires Mithril Medicine and Reports Early Claude Drug-Development Results**

*Becker's Hospital Review · October 27, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Anthropic now runs its own drug programmes after buying a one-person rare-disease biotech, and it has published early, mostly preclinical results from six pharma and biotech partners.

Mithril founder Cody Tranbarger used Claude to manage contracts, logistics and experiments across 11 outside labs, and took a muscular dystrophy candidate from idea to promising mouse results in under 18 months; Anthropic says a drug could enter human trials as soon as late 2028 if the candidates hold up. The partners are BigHat Biosciences, Bristol Myers Squibb, Divergence Labs, Enveda, Generate Biomedicines and The Column Group. At Enveda, Claude reviewed 1,061 pages of manufacturing records, caught all 11 issues human reviewers found plus seven they missed, and cut review time by 89%. Bristol Myers Squibb is using Claude to assess early-stage clinical trial plans and plans to expand the work in 2027.

**Why it matters:** A frontier lab owning drug assets puts it in direct competition with the AI-biotech platforms it also supplies. The Enveda result matters beyond pharma, because document-heavy quality and compliance review looks much like regulated workflows in banking and payments. All figures are reported by Anthropic and its partners and have not been independently verified.

### 2. 🤖 **Axiom's AI Agent Swarm Formally Verifies the 15,000-Page "Enormous Theorem"**

*OfficeChai · October 27, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Axiom Math says its AI agents produced the first complete computer-checked proof of the Classification of Finite Simple Groups, often described as the longest proof in mathematics.

The original proof spans about 15,000 pages across more than 500 papers by roughly 100 mathematicians. Axiom says swarms of AI agents worked for 24 days and wrote about 43.1 million lines of code in the Lean proof assistant. Along the way they surfaced 34 serious problems in the published record. One, tied to the Unbalanced Group Theorem, needed a new piece of mathematics, developed with input from original classification contributors Richard Lyons and Ronald Solomon. Axiom is releasing the full errata list to the mathematics community.

**Why it matters:** The value here is verification rather than generation: Lean checks every logical step, so the output can be audited without trusting the AI that wrote it. That pattern of a machine checker governing autonomous agents is directly relevant to any regulated organisation weighing agentic AI for high-stakes work.

### 3. 📊 **Dassault Systèmes Closes $1.8B ArisGlobal Deal to Build an AI Life Sciences Platform**

*Dassault Systèmes via GlobeNewswire · October 28, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Dassault Systèmes has completed its acquisition of ArisGlobal and will combine it with BIOVIA and MEDIDATA into one AI-native platform that runs from scientific discovery through to patient outcomes.

The company paid $1.8 billion in cash at closing, plus up to $200 million more tied to multi-year AI-related revenue milestones. ArisGlobal's platform handles regulatory, safety, quality and medical-affairs data for more than 200 clients, including half of the top 50 global biopharma companies. Dr Ashwini Zenooz, previously at Salesforce and the US Department of Veterans Affairs, has been appointed Executive Vice President and CEO of the new Life Sciences and Healthcare sector.

**Why it matters:** Linking discovery modelling (BIOVIA) with clinical (MEDIDATA) and safety data creates feedback loops that standalone discovery tools lack. The earn-out tied to AI revenue shows how acquirers now price AI capability. For pharma and CRO operations in Asia, a more consolidated vendor stack raises familiar questions about concentration risk and data residency.

## Key Takeaways

- AI developers are moving up the value chain, from selling models to owning research outcomes: Anthropic now has its own drug programmes, and Axiom is publishing new mathematics.
- Verifiable output is becoming the differentiator. Formal proof checking and documented error-catching rates carry more weight than benchmark scores.
- Incumbents are answering with consolidation and AI-linked earn-outs, betting that integrated data across the drug lifecycle will beat point tools.

## What to Watch

Watch for independent review by group theorists of Axiom's errata list and its new Unbalanced Group Theorem argument. On the Anthropic side, watch whether the Mithril candidates keep progressing toward the late-2028 human-trial target and whether the Bristol Myers Squibb collaboration expands in 2027 as planned.
