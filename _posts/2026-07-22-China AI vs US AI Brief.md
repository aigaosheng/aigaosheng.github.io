## Strategic Takeaways

* **Open-source AI has become a major battleground**: Chinese AI companies are increasingly leveraging open models as a way to accelerate adoption and compete globally.
* **US strategy is shifting toward ecosystem control**: Policy focus is expanding from frontier models to infrastructure, security, intellectual property, and national AI capability.
* **The AI race is no longer only about model benchmarks**: Compute access, developer ecosystems, deployment costs, and international partnerships are becoming equally important competitive factors.
* **China’s challenge is efficiency; the US advantage remains ecosystem depth**: The next phase of competition will likely depend on who can build the strongest combination of models, chips, talent, infrastructure, and global adoption.
