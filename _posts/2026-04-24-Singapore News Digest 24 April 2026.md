# **Singapore News Digest**

**Period: 23–24 April 2026**

---

## **Finance & Economy**

Singapore inflation is projected to remain elevated in 2026, with core inflation rising to around 1.7% and full-year forecasts revised upward due to persistent energy and geopolitical pressures. Analysts expect continued tightening bias from MAS to manage price stability amid global cost pressures.
Source: [https://www.minichart.com.sg/2026/04/24/singapore-inflation-set-to-rise-in-2026-amid-higher-energy-costs-and-global-tensions/](https://www.minichart.com.sg/2026/04/24/singapore-inflation-set-to-rise-in-2026-amid-higher-energy-costs-and-global-tensions/) ([Minichart][1])

---

## **Technology & AI**

Singapore is increasingly positioning itself as a **neutral global AI hub**, attracting major US and Chinese AI firms seeking stable regulatory ground amid geopolitical tensions. Firms including OpenAI-linked ecosystems and Anthropic-backed initiatives are expanding local presence, supported by sovereign capital and business-friendly policies.
Source: [https://www.reuters.com/world/china/singapore-emerging-neutral-ground-ai-firms-navigate-sino-us-rivalry-2026-04-24/](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/singapore-emerging-neutral-ground-ai-firms-navigate-sino-us-rivalry-2026-04-24/) ([Reuters][2])

---

## **Startups & Business**

A Singapore-based AI infrastructure platform is gaining traction as enterprises shift toward **multi-model AI strategies**, with over 70% of firms reportedly using multiple foundation models to reduce dependency risk and improve efficiency in production systems.
Source: [https://www.techfocusasia.com/article/907475988-the-end-of-single-model-dependency-why-enterprises-are-switching-to-unified-ai-api-platforms-in-2026](https://www.techfocusasia.com/article/907475988-the-end-of-single-model-dependency-why-enterprises-are-switching-to-unified-ai-api-platforms-in-2026) ([techfocusasia.com][3])

---

## **Policy & Government**

Singapore and the IAEA signed a new **2026–2031 technical cooperation framework**, expanding national capabilities in nuclear safety, energy research, and radiation protection as the country evaluates long-term energy diversification options.
Source: [https://www.nea.gov.sg/media/news/news/index/singapore-signs-fourth-country-prog-framework-with-international-atomic-energy-agency](https://www.nea.gov.sg/media/news/news/index/singapore-signs-fourth-country-prog-framework-with-international-atomic-energy-agency) ([nea.gov.sg][4])

---

## **Infrastructure / Real Estate / Transport**

The Ministry of Home Affairs ordered blocking of six foreign-operated websites impersonating Singapore news platforms, citing risks of coordinated misinformation campaigns targeting local audiences. The move reflects heightened digital information security enforcement.
Source: [https://www.mha.gov.sg/media-room/newsroom/six-inauthentic-websites-blocked-for-potential-threat-of-being-used-to-mount-hostile-information-campaigns-against-singapore](https://www.mha.gov.sg/media-room/newsroom/six-inauthentic-websites-blocked-for-potential-threat-of-being-used-to-mount-hostile-information-campaigns-against-singapore) ([Ministry of Home Affairs][5])

---

## **Society / Education / Environment**

Singapore launched a **$5 million Climate Adaptation Package** under SG Eco Fund, supporting community projects focused on heat resilience, flood protection, and water conservation as part of national efforts to strengthen climate preparedness.
Source: [https://www.mse.gov.sg/take-action/sgecofund/climate-adaptation-package](https://www.mse.gov.sg/take-action/sgecofund/climate-adaptation-package) ([mse.gov.sg][6])

## **Additional Notable Context (Cross-Sector)**

Singapore continues strengthening its role in global AI and finance ecosystems, with increased collaboration between sovereign funds and frontier AI firms such as Anthropic, highlighting deep integration between capital, research, and enterprise AI adoption.
Source: [https://www.channelnewsasia.com/singapore/gic-anthropic-claude-artificial-intelligence-tech-leaders-6075611](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/singapore/gic-anthropic-claude-artificial-intelligence-tech-leaders-6075611) ([CNA][7])
