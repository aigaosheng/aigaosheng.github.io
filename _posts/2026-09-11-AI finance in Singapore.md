### 1. **Singapore Banks Put Tokenised Deposits Into Live Interbank Payments**

* **Source**: Fintech Singapore · 11 September 2026
* **Summary**: DBS, OCBC and UOB have completed live Singapore-dollar interbank transactions using tokenised deposits, marking a significant step beyond controlled blockchain experiments. The development demonstrates that tokenised bank money can support real-world settlement between Singapore's major domestic banks.
* **Why It Matters**: Programmable deposits could become an important financial-technology layer for 24/7 settlement, treasury automation and eventually AI-agent transactions. For Singapore, the convergence of tokenised money, APIs and AI creates infrastructure for more autonomous financial workflows.

## Strategic Takeaway

**Singapore's AI-finance opportunity is moving from experimentation toward infrastructure.** Three layers are converging: **AI agents**, **programmable/tokenised money**, and **enterprise-grade governance and security**. The emerging competitive battlefield is therefore less about which bank has the best chatbot and more about which institution can safely connect AI agents to real financial assets, payment rails, customer data and regulated decision workflows.
