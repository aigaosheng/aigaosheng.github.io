### Opensource LLM Model Brief — 2026-06-24

## Top Stories

### 1. Zhipu AI Hits $140B Valuation Peak Following GLM-5.2 Release
- **China Daily** · 2026-06-23
- **Summary**: Chinese AI developer Zhipu AI briefly crossed a HK$1 trillion ($127 billion) market valuation, becoming the country's first LLM firm to do so. The surge followed the release of its open-source GLM-5.2, a Mixture-of-Experts model with 744-753 billion parameters designed for autonomous task execution . The model reportedly narrows the gap with leading proprietary systems to within 1-4% on key evaluations .
- **Why It Matters**: The market's reaction signals strong investor confidence in open-source models as commercially viable alternatives to closed giants. GLM-5.2's massive 1-million-token context window and local-run capability position it as a powerful option for enterprises seeking data sovereignty .
- **URL**: [Zhipu AI first Chinese LLM firm to briefly hit HK$1 trillion valuation](https://global.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202606/23/WS6a3a26dea310986e2b4616dc.html)

### 2. Europe's Sovereign AI Push: "Soofi S" Model Released
- **TU Darmstadt** · 2026-06-23
- **Summary**: The Soofi Consortium, backed by €20 million from the German government, presented its first model, "Soofi S." This 30-billion-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model was trained from scratch on 27 trillion tokens using European infrastructure . It achieves top scores among open-source models in its size class for English and leads in German benchmarks, emphasizing transparency by publishing model weights and training methodologies .
- **Why It Matters**: This represents a significant step toward "AI sovereignty," offering businesses and public sectors a transparent alternative to non-European models. By releasing technical documentation, the project aims to build trust and enable customization for sensitive applications .
- **URL**: [AI for Europe](https://www.tu-darmstadt.de/universitaet/aktuelles_meldungen/einzelansicht_566528.en.jsp)

### 3. Multiverse Computing & NVIDIA Release Compact "Pulsar 16B" Model
- **The Manila Times** · 2026-06-23
- **Summary**: Multiverse Computing, in collaboration with NVIDIA, launched Pulsar 16B, an open reasoning model under Apache 2.0. Using advanced compression, it delivers the performance of a 30B-parameter model in a 16B footprint with only 3.1B active parameters . It matches or outperforms larger models like gpt-oss-20B on benchmarks such as AIME 2025 and GPQA-Diamond, while offering a 43% increase in system throughput .
- **Why It Matters**: Pulsar 16B addresses the cost and hardware barriers to deploying frontier AI, making high-performance reasoning viable for single-node, on-premises, and high-concurrency agentic workflows .
- **URL**: [Multiverse Computing Launches Pulsar 16B in collaboration with NVIDIA](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/06/23/tmt-newswire/globenewswire/multiverse-computing-launches-pulsar-16b-in-collaboration-with-nvidia-frontier-grade-reasoning-at-half-the-parameters/2371017)

### 4. Open-Source "RaDaR" Model Accelerates Rare Disease Diagnosis
- **arXiv** · 2026-06-23
- **Summary**: Researchers introduced RaDaR (Rare Disease navigatoR), a specialized 32B-parameter open-source reasoning model. In a randomized physician-assistance trial, RaDaR improved diagnostic accuracy by 21.44 percentage points compared to internet searches alone . It also demonstrated the ability to prioritize correct diagnoses 1.87 months before clinical suspicion in a retrospective cohort .
- **Why It Matters**: This highlights the potential of specialized, open-source models to solve critical societal challenges in data-scarce environments. It demonstrates that focused reasoning models can outperform generalist giants like DeepSeek-R1 in specific, high-stakes domains .
- **URL**: [A specialized reasoning large language model for accelerating rare disease diagnosis](https://export.arxiv.org/abs/2606.24510)

### 5. OSI Launches "Open Source AI Fellowship" at the UN
- **TMCnet** · 2026-06-23
- **Summary**: The Open Source Initiative (OSI) launched a two-year Open Source AI Fellowship at UN Open Source Week. Supported by Duke University and industry partners like Red Hat, AWS, and Google, the program will research the value flow of open-source AI systems and analyze models released since the Open Source AI Definition (OSAID) v1.0 . This follows the G7's recent adoption of terminology based on the OSAID .
- **Why It Matters**: The fellowship represents a formal push to define and measure what "Open Source AI" truly means in practice, moving beyond a mere label. This institutional effort will shape future policy and governance for the entire open-source AI ecosystem .
- **URL**: [Open Source Initiative Launches Open Source AI Fellowship at UN Open Source Week](https://www.tmcnet.com/usubmit/2026/06/23/10404326.htm)

### 6. Developers Successfully Run Massive GLM-5.2 on Local Hardware
- **Yahoo News** · 2026-06-24
- **Summary**: The buzz around Z.ai's GLM-5.2 has intensified as developers demonstrated its ability to run locally on high-end Apple hardware.
