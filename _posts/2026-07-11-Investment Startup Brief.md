# Investment Startup Brief — 2026-07-11

## Top Stories

### 1. **Lawn Tennis Association Enters Venture Capital to Back Sports Startups**

* **Source** · Publish Date: ([Financial Times][1])
* **Summary**: The UK’s Lawn Tennis Association (LTA) is moving into venture investing through a partnership with Redrice Ventures to support sports-related startups. The initiative targets areas including smart court technology, online coaching, and sports nutrition while creating new revenue opportunities beyond traditional tennis operations. ([Financial Times][1])
* **Why It Matters**: Sports organizations are increasingly becoming strategic investors, using venture capital to capture growth in adjacent technology markets and diversify business models. ([Financial Times][1])

### 2. **Ashton Kutcher’s Decimal Capital Targets $500M Fund for Next-Generation Technology Startups**

* **Source** · Publish Date: ([The Wall Street Journal][2])
* **Summary**: Decimal Capital, founded by Ashton Kutcher and Morgan Beller, is seeking to raise a $500 million venture fund focused on emerging technology opportunities. The firm plans to invest across early and growth-stage companies, with a focus on technologies positioned around the “post-AGI” market. ([The Wall Street Journal][2])
* **Why It Matters**: The fund reflects continued investor appetite for large-scale AI and frontier technology bets, while highlighting the growing role of specialized venture firms targeting next-wave computing opportunities. ([The Wall Street Journal][2])
* **URL**: [Ashton Kutcher's Decimal Capital Seeks $500 Million](https://www.wsj.com/pro/venture-capital/ashton-kutchers-decimal-capital-seeks-500-million-93947791)

### 3. **Indian Startup Funding Reaches $72M Weekly Level as Investors Maintain Activity**

* **Source** · Publish Date: 2026-07-11 ([Inc42 Media][3])
* **Summary**: Indian startups raised approximately $71.9 million across 17 deals during the week ending July 10, 2026. While funding declined from the previous week, investor activity remained distributed across sectors, with new venture funds and IPO-related developments continuing. ([Inc42 Media][3])
* **Why It Matters**: India’s startup ecosystem continues to attract venture attention despite selective capital deployment, suggesting investors are prioritizing quality opportunities rather than broad market expansion. ([Inc42 Media][3])
* **URL**: [From Elevate Education To Aukera — Indian Startups Raised $72 Mn This Week](https://inc42.com/features/from-elevate-education-to-aukera-indian-startups-raised-72-mn-this-week/)

### 4. **India’s Weekly Tech Funding Momentum Improves With Yotta Data Services Raise**

* **Source** · Publish Date: 2026-07-11 ([The Economic Times][4])
* **Summary**: Indian technology funding activity increased to roughly $228 million across 21 deals during the reported week, supported by a major fundraising round from Yotta Data Services. The increase represents stronger year-over-year momentum in startup investment activity. ([The Economic Times][4])
* **Why It Matters**: The rebound indicates renewed investor confidence in technology infrastructure and AI-related businesses, particularly as demand for compute and digital services expands. ([The Economic Times][4])
* **URL**: [ETtech Deals Digest: Yotta fundraise lifts weekly tech funding to $228 million](https://m.economictimes.com/tech/funding/ettech-deals-digest-yotta-fundraise-lifts-weekly-tech-funding-to-228-million-up-95-on-year/articleshow/132315805.cms)
