### 2. 🌐 United Nations Highlights Human-Centric Digital Governance Through New AI Advocacy Role

*Tech Insider · October 11, 2026*

**Bottom line:** A reported UN-linked advocacy initiative is bringing greater public attention to human-centric digital governance, corporate accountability, and the societal implications of AI deployment.
