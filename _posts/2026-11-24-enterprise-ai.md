# Enterprise AI Brief: Sunway Builds Sovereign AI Platform With HPE

**Today:** New deployments and platforms across Asia and other markets show enterprises building shared data and governance foundations to move AI from experimentation into operational workflows.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **Sunway Selects HPE for Sovereign AI Backbone**

*HPE · November 24, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Malaysia’s Sunway Group selected HPE Private Cloud AI to establish a shared AI environment across its businesses.

HPE says the platform will combine its infrastructure with NVIDIA accelerated computing and software, with data kept within Sunway’s own ecosystem. Initial applications include productivity tools, knowledge assistants and content creation; HPE Services will also support use-case development and staff training.

**Why it matters:** A common, multi-tenant foundation could let Sunway’s 13 divisions develop and assess use cases under a more consistent governance model. The deal is a notable Southeast Asian example of a conglomerate pairing sovereign infrastructure with a plan to scale agentic AI.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.hpe.com/us/en/newsroom/press-release/2026/11/sunway-group-accelerates-secure-and-sovereign-enterprise-wide-artificial-intelligence-with-hpe.html)

### 2. 🤖 **OceanBase Launches AI Data Platform**

*PR Newswire · November 24, 2026*

**Bottom line:** OceanBase introduced OceanLake, an integrated data platform designed to support enterprise AI applications and agents.

The company says the platform combines database, data warehouse and data lake capabilities for structured and unstructured information. OceanBase also open-sourced OceanSeek, an AI database for agent scenarios, and reported that international revenue doubled in 2026, with Southeast Asia as its main overseas market.

**Why it matters:** The launch links enterprise AI readiness to data infrastructure that can span transactional, analytical and agent workloads. OceanBase’s regional customer base in payments and financial services makes its expansion relevant to Asia’s banks, fintechs and other data-intensive businesses.

### 3. 🤖 **Great Ormond Street Starts Agentic AI Programme**

*Great Ormond Street Hospital · November 24, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Great Ormond Street Hospital is beginning a 15-month programme with Hyland and AWS to test agentic AI in hospital operations.

The NHS England-funded programme will first assess supplier reconciliation and staff access to policy information, running tools alongside existing manual processes. The hospital says each phase will be reviewed for safety, accuracy, governance and operational value, with staff oversight and no AI clinical decision-making.

**Why it matters:** The staged evaluation offers a practical model for testing agents in complex, regulated operations before expanding their remit. Its findings, expected to be shared across the NHS, could inform how healthcare providers assess workflow automation and procurement.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.gosh.nhs.uk/news/gosh-leading-first-of-its-kind-agentic-ai-programme-in-an-nhs-hospital-in-collaboration-with-hyland-and-amazon-web-services/)

### 4. 🤖 **Salesforce Contact Centre Reaches ANZ**

*Salesforce · November 24, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Salesforce made its Contact Centre generally available in Australia and New Zealand, combining AI agents, human service teams, voice, digital channels and CRM data.

The company says the platform supports AI-to-human handoffs with customer history and transcripts available to staff. It also reports an average 76% resolution rate for its Fin service agent across more than 12,000 customers; this is a company-reported figure.

**Why it matters:** The ANZ launch brings an integrated agent-enabled contact centre to a region where firms serve customers across multiple channels and jurisdictions. Buyers will need to assess how the shared customer record, local data-residency provisions and reported resolution rates fit their own service operations.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.salesforce.com/au/news/press-releases/2026/11/24/salesforce-contact-centre-now-generally-available-in-australia-and-new-zealand/?bc=OTH)

### 5. 🤖 **Atos Opens Sovereign Agentic Studio in China**

*GlobeNewswire · November 24, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Atos launched a China-based Sovereign Agentic Studio to help organisations develop and deploy agentic AI under local operating requirements.

The company describes two components: an orchestrator for end-to-end service workflows and an AI Control Plane for managing model access, usage and costs. Atos says the control plane connects enterprise applications to more than 50 LLM providers and 320-plus models.

**Why it matters:** The launch reflects demand for enterprise AI services that combine orchestration with visibility into model usage and spending. For organisations operating across Asia, the studio’s focus on data sovereignty and regional compliance may be relevant where deployment choices are shaped by local requirements.

### 7. 🤖 **Levenza Makes Personal AI Agents Globally Available**

*GlobeNewswire · November 23, 2026*

### 8. 🤖 **Datassential Connects Industry Data to AI Tools**

*PR Newswire · November 24, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Datassential launched an AI agent that connects its foodservice operator data to enterprise AI tools, alongside a global menu-analysis product.

The agent uses Model Context Protocol to make the company’s operator database available to tools including Claude, ChatGPT and Copilot. Datassential says users can ask questions in plain language and access the data through a secure, read-only connection.

**Why it matters:** The product illustrates how specialist data providers are making their information available inside customers’ existing AI environments. For food and beverage companies, the value will depend on how well the connected data supports sales targeting, market analysis and product decisions.

## Key Takeaways

- Several announcements pair agents with shared data foundations, governance controls or existing business systems, indicating that integration remains central to enterprise deployment.
- Asia is a prominent part of this edition, from Sunway’s sovereign AI infrastructure and OceanBase’s regional expansion to new product availability in Singapore and China.
- Early adoption claims are mostly company-reported; customer usage, workflow outcomes and clearly defined delivery milestones will provide stronger evidence of business value.

## What to Watch

Watch for Sunway’s first scaled use cases and OceanBase’s enterprise deployments of OceanLake, particularly in Southeast Asia. Great Ormond Street Hospital’s phased reviews will also offer evidence on how agent performance, oversight and operational value are assessed in practice.
