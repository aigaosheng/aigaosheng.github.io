### 2. SEC publishes notice advancing exchange rules for trading securities in tokenized form
- **SEC** · 2026-06-05
- **Summary**: The SEC posted a notice of filing and immediate effectiveness for an NYSE American rule change to enable the trading of securities on the exchange in tokenized form. While this is a rulemaking and market-structure step rather than a market launch, it is one of the clearest official signals that regulated U.S. exchange plumbing is adapting to tokenized securities. The notice gives market participants a concrete regulatory document to track rather than relying on speeches or industry speculation.
- **Why It Matters**: Tokenized assets need regulated secondary-market rails to scale. Formal exchange-rule progress is more consequential than promotional commentary because it can directly shape how tokenized securities trade within existing U.S. market structure.
- **URL**: https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/self-regulatory-organization-rulemaking/sr-nyseamer-2026-36
