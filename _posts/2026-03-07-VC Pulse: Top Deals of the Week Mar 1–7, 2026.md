## 📊 Trend Commentary & Actionable Insights

**1. AI Infrastructure is the New Semiconductor Race.**
Ayar Labs' $500M raise underscores a growing consensus that copper interconnect limits are AI's next hard bottleneck, with NVIDIA and AMD placing direct bets on photonic solutions. This is a picks-and-shovels play — investors should watch the CPO (co-packaged optics) supply chain closely.

**2. Commercial Space is Having a Moment.**
Three separate space deals this week (Sierra Space, Vast, PLD Space) totaling ~$1.26B signal that LEO infrastructure and launch vehicles are graduating from speculative to strategic. Wayve's $1.2B autonomous vehicle raise and space mega-deals reflect rising investor appetite for deep-tech partnerships with industrials and sovereigns.

**3. Mental Health Tech is Consolidating Around Winners.**
Grow Therapy has become one of the few behavioral health companies to sustain large funding rounds as the market rationalized from COVID-era enthusiasm, now nearing $1B revenue — a sign the sector is entering a high-growth, winner-takes-most phase. Ease Health's a16z-backed stealth launch signals continued institutional conviction.

**4. Sequoia + a16z Domination Continues.**
Both firms appear in multiple deals this week across fintech (Rowspace), health (Grow Therapy, Ease Health), and deep tech (Nominal, Science Corp.), reinforcing their position as the most active post-seed investors, each participating in 100+ rounds in 2025.

**5. Crypto Cooling, AI Heating.**
Crypto startups raised just $883M in February — a 13% year-over-year decline — as investors shift toward revenue-generating AI and infrastructure plays. Reallocation from Web3 to AI infrastructure is now a macro theme.
