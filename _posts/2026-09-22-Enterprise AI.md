### 9. **Banks Warn That AI Shopping Agents Could Create New Fraud and Privacy Risks**

* **Source**: Reuters · September 22, 2026
* **Summary**: Banks including NatWest, Bank of America, ING, Capital One and Commonwealth Bank of Australia warned about emerging risks from AI-powered shopping agents. Concerns include scams, incorrect purchases, payment-method manipulation, exposure of card information and inadequate transparency when agents transact on behalf of consumers.
