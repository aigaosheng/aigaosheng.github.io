## Market Insight

The venture market continues to exhibit two dominant trends. First, investment capital remains heavily concentrated in AI infrastructure, semiconductor, robotics, and physical AI companies capable of supporting the next generation of intelligent systems. Second, while exit markets are gradually reopening through IPOs and acquisitions, fundraising remains challenging for emerging venture firms, with institutional investors preferring established managers and larger funds.

These dynamics suggest that capital availability remains abundant for category leaders and strategic technologies, while competition for early-stage funding outside priority sectors remains selective.
