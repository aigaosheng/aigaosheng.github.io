### 4. **Agoda Report Finds Agent Adoption Accelerating Faster Than Enterprise Readiness in Southeast Asia and India**

* **Summary**: Agoda's 2026 AI Developer Report found that 53% of surveyed developers across Southeast Asia and India have AI agents in production or broad organizational use, while only 38% consider their codebases ready for full autonomy. # Executive Takeaways

* **The enterprise AI control plane is emerging as a distinct infrastructure layer.** Identity, permissions, policy enforcement, observability, cost controls, and auditability are increasingly being designed specifically for AI agents.
* **Agent adoption is moving faster than organizational readiness.** Cost, integration complexity, data quality, evaluation, and governance remain major constraints on autonomous deployment.
* **The next enterprise AI battleground is workflow execution.** Products increasingly connect models directly to CRM, email, market data, operational systems, and business processes rather than stopping at conversational assistance.
* **Data infrastructure is becoming agent infrastructure.** Structured, current, permission-aware enterprise data is increasingly a prerequisite for reliable autonomous action.
* **Agentic commerce is extending the AI stack into transactions.** Product discovery, inventory, checkout, identity, authorization, and payments will need to become machine-operable as software agents increasingly participate in purchasing decisions.
