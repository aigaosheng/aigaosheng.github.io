### AI Fintech Brief — 2026-06-20

## Top Stories

### 1. LUMIQ Raises Strategic Funding to Scale Autonomous AI Decisioning in Financial Services
- **The Manila Times** · 2026-06-19
- **Summary**: LUMIQ, an AI-native fintech, has secured a strategic funding round led by Bajaj Finserv to expand its "LiteCone" platform. The platform deploys autonomous, auditable AI agents that make final decisions in regulated financial processes—such as underwriting and claims—rather than just providing recommendations. This allows institutions to cut decision times from days to minutes, with one leading life insurer reducing underwriting costs by 25% .
- **Why It Matters**: This signifies a major shift from AI as an "assistant" to AI as an "actor" in highly regulated environments. The investment validates the market need for explainable and compliant autonomous systems, potentially redefining operational efficiency benchmarks across banking and insurance sectors.

### 2. SBI Holdings Backs Pints AI for Compliance-First Agentic Platform
- **LeapRate** · 2026-06-19
- **Summary**: SBI Holdings has co-led an early-stage funding round for Pints AI, a Singapore-based company developing "Autothought," an agentic AI platform for financial institutions. The platform automates manual processes like underwriting and client onboarding while generating traceable audit trails and ensuring model sovereignty. Deployed at 12 institutions, it has already cut underwriting and onboarding times by 40% and 70%, respectively .
- **Why It Matters**: The investment highlights the growing importance of "compliance-first" AI in regulated markets, a key differentiator for APAC fintechs. Pints AI's success demonstrates that agentic AI can deliver significant cost savings while adhering to strict privacy and regulatory standards, a crucial requirement for widespread institutional adoption.
- **URL**: [SBI Holdings Backs Pints AI in Early Funding Round for Compliance-First Agentic Platform](https://www.leaprate.com/forex/institutional/sbi-holdings-backs-pints-ai-in-early-funding-round-for-compliance-first-agentic-platform/)
