### US AI Plus Brief — 2026-06-20

## Top Stories

### 1. Trump Says Anthropic No Longer a National Security Threat After G7 Meeting
- **Axios / Bloomberg** · 2026-06-20
- **Summary**: President Trump stated in an Axios interview that he no longer views AI company Anthropic as a national security threat, a reversal from his position just a week prior. This shift follows a meeting with CEO Dario Amodei at the G7 Summit and the company's compliance with administration directives to restrict foreign access to its advanced AI models .
- **Why It Matters**: This de-escalation signals a potential thaw in the administration's aggressive stance towards AI leaders, though the underlying tensions and regulatory scrutiny remain. The Commerce Department's unprecedented intervention into Anthropic's operations last week  caused significant disruption, and the company's future IPO valuation could be sensitive to regulatory burdens .
- **URL**: [Trump tells Axios that he doesn’t see Anthropic as US security threat](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/world/trump-tells-axios-that-he-doesnt-see-anthropic-as-us-security-threat/article71125000.ece)

### 2. Anthropic Models Remained Disabled as Export Curbs Stay in Place
- **Digital Today** · 2026-06-20
- **Summary**: Despite Trump’s softened rhetoric, existing restrictions on Anthropic remain. Foreign nationals still require Commerce Department approval to access the **Mythos 5** and **Fable 5** models , which the company disabled for all users on June 12 to comply with a direct presidential order . A separate Pentagon supply-chain risk ban on Anthropic, imposed in March, is also still in effect .
- **Why It Matters**: The hard regulatory reality persists regardless of the President’s personal assurances. This ongoing uncertainty creates a complex operational environment for Anthropic and could affect its standing with government clients, while the company has expressed a desire for a swift resolution in partnership with the administration .
- **URL**: [Trump backtracks on Anthropic threat claims, keeps access curbs and federal ban](https://www.digitaltoday.co.kr/en/view/70932/trump-backtracks-on-anthropic-threat-claims-keeps-access-curbs-and-federal-ban)

### 3. Trump Praises AI's Potential, Suggests Companies Build Own Power Plants
- **Axios / KuCoin** · 2026-06-20
- **Summary**: In the same interview, Trump lauded AI's transformative potential, stating it could surpass the internet in impact and help discover medical cures 25 years early. He also advised AI companies to build their own power plants to meet the massive energy demands of the technology, reinforcing that the US remains significantly ahead of China in AI development .
- **Why It Matters**: This highlights a critical strategic bottleneck for the AI industry: energy infrastructure. His comments suggest a policy direction that encourages private investment in energy capacity to sustain US leadership, framing AI dominance as a matter of national strategic importance .
