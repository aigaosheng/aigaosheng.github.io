# Quantum Brief: EU Proposes Quantum Act And Equity Fund

**Today:** Europe is proposing a framework to turn quantum research into industrial capacity, while a Singapore-backed bank programme and new industry projects show how institutions are building practical expertise.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🏦 **European Commission Proposes Quantum Act**

*European Commission · November 25, 2026*

**Bottom line:** The Commission’s proposed Quantum Act would coordinate European quantum research and industry efforts, strengthen supply chains and prepare digital systems for post-quantum cryptography.

The proposal also lays the groundwork for a Sovereign Tech Equity Fund to finance strategic technologies, including quantum. The Commission says the Act would connect EU and national programmes across research, industrial deployment and security; the proposal does not set a fixed budget for the fund.

**Why it matters:** The initiative combines support for quantum companies and infrastructure with supply-chain resilience and cryptographic preparation. For Asian firms, it is a signal to track as Europe develops its own quantum capabilities and procurement priorities.

### 2. ⚛️ **Standard Chartered Expands Quantum Finance Research**

*The Quantum Insider · November 25, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Standard Chartered plans to extend its quantum computing research programme after a first year supported under an MAS-administered scheme.

The bank’s work covered language modelling, time-series forecasting and derivatives pricing, with research involving OQC, Quantinuum, Rigetti and Qubitra. Standard Chartered reported that tests showed efficiency gains in selected scenarios, including comparable derivatives-pricing accuracy with fewer computational iterations; these are research results reported by the bank, not evidence of production deployment.

**Why it matters:** The programme gives a Singapore-based bank experience testing where quantum and quantum-inspired methods might complement conventional systems. The next phase can help financial institutions assess use cases while building technical knowledge before the hardware is mature.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/11/25/standard-chartered-expands-quantum-computing-research-program-following-successful-research-phase/)

### 3. ⚛️ **Pasqal Leads European Fault-Tolerance Project**

*Pasqal · November 24, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Pasqal is coordinating a three-year European Defence Fund research project with ParityQC and Thales Nederland to advance fault-tolerant quantum computing using neutral atoms.

CoPaNA will combine parity-based error correction with Pasqal’s neutral-atom hardware and examine hardware-software integration and potential defence applications. The announcement describes research goals, not a working fault-tolerant machine or a project budget.

**Why it matters:** The project brings hardware, architecture and defence expertise together around error correction, a key challenge to scaling quantum systems. Pasqal says the work complements its roadmap, which targets early fault-tolerant capabilities in 2029.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.pasqal.com/newsroom/pasqal-leads-european-defence-fund-project-to-advance-fault-tolerant-quantum-computing-with-neutral-atoms/)

### 4. 🔒 **SEALSQ Plans Quantum Highway Expansion**

*SEALSQ · November 24, 2026*

**Bottom line:** SEALSQ says it is working with data centre providers to connect Quantum Highway hubs across the United States, India and Europe.

The company describes a planned network combining local data centre infrastructure with hardware-based trust and post-quantum cryptography. It is also exploring an additional Asia-Pacific hub; the announcement does not identify a location or confirm a deployment.

**Why it matters:** The proposal links quantum-computing access with the security and local-control requirements of distributed infrastructure. Its Asia-Pacific plans remain exploratory, but any concrete regional site or partner would be relevant to enterprises assessing quantum-safe connectivity.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.sealsq.com/investors/news-releases/sealsq-is-working-with-data-center-providers-to-connect-its-quantum-highway-hubs-across-the-u.s.-and-europe-to-enhance-cybersecurity-protections)

## Key Takeaways

- Quantum initiatives are increasingly pairing research with plans for industrial capacity, infrastructure and supply-chain resilience.
- Financial institutions are testing targeted quantum and quantum-inspired use cases, while treating current results as research rather than production capability.
- Post-quantum preparation is appearing alongside quantum-computing expansion, making cryptographic readiness relevant to infrastructure planning.

## What to Watch

Watch how the European Parliament and Council respond to the Commission’s Quantum Act proposal, and whether the proposed equity fund gains defined terms or funding commitments. Pasqal’s CoPaNA presentation at the European Defence Agency workshop on November 26 and Standard Chartered’s next research phase are near-term signals to follow.
