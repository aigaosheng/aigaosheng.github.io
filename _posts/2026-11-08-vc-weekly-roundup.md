## The week in one paragraph

Capital kept concentrating in fewer, larger rounds: AI infrastructure, defense, space and physical AI took most of the big cheques. OpenAI's first close landed at a lower valuation than it first sought, and Sequoia announced about $10B of new funds while shrinking its footprint. In Europe, US funds co-led a EUR 100M hypersonic-missile round.

## Trends

1. **Mega-round concentration.** The largest US rounds this week were defense, drones, space and AI infrastructure, each in the hundreds of millions to billions.
2. **Inference and compute are the hot layer.** fal doubled its valuation in about six months, and Applied Compute raised $350M at $3.25B.
3. **Defense and sovereign tech.** Shield AI, Hypersonica, CesiumAstro and Northwood show defense and space attracting growth capital, including US funds in Europe.
4. **Agentic AI for enterprise ops and security.** Sentra, Traversal and Ocient raised $100M or more each.
5. **VC reshaping.** Sequoia is contracting to a Bay Area-centered firm, while Creandum scales up in San Francisco.
6. **Pricing discipline at the top.** OpenAI's first close came in below earlier targets, which reports link to an AI equity sell-off (attribution is from secondary sources).

## Actionable insights

- **Track OpenAI's remaining closes.** Check whether the round reaches ~$20B and whether $1.25T is pre- or post-money. The answer changes comparables.
- **Underwrite inference on supply, not just demand.** Ask for compute contracts and gross margin before paying for growth.
- **Defense in Europe:** follow procurement budgets and export-control exposure, not only round size.
- **Sequoia and Creandum moves:** expect more competition for European seed and Series A deals from US-anchored funds.
- **Fintech and payments:** note strategic backers (Capital One Ventures in Sentra, Mastercard and J.P. Morgan Payments in Firmly) as signals of incumbent interest.
