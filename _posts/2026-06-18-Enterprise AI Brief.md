### 5. Celebrus Launches Conversational Analytics with Live First-Party Data
- **CMSWire** · 2026-06-17
- **Summary**: Celebrus Technologies has launched Celebrus AI, a conversational analytics capability that allows business users to query live, identity-resolved first-party behavioral data using Anthropic Claude, Microsoft Copilot, or OpenAI ChatGPT. By enabling conversational queries against complete, compliant first-party data within the customer's governance boundary, it unlocks customer data value without compromising privacy—particularly relevant for financial services, healthcare, and insurance .
