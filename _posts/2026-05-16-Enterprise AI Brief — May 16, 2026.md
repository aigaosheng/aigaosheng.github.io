### Enterprise AI Brief — May 16, 2026

The enterprise AI landscape is moving decisively from experimentation to execution this week, with major shifts in market leadership, infrastructure strategy, and workforce design. As organizations scale beyond pilot programs, the battleground has shifted to agentic operations, data sovereignty, and measurable business outcomes.

- **Anthropic Surpasses OpenAI in Enterprise Market Share for First Time**

- **NTT DATA Research: Enterprise AI Hits Infrastructure and Sovereignty Limits**
- **Source** · IT Brief New Zealand · May 15, 2026
- **Summary** — New research from NTT DATA reveals that more than 95% of enterprises consider private and sovereign AI important, yet only 29% are making it a near-term priority. Only 38% of respondents expressed high confidence in their cloud security posture.
- **Why It Matters** — As data sovereignty becomes a core design constraint rather than a compliance afterthought, organizations face fundamental architecture decisions that will determine their ability to deploy AI across multiple jurisdictions.
