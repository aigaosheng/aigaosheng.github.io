- **Source**: Reuters · 2026-07-14

### 3. CXMT Sets Date for Asia's Largest IPO of 2026
- **Source**: Reuters · 2026-07-14
- **Summary**: Chinese memory-chip manufacturer ChangXin Memory Technologies (CXMT) plans to list on Shanghai's STAR Market on July 27, targeting approximately 29.5 billion yuan in new funding. Strong AI server demand has accelerated the company's expansion and elevated it to the world's fourth-largest DRAM supplier.
- **Why It Matters**: The IPO highlights China's determination to build domestic semiconductor capacity and reduce dependence on foreign AI memory suppliers.
- **URL**: https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/chinas-changxin-memory-technologies-sets-july-27-listing-date-sources-say-2026-07-14/
