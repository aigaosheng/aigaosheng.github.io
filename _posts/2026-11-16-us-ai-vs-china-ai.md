# US AI vs China AI Brief: Chinese AI Stocks Slide on Chip Curb Fears

**Today:** Only one qualifying, verifiable story emerged in this window, and it shows markets pricing in tighter US chip limits on Chinese AI developers.

## Top Stories

### 1. 📊 **Chinese AI Stocks ZAI and Minimax Tumble on Chip Restriction Fears**

*Investing.com · November 15, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Investors are marking down China's listed AI model developers on concern that Washington will restrict their access to the latest Nvidia hardware.

Investing.com reported that ZAI and Minimax led losses among Chinese AI names, with shares down 13.9% and 11.2% respectively. Both stocks are down roughly 18% to 25% in November. Minimax has given back all its gains since its January IPO, and ZAI is close to its own IPO price. The article ties the selloff to the Nvidia governance row and to fears that Chinese developers could lose access to offshore servers running the newest Nvidia chips. It notes that Tencent, Alibaba and Baidu rose while Kuaishou slipped 1.2%.

**Why it matters:** Pure-play Chinese model developers look more exposed to compute-access risk than the large platform companies, which may have more domestic chip options and larger balance sheets. For Singapore and wider Southeast Asia, the concern centres on offshore data-centre capacity, which is where reported US scrutiny of Chinese compute rentals is focused.

## Key Takeaways

- Chip-access policy is now a visible driver of equity valuations for China's newly listed AI developers, not only a geopolitical issue.
- Market reaction is uneven: smaller model developers fell sharply, while the larger platform companies named in the report rose.
- Compute sourced through third-country data centres remains the central pressure point in US-China AI competition.

## What to Watch

Watch whether Commerce publishes any rule on remote or offshore access to advanced chips, and the Senate Banking Committee testimony by Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick that CBS News reported is planned for December 10. Also track whether ZAI and Minimax recover or fall further.
