### 1. Malaysia begins enforcing an under-16 social media ban
- **AP News** · 2026-06-01
- **Summary**: Malaysia began enforcing rules that bar children under 16 from holding accounts on major social media platforms, including Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube. The policy requires age verification for platforms with at least 8 million local users and adds stronger obligations around harmful-content mitigation, manipulated-content labeling, and platform governance. The move places Malaysia among the most aggressive governments globally in shifting youth online-safety enforcement from parents to platforms.
- **Why It Matters**: This is one of the clearest signs that AI-adjacent platform harms, including manipulated media and algorithmic exposure risks, are accelerating hard-regulation of social platforms. It raises the compliance bar for global platforms operating across Asia and adds momentum to age-assurance and trust-and-safety infrastructure as strategic product requirements.
