## Key Takeaway

Enterprise AI is clearly entering a **capital-intensive consolidation phase**:  
- Large contracts (HCLTech–Mercedes-Benz scale deals) show AI is now embedded in core enterprise infrastructure  
- Vendors like SAP are reallocating budgets aggressively toward AI platforms  
- “Agentic AI” is emerging as the dominant architectural direction, pushing toward autonomous enterprise systems  

The competitive edge is shifting from experimentation → deployment → operational autonomy.
