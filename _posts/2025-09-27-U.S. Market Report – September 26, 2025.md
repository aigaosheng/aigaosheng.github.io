**U.S. Market Report – September 26, 2025**

---

### Executive Summary

U.S. equities closed higher on Friday, with major indexes snapping a three-day losing streak. Investors appeared to overlook persistent inflation data and new tariffs announced by President Trump, focusing instead on favorable economic indicators. Foreign investment in U.S. stocks surged to record levels, signaling confidence despite geopolitical tensions.

---

### Detailed Analysis

#### Market Overview

* **Major Indexes**: The S&P 500 ETF (SPY) rose 0.56% to $661.82, the Nasdaq-100 ETF (QQQ) gained 0.42% to $595.97, and the Dow Jones ETF (DIA) increased 0.63% to $462.28.

* **Inflation & Tariffs**: Despite sticky inflation data and President Trump's new round of tariffs, stocks climbed, with investors looking past these concerns. ([The Wall Street Journal][1])

#### Sector Movements

* **Technology**: Microsoft (MSFT) rose 0.89% to $511.46, while Meta Platforms (META) declined 0.70% to $743.75.

* **Consumer Goods**: Amazon (AMZN) gained 0.75% to $219.78.

* **Automotive**: Tesla (TSLA) surged 3.98% to $440.40, driven by strong investor sentiment.

* **Semiconductors**: NVIDIA (NVDA) edged up 0.28% to $178.19.

#### Foreign Investment

Foreigners are buying U.S. stocks at a record pace, despite trade war concerns. This influx suggests confidence in the U.S. market's resilience and long-term prospects. ([Bloomberg][2])
