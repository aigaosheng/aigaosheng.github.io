## Top Stories  

### 1. White House to Expand AI Power Cost Pledge to Utilities and Data Centers  
- **Reuters via CNA** · 2026-07-13  
- **Summary**: The White House plans to convene utility companies and data center developers to sign a voluntary pledge ensuring the rapid growth in AI-driven electricity demand does not increase power bills for households. This follows a previous "Ratepayer Protection Pledge" signed by major tech firms like Amazon, Google, and Microsoft, and the new initiative aims to broaden these commitments.   
- **Why It Matters**: As AI infrastructure scales, the cost of grid upgrades is a critical friction point. This move signals a continued executive push to align AI expansion with consumer protection, aiming to preempt a political backlash over rising energy costs while maintaining the pace of development.  
- **URL**: [Read more](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/business/white-house-rally-utilities-data-centers-ai-power-cost-pledge-sources-say-6250831)  

### 2. Senator Markey Unveils Comprehensive "AI Accountability Agenda"  
- **Senator Ed Markey (.gov)** · 2026-07-10  
- **Summary**: Senator Edward J. Markey released a new legislative vision for AI policy, "The AI Accountability Agenda: Taking Power Back from Big Tech." The agenda includes six priorities, such as protecting workers from automated surveillance, safeguarding children's privacy, preventing AI bias in civil rights decisions, and mandating human override options in healthcare AI. It also includes a discussion draft requiring federal certification for new data centers and investments in clean energy.   
- **Why It Matters**: This agenda represents a significant, comprehensive legislative counterweight to the executive branch's deregulatory approach. It proposes concrete, sector-specific guardrails that would impose substantial compliance burdens on AI developers and data center operators if enacted.  
- **URL**: [Read more](https://www.markey.senate.gov/news/press-releases/senator-markey-releases-the-ai-accountability-agenda-taking-power-back-from-big-tech)  

### 3. Anthropic Regains Access to Export Markets for Advanced AI Tools  
- **BBC** · 2026-07-01  
- **Summary**: The US government has lifted the export controls on Anthropic's most advanced AI models, Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5, just weeks after a suspension order was issued over national security concerns. Anthropic stated it would begin restoring global access to the models, which the company believes were recalled due to a "narrow potential jailbreak."   
- **Why It Matters**: The quick reversal highlights the tension between rapid AI commercialization and national security concerns. It also suggests that industry-government negotiations can lead to swift resolutions, easing a key business risk for frontier AI labs regarding the international deployment of their most powerful models.  
- **URL**: [Read more](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cdr42623e1do)  

*Note: A separate Reuters report indicates that in late June, the administration partially reversed the ban to allow Mythos 5 release to "trusted" US organizations only. The full lifting of export controls reported by the BBC represents a significant further step.*   

### 4. Presidential Directive Accelerates AI Adoption in National Security  
- **The White House (.gov)** · 2026-06-04  
- **Summary**: President Donald J. Trump signed a National Security Presidential Memorandum on AI, directing the rapid onboarding of advanced AI models for warfighters and intelligence professionals. The order mandates the buildout of high-security computing facilities, establishes an AI National Security Strategic Reserve of experts, and revokes the previous administration's NSM-25, which it labels as "outdated" and "ideological."   
- **Why It Matters**: This directive formalizes the push to embed frontier AI directly into defense and intelligence operations, creating a significant demand channel for AI vendors. It also sets a clear strategic direction for defense procurement, prioritizing speed and multi-vendor integration.  
- **URL**: [Read more](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2026/06/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-signs-historic-directive-on-ai-in-the-national-security-enterprise/)  

### 5. US Formalizes Plan for 30-Day Pre-Release Government Access to AI Models  
- **CGTN (AFP)** · 2026-06-03  
- **Summary**: President Trump signed an executive order creating a voluntary framework for AI developers to provide the US government with access to their most powerful models up to 30 days before public release. The order, which represents a compromise between an initial 90-day proposal and industry requests for 14 days, is seen as a key step in managing national security risks associated with frontier AI.   
- **Why It Matters**: This sets a new de facto standard for government-industry collaboration on AI safety in the US. While voluntary, it creates a norm of early notification and access that will shape the release strategies for all leading AI labs and could influence global regulatory standards.  
- **URL**: [Read more](https://news.cgtn.com/news/2026-06-03/Trump-signs-AI-order-giving-government-access-to-powerful-models-1NFrHYxHt9C/index.html)
