# **AI Avatars Move Beyond Text: Lemon Slice Raises $10.5M to Bring Interactive Video Bots to Life**

In a big vote of confidence for the future of interactive AI, **Lemon Slice**—a startup from the **Y Combinator** accelerator—has just secured **US $10.5 million in seed funding** to scale its pioneering video-based avatar technology. The round was led by **Matrix Partners** and **Y Combinator**, with participation from notable angels like Dropbox CTO **Arash Ferdowsi**, Twitch CEO **Emmett Shear**, and even music duo **The Chainsmokers**. ([Tech in Asia][1])

At a time when conversational AI has become ubiquitous in customer support, education, and digital assistants, Lemon Slice is pushing the envelope by giving these interactions a **face, voice, and real-time video presence**—effectively moving AI beyond static text and robotic clips toward something that feels genuinely alive. ([SiliconANGLE][2])

---

## **From One Photo to a Live, Conversational Avatar**

What makes Lemon Slice’s tech stand out is its flagship model, **Lemon Slice-2**:

* 🌟 **Zero-shot video avatars** — Upload a **single image** (whether a headshot, cartoon, or painting) and Lemon Slice-2 creates a fully animated, conversational avatar instantly. No extensive training data needed. ([SiliconANGLE][2])
* 💬 **Real-time interaction** — These avatars can talk, gesture, and respond live in sync with an AI agent, streaming at around **20 frames per second on a single GPU**—a notable performance benchmark for video generation. ([NewsBytes][3])
* 🔌 **Plug-and-play integration** — Businesses and developers can use Lemon Slice’s API or an **embeddable widget** to drop interactive avatars into websites and apps with minimal coding. ([Skool][4])
* 🎭 **Flexible character types** — The model supports **humans and non-humans alike**, letting brands craft experiences from friendly customer support guides to playful mascots. ([Skool][4])

These video avatars are positioned to go beyond novelty. Lemon Slice says they could power everything from **AI tutors and digital sales assistants** to **corporate onboarding bots**—environments where visual and emotional engagement matters. ([Tech in Asia][1])

---

## **Safety First: Guardrails and Moderation Built In**

With great realism comes responsibility. Lemon Slice has implemented **safety measures** to prevent unauthorized face or voice cloning, including **consent attestations** and AI-based content moderation. Avatars clearly signal to users when they’re interacting with AI, reinforcing transparency. ([Skool][4])

These precautions reflect broader industry concerns about misuse of generative AI—especially realistic avatars that could otherwise mimic real people without permission.

---

## **Why This Matters Now**

The rise of AI agents—especially text-based ones like chatbots and virtual assistants—is well underway. Lemon Slice’s video-first approach points to the *next frontier*: **avatar-led, interactive experiences that feel more human than chatbot windows ever could**. As digital communication increasingly mirrors face-to-face interaction, this shift could redefine how users connect with AI across sectors including education, e-commerce, training, and support. ([SiliconANGLE][2])

---

## **Glossary**

* **Zero-shot** – A capability where a model performs a task (e.g., generating an avatar) without requiring task-specific training data. ([SiliconANGLE][2])
* **API (Application Programming Interface)** – A set of tools that allows different software systems to communicate or integrate with one another. ([Skool][4])
* **Diffusion Model** – A type of generative AI model that learns to create new data (like images or video) by iteratively denoising random noise. ([Venture Capital][5])
