## Executive Takeaway

**Enterprise AI is moving from copilots to company-aware operating systems.**

The strongest signal today is not another frontier-model launch. It is the growing competition to control the **enterprise context layer**: company knowledge, identity, permissions, workflows, systems of record and institutional memory.

Three strategic shifts are becoming clearer:

1. **Context is becoming the enterprise AI moat.** Agents need access to trustworthy organizational knowledge and business context before autonomy can deliver reliable value.
2. **Vertical agents are replacing generic copilots.** Legal, finance, customer operations and other regulated domains increasingly require specialized skills, connectors and governance.
3. **AI economics are becoming operational economics.** As agents execute increasingly complex workflows, enterprises must manage inference costs, permissions, orchestration, observability and accountability—not merely AI subscriptions.

The emerging enterprise architecture is therefore less **“LLM + chatbot”** and more **“enterprise data + context + agents + workflow orchestration + governance.”**
