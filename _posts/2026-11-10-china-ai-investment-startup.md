# AI Investment & Startup in China Brief: AI Chipmaker Denglin Slides After Hong Kong IPO

**Today:** A wobbly Hong Kong trading debut for GPU maker Denglin, alongside four fresh funding rounds spanning physical-world AI, materials science, industrial robotics and enterprise agent infrastructure, shows China's AI capital markets and venture pipeline both staying active even as public-market sentiment cools.

## Top Stories

### 1. 📊 **Denglin Technology Shares Slip Below Issue Price on Hong Kong Debut**

*TechNode (动点科技) · November 10, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Suzhou-based GPU designer Denglin Technology raised roughly HK$1.41 billion pricing its Hong Kong IPO at the bottom of its range, and its shares fell as much as 6% below the HK$231 offer price on debut.

Denglin, which positions itself as a general-purpose AI compute GPU (GPGPU) provider, priced 6.125 million H-shares and drew just under half of the offering from cornerstone investors. The company reported roughly RMB 200 million in revenue for the first four months of 2026, already exceeding its full-year 2025 total, with enterprise private-cloud deployments making up 83% of that revenue.

**Why it matters:** Denglin's soft debut, following a roughly 40% drawdown in China's STAR50 AI and chip index over the past four months, suggests Hong Kong investors are growing more selective about domestic AI chip valuations even as the IPO pipeline for Chinese GPU makers stays open — a signal worth watching for Singapore and regional investors tracking China-linked AI equity exposure.

### 2. 🤖 **Four-Month-Old TensorWorld Raises Over $100M for Physical-World AI**

*QbitAI (量子位) · November 10, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Beijing startup TensorWorld (物原科技), founded only in July 2026, has raised more than $100 million across two rounds in a single month to build physics-based foundation models for robotics and autonomous driving.

Investors spanning its angel and angel-plus rounds include Sequoia China, Ant Group, Meituan's corporate venture arm, NIO Capital and IDG Capital. The company's founding team has academic ties to Tsinghua, MIT, Stanford and UC Berkeley, and its approach aims to have models learn physical dynamics rather than just predict video frames.

**Why it matters:** The speed and breadth of backers — spanning fintech, delivery and automotive investors — underscores how quickly Chinese capital is consolidating around "physical AI" as the next platform bet after large language models, a trend enterprise AI and robotics buyers across Asia should track closely.

### 3. 🔬 **Qingxi Zhiyuan Lands Near-RMB 100M Round for AI-Designed Materials**

*PEdaily (投资界) · November 10, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Beijing-based Qingxi Zhiyuan closed an angel round worth nearly RMB 100 million to build an AI-driven R&D platform for rare-earth and semiconductor materials.

Backers include Hillhouse Innovation, Unitree Robotics, Zhongxin Juyuan and several industry-linked funds. The company says its new rare-earth permanent magnet material is already in pilot conversion, targeting mass production in 2027, and that it has signed several million yuan in orders across manufacturing, semiconductor and aerospace clients within six months of founding.

**Why it matters:** The round illustrates growing VC appetite for AI applied to materials discovery rather than just software, a vertical that feeds directly into robotics and electronics supply chains that Southeast Asian manufacturers also depend on.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://news.pedaily.cn/202611/571607.shtml)

### 4. 🤖 **Forvision Intelligent Closes Pre-A Round for Industrial Robotics**

*PEdaily (投资界) · November 10, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Guangzhou-based Forvision Intelligent (富唯智能) raised tens of millions of yuan in a Pre-A round led by Youshan Capital to push industrial embodied AI from lab demonstrations into production lines.

The funding will support Forvision's "whole-arm manipulation" robotics technology, which the company says is already in commercial use across new-energy, 3C electronics and automotive-parts manufacturing. Existing strategic shareholder Heretai (和而泰) also increased its stake in the round.

**Why it matters:** The deal adds to a string of embodied-AI robotics rounds in China this week and signals continued investor confidence that industrial automation, rather than consumer robotics, is the nearer-term commercial path — relevant for manufacturers across the region evaluating AI-driven automation vendors.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://news.pedaily.cn/202611/571637.shtml)

### 5. 📊 **Tsinghua PhD Student's ZhiYi Tech Raises Seed Round for AI Agent Memory**

*PEdaily (投资界) · November 10, 2026*

**Bottom line:** ZhiYi Tech (知驿科技), founded in August 2026 by a 22-year-old Tsinghua PhD candidate, raised tens of millions of yuan in a seed round led by Zhuoyuan Asia to build an enterprise AI gateway with long-term "experience memory."

The startup's "Team Agent" concept lets AI agents share context and accumulated task experience across a team while staying model-neutral. It says its first product is due to launch in mid-November, with early users including compute and gaming startups and a signed deal with a leading embodied-AI firm.

**Why it matters:** The round is a small-ticket example of investors betting on agent-orchestration infrastructure rather than foundation models themselves — a layer directly relevant to enterprise AI and fintech teams building multi-agent workflows.

## Key Takeaways

- Capital is broadening beyond foundation models into physical AI, materials science, industrial robotics and agent infrastructure, with five distinct China AI deals announced in a single day.
- Public-market appetite for Chinese AI hardware names looks more cautious than private markets: Denglin's IPO priced at the low end and still traded below issue price, even as venture rounds for AI chips and robotics continue closing quickly.
- State-linked and strategic investors (Ant Group, Meituan, NIO Capital, Unitree, Hillhouse) are increasingly co-investing alongside traditional VC in embodied-AI and materials startups, blurring the line between corporate strategic bets and financial investment.

## What to Watch

Watch Denglin's share price in the days following its debut for signs of whether investor caution toward China's AI-chip sector (after the STAR50 index's recent drawdown) is easing or deepening, and watch whether TensorWorld's rapid back-to-back rounds attract a formal Series A announcement given the pace of its fundraising so far.
