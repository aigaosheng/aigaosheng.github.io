# Quantum Brief: Spain and Portugal Central Banks Launch Post-Quantum Program

**Today:** Payments and banking readers tracking post-quantum migration get two signals: two central banks are coordinating, and Cloudflare is shipping a region-locked, quantum-resistant key service.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🏦 **Spain and Portugal Central Banks Sign Post-Quantum Cooperation Pact**

*Banco de España · October 23, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Two euro-area central banks have committed to a joint program to coordinate how their financial sectors move to quantum-resistant cryptography.

Banco de España and Banco de Portugal signed a memorandum of understanding in Lisbon to set up a Post-Quantum Cryptography Cooperation Program. They say uncoordinated migration can create systemic risk, including through shared service providers, market infrastructures and interoperability dependencies. The program runs in three planned phases: sharing information and methods, then assessing shared dependencies and migration priorities, then testing and interoperability work, with an industry working group to support the later phases.

**Why it matters:** Supervisors are framing post-quantum migration as a coordination problem across banks, vendors and market infrastructure, not a single-firm IT upgrade. Asia-based payments and BaaS providers that depend on shared processors and card schemes should expect similar dependency-mapping questions from their own regulators (opinion).

### 2. 🔒 **Cloudflare Launches Closed Beta of Post-Quantum GeoKey Manager**

*Cloudflare Blog · October 23, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Cloudflare is pairing post-quantum key protection with regional controls so customer decryption keys can only be unwrapped on servers in an approved geography.

The beta wraps keys using a hybrid of ML-KEM-768 and X25519, and requests fail closed if a server lacks the required regional root key. General availability is planned for the first quarter of 2027. It starts with Cloudflare One paths such as Gateway and Tunnel, while CDN integration is a separate effort still to come. Cloudflare notes the feature governs where keys are unwrapped, not where traffic is routed or inspected, which falls under its Regional Services.

**Why it matters:** Data-residency obligations and "harvest now, decrypt later" risk are converging for regulated firms, and this is an early vendor attempt to address both in one control. Buyers should check exactly which services and traffic paths the beta covers before relying on it for compliance.

### 3. ⚛️ **IBM Says All Nine Winners of Japan's NEDO Quantum Challenge Used Its Hardware**

*IBM Quantum Blog · October 23, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Japan's first national quantum prize competition ran heavily on IBM systems, according to IBM's own account of the results.

NEDO, which operates under Japan's trade ministry METI, presented Season 1 awards on October 15 across Society 5.0, Quality of Life and Cool Japan tracks. IBM reports that all nine prize-winning teams used its quantum computers, six exclusively, and that 65 of 80 participating teams chose IBM. Winners include Shionogi, first place in drug discovery, whose final results came from simulation, and Q-Chainners, third place in Society 5.0 for a quantum hash function aimed at more trustworthy transactions, run on four IBM systems.

**Why it matters:** For Asia-based readers, the results show Japan's early application ecosystem clustering around one vendor's cloud and still at proof-of-concept stage. The transaction-trust entry is the closest to fintech. This is vendor-authored reporting, so platform-share figures should be read with that in mind.

### 4. 📊 **New York Study Ranks State Second in U.S. Quantum Activity**

*Office of Governor Kathy Hochul · October 23, 2026*

**Bottom line:** A state-commissioned study places New York second nationally in quantum businesses, research groups and patents, and ties the finding to a $360 million public commitment.

The study counts 134 operating entities, 219 research groups and 734 patent families in the state. The $360 million comprises $300 million for a Stony Brook research hub and $60 million for up to four regional commercialization hubs, which the study estimates could support $2.3 billion to $5 billion in economic activity and 7,200 to 10,100 job-years through 2036. The release also describes the state's financial services sector as one of the strongest concentrations of potential quantum users in the country.

**Why it matters:** Subnational governments are now competing directly for quantum talent and capital, a pattern also visible in Asia. The economic-impact figures are projections from a state-commissioned study, not realized outcomes.

## Key Takeaways

- Post-quantum migration is shifting from advice to operating structure: a phased, supervisor-led program in Iberia and a vendor product that ties quantum-resistant keys to data residency.
- Hybrid cryptography is the working pattern: Cloudflare pairs ML-KEM-768 with X25519 rather than replacing classical methods outright.
- Public programs are steering quantum ecosystems toward hubs and shared platforms, from New York's state funding to Japan's prize competition, where IBM reports most teams chose its cloud.
