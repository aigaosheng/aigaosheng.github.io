# Enterprise AI Brief: ServiceNow And LTM Expand Agentic AI Partnership

**Today:** Three fresh vendor moves show enterprise agentic AI shifting from pilots toward governed, enterprise-wide rollouts, with one deal carrying a direct Singapore government angle.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **ServiceNow and LTM Deepen Partnership to Scale Agentic AI**

*Business Wire · November 5, 2026*

**Bottom line:** ServiceNow and IT services firm LTM are expanding their alliance to push agentic AI from isolated pilots into governed, enterprise-wide deployment across 700-plus shared customers.

LTM, an Elite ServiceNow Partner, has deployed 1,500 ServiceNow AI agents internally and is positioning that rollout as a "client-zero" blueprint for customers. The companies said ServiceNow's Super Intelligence Control Tower gives LTM centralised oversight of its AI agent inventory, with LTM citing gains of up to 80% in AI asset management efficiency and 87% faster senior management review cycles.

**Why it matters:** The deal underscores a pattern among enterprise software vendors and their systems-integrator partners: governance and oversight tooling, not raw model capability, is now the gating factor for scaling agents beyond pilots. For Asia-based enterprises evaluating similar rollouts, LTM's internal deployment offers a concrete reference point for the controls boards will expect before approving agent fleets.

### 2. 🤖 **Salesforce Expands Agentforce Coworker Across Teams, Mobile and Desktop**

*Salesforce · November 5, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Salesforce says its Agentforce Coworker tool has grown 21-fold in three months to more than 185,000 users, and is now expanding into Microsoft Teams, iOS and desktop apps.

The company added Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 connectivity, cross-device conversation history and reusable "Skills" workflows, pricing the always-on digital assistant at US$50 per user per month. The Adecco Group cited the tool for giving staff a single interface across candidate and client context as it rolls Coworker out across 40 countries.

**Why it matters:** Rapid usage growth at this scale signals that "AI coworker" tools are moving past early adopters into mainstream enterprise workflows. Multinational employers in Singapore and the wider region running global HR and client-service operations will watch cross-platform reach and per-seat pricing closely as they budget for 2027 AI spend.

### 3. 🔒 **Cohere Partners Singapore's CSIT on Secure Enterprise AI for National Security**

*PR Newswire · November 5, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Cohere is expanding a pilot into a full partnership with Singapore's Centre for Strategic Infocomm Technologies (CSIT) to deploy its enterprise AI models and North platform inside highly secure, on-premises government networks.

CSIT, a technical agency under Singapore's Ministry of Defence, will use the platform for secure knowledge retrieval, automated analysis and report generation, and agentic workflows for thousands of users, with centralised governance built in. Cohere said its APAC customer base has grown fivefold over the past year and that it plans to quadruple its regional presence by the end of 2027.

**Why it matters:** The deal is a visible example of a foreign AI vendor earning sovereign-grade trust from a Singapore security agency, a bar that is typically higher than commercial enterprise deals. It also signals growing competition among frontier AI vendors for regulated, high-security public-sector contracts across Asia.

## Key Takeaways

- Governance and oversight tooling, not model quality, is the recurring theme separating enterprise AI pilots from enterprise-wide rollouts, echoing the Gartner and Capgemini findings from earlier this week.
- Vendors are increasingly citing their own internal agent deployments (LTM's 1,500 agents, Salesforce's own usage data) as proof points to sell governed-agent narratives to customers.
- Singapore continues to attract high-trust AI deployments beyond banking and fintech, with security and defence agencies now also adopting frontier AI platforms.

## What to Watch

Watch for usage and governance metrics from ServiceNow's and Salesforce's upcoming quarterly earnings calls, and for any further detail CSIT or Cohere disclose on the scope and timeline of the Singapore deployment.
