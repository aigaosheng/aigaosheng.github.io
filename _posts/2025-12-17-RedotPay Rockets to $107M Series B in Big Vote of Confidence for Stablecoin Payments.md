**RedotPay Rockets to $107M Series B in Big Vote of Confidence for Stablecoin Payments**

In a clear signal that digital money is stepping out of crypto-only orbit and into global payments, Hong Kong-based fintech **RedotPay has closed an oversubscribed US$107 million Series B round** — a massive milestone that underscores investor belief in stablecoins as real-world payment infrastructure. ([redotpay.com][1])

Leading the round was **Goodwater Capital**, joined by top crypto and fintech investors including **Pantera Capital, Blockchain Capital, and Circle Ventures**, with ongoing support from existing backers. ([redotpay.com][1])

The fresh capital pushes RedotPay’s **total funding in 2025 to about US$194 million**, reflecting rapid growth and the market’s rising appetite for blockchain-enabled payments that bridge crypto and traditional finance. ([redotpay.com][1])

---

### **From Crypto Curiosity to Payments Powerhouse**

RedotPay isn’t just another wallet app — it aims to make digital assets *spendable* everywhere, offering a suite of products that include:

* **Stablecoin-linked payment cards** that let users spend crypto like cash anywhere Visa/Mastercard are accepted,
* **Cross-border payout rails** for fast, low-cost international transfers,
* **Multi-currency accounts and marketplace access** for holding and moving stablecoins and other digital assets. ([HTX][2])

That strategy appears to be working. The company now claims:

* **6 million+ registered users** across 100+ countries,
* **Annualized payment volume exceeding US$10 billion**,
* **More than US$150 million in annual revenue** — and growing profitably. ([HTX][2])

Growth has been explosive in 2025, with transaction volumes nearly *tripling year-over-year* as millions of users embrace stablecoin spending for real-world use cases. ([CoinMarketCap][3])

---

### **Why Investors Are Betting Big**

Investors see RedotPay as more than a crypto app — they see it as a *bridge between traditional money and digital currency rails*.

Stablecoins — digital assets pegged to fiat currencies like the US dollar — offer:

* **Faster settlement times** than traditional cross-border transfers,
* **Lower fees**, especially across borders,
* **Predictable value** compared with volatile cryptocurrencies, and
* **Financial access in regions where banking infrastructure is weak or costly.** ([TechNews180][4])

Backers say this combination could reshape how money moves globally — especially for remittances, international commerce, and everyday payments.

---

### **What’s Next for RedotPay**

According to company leadership, the Series B funds will be used to:

* Pursue **strategic acquisitions**,
* Secure **additional licenses in new jurisdictions**,
* Expand **compliance and regulatory teams**, and
* Grow engineering and product development as the company **expands into new markets**. ([redotpay.com][1])

This push aligns with RedotPay’s broader mission: to make stablecoin-powered financial services accessible, secure, and mainstream.

---

## **Glossary**

**Stablecoin**
A type of cryptocurrency designed to maintain a stable value by being pegged to a reserve asset — typically a fiat currency like USD. The stability makes it suitable for everyday payments and transfers.

**Series B Funding**
A significant round of venture capital financing that helps startups scale products, expand to new markets, or build out infrastructure after initial product–market fit.

**Annualized Payment Volume**
A projection of total transaction value processed over a year, based on current or recent performance.
