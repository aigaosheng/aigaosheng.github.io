## Executive Takeaway

The US AI landscape on 2026-07-11 is increasingly defined by three strategic themes:

1. **Infrastructure constraints** — chips, electricity, and data centers are becoming critical bottlenecks.
2. **Enterprise commercialization** — AI adoption is shifting from experimentation toward production-scale systems.
3. **Capital discipline** — investors are evaluating whether AI spending levels can generate sustainable returns.

The next competitive phase of AI will likely be determined by execution capacity: access to compute, deployment efficiency, and the ability to convert models into reliable business systems.
