### Payment Brief — 2026-05-21

## Top Stories

### 1. Trump Orders Fed to Evaluate Fintech Access to Payment Rails
- **The Paypers** · 2026-05-20
- **Summary**: President Trump signed an executive order directing the Federal Reserve to comprehensively evaluate its regulatory framework for Reserve Bank payment account access. The order specifically explores extending such access to fintech and crypto firms, adding political impetus to the Fed's existing proposal on "skinny master accounts" first published in December 2025. This follows the Kansas City Fed's March 2026 approval of a limited-purpose account for Kraken's parent company, Payward.
- **Why It Matters**: Direct access to Fed payment rails for non-banks would structurally reshape the US payments landscape, reducing fintech and crypto firms' dependence on intermediary banks. Traditional banking groups oppose the move, citing risks and uneven regulatory playing fields, setting the stage for a major policy battle over the future of US payment infrastructure.
- **URL**: [Trump Orders Fed to Evaluate Fintech Access to Payment Rails](https://www.thepaypers.com/payments-general/trump-orders-fed-to-evaluate-fintech-access-to-payment-rails/1272829)

### 2. Visa Reports Fraud Shifts to AI-Powered Social Engineering
- **Visa (via BusinessWire)** · 2026-05-20
- **Summary**: Visa's Spring 2026 Biannual Threats Report reveals that while network-level payment security is strengthening, criminals are pivoting to AI-enabled social engineering scams. Device token fraud declined 9.6% year-over-year, but global ransomware activity increased 26%, with only 23% of victims paying—the lowest rate on record—as organizations improve resilience.
- **Why It Matters**: The migration of fraud from technical system breaches to human manipulation represents a fundamental shift in threat models. Payment firms must now invest in AI-powered defense systems and consumer education, not just transactional security, as traditional authentication methods prove insufficient against AI-generated deception.
- **URL**: [Visa Spring 2026 Biannual Threats Report](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260520712345/en/Visa-Spring-2026-Biannual-Threats-Report-Fraud-Shifts-to-AI-Powered-Social-Engineering)

### 3. Agentic Commerce Faces Accountability Conundrum, Says US Payments Forum
- **US Payments Forum (via Business Insider)** · 2026-05-20
- **Summary**: The US Payments Forum's Spring 2026 Market Snapshot highlights growing pressure to make agentic commerce feasible, with Bank of America speakers noting unresolved questions about liability when AI agents initiate transactions. "Friendly fraud" risks emerge as consumers may accuse agents of unauthorized purchases, creating chargeback influxes for merchants, issuers, and networks. Tokenization lifecycle management is also emerging as a critical but invisible operational challenge.
- **Why It Matters**: As 75% of retailers deem AI agents essential for 2026 operations, the lack of standards for intent capture, consent, and dispute workflows presents systemic risk. The industry must develop guardrails for AI-initiated transactions before agentic commerce can scale safely.
- **URL**: [Agentic Commerce Faces Accountability Conundrum](https://www.businessinsider.com/agentic-commerce-accountability-payments-forum-2026-5)
