### 2. EngineAI opens Shenzhen humanoid-robot manufacturing base and starts T800 deliveries
- **PR Newswire** · 2026-05-29
- **Summary**: EngineAI announced the launch of its Honghualing intelligent manufacturing base in Shenzhen and said the first batch of its T800 full-size humanoid robots has rolled off the production line. ### 3. BYD launches Xuanji A3, billed as China’s first 4nm smart-driving chip
- **TechNode** · 2026-05-29
- **Summary**: BYD unveiled its self-developed Xuanji A3 chip, calling it China’s first 4nm autonomous-driving chip. The chip is designed to support L3 and L4 autonomous-driving capabilities, and TechNode reported that a three-chip configuration can deliver more than 2,100 TOPS of computing power.
- **Why It Matters**: The launch points to deeper vertical integration in China’s EV and autonomous-driving stack, reducing reliance on third-party silicon while intensifying competition with Huawei, Nvidia and other automotive AI chip suppliers.
- **URL**: https://technode.com/2026/05/29/byd-launches-xuanji-a3-calls-it-chinas-first-4nm-smart-driving-chip/

### 6. China rolls out digital identity system for humanoid robots
- **India Today** · 2026-05-29
- **Summary**: China has launched a Humanoid Full Lifecycle Management Service Platform that assigns humanoid robots a unique 29-digit identification code. The system is designed to track robots across manufacturing, sales, deployment, maintenance, recycling and retirement, with reporting indicating more than 100 manufacturers and over 28,000 robots across 200 models already connected to the platform.
- **Why It Matters**: As humanoid robots move toward commercial deployment, lifecycle traceability and accountability are becoming governance priorities. China’s digital-ID approach may become an early template for safety, recalls and compliance in embodied AI.
- **URL**: https://www.indiatoday.in/amp/technology/news/story/china-is-launching-digital-id-system-for-humanoids-each-robot-will-get-its-own-unique-code-2918939-2026-05-29
