## Key Takeaway

China’s AI ecosystem is entering a **dual transition phase**:

* Rapid **frontier model competitiveness (GLM-5.2, open-source expansion)**
* Simultaneous **strategic tightening (investment controls, IP protection, sovereign compute buildout)**

The net effect is a system optimizing for **scale, cost efficiency, and domestic control**, increasingly positioning China as a structurally independent AI power bloc.
