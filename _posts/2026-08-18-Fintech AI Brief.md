# Fintech AI Brief — 2026-08-18

## Top Stories 

### 1. **Razorpay Bets on Vulcan, a Payments-Specific AI Foundation Model**

* **Source**: YourStory · August 18, 2026
* **Summary**: Razorpay has introduced Vulcan, which it describes as India's first AI foundation model designed specifically for payments. The company says the model was developed with NVIDIA and AWS and trained using nearly 3 trillion data points across 4 billion payments, targeting improvements in transaction reliability, security and payment intelligence.
* **Why It Matters**: This signals a move beyond general-purpose LLM adoption toward domain-specific foundation models trained on proprietary financial data. Payments companies with sufficient transaction scale may increasingly treat their data as a strategic AI asset.
* **URL**: [https://yourstory.com/ai-story/razorpay-ai-foundation-model-vulcan-india-payments-challenge](https://yourstory.com/ai-story/razorpay-ai-foundation-model-vulcan-india-payments-challenge)

### 3. **FinCEN's Proposed AML Framework Highlights AI and Federated Learning**

* **Source**: FinTech Global · August 18, 2026
* **Summary**: A new analysis of FinCEN's proposed AML/CFT framework highlights how regulators are encouraging financial institutions to experiment with technologies including artificial intelligence, federated learning and other advanced monitoring tools. The approach emphasizes demonstrable effectiveness while creating greater space for technological experimentation.
* **Why It Matters**: AML is one of the clearest areas where AI can generate measurable value, but data-sharing constraints have limited model development. Regulatory recognition of privacy-preserving approaches such as federated learning could encourage more cross-institution AI collaboration.
* **URL**: [https://fintech.global/2026/08/18/fincens-new-aml-rule-rewards-banks-that-collaborate/](https://fintech.global/2026/08/18/fincens-new-aml-rule-rewards-banks-that-collaborate/)
