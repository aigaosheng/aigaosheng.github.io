# AI Finance in Singapore Brief: MAS Launches Future of Finance Institute

**Today:** Singapore's central bank has moved from AI guidance to AI infrastructure, standing up a dedicated institute to coordinate how banks, insurers and fintechs adopt and govern AI.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🏦 **MAS's Future of Finance Institute Begins Operations, Leads With AI**

*Monetary Authority of Singapore · November 3, 2026*

**Bottom line:** MAS has operationalised the Future of Finance Institute (FFI), a public-private body tasked with scaling safe AI adoption across Singapore's financial sector, starting with three dedicated AI programmes.

The FFI, first announced in June 2026, is now running three pillars: PathFin.ai, which matches financial institutions with market-ready AI solutions through a Knowledge Hub and cross-sector Communities of Practice; BuildFin.ai, which develops guidance and shared infrastructure for deploying AI, including agentic AI, with industry and research partners; and SafeFin.ai, which pools institutions to build practical good practices around MAS's Guidelines on AI Risk Management and to enhance the existing MindForge AI Risk Management Toolkit. Alan Lim, who also heads MAS's Financial Infrastructure and AI Office, has been named interim CEO, and MAS said FFI will later expand into tokenisation and other innovation areas.

**Why it matters:** By folding fragmented AI pilots and risk frameworks into a single institute, MAS is signalling it wants Singapore to be the reference point for how agentic AI gets deployed safely in finance across Asia, giving banks, insurers and fintechs a formal channel to shape standards before supervisory expectations tighten.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2026/future-of-finance-institute-commences-operations-starting-with-ai-in-finance)

## Key Takeaways

- MAS is shifting from issuing AI risk guidance to building standing institutional infrastructure, formalising what were previously ad hoc frameworks like SAFR and MindForge under one coordinating body.
- Pairing FFI's interim leadership with MAS's own Financial Infrastructure and AI Office keeps agentic AI policy and industry implementation tightly linked rather than delegated to a fully independent body.
- The institute's three-pillar structure gives financial institutions, tech vendors and researchers a formal venue to influence AI standards before they harden into supervisory requirements.

## What to Watch

Watch for FFI's first published use cases and Communities of Practice outputs under PathFin.ai, and for how the enhanced MindForge AI Risk Management Toolkit under SafeFin.ai is taken up as institutions prepare for MAS's AI Risk Management Guidelines.
