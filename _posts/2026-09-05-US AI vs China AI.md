### 1. **US and China Plan Bilateral AI Safety Talks Ahead of Washington Summit**

* **Source**: Reuters / The Jakarta Post · 2026-09-05
* **Summary**: The United States and China are preparing for high-level bilateral discussions devoted to frontier AI safety and cyberattack monitoring in mid-September.
