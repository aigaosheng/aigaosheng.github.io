# Payments Brief: European Schemes Launch Agentic Commerce Local Payments Council

**Today:** If you run local payment rails, issue stablecoins in Asia or sell transaction-banking software, today's four stories bear directly on your 2027 roadmap.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **European Local Payment Schemes Form a Council to Set Agentic Commerce Rules**
*FinTech Futures · October 26, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Europe's domestic payment schemes are banding together so that AI shopping agents can use non-card rails, rather than defaulting to cards.

PPRO, Poland's BLIK, Belgium's Bancontact, the European Payments Initiative (the group behind Wero) and Greek clearing house DIAS have founded the Local Payments Council for Agentic Commerce. The council aims to build a shared industry standard for plugging AI agents into established local payment systems. Its agenda has five parts: keeping humans in control of approvals so an agent cannot authorise a payment on its own; letting agents prove to banks and fintechs that they are legitimate when they use non-card rails; a defined way for users to delegate authority to agents; rules for disputes, refunds and liability; and how agents identify eligible merchants.

**Why it matters:** Card networks have set most agentic-payment standards so far, including Visa's Agentic Ready programme. A scheme-led alternative gives Asia's QR and real-time rail operators (PayNow, QRIS, DuitNow, QR Ph) a model to follow if they want agent traffic to run on account-to-account rails.

### 2. 💳 **Standard Chartered-Led Anchorpoint Plans Retail Stablecoin but Sets No Date**
*South China Morning Post · October 26, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Hong Kong's two licensed stablecoin issuers are taking different paths to retail customers: HSBC is already live, and Anchorpoint says it will not rush.

Anchorpoint, the stablecoin joint venture led by Standard Chartered, said it intends to launch a retail product on a public blockchain. Its leadership called the approach prudent and pointed to the extra considerations of offering a public-chain token to consumers. The comments came after HSBC launched its Hong Kong dollar-backed HSBC RedCoin on Friday to the 3.3 million users of its PayMe app. Anchorpoint started a Tokenised Money Adoption Programme last month to support education and research on tokenised money.

**Why it matters:** HSBC chose to distribute through an existing app, while Anchorpoint is moving cautiously on public-chain retail use. That split is a live test of how regulated stablecoins reach consumers in Asia. Issuers and wallet operators in Singapore and the region should watch which model gains usage first.

### 3. 📊 **Tech Mahindra Buys Finastra's Cash Management and Virtual Accounts Suite**
*FinTech Futures · October 26, 2026*

**Bottom line:** An Indian IT services group now owns a core corporate cash and virtual-accounts platform, continuing Finastra's break-up.

Tech Mahindra has acquired three Finastra products: Fusion Cash Management, Fusion Virtual Accounts and Fusion Liquidity Management. Financial terms were not disclosed. Tech Mahindra says the deal also covers intellectual property for payables, receivables, cash-flow forecasting, APIs and balance reporting, and the products will join its banking and financial services portfolio. Finastra has already sold its Universal Banking business, its US mid-market banking operations and its Treasury and Capital Markets unit.

**Why it matters:** Virtual accounts and liquidity tools sit at the centre of corporate and embedded-finance payment flows. A change of owner could affect product roadmaps, support and pricing for banks and BaaS providers on these platforms. Current clients should seek clarity on continuity.

### 4. 🏦 **RBI Opens New Data Centre for RTGS and NEFT as UPI Passes 24 Billion Monthly Transactions**
*Business Today · October 26, 2026*

**Bottom line:** India is adding resilience to its core payment infrastructure as UPI volumes keep climbing.

Prime Minister Narendra Modi inaugurated a Reserve Bank of India data centre and cybersecurity training institute in Bhubaneswar. The campus will host mission-critical systems including RTGS and NEFT. Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman said UPI now handles more than 2,400 crore transactions a month. NPCI data shows 24.07 billion UPI transactions in September.

**Why it matters:** Volume at this scale makes operational resilience a regulatory priority. For firms in the India corridors, including the UPI-PayNow link, it is a reminder that central banks increasingly see payment uptime and cyber capability as core infrastructure policy.

## Key Takeaways
- **Rail owners are claiming a stake in agentic commerce.** Local schemes no longer want to leave agent-payment standards to the card networks. Authorisation, liability and agent identity are the contested areas.
- **Bank-issued stablecoins are reaching retail in Asia at different speeds.** Distributing through an existing app gives an early lead, but issuers on public chains are going more slowly on purpose.
- **Payment infrastructure is consolidating and hardening.** Core transaction-banking software is changing hands, and central banks are investing directly in resilience for high-volume rails.
