### 1. Mecka AI raises $60 million to build robotics training data infrastructure
- **Fortune** · 2026-06-01
- **Summary**: New York-based Mecka AI disclosed $60 million in total financing across a previously unannounced $25 million Series A closed in November and a $35 million follow-on investment. The company is building data pipelines for robotics models using human motion data captured through body sensors and iPhones, with Framework Ventures leading and Menlo Ventures, SV Angel, Kindred Ventures, and angel Ted Xiao participating.
- **Why It Matters**: The deal is a strong signal that investors are broadening beyond foundation models into enabling infrastructure for embodied AI. Startups supplying proprietary data and tooling to robotics companies are becoming a distinct venture category.

### 2. Samsung launches a third 200 billion won life sciences venture fund
- **CHOSUNBIZ** · 2026-06-01
- **Summary**: Samsung said it is creating a third Samsung Life Science Fund with 200 billion won in capital, backed by Samsung C&T, Samsung Biologics, and Samsung Bioepis and managed by Samsung Venture Investment. The new vehicle extends Samsung’s structured push into bio-venture investing as it looks to deepen access to emerging therapeutic platforms and strategic biotech partnerships.
- **Why It Matters**: Corporate venture capital remains an important source of non-dilutive strategic validation for biotech startups, especially in capital-intensive segments. A new Samsung fund also reinforces Asia’s role as a meaningful source of startup capital in life sciences.

### 3. MokN lands $15 million as GV makes its first French startup bet
- **Tech Funding News** · 2026-06-01
- **Summary**: French identity-security startup MokN raised $15 million, with GV backing the company in what the outlet described as the firm’s first investment in a French startup. The company is focused on stopping identity theft before compromise occurs, positioning itself in the higher-priority fraud prevention and digital trust layer.
- **Why It Matters**: The financing highlights continuing investor appetite for cybersecurity startups addressing identity, one of the most commercially urgent threat vectors. GV’s participation also gives the round outsized signaling value for the European startup market.

### 4. Samsung SDS, Samsung Securities, and Samsung Card buy into Dunamu
- **CHOSUNBIZ** · 2026-06-01
