# Enterprise AI Brief: IBM and Microsoft Launch Imagine Enterprise

**Today:** Two of enterprise tech's biggest players unveiled a joint agentic platform aimed squarely at regulated industries, while a wave of governance and recovery tooling from ServiceNow, Commvault and others shows vendors racing to keep AI agents under control as adoption outpaces integration.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **IBM and Microsoft Unveil Imagine Enterprise Agent Platform**

*IBM Newsroom · November 10, 2026*

**Bottom line:** IBM and Microsoft combined forces on Imagine Enterprise, a platform pairing 6,000-plus prebuilt IBM Consulting agents with Microsoft's cloud, security and governance stack to target regulated sectors first.

The platform launches with frameworks for financial services, insurance and retail, built on IBM Consulting Advantage alongside Microsoft Foundry, Copilot Studio, Fabric, Entra, Purview and Defender. Wealth manager SEI, which oversees roughly $2 trillion in assets, is cited as an early case study exploring the platform, and IBM and Microsoft said coverage will later extend to manufacturing, energy, public sector and mobility.

**Why it matters:** The tie-up signals that large systems integrators and hyperscalers now see pre-governed, industry-specific agent bundles as the path to enterprise scale, a model regulated Asian financial institutions under MAS-style risk frameworks are likely to watch closely as they evaluate vendor-managed versus self-built agent stacks.

### 2. 🔒 **ServiceNow Adds Agentic Continuous Controls Monitoring**

*Business Wire · November 10, 2026*

**Bottom line:** ServiceNow is adding agentic AI to its Integrated Risk Management product so AI agents configure, interpret and act on control checks instead of waiting for periodic audits.

The system draws on more than 100 packaged connections to watch systems beyond ServiceNow itself, including cloud, container, identity and on-premise tools, flagging and acting on control breaks as they happen. General availability is planned for the first quarter of 2027.

**Why it matters:** Continuous, machine-speed control monitoring could reshape how compliance teams satisfy audit and risk-reporting obligations, a capability relevant to MAS- and BSP-regulated institutions that currently rely on periodic manual control testing.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261110544930/en/)

### 3. 🔒 **Commvault Partners With NVIDIA, Databricks and Zscaler on AI Resilience**

*PR Newswire · November 10, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Commvault unveiled Recovery Studio and an expanded AgentRecover tool alongside new integrations with NVIDIA, Databricks and Zscaler aimed at helping enterprises recover from AI-related data incidents and govern "shadow AI" use.

AgentRecover now tracks agents built on Microsoft Foundry and Amazon Bedrock AgentCore, logging their activity so teams can see what an agent touched if it misbehaves. The Zscaler partnership adds AI discovery and policy enforcement to flag unsanctioned AI tools, while the NVIDIA integration organizes protected data for retrieval by AI systems, with broader availability expected through 2027.

**Why it matters:** As agent sprawl grows, data-protection vendors are repositioning backup and recovery as an AI governance layer, a shift enterprises handling customer or KYC data should track given PDPA-style data-handling obligations.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/commvault-announces-strategic-partnership-with-zscaler-to-help-enterprises-adopt-ai-with-confidence-control-and-resilience-302934264.html)

### 4. 📊 **Survey Finds Only 9% of Enterprises Have Fully Integrated AI**

*ManpowerGroup (Experis) · November 10, 2026*

**Bottom line:** A survey of nearly 1,930 technology leaders across 12 countries found that while 66% of organizations are increasing AI investment, only 9% describe AI as fully integrated into operations and strategy.

Experis, part of ManpowerGroup, reported that 19% of respondents are scaling AI more broadly, 32% are integrating it into select areas, and 30% remain in research or pilot stages. Separately, 78% of senior technology leaders said they are also raising cybersecurity spending alongside AI investment.

**Why it matters:** The gap between rising AI budgets and actual integration underscores why governance and recovery tooling, like the ServiceNow and Commvault launches above, is becoming a bigger share of enterprise AI spend than model access itself.

### 5. 🤖 **Rackspace and Together AI Sign Dedicated AI Inference Infrastructure Deal**

*Rackspace Investor Relations · November 10, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Rackspace and Together AI signed a multiyear strategic agreement under which Rackspace plans to build and operate dedicated NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 infrastructure, with Spectrum-X networking, to support Together AI's inference workloads.

Deployment is targeted for 2027 and remains conditional on financing and facility availability; the companies also signed a non-binding letter of intent to explore a joint go-to-market for enterprise AI inference.

**Why it matters:** Dedicated inference capacity deals are becoming a parallel track to model partnerships, and the financing conditions attached to this one are a reminder that announced AI infrastructure capacity does not always convert to deployed capacity on schedule.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://ir.rackspace.com/news-releases/news-release-details/rackspace-and-together-ai-announce-strategic-agreement-support)

### 6. 🔒 **Zingtree Launches Agentic Governance Platform for Regulated Contact Centers**

*GlobeNewswire · November 10, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Zingtree launched an agentic governance and orchestration platform built specifically for contact centers and customer operations in regulated sectors such as healthcare, insurance and financial services.

The platform enforces deterministic rules, such as identity verification and eligibility checks, before an agent can act, blocks unapproved actions and logs decisions for audit, and can be deployed headlessly via API or MCP across three product tiers.

**Why it matters:** The launch reflects demand from compliance-heavy customer operations for agents that cannot improvise past approved scripts, a requirement that will resonate with contact centers at banks and insurers operating under strict conduct-risk rules.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/10/3399502/0/en/zingtree-launches-first-agentic-governance-and-orchestration-platform-built-for-contact-centers-customer-operations-in-complex-regulated-industries.html)

## Key Takeaways

- The center of gravity in enterprise AI is shifting from raw model access to governance, recovery and controls tooling, as vendors across IBM, ServiceNow, Commvault and Zingtree all launched oversight-focused products on the same day.
- Regulated industries, financial services and insurance in particular, are the first target for nearly every major launch this cycle, reinforcing that compliance-ready agent deployment is now a competitive differentiator.
- Survey data shows enterprise AI investment is outpacing integration, suggesting the governance and recovery products launched this week address a real and growing gap rather than a hypothetical risk.

## What to Watch

Watch for ServiceNow's Q1 2027 general availability of agentic continuous controls monitoring and Rackspace's 2027 infrastructure deployment timeline, both of which are conditional and worth confirming as the dates approach.
