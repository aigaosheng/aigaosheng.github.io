### 1. Reuters Institute Report: AI Chatbots and Video Drive Historic Shift in News Consumption
- **Bernama** · 2026-06-20
- **Summary**: The Reuters Institute Digital News Report 2026, based on nearly 100,000 interviews across 48 markets, reveals a watershed moment for news: social media and video networks have surpassed all other sources as the most widely used news gateway globally (54% of audiences) . The report notes that 10% of people now use AI chatbots for news, rising to 16% among under-35s, and that "the feed has replaced the front page" as the primary mode of content discovery .
- **Why It Matters**: This signals a permanent restructuring of the media landscape. Traditional publishers, already hit by the first wave of social media, now face a second wave where their TV and video interests are being disrupted. The shift to AI-curated, personality-driven, and video-first content creates profound challenges for monetization, audience loyalty, and the role of institutional journalism.

### 2. Canada Tables Legislation to Ban Social Media for Under-16s, Regulate AI Chatbots
- **Vietnam.vn** · 2026-06-11
- **Summary**: The Canadian government has introduced a new digital safety bill that proposes a ban on social media use for children under 16 and the establishment of a new regulatory body to enforce safety standards for AI chatbots . The creation of a dedicated digital regulator signals a new era of proactive, rather than reactive, governance for online platforms and AI, potentially influencing other nations' approaches.

### 4. ChatGPT Vulnerability Exploited to Generate Graphic Content
- **The Indian Express** · 2026-06-20
- **Summary**: Cybersecurity firm Mindgard discovered a method to bypass ChatGPT's image-generation safeguards using a simple, seemingly harmless text prompt ("restore the attached photo" with no file attached). Researchers were able to generate highly sexualized and violent imagery, which worsened with minor modifications to the prompt . OpenAI acknowledged the issue, stated it has since implemented additional safeguards, and is working on a fix that prompts users to upload a missing image instead of generating content based on its absence .
- **Why It Matters**: This incident underscores the fragility of current AI safety guardrails and the ongoing cat-and-mouse game between developers and researchers. It highlights that even sophisticated models can be manipulated through simple, non-obvious prompts, raising critical questions about the reliability of AI content moderation and the potential for misuse at scale.

### 7. Drop in News Interest and Trust Accelerates Globally
- **Malay Mail** · 2026-06-20
- **Summary**: The Reuters Institute report reveals a sharp global decline in interest and trust in news. Since 2021, the proportion of people 'extremely' interested in news has fallen by 13%, and trust is at its lowest level since 2015 (37%). Concerns about fake news have risen to 62% on average . The report notes this drop is linked to a shift towards social media and video networks, which are trusted less than traditional media .
- **Why It Matters**: The findings point to a fundamental challenge to the information ecosystem. As audiences move to platforms where trust is lower and misinformation is prevalent, the societal contract for accurate, reliable information is fracturing. For news organizations, this means navigating a "battle for a share of attention" in places where audience loyalty is lowest and monetization is most difficult .
