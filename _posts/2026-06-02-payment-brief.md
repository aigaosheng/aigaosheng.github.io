## Top Stories

### 1. UK Payments Initiative launches to push account-to-account payments against card rails
- **Finextra** · 2026-06-02
- **Summary**: The newly launched UK Payments Initiative has gone live with backing from major banks including Barclays, NatWest, Lloyds and HSBC, alongside fintech and payments firms. Its aim is to accelerate pay-by-bank adoption and reduce merchant dependence on Visa and Mastercard by building commercially viable account-to-account payment infrastructure. The launch also includes TrueLayer’s “Bank on File” capability for recurring bank-account-based payments.
- **Why It Matters**: This is one of the clearest recent attempts by incumbent banks and fintechs to create a scaled alternative to card networks in the UK. If adoption broadens, it could pressure card economics, improve merchant payment optionality, and advance open-banking-based recurring payments.
- **URL**: https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/47842/uk-payments-initiative-launches-to-challenge-visa-and-mastercard-stranglehold
