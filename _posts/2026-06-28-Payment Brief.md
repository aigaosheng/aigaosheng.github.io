### 1. **Philippines Tightens Payment Authentication Rules as Banks and E-Wallets Move Beyond OTPs**
- **The Philippine Star** · 2026-06-28
- **Summary**: The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) has implemented stronger authentication requirements for higher-risk digital payment transactions, requiring covered banks and e-wallet providers to move away from vulnerable SMS and email OTP methods. The new framework encourages adoption of biometrics, behavioral analytics, adaptive authentication, and passwordless security technologies.
- **Why It Matters**: Payment providers globally are shifting from customer-driven security checks toward AI-powered risk assessment. The change highlights the growing industry priority of balancing fraud prevention with frictionless checkout experiences.
