### 2. Archax Launches $GOVY, the First 24/7 Perpetual Tokenized T-Bill
- **Archax** · 2026-06-17
- **Summary**: Archax has launched $GOVY, the first tokenized Treasury bill product designed for 24/7 trading and perpetual rolling of short-dated US T-bills . Built on Ethereum, Hedera, and Stellar, the product offers direct, legally-enforceable beneficial entitlement to the underlying assets, which are held in regulated custody by Northern Trust . Crucially, $GOVY is aligned with HQLA Level 1 principles, positioning it as a potential regulatory capital-qualifying collateral instrument for banks .
- **Why It Matters**: This launch is a major step forward for institutional DeFi. By creating a tokenized asset that can be used for regulatory capital requirements, Archax has opened a significant new use case for RWAs, potentially driving massive demand from the banking sector .
- **URL**: [Archax RWA Weekly Update, June 17 2026](https://archax.com/market-updates/archax-rwa-weekly-update-june-17-2026)
