## **DeepWay’s $173M Pre-IPO Boost: China’s Smart Trucking Vision Hits Hong Kong’s Capital Stage**

Chinese trucking technology firm **DeepWay Technology Co.** just secured about **¥1.2 billion (US$173 million)** in a pre-IPO funding round ahead of a planned listing on the **Hong Kong Stock Exchange**—a milestone that signals growing investor confidence in electrification and autonomous logistics. ([chinadailyhk][1])

This capital infusion, led by heavyweight strategic backers like **Temasek-backed ABC Impact, Lenovo Capital,** and **battery maker Sunwoda Electronic**, fortifies DeepWay’s balance sheet and sharpens the startup’s edge as it prepares to go public. ([chinadailyhk][1])

### **Why This Matters**

Founded in 2020 and headquartered in Anhui Province, DeepWay develops **electric, smart heavy-duty trucks** equipped with assisted-driving tech and intelligent freight solutions designed for the future of logistics. Despite strong revenue growth—about **¥1.5 billion in the first half of 2025**—the company hasn’t yet turned a profit, a common state for high-growth tech firms scaling capital-intensive hardware and software stacks. ([chinadailyhk][1])

The pre-IPO round comes as **Hong Kong’s capital markets continue to attract Chinese tech and hard-tech listings**, helping diversify beyond traditional sectors like finance and real estate. Analysts see DeepWay’s forthcoming IPO as part of a broader trend where technology, manufacturing and innovation plays are gaining traction among global investors. ([chinadailyhk][2])

### **DeepWay’s Strategic Position**

DeepWay is not just another truck maker; it’s part of China’s push toward **new-energy vehicles (NEVs)** and **autonomous commercial transport**—two areas that are drawing massive venture and institutional investment. The company also benefits from alliances with major investors and strategic backers, including early support from tech giant **Baidu Inc.** and other venture partners. ([chinadailyhk][1])

This backing gives DeepWay access to core technologies and boosts its credibility in a crowded field where electrification and AI-driven logistics solutions are fast becoming competitive differentiators. ([ChinaBiz Insider][3])

### **Challenges and Opportunities Ahead**

While the funding round is a sign of bullish sentiment, DeepWay’s path to profitability isn’t guaranteed. Heavy-duty electric trucks and intelligent freight systems require deep R&D investments, supply chain scaling, and infrastructure ecosystem development. Still, the fresh capital, if paired with a successful IPO, could power deeper market expansion into **Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and Australia**, as well as intensified product refinement. ([ChinaBiz Insider][3])

The broader backdrop is favorable: demand for new-energy heavy trucks in China is surging, with market forecasts predicting accelerated penetration of electrified freight vehicles over the next decade. This creates an opportunity for DeepWay to emerge as a leader in a sector where global incumbents are also vying for share. ([ChinaBiz Insider][3])

### **Glossary of Key Terms**

* **Pre-IPO funding** – Capital raised by a company before its initial public offering to strengthen operations, R&D, or market expansions.
* **New-Energy Vehicle (NEV)** – Vehicles powered by alternative energy sources like electricity or hydrogen instead of traditional internal combustion engines.
* **Assisted driving** – Technologies that help drivers with functions like lane-keeping and adaptive cruise control, typically classified as Level 1-2 automation.
* **Hong Kong Stock Exchange (HKEX)** – One of Asia’s leading public markets where companies list shares to raise investment from global capital.
