# How the ACLU, Cloudflare & Firefox Are Battling for an Open and Secure Internet

The internet of tomorrow is at stake—and the fight is already under way. At the Fast Company Innovation Festival in New York on September 18, 2025, leading voices from the American Civil Liberties Union (ACLU), Cloudflare, and Firefox (via Mozilla) sounded the alarm. They warn that without immediate action, the internet risks becoming a surveillance zone with throttled access, fractured under national controls and dominated by AI-driven gatekeepers. 

## The Core Threats

**1. Loss of anonymity and mass tracking**  
Daniel Kahn Gillmor of the ACLU highlighted a looming future where every online action—click, search, site visit—could be tied directly to one’s identity. With pervasive “super cookies,” device IDs, and tracking hooks, pseudonymity may vanish. This threatens privacy and free expression, especially around sensitive topics, censoring not by law but by chilling self-censorship.[3][11]

**2. Fragmented internets and government control**  
Cloudflare’s Alissa Starzak warned of growing government demands for “sovereign internets”—national or regional networks under local control rather than a unified global web. Recent U.S. federal broadband funding shifts favor satellite providers over fiber deployment, risking varied access quality that deepens digital divides. Meanwhile, patchwork international regulations like the EU Digital Markets Act advance regional control over platforms and data, deepening fragmentation.[12][13][14][15]

**3. AI-powered information monopolies**  
Mozilla’s Anthony Enzor-DeMeo cautioned that AI-integrated browsers and platforms may centralize control of search and recommendations among a few Big Tech firms. Mozilla’s recent integration of Perplexity AI search offers an alternative emphasizing privacy and transparency, fighting back against the opaque filter bubbles that can cage user information.[16][17][18]

## What’s At Stake

- **Privacy & self-expression:** Without anonymity, users avoid exploring or discussing critical topics, limiting democratic discourse.[3]
- **Access to information:** AI-driven platform monopolies risk echo chambers where users see only curated worldviews, reducing exposure to diverse ideas.[16]
- **Business models & infrastructure:** Dominant ad-tracking models are under strain, while Cloudflare promotes new privacy-respecting approaches including AI sovereignty and content independence initiatives that block unauthorized AI crawlers by default.[2][12]

## Why This Matters Now

The convergence of AI, tracking advancements, and regulatory fragmentation poses a defining moment to shape the internet’s future. U.S. federal policy emphasizes AI innovation and cybersecurity, backed by Cloudflare’s efforts to enable open-source AI and secure deployment at the network edge. Yet disparities in broadband funding and growing sovereign internet demands threaten global access and integration. The architecture of tomorrow’s web, whether open or closed, is being built today.[13][19][2]

## What Can Be Done

- Advocate for open standards and interoperable infrastructure to avoid vendor lock-in and national silos.  
- Support policies protecting pseudonymity and severely restricting pervasive cross-site tracking.  
- Innovate privacy-first business models that do not rely on invasive ad profiling.  
- Choose and promote tools and platforms prioritizing privacy, transparency, and openness.

***

### Glossary

- **Super cookie:** Persistent tracking identifier that cannot be easily deleted.  
- **Filter bubble:** Algorithm-driven content personalization that limits exposure to differing views.  
- **Sovereign internet:** National or regional internet infrastructure controlled by governments, fragmenting the global web.  
- **Recommendation engine:** AI algorithms that suggest content or search results, shaping what users see.  
- **Pseudonymity:** The ability to interact online under an alias, preserving privacy while engaging digitally.  
- **Content independence:** Cloudflare’s initiative blocking unauthorized AI crawlers by default to protect web content.  
- **AI sovereignty:** Decentralized control of AI infrastructure fostering diverse, locally governed AI ecosystems.

***

### In Summary

The battle for an open and secure internet is urgent and ongoing. Recent events—from policy shifts affecting broadband access to AI’s growing role in content control—underscore the risks of surveillance, fragmentation, and monopolization. But the journey to preserve anonymity, open access, and trustworthy AI governance is not just a technical challenge, but a social imperative championed today by the ACLU, Cloudflare, and Firefox. The path chosen now will define the freedoms and opportunities of the digital generations to come.
