# Investor Commentary: Key Trends

## 1. AI Infrastructure Becomes the New “Picks and Shovels” Market

The biggest capital flows continue moving beyond foundation models into:

* AI compute platforms
* AI agent infrastructure
* Specialized chips
* Enterprise AI deployment layers

Recent mega-rounds for SambaNova, Together AI, and Prime Intellect show investors are betting that the AI ecosystem will require massive infrastructure comparable to cloud computing. ([TechCrunch][1])

**Investment implication:**
Companies enabling enterprises to deploy proprietary AI agents may capture long-term platform value.

---

## 2. Vertical AI Is Creating New Unicorn Categories

AI startups are increasingly targeting specific professional workflows:

* Legal → Norm
* Software engineering → Emergent
* Enterprise operations → Prime Intellect

The market is shifting from:

> “AI assistant”

toward:

> “AI employee capable of completing business processes”

([TechCrunch][3])

**Investment implication:**
The strongest opportunities may come from companies with:

* proprietary workflow data
* enterprise integrations
* measurable ROI
* human-in-the-loop reliability

---

## 3. Enterprise AI Security Becomes a New Investment Theme

Oak’s launch highlights a growing concern:

Traditional identity systems were designed for humans and cloud applications. AI agents introduce:

* machine permissions
* autonomous actions
* agent accountability

([TechCrunch][5])

**Potential winners:**

* AI governance
* agent security
* AI observability
* compliance automation
