### 1. Baillie Gifford Launches First Fully On-Chain Tokenized Fund in the UK
- **Cryptopolitan** · 2026-06-23
- **Summary**: £286 billion asset manager Baillie Gifford has launched the Enhanced Yield Fund (BAGEY), which it claims is the UK's first fully tokenized investment fund that runs entirely on-chain rather than just using a digital wrapper. The fund is structured as a UK-regulated Open-Ended Investment Company and invests in short-term corporate bonds, with BNY handling tokenization and wallet services . The fund operates on both Ethereum and Solana blockchains and is currently available to qualified investors in the UK, Switzerland, and the Cayman Islands .
- **Why It Matters**: This represents a significant step beyond pilot programs, demonstrating how a major traditional asset manager can deploy a regulated, yield-bearing product natively on public blockchains. It sets a new standard for what "tokenization" can mean in practice.
- **URL**: [Read more](https://www.bgportable.com/news/detail/12560605472731)

### 3. Tokenized RWA Market Cap Surpasses $51 Billion, Led by Private Credit
- **CoinPaprika** · 2026-06-23
- **Summary**: According to a Bernstein research note, the market capitalization of tokenized real-world assets has surpassed $51 billion, a 40% increase year-to-date despite a 20% decline in the broader crypto market. Private credit dominates the asset mix at 47%, followed by U.S. Treasuries at nearly 30% . The report also highlights that tokenized equities grew 130% to $1.6 billion, with monthly transfer volumes reaching $5.3 billion in June, up from $500 million in September 2025 .
- **Why It Matters**: This data confirms that the tokenization sector is experiencing robust, counter-cyclical growth driven by yield-bearing assets like credit and Treasuries. The rapid increase in equity tokenization volume and the race among different business models to set the standard signal a pivotal moment for the industry's evolution .
