# FintechNews.sg – Weekly Newsletter

**Period:** February 21 – February 28, 2026

## 📝 Executive Summary

* This week’s fintech news highlights continued **payments infrastructure expansion** and **strategic leadership moves** across Singapore and the APAC region. Key market participants including Mastercard and Mambu are enhancing regional footprints, while new research underscores evolving investment patterns.
* **Regulatory and risk management developments** are visible with DBS tweaking fee structures and implementing anti-fraud protections for digital banking users. These changes reflect broader industry focus on balancing growth with security.
* On the **funding and capital front**, Luxembourg launched an ASEAN-focused inclusion fintech fund, and KPMG reports suggest Asia Pacific fintech funding may be stabilising with a stronger emphasis on AI and scalable infrastructure.
* **APAC consumer sentiment around AI** reveals strong engagement with AI tools during the shopping journey, though security concerns temper adoption of autonomous payment features.

## 📈 Key Trends

* **Payments infrastructure continues regional rollout**, with platforms and incumbents expanding capabilities across SEA markets.
* **Leadership and talent moves** signal strategic prioritisation of digital payments and financial services transformation in Singapore.
* **Fintech funding is becoming more disciplined**, with capital flowing into AI-enabled and infrastructure play rather than broad-based consumer fintech.
* **Security and risk management remain priorities**, as reflected in core banking product changes and consumer attitudes toward AI.

* **DBS to Impose 12-Hour Cooling Period for High-Risk Account Changes from 7 March** (23 Feb 2026)
  DBS will introduce a 12-hour delay on select high-risk digital banking activities to help customers detect and stop unauthorised changes as part of anti-scam measures.
