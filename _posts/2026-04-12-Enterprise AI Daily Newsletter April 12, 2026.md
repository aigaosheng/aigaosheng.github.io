## Key Takeaways

* Enterprise AI is entering the **execution phase**, where deployment—not experimentation—is the focus
* **Workflow integration and governance** are now the primary success factors
* **Autonomous systems** are emerging, shifting AI from support tools to operational decision-makers
* High failure rates highlight the need for **strong architecture, compliance, and change management**
