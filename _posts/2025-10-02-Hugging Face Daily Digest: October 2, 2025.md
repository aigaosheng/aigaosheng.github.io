**Hugging Face Daily Digest: October 2, 2025**

### 🧠 New Model Releases

In the past 24 hours, Hugging Face has seen notable additions to its model hub:

* **ServiceNow-AI/Apriel-1.5-15b-Thinker**: A 15B parameter model designed for advanced reasoning tasks, emphasizing mid-training techniques for enhanced performance. ([Hugging Face][1])

* **inclusionAI/Ring-mini-linear-2.0**: A 16B parameter model optimized for efficient text generation, reflecting Ant Group's commitment to advancing AGI research.

* **Qwen/Qwen-Image-Edit-2509**: An image-to-image model tailored for editing tasks, showcasing the versatility of multimodal models.

These releases underscore the ongoing trend towards more specialized and efficient models in the AI community.

### 🔬 Research Initiatives

Recent research papers highlight significant advancements:

* **API Prediction for Code Generation**: A novel technique to expand code and index for predicting required APIs, enhancing auto-completion and agentic AI applications. ([Hugging Face][3])

* **EditReward-Bench**: A proposed reinforcement learning-based benchmark for instruction-guided image editing, aiming to improve model performance on complex tasks. ([Hugging Face][4])

* **Open Collaboration in Open Source AI**: An exploration of practices, motivations, and governance in 14 open LLM projects, emphasizing the importance of community-driven development. ([Hugging Face][5])
