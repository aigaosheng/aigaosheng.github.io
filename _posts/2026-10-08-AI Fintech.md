# AI Fintech Brief — 2026-10-08

**Today:** Agentic AI is moving deeper into financial workflows, with banks enabling governed actions, fintechs funding AI-native advisory tools, and payment infrastructure adapting for autonomous agents.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **Backbase launches agentic AI for customer and employee banking workflows**

*The Paypers · 2026-10-08*

**Bottom line:** Backbase has made Conversational Banking generally available, allowing governed AI agents to execute approved banking actions rather than simply answer customer questions.

The platform lets customers describe requests in natural language while agents use connected banking systems and customer context to perform authorised actions. Examples include blocking and replacing a lost card, checking payments and advancing service requests, with cases requiring human judgement escalated to employees with the relevant context.

**Why it matters:** This marks a shift from conversational AI toward executable banking workflows, making permissions, auditability and human escalation central to how banks deploy agentic systems.

### 2. 📊 **TODAY raises €2.8M to scale agentic AI for financial advisors**

*TODAY · 2026-10-08*

**Bottom line:** Financial-advisor AI startup TODAY raised €2.8 million in seed funding to expand its agentic platform for automating administrative and client-facing work.

The Copenhagen- and Berlin-based company says more than 3,500 advisors at insurers, distributors and brokers use its platform for tasks including meeting notes, administrative automation and improving client interactions. The round was co-led by High-Tech Gründerfonds and Insurtech Gateway, with additional participation from SeedX, Lucid Capital and angel investors.

**Why it matters:** The investment highlights a growing fintech opportunity beyond banking chatbots: specialised AI agents that automate regulated financial-sales workflows while preserving compliance and advisor oversight.

### 3. 💳 **StraitsX outlines how AI agents can make payments through x402**

*StraitsX · 2026-10-08*

**Bottom line:** StraitsX is extending its payments infrastructure toward agentic commerce through support for x402, a protocol designed to let software agents initiate and settle payments programmatically.

The company describes how AI agents can detect when a payment is required, make an authorised payment and receive access to a service without relying on conventional human checkout flows. The approach points toward machine-to-machine transactions in which payment credentials, permissions and settlement infrastructure become part of an agent's operating environment.

**Why it matters:** Agentic commerce will require payment rails designed for software actors rather than only human cardholders, creating a new infrastructure layer around agent identity, authorization, limits and automated settlement.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.straitsx.com/blog)

### 4. 🤖 **Agentic Finance Summit brings banks, payment networks and investors together in Singapore**

*More & More · 2026-10-08*

**Bottom line:** A 400-person executive summit in Singapore is putting agentic trading, payments, wallets and compliance at the center of institutional-finance discussions.

The Agentic Finance Summit brings together executives from banks, exchanges, payment networks, funds and regulators to examine how autonomous systems can operate inside financial infrastructure. Its agenda includes agentic banking, agentic payments, autonomous trading, agentic wallets and the regulatory limits on AI systems acting with financial authority.

**Why it matters:** The breadth of the agenda signals that agentic finance is expanding from an AI-product discussion into a financial-infrastructure and governance issue spanning payments, capital markets, custody and regulation.
