### US AI Brief — May 8, 2026

### Top Stories

- **White House Weighs AI Pre-Approval Amid Policy Shift**
    - **Source:** The Daily Signal · May 7, 2026
    - **Summary:** The Trump administration is actively discussing an executive order to create a formal pre-release review system for frontier AI models, a significant departure from its previous deregulatory stance. Officials are divided on the stringency of the review, but the shift is fueled by security concerns over models like Anthropic's Mythos and risks of AI-powered cyberattacks. National Economic Council Director Kevin Hassett drew parallels to the FDA's drug approval process, suggesting a rigorous pre-market safety standard.
    - **Why It Matters:** Moving to a pre-approval framework would fundamentally alter the AI industry's operating model in the U.S., potentially creating barriers to entry and impacting the speed of innovation.
    - **URL:** [White House Considers AI Regulation Executive Orders](https://www.dailysignal.com/2026/05/07/trump-mulls-ai-regulation-orders/)

- **White House Quickly Clarifies AI Vetting Stance; Expands Voluntary Deals**
    - **Source:** Gate 新闻 · May 8, 2026
    - **Summary:** Following Hassett's comments, the White House swiftly moved to clarify its position, stating the remarks were "taken out of context" and emphasizing a "partnership" with AI firms rather than "government regulation." Despite this, the Commerce Department's CAISI expanded its voluntary pre-deployment assessment agreements to include Google DeepMind, Microsoft, and xAI, joining OpenAI and Anthropic.
    - **Why It Matters:** This fast-moving narrative exposes a key tension within the administration between aggressive national security positions and an industry-friendly, pro-innovation policy.
    - **URL:** [白宮在 24 小時內撤回對 AI 模型預先審查的作法，但擴大自願評估](https://miniapp.gate.com/zh-tw/news/detail/white-house-backtracks-on-ai-model-pre-review-within-24-hours-but-expands-20920615)

- **OpenAI Launches Voice Model with GPT-5-Class Reasoning**
    - **Source:** The New Stack · May 7, 2026
    - **Summary:** OpenAI has released three new real-time audio models: GPT-Realtime-2, which features "GPT-5-class reasoning" and a significantly expanded context window; GPT-Realtime-Translate for live translations; and GPT-Realtime-Whisper for fast transcriptions. The new voice models are designed to handle more complex, multi-step requests and offer significantly more natural conversation.
    - **Why It Matters:** This makes advanced voice-based AI agents more capable, faster, and more practical for business applications like customer support and virtual assistants.
    - **URL:** [OpenAI brings GPT-5-level reasoning to its speech models](https://thenewstack.io/openai-gpt-5-level-speech/)

- **Microsoft Rolls Back AI, Cancels Xbox Copilot and Rethinks Windows Integration**
    - **Source:** PCMag · May 7, 2026
    - **Summary:** Microsoft has canceled plans to bring Copilot to Xbox consoles and is removing it from its mobile app in a "surprising cost-cutting move." Furthermore, a Microsoft VP stated that the company should "remove Copilot from places where it doesn't live up to its promise," indicating a potential pullback within Windows 11 as well.
    - **Why It Matters:** This marks a strategic shift from indiscriminately integrating AI into all products to a more ROI-focused, selective deployment strategy, which may influence other major tech companies.
    - **URL:** [Microsoft VP Backs Removing Copilot From Less Useful Corners of Windows](https://me.pcmag.com/en/ai/36979/microsoft-vp-backs-removing-copilot-from-less-useful-corners-of-windows)

- **Anthropic Secures SpaceX GPUs and Launches Multi-Agent Tools**
    - **Source:** eWeek · May 7, 2026
    - **Summary:** Anthropic has signed a deal with SpaceX to lease all 300 megawatts of its Colossus 1 supercomputer, securing over 220,000 Nvidia GPUs to dramatically boost Claude's capabilities and double usage limits. Separately, the company launched "Claude Add-Ins" for Microsoft Excel, PowerPoint, and Word, and introduced "dreaming" and multi-agent orchestration tools for its managed agents, allowing them to learn and improve across sessions.
    - **Why It Matters:** The GPU deal alleviates a major compute bottleneck for Anthropic, while the new agentic and productivity tools directly target enterprise workflows, positioning it as a stronger competitor to Microsoft and Google.
    - **URL:** [Anthropic Books SpaceX GPU Rocket for Claude](https://www.eweek.com/newsletter/daily-tech-insider/2026-05-07/)

    - **Why It Matters:** This opens the door for major AI model providers to integrate directly into Apple's ecosystem, creating a new distribution channel and challenging the dominance of default AI assistants.
