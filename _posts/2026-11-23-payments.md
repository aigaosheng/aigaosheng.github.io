# Payments Brief: MAS and PBC Explore Fast Payment Link

**Today:** A proposed Singapore–China payment link and new bank-connected stablecoin pilots show how regional payment infrastructure is expanding across both conventional and token-based rails.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🌐 **MAS and PBC Explore Fast Payment Link**

*Monetary Authority of Singapore · November 23, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Singapore and China are exploring a link between their fast payment systems for small-value transfers.

MAS said the proposed connection would complement existing local-currency payment and settlement channels between the two markets. The announcement came at the Singapore–China Joint Council for Bilateral Cooperation meeting on November 23.

**Why it matters:** A link would extend Singapore’s existing PayNow connections with regional payment systems to another major trading partner. The proposal is still at the exploration stage, so the scope, participating services and timeline remain to be determined.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2026/singapore-and-china-strengthen-financial-cooperation-with-new-rmb-initiatives)

### 2. 🌐 **OCBC and StraitsX Pilot Bank-Linked Stablecoin Payments**

*StraitsX · November 23, 2026*

**Bottom line:** OCBC and StraitsX are testing cross-border stablecoin payments that settle into participating businesses’ existing OCBC accounts.

StraitsX’s Dedicated Wallet Address infrastructure is intended to support collection, payment identification, on-chain compliance and reconciliation; OCBC provides the banking relationship and fiat off-ramp. NTT DOCOMO ASIA is the first participating customer, and the pilot will initially cover selected cross-border business payment flows.

**Why it matters:** The model tests whether businesses can use stablecoin settlement without managing the underlying tokens or a separate stablecoin platform. For Singapore banks and payment providers, the pilot offers a practical test of how on-chain collection can connect to existing business banking operations.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.straitsx.com/blog-post/straitsx-and-ocbc-pilot-cross-border-stablecoin-payments-into-business-bank-accounts)

### 3. 🏦 **BSP Proposes Wider Access to Payment Systems**

*The Philippine Star · November 23, 2026*

**Bottom line:** The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas is proposing rules for payment initiation providers and clearing sponsorship arrangements.

Under the draft, qualified third parties could initiate payments with customer consent but could not hold customer funds; proposed providers would need at least ₱5 million in capital. The proposal would also let smaller institutions use larger banks to clear transactions, with the BSP seeking comments until December 11.

**Why it matters:** The framework could widen access to digital payment services and clearing infrastructure while keeping funds and transaction approval with banks and e-money issuers. Fintechs considering the Philippine market will need to assess the proposed authorisation, capital and consumer-protection requirements.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://qa.philstar.com/business/2026/11/23/2571909/bsp-seeks-wider-access-digital-payment-systems/amp/)

### 4. 🌐 **M-DAQ and Circle Develop USDC Payment Services**

*Fintech News Singapore · November 23, 2026*

**Bottom line:** M-DAQ Global and Circle are working on a single integration for institutional clients to convert local currencies into USDC and back.

The planned service would combine USDC with M-DAQ’s existing FX and payment services. M-DAQ also plans to use the infrastructure for its own cross-border fund flows and liquidity management.

**Why it matters:** The partnership aims to join stablecoin settlement with FX and local payment access across ASEAN and other markets. The service is still in development, so corridor coverage, operating details and launch timing have not yet been established.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://fintechnews.sg/142152/digitalassets/m-daq-circle/)

### 5. 🌐 **YeePay Adds Banking and Payments Partners**

*PR Newswire · November 23, 2026*

**Bottom line:** YeePay announced new partnerships with ClearBank and Finmo, alongside MoUs with Banking Circle, KBank and GLN.

The announcements followed YeePay’s participation in the Singapore FinTech Festival, held November 18–20. The company presented cross-border payment, FX, collection and payout services for businesses operating across markets.

**Why it matters:** The partner mix points to a strategy of extending payment connectivity through regional and international institutions. The announcement does not specify which services or routes will launch first, making implementation details the next measure of commercial progress.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/yeepay-showcases-one-stop-payment-network-and-expands-strategic-partnerships-at-singapore-fintech-festival-2026-302947875.html)

### 6. 🌐 **Kukon and Triple-A Plan Korea–ASEAN Payment Links**

*Bloomingbit · November 23, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Kukon and Singapore-based Triple-A plan to connect Korean payment and withdrawal infrastructure with stablecoin payment and settlement services.

The companies signed their MoU at the Singapore FinTech Festival on November 19 and disclosed it on November 23. Their plans include expanding stablecoin top-ups, QR payments and Korean-won ATM withdrawals, as well as designing and testing a cross-border remittance and settlement model.

**Why it matters:** Kukon says its Singapore subsidiary could serve as a hub for a two-way Korea–ASEAN payments network. The planned connection remains exploratory, with operating scope and rollout still to be tested.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://en.bloomingbit.io/feed/news/125020)

### 7. 🤖 **Visa Crypto Labs Tests Wallet for Paid AI Tasks**

*Crypto.news · November 23, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Visa Crypto Labs introduced an experimental wallet that lets eligible US Visa cardholders verify AI agents and receive OUSD for accepted tasks.

The Visa card verifies the agent’s owner; task payments go to the owner’s wallet in OUSD, a stablecoin issued by Bridge. Access is limited to approved US accounts, and the report says the marketplace does not guarantee earnings.

**Why it matters:** The experiment brings payment credentials, agent identity and stablecoin payouts into one workflow. Its initial US eligibility and task-market focus make adoption and completed payment activity key signals to watch before drawing conclusions about broader commerce use.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://crypto.news/visa-ai-agent-wallet-earn-ousd-tempo-taskmarket/)

## Key Takeaways

- Singapore–China payment connectivity is being explored alongside bank-connected stablecoin pilots, extending the region’s mix of cross-border rails.
- Several stablecoin initiatives are designed to connect token settlement to familiar banking, FX and local payment infrastructure.
- Payment access is also changing through proposed third-party initiation rules and experiments that give AI agents defined ways to transact.

## What to Watch

Watch for details on the scope and participants in the MAS–PBC fast-payment study, and whether OCBC and StraitsX expand beyond selected pilot flows. The BSP’s comment period for its proposed rules runs until December 11.
