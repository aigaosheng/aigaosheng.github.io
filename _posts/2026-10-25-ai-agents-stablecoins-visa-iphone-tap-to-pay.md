## 1. Top Headlines

- [Visa pilots AI agent payments between buyer and supplier platforms](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/48610/visa-pilots-ai-agent-payments-between-buyer-and-supplier-platforms) (Finextra): Visa, BILL and Billtrust have launched what they call the first live pilot of trusted AI agents completing approved B2B invoice payments through supplier portals. If it scales, it could remove the manual entry that holds back automated supplier payments. More below.

- [Visa targets trust in business payments](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/48629/visa-targets-trust-in-business-payments) (Finextra): Visa introduced tools covering supplier validation, 3-D Secure authentication for commercial virtual cards, and controls on spend, merchant category, location and timing. It also added AI-driven supplier scoring to help banks find suppliers likely to accept commercial cards. The focus is fraud control as virtual card use grows.

- [Mesh makes stablecoin and crypto balances spendable across Visa network](https://www.finextra.com/pressarticle/111346/mesh-makes-stablecoin-and-crypto-balances-spendable-across-visa-network) (Finextra): Mesh will let wallet apps offer in-store and online spending straight from crypto or stablecoin balances over Visa's network. Platforms can enable it through a single integration without building a card program, lowering the barrier for wallets to offer payments.

- [Fintechs top banks and stablecoins for cheap cross-border payment - BIS research](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/48630/fintechs-top-banks-and-stablecoins-for-cheap-cross-border-payment---bis-research) (Finextra): A BIS paper finds fintech transfer operators such as Wise and Remitly cut cross-border costs well below bank wires. Stablecoins lag because exchange fees and thin liquidity outside the US dollar add friction.

- [UK sets out proposal to open up access to iPhone contactless payments](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/48618/uk-sets-out-proposal-to-open-up-access-to-iphone-contactless-payments) (Finextra): The UK competition regulator wants Apple Pay rivals to reach the iPhone's contactless technology on lower fees and fewer technical barriers. If adopted, banks and fintechs could launch their own tap-to-pay wallets.

- [Klarna expands Fiserv partnership to enterprise merchants](https://www.finextra.com/pressarticle/111349/klarna-expands-fiserv-partnership-to-enterprise-merchants) (Finextra): Klarna will be available on Fiserv's Commerce Hub in the US, Canada, the UK and select European markets, in store and online. It follows a launch at 100,000 US in-store locations via Clover, showing buy now, pay later pushing into physical retail.

- [Cross River Bank to buy customer loyalty-focused paytech Accrue](https://www.fintechfutures.com/m-a/cross-river-bank-acquires-accrue) (FinTech Futures): The deal will let Cross River's business customers embed own-branded wallets in their offerings. It points to embedded finance providers bundling wallets and loyalty into their core proposition.

- [Money20/20 USA 2026: Agentic commerce takes centre stage on day two](https://www.fintechfutures.com/ai-in-fintech/money2020-usa-2026-agentic-commerce-take-centre-stage-on-day-two) (FinTech Futures): AI payments dominated Tuesday's discussions as the industry weighed the opportunities and challenges of autonomous transactions. Trust and accountability for agents appear to be the open questions.

- [Money20/20 USA 2026 wraps with cross-border payments and open banking in focus](https://www.fintechfutures.com/fintech/money20-20-usa-2026-wraps-with-cross-border-payments-and-open-banking-in-focus) (FinTech Futures): The event closed on Wednesday with cross-border payments and open banking high on the agenda. Together with agentic commerce, these are likely to shape 2027 payments roadmaps.

## 2. In-Depth Highlight

Visa, BILL and Billtrust have launched a live pilot in which trusted AI agents initiate and complete approved invoice payments end-to-end through supplier portals, [Finextra reports](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/48610/visa-pilots-ai-agent-payments-between-buyer-and-supplier-platforms). Supplier portals usually require human authentication, which limits how far payments can be automated. Here, BILL's payment agent is registered through Visa's Trusted Agent Protocol and Agentic Directory and uses tokenised credentials, while Billtrust uses the same protocol to recognise and authenticate the agent.

In its [announcement](https://usa.visa.com/about-visa/newsroom/press-releases.releaseId.22856.html), Visa says buyers do not need to share portal logins and the agent only executes payments the buyer has already approved. Visa also says buyer platforms could extend card acceptance to suppliers that mainly supported automated payments through ACH or check. Why it matters: agentic commerce is moving from consumer shopping demos to an accounts-payable workflow, where authentication, tokenisation and payment controls decide whether finance teams will trust it. This is a pilot, so scale and supplier adoption remain to be shown.

## 3. Market & Industry Insight

Stablecoins are reaching the checkout through card rails. Mesh's Visa partnership targets a network that, per [Finextra's coverage](https://www.finextra.com/pressarticle/111346/mesh-makes-stablecoin-and-crypto-balances-spendable-across-visa-network), spans upwards of 175 million merchant locations in more than 200 countries and territories, with US$14.2 trillion in payments volume in fiscal 2025. Mesh orchestrates how each purchase is funded and converted across crypto, stablecoins and fiat, and more than 200 enterprises already use its network. The model keeps merchants and cardholders on familiar rails while wallets and platforms handle the digital-asset side.

The cost story is less settled. The [BIS working paper](https://www.bis.org/publications/working-paper-1385-competing-rails-crossborder-payments-banks-fintechs-and-stablecoins) finds fintech money transfer operators substantially cheaper than bank wires, and says stablecoin competitiveness depends on market liquidity, fiat access, compliance and trust in intermediaries, not blockchain alone. For payment providers, the lesson is that every conversion step in the chain costs money, whichever rail is used.

Agent-initiated payments add a third pressure: proving who is acting and under what authority. The Visa pilot answers that with agent registries, tokens and invoice-specific controls, and the topic dominated Money20/20 this week.

## 4. Company & Startup Spotlight

**Mesh:** A crypto payments network that connects wallets, exchanges and platforms, and now acts as program manager for the Visa partnership. Per [Finextra](https://www.finextra.com/pressarticle/111346/mesh-makes-stablecoin-and-crypto-balances-spendable-across-visa-network), it already counts more than 200 enterprises on its network. Watch it as a test of whether wallet-to-merchant spending can scale without separate crypto cards.

**Whop:** A US platform that helps entrepreneurs run digital storefronts, accept fiat and digital payments, send invoices and automate services with AI. It has [raised a US$200 million Series D](https://www.fintechfutures.com/venture-capital-funding/whop-200m-series-d) at a US$5.1 billion valuation, led by Sequoia Capital with Shopify Ventures participating. It is the company's second raise this year, after a February round from Tether at US$1.6 billion.

## 5. Regulatory & Policy Watch

- The UK competition regulator is [consulting on iPhone contactless access](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/48618/uk-sets-out-proposal-to-open-up-access-to-iphone-contactless-payments). The proposals would require Apple to provide access through both main contactless standards, and the regulator is not proposing to set the price of access. The consultation runs until 17 November.

- The European Banking Authority and the UK Financial Conduct Authority have [contacted Google over a major cloud outage](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/48637/uk-and-eu-financial-regulators-seek-answers-from-google-over-cloud-outage) that knocked out several bank websites for around six hours. The UK Treasury Committee has asked the Bank of England and FCA about their response. Banks remain responsible for their services, and the UK put the four largest cloud providers under direct oversight in July, so payment firms should expect more scrutiny of cloud concentration.

## 7. What's Next

The UK iPhone contactless consultation closes on 17 November, and Apple's response is worth watching. Regulators have yet to say what they learned from the Google Cloud outage, and the Treasury Committee's questions to the Bank of England and FCA are outstanding. On the commercial side, watch which wallets go live first on Mesh's Visa integration and whether the BILL and Billtrust agent pilot expands beyond its first live phase.
