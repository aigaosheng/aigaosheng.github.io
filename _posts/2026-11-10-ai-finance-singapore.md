# AI Finance in Singapore Brief: MAS Previews AI Agenda For Singapore Fintech Festival

**Today:** With Singapore FinTech Festival two weeks out, MAS and industry players are staking out positions on AI ahead of the event, giving executives an early read on where regulatory and vendor priorities are heading.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🏦 **MAS Previews AI-Heavy Agenda For Singapore Fintech Festival 2026**

*Fintech News Singapore · November 10, 2026*

**Bottom line:** The Monetary Authority of Singapore and the Global Finance & Technology Network have set out an AI-heavy agenda for Singapore FinTech Festival 2026, running November 18-20, built around five "forces" reshaping finance: technology, geoeconomics, capital, talent and policy.

AI threads through several tracks: technology sessions will cover using AI to lift productivity while containing AI-enabled scams, cyber threats and quantum risk; capital-markets discussions will probe whether revenue growth can sustain an AI investment boom that has pushed AI-linked firms to roughly 40% of S&P 500 market capitalisation; and talent sessions address reskilling as AI reshapes finance jobs. The festival, organised with the Association of Banks in Singapore as a collaborator, will also run the Global Fintech Hackcelerator Demo Day and an Investor Hours programme.

**Why it matters:** As Asia's flagship fintech event, SFF's agenda signals where MAS wants to steer industry priorities over the next year, from responsible AI deployment to balancing innovation against fraud and systemic risk — shaping the compliance and investment conversations financial institutions will have through 2027.

### 2. 📊 **BIS Survey Finds MAS Among Central Banks Scaling Up AI Use**

*Fintech News Singapore · November 10, 2026*

**Bottom line:** A Bank for International Settlements survey of 65 central banks found nearly two-thirds now treat AI as a strategic priority, up from under half in 2024, and named the Monetary Authority of Singapore among regulators already applying AI to supervision.

The survey, run by the BIS's Irving Fisher Committee, found more than two-thirds of central bank AI projects have reached production, versus roughly half two years earlier, though compliance-monitoring capacity for AI itself was rated only "moderate" by about half of respondents. The underlying report states that MAS and the Hong Kong Monetary Authority are deploying AI for off-site monitoring and anomaly detection, alongside other Asian regulators using AI to review board minutes and monitor listed-company disclosures.

**Why it matters:** As supervisors expand their own AI toolsets, Singapore-based financial institutions should expect sharper, faster scrutiny of AI-related risk disclosures and controls, raising the bar for internal AI governance ahead of MAS's risk management guidelines phasing in from October 2027.

### 3. 🤖 **NEC Showcases AI Tools For Banks And Insurers At SFF 2026**

*PR Newswire · November 10, 2026*

**Bottom line:** NEC Asia Pacific says it will exhibit a suite of AI tools for financial institutions at Singapore FinTech Festival 2026, including an agentic-AI workflow assistant, image analytics for motor-insurance claim fraud detection, and wealth-management services built with partner Avaloq.

According to the release, NEC's offerings span treasury decision-making, supply-chain financing with partner TASConnect, and insurance claims processing, with additional input from consultancy Synpulse. NEC has scheduled fireside chats during the festival on agentic AI in treasury and tokenisation in supply-chain finance.

**Why it matters:** The showcase reflects how established technology integrators are packaging agentic AI and fraud-detection tools specifically for Singapore-based banks and insurers; procurement teams should treat these as vendor claims to validate independently rather than proven outcomes.

### 4. 🤖 **Newgen And NUS Digital Finance Institute Partner On AI**

*PR Newswire · November 10, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Enterprise software vendor Newgen Software has signed a non-binding memorandum of understanding with the Asian Institute of Digital Finance (AIDF) at the National University of Singapore to collaborate on AI research and enterprise applications for financial services.

Under the MoU, the two plan joint research and development, translating findings into industry use cases, engaging startups and regulators, and running capability-building and education programmes; no funding figures, named projects or timelines were disclosed. AIDF was jointly founded by NUS, MAS and Singapore's National Research Foundation.

**Why it matters:** The tie-up adds to a growing roster of industry-academia partnerships channelled through AIDF, aimed at turning Singapore's AI research base into deployable financial-sector tools, though the absence of concrete deliverables means its practical impact remains unproven for now.

## Key Takeaways

- MAS is using the run-up to Singapore FinTech Festival to frame AI simultaneously as a productivity opportunity and a fraud/systemic-risk concern, rather than treating the two as separate workstreams.
- Central bank supervisors, including MAS, are adopting AI for their own oversight work faster than they are finalising compliance-monitoring capacity for AI — a gap worth watching as guidelines phase in.
- Vendor and academic activity around SFF (NEC, Newgen/AIDF) shows Singapore positioning itself as a hub for enterprise and research-based finance AI, though most announcements so far are pre-event previews rather than completed deployments.

## What to Watch

Singapore FinTech Festival runs November 18-20, 2026, with the Global Fintech Hackcelerator Demo Day on November 18 and the Fintech Excellence Awards on November 19 — expect concrete product launches, MAS announcements and Hackcelerator winners (including the GXS Bank, Julius Baer and Zurich Insurance challenge tracks) to emerge from those dates.
