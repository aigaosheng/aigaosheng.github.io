### 1. Anthropic Launches "Claude Tag" to Embed AI Agents in Slack Workflows
- **The Hindu** · 2026-06-24
- **Summary**: Anthropic has launched "Claude Tag," a new feature that allows its AI assistant, Claude, to be summoned directly within Salesforce's Slack app. Users can type "@Claude" in a thread to have the AI read conversations, break down tasks, and proactively flag updates across an organization. It is available in beta for Claude Enterprise and Team customers, with plans to expand to other platforms . This builds on Salesforce's strategic partnership with Anthropic to integrate Claude, Slack, and Agentforce, signaling a move to make Slack the central hub for enterprise AI agents .
- **Why It Matters**: The launch represents a major shift in enterprise AI, moving from standalone chatbots to integrated digital coworkers that operate within core business workflows. By embedding Claude into Slack, Anthropic and Salesforce are positioning AI to become a collaborative team member, capable of executing tasks and managing complex workflows directly where employees already work .
- **URL**: [Anthropic launches Claude Tag in Slack with plans for wider rollout](https://www.thehindu.com/sci-tech/technology/anthropic-launches-claude-tag-in-slack-with-plans-for-wider-rollout/article71140647.ece)
