### 1. SoFi Acquires Composer to Launch AI-Powered Automated Investing Platform
- **Reuters via Yahoo Finance** · 2026-06-23
- **Summary**: SoFi Technologies has acquired AI startup Composer to launch "Composer by SoFi," a platform enabling retail investors to create, test, and automate sophisticated trading strategies using plain English. The acquisition aims to democratize access to systematic investing tools traditionally reserved for institutional firms .
- **Why It Matters**: This move intensifies the competition among retail brokerages to differentiate themselves in a commoditized market. By embedding AI-driven strategy automation, SoFi aims to deepen user engagement and enhance the value proposition of its "SoFi Plus" subscription service .

### 2. Backbase Acquires Kasisto to Advance Agentic Banking
- **PYMNTS.com** · 2026-06-23
- **Summary**: Dutch fintech Backbase has acquired Kasisto, a specialist in agentic AI and financial services intelligence. ### 3. MoonPay Acquires AI Bookkeeping Startup Entendre

### 4. HSBC Wealth Survey Reveals AI's Limits with High-Net-Worth Clients
- **Bloomberg via The Edge Singapore** · 2026-06-24
- **Summary**: A survey by HSBC of about 10,000 affluent individuals found that 62% still rely on human professionals for investment ideas, while only 12% cite AI as the most influential factor. The survey highlights that wealthy clients value human judgement, context, and accountability for making investment decisions .
- **Why It Matters**: While AI excels at analysis and research, it struggles to replace the trust and nuanced advisory required for high-net-worth clients. This suggests a hybrid model will prevail, where AI augments rather than replaces human advisors, especially as Gen Z and millennials show greater comfort with AI integration .
- **URL**: [Read more](https://www.theedgesingapore.com/news/banking-finance/hsbc-wealth-survey-shows-ai-losing-out-humans-key-areas)
