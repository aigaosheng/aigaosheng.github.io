# US AI vs China AI Brief — 2026-10-04

**Today:** The US is prioritizing faster AI development and global scientific leadership, while China is turning AI into a broad national capability through education and workforce deployment.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **US leads international push for AI-powered scientific discovery**

*The White House · October 4, 2026*

**Bottom line:** The United States is positioning AI-powered scientific discovery as a strategic national capability and is building an international coalition around that vision.

The White House said the US and 16 other countries endorsed the Kyoto Vision for a Golden Age of Science, calling for broader access to AI systems, computing infrastructure, scientific data and experimental facilities. The initiative explicitly highlights “super intelligence” as a tool for accelerating scientific discovery and expanding autonomous experimentation.

**Why it matters:** The competition with China is increasingly about more than foundation-model benchmarks; it is also about which country can convert frontier AI into faster scientific and industrial progress. A US-led international research network could strengthen America's advantage through access to global talent, data, compute and research partnerships.

### 2. 🏦 **Bessent says the US cannot afford to cede AI leadership to China**

*SBS News · October 4, 2026*

**Bottom line:** US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent is advocating “safe acceleration” rather than slower AI development, explicitly framing continued US leadership over China as a strategic imperative.

Bessent said the United States must not lose its leading position in AI and argued that excessive fear over advanced AI should not translate into policies that slow American development. He also emphasized that AI companies and research laboratories should take greater responsibility for managing safety risks.

**Why it matters:** The policy debate is becoming a competitive question: Washington is weighing AI safeguards against the perceived cost of slowing US companies relative to China. That favors a regulatory model centered on industry responsibility and targeted controls rather than broad restrictions on frontier development.

### 3. 🤖 **Beijing expands AI education across 50 model schools and universities**

*ChinaTechNews.com · October 4, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Beijing is accelerating AI education at scale, designating 50 schools and universities as model institutions for integrating AI into teaching, curriculum and professional training.

The Beijing Municipal Education Commission selected 45 primary and secondary schools and five higher-education institutions for the new program. More than 1,400 Beijing primary and secondary schools, serving about 1.83 million students, already have introductory AI classes, with the city reporting a 91% AI-application rate among primary and secondary students.

**Why it matters:** China is treating AI literacy as national infrastructure rather than a niche technical skill. The long-term competitive advantage could come not only from today's models or chips, but from building a much larger population of workers accustomed to using AI throughout education and professional life.
