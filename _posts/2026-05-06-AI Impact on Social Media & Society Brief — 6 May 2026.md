## Top Stories

### 1. AI Deepfake Videos Impersonating Doctors Trigger Health Misinformation Concerns

**Axios · 6 May 2026**
AI-generated deepfake videos are being used to impersonate medical professionals and promote fraudulent health products and misleading medical advice. Medical associations warn that this is escalating into a public health risk, as synthetic “expert endorsements” are becoming harder to distinguish from real clinical guidance.

**Why It Matters:**
This signals a critical expansion of AI misuse beyond politics into healthcare, where misinformation can directly affect physical well-being and public trust in medical systems.

### 2. Political Deepfake Controversy Sparks Renewed Debate on Synthetic Media Regulation

**Reuters · 5 May 2026**
Manipulated AI-generated images targeting political figures, including Italy’s Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni, circulated widely online. The incident triggered public condemnation and renewed calls for stronger controls over synthetic media distribution on social platforms.

**Why It Matters:**
This reinforces how AI-generated content is now a direct factor in political communication risk, accelerating regulatory pressure on platforms to detect and label synthetic media at scale.
