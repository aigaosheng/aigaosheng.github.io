# Enterprise AI Brief: Estée Lauder and Microsoft Expand AI Partnership

**Today:** Global brands are locking in platform-level AI commitments even as fresh Asia-Pacific data shows execution still lagging far behind ambition.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **Estée Lauder Expands Microsoft Copilot to 14,000+ Staff**

*The Estée Lauder Companies newsroom · October 30, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Estée Lauder is scaling a multi-year Microsoft partnership to put Copilot in front of more than 14,000 employees while building unified data platforms meant to underpin AI-driven personalization.

The beauty group said it has expanded Copilot across its global office-based workforce, spanning roughly 24 brands and about 150 markets, as part of three strategic priorities: new capabilities and growth, decision intelligence, and productivity and automation. A central piece is a Unified Data Platform, alongside a new Unified Consumer Data Platform meant to connect data across brands, regions and channels to support more personalised consumer experiences.

**Why it matters:** The move signals large consumer goods companies shifting from departmental copilot pilots to workforce-wide rollouts tied to a single data backbone — a pattern enterprise AI buyers in Asia's retail and consumer sectors are likely to weigh as they balance platform lock-in against speed of deployment.

### 2. 📊 **Baseten Nears $30 Billion Valuation in New $2 Billion Raise**

*Bloomberg · October 30, 2026*

**Bottom line:** AI inference platform Baseten is raising roughly $2 billion across two tranches at valuations of $26 billion and then $30 billion, more than doubling its $13 billion valuation from just four months ago.

Sands Capital is expected to lead the round, with existing backers including Altimeter Capital, IVP and Spark Capital. Baseten provides infrastructure that lets enterprises deploy and run multiple AI models through a single platform, and the report frames the jump as a sign that spending on AI inference is starting to rival, and may eventually exceed, spending on model training.

**Why it matters:** The valuation surge shows enterprise AI budgets shifting from one-off model training toward the recurring infrastructure needed to run agents and applications in production — a cost center that enterprise and fintech buyers, including in Asia, will need to plan for directly rather than treat as a side expense.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-30/baseten-set-for-30-billion-valuation-with-new-funding)

### 3. 🌐 **Samsung's Top Executives Meet OpenAI's Altman on AI Strategy**

*The Korea Herald · October 31, 2026*

**Bottom line:** About 50 senior Samsung Group executives met OpenAI chief executive Sam Altman in Yongin, South Korea, to discuss how AI should reshape corporate decision-making over the next decade.

The session, themed "The Next 10 Years Shaped by AI: The Enterprise's Choice," brought together presidents and other leaders from Samsung's electronics, finance and biotechnology affiliates. Samsung said it has trained about 2,300 executives in generative AI since June 2026 and aims to extend training to all employees by February 2027, building on its earlier rollout of ChatGPT Enterprise and Codex across its Device eXperience division.

**Why it matters:** One of Asia's largest conglomerates is treating AI fluency as a board-level, group-wide mandate rather than a single-department pilot — a model other APAC enterprises weighing large OpenAI or Anthropic enterprise contracts are likely to study closely.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10921312)

### 4. 🤖 **Only 2% of APAC Telcos Run AI Agents at Scale**

*Tribune (Philippines), citing TM Forum research · October 31, 2026*

**Bottom line:** New TM Forum research found just 2% of Asia-Pacific telecom operators have deployed AI agents at scale in their core systems, even though 89% of their CIOs call AI critical to competitiveness.

The survey, released ahead of TM Forum's Innovate Asia 2026 event, cited skills shortages (49%), poor data quality (43%), implementation costs (42%) and concerns about AI inaccuracy (39%) as the main barriers to deployment. It found 72% of operators still treat AI as experimental, and only 55% plan to prioritise core-system integration over the next 18 months.

**Why it matters:** The gap between stated ambition and actual execution is a cautionary data point for any APAC enterprise — including banks and payment providers — accelerating agentic AI roadmaps without first resolving underlying data-quality and skills gaps.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://tribune.net.ph/2026/10/30/telcos-struggle-to-deploy-ai-at-scale)

### 5. 🤖 **ZoomInfo Plugs Go-to-Market Data Into Snowflake's AI Agents**

*ZoomInfo newsroom · October 30, 2026*

**Bottom line:** ZoomInfo launched an MCP connector and API inside Snowflake's AI Data Cloud, letting Snowflake Cortex Agents query ZoomInfo's company and contact data in natural language.

The integration, delivered through ZoomInfo's GTM.AI platform, gives agents access to data on more than 100 million companies and 500 million contacts, with OAuth 2.0 authentication that inherits a user's existing role-based permissions and logs activity through ZoomInfo's audit framework. It joins a growing roster of GTM.AI integrations spanning Salesforce, HubSpot, Microsoft, Claude and ChatGPT.

**Why it matters:** Data and CRM vendors are racing to become the trusted data layer behind enterprise AI agents rather than building competing agents themselves — a distinction enterprise procurement teams should probe when comparing vendor roadmaps.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://ir.zoominfo.com/news-releases/news-release-details/zoominfo-launches-new-mcp-connector-and-api-snowflake)

## Key Takeaways

- Enterprise AI's center of gravity is shifting from pilots to platform-level commitments, visible in both a consumer brand's workforce-wide rollout (Estée Lauder) and a conglomerate-wide strategy session (Samsung).
- Capital is flowing increasingly toward inference and run-time infrastructure rather than model training alone, as shown by Baseten's valuation more than doubling in four months.
- Asia-Pacific data points to a persistent execution gap: high executive conviction about AI's importance is not yet translating into at-scale deployment, especially in telecom.

## What to Watch

Watch whether Samsung's push to train all employees in generative AI by February 2027 translates into an expanded enterprise contract with OpenAI, and whether TM Forum's Innovate Asia event prompts APAC telecom operators to commit to firmer agentic AI deployment timelines.
