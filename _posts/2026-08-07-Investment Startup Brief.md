## Market & Investment Insight

### AI Capital Concentration Continues, but Vertical AI Attracts New Funding

The latest startup investment activity shows two parallel trends:

* **Mega-scale AI infrastructure companies** continue attracting billion-dollar financing rounds as investors compete for exposure to foundational AI platforms.
* **Vertical AI startups** focused on commerce, infrastructure, healthcare, and industrial workflows are attracting early-stage capital as investors search for differentiated applications with clear business value.

The broader venture market is increasingly rewarding startups that combine AI capabilities with domain expertise, proprietary data, and measurable enterprise impact.
