# Payment Brief — 2026-08-19

## Top Stories

### 1. **Visa Seeks New Stablecoin Settlement Partner After Mastercard’s BVNK Acquisition**

* **Source**: Blockhead · August 19, 2026
* **Summary**: Visa is reportedly seeking a new partner for stablecoin settlement and over-the-counter infrastructure after Mastercard's acquisition of BVNK placed Visa's former provider under the ownership of a direct competitor. The search reportedly requires broad regulatory coverage across the US, Canada, UK and Singapore and comes as Visa expands its own stablecoin infrastructure.
* **Why It Matters**: The episode highlights how stablecoin infrastructure is becoming strategically important to the major card networks. Consolidation could increasingly force payment incumbents to secure direct control over settlement capabilities rather than depend on shared third-party providers.

### 2. **MoMo Expands QR Payments to More Than 60 International Markets**

* **Source**: Fintech News Vietnam · August 19, 2026
* **Summary**: Vietnamese e-wallet MoMo has expanded its international QR payment capability to more than 60 markets through connections with networks including Napas, Alipay, WeChat Pay, PayPay and PromptPay. The service supports overseas payments across destinations including China, Japan, Singapore, South Korea and Thailand.
* **Why It Matters**: Cross-border QR interoperability is moving digital wallets beyond domestic ecosystems. For payment providers, international acceptance networks could become a major differentiator as Asian travel and cross-border commerce continue to digitize.

### 5. **M-PESA Ethiopia Launches Merchant Campaign to Accelerate Digital Payments**
