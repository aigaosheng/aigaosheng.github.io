## Top Stories

### 1. Defence Tech Startup Helsing Valued at $18 Billion in Series E
- **Bloomberg** · 2026-07-13
- **Summary**: Munich-based defence startup Helsing has closed a $1.8 billion Series E funding round, valuing the company at $18 billion. The round saw participation from the growth equity arm of Goldman Sachs Alternatives, Dragoneer Investment Group, Iconiq, the Canada Pension Plan Investment Board, and JPMorgan Chase. This cements Helsing's position as Europe's largest defence startup .
- **Why It Matters**: The massive round signals strong institutional appetite for defence technology, a sector increasingly viewed as a strategic investment priority amid geopolitical tensions. The valuation validates the thesis that AI-driven defence platforms can command premium multiples comparable to top-tier enterprise software companies.

### 2. Paradigm Expands Beyond Crypto with $1.2 Billion Fund
- **Parsers VC** · 2026-07-13
- **Summary**: Paradigm, a venture capital firm historically focused on cryptocurrency, has raised a new $1.2 billion fund that will broaden its investment mandate to include artificial intelligence, robotics, and other "technical frontier" sectors. The firm has already deployed capital from the new fund into companies such as autonomous drone delivery firm Zipline and defence and space startup True Anomaly .
- **Why It Matters**: Paradigm's strategic pivot is a bellwether for venture capital. It reflects a market-wide shift toward deep tech and AI, where the scale of opportunity now rivals, and perhaps exceeds, the early days of crypto. This could trigger further capital rotation from crypto-native funds into multi-sector tech strategies.
- **URL**: [Read more](https://parsers.vc/news/260713-paradigm-unveils-1-2-billion-fund--broadens/)

### 3. Carlyle to Acquire Controlling Stake in Indian Defence Tech Firm Micropack
- **VC Circle** · 2026-07-13
- **Summary**: Private equity giant Carlyle is set to invest Rs 1,175 crore ($125 million) for a controlling 60% stake in Bengaluru-based defence electronics parts maker Micropack. The deal, which will see Carlyle reduce its stake in phases while retaining a minimum of 51%, marks its first acquisition in the Indian defence technology space. Co-investors include Yali Capital and Mathew Cyriac of Florintree .
- **Why It Matters**: This investment underscores a growing trend of global PE firms entering the defence supply chain sector in India. It signals confidence in India's defence manufacturing ecosystem and likely follows a thesis of increasing domestic and export demand for electronic components in aerospace, defence, and space systems.

### 4. Singapore's SimpleAI Raises $15M for Acquisition-Led Growth
- **DealStreetAsia** · 2026-07-12
- **Summary**: Singapore-based accounting automation startup SimpleAI has raised a total of $15 million, comprising a $5 million seed round and a $10 million debt facility. The company plans to use the debt to acquire accounting and fund administration firms across Asia Pacific, with an immediate focus on Singapore and Australia. This marks a strategic shift from organic to acquisition-led growth .
- **Why It Matters**: SimpleAI's hybrid equity-debt financing model is innovative for early-stage startups. It demonstrates a path to rapid scale by using AI agents as the technology integration platform to roll-up fragmented, traditional service businesses—a playbook that could be replicated by other vertical AI startups.

### 6. Corporate Venture Capital and "Venture Clienting" Strategy Gains Traction
- **Global Venturing** · 2026-07-13
- **Summary**: Analysis of recent deals—including Proxima Fusion's €411M round with strategic investors RWE and Google, and Eni's $225M investment in lithium miner EnergyX—highlights the integration of CVC with "venture clienting." In the Eni-EnergyX deal, Eni secures rights to approximately 25% of future lithium production, making it both an investor and a client .
- **Why It Matters**: This dual role of strategic investor and customer de-risks investments for both parties. For the startup, it provides a guaranteed offtake, credibility, and a pathway to revenue. For the corporate, it secures supply chains and validates technology before full-scale deployment.
- **URL**: [Read more](https://globalventuring.com/corporate/overview/when-innovation-meets-capital-and-customers/)
