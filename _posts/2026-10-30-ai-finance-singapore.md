# AI Finance in Singapore Brief: Aspire Launches AI Accounting Platform

**Today:** Singapore fintechs are pushing AI deeper into core finance workflows, with the day's lone qualifying development showing how AI-driven automation is expanding from payments into bookkeeping for SMEs.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **Aspire Launches AspireBooks, an AI-Powered Accounting Platform for SMEs**

*Fintech News Singapore · October 30, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Aspire is moving beyond its core business payments offering into AI-automated bookkeeping, betting that small businesses will pay for software that removes manual accounting work.

Aspire's new AspireBooks service uses AI to classify transactions, recommend accounting categories and tax treatments, and automatically match payments with invoices and bills, alongside multicurrency accounting and financial reporting. The company said internal research found 41% of its customers still manage bookkeeping manually, and it developed the product with Singapore Accountants to align with local accounting standards. AspireBooks is priced at S$39 a month, with a three-month free introductory period for Singapore businesses.

**Why it matters:** The launch signals that Singapore fintechs are treating AI-driven back-office automation as a competitive battleground, not just a payments feature, which raises the bar for incumbent accounting software vendors and digital banks targeting the same SME segment across Southeast Asia.

## Key Takeaways

- Singapore fintechs are extending AI beyond payments and lending into adjacent back-office functions like bookkeeping, widening the competitive surface against accounting software incumbents.
- Local compliance partnerships, such as Aspire's work with Singapore Accountants, are becoming a differentiator for AI products aimed at regulated SME financial workflows.
- Pricing and free-trial strategies (S$39/month, three months free) suggest fintechs are prioritising rapid SME adoption of AI tools over near-term monetisation.
