# Payment Brief — 2026-08-24

### 1. **Walmart Begins Accepting Apple Pay and Google Pay Across US Stores**

* **Source**: The Paypers · 24 August 2026
* **Summary**: Walmart has begun introducing Apple Pay and Google Pay support through Tap to Pay across its US stores and Sam's Club. The move represents a major reversal for one of the largest US retailers, which had historically resisted supporting the dominant third-party mobile wallets.
* **Why It Matters**: Walmart's adoption materially expands contactless wallet acceptance in US retail and removes a significant friction point for consumers accustomed to tap-to-pay experiences.
