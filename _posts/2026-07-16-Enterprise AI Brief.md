### 1. Airbus Selects Scaleway for Sovereign Enterprise AI and Defense Workloads
- **Source**: Reuters · **2026-07-16**
- **Summary**: Airbus signed a multi-year agreement with French cloud provider Scaleway to host AI-powered engineering and defense applications.
