### 1. Trump Shares AI-Generated “Everybody Loves Trump” Video on Truth Social
- **News18** · 2026-06-06
- **Summary**: US President Donald Trump shared an AI-generated montage on Truth Social featuring himself in various fantastical scenarios (riding a camel, a lion, and a motorcycle in India) set to a self-praising soundtrack titled “Everybody Loves Trump.” The video quickly went viral, spreading across X, Instagram, and TikTok. The song was created by Anthony Constantino, a Trump-backed Republican candidate.
- **Why It Matters**: This represents a significant escalation in a world leader using generative AI to construct a “digital personality cult.” It blurs the line between political communication and propaganda, demonstrating how AI can be used to manufacture hyper-engaged, yet potentially misleading, narratives at scale.

### 2. “AI Slop” Creates Authenticity Crisis for African Media and Social Platforms
- **Panapress** · 2026-06-06
- **Summary**: Analysts warn that low-quality, automated “AI slop” is flooding African social media, burying authentic voices and eroding trust in digital content. In response to the decline in human editorial oversight, collectives in Lagos, Uganda, and Tanzania have launched verification badges for human-authored content. The African Union’s AI strategy is now at risk due to unreliable information infrastructure.
- **Why It Matters**: The crisis highlights a global shift in the fight against misinformation—from solely detecting fakes to economic incentives for human creation. As English-language markets build detectors, non-English and minority-language media face an existential threat from algorithmically generated content that is just “good enough” to be dangerous.
