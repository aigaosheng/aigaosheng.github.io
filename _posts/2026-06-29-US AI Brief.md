### 1. US Government Eases Restrictions on Anthropic's Mythos 5 Model
- **EBN News** · 2026-06-29
- **Summary**: The US Commerce Department has reauthorized limited access to Anthropic's high-performance AI model 'Mythos 5' for trusted partners, after imposing broad restrictions two weeks ago over national security and "jailbreak" concerns. The company is still negotiating with the government to restore general access to its public-facing model, 'Fable 5' .
- **Why It Matters**: This marks a significant shift toward a "trusted partner" model for regulating advanced AI . For Anthropic, which is preparing for an IPO, managing these regulatory risks has become a critical factor in its valuation .

### 3. Rep. Moran Introduces Bill for 7-Day AI Incident Reporting
- **Review of AI Law** · 2026-06-29
- **Summary**: Congressman Nathaniel Moran introduced the "AI Incident Reporting Act," requiring developers of advanced AI models to report safety incidents, security breaches, and dangerous capabilities to the Commerce Department within seven days. The bill mandates that the Secretary of Commerce notify Congress within 48 hours for the most severe incidents .
- **Why It Matters**: This bill represents a move toward formal, federal-level reporting requirements for AI safety. It would create a rapid-response framework for managing catastrophic AI risks, a first step toward making such risks public .
