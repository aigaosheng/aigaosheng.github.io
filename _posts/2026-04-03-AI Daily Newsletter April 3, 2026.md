## 🔥 Top Stories (Published ≤24 Hours)

### 1. **Microsoft Launches Three New Foundational AI Models**
Microsoft unveiled MAI-Transcribe-1, MAI-Voice-1, and MAI-Image-2 through Microsoft Foundry, positioning itself to compete directly with OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic. MAI-Transcribe-1 claims world-leading accuracy across 25 languages at $0.36/hour; MAI-Voice-1 generates 60 seconds of natural speech in 1 second; MAI-Image-2 ranks top-3 on Arena.ai leaderboard. [[53]](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/technology/tech-news/microsoft-launches-in-house-ai-models-as-it-takes-on-openai-google-and-anthropic/articleshow/129984512.cms)

OpenAI acquired the popular Silicon Valley livestream show TBPN for an undisclosed sum, signaling a strategic shift toward controlling its narrative amid growing public scrutiny. Hosts will retain editorial independence while reporting to OpenAI's VP of Global Affairs. [[58]](https://www.wired.com/story/openai-acquires-tbpn-buys-positive-news-coverage/)
