### 3. McKinsey Report Argues Workflow Redesign, Not AI Adoption, Delivers Financial Gains
- **Tribune India** · 2026-07-13
- **Summary**: A new report from QuantumBlack, AI by McKinsey, asserts that simply adding AI to existing processes yields only incremental gains . The report finds that the "real breakthrough comes from reinventing execution, not augmenting it" and that redesigning work around hybrid human-AI teams leads to substantially better outcomes .
- **Why It Matters**: This aligns with the emerging consensus that sustainable competitive advantage in AI comes from deep operational redesign, not just technology acquisition. The report's finding that over 80% of companies use AI in some function, but few see P&L impact, underscores the "last-mile" challenge of enterprise AI.
- **URL**: [AI delivers bigger financial gains only when companies redesign workflows: McKinsey](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/ai-delivers-bigger-financial-gains-only-when-companies-redesign-workflows-mckinsey/)

### 4. New Open Benchmark 'Enterprise-Bench' Aims to Create Global Standard for Evaluating AI Agents
- **UK Tech News** · 2026-07-13
- **Summary**: DevRev, in collaboration with the Laude Institute, has released Enterprise-Bench, an open benchmark to evaluate AI agents on organizational complexity, including fragmented data and permission boundaries . In its initial results, DevRev's "Computer" significantly outperformed "Claude Code" in accuracy and token efficiency when using the same underlying model .
- **Why It Matters**: The lack of standardized, neutral evaluation metrics is a major hurdle for enterprises. This initiative could help shift the conversation from vendor marketing to verifiable performance, offering a critical tool for procurement and strategy teams to make more informed decisions.
- **URL**: [New Open Benchmark Creates Global Standard for Evaluating Enterprise AI. DevRev Tops the Leaderboard.](https://uktechnews.co.uk/2026/07/13/new-open-benchmark-creates-global-standard-for-evaluating-enterprise-ai-devrev-tops-the-leaderboard/)
