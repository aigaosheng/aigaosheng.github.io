# Enterprise AI Brief — 2026-09-06

## Top Stories 

### 1. **Philippine Enterprises Reach 92% AI Adoption, but Governance Is Falling Behind**

* **Source**: BusinessMirror · September 6, 2026
* **Summary**: A nationwide survey of 175 Philippine organizations found that 92% have used AI in some capacity. The adoption is largely bottom-up, with employees experimenting with AI tools before formal enterprise policies and controls are established. The report highlights a growing gap between employee-level AI usage and organizational governance.
* **Why It Matters**: The Philippines illustrates a broader enterprise pattern: AI adoption can become widespread before IT, security, procurement, and governance frameworks catch up. Enterprises increasingly need sanctioned AI platforms, identity controls, data policies, and usage visibility rather than simply more AI access.

### 2. **Mambu Pushes AI Into the Core Banking Architecture**

* **Source**: TechCoffeeHouse · September 6, 2026
* **Summary**: Mambu's Intelligent Core architecture brings core banking, payments, and agentic AI together on a composable platform. The architecture includes an MCP-based connectivity layer, AI Insights, and pre-built agents capable of executing workflows within configurable guardrails. The approach is designed to let AI operate directly against trusted operational banking data rather than sit as a separate assistant layer.
* **Why It Matters**: Enterprise AI is moving from conversational interfaces toward systems that can act on transactional infrastructure. In regulated industries, the competitive advantage will increasingly depend on combining real-time data, workflow permissions, auditability, and agent execution in one architecture.
* **URL**: [https://techcoffeehouse.com/2026/09/06/mambu-launches-intelligent-core-to-unite ai-and-banking/](https://techcoffeehouse.com/2026/09/06/mambu-launches-intelligent-core-to-unite ai-and-banking/)

### 3. **F5 and MuleSoft Add Runtime Security to Agentic AI Workflows**

* **Source**: TechCoffeeHouse · September 6, 2026
* **Summary**: F5 and MuleSoft announced an integration that brings F5 AI Guardrails into MuleSoft Agent Fabric. The integration provides inline controls for prompt injection, jailbreaks, harmful outputs, and sensitive-data exposure across agentic workflows. The companies position the technology as a unified security and governance layer for both Salesforce-powered agents and custom enterprise applications.
* **Why It Matters**: Agent deployment is creating a new security boundary: organizations must secure not only models and applications, but also the traffic and actions generated by autonomous agents. Runtime guardrails and centralized policy enforcement are becoming core components of enterprise AI infrastructure.

### 4. **ERP Vendors Move AI Agents From Copilots Toward Operational Execution**

* **Source**: ERP News · September 6, 2026
* **Summary**: Rootstock's latest ERP developments highlight the transition from AI assistants that retrieve information to agents participating directly in manufacturing workflows. Its Sales and Purchasing agents are being piloted for delivery commitments, lot traceability, purchasing delays, and alternative sourcing. The agents connect AI reasoning to live ERP data spanning inventory, orders, supply availability, and production.
* **Why It Matters**: This is a critical architectural shift for enterprise AI: value increasingly comes from connecting agents to systems of record and allowing them to influence operational decisions. ERP platforms with native AI access may have an advantage over standalone AI applications that require extensive integration work.
* **URL**: [https://erpnews.com/rootstock-pushes ai-agents-into-production-workflows-with-summer-26-erp-release/](https://erpnews.com/rootstock-pushes ai-agents-into-production-workflows-with-summer-26-erp-release/)
