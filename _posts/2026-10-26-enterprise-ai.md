# Enterprise AI Brief: Backbase Launches AI Agents for Bank Software Delivery

**Today:** If you build, buy or govern AI agents in banking or IT services, today's stories show agents moving into regulated delivery work while the money behind AI shifts to capital markets.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **Backbase Launches Delivery Factory, Putting Role-Based AI Agents Into Bank Software Projects**
*FinTech Global · October 26, 2026*

**Bottom line:** A major core banking platform vendor now ships its own specialist agents for requirements, architecture, coding, migration and upgrades, with humans approving each step.

Amsterdam-based Backbase launched Delivery Factory, which assigns dedicated agents to roles such as business analyst, solution architect and backend, mobile and web development. It also adds a Migration Agent and an Upgrade Agent for its Banking OS. Backbase says that across more than 10 implementations over the past year, teams worked twice as fast as conventional teams and 30% faster than teams using general-purpose coding assistants. The publisher notes these figures were supplied by the company. Engineers carry out approved plans in the bank's own repositories and pipelines, and delivery leads keep control of scope and decisions.

**Why it matters:** Domain-specific agents tied to a vendor's platform are starting to compete with generic coding copilots inside banks. The plan-approval checkpoints also fit more naturally with the change-management controls regulated institutions already run.

### 2. 📊 **Meta Launches About $35B in Stock Offerings Within an Up-to-$95B AI Funding Plan**
*Quartz · October 26, 2026*

**Bottom line:** One of the largest AI spenders is now funding its buildout through equity markets, not just cash flow and debt.

Meta launched concurrent offerings on Monday: about $22.5B of Class A common stock and about $12.5B of depositary shares tied to mandatory convertible preferred stock. It also plans an at-the-market program of about $60B once its new non-voting Class C shares begin trading. Meta says that program is not expected before the first quarter of 2027, and no agreement has been signed.

**Why it matters:** Hyperscale AI capex at this size supports continued compute supply and price competition for enterprise buyers. It also means vendor balance sheets and investor appetite now shape the AI roadmap, which belongs in vendor-risk and outsourcing assessments.

### 3. 📊 **AI Revenue Grows at TCS and Infosys While Productivity Gains Squeeze Traditional Work**
*Nikkei Asia · October 26, 2026*

**Bottom line:** AI is both lifting and eroding revenue at India's largest IT services firms.

TCS's annualized AI revenue grew more than 19% in the July–September quarter, and Infosys's AI revenue rose 29% quarter on quarter. Analysts quoted by Nikkei say deploying AI across legacy systems is producing higher-margin contracts. At the same time, AI-driven productivity is shrinking revenue in traditional service lines.

**Why it matters:** Outsourcing contracts priced on headcount are under pressure. Asian banks and fintechs that rely on Indian IT partners have room to negotiate for productivity gains to be passed through, or for outcome-based pricing.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://asia.nikkei.com/business/technology/artificial-intelligence/for-india-s-it-giants-tcs-and-infosys-ai-both-dents-and-supports-growth)

### 4. 🤖 **Indian Bank Chiefs Urge Moving AI From Pilots to Measurable Outcomes, With Accountability Kept In-House**
*Business Standard · October 26, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Senior Indian bankers say the next phase of AI is about production results, and that responsibility for outcomes cannot be handed to the machines.

At the Business Standard BFSI Insight Summit in Mumbai on Monday, chiefs from IndusInd Bank, RBL Bank and IDFC First Bank discussed using AI in lending, productivity and customer experience. They stressed accountability, data security and governance. One speaker said AI adoption is not a race and that banks will move at different speeds. Another said the bank stays accountable for outcomes whatever operating model it uses.

**Why it matters:** This is the governance stance regional fintechs should design for. Every production agent needs a named human owner, explainable decisions and an audit trail before it scales.

### 5. 🤖 **Atos Launches Genesis Intelligence Suite With Model Routing, an SLM Factory and an Agent Marketplace**
*GlobeNewswire (Atos) · October 26, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Atos is pitching sovereignty and cost control as the way to move enterprise AI from pilots to production.

The Paris-based group's new suite combines data management, model development and deployment, and orchestration in one environment, and includes Atos Codex. It offers model routing and cost optimization, built-in governance and compliance controls, and a Small Language Model Factory for building locally deployable models. It also includes an enterprise portal with a marketplace of customizable generative AI agents.

**Why it matters:** Small, locally hosted models and in-country control are becoming standard requirements for firms bound by data-residency and outsourcing rules. That applies across Southeast Asia's regulated financial sector.

### 6. 🤖 **Interact Opens 700 Intranet Capabilities to Enterprise AI Assistants via MCP**
*GlobeNewswire (Interact Software) · October 26, 2026*

**Bottom line:** MCP keeps spreading as the default way to plug workplace systems into enterprise agents, with existing permissions carried over.

Interact MCP connects compatible enterprise AI assistants and agents to 700 intranet capabilities across 26 areas, built on the company's Cue and Conductor products. The connection works in both directions, and the company says it respects existing permissions and governance.

**Why it matters:** As internal knowledge and actions become reachable by agents, inheriting existing permissions becomes the key control. Firms should check that agent access never exceeds the human user's own rights.

## Key Takeaways
- Vendors are shifting from general-purpose copilots to domain-specific agents with human approval steps built into the workflow, especially in banking.
- AI economics are being rebuilt on both sides: hyperscalers are turning to equity markets for capex, and IT services firms are trading headcount revenue for higher-margin AI work.
- Governance messages from Asian bank leaders and vendors converge on the same points: accountability stays with the institution, permissions are inherited and data stays local.

## What to Watch
Watch how the market absorbs Meta's $35B offering this week. Also watch whether other core banking and IT services vendors answer Backbase with their own role-based delivery agents and independently verified productivity figures.
