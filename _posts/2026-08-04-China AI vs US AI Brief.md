# Strategic Outlook

China’s AI strategy is increasingly centered on **open-weight models, cost efficiency, and rapid ecosystem expansion**, while the US continues to lead in many areas of frontier model development, advanced chips, and commercial AI platforms.

The competitive landscape is shifting from a simple “best model wins” race toward a broader contest involving:

* AI infrastructure independence
* Open-source adoption
* Developer ecosystems
* Enterprise deployment costs
* Semiconductor supply chains
* Global market influence

The next phase of the China-US AI rivalry will likely be determined by which ecosystem can convert technical capability into widespread real-world adoption.
