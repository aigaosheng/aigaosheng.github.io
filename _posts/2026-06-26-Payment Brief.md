### 1. **Zambia Completes Cheque Phase-Out, Ending Traditional Paper Payment Instrument**
- **Bank of Zambia** · 2026-06-26
- **Summary**: June 26, 2026 marks the final day for cheques to be used as a payment instrument in Zambia, completing the central bank's multi-year phase-out program . The last day for depositing cheques at commercial banks was June 24, 2026, with interbank cheque clearing officially ending today . The Bank of Zambia has been encouraging individuals and businesses to transition to faster, safer, and more convenient digital payment alternatives throughout the transition period .
- **Why It Matters**: Zambia becomes one of the latest countries to eliminate paper cheques entirely, accelerating the shift toward real-time digital payments in emerging markets. This milestone demonstrates how regulatory mandates can successfully drive payment modernization and reduce reliance on legacy payment infrastructure.

### 2. **Bluefin and LANDI Global Partner on Direct-to-Processor P2PE Security Solution**
- **Global Fintech Series** · 2026-06-25
- **Summary**: Bluefin Payment Systems announced a strategic partnership with LANDI Global to deliver a direct-to-processor Point-to-Point Encryption (P2PE) solution for smart payment terminals . The joint solution combines LANDI's Android-based smart terminals with Bluefin's PointConex platform, enabling PCI-validated P2PE without requiring merchants, ISVs, gateways, or processors to redesign existing payment flows . The no-code proxy infrastructure layer secures sensitive cardholder data in transit while preserving existing processor message formats and certifications.
- **Why It Matters**: This partnership addresses growing security challenges in modern payment environments by embedding encryption at the point of interaction without disrupting existing payment ecosystems. The solution reduces PCI DSS scope and accelerates deployment timelines for organizations adopting smart terminal technology.
- **URL**: [Read more](https://globalfintechseries.com/banking/digital-payments/bluefin-and-landi-global-partner-to-deliver-direct-to-processor-p2pe-for-smart-payment-terminals/)

### 3. **Nacha Deadline for Return Efficiencies Comment Period Closes June 26**
- **Nacha** · 2026-06-26
- **Summary**: Today marks the deadline for industry participants to submit comments on Nacha's Request for Comment regarding Return Efficiencies and Return Time Frames . The comment period is part of Nacha's ongoing efforts to improve ACH network performance and efficiency . Responses to this Request for Information are critical for shaping future ACH operating rules related to return processing.
- **Why It Matters**: Changes to ACH return efficiencies could significantly impact payment processing times, fraud detection capabilities, and operational costs for financial institutions and payment processors. Industry input on this RFC will influence the next generation of ACH network rules.
