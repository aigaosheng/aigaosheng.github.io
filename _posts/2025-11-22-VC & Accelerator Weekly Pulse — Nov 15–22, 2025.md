## **Market Commentary — Trends & Implications**

* **AI Infrastructure Dominance**: SoftBank’s multi-billion capex underscores the race for physical compute capacity — not just software.
* **Medical Intelligence Breakout**: Function’s Series B and launch of its Medical Intelligence Lab show increasing investor conviction in proactive, data-driven healthcare.
* **Key Risks**: Regulatory hurdles, data integration, and payer adoption (Function); capex concentration and long timelines (SoftBank).
* **Actionable Insights**:

  1. For health-AI investors: prioritize startups with deep clinical/data-platform capability and define clear payer / patient value metrics.
  2. For infra investors: push for milestone-based investment and offtake agreements to de-risk large capex plays.
  3. For portfolio strategy: allocate across the AI stack — from hardware (infra) to vertical AI (health, fintech).
