**Source:** Reuters | **Published:** May 4, 2026
**Summary:** India’s securities regulator is preparing guidance on risks associated with AI in financial markets, including misuse, market manipulation, and systemic vulnerabilities. The advisory will set expectations for firms deploying AI in trading and operational processes.
**Why It Matters:** Signals **proactive regulatory intervention in AI-driven finance**, with likely global implications for compliance frameworks in capital markets.
