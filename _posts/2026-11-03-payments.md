# Payments Brief: Standard Chartered Launches Tokenised Deposits In Hong Kong

**Today:** Hong Kong FinTech Week produced a cluster of concrete payments moves - from bank-grade tokenised deposits to agentic-commerce pilots - that show infrastructure upgrades moving from pilot to production faster than expected.

## Top Stories

### 1. 💳 **Standard Chartered Launches Tokenised Deposits for Corporate Clients in Hong Kong**

*Blockhead · November 3, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Standard Chartered became the first market in its group to offer corporate clients a tokenised deposits product, plugging directly into the HKMA's EnsembleTX settlement platform.

The solution lets corporate treasurers, banks, investment platforms and digital asset businesses transfer tokenised deposits across participating banks with interbank settlement in central bank money, 24/7. It launches as the HKMA expands EnsembleTX to 12 participating banks - including HSBC, Bank of China (Hong Kong), China Construction Bank (Asia) and ICBC (Asia) - plus pioneers such as Ant International and BlackRock, with China AMC, Finloop and PantherTrade already running live transactions.

**Why it matters:** This is one of the first commercial-grade bank products built on a central bank-backed tokenised settlement rail, signalling that 24/7 interbank settlement is moving past proof-of-concept. For Singapore and other Asian financial centres, it raises the competitive bar on tokenised money infrastructure and treasury efficiency.

### 2. 🌐 **XTransfer and Bank of China (Hong Kong) Partner on Cross-Border Payments**

*Media OutReach Newswire (via Manila Times) · November 3, 2026*

**Bottom line:** XTransfer became Bank of China (Hong Kong)'s first corporate partner under a new cooperation model covering global collections, payments and foreign exchange for trade businesses.

The deal combines XTransfer's B2B cross-border settlement network, which serves more than one million business customers, with BOCHK's RMB clearing and cross-border banking expertise, aiming to cut intermediary steps for SMEs moving money across multiple currencies and markets.

**Why it matters:** Bank-fintech tie-ups like this extend cheaper, faster trade settlement to SMEs that have historically been underserved by correspondent banking - a model relevant to trade corridors linking China, Hong Kong and Southeast Asia, including Singapore.

### 3. 🤖 **EX.IO Lab Demonstrates Agentic Payment Pathway at Hong Kong FinTech Week**

*PR Newswire · November 3, 2026*

**Bottom line:** EX.IO Lab, the innovation arm of SFC-licensed virtual asset platform EX.IO, showed a sandbox proof of concept linking AI-driven bank transfers directly to wealth management investments.

The demonstration moved funds from a bank account into real-world-asset investment products through a single AI agent workflow, inside a bank's sandbox environment, with user authorisation, defined risk limits and compliance controls built in. The company stressed this is a sandbox trial, not a commercial launch or regulatory approval.

**Why it matters:** It is an early, concrete illustration of agentic commerce collapsing banking and investment steps into one automated flow - a direction regulators including the HKMA are now actively trying to govern through parallel initiatives on agent identity and authorisation.

### 4. 💳 **Alchemy Pay Brings Fiat-to-Crypto Payments to Telegram's Walt App**

*crypto.news · November 3, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Alchemy Pay integrated on- and off-ramp payment services into Telegram's Walt wallet, letting users in 173 countries buy and sell crypto without leaving the app.

The integration supports more than 300 payment channels and over 50 fiat currencies, including card and local bank transfer rails, and covers 14 tradable assets within Walt, formerly branded Wallet in Telegram until September 2026.

**Why it matters:** Embedding fiat on-ramps inside a chat app with a vast user base lowers the friction for mainstream crypto and stablecoin adoption, a trend payments providers across Asia will need to track as super-app-style wallets compete for the same transaction flow.

### 5. 📊 **Stablecoin Payments Firm RedotPay Deepens Hong Kong Investment**

*Media OutReach Newswire (via Thailand Business News) · November 3, 2026*

**Bottom line:** RedotPay, a stablecoin-based payments platform serving more than nine million users across 100-plus countries, said it will expand hiring and infrastructure in Hong Kong under the government's OASES strategic enterprise programme.

The firm has added more than 125 staff since 2025, expanded its Central office and opened an R&D centre in the Hong Kong-Shenzhen Innovation and Technology Park, after joining the OASES cohort in April 2026.

**Why it matters:** The expansion adds to evidence that Hong Kong's stablecoin-friendly licensing push is pulling in payments infrastructure players and jobs, reinforcing its bid to compete with Singapore as a regional digital-asset payments hub.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.thailand-business-news.com/pr-news/redotpay-deepens-hong-kong-investment-as-oases-strategic-enterprise-partner/)

## Key Takeaways

- Tokenised deposits are shifting from regulatory pilots to bank-branded commercial products, with Standard Chartered's launch the clearest signal yet that 24/7 interbank settlement is becoming a sellable treasury feature.
- Hong Kong is using Hong Kong FinTech Week to stack payments, stablecoin and agentic-commerce announcements in a single week, intensifying competition with Singapore for regional fintech infrastructure and talent.
- Agentic commerce is moving from framework talk to working sandbox demonstrations, with banks, exchanges and regulators now racing in parallel to define how AI agents get authorised to move money.

## What to Watch

Watch whether other EnsembleTX participant banks follow Standard Chartered with their own tokenised deposit products, and whether MAS responds with comparable commercial pilots ahead of the Singapore FinTech Festival sessions on stablecoins and RWAs scheduled for November 18-19.
