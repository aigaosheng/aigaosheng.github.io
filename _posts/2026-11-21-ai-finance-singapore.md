# AI Finance in Singapore Brief: MAS Advances Know Your Agent Protocol

Today: Singapore is moving beyond AI experimentation in financial services towards trusted agentic transactions, with MAS developing common infrastructure for agent identity and authority while banks focus on measurable productivity and human oversight.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 Huawei Unveils Enterprise Financial AI Scaling Platform

Huawei · November 19, 2026

Bottom line: Huawei is positioning enterprise-grade AI infrastructure and reusable agent capabilities as the next competitive foundation for financial institutions.

At the Singapore FinTech Festival, Huawei presented its Fintelligent AI Solution, built around three components: an Agent Factory, a Token Factory and a Data-Knowledge Factory. The architecture combines hybrid infrastructure, standardised agent engineering and reusable enterprise knowledge to support production-scale financial AI.

Huawei also outlined a model involving customer-facing and employee-facing super-agents coordinating specialised agents across credit, marketing, risk management and wealth management.

Why it matters: Financial institutions increasingly need more than access to foundation models. Their competitive advantage will depend on integrating proprietary data, domain knowledge, business workflows and governance into reusable AI capabilities. This architecture is particularly relevant to banks seeking to scale beyond isolated generative AI pilots.

### 2. 🤖 Singapore Banks Report AI Productivity Gains, Retain Human Oversight

CNA · November 20, 2026

Bottom line: DBS, OCBC and UOB see AI improving productivity and supporting business growth, but maintain human oversight for consequential financial decisions.

Senior executives from the three banks discussed AI adoption at the Singapore FinTech Festival. Their perspectives indicate that AI is delivering practical operational benefits while financial institutions continue exploring more autonomous systems.

The discussion highlights the distinction between deploying AI to assist employees and allowing AI systems to make or execute decisions independently.

Why it matters: Banks need to measure AI adoption through business outcomes rather than model capabilities alone. Human accountability, escalation procedures and decision controls remain important as agentic AI enters customer-facing and financial workflows.

### 3. 🏦 MAS Warns AI Agents Could Introduce Financial Stability Risks

TNGlobal · November 20, 2026

Bottom line: MAS is highlighting financial stability risks as AI agents gain the ability to initiate payments, allocate funds and make financial decisions.

Managing Director Chia Der Jiun warned that autonomous agents could accelerate money movements and potentially require financial institutions to impose transaction limits. Key concerns include verifying customer authorisation, enforcing agent mandates and determining accountability when agents execute incorrect transactions.

MAS is advancing its Safeguards for Agentic Finance at Runtime (SAFR) programme, including a proposed Know Your Agent (KYA) protocol covering agent identity, representation, authority, mandate and provenance. An Agentic Money Sandbox will also explore AI agents interacting with programmable money in corporate treasury and payment services.

Why it matters: Agentic finance introduces a new control layer between customers and financial institutions. Banks will need to develop agent authentication, permission management, transaction monitoring and auditability rather than relying solely on conventional customer authentication.

For fintech developers, KYA could become an important interoperability requirement when building financial agents that interact with multiple institutions.

## Key Takeaways

- Financial AI is moving towards production infrastructure: Reusable agents, enterprise knowledge and hybrid deployment architectures are becoming central to scaling adoption.
- Trust is becoming a prerequisite for agentic finance: Identity, authority, accountability and transaction controls must be established before agents can safely handle financial activities.
- Human oversight remains commercially and operationally important: Singapore banks are pursuing productivity gains without abandoning accountability for consequential decisions.

## What to Watch

Watch for MAS and the Future of Finance Institute's next steps on the KYA protocol and Agentic Money Sandbox, particularly the emergence of common agent identity standards, transaction authorisation mechanisms and participating financial institutions. The development of practical bank-level agent controls will be an important indicator of how quickly agentic finance can move from experimentation into production.
