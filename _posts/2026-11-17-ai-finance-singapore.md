# AI Finance in Singapore Brief: ANEXT Bank Brings AI-Scored Loans to Lazada Sellers

**Today:** On the eve of the Singapore FinTech Festival, the one verified AI-finance development is a Singapore digital bank putting AI-driven SME credit decisions inside a major e-commerce platform.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **ANEXT Bank Offers AI-Assessed Working Capital Loans to Lazada Sellers in Singapore**

*Fintech News Singapore · November 17, 2026*

**Bottom line:** ANEXT Bank has built its CreditNow lending service into Lazada's seller dashboard, using live transaction data and AI-driven credit assessment to offer unsecured loans of S$5,000 to S$300,000.

Eligible Lazada sellers in Singapore can now apply for unsecured working capital financing directly from their seller dashboard. They need no extra documents, and they verify their identity and sign online with Singpass and facial verification. ANEXT, a digital wholesale bank regulated by MAS, says the credit decision draws on sellers' real-time Lazada transaction data and that application time has fallen from about 30 minutes to about one minute. Its CEO, Qiu Kai, credits Ant International's financial AI technology for this. CreditNow first launched on EPOS in the first quarter of 2026 and, according to the bank, now reaches more than 6,000 Singapore merchants.

**Why it matters:** The deal shows that AI underwriting on platform data is becoming a practical way to reach SMEs that traditional banks underserve, and Singapore's digital wholesale banks are competing on how deeply they can embed into merchant platforms. If the model holds up through a full credit cycle, it could be repeated across Southeast Asian e-commerce, where Ant-linked lenders already have reach.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://fintechnews.sg/141354/digital-banking-news-singapore/anext-bank-lazada-loans/)

## Key Takeaways

- In Singapore SME finance, AI credit scoring is moving from standalone apps into the platforms where merchants already work, and lenders that get platform data access gain a distribution advantage.
- The one-minute application time and the 6,000-merchant figure are ANEXT's own claims. Default rates and loss performance have not been published.
- Singpass-based digital identity is still a core part of Singapore's embedded lending stack, letting lenders onboard without documents alongside automated credit decisions.

The Singapore FinTech Festival runs from 18 to 20 November at Singapore EXPO, with agentic AI and AI-enabled payments on the MAS agenda. Watch for bank and regulator announcements there, and for any first outputs from MAS's Future of Finance Institute, which began operations this month with a focus on AI in finance.
