### 3. Drata Expands Platform to Govern Enterprise AI Agents
- **Source** · IT Brief Australia  · 2026-06-13
- **Summary**: Drata has expanded its trust management platform to cover governance of enterprise AI agents, now in early access for financial services, healthcare, and software customers. The expansion follows a 30% rise in AI-specific questions during security reviews, with 89% of vendors leaving these queries unanswered. Drata's system creates an inventory of agents, maps each to an owner and permissions, monitors actions against policy rules in real time, and records decisions in a tamper-evident audit trail.
- **Why It Matters**: As AI agents gain autonomy, governance has become the primary bottleneck to deployment. Drata is positioning itself as the security layer for the AI agent wave, analogous to what CrowdStrike did for endpoints and Wiz for cloud. The product addresses a critical enterprise need: proving control over AI systems to boards, auditors, and regulators.

### 4. ChatSee.ai Raises $6.5M to Address AI Agent Failures in Production
- **Source** · TMCnet  · 2026-06-12

### 5. AI Price War Intensifies, Piling Pressure on OpenAI and Anthropic
- **Source** · Mint Mumbai  · 2026-06-13
- **Summary**: A price war is emerging in the enterprise AI market as companies increasingly use routing tools that dynamically switch between cheaper AI models—including those from Chinese companies like Alibaba and DeepSeek—reserving premium models only for complex tasks. These cost-saving tools can reduce AI-assisted work costs by as much as 95%, according to executives. OpenAI is reportedly considering drastic price cuts ahead of similar expected cuts from Anthropic, while Citadel Securities notes a recent decline in a widely followed AI spending index.
- **Why It Matters**: Price pressure threatens the business models of premium AI providers who have spent billions on model development. If enterprises can achieve acceptable results with cheaper or open-source models for most use cases, the value capture in the AI stack may shift from model providers to optimization and routing layers. This could reshape the economics of the entire enterprise AI industry.
