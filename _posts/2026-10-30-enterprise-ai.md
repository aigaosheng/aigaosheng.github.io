# Enterprise AI Brief: Amazon's AgentCore Deployments Double In Q3

**Today:** Fresh Q3 earnings and vendor moves show agentic AI shifting from pilots to production at scale, with Amazon, Cognizant and Tencent all reporting concrete adoption numbers rather than roadmaps.

## Top Stories

### 1. 📊 **Amazon's Bedrock AgentCore Usage Doubles as AWS Posts 39% Growth**

*PYMNTS · October 29, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Amazon's Q3 earnings showed enterprise AI agents moving decisively into production, with AgentCore deployments doubling quarter-on-quarter even as AI capital spending pushed free cash flow negative.

Amazon reported AWS sales up 39% to $46 billion, and said tens of thousands of customers now use Bedrock AgentCore, deploying twice as many agents in Q3 as in the prior three quarters combined. CEO Andy Jassy said enterprises increasingly want multi-model access with strong security controls, positioning AWS as the backbone for production-grade agents at companies including 3M and Southwest Airlines. The buildout came at a cost: trailing free cash flow swung from a $14.8 billion inflow to a $42.4 billion outflow, driven largely by roughly $220 billion in AI capex this year.

**Why it matters:** The scale of AgentCore adoption signals that large enterprises are past the pilot stage on agentic AI, a trend Asian banks and telcos evaluating multi-model agent infrastructure will watch closely when choosing cloud and governance partners.

### 2. 🤖 **Cognizant Commits 25,000 Staff to OpenAI Delivery Unit**

*PR Newswire · October 30, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Cognizant has formed a dedicated OpenAI Frontier Capability Group of more than 25,000 professionals to help enterprise clients move Codex and ChatGPT into production systems.

The unit focuses on three areas: AI-native software engineering, legacy application modernization, and agentic customer operations. Cognizant said more than 21,000 of its associates have already earned over 40,000 OpenAI learning badges, and it plans to scale to 5,000 Frontier Certified Engineers and 10,000 Frontier Business Operators. The company cited an internal deployment at a biopharmaceutical client where an OpenAI-powered assistant handled more than 5 million conversations and cut content creation time by 60%.

**Why it matters:** The scale of dedicated, certified delivery headcount signals that systems integrators see enterprise agent rollout as a staffing and training problem as much as a technology one, a model Asia-based IT services firms competing for the same transformation budgets will likely mirror.

### 3. 💳 **Tencent Launches WorkBuddy Enterprise Agent, Teases Hy4 Model**

*Caixin Global · October 30, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Tencent unveiled Buddy One, an integrated enterprise workspace built around its WorkBuddy AI agent, as it races Alibaba and ByteDance to turn standalone chatbots into agents that complete multi-step office tasks.

WorkBuddy operates across Tencent's Docs, Meeting and Cloud Drive products through a single account, aiming to resolve the data-retrieval friction of switching between separate tools. Tencent Cloud executive Dowson Tong said enterprise agents consume substantially more AI tokens than consumer chatbots such as Yuanbao, and previewed upcoming updates to its Hy4 model family. Tencent shares closed down 2.6% on the announcement day.

**Why it matters:** The shift from chatbot to integrated workflow agent mirrors what AWS, Cognizant and Salesforce are each pushing in their own ecosystems, underscoring that the agent interface layer, not just the model, is becoming the next competitive battleground across US and China enterprise software stacks.

### 4. 🌐 **SambaNova and Emergys Partner on Enterprise Agentic AI Rollouts**

*Business Wire · October 29, 2026*

**Bottom line:** SambaNova and IT services firm Emergys have formed a partnership aimed at moving agentic AI from pilot to production for regulated and sovereign enterprise customers.

The deal pairs SambaNova's inference platform, built on its Reconfigurable Dataflow Unit chips, with Emergys' track record deploying and operating business-critical systems for large enterprises. The companies pointed to a prior Emergys deployment of a sovereign AI platform for a major energy company that extended AI assistants into job safety analysis, finance, audit and engineering functions, reporting a 40% cost reduction. The partners said they will jointly target enterprise, regulated and sovereign AI opportunities globally.

**Why it matters:** Sovereign and regulated-sector AI deployment is a live concern for MAS-supervised financial institutions in Singapore and the region, making partnerships that pair chip-level inference with production operations support directly relevant to compliance-conscious AI procurement.

## Key Takeaways

- Vendors are now reporting hard adoption numbers for agentic AI — doubled deployments, tens of thousands of customers, 25,000 dedicated staff — rather than pilot anecdotes, suggesting 2026 is the year production rollout claims face real scrutiny.
- Systems integrators and cloud providers alike are racing to own the "last mile" of agent deployment: governance, staffing and operations, not just the underlying model.
- Heavy AI capital spending is now visibly straining free cash flow at major cloud providers, a dynamic enterprise buyers should factor into vendor pricing and roadmap stability expectations.

## What to Watch

Watch for Q3/Q4 earnings commentary from Microsoft, Google Cloud and Oracle on agent-specific usage metrics, and for MAS supervisory guidance on agentic AI in financial services as sovereign and regulated AI partnerships like SambaNova-Emergys multiply across Asia.
