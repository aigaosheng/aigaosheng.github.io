### 1. Snowflake and Anthropic expand their enterprise AI partnership
- **Snowflake** · 2026-06-01
- **Summary**: Snowflake said enterprise adoption of Anthropic’s Claude models inside Snowflake Cortex AI is accelerating, with joint customers including Basis, Block, Carvana, eSentire, Indeed, and Notion. The announcement emphasizes that Claude is being used directly on governed enterprise data inside Snowflake, and that the companies are extending co-development across Cortex Code, Snowflake Intelligence, Claude Marketplace, and security-focused workflows.
- **Why It Matters**: This is a strong signal that enterprise AI buying is shifting from pilot projects to governed production deployments. The story also reinforces that data-platform vendors and model providers are competing on integrated control, security, and workflow depth—not just model quality.
