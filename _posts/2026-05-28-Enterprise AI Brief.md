### 1. Snowflake Acquires Natoma to Solve AI Agent Governance Crisis
- **InfoWorld** · 2026-05-28
- **Summary**: Snowflake announced plans to acquire Natoma, a startup specializing in governance, security, and connectivity for AI agents via the Model Context Protocol (MCP). Natoma's platform provides MCP-based tool access with identity controls and auditability, which will be integrated into Snowflake to help enterprises securely connect Cortex Agents to SaaS, cloud, and on-prem systems.
- **Why It Matters**: As enterprises move agentic AI from pilots to production, MCP is becoming the "connective tissue" for agents. However, analysts warn that without governance, MCP creates shadow AI risk. Snowflake is positioning itself as the control plane for the agentic era, addressing CIOs' critical need to govern what agents can see, touch, and trigger.
- **URL**: [Snowflake to acquire MCP-focused Natoma to boost governance for AI agents](https://www.infoworld.com/article/4178165/snowflake-to-acquire-mcp-focused-natoma-to-boost-governance-for-ai-agents-2.html)
