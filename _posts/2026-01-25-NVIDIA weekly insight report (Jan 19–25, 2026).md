# **NVIDIA weekly insight report (Jan 19–25, 2026)**

---

### **1) GeForce NOW: Flight Controls Support & New Games**

**Executive Summary:**
On January **22, 2026**, NVIDIA’s official GeForce NOW community blog announced **flight control peripheral support** for cloud gaming alongside the addition of **four new game titles**. This improves accessibility for simulation and flight genres on cloud platforms. ([NVIDIA Blog][1])

**In‑Depth Analysis:**

* **Strategic Context:** NVIDIA continues to expand its cloud gaming ecosystem, reinforcing GeForce NOW as a leading platform for high‑performance streaming with broader hardware compatibility.
* **Market Impact:** By adding flight control support, NVIDIA positions Geforce NOW to capture niche enthusiast segments and differentiate from competitors focusing primarily on game libraries without peripheral integration.
* **Tech Angle:** Peripheral support in cloud gaming is non‑trivial technically; low‑latency mappings for specialized hardware signal maturity in NVIDIA’s streaming stack, likely leveraging adaptive input buffering and synchronization with RTX‑backed cloud instances.

**Source:** NVIDIA GeForce NOW Community Blog — *Flight Controls Are Cleared for Takeoff on GeForce NOW* ([NVIDIA Blog][1])

### **2) NVIDIA & Eli Lilly: AI Co‑Innovation Lab for Drug Discovery**

**Executive Summary:**
On **January 12, 2026**, NVIDIA and Eli Lilly announced the creation of a **co‑innovation AI lab** targeting breakthroughs in drug discovery, with **up to $1 billion** committed over five years. Built on NVIDIA’s BioNeMo platform and Vera Rubin infrastructure, this joint lab aims to transform medicine development via accelerated simulation, modelling, and robotics. ([NVIDIA Investor Relations][2])

**In‑Depth Analysis:**

* **Strategic Context:** This initiative exemplifies NVIDIA’s pivot beyond traditional datacenter compute into **domain‑specific AI solutions**. Life sciences represent a high‑growth use case for next‑gen AI compute frameworks.
* **Market Impact:** By aligning with a major pharmaceutical leader, NVIDIA strengthens its narrative as a platform provider enabling **industry‑wide AI adoption** rather than just hardware sales. This may also influence enterprise buying decisions for AI infrastructure.
* **Tech Angle:** Leveraging NVIDIA BioNeMo alongside the expected Rubin architecture combines **foundation models** with high‑performance computing, potentially lowering the time and cost of in silico drug discovery—an advantage both to researchers and NVIDIA’s ecosystem partners.

**Source:** NVIDIA Press Release — *NVIDIA and Lilly Announce Co‑Innovation AI Lab* ([NVIDIA Investor Relations][2])
