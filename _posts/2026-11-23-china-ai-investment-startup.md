# AI Investment & Startup in China Brief: Ant Leads Simplexity’s $100 Million Round

**Today:** Fresh funding for Simplexity and a renewed Hong Kong offering by Changzhou Microintelligence show investors backing embodied AI while public-market terms face scrutiny.

## Top Stories

### 1. 📊 **Ant Leads Simplexity’s $100 Million Round**

*Bloomberg · November 22, 2026 (11:00 PM UTC)*

**Bottom line:** Ant Group led a $100 million pre-Series A round valuing Chinese physical AI startup Simplexity Robotics at $1.6 billion.

Simplexity co-founder Wang Kai told Bloomberg that Alibaba, Tencent, HongShan Capital and Lanchi Ventures also participated. The round gives the startup backing from several major Chinese technology investors as it competes in robotics.

**Why it matters:** The investor lineup points to continued strategic interest in embodied AI, even as China’s robotics market becomes more crowded. For Asian businesses assessing the sector, the next test is whether these investments translate into commercially useful deployments.

### 2. 📊 **Microintelligence Reopens Hong Kong IPO**

*Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing · November 23, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Changzhou Microintelligence’s prospectus sets a new Hong Kong offering of up to HK$584.9 million for its embodied intelligent industrial robotics business.

The offering covers about 37.3 million H shares at HK$12.07 to HK$15.69 each, with a provision allowing the price to be lowered to HK$10.87. The company plans to allocate 60.3% of net proceeds, or HK$273 million, to research and development of its industrial robot technologies.

**Why it matters:** The prospectus gives investors a concrete view of how one Chinese industrial AI company plans to fund product development through public markets. Its subscription demand and final pricing will offer a near-term signal of investor appetite for embodied AI businesses.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/1123/2026112300007.pdf)

## Key Takeaways

- Strategic investment and public-market fundraising are both supporting China’s embodied AI companies.
- Simplexity’s round brings major technology investors together behind one robotics startup.
- Microintelligence’s offering ties its fundraising plan directly to industrial robot R&D.

## What to Watch

Microintelligence’s subscription period closes December 1, with its offer price expected by December 2; Simplexity has not disclosed how it will use its new funding or detailed deployment milestones.
