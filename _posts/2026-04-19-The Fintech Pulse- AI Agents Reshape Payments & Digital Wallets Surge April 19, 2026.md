## **2. In‑Depth Highlight: Agentic AI Takes Center Stage in Fintech**

The fintech sector is witnessing a pivotal shift as "agentic AI" moves from pilot projects to production-grade financial infrastructure. This week, Slash Financial's $100M Series C and launch of its "Twin" AI agent exemplifies this trend: Twin leverages contextual access to a company's entire financial stack to autonomously surface insights and execute actions like payments or expense categorisation [[Source: Fintech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/venture-capital-funding/new-unicorn-slash-raises-100m-series-c-debuts-twin-ai-agent)]. Why it matters: Unlike traditional AI that offers recommendations, agentic systems can *act* within defined parameters, reducing manual workflows and accelerating decision cycles. Key players include Slash, Ratio (focused on lending automation), and legacy institutions like American Express, which acquired Hyper to embed similar capabilities. Market impact: As regulatory frameworks evolve (e.g., EU AI Act), fintechs that deploy transparent, auditable agentic systems will gain competitive advantage—while those relying on manual processes risk obsolescence. Expect increased investor scrutiny on AI governance alongside performance metrics in 2026 funding rounds.

## **3. Market & Industry Insight: The Digital Wallet Convergence**

Digital wallets are no longer just payment conduits—they're evolving into unified financial hubs. With over 70 million merchants globally now accepting wallet-based payments, the focus has shifted from adoption to *integration*: wallets now bundle remittances, savings, credit, and even identity verification [[Source: SQ Magazine](https://sqmagazine.co.uk/digital-wallet-statistics/)]. This convergence is accelerated by regulatory tailwinds (e.g., Nigeria's licensing of Flutterwave) and infrastructure partnerships (e.g., Capitec + Wise). Data point: Juniper Research projects digital wallet transaction value will exceed $12T globally by 2026, driven by emerging markets and embedded finance. For executives, the strategic imperative is clear: prioritize interoperability and user experience over feature proliferation. Wallets that seamlessly connect to open banking rails, support multi-currency settlements, and leverage AI for personalized financial guidance will capture disproportionate value in the next growth phase.
