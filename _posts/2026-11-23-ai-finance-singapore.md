# AI Finance in Singapore Brief: UnionPay and Debia Explore Agentic Payments

**Today:** Singapore-linked payment firms are moving agentic finance from discussion toward merchant integration and product pilots, while questions of authorization and oversight remain central.

## Top Stories

### 1. 💳 **UnionPay and Debia Explore Agentic Payments**

*PR Newswire · November 23, 2026*

**Bottom line:** UnionPay International and Singapore-based merchant acquirer Debia signed an MoU to explore accepting payments initiated by AI agents.

The MoU was signed at the Singapore FinTech Festival on November 18 and announced publicly on November 23 at 10:00 CST, which is SGT. The companies will examine how agents could recommend products, support purchase decisions and initiate payments; Debia plans to integrate UnionPay’s agentic payment solution into merchants’ acquiring systems.

**Why it matters:** Merchant acceptance could become a practical bottleneck for agent-led commerce. The collaboration puts a Singapore acquirer in the implementation path, though the announcement describes an exploration rather than a launched service.

### 2. 🤖 **WooshPay Demonstrates Conversational Payment Agent**

*PR Newswire · November 23, 2026*

**Bottom line:** WooshPay demonstrated an AI payment assistant and said it plans to pilot the service in the first quarter of 2027.

The company’s announcement was published November 23 at 04:40 ET, after the stated cutoff. In the demonstration, users make payment requests through conversation, then select a matched card and verify the transaction by SMS. WooshPay said the planned pilot will explore agent-led product comparisons and orders, as well as payments between agents.

**Why it matters:** The demo illustrates a payment flow that retains user selection and verification before execution. For regional payment providers, the next signal will be whether the planned pilot moves beyond demonstration and how authorization is handled in real transactions.

### 3. 🌐 **MetaComp Links Agentic Finance With Stablecoins**

*The Business Times · November 23, 2026*

**Bottom line:** MetaComp co-president Tin Pei Ling argues that stablecoins could complement AI agents’ ability to initiate and optimize payments, with controls needed to manage risk.

The Business Times published its report at 12:44 PM on November 23. It describes MetaComp’s StableX Know Your Agent framework, which defines an agent’s identity, authority and accountability, and its VisionX system for monitoring risk across fiat and blockchain activity. MetaComp said its payment network spans six Southeast Asian markets.

**Why it matters:** The report connects agent governance with cross-border settlement infrastructure, a relevant combination for firms serving businesses across Southeast Asia. Its claims about network reach and capabilities are company-reported.

### 4. 📊 **Longbridge Receives Fintech Awards Recognition**

*PR Newswire · November 23, 2026*

**Bottom line:** AI-native financial technology group Longbridge announced awards recognition in Hong Kong and Singapore, including a Singapore FinTech Festival finalist position for its AI arm.

The company’s announcement was published November 23 at 17:43 CST, which is SGT. Longbridge said its institutional technology arm received a Silver Award at the Hong Kong ICT Awards, while Longbridge AI Technologies was named a finalist in the Singapore FinTech Festival’s Thematic AI category.

**Why it matters:** The recognition offers a limited signal of how AI-focused financial technology firms are positioning across regional markets. Awards status does not establish adoption or commercial impact, so product deployment and customer traction remain the more material measures to watch.

## Key Takeaways

- Agentic payments are drawing activity from both international payment networks and Singapore-based merchant and cross-border providers.
- Product demonstrations and exploratory agreements are emerging, but the announcements do not yet establish broad merchant adoption.
- Identity, user authorization and transaction monitoring remain important design issues as AI agents gain payment capabilities.

## What to Watch

Watch for the Q1 2027 pilot WooshPay says it plans, and for concrete integration or testing outcomes from UnionPay and Debia. Deployment details, including how merchants verify agent authority and handle disputed transactions, will indicate how these proposals translate into practice.
