## Market & Industry Insight

The enterprise AI market continues shifting from **model experimentation** toward **production governance**.

Three themes dominate today's developments:

* **Security first** — AI identity protection, red-teaming and governance frameworks are becoming foundational enterprise capabilities.
* **Operational AI agents** — Vendors are introducing increasingly autonomous AI systems designed to execute business workflows rather than simply assist employees.
* **ROI pressure** — Investors and enterprise buyers are demanding measurable business outcomes to justify continued AI infrastructure spending.

Together, these signals indicate that enterprise AI is entering a new phase where competitive advantage depends less on deploying the latest model and more on operating AI safely, efficiently and at enterprise scale.
