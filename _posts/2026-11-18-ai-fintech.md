# AI Fintech Brief: Stitch Launches Agentic AI for Bank Operations

**Today:** Only one story met the brief's strict window and source-verification bar today, but it speaks to a live question for bank technology leaders: how to move AI agents from pilots into core operations.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **Stitch Launches Stitch AI, Putting Agents Inside Bank Operating Systems**

*Stitch (via PR Newswire) · November 18, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Riyadh-based fintech Stitch has launched an agentic AI layer that runs inside its banking operating system, aiming to take bank AI beyond proof-of-concept work.

Stitch AI has three parts. Copilot lets bank staff handle account and product tasks in plain language. Prebuilt Stitch Agents cover onboarding, credit decisions and collections. Agent Harness lets banks build and test their own agents with built-in controls. According to Stitch, the tools work directly on core systems, mask sensitive customer data, and let each bank choose where the software is deployed and which AI model it uses. CEO Mohamed Oueida presented the launch as a step past pilots; the announcement was issued from Dubai and Riyadh.

**Why it matters:** Model choice, data masking and controlled deployment are the features regulated banks tend to ask about first, so this launch is a useful gauge of how vendors are packaging agentic AI for them. The capability claims come from the company's own release; there is no customer deployment or performance data in it, so the real test is bank adoption in the Gulf and beyond.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://prnewswire.co.uk/news-releases/stitch-launches-stitch-ai-bringing-agentic-ai-directly-into-banking-operations-302943496.html)

## Key Takeaways

- Vendors are bundling agents, staff copilots and build-your-own tooling into a single banking platform, rather than selling standalone AI add-ons.
- Deployment location, model choice and data masking are now headline selling points, which suggests they are central to bank buying decisions.
- Onboarding, credit decisions and collections are the operational areas vendors are targeting first with prebuilt agents.

## What to Watch

Look for named bank customers and measured results, such as turnaround times or error rates, for Stitch AI, since the launch release does not provide them. Also watch whether other core-banking vendors announce comparable agent frameworks with built-in controls.
