*Covering developments published in the 48h to 2026-06-03 19:41:11 (+0800).*

## Top Stories

### 1. DeepSeek lines up roughly $7 billion for its first external funding round
- **Reuters** · 2026-06-03
- **Summary**: Reuters reported that Chinese AI startup DeepSeek is set to raise about 50 billion yuan in its first fundraising round, with Tencent and CATL among the prospective investors. The deal would value the company at roughly 350-400 billion yuan post-money, underscoring how quickly DeepSeek has become a strategic asset in China’s AI stack. The report also suggests major domestic capital is consolidating around national AI champions rather than only platform incumbents.
- **Why It Matters**: This is the clearest sign yet that China’s leading AI startups are moving into megafundraising territory comparable to top global peers. A deal of this size would materially strengthen DeepSeek’s position in model training, infrastructure procurement, and ecosystem influence.
- **URL**: https://za.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/deepseek-slated-to-draw-7-billion-in-maiden-fundraising-sources-say-4311462

### 2. Alibaba opens Qwen to external apps as China’s AI agent race accelerates
- **Nikkei Asia** · 2026-06-03
- **Summary**: Nikkei Asia reported that Alibaba is opening Qwen to external applications, a move aimed at broadening the model’s reach beyond Alibaba’s own properties and intensifying competition in AI agents. The shift reflects a platform strategy: Alibaba is trying to make Qwen a default orchestration layer for third-party services rather than just a standalone model family. It also signals that distribution and agent integration are becoming as important as raw model performance in China’s next AI phase.
- **Why It Matters**: China’s AI competition is moving from chatbot adoption to ecosystem control. If Alibaba succeeds in embedding Qwen across outside apps, it could gain a defensible position in agent workflows, developer mindshare, and enterprise deployment.
- **URL**: https://asia.nikkei.com/business/technology/artificial-intelligence/alibaba-opens-qwen-to-external-apps-as-china-s-ai-agent-race-intensifies

### 3. China folds AI and data protections into tighter trade-secret rules
- **The Straits Times** · 2026-06-03
- **Summary**: The Straits Times reported that China has added data and AI-related protections to its trade-secret framework, tightening legal guardrails around sensitive information leakage. The change appears designed to cover high-value training data, model-related know-how, and other intangible assets that are increasingly central to AI competition. It comes as China balances faster commercialization with stronger control over strategic technologies and information flows.
- **Why It Matters**: For Chinese AI companies and foreign firms operating in China, compliance risk is rising alongside the strategic value of proprietary data and model assets. The move reinforces that IP protection in China’s AI sector is expanding beyond classic source code and formulas into broader data-centric knowledge assets.
- **URL**: https://www.straitstimes.com/asia/east-asia/china-adds-data-and-ai-to-trade-secret-rules-to-block-leaks

### 4. China’s copyright authorities launch a 2026 campaign targeting AI training-data compliance
- **Xinhua / Economic Information Daily** · 2026-06-03
- **Summary**: Chinese authorities launched the “Jianwang 2026” anti-piracy campaign, and one of its named focus areas is copyright compliance in AI, including the legality of large-model training corpora. The campaign is being run jointly by the National Copyright Administration, MIIT, the Ministry of Public Security, and the Cyberspace Administration of China. It indicates that regulators are moving from broad AI governance principles toward enforcement in training-data sourcing.
- **Why It Matters**: This raises the stakes for model developers, data providers, and platform operators in China. Copyright compliance in training datasets is becoming a live enforcement issue, not just a policy discussion, which could reshape sourcing practices and licensing markets.
- **URL**: https://www.news.cn/tech/20260603/ec42c4f9ac3640d58bd4fd4d50d88ea8/c.html
