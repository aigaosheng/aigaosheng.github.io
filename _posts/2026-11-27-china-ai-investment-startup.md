# AI Investment & Startup in China Brief: Evoken Valued at $3.2B

**Today:** Chinese AI application developer Evoken nearly doubled its valuation in under six months on the back of AI video tool revenue, while two embodied intelligence startups secured major rounds targeting commercial deployment.

## Top Stories

### 1. 📊 **Liblib Parent Evoken Raises Nearly $200M at $3.2B Valuation as AI Video Tool Drives Revenue**

*JRJ / Guandian · Nov 27, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Evoken, the company behind AI image and video platform Liblib, has closed a new round of nearly $200 million at a post-money valuation of $3.2 billion, a roughly 56% increase from its $2.05 billion valuation in June.

The company is already negotiating additional funding at a higher valuation, according to the report . Its video tool LibTV, launched after the previous round was in negotiation, has become Evoken's largest revenue source and pushed monthly revenue to tens of millions of dollars, placing it among China's top AI application companies by revenue . Evoken is described as one of the few Chinese AI application companies still able to attract large checks from top-tier USD funds .

**Why it matters:** Evoken's rapid valuation climb demonstrates that AI application-layer companies with proven revenue traction can still command premium valuations even as Chinese AI investment broadly cools. For Asia-based investors and enterprise buyers, LibTV's emergence as the primary revenue driver underscores that generative video tools are finding commercial traction faster than many expected.

### 2. 📊 **Zero Power Robotics Closes Nine-Figure Yuan Round for Embodied AI Expansion**

*36Kr · Nov 27, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Embodied intelligence startup Zero Power Robotics has completed a new funding round worth hundreds of millions of yuan, with proceeds earmarked for scaling verified deployment scenarios and reserving computing power for an API launch at year-end.

Investors in the round include Zero2IPO Industrial Investment, Xizhou International, Joyu Asia, Yuemin Sufeng, Wuxi Binhu, Hefei Construction Investment, Hefei Industrial Investment Guozheng, and Jack Group, alongside continued participation from existing shareholders Tongchuang Weiye, Lihe Fund, and Relay Angel . The company plans to open its "Picking API" at the end of the year .

**Why it matters:** Zero Power's focus on commercializing physical picking tasks through an API model signals a shift from demo-driven robotics funding toward revenue-generating deployment infrastructure. The involvement of multiple local government investment vehicles reflects continued state-backed support for embodied AI as a strategic sector.

### 3. 📊 **Zero-Space Robotics Completes Hundreds-of-Millions Yuan Series A Led by Tsinghua-Linked Team**

*STAR Market Daily · Nov 27, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Hefei-based embodied intelligence company Zero-Space Robotics has closed a Series A round worth hundreds of millions of yuan, with investors including Qingke Industrial Investment, Guolian Group, and Hefei state-backed funds.

The company was founded in January 2025 by a team from Tsinghua University's intelligent robotics laboratory and has launched the ZERITH Z1 humanoid robot . Proceeds will support efforts to improve robot manipulation through real physical interaction and drive commercial deployment of embodied intelligence .

**Why it matters:** Zero-Space's rapid progression from founding to a nine-figure Series A in under two years highlights the intensity of competition in China's humanoid robotics sector, where university spinouts with strong technical pedigrees can attract significant capital quickly. The concentration of Hefei-based state investment in both Zero Power and Zero-Space suggests the city is positioning itself as a hub for embodied AI manufacturing.

### 4. 🤖 **NeuxMind.AI Raises Hundreds of Millions in Series B for Enterprise AI Agent Platform**

*Huanqiu / NetEase · Nov 26, 2026*

**Bottom line:** AI services company NeuxMind.AI (星尘浩宇) has completed a Series B round worth hundreds of millions of yuan led by Saize Capital, with funds allocated to its enterprise AI platform and global service system.

Founded in 2021 by a Chinese team with primary operations targeting overseas markets, NeuxMind.AI has deployed a financial audit agent for a large retail bank in a major overseas market that has run continuously for over 300 days, saving the client hundreds of dollars in operational costs . The company is building an "enterprise AI brain" with three layers: Agent Infra (compute, models, data), an AgentOS platform, and scenario applications .

**Why it matters:** NeuxMind's overseas-first strategy and focus on long-running, production-grade agents reflect a maturing approach among Chinese AI startups—building for international enterprise clients where willingness to pay for automation is higher, rather than competing in the crowded domestic consumer market.

## Key Takeaways

- **Application-layer revenue is the new valuation anchor:** Evoken's ability to nearly double its valuation in six months on the back of LibTV's tens-of-millions monthly revenue signals that investors are rewarding demonstrated commercial traction over model benchmarks.
- **Embodied AI funding is concentrating in Hefei:** Two of today's three robotics rounds involve Hefei state investment vehicles, suggesting the city is building a deliberate cluster strategy around humanoid and industrial robotics.
- **Chinese AI startups are looking outward:** NeuxMind's overseas enterprise deployments and Evoken's USD-denominated fundraising both indicate that global markets and international capital remain accessible for Chinese AI companies with compelling products.

## What to Watch

Whether Evoken's reported higher-valuation talks close before year-end, and whether Zero Power's Picking API launch at year-end converts embodied AI from a capital-intensive R&D story into a recurring-revenue software model.
