# AI Finance in Singapore Brief: Singapore Leads Region in Agentic AI Adoption

**Today:** A new regional survey shows Singapore's finance and accounting professionals adopting agentic AI faster than peers elsewhere in Asia-Pacific, a signal that front-line use is running ahead of the governance frameworks MAS is still rolling out.

## Top Stories

### 1. 📊 **Singapore Finance Professionals Lead Region in Agentic AI Use**

*PR Newswire (CPA Australia) · November 4, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Singapore recorded the strongest demand for agentic AI of any market in CPA Australia's 2026 Business Technology Report, with adoption among finance professionals outpacing peers in Australia, Hong Kong, Malaysia and mainland China.

CPA Australia surveyed 1,679 accounting and finance professionals across the five markets and found 93% of Singapore businesses use AI, versus an 89% survey average, while 47% already run agentic AI systems capable of completing multi-step tasks with minimal prompting. Fifty-six percent of Singapore respondents expect to increase their use of agentic AI over the next 12 months, the highest of any market surveyed against a 45% average, though only 12% describe AI as deeply embedded across their organisation.

**Why it matters:** The gap between fast-moving front-line adoption and shallow organisational embedding is exactly the risk MAS's AI Risk Management Guidelines, effective from October 2027, are meant to close, putting pressure on compliance and risk teams to catch up with finance functions already running agentic tools. Regionally, the finding reinforces Singapore's position as the proving ground for agentic AI in financial services, a role MAS is now trying to formalise through the newly operational Future of Finance Institute.

## Key Takeaways

- Agentic AI use among Singapore finance professionals (47%) is far ahead of deep organisational embedding (12%), widening the gap that MAS's phased AI Risk Management Guidelines are designed to close by October 2027 and 2028.
- Singapore's lead over Hong Kong, Malaysia, mainland China and Australia in agentic AI adoption reinforces its status as the region's financial AI testbed, a position the newly launched Future of Finance Institute aims to institutionalise.
- With real-world adoption outrunning risk frameworks, boards and audit committees at financial institutions and professional services firms face rising pressure to formalise AI governance well before regulatory deadlines bite.
