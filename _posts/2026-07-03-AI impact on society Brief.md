- **Source**: Business Insider · **2026-07-03**
- **Summary**: Goldman Sachs Chief Economist Joseph Briggs estimates that AI could eventually displace approximately 15 million U.S. workers, equivalent to roughly 9% of the workforce. However, he argues historical technological revolutions suggest most displaced workers will transition into newly created occupations over time rather than remain permanently unemployed. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}
