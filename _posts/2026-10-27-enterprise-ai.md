# Enterprise AI Brief: Anthropic Files for IPO, Reports $16.3B Half-Year Revenue

**Today:** If you buy, build or govern AI agents, today's news covers the vendors' next layer of controls (identity, gateways, shared business definitions) and a first public look at how much enterprises are spending with one major model provider.

## Top Stories

### 1. 📊 **Anthropic Files for IPO, Disclosing Rapid Enterprise Revenue Growth**
*CNN · October 26, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Anthropic's IPO filing gives the market its first audited view of how large enterprise spending on frontier AI has become.

The prospectus shows first-half 2026 revenue of $16.3 billion, up 1,300% year on year. Anthropic says it has more than 14,000 enterprise customers, and about 250 of them each contribute $10 million or more in annual revenue. The filing also contains 93 pages of risk factors, including the possibility that liability from extreme harm could exceed the company's insurance cover.

**Why it matters:** This is a major AI lab disclosing how concentrated its enterprise revenue is. Procurement and risk teams in Asia can now judge supplier dependency and vendor viability using filed numbers instead of press claims.

### 2. 🔒 **IBM Makes Agent Identity and Vault 2.2 Generally Available to Govern AI Agents**
*IBM Newsroom · October 27, 2026*

**Bottom line:** IBM is treating AI agents as non-human identities. Each agent gets an owner, scoped short-lived credentials, and access that can be revoked in real time.

At TechXchange 2026, IBM made Agent Identity and HashiCorp Vault 2.2 generally available. Agent Identity records who owns each agent and what authority it holds. Vault issues expiring credentials and can revoke a session if an agent goes beyond its approved permissions. Two watsonx Orchestrate features are in tech preview: one finds agents built on Amazon Bedrock, Microsoft Foundry and Google Gemini, and the other runs agents in isolated sandboxes with a kill switch. Pre-deployment evaluation and per-agent token cost tracking are generally available. IBM cites a Gartner estimate that the average Fortune 500 firm will run more than 150,000 agents by 2028, while only 13% of organisations believe their agent governance is adequate.

**Why it matters:** Least-privilege access, ownership records and a kill switch match the access-control and accountability questions that auditors already ask about service accounts. Regulated fintechs can use this release as a reference design even if they don't buy IBM.

### 3. 🤖 **SAP Adds Agent Gateway, Enterprise AI Gateway and EU AI Act Tooling at TechEd**
*PR Newswire (SAP SE) · October 27, 2026*

**Bottom line:** SAP is opening Joule to third-party agents and adding a single control point for all AI traffic.

Joule Work gets new planning and orchestration features for multistep workflows over A2A and MCP. Third-party agents can now call Joule Agents through a governed agent gateway. A new SAP Enterprise AI Gateway will bring authentication, policy enforcement, routing, monitoring, usage tracking and auditing into one place. An AI Governance Assistant aims to help with EU AI Act compliance. Joule Studio offers more than 70 models, and SAP says custom agents will run free on its managed runtime through December 2026 for selected customers.

**Why it matters:** ERP vendors now want to be the policy layer for agents that touch finance and supply-chain data. Firms running SAP should decide early whether governance will sit with the vendor or in their own stack.

### 4. 🏦 **Singapore Launches Champions of AI With 24 Companies, Plus SME Agentic AI Workshops**
*Xinhua · October 27, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Singapore has moved from announcing AI support in its Budget to running a funded programme with named participants.

Enterprise Singapore said the Champions of AI programme will help 24 Singapore-based companies build long-term AI transformation roadmaps, find expertise and upskill staff, and more firms will join over time. For SMEs, agentic AI workshops follow a pilot in which more than 280 small businesses used agentic tools for marketing, sales and customer service. More workshops start next month and will expand in 2027. A separate Catalytic AI Solutions initiative will start with advanced manufacturing, trade and logistics. A*STAR also launched a materials accelerator backed by about S$150 million (about US$117 million).

**Why it matters:** The cohort companies are likely to become early, visible buyers of agentic AI. Local vendors and integrators now have a clearer pipeline, and programme benchmarks could shape what "good" adoption looks like across the market.

### 5. 🤖 **Oracle Announces Semantic Layer to Stop AI Agents Misreading Business Metrics**
*Tech Times · October 27, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Oracle is addressing a common reason agents give wrong answers: they read the correct data but apply the wrong definition of a metric.

At Oracle AI World, Oracle announced a Business Semantic Layer that gives agents shared, governed definitions of metrics, hierarchies and KPIs. It also announced a Business Simulation Engine that brings Essbase planning, including write-back and scenario sandboxing, into its Autonomous AI Lakehouse. Both are expected within 12 months, with no pricing disclosed. Oracle also expanded SQL support for Apache Iceberg tables. Analysts quoted in the article warn that metric definitions built in Oracle's layer would be hard to move to another platform.

**Why it matters:** Reporting and reconciliation teams need agents that use the same definitions of "revenue" or "settled amount" every time. Buyers should weigh that consistency against the lock-in risk.

### 6. 🤖 **Backbase Launches Delivery Factory, Pairing Bank IT Teams With Specialist AI Agents**
*FinTech Global · October 27, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Backbase is selling AI agents built for banking software delivery rather than general coding assistants.

Delivery Factory assigns agents to defined roles: business analyst, solution architect, and backend, mobile and web. Engineers execute and verify the approved plans, and delivery leads keep control of scope and decisions. Backbase reports, from more than 10 implementations, that teams worked twice as fast as conventional teams and 30% faster than teams using general-purpose coding assistants. These figures are company-supplied. New Migration and Upgrade agents target platform moves and maintenance. Backbase says more than 120 banks use its technology.

**Why it matters:** Banking vendors are now competing on domain context, not just models. Bank technology leads should ask for their own baseline metrics before accepting vendor productivity claims.

## Key Takeaways
- Most of today's product news is about controls rather than new agents. IBM, SAP and Oracle all shipped identity, gateways or shared definitions, which suggests that governance, not capability, is now what holds back scale.
- Enterprise AI demand is becoming measurable. Anthropic's filed revenue and Singapore's named cohort both show spending moving from pilots to programmes.
- Productivity figures in vendor launches are still mostly self-reported. Buyers in regulated sectors should set their own baselines before signing.

## What to Watch
Watch for Anthropic to set a price range for its offering. Expect more agent-governance announcements as IBM TechXchange and Oracle AI World run through October 29.
