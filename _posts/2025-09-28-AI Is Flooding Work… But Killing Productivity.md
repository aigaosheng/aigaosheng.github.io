### 🚨 AI Is Flooding Work… But Killing Productivity

Companies rushed to adopt generative AI, expecting instant efficiency gains. Reality check: **95% report no measurable ROI**, even as AI usage **doubled at work since 2023** .

💡 **Enter AI-generated workslop**: tons of AI output that **needs human rework**, adding extra hours instead of saving them. Some teams spend **up to 30% of their workweek managing AI output** rather than high-value tasks.

---

### 🔑 Key Takeaways

* **More AI ≠ Smarter Work** – Output volume is meaningless without strategy.
* **Human-AI Collaboration Matters** – Design workflows where AI reduces mundane effort.
* **Measure Real Gains** – Track productivity improvements, not just AI usage metrics.

---

### 💭 Reflection

The AI hype risks **mindless automation**. Leaders must focus on **quality over quantity**, amplifying human skill instead of generating noise. Without careful integration, AI might stall—or even regress—productivity.
