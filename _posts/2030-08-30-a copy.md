# RWA Brief: BNY and Citadel Advance Tokenized Treasury Repo

**Today:** Tokenization is moving deeper into institutional financial-market workflows, with a Treasury-backed repo transaction demonstrating how digital securities can support collateral mobility, while Singapore's MAS emphasizes the standards and settlement infrastructure needed for commercial adoption.

## Top Stories

### 1. 💳 BNY and Citadel Securities Complete Tokenized Treasury Repo Workflow

*BNY · October 13, 2026*

**Bottom line:** BNY and Citadel Securities have completed a triparty repurchase agreement workflow using a tokenized US Treasury, extending tokenization into institutional collateral management and short-term financing.

BNY represented a two-year US Treasury note as a tokenized security on its private digital ledger and settled the transaction near-instantly, rather than through the standard T+1 settlement cycle. The Treasury was subsequently used as collateral in an overnight triparty repo, connecting securities settlement with financing activity in a single workflow.

The transaction marks the first use of tokenized assets on a major triparty repo platform, according to BNY. It demonstrates how tokenized securities could move beyond issuance and ownership records into established institutional market infrastructure.

**Why it matters:** For banks, custodians and institutional investors across Asia, the strategic opportunity extends beyond faster settlement. Tokenized collateral could improve liquidity management and the operational efficiency of secured financing, provided that legal enforceability, risk controls and interoperability are maintained.

### 2. 📊 Franklin Templeton and Animoca Brands Collaborate on Institutional Tokenized Investments

*Animoca Brands · October 9, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Franklin Templeton and Animoca Brands are collaborating to expand institutional access to tokenized real-world assets through NUVA, an Animoca Brands-backed vault marketplace.

The partnership aims to broaden NUVA's range of investment opportunities beyond its existing Provenance blockchain-based assets by bringing in assets from a wider pool of issuers. The companies are also exploring the co-design of tokenized cultural assets, extending the collaboration beyond conventional financial instruments.

NUVA launched in May 2026, and the partners' announcement cited more than US$30 billion in total value locked on the Provenance Blockchain as of September 24, 2026. That figure describes the blockchain's total value locked, not the value of assets available through NUVA or the partnership.

**Why it matters:** The collaboration highlights a potential distribution model for tokenized investments: combining established asset-management capabilities with digital-asset platforms that can connect investors to multiple issuers. For Asian financial institutions, the key questions are how institutional-grade assets will be selected, how investor eligibility and custody will work, and whether the platform can deliver meaningful distribution without fragmenting liquidity.

### 3. 🏦 MAS Sets Out Priorities for Scaling Commercial Asset Tokenization

*Monetary Authority of Singapore · October 9, 2026*

**Bottom line:** MAS is shifting the focus from proving tokenization's technical feasibility towards coordinated commercial adoption, supported by common industry standards, regulatory clarity and trusted digital settlement assets.

In a keynote address at INSEAD's Digital Finance & Agentic AI Summit, MAS Managing Director Chia Der Jiun said that tokenization's potential has been established across multiple asset classes, but activity remains small relative to conventional financial markets. The next challenge is coordinating customers, counterparties and financial-market infrastructure around use cases that deliver clear benefits to the wider ecosystem.

MAS highlighted the importance of shared approaches to asset servicing, disclosure, interoperability and risk management. It also pointed to the role of tokenized bank deposits, regulated stablecoins and central bank digital currencies in supporting different settlement needs, including the planned next step of settling tokenized MAS Bills with wholesale Singapore-dollar CBDC.

**Why it matters:** Singapore's approach places market infrastructure and settlement certainty at the centre of institutional tokenization. Banks, asset managers and fintech providers should monitor how common standards, legal clarity and wholesale settlement capabilities develop, as these factors will influence whether pilots become repeatable commercial services.

## Key Takeaways

- **Tokenization is moving into core financial-market operations.** The BNY transaction connects digital securities with repo financing, demonstrating a use case beyond token issuance and secondary-market trading.
- **Distribution and infrastructure are developing together.** The Franklin Templeton–Animoca collaboration targets access to a broader range of investment assets, while institutional settlement initiatives address how those assets can move and be financed.
- **Interoperability and trusted settlement remain decisive.** MAS's emphasis on common standards, legal clarity and high-quality settlement assets highlights the coordination required to scale beyond isolated pilots.

## What to Watch

Watch for additional live transactions that combine tokenized securities, collateral management and financing, particularly where institutions can demonstrate repeatable operational benefits. In Singapore, progress towards settling tokenized MAS Bills with wholesale SGD CBDC will be an important signal of how tokenized assets and central-bank money can operate together on shared infrastructure.
