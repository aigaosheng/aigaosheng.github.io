# Payments Brief: Japan's Megabanks Complete Yen Stablecoin Pilot

Today: Commercial banks and major payment networks are turning multi-currency stablecoins and tokenised deposits into production-grade infrastructure for global treasury and cross-border settlement.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🏦 Japan's Megabanks Complete Joint Yen Stablecoin Cross-Border Settlement Pilot

Crypto Briefing · October 16, 2026

Bottom line: Mitsubishi UFJ, Mizuho, and SMBC demonstrated that a shared trust-based yen token can execute corporate cross-border transfers under Japan's Payment Services Act ahead of 2027 commercialization.

Mitsubishi UFJ Bank, Mizuho Bank, and Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corporation completed a joint proof-of-concept modeling cross-border treasury payments for trading conglomerate Mitsubishi Corporation. The trial utilized a digital token legally structured as a specified trust beneficiary right, with Mitsubishi UFJ Trust and Banking Corporation serving as trustee and underlying bank deposits securing the token. Conducted under Japan's Financial Services Agency FinTech Proof-of-Concept Hub, the architecture enables verified corporate clients across all three banking networks to transfer value seamlessly without a fixed issuance ceiling.

Why it matters: The pilot proves that Japan's top financial institutions can deploy interoperable digital currency on shared infrastructure, presenting a viable bank-led alternative to SWIFT correspondent rails for Asian corporate liquidity.

### 2. 🔒 Episode Six Debuts Real-Time 'Verified Backing' for Stablecoins and Tokenised Deposits

FinTech News Singapore · October 16, 2026

Bottom line: Episode Six added pre-settlement verification to its Token Control Ledger to eliminate counterfeit minting and double-redemption risks for bank-issued digital assets.

The feature introduces real-time controls at issuance and redemption, the two stages where institutions encounter acute counterparty and balance-sheet risk. Because validation records remain anchored within the issuing bank's ledger, verification persists even if a digital token traverses multiple external wallets or blockchain networks prior to redemption. The solution is now operational across more than 50 markets where Episode Six operates, replacing traditional retroactive reconciliation with upfront transaction validation.

Why it matters: As commercial banks roll out tokenised deposits and stablecoins, real-time ledger verification removes a key compliance roadblock that previously prevented regulated lenders from handling programmable on-chain money.

### 3. 💳 Visa Direct Commercializes 24/7 USDC Prefunding with Inyo Global

crypto.news · October 16, 2026

Bottom line: Visa transitioned its stablecoin settlement rails from pilot testing to commercial production, allowing businesses to fund cross-border payouts around the clock in USDC.

Payments infrastructure provider Inyo Global has become the first commercial enabler to deploy Visa Direct's stablecoin prefunding capability into production. The service allows corporate clients and remittance providers to bypass banking cut-off windows by topping up payout balances in near real time via Circle's USDC. Through Inyo's unified API integration, corporate clients can initiate near-instant domestic and cross-border disbursements across more than 180 countries.

Why it matters: Eliminating multi-day fiat prefunding frees up trapped working capital for cross-border money transfer operators, accelerating enterprise transition to 24/7 blockchain liquidity.

### 6. 🌐 AMRO Outlines Blueprint for Multilateral Wholesale Payment Integration in ASEAN+3

ASEAN+3 Macroeconomic Research Office · October 16, 2026

Bottom line: AMRO called for ASEAN+3 economies to advance beyond bilateral retail QR links toward multilateral wholesale platforms, common FX settlement, and digital asset standards.

In a keynote address, AMRO Director Yasuto Watanabe highlighted that only 35% of retail and 55% of wholesale cross-border payments currently achieve the G20 roadmap target of one-hour settlement. Watanabe emphasized four regional imperatives: scaling multilateral connections via Project Nexus, closing wholesale gaps with safe foreign exchange settlement and liquidity management, coordinating supervisory frameworks on stablecoins and CBDCs, and building backup payment corridors. The speech stressed that regional financial stability requires interoperable wholesale architectures rather than fragmented bilateral agreements.

Why it matters: For fintechs and banks operating in Southeast and East Asia, regulatory alignment around Project Nexus and digital asset safeguards will determine how quickly local-currency cross-border trade can scale.
