# AI Investment & Startup in China Brief: Jiutian Chuangzhi Raises Nearly RMB 100 Million

**Today:** Fresh funding for outdoor robotics and energy maintenance, alongside a new spatial AI venture, highlights investment in AI tied to physical-world work.

## Top Stories

### 1. 📊 **Jiutian Chuangzhi Raises Nearly RMB 100 Million**

*投资界 · November 25, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Shenzhen-based Jiutian Chuangzhi raised nearly RMB 100 million in Series A funding from Yidao Capital, Qingke Chanye Investment and Nanshan Zhanxintou.

The company says it will use the capital for embodied AI model and outdoor robot platform development, product iteration, overseas expansion, and manufacturing and supply-chain capabilities. It reports deployments in nearly 100 cities and more than 20 countries, including Singapore’s Gardens by the Bay.

**Why it matters:** The round backs an AI robotics business with an existing regional footprint, while linking model work to field deployment and manufacturing. Its reported Singapore presence offers local operators a nearby reference point for outdoor autonomous systems.

### 2. 📊 **Jitai Raises Nearly RMB 100 Million in B+ Round**

*具身之家 · November 25, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Energy-maintenance robotics company Jitai completed a B+ round of nearly RMB 100 million, bringing its reported post-money valuation to RMB 1.5 billion.

The company develops wall-climbing robots and AI maintenance software for large energy facilities, targeting hazardous work at height. The report did not name investors; it said Lanqiao Capital served as long-term financial adviser.

**Why it matters:** The financing points to investor interest in AI-enabled industrial maintenance, where robotics can address specific operating risks. For energy operators across Asia, the commercial test will be whether these systems can deliver reliable inspections and maintenance at scale.

### 3. 🤖 **HKUST Professor Launches Spatial AI Startup**

*36氪 · November 25, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Computer vision researcher and former Apple spatial intelligence scientist Long Quan announced the launch of Shenzhen-based Envision AI, also known as Gezhi Technology.

The company is developing models that represent and reason across text, images, spatial information and physical information. Its founding team includes Oxford professor Philip Torr and former researchers and executives from Apple, Ant Group and Tencent; the report says the company has begun commercial partnerships and generated revenue.

**Why it matters:** The launch brings senior academic and industry experience into China’s emerging spatial and physical AI startup field. Its next signal will be whether the proposed models can support repeatable products and commercial use beyond early partnerships.

## Key Takeaways

- Two of the funding rounds back robotics tied to defined operating environments: outdoor cleaning and energy infrastructure maintenance.
- Jiutian Chuangzhi’s reported Singapore deployment connects China’s embodied AI funding to a live Southeast Asian operating context.
- Envision AI’s launch adds a spatial intelligence venture led by experienced researchers, with product performance and commercial traction still to be demonstrated publicly.

## What to Watch

Track whether Jiutian Chuangzhi translates its funding into expanded deployments and whether Jitai discloses its investors or customer rollout plans. For Envision AI, watch for public model demonstrations, product details and further evidence of commercial partnerships.
