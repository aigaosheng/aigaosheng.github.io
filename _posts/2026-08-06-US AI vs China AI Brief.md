## Strategic Takeaways

* **Policy competition is accelerating.** The United States is emphasizing open AI development while maintaining targeted security oversight, seeking to preserve innovation leadership without broadly restricting domestic developers.
* **The rivalry extends beyond AI models.** Compute infrastructure, semiconductor supply chains, critical minerals, and manufacturing capabilities are becoming central elements of AI competitiveness.
* **Markets are becoming more selective.** Investors continue to support AI's long-term potential but are increasingly scrutinizing the enormous capital expenditures required for frontier AI infrastructure.
* **Geopolitical tensions remain elevated.** Technology controls, sanctions, and industrial policies continue to shape the competitive landscape between the United States and China, influencing both commercial AI development and national security strategies.
