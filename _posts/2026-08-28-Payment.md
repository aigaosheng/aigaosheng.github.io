# Payment Brief — 2026-08-28

## Top Stories 

### 1. **Stripe and Advent abandon pursuit of PayPal**

* **Source**: Reuters · August 28, 2026
* **Summary**: A consortium involving Stripe and private-equity firm Advent International has reportedly ended its effort to acquire PayPal. The group had previously offered $60.50 per share, valuing PayPal at more than $53 billion, but the proposal faced concerns over valuation, regulatory complexity and financing.
* **Why It Matters**: The collapse removes what could have been the largest fintech acquisition in history and leaves PayPal's turnaround strategy under CEO Enrique Lores as the central driver of its future. It also underscores the difficulty of consolidating major global payments platforms despite intensifying competitive pressure.
* **URL**: [https://www.reuters.com/business/advent-stripe-consortium-is-said-drop-pursuit-paypal-bloomberg-news-reports-2026-08-28/](https://www.reuters.com/business/advent-stripe-consortium-is-said-drop-pursuit-paypal-bloomberg-news-reports-2026-08-28/)

### 2. **Dunamu and Visa partner to explore stablecoin payments and AI-driven finance**

* **Source**: The Block · August 28, 2026
* **Summary**: South Korean digital-asset company Dunamu, the operator of Upbit, has entered a strategic partnership with Visa to explore stablecoin-based payments, cross-border remittances and AI-driven financial services. The companies will also examine agentic commerce and potential future use of Open Standard's Open USD.
* **Why It Matters**: The partnership brings together a major global card network and one of South Korea's largest digital-asset infrastructure players. It signals further convergence between traditional payment rails, stablecoin settlement and AI agents capable of initiating commercial transactions.
* **URL**: [https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-08-28-dunamu-visa-partner-stablecoin-ai-ousd-412988](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-08-28-dunamu-visa-partner-stablecoin-ai-ousd-412988)

### 3. **European payments industry publishes new 2026 market landscape**

* **Source**: The Paypers · August 28, 2026
* **Summary**: Payments Association EU, Deloitte and The Paypers have launched the report *Key Players in the EU Payments Landscape - 2026*. The report reviews national payments markets across Europe and maps major participants by their role in the payments value chain and relevant financial indicators.
* **Why It Matters**: As European payments become more competitive and regulatory initiatives reshape market structure, a clearer view of the ecosystem is increasingly important for banks, fintechs and investors evaluating consolidation, partnerships and cross-border expansion.

### 4. **Interoperability emerges as the central challenge for digital money**

* **Source**: Finextra · August 28, 2026
* **Summary**: A new analysis argues that the main risk facing the next generation of digital money is fragmentation rather than adoption. As CBDCs, tokenised deposits and stablecoins move beyond pilots, the ability of different monetary systems and financial infrastructures to interoperate will become increasingly important for cross-border payments, settlement and tokenised markets.
* **Why It Matters**: The payments industry is moving from experimentation toward infrastructure design. Institutions that build around open standards, connectivity and interoperability may be better positioned as tokenised money and assets scale across jurisdictions.
* **URL**: [https://www.finextra.com/the-long-read/1736/the-future-of-digital-money-depends-on-interoperability](https://www.finextra.com/the-long-read/1736/the-future-of-digital-money-depends-on-interoperability)
