### 1. **Chemistry Raises $500 Million Second Venture Fund to Back Early-Stage Software Startups**

## Key Investment Themes

- **AI infrastructure remains the dominant capital magnet**: Large funding rounds continue flowing into compute, model infrastructure, and AI deployment platforms.
- **Physical AI is emerging as a major venture category**: Robotics and industrial automation startups are attracting early-stage investor attention.
- **Deep tech funding is rebounding**: Energy, fusion, and climate-related technologies are seeing renewed interest from strategic investors.
- **Venture capital is becoming more concentrated**: Capital is increasingly flowing toward startups with strong technical differentiation and scalable markets.
