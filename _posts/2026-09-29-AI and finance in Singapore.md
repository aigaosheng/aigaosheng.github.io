# AI and finance in Singapore Brief — 2026-09-29

**Today:** Singapore’s AI-finance story is shifting toward scaled workforce adoption, agentic-AI controls and AI-powered payment infrastructure.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **Singapore financial institutions commit to AI training for more than 80,000 employees**

*Human Resources Director · 29 Sep 2026*

**Bottom line:** Twenty-three financial institutions have committed to train more than 80,000 Singapore-based employees in critical AI skills by 2028.

The commitment is part of the Institute of Banking and Finance’s AI Workforce Co-Lab, covering banking, insurance and asset management. More than half of the targeted workforce has already received training, while the programme is also testing job redesign and new career pathways.

**Why it matters:** Singapore’s financial-sector AI strategy is moving beyond individual use cases toward institution-wide workforce transformation, with AI governance, practical applications and redesigned operational roles becoming core capabilities.

### 2. 🔒 **Sumsub launches APAC council for trusted agentic AI**

*PR Newswire · 29 Sep 2026*

**Bottom line:** Sumsub has launched an APAC-focused council in Singapore to develop practical frameworks for identity, delegated authority, security, accountability and oversight of AI agents.

The council brings together potential participants from policy, academia, technology, industry and civil society. Its initial work will examine agentic commerce and the governance requirements that arise when AI agents act on behalf of consumers or businesses.

**Why it matters:** As financial AI moves from recommendations toward autonomous actions, identity and delegated-authority controls become infrastructure requirements alongside conventional fraud, compliance and cybersecurity controls.

### 3. 💳 **AsiaPay pilots AI-powered voice payments for merchants**

*PR Newswire · 29 Sep 2026*

**Bottom line:** AsiaPay is piloting an AI-powered Voice Pay experience that lets consumers initiate payments through natural speech while its payment engine handles authentication, routing and fraud protection.

The service connects voice instructions to customers’ preferred payment methods, including digital wallets and cards. AsiaPay says existing merchants can activate the experience without development changes through its zero-code enablement approach.

**Why it matters:** Voice interfaces could become another entry point for agentic commerce, pushing payment providers to combine conversational AI with authentication, fraud scoring and transaction routing.

### 4. 🏦 **Responsible Fintech Institute expands expertise in AI and digital-finance governance**

*PR Newswire · 29 Sep 2026*

**Bottom line:** Singapore’s Responsible Fintech Institute has expanded its leadership and advisory expertise across law, governance, digital policy and cross-border capital markets as AI and digital assets reshape finance.

RFI appointed four Executive Fellows and elevated Distinguished Professor Patrick H. M. Loh to Co-Founder. The institute said the appointments will support principles-based industry frameworks and engagement with regulators and standard-setting bodies.

**Why it matters:** The development highlights the growing demand for governance frameworks that connect AI and digital-finance innovation with legal accountability, transparency and cross-border standards.

### 5. 🔒 **GovWare 2026 puts AI governance and financial-sector resilience on the agenda**

*PR Newswire · 29 Sep 2026*

**Bottom line:** GovWare 2026 will examine AI governance, technology concentration risk and post-quantum readiness specifically in the context of financial-sector critical infrastructure.

The Singapore cybersecurity conference will feature a Financial Services Critical Information Infrastructure Forum focused on maintaining financial services during disruption. A simulated “Keeping Money Moving” incident will examine operational resilience alongside emerging AI and cyber risks.

**Why it matters:** Financial institutions adopting increasingly autonomous AI systems face a combined technology, cyber and operational-resilience challenge rather than an AI-only governance problem.
