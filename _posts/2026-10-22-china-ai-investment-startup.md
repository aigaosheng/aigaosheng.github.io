# AI Investment & Startup in China Brief: DeepSeek Reportedly Raises $15 Billion Round

**Today:** One story cleared the filters since our last edition, a reported 100 billion yuan raise by DeepSeek that matters to anyone tracking how China's AI labs are funded.

## Top Stories

### 1. 📊 **DeepSeek Reportedly Raises About $15 Billion in One of China's Largest Rounds**
*TechCrunch · October 22, 2026*

**Bottom line:** DeepSeek has reportedly raised roughly 100 billion yuan, about $15 billion, with Chinese industrial and internet groups among the backers.

TechCrunch, summarising Bloomberg's reporting, says the Hangzhou-based lab raised the money just two days after the US ordered federal civilian agencies to remove its technology over security concerns. The report names battery maker CATL and Tencent among the potential investors. It describes the proceeds as funding for AI infrastructure and open models for domestic and international markets. The round follows a June raise of more than $7 billion at a $50 billion valuation, and TechCrunch notes earlier reports of a potential China IPO as early as next year. DeepSeek did not immediately comment, and the report does not give a valuation for this round.

**Why it matters:** The size of the round, and the presence of strategic backers from outside traditional venture capital, show that Chinese AI labs can fund frontier-scale spending domestically even under US restrictions. For Asia-based fintech and enterprise buyers, the practical question is how a lab with this much capital, and an eventual listing, will compete on open models and pricing. The US government action also signals that sourcing from Chinese AI vendors carries policy risk in some jurisdictions. Sourcing and governance decisions of that kind should be reviewed with your compliance and risk teams.

## Key Takeaways
- Round sizes are climbing fast: DeepSeek went from a raise above $7 billion in June to a reported $15 billion now, while Moonshot AI secured $2 billion in May and Manus raised more than $500 million this month, according to the same report.
- Capital is coming from strategic corporate backers such as CATL and Tencent, not only from venture funds.
- Public markets are the next stage: DeepSeek has been linked to a possible China IPO, and Zhipu AI and MiniMax, listed in Hong Kong in January, had market capitalisations of roughly $29 billion and $7.2 billion, respectively, at the time of the report.

## What to Watch
Confirmation of the final close, the full investor list and the valuation, none of which the initial report settled. DeepSeek's reported IPO preparations are the longer-term signal.
