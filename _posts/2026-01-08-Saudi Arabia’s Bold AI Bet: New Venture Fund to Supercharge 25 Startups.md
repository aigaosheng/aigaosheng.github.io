**Saudi Arabia’s Bold AI Bet: New Venture Fund to Supercharge 25 Startups**

Saudi Arabia is stepping up its push into the global artificial intelligence economy with the launch of a pioneering **AI-focused venture fund** designed to back early and growth-stage startups that either *use AI as a core business driver* or are *AI-native enterprises*. The initiative, a strategic partnership between **Red Sea Global (RSG)** and **Bunat Ventures Limited (Bunat VC)**, aims to **support around 25 AI startups over the next three years** — bringing not just capital but real-world testing opportunities to promising technology companies. ([redseaglobal.com][1])

At its core, the fund is more than just a financing vehicle. Startups that join the portfolio will gain **access to RSG’s operational environments** — including its luxury tourism destinations like *The Red Sea* and *AMAALA*, as well as partner infrastructure — which can act as *“living labs”* for piloting and validating AI technologies in everyday settings. This combination of money and real-world application sets this initiative apart from traditional capital infusions by enhancing product-market fit and reducing time-to-market hurdles for founders. ([Middle East AI News][2])

**What Makes This Fund Strategic**
Saudi Arabia has been widely increasing its investment into tech and innovation as part of broader efforts to diversify its economy beyond oil — notably through initiatives like Humain (the state-backed AI company) and large external AI funding commitments. The launch of this dedicated AI startup fund complements those efforts by seeding the local ecosystem with talent, tech, and commercial validation opportunities. ([Wikipedia][3])

This fund also reflects a broader shift in how venture capital is deployed in the region: *capital tied to infrastructure and operational access* (e.g., tourism or hospitality environments) rather than pure financial bets. That could make Saudi Arabia an increasingly attractive ecosystem for **AI startups looking for both funding and practical environments to scale quickly.** ([redseaglobal.com][1])

**Why It Matters**

* 📈 **Startup acceleration:** By combining funding with real-world testing spaces, the fund tackles two perennial startup challenges — *access to capital* and *access to customers/test environments* — in one package. ([Middle East AI News][2])
* 🌍 **Ecosystem building:** Helping cultivate local AI talent, retain Saudi founders, and draw international startups into the Kingdom’s innovation landscape. ([Arab News][4])
* 💡 **Economic diversification:** Aligns with Saudi Vision 2030 goals to evolve from oil dependence toward knowledge-based industries, especially in tech and AI. ([Wikipedia][3])

### 📘 Glossary

* **AI-native**: A company whose core product or business model fundamentally depends on artificial intelligence technologies — not merely using AI as an add-on feature.
* **Pre-seed / Growth-stage**: Early startup stages. *Pre-seed* refers to the very first investments to validate an idea, while *growth* relates to funding that helps scale after initial proof of concept.
* **Living Lab**: A real-world environment where products, services, and technologies can be tested with actual users and operational challenges before full market launch.
