# AI Investment & Startup in China Brief: MiniMax and Z.ai Shed $150 Billion From Peaks

**Today:** Listed Chinese AI model developers are still losing ground in public markets, which matters if you track China AI valuations, vendor risk or the listing pipeline.

## Top Stories

### 1. 📊 **China AI Model Stocks Lose $150 Billion From Peaks, With No Relief Yet**
*Bloomberg · October 23, 2026*

**Bottom line:** The combined market value of MiniMax and Z.ai is $150 billion below their highs, and Bloomberg sees no sign the selloff is easing.

Bloomberg reports that, as of Thursday's close, MiniMax and Z.ai (also known as Zhipu) were together worth $150 billion less than at their respective peaks. It calls the slide a sharp reversal from the heavily anticipated market debuts of both companies in January. Bloomberg cites heated competition and the threat of rival listings next year as pressures on the shares.

**Why it matters:** These two stocks are the main public benchmark for valuing Chinese AI model developers. A prolonged slide could make late-stage private rounds and planned listings harder to price, and enterprises building on these models may want to track the providers' funding position.

### 2. 📊 **MiniMax Falls Below Its Hong Kong IPO Price as Sector Selloff Deepens**
*Caixin Global · October 23, 2026*

**Bottom line:** MiniMax, which went public in Hong Kong, now trades below its IPO price after falling about 88% from its March peak.

Caixin reports that MiniMax shares dropped below their IPO price on Thursday, extending a retreat from a March 18 high of HK$1,330. It ties the move to intensifying competition on model pricing and a broader technology selloff. Investors are questioning whether AI spending is sustainable and whether Chinese developers can keep pace with new releases and price cuts from US rivals.

**Why it matters:** A listed AI developer trading under its issue price is a visible test of pricing power for Chinese model providers. In our read, falling model prices help enterprise buyers on cost, but the margin and funding pressure on vendors is worth watching for anyone with a long-term dependency.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2026-10-23/minimax-shares-fall-below-ipo-price-amid-ai-sector-sell-off-102496214.html)

## Key Takeaways
- The signal today is public-market repricing: the two listed Chinese model developers have given back a large share of their post-IPO gains.
- Both reports point to competition, covering model pricing, release cadence and US rivals' price cuts, as a main pressure on valuations.
- Rival AI listings expected next year are now part of the valuation debate, not just a backdrop.

## What to Watch
Whether MiniMax and Z.ai stabilize or keep sliding as investors weigh the prospect of rival AI listings next year, as Bloomberg describes. Neither source gives a dated catalyst.
