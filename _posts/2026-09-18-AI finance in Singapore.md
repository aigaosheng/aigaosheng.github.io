### 2. **Partior and LSEG DiSH develop 24/7 multi-bank liquidity framework**

* **Source**: TechNode Global · 18 September 2026
* **Summary**: Singapore-headquartered Partior and LSEG Digital Settlement House are developing a multi-settlement-bank liquidity framework for round-the-clock cross-border payments. The model combines Partior's multi-currency clearing and settlement network with LSEG DiSH trust accounts, allowing settlement liquidity to move across participating banks and currencies. Testing is underway, with production and additional bank onboarding targeted from Q1 2027.
* **Why It Matters**: Real-time payment messaging increasingly needs equally flexible settlement liquidity. The initiative could reduce reliance on fragmented pre-funded correspondent arrangements and strengthen Singapore's position in next-generation cross-border financial infrastructure.
* **URL**: [https://technode.global/2026/09/18/partior-lseg-dish-24-7-multi-bank-liquidity-framework/](https://technode.global/2026/09/18/partior-lseg-dish-24-7-multi-bank-liquidity-framework/)
