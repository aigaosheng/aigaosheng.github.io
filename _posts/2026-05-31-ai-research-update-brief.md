### 1. MIT’s MeMo lets teams update LLM knowledge without retraining
- **VentureBeat** · 2026-05-30
- **Summary**: VentureBeat reported on MeMo, a framework from MIT-affiliated and collaborating researchers that stores new knowledge in a dedicated smaller memory model rather than retraining the base LLM. The reported result is a modular approach that can work with open- or closed-model stacks and improve performance on knowledge updates while avoiding the cost and instability of full fine-tuning. The article was originally published at 12:28 pm PT on 2026-05-29, which falls within the required 48-hour window.
- **Why It Matters**: Continuous knowledge updating remains a major bottleneck for production AI systems. A practical modular-memory approach could materially reduce serving and maintenance costs for enterprise LLM deployments.
