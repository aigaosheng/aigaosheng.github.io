**In-Depth Highlight**
The launch of 'FrauDfense Check' by BBVA, Santander, and CaixaBank marks a pivotal shift from siloed to collaborative defense against financial crime. By securely sharing fraud data, they are building a collective shield that is more effective than any individual system. This model is not just about immediate risk prevention; it's a proactive compliance blueprint for the upcoming EU Payment Services Regulation, signaling a new era of industry-wide cooperation to safeguard the ecosystem.

**Market Insight**
This week underscores the rise of **Agentic AI** and a geographical talent shift. As AI moves to autonomous workflows, the challenge of regulation takes center stage. Simultaneously, the launch of Dharsi.ai in Dubai highlights how the Middle East is aggressively competing with traditional hubs to attract top AI talent and foster next-generation fintech innovation.
