# Payments Brief: Ripple Wins Final OCC Approval for Trust Bank

**Today:** Stablecoins moved further into regulated banking infrastructure while PayPal and Euronet showed how uneven growth is across checkout and cross-border remittances.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🏦 **Ripple Secures Final OCC Approval for Its National Trust Bank**
*Bloomberg · October 27, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Ripple now has full US federal approval for a trust bank to hold the reserves behind its RLUSD stablecoin.

Ripple said the US Office of the Comptroller of the Currency granted final approval to establish Ripple National Trust Bank. This completes a process that began with conditional approval last December. Once open, the bank will be authorised to manage the reserves backing Ripple USD (RLUSD) and to provide fiduciary custody of digital assets.

**Why it matters:** Federal supervision of reserves is becoming the entry ticket for stablecoins that want bank and corporate treasury business. Asian banks and payment firms evaluating USD stablecoins for settlement now have another federally overseen reserve structure to assess.

### 2. 💳 **ACI Worldwide and Circle Pilot USDC Settlement on Colombia's Instant Payments Rails**
*ACI Worldwide · October 27, 2026*

**Bottom line:** A major payments software vendor is packaging stablecoin settlement as an add-on to existing bank infrastructure, not a replacement for it.

The collaboration combines ACI Connetic, ACI's cloud-native payments orchestration platform, with Circle's USDC infrastructure. The aim is to show how banks can run stablecoin settlement alongside traditional rails. The first implementation is in Colombia, where ACI built the central infrastructure for the Bre-B instant payments system. ACI says Connetic will also support other networks and digital asset providers, not only USDC.

**Why it matters:** An orchestration-layer approach lets banks and BaaS providers test stablecoin settlement inside their existing treasury, governance and operations processes. That lowers integration cost and risk compared with building separate crypto stacks. The model could carry over to the other real-time schemes ACI supports.

### 3. 📊 **PayPal Raises 2026 Profit Forecast Despite Revenue Shortfall**
*Quartz · October 27, 2026*

**Bottom line:** PayPal's turnaround is lifting earnings, but revenue growth remains in low single digits.

- **Revenue:** Third-quarter net revenue rose 2% to USD 8.6 billion, below analyst expectations.
- **Transaction margin dollars:** Up 2% to USD 4.0 billion.
- **Earnings per share:** Non-GAAP EPS rose 8% to USD 1.45. GAAP EPS fell 4% to USD 1.25.
- **Volume and accounts:** Total payment volume grew 6% to USD 487.1 billion (7% currency-neutral). Active accounts rose 0.6% to 440 million.
- **Guidance:** PayPal lifted full-year adjusted EPS guidance to about USD 5.60, from USD 5.38.
- **Cost savings:** The company says it is on track for USD 700 million in gross run-rate savings by end-2026.

**Why it matters:** Volume is growing faster than revenue, which points to continued pricing pressure in online checkout. EPS growth driven by cost cuts has limits. Merchants and partners in Asia should watch whether PayPal reinvests in branded checkout or keeps prioritising margin.

### 4. 🌐 **Euronet Cuts 2026 Outlook as US Remittance Corridors Weaken**
*GlobeNewswire (Euronet Worldwide) · October 27, 2026*

**Bottom line:** US immigration policy is now a measurable drag on remittance economics, even as digital channels keep growing.

- **Group results:** Third-quarter revenue rose 1% to USD 1,161.1 million.
- **Cross-Border Payments segment:** Revenue fell 5% to USD 431.0 million and operating income fell 37% to USD 37.3 million.
- **Digital transactions:** Up 34% to 8.1 million within the segment.
- **Revised 2026 outlook:**
  - about USD 4.375 billion of revenue
  - about USD 725 million of adjusted EBITDA
  - adjusted EPS growth of about 10%
- **Reasons given:** Immigration-related pressure in several key corridors, wider economic pressure, and FX changes.
- **Separately:** Euronet signed a letter of intent with Diebold Nixdorf to work together on ATM networks.

**Why it matters:** Receive markets that depend heavily on US outbound remittances, such as the Philippines, face corridor risk that pricing alone cannot offset. Digital remittance growth is the main counterweight for incumbent money transfer operators.

### 5. 💳 **BlackRock Joins SBI and Startale to Link Tokenised Funds With Yen Stablecoin JPYSC**
*crypto.news · October 27, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Japan's trust-bank-issued yen stablecoin is being positioned as the cash leg for tokenised money market funds, with BlackRock now exploring the link.

BlackRock Financial Management and BlackRock Japan signed a memorandum of understanding with SBI Holdings and Startale Group. Under it, they will study how tokenised money market funds could connect with JPYSC. The work covers technical infrastructure, distribution and regulatory requirements. No specific fund, blockchain, timetable or commercial launch has been announced. JPYSC is issued through SBI Shinsei Trust Bank and distributed by SBI VC Trade.

**Why it matters:** The emerging template for on-chain treasury in Asia pairs a regulated stablecoin with tokenised money market funds. BlackRock's involvement lends weight to Japan's framework at a time when Singapore and Hong Kong are also building stablecoin regimes.

### 6. 📊 **RedotPay Weighs Confidential US IPO Filing by Year-End**
*crypto.news · October 27, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Hong Kong's stablecoin card issuer is moving back towards a US listing, which will test public-market appetite for crypto payment firms.

Citing an October 26 Bloomberg report, crypto.news says RedotPay is considering a confidential SEC filing before end-2026. It is also discussing a pre-IPO funding round that could value the company at USD 4–5 billion. JPMorgan, Goldman Sachs, Jefferies and Barclays are reportedly involved. People familiar with the business put customers at more than 9 million and annualised revenue above USD 180 million; RedotPay has not published audited figures to confirm this. A lawsuit by Binance affiliates seeking nearly USD 473 million remains unresolved, and RedotPay denies the claims.

**Why it matters:** Stablecoin-linked cards are one of the fastest-growing issuing segments in Asia. A prospectus would give issuers, BIN sponsors and regulators their first detailed public view of the model's economics and compliance controls.

## Key Takeaways
- Stablecoins are being placed inside familiar regulated structures: a federal trust charter (Ripple), trust-bank issuance (JPYSC) and bank orchestration platforms (ACI). This is lowering the barrier for banks to take part.
- Incumbent payment firms are relying on cost cuts and digital channels to offset weak core growth. Remittance volumes are now exposed to US policy risk, not just competition.
- Capital is returning to stablecoin payment firms, but licensing and litigation remain the gating factors for listings.

## What to Watch
Visa reports fiscal Q4 and full-year 2026 results after the US market close on October 27 (early October 28 SGT). Mastercard follows with Q3 results on October 29. Watch cross-border volume trends and any stablecoin or agentic payment metrics.
