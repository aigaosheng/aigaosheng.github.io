# FintechNews.sg – Weekly Newsletter

**Period: 7 March 2026 – 14 March 2026**
**Source:** https://fintechnews.sg | **Published:** 14 March 2026

---

## 📝 Executive Summary

Agentic AI payments moved from pilot to live transaction this week, with Mastercard completing Singapore's first authenticated AI agent-initiated payment in partnership with DBS and UOB. Simultaneously, Mastercard expanded its agentic commerce suite with the Virtual C-Suite tool for SMEs and rolled out a cross-border payments platform for APAC banks — cementing its position as the week's most active player across AI, SME finance, and crypto with its new 85-firm Crypto Partner Program.

Stablecoins attracted the week's largest funding deal: KAST secured US$80 million in Series A funding to scale its stablecoin banking platform globally, while MetaComp raised US$35 million Pre-A+ for its Web2.5 unified payments and wealth management offering. The Money20/20 Asia APAC Fintech report also flagged stablecoins as a growing institutional priority as regulatory clarity improves across the region.

Significant M&A activity reshaped Southeast Asia's digital banking landscape. Kredivo Group (Indonesia) acquired Vietnam-based Timo digital bank, marking one of the year's most notable cross-border consolidation moves in the region's fintech sector. Concurrently, EBANX confirmed it will open its APAC headquarters in Singapore on 24 March, following its MAS Major Payment Institution licence — reinforcing the city-state's role as the region's payments hub of choice.

Infrastructure and payments technology saw active investment: BPC's SmartVista went live at BIDC in Cambodia; OpenWay expanded its Way4 platform to support UnionPay products for BigPay; Visa launched its Intelligent Authorisation tool for acquirers; SUNRATE rebranded its China entity to SUNRATE Pay post-regulatory approval; and Thredd reshuffled leadership ahead of a credit product launch. DBS extended its SecureFX FX rate-locking tool to all corporate customers in Singapore.

The human side of the industry was in focus too: Chubb appointed Seshadri Iyer as EVP for digital transformation, Thunes made two senior hires, and FintechNews.sg published its 2026 Top Women in Fintech and Top Women in Banking in Asia features. Thailand was confirmed as ASEAN's digital payments leader in new market analysis, while Alteryx crossing US$1 billion in ARR underscored accelerating enterprise AI adoption in finance.

## 📈 Key Trends & Insights

- **Agentic commerce is moving to live production in Singapore.** Mastercard's authenticated AI-agent payment with DBS and UOB signals that Singapore's financial institutions are rapidly building the trust and infrastructure layer for AI-initiated transactions. Operators should assess readiness for agentic payment flows within 12–18 months.

- **Stablecoins are attracting institutional capital at scale.** KAST's US$80M raise and the Money20/20 Asia APAC report both confirm that clearer regulation is unlocking serious investment into stablecoin banking rails. Platforms without a stablecoin strategy may find themselves structurally disadvantaged within 24 months.

- **Southeast Asian fintech consolidation is accelerating.** Kredivo's acquisition of Timo is symptomatic of a maturing ecosystem where stronger players absorb smaller digital banks to gain cross-border footholds. Expect more M&A activity across Indonesia, Vietnam, and the Philippines as growth-stage fintechs seek regional scale.

- **Singapore doubles down as the APAC fintech gateway.** EBANX's APAC HQ announcement, MetaComp's US$35M raise, and Mastercard's active product launches all point to Singapore's continued magnetism for global fintechs seeking APAC regulatory anchoring and talent access.

- **AI in finance has crossed the billion-dollar ARR threshold.** Alteryx surpassing US$1B in ARR is a data point that enterprise AI adoption in financial workflows is no longer speculative — it is core infrastructure. The pace of AI tooling adoption in compliance, reconciliation, and analytics will intensify through 2026.

- **Cross-border SME payments remain a major unsolved problem.** Mastercard's dedicated APAC SME cross-border payments suite addresses a persistent gap — SMEs in the region continue to face high costs and slow settlement. This is a clear white space for embedded finance and BaaS platforms.

- **Thailand emerges as ASEAN's digital payments benchmark.** New data confirms Thailand as the region's leader in cashless payment transformation, driven by PromptPay adoption and government-led digital economy policies — making it a reference market for regulators and operators across the bloc.

## 🇸🇬 Singapore

### AI in Finance / Payments

- **Mastercard Completes First Live AI Agent Payment in Singapore With DBS and UOB** (March 4, 2026)
  Mastercard completed its first live, authenticated AI agent-initiated payment transaction in Singapore with DBS and UOB, using its Agent Pay framework. An AI agent booked a ride to Changi Airport via mobility provider hoppa, with each transaction secured by a unique agentic token and Mastercard Payment Passkeys.
