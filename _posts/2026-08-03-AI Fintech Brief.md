### 1. **New York Fed: AI Investment Boom Does Not Yet Constitute a Financial Bubble**

* **Source**: Reuters · 2026-08-03
* **Summary**: New York Federal Reserve President John Williams said current enthusiasm surrounding artificial intelligence reflects genuine productivity expectations rather than speculative excess. While acknowledging increasing leverage in AI investments, he stated that financial stability risks remain manageable and the Federal Reserve continues to monitor developments closely.
* **Why It Matters**: The comments provide an important signal for investors and financial institutions deploying significant capital into AI infrastructure, fintech, and enterprise automation, suggesting regulators currently view AI-driven investment as fundamentally supported rather than systemically dangerous.
