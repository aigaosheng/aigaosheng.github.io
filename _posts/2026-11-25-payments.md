# Payments Brief: SBI and SMFG Activate Solana Venture

**Today:** Banks and payment providers are advancing new rails for stablecoins, round-the-clock settlement and agent-led transactions, with several developments tied to Asia.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🌐 **SBI and SMFG Activate Solana Venture**

*SBI Holdings · November 25, 2026*

**Bottom line:** SBI Holdings, SMFG and the Solana Foundation have completed a new joint venture structure and started full operations as SBI Solana Global.

The venture plans to develop domestic and cross-border payment and remittance infrastructure using stablecoins, alongside tokenised assets and on-chain asset management. Its stated payment plans also include exploring micropayments by AI agents.

**Why it matters:** Japan’s financial groups now have an operating vehicle to pursue on-chain payment infrastructure across Asia. The announcement sets out business plans, not a live payment service.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.sbigroup.co.jp/english/news/pdf/2026/1125_a_en.pdf)

---

### 2. 🤖 **NETS and Ant Explore Agentic Payments**

*The Paypers · November 25, 2026*

**Bottom line:** NETS and Ant International are exploring how AI agents could pay through NETS wallets and merchants in Singapore.

Under their MoU, NETS plans to join Ant International’s Agentic Mobile Protocol, while the companies explore agent-authorised payments and deployment within NETS’ merchant acquiring infrastructure. They are also considering interoperability between their Know-Your-Agent frameworks.

**Why it matters:** The work could connect agent payment controls to a national payment rail and a broad merchant network. The proposals remain exploratory, so implementation and adoption are the next signals to watch.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://thepaypers.com/payments/news/nets-and-ant-international-partner-on-agentic-payments-in-singapore)

---

### 3. 🌐 **Ant and BNY Explore 24/7 FX and Payments**

*Business Wire · November 25, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Ant International and BNY signed an MoU to explore round-the-clock FX, real-time treasury and interoperable global payments.

The companies said possible areas include tokenised deposits and money market funds, as well as links between tokenised and traditional funds. Any work on those areas remains subject to internal and regulatory approvals.

**Why it matters:** The collaboration brings Ant’s real-time treasury capabilities together with BNY’s institutional cash management reach. For corporate treasurers, the strategic question is whether these discussions can make liquidity available across more hours and payment formats.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261124860461/en/)

---

### 4. 💳 **Antom Applies PayNow Deep Links to Lazada**

*The Paypers · November 25, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Antom’s PayNow deep-linking sends shoppers to their banking app with payment details prefilled, then returns them to the merchant.

The Paypers reports that Lazada saw about a 9% increase in successful transactions after implementation. Antom and its subsidiary 2C2P are also exploring a GovWallet use case with GovTech Singapore under the PayNow Gen2 initiative.

**Why it matters:** The reported conversion lift points to the value of reducing checkout friction on instant-payment rails. More PayNow Gen2 use cases could extend those gains into both ecommerce and digital public services.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://thepaypers.com/payments/news/antom-introduces-paynow-deep-linking-for-lazada-under-paynow-gen2)

### 5. 🌐 **OSL and M-DAQ Plan ASEAN Payments Collaboration**

*OSL · November 25, 2026*

**Bottom line:** OSL and M-DAQ plan to explore combining stablecoin infrastructure with foreign exchange and local payment capabilities.

The proposed collaboration would pair M-DAQ’s FX conversion and local payment coverage across G10 and ASEAN markets with OSL’s stablecoin infrastructure. The companies said the arrangement is intended to support cross-border operations and fiat-to-stablecoin conversion.

**Why it matters:** Linking regional payment access with stablecoin conversion could give businesses another way to manage cross-border flows. The announcement describes an intention to explore the collaboration, not a launched service.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.osl.com/en/newsroom/osl-and-m-daq-plan-collaboration-to-advance-cross-border-financial-connectivity)

### 6. 💳 **Huawei and CMA Launch National Payment Solution**

*PR Newswire · November 25, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Huawei and CMA Small Systems have launched an integrated payment systems solution for central banks and financial institutions.

The offering combines CMA’s payment software with Huawei’s computing, storage, networking and cloud infrastructure. It supports high-availability and disaster-recovery deployments, with phased implementation and expansion options.

**Why it matters:** The package targets institutions modernising critical payment infrastructure, where resilience and continuity are core requirements. The companies have not named customer deployments or markets adopting the solution.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/huawei-and-cma-jointly-launch-national-payment-solution-to-accelerate-global-payments-transformation-302950220.html)

### 7. 🤖 **LianLian XCard Enables Agent-Initiated Spending**

*PR Newswire · November 25, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Lianlian DigiTech’s XCard lets authorised AI agents initiate business card payments within preset limits.

The product combines multi-currency payments and expense management for global businesses. Lianlian says agents can use one-time payment tokens and dynamic security credentials, while businesses set transaction permissions and budgets.

**Why it matters:** Agent spending depends on controls that connect identity, authorisation and payment credentials. The card brings those controls into a corporate spending product, though the release does not establish the scale of current usage.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/lianlian-digitech-launches-lianlian-xcard-closing-the-loop-between-ai-task-execution-and-agentic-payments-302950355.html)

### 8. 🤖 **Natural Launches Card Acceptance for AI Agents**

*The Paypers · November 25, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Natural Accept gives businesses tools to collect card payments through checkout links, embedded checkout or phone calls.

The product tokenises card details within Natural’s payment environment and supports refunds, disputes and payment tracking. Natural says collected funds settle into its wallet, where agents can use authorised balances for subsequent payments.

**Why it matters:** The product targets the payment handoff that can interrupt agent-run business workflows. Combining collections and disbursements in one ledger may simplify reconciliation, subject to adoption and operational performance.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://thepaypers.com/payments/news/natural-launches-natural-accept-for-agent-led-card-payments)

### 9. 💳 **Mastercard and areeba Launch Lebanon Travel Payments**

*The Paypers · November 25, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Mastercard and Lebanon-based areeba have launched a virtual card programme for business-to-business travel payments.

Travel companies can use virtual cards issued for individual bookings to pay hotels, airlines and other suppliers. The programme is designed to digitise supplier payments and provide more visibility over transactions associated with each booking.

**Why it matters:** The launch extends virtual card infrastructure into travel supplier payments in Lebanon and the wider Levant. Adoption by travel businesses will show whether the model can reduce manual payment processes and improve spend tracking.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://thepaypers.com/payments/news/mastercard-and-areeba-launch-b2b-travel-payments-programme-in-lebanon)

### 10. 📊 **Mercuryo Reports Turkish Stablecoin On-Ramp Growth**

*PR Newswire · November 25, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Mercuryo says its Turkish on-ramp volume rose 141% in 2026 through November 19, with stablecoins making up 74% of volume.

The company says stablecoins accounted for 62% of Turkish on-ramp volume in 2025, while repeat buyers represented 94% of this year’s volume. Mercuryo and MetaMask also announced a fee-waiver campaign for eligible new users in Turkey.

**Why it matters:** The figures point to strong stablecoin demand on one provider’s platform, but do not represent the whole Turkish market. The campaign may offer a further signal of how lower entry fees affect card-funded on-ramp activity.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/turkey-on-ramp-volume-more-than-doubles-in-2026-as-market-outperforms-according-to-mercuryo-data-302950382.html)

## Key Takeaways

- Banks and payment firms are exploring how stablecoins and tokenised deposits could support payments and treasury beyond conventional operating hours.
- Agentic payment proposals increasingly pair transaction permissions with identity and credential controls.
- Asia-focused payment development spans both new digital rails and improvements to existing QR, card and national payment infrastructure.

## What to Watch

Track whether the SBI Solana Global venture moves from stated plans to launched payment services, and whether the Ant–BNY and OSL–M-DAQ explorations produce concrete products or corridors. For agentic commerce, watch for implementation details and merchant availability from NETS, Ant International and Lianlian.
