### 1. Crypto Critic Nouriel Roubini Launches Tokenized ETF
- **FA Mag** · 2026-06-23
- **Summary**: Nouriel Roubini, the economist famous for his harsh criticism of cryptocurrencies, is launching a digital token called USAFi.
