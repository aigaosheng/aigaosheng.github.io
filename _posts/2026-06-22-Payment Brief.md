### 1. Avalanche Launches Institutional Payments Collective With 28 Financial Giants
- **AInvest** · 2026-06-21
- **Summary**: Avalanche has formally established the Avalanche Payments Collective, a consortium of 28 organizations including Franklin Templeton, VanEck, and Paxos. The initiative aims to unify stablecoin issuance, custody, and settlement into a single, institutional-grade network for cross-border transactions across 150+ countries and 96 currencies .
- **Why It Matters**: This represents a major shift from blockchain as a trading platform to a comprehensive financial services base. By formalizing an ecosystem that includes asset managers and payment processors, Avalanche is positioning itself as a direct competitor to traditional correspondent banking networks for enterprise treasury and settlement operations.

### 6. South Korea's FSC Overhauls Sandbox to Grant Exclusive Rights at Designation
- **The Asia Business Daily** · 2026-06-21
- **Summary**: South Korea's Financial Services Commission (FSC) announced major reforms to its fintech sandbox. The key change is granting "exclusive operation rights" immediately upon sandbox designation (previously only after formal licensing). The FSC also expanded commercialization cost support and relaxed initial financial soundness reviews .
- **Why It Matters**: This is a significant regulatory shift for fintechs globally. By granting exclusive rights at the sandbox stage, the FSC provides crucial regulatory certainty and a competitive moat for early innovators, potentially turning South Korea into a more attractive environment for fintech startups and investment.
