### 1. Zhipu AI's Market Cap Surpasses HK$1 Trillion, Outpacing Major Tech Giants
- **Sohu** · 2026-06-22
- **Summary**: Chinese AI unicorn Zhipu AI saw its market capitalization surpass HK$1 trillion, making it the first AI company in Hong Kong's history to reach this milestone and overtaking traditional tech behemoths like Xiaomi, Meituan, and Baidu. The rally follows the open-source release of its flagship GLM-5.2 model, which has topped global leaderboards for its "long-horizon task" capabilities. - **Why It Matters**: This valuation marks a pivotal moment where the market is ascribing leading-company status to a domestic AI firm, signaling a shift in investor confidence from consumer internet to foundational AI. The combination of GLM-5.2's performance and its full compatibility with domestic AI chips (like Huawei Ascend) is establishing a viable, sovereign AI ecosystem that could compete for global users seeking alternatives to US models.
