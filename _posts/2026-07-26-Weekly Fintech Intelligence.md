# 2. In-Depth Highlight

## **Ant International secures US$1.2 billion to accelerate global payments**

Ant International announced a US$1.2 billion Series A funding round supported by Ant Group and Alibaba Group. The financing strengthens the company's position as one of the world's largest digital payment infrastructure providers and provides additional resources for expanding cross-border commerce, treasury services, and AI-enabled financial products. The investment arrives as global payment providers compete to build interoperable payment networks connecting merchants, banks, and digital wallets across multiple jurisdictions. It also highlights that investors remain willing to commit significant capital to profitable fintech infrastructure companies despite a more disciplined venture funding environment. For regulators and financial institutions, Ant's continued expansion reinforces the growing importance of cross-border payment interoperability and digital financial infrastructure. ([FinTech Futures][2])

# 3. Market & Industry Insight

Artificial intelligence continues to dominate strategic investment across financial services. Rather than focusing solely on customer-facing chatbots, banks are increasingly deploying AI to improve software development, compliance, fraud detection, operational efficiency, and employee productivity. Deutsche Bank's AI hackathon and Bank of America's expanding generative AI deployment illustrate this broader enterprise transformation. ([Finextra Research][1])

Payments infrastructure also remains one of fintech's strongest growth areas. Cross-border payment partnerships, venture funding for payment platforms, and trade finance acquisitions all point toward continued investment in modern financial infrastructure. According to Crunchbase data reported by Finextra, global fintech venture funding reached **US$28.6 billion during the first half of 2026**, representing approximately **23% year-over-year growth**, although investors remain increasingly selective about where capital is deployed. ([Finextra Research][3])
