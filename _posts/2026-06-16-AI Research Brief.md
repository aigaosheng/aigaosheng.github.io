### 2. Game-Changing Chip Cooling Cuts AI Data Center Power Use by 90%
- **Aju Press** · 2026-06-16
- **Summary**: KAIST researchers have developed a liquid cooling technique that etches microscopic water channels directly into chips, achieving a coefficient of performance (COP) over 100,000—ten times the previous world record. This allows data centers to use room-temperature water, reducing cooling power consumption by 90% without needing expensive materials.
- **Why It Matters**: As AI models grow, the energy cost of cooling is becoming a critical bottleneck. This breakthrough directly addresses the escalating power and operational costs of running large-scale AI infrastructure, enhancing sustainability and economic viability.

### 5. Pioneering Framework Enables Reliable LLM Use in Industrial Production Lines
- **Chinese Academy of Sciences** · 2026-06-16
- **Summary**: A research team from the Chinese Academy of Sciences’ Institute of Industrial AI won the Best Paper Award at ICRA 2026 for their "IMR-LLM Framework." This framework segments LLM task planning into two stages—first understanding and allocating tasks, then generating specific code—significantly reducing errors and enabling reliable use of LLMs in complex industrial settings, such as shipbuilding.
- **Why It Matters**: This addresses a critical barrier to AI adoption in manufacturing: reliability. ### 6. Recursive Language Models Offer Path to Rival Frontier AI Efficiency
- **Scientific American** · 2026-06-16
- **Summary**: MIT graduate student Alex L. - **Why It Matters**: RLMs offer a potential shortcut to high-performance AI that is less dependent on immense compute resources.
