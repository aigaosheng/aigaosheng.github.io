# Payments Brief: HSBC's RedCoin Stablecoin Reaches 50,000 Users

**Today:** Hong Kong FinTech Week produced a dense cluster of stablecoin, tokenised-deposit and agentic-payment announcements that show infrastructure moving from pilot to commercial use across major banks, card networks and payment processors.

## Top Stories

### 1. 💳 **HSBC's RedCoin Stablecoin Signs Up 50,000 Users in a Week**

*Fintech News Hong Kong · November 3, 2026*

**Bottom line:** HSBC's HKD-pegged RedCoin stablecoin attracted 50,000 users within roughly a week of launch, an early signal that bank-issued stablecoins can scale quickly once regulatory clarity is in place.

Group CEO Georges Elhedery disclosed the figure at Hong Kong FinTech Week x StartmeupHK 2026, noting RedCoin is currently available to PayMe clients, with HSBC and Hang Seng clients to follow. Elhedery said the bank is targeting "multi-million users" and credited the pace of adoption to operating "under the regulatory environment set by the HKMA."

**Why it matters:** RedCoin is one of only two stablecoins issued under Hong Kong's licensing regime, alongside Anchorpoint Financial's token; early traction at this scale gives other MAS- and HKMA-regulated banks a concrete adoption benchmark as Singapore finalises its own stablecoin rules.

### 2. 🌐 **Ant International Completes Cross-Bank Tokenised Deposit Transfers**

*Fintech News Hong Kong · November 3, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Ant International settled live Hong Kong dollar transfers with Bank of China (Hong Kong), China Construction Bank (Asia) and HSBC in under five minutes using central bank money under the HKMA's EnsembleTX programme.

The transactions, run between September and October 2026 on Ant's WhaleRTP blockchain settlement platform, cut reliance on pre-funding and gave participating banks round-the-clock liquidity access. Ant said the next phase pairs this rail with its FalconTST forecasting tool to support "agentic treasury," where AI systems execute cash-management decisions automatically.

**Why it matters:** Interbank tokenised-deposit settlement at commercial scale strengthens the case that tokenised deposits, not stablecoins, will carry mainstream wholesale payment volumes — a distinction regional treasurers and MAS-licensed institutions will need to track closely.

### 3. 🌐 **TenPay Global and UnionPay International Enable Instant WeChat Remittances**

*Fintech News Hong Kong · November 3, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Tencent's TenPay Global and UnionPay International signed an MoU to let money move both ways between TenPay Global's platform and UnionPay's partner network, landing in WeChat wallets within one minute, 24 hours a day.

The companies said identity verification and compliance screening complete in milliseconds under the new arrangement. TenPay Global CEO Wenhui Yang said the goal is to make "sending money as simple as sending a message."

**Why it matters:** Linking a card network's global reach to WeChat's remittance rails could meaningfully shorten settlement times for the large Chinese diaspora and SME corridors across Asia, intensifying competition for incumbent remittance providers.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://fintechnews.hk/41776/hong-kong-fintech-week-news/tenpay-global-upi-partnership-remittances/)

### 4. 💳 **Payment Asia Completes Live HKD Stablecoin Settlement With G+D China**

*Fintech News Hong Kong · November 3, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Payment Asia and G+D China signed an MoU on cold-wallet and agentic payment technology, alongside a completed B2B settlement in HKDAP, a Hong Kong dollar stablecoin, issued and redeemed through Anchorpoint Financial.

In the disclosed example, logistics partner ShipAny was paid in HKDAP for delivering point-of-sale terminals, with the token also used to cover staff bookings on Payment Asia's Flow platform. The MoU covers Hong Kong, Macao, Australia, New Zealand and Canada, and includes a card-based "TruSafe" cold wallet alongside new AI tools for merchant risk scoring.

**Why it matters:** A completed, operational stablecoin payment to a real supplier — rather than a pilot — is an early proof point that regulated HKD stablecoins can settle routine B2B invoices outside the banking rail.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://fintechnews.hk/41872/hong-kong-fintech-week-news/payment-asia-stablecoin-agentic-payments-gd-china/)

### 5. 💳 **Pine Labs and Stripe Launch Unified Commerce Platform in Singapore**

*Fintech News Singapore · November 4, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Pine Labs and Stripe will let eligible Singapore merchants manage online and in-store card payments through a single dashboard starting this month, with regional expansion planned for 2027.

The platform combines Stripe's online payment infrastructure with Pine Labs' in-store terminals, giving merchants a consolidated transaction view, multi-issuer instalment plans and cross-channel promotional tools. No merchant volume or financial terms were disclosed.

**Why it matters:** Unified online-and-offline acceptance addresses a long-standing pain point for enterprise retailers juggling separate vendors, and positions both companies to compete more directly for Southeast Asian omnichannel merchants once the platform expands regionally.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://fintechnews.sg/140521/payments/pine-labs-stripe-singapore-unified-commerce/)

### 6. 🤖 **Sunrate Launches Mastercard-Certified Agentic Card for AI-Led Payments**

*Fintech News Hong Kong · November 3, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Sunrate introduced an "Agentic Card," a multi-currency virtual card that lets AI agents execute pre-approved business payments within spending limits, merchant restrictions and validity periods set by the business.

Each card is tied to a specific task and expires automatically on completion, with all actions logged for audit. Sunrate said the product has completed Mastercard's Agent Pay certification for identity verification and transaction authorisation, targeting procurement, travel and supplier-payment use cases.

**Why it matters:** Card-network certification is a gating step for agentic commerce to move from demo to production; enterprises evaluating AI-driven procurement will watch whether controls and auditability hold up at scale.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://fintechnews.hk/41842/hong-kong-fintech-week-news/sunrate-agentic-card/)

### 7. 🌐 **Razorpay Secures IFSCA Licence for GIFT City Cross-Border Payments**

*Fintech News Singapore · November 4, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Razorpay obtained a Payment Service Provider licence from India's International Financial Services Centres Authority, enabling a single platform for brokers and wealth managers to handle outbound and inbound cross-border investment flows through GIFT City.

The company said platforms can go live in two to three weeks versus several months previously, with RBL Bank and ICICI Bank integrations already active. Indian residents' outbound overseas investment rose 56% year-on-year in FY26 to USD 2.65 billion, a flow regulators are steering through GIFT City.

**Why it matters:** Consolidating fragmented banking, compliance and remittance workflows into one licensed platform lowers the barrier for Indian cross-border investment activity, a trend regional payment infrastructure providers should monitor as a template for other jurisdictions.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://fintechnews.sg/140423/fintech-india/razorpay-gift-city-ifsca-psp-license/)

## Key Takeaways

- Hong Kong's EnsembleTX programme is generating multiple, separate commercial-scale tokenised-deposit transactions in the same week, reinforcing tokenised deposits as the near-term default for wholesale settlement over stablecoins.
- Card networks are now certifying agentic payment products (Sunrate's Mastercard Agent Pay certification), shifting agentic commerce from pilots toward auditable, production-grade controls.
- Regulated HKD and Hong Kong-licensed stablecoins (RedCoin, HKDAP) are already clearing real user and B2B volumes, giving MAS a live reference point as it finalises Singapore's stablecoin framework.

## What to Watch

Hong Kong FinTech Week runs through November 6, 2026, so further stablecoin, tokenised-deposit and agentic-payment announcements from banks and card networks are likely before the week closes.
