## 🧠 **2. In-Depth Highlight**

### **The Next Phase of Payments: Agentic AI Meets Digital Wallets and Real-Time Rails**

The fintech industry is entering a new era where agentic AI — autonomous software capable of executing complex financial actions with minimal human input — is reshaping payments and digital wallet experiences. As highlighted in FinTech Futures’ year-in-review report, 2025 saw innovation move beyond generative AI experimentation toward full-scale integration of AI agents into everyday financial workflows. ([FinTech Futures][1])

This trend is not just theoretical: companies like Citi are exploring how AI and digital assets can streamline cross-border payments and improve merchant acceptance, while start-ups such as those raising venture capital (e.g., AI startup Flex) demonstrate investor confidence in this space. ([FinTech Futures][2])

Agentic AI promises to automate onboarding, fraud monitoring, compliance, and the actual execution of payments through digital wallets and instant rails — reducing friction and operational cost for financial institutions and their customers alike. It also introduces new challenges for security, risk management, and regulatory oversight, making stakeholder collaboration essential. ([FinTech Futures][1])

As 2026 approaches, agentic commerce (the interaction between AI agents and payments infrastructure) is expected to accelerate, influencing not just product roadmaps but also how consumers and businesses interact with money daily.

## 📊 **3. Market & Industry Insight**

**AI’s Strategic Inflection Point in Fintech:**
2025 has marked a clear shift from standard AI applications (like chatbots and personalization engines) to *agentic AI* — systems that can autonomously complete multi-step tasks such as initiating payments, resolving disputes, or optimizing transaction workflows. Industry forecasts suggest that these technologies will form part of the core financial operating system by 2026, fundamentally altering how workflows are executed and reducing the need for manual intervention. ([FinTech Futures][6])

This new model benefits both enterprises and end-users: organizations reduce cost and error rates, while users enjoy faster, more responsive services. For example, AI systems integrated into digital wallets can proactively manage fraud flags, prioritize secure rails, and anticipate payment preferences, creating smoother commerce pathways.

**Digital Wallets and Instant Payments Keep Growing:**
Digital wallets continue to rise in prominence globally, driven by consumer demand for convenience and contactless experiences. Businesses are increasingly integrating wallets and instant account-to-account (A2A) capabilities to capture market share and enhance checkout conversion rates. ([Finextra Research][7])

Moreover, stablecoins and tokenised assets are expanding the boundaries of wallet functionality — enabling cheaper and faster cross-border settlement and potential new monetisation models for fintech platforms.
