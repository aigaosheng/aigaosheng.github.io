**Asia’s First Global AI Infrastructure Fund Goes On-Chain: $150M SKYD Token Bridges Web3 and Real-World Assets**

>
In a bold move that marries artificial intelligence with blockchain, Hong Kong’s Tykhe Capital and Malaysia’s Skyvast Digital have launched a **US$150 million** tokenized fund — giving investors direct access to the booming AI infrastructure space.

---

## The Big Picture

* **Who’s behind it:** The fund is a joint effort between Tykhe Capital Group (Hong Kong) and Skyvast Digital (Malaysia), via Pioneer Asset Management (Tykhe’s licensed subsidiary). ([Malay Mail][1])
* **What it is:** Branded as the **SKYD Token**, this is being billed as the world’s first global-grade AI infrastructure tokenized fund. ([Malay Mail][1])
* **How it works:** Rather than issuing traditional equity or debt, the token gives investors a **revenue-share**: they get a slice of 15% of Skyvast’s earnings from its AI infrastructure business. ([South China Morning Post][2])
* **Minimum entry:** Subscriptions start from just **US$1,000**, making what was once institutional-only exposure more accessible. ([Yahoo Finance][3])

---

## Why This Matters

1. **Liquidity + Real-World Exposure:** By tokenizing a revenue-share deal, the fund combines real-world infrastructure (like data centres and energy-linked AI campuses) with the liquidity and tradability advantages of blockchain-based tokens. ([Malay Mail][1])
2. **Bridging Web3 and Traditional Finance:** This isn’t pure crypto speculation — it’s a tokenized security under a regulated framework in Hong Kong. ([Malay Mail][1])
3. **Addressing an AI Bottleneck:** According to IDC data cited in the announcement, around 70% of ASEAN AI firms cite limited compute and infrastructure as a top blocker — even as demand in the region surges. ([Malay Mail][1])
4. **Risk Mitigation:** Skyvast’s infrastructure strategy is “multi-vendor,” which helps spread risk so that returns aren’t overly dependent on one hardware or energy supplier. ([TNGlobal][4])
5. **Regulatory Strength:** Hong Kong’s regulatory environment is positioning itself as a digital-finance hub, and this fund aligns with that broader fintech strategy. ([Yahoo Finance][3])

---

## Implications & Challenges

* **Democratizing AI Investing:** Lower barriers to entry mean more participants — not just big funds, but also smaller, tech-savvy investors — can gain exposure to AI infrastructure.
* **Market Validation for Tokenized Funds:** Success here could validate tokenization as a tool for real-asset investing, not just speculative crypto trades.
* **Regulatory Risk:** While Hong Kong’s framework is strong, tokenized assets still live in a relatively new regulatory frontier — risks around compliance, investor protection, and legal clarity remain.
* **Performance Dependency:** Returns depend on Skyvast’s actual infrastructure revenue. If AI demand slows, or if their operations underperform, token holders may feel the impact.
* **Secondary Liquidity:** One promise of tokenization is liquidity, but the actual viability of secondary trading (how many buyers/sellers, how active markets become) will be a key test.

---

## Glossary

* **Tokenized Fund:** An investment vehicle where shares are represented as digital tokens (on a blockchain) instead of traditional paper or digital certificates.
* **Revenue-Share Token:** A token that entitles the holder to a portion of future revenues from a project or business, rather than ownership of the company itself.
* **Web3:** A vision for a decentralized internet built on blockchain, digital assets, and smart contracts.
* **Real-World Assets (RWA):** Physical or traditional financial assets (like real estate, infrastructure, or revenue streams) that are represented in digital token form.
* **Security Token Offering (STO):** The issuance and sale of tokens that qualify as securities, typically regulated similarly to traditional securities.

---

## Bottom Line

The SKYD Token fund represents a pioneering step — not just in bringing AI infrastructure to blockchain investors, but in proving how regulated tokenization can unlock real-world value in a liquid, accessible way. If it succeeds, it could become a blueprint for how Web3 can fund the next generation of digital infrastructure.
