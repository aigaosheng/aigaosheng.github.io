**Tencent Unveils Hunyuan3D 3.0: A Leap Forward in 3D AI Generation**

At the 2025 Tencent Global Digital Ecosystem Conference, the company introduced Hunyuan3D 3.0, a model boasting triple the precision and enhanced realism for creators and developers.

Tencent has launched Hunyuan3D 3.0, an advanced AI model that triples geometric resolution and supports ultra-high-definition voxel modeling, marking a significant advancement in 3D generative AI capabilities.

On September 16, 2025, at the Tencent Global Digital Ecosystem Conference in Shenzhen, Tencent introduced Hunyuan3D 3.0, a model that enhances 3D generation precision by threefold, achieving a geometric resolution of 1536³ and supporting 3.6 billion voxels. ([ST Daily][1])

Hunyuan3D 3.0 incorporates the innovative 3D-DiT hierarchical sculpting model, significantly improving modeling accuracy and detail generation. ([Oeeee][2])

The model has been integrated into Tencent's Hunyuan3D AI creative engine, offering free access to users and available via Tencent Cloud API. ([Oeeee][2])

The model has been adopted by leading 3D printing companies like Tuozhu Technology and Chuangxiang 3D, enhancing modeling efficiency. ([ST Daily][1])

Hunyuan3D 3.0 is part of Tencent's broader strategy to expand its AI capabilities globally, with over 30 new models released in the past year and a commitment to open-source development. ([Oeeee][2])
