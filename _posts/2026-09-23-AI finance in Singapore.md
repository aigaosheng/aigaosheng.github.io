### 1. **Sea to Deploy NVIDIA Vera Rubin Across Monee, Shopee and Garena**

* **Source**: Sea · 23 September 2026
* **Summary**: Singapore-headquartered Sea announced that it will become the first enterprise in Southeast Asia to adopt NVIDIA's Vera Rubin platform, expanding its AI infrastructure for model and agent development. In financial services, Sea's Monee already applies transformer-based models to fraud detection and credit-risk assessment using behavioural and transactional data. Sea said recent enhancements increased credit approval rates by around 10% while maintaining a similar risk level.
* **Why It Matters**: AI infrastructure is moving deeper into Singapore's financial-services operating model, with credit underwriting and fraud prevention becoming direct beneficiaries of large-scale model deployment. The development also illustrates how regional digital platforms can combine commerce, payments and AI-generated financial intelligence.

### 2. **Singapore Positions AI Infrastructure as a Capital-and-Finance Opportunity**

* **Source**: The Business Times · 23 September 2026
* **Summary**: Singapore will focus on maximising the economic value generated from AI infrastructure rather than simply expanding computing capacity, Senior Minister of State Tan Kiat How said at the Asia-Pacific Finance Forum. He highlighted the need to connect compute, energy, capital and businesses across Southeast Asia rather than requiring every country to build a complete AI ecosystem. Singapore is also emphasising security, sustainability and efficient use of scarce data-centre resources.
* **Why It Matters**: For financial institutions, AI infrastructure is increasingly an investment, financing and risk-management issue rather than merely a technology budget. Singapore's positioning could strengthen its role as a regional hub connecting capital markets, banks, data centres and enterprise AI deployment.

### 6. **Singapore's AI Boom Becomes a Financial-Stability Variable**

* **Source**: MCI Group · 23 September 2026
* **Summary**: Singapore's financial system remains supported by easing borrowing costs, tighter credit spreads and continued bank-credit growth, according to the Monetary Authority of Singapore's 2026 Financial Stability Review. MAS also highlighted the two-sided impact of AI investment: AI-linked economies can benefit from stronger investment and exports, but are more exposed to a reversal in the AI cycle. Highly leveraged companies and businesses with thinner liquidity buffers remain more vulnerable to shocks.
* **Why It Matters**: AI is now sufficiently important to Singapore's economy that its investment cycle has become a financial-stability consideration. Banks, investors and corporate treasuries therefore need to consider both the productivity upside of AI spending and the financing, valuation and concentration risks created by an abrupt slowdown.
* **URL**: [https://mcigroup.my/mci-business/singapore-says-financial-system-remains-resilient-has-adequate-buffers-amid-global-uncertainty/](https://mcigroup.my/mci-business/singapore-says-financial-system-remains-resilient-has-adequate-buffers-amid-global-uncertainty/)
