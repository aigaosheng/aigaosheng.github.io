### 1. Turkey Seeks Entry into Europe's SEPA Payment Network
- **Source**: Reuters · 2026-07-01
- **Summary**: Turkey announced its interest in joining the Single Euro Payments Area (SEPA), with financial institutions actively working toward integration. SEPA membership would enable faster, lower-cost euro-denominated cross-border transfers across 41 participating countries.
- **Why It Matters**: If successful, Turkey's participation would significantly improve payment interoperability between Turkish banks and Europe, benefiting trade, remittances, and financial services.

- **Cross-border payments** remain a strategic priority, highlighted by Turkey's interest in joining SEPA.
- **Transit payments** continue expanding through open-loop contactless acceptance, with Taipei Metro adding JCB support.
- **Mobile money** remains a major growth engine across emerging markets, particularly in Africa.

- **Everyday digital payments** are expanding into urban mobility and municipal services, reinforcing consumer adoption of contactless and mobile wallets.
