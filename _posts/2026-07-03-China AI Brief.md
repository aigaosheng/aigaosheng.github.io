# China AI Brief — 2026-07-03

## Top Stories

### 1. Kling AI Raises US$2.8 Billion From Alibaba, Tencent and Baidu
- **Source**: Reuters · 2026-07-03
- **Summary**: Kuaishou's AI video platform Kling AI secured more than RMB19 billion (approximately US$2.8 billion) in new financing from Alibaba, Tencent and Baidu. The funding values Kling AI at roughly US$15 billion before the investment and provides significant capital for foundation model development and commercial expansion.
- **Why It Matters**: This is one of China's largest AI financing rounds of 2026 and demonstrates continued confidence from the country's leading technology companies in generative AI infrastructure.

### 2. Alibaba Restricts Internal Use of Anthropic's Claude Code
- **Source**: Reuters · 2026-07-03
- **Summary**: Alibaba has reportedly instructed employees to stop using Anthropic's Claude Code because of security and compliance concerns related to user-identification features. Staff are being directed toward Alibaba's internally developed coding assistant instead.
- **Why It Matters**: The decision highlights growing technological decoupling between Chinese and U.S. AI ecosystems while reinforcing investment in domestic developer tools.
- **URL**: https://www.reuters.com/world/china/alibaba-ban-claude-code-workplace-over-alleged-backdoor-risks-source-says-2026-07-03/
