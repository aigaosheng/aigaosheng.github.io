**Singapore’s Olea Secures $30M Series A, Backed by SC Ventures and Global Investors to Transform Trade Finance**

In a major vote of confidence for fintech innovation in Southeast Asia, Singapore-based trade finance platform **Olea** has just closed a **US $30 million Series A funding round**, led by Spanish banking giant **BBVA** and joined by key strategic investors including **SC Ventures**—the venture arm of Standard Chartered Bank—as well as XDC Network and others. ([Tech in Asia][1])

This fresh capital marks a pivotal moment for Olea, which launched in 2022 and has rapidly positioned itself as a platform that **bridges the gap between global liquidity and underserved trade networks**. Since inception, the company has facilitated **over US $3 billion in trade financing**—a strong signal of demand for scalable, tech-enabled solutions that help businesses secure funding across complex global supply chains. ([Tech in Asia][2])

### A Funding Round with a Strategic Cast

The Series A was **led by BBVA**, which brings not only capital but banking experience and global reach to support Olea’s expansion across Europe, the U.S., Latin America, and Asia. SC Ventures—**both an early incubator of Olea and a continued backer**—participated alongside XDC Network, a Layer-1 blockchain protocol aimed at enabling tokenized and stablecoin-enabled trade workflows. ([Tech in Asia][1])

With this backing, Olea signals a clear ambition: to leverage **AI, Web3 readiness, and embedded finance capabilities** that respond to evolving enterprise needs while expanding its origination footprint in high-growth markets. ([TNGlobal][3])

### Why This Matters

Trade finance—an essential part of global commerce—is often fragmented, slow, and reliant on traditional banking rails. Olea’s model brings **institutional governance, risk analytics, and digital infrastructure** to the table, helping suppliers and buyers access funding more efficiently. ([TNGlobal][3])

For companies operating across borders and especially for small and midsize enterprises (SMEs), this can mean **faster access to working capital**, **improved cash flow**, and **greater participation in international trade**—a goal that aligns with broader efforts in the region to modernize financial ecosystems and support inclusive economic growth. ([EuropaWire][4])

### What’s Next for Olea

The new funds are earmarked for:

* **AI-driven analytics and risk tools**
* **Web3 and tokenization readiness**
* **Expansion of global trade corridors**
* **Deepening embedded finance offerings**
  These priorities underscore a **hybrid vision of finance and technology**, combining data intelligence with institutional capital to unlock new levels of efficiency and transparency in global trade. ([TNGlobal][3])

### **Glossary**

**Series A:** Early stage venture capital round focused on scaling product and market fit.
**Trade Finance:** Financial products that support international trade, helping companies pay for goods and services across borders.
**Embedded Finance:** Financial services integrated directly into non-financial platforms.
**Tokenization:** The process of converting rights to an asset into a digital token on a blockchain.
**Web3:** A decentralized iteration of the internet built on blockchain technologies.
