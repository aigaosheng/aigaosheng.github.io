## Top Stories 

### 1. **Natural Secures $100 Million Facility to Scale AI Agent Payments**

* **Source**: FinTech Futures · 2026-08-21
* **Summary**: Agentic payments start-up Natural has secured a credit facility of up to $100 million from Upper90 Capital Management. The funding is intended to support its proprietary technology stack, which enables AI agents to hold and transact with real money, following the company's recent $30 million Series A.
* **Why It Matters**: The financing highlights growing investor conviction that AI agents will become economic actors requiring dedicated payment, identity, risk and spending infrastructure.

### 2. **Banks Prepare Their Payment Infrastructure for AI Commerce**

* **Source**: PYMNTS · 2026-08-21
* **Summary**: Banks are increasingly examining how payment infrastructure must evolve for AI-driven commerce, where software agents may initiate transactions and interact autonomously with financial systems. The transition raises implementation, authentication and operational-risk challenges alongside the potential for more automated commerce.
* **Why It Matters**: Agentic commerce could force banks to redesign payment authorization and identity systems rather than simply adding AI features to existing customer interfaces.
* **URL**: [https://www.pymnts.com/news/payments-innovation/2026/banks-prepare-payments-for-ai-commerce/](https://www.pymnts.com/news/payments-innovation/2026/banks-prepare-payments-for-ai-commerce/)
