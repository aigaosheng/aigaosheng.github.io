# Payments Brief: Binance Restores Withdrawals After Record Hack

**Today:** A record crypto theft and two competing bank-backed stablecoin pushes in Hong Kong show how quickly centralized backstops and local-currency digital dollars are reshaping settlement risk across Asia.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🔒 **Binance Resumes Withdrawals After Covering 95% of Its $6.3 Billion Hack**

*CBS News · November 1, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Binance restored withdrawals for bitcoin, ether and several other assets after replacing the bulk of the record $6.3 billion stolen from the exchange in late October, while stablecoin issuers' emergency freezes highlighted how centralized rails can still intervene on "decentralized" money.

Binance began a phased reopening covering 15 assets across 15 blockchain networks after funding replacement tokens from its Secure Asset Fund for Users, futures insurance fund and corporate treasury. The FBI has attributed the theft to North Korea's TraderTraitor group, the same actor linked to the $1.5 billion Bybit hack in February 2025. Tether and Circle separately froze billions in stablecoins tied to the stolen funds, and Binance said the remaining roughly 5% of losses involve less liquid assets that will take longer to replace.

**Why it matters:** For banks and BaaS providers holding or settling through exchange-custodied stablecoins, the episode is a live case study in counterparty concentration risk; MAS and the Hong Kong Monetary Authority, both mid-way through licensing local stablecoin issuers, will be watching how quickly issuer-level freezes can contain contagion versus how much damage occurs before they do.

### 2. 💳 **Standard Chartered-Backed Anchorpoint to Launch HKDAP Stablecoin on Solana**

*Bitcoin.com News · November 1, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Anchorpoint, the Hong Kong Monetary Authority-licensed consortium led by Standard Chartered with Animoca Brands and HKT, has agreed to issue its HKD-pegged stablecoin HKDAP natively on Solana to expand retail and institutional access.

The collaboration, formalised through an October 30 memorandum of understanding with the Solana Foundation, will enable native minting and redemption of HKDAP on the network rather than relying solely on wrapped or bridged tokens. The Solana Foundation has also joined Anchorpoint's Tokenised Money Adoption Programme alongside PwC. No commercial launch date has been confirmed, and the move follows HSBC's rival RedCoin stablecoin, already live through its PayMe wallet with roughly 3.3 million users.

**Why it matters:** Hong Kong is turning into a direct competitive test of bank-backed, local-currency stablecoins, giving Singapore-based BaaS and payments players a nearby benchmark for sequencing multi-currency stablecoin strategy ahead of MAS's own framework and this month's Singapore FinTech Festival discussions.

### 3. 📊 **Stablecoin Supply Slips to $298.5 Billion, Erasing 2026's Gains**

*TokenPost · November 1, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Total stablecoin supply fell to $298.5 billion, down $3.1 billion since October 28 and its lowest level since August, as redemptions in the two largest tokens outpaced new issuance.

Tether's USDT supply has fallen $5.3 billion and Circle's USDC is down $4 billion since the start of the year, fully reversing the sector's gains from a May peak of $315.5 billion. Smaller stablecoin issuers collectively added $7.2 billion over the same period, meaning overall market share is quietly shifting away from the two incumbents even as total supply stagnates.

**Why it matters:** A plateau in dollar-stablecoin growth just as US and Hong Kong regulators finalize competing issuance frameworks suggests payment providers should hedge settlement exposure across multiple stablecoin issuers rather than assuming continued USDT/USDC dominance, a consideration for any BaaS platform routing cross-border flows through stablecoin rails.

## Key Takeaways

- Stablecoin issuers Tether and Circle demonstrated they can freeze billions in exposed assets within days of a major hack, confirming that centralized intervention points remain a core part of "decentralized" settlement risk management.
- Hong Kong's stablecoin race is now a direct contest between bank consortia — HSBC's RedCoin versus Standard Chartered-backed Anchorpoint's HKDAP — a dynamic Singapore-licensed issuers are likely to replicate as MAS advances its own framework.
- Dollar-stablecoin supply growth has stalled for the year even as smaller, often regionally issued tokens gain share, an early signal that settlement concentration in USDT and USDC may loosen.

## What to Watch

Watch for MAS commentary on Singapore's stablecoin and interoperability roadmap at the Payments Leaders Forum on 11 November and the Singapore FinTech Festival from 18-20 November, and monitor whether Binance completes recovery of the remaining 5% of stolen funds without further stablecoin freeze or release actions.
