# FintechNews.sg – Weekly Newsletter  
**Period: March 30, 2026 – April 5, 2026**

---

## 📝 Executive Summary

This week saw significant consolidation and regulatory movement across APAC fintech. Western Union's completion of its Dash acquisition marks the first major digital wallet deal in APAC, signaling growing M&A activity as global players seek local footholds [[157]]. Simultaneously, Vietnam introduced a 0.1% crypto transaction tax while advancing its licensing framework for local platforms—a pivotal step toward regulated digital asset markets in Southeast Asia [[36]].

Stablecoin infrastructure gained momentum with Nium enabling card issuance across Visa/Mastercard networks and Franklin Templeton announcing plans to acquire 250 Digital to launch a dedicated crypto unit, reflecting institutional appetite for tokenized finance [[63]]. In payments, Visa deployed generative AI tools to combat rising dispute volumes, while Thunes launched a cross-border payout service targeting the global gig economy—highlighting AI and embedded finance as key growth vectors [[52]][[171]].

Singapore remains a regulatory bellwether: MAS partnered with industry to strengthen gold trading infrastructure [[120]], while OCBC adjusted savings account rates amid a regional shift toward customer retention over acquisition [[168]]. Thailand's virtual bank race accelerated as SCBX formed BankX Bank, and APAC fintechs broadly pivoted strategies toward user retention as market maturity sets in.

---

## 📈 Key Trends

- **Stablecoin Infrastructure Maturation**: Multiple launches (Nium [[63]], PayPal expansion, Franklin Templeton) indicate enterprise-grade stablecoin rails are moving from pilot to production across APAC.
- **AI Integration in Fraud & Dispute Management**: Visa's generative AI tools [[52]] and rising industry focus on "glass box" AI models signal a shift toward explainable, real-time fraud prevention.
- **Regulatory Clarity Driving Crypto Adoption**: Vietnam's crypto tax [[36]] and licensing progress, alongside Singapore's gold trading framework [[120]], show regulators enabling—not restricting—digital asset innovation.
- **Cross-Border Payments for Gig Economy**: Thunes' new payout service [[171]] and regional payment method harmonization efforts reflect demand for frictionless, multi-currency worker compensation.
- **Digital Banking Market Correction**: APAC fintech apps are pivoting from user acquisition to retention as growth slows, with banks like OCBC adjusting product economics accordingly [[168]].
