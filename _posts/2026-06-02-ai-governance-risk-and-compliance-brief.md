### 1. WHO publishes new paper on governing AI in evidence-informed health policy
- **World Health Organization** · 2026-06-02
- **Summary**: WHO released a new discussion paper on how AI is changing evidence-informed health policymaking, focusing on risks across the policy cycle from problem definition through implementation and monitoring. The paper calls for algorithmic impact assessments, human verification, multidisciplinary oversight, and “augment, not automate” deployment principles for public-sector use of AI in health policy.
- **Why It Matters**: This is a substantive governance signal from a global standard-setting body, extending AI oversight beyond clinical tools into policy formation itself. For health systems, regulators, and vendors, it reinforces expectations for documented controls, human accountability, and bias management in AI-enabled decision support.
- **URL**: https://www.who.int/news/item/02-06-2026-new-who-discussion-paper-sets-out-opportunities-and-risks-of-ai-in-evidence-informed-health-policy
