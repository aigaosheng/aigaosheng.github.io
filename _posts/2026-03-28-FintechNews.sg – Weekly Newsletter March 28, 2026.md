# FintechNews.sg – Weekly Newsletter
**Period: March 21 – March 28, 2026**

---

## 📝 Executive Summary

This week saw significant momentum in **cross-border payments infrastructure** and **stablecoin integration**, with Visa joining the Canton Network as a Super Validator and Triple-A partnering with Circle for stablecoin settlements—signaling growing institutional adoption of onchain payment rails across APAC. Singapore remains a strategic hub, with EBANX establishing its APAC headquarters there and multiple firms (Tazapay, dtcpay) securing funding to scale stablecoin-enabled payment networks.

**Regulatory clarity** continues to drive digital asset activity: Thailand's SEC opened public consultation on Travel Rule compliance, while Vietnam advanced toward licensing its first crypto exchanges. MAS reinforced its proactive stance with the release of an AI Risk Toolkit and expansion of the BLOOM initiative, now joined by Ripple for tokenised trade settlement pilots.

**AI adoption in financial services** accelerated notably, with HSBC appointing its first Chief AI Officer amid workforce restructuring, Mastercard deploying generative AI for fraud detection, and MAS collaborating with 24 institutions on Project Mindforge to address AI vendor risk. These developments underscore a sector-wide shift from AI experimentation to operational integration—with risk governance keeping pace.

---

## 📈 Key Trends

- **Stablecoins move from pilot to production**: Multiple partnerships (PayPal, Circle, Thunes, Triple-A) indicate institutional-grade stablecoin infrastructure is now viable for cross-border B2B settlements in APAC.
- **AI risk governance becomes non-negotiable**: Regulators (MAS) and institutions are co-developing frameworks like Project Mindforge, reflecting heightened focus on third-party AI vendor accountability.
- **Consolidation in digital banking**: Kredivo's acquisition of Vietnam's Timo and Revolut's profitability in Singapore signal maturation—scale and unit economics now outweigh growth-at-all-costs strategies.
- **Cross-border QR and UPI expansion**: WeChat Pay-LankaPay integration and India-Bhutan UPI postal remittance highlight the region's push for interoperable, low-cost remittance corridors.
- **Payments innovation targets SMEs and travel**: Mastercard's APAC SME suite, Trip.com/Checkout.com partnership, and FOMO Pay's multi-rail soundbox reflect vertical-specific payment optimisation.
