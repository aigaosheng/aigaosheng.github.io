# Payment Brief — 2026-07-22

## Top Stories

### 1. **Bir Partners With UnionPay to Expand Card Acceptance Across Azerbaijan**

* **Source**: The Paypers · 2026-07-22
* **Summary**: Azerbaijan-based payment ecosystem Bir has partnered with UnionPay International to expand UnionPay card acceptance across the country. The first rollout enables UnionPay cardholders to make online purchases at more than 1,000 merchants and withdraw cash through nearly 1,300 Birbank-operated ATMs. The partnership will later extend to POS terminals and mobile payment acceptance.
* **Why It Matters**: The move highlights the continued globalization of payment networks and the strategic importance of cross-border acceptance infrastructure. Regional payment ecosystems are increasingly competing by connecting domestic users with international commerce flows.

### 2. **Zip Co Exits New Zealand BNPL Market After Strategic Review**

* **Summary**: Australian Buy Now, Pay Later provider Zip Co announced plans to close its New Zealand operations following a strategic review. The company said it will focus resources on its Australian and US markets, where it sees stronger growth opportunities and improving profitability.
* **Why It Matters**: The decision reflects broader BNPL industry consolidation as providers prioritize profitable markets, operational efficiency, and sustainable growth over geographic expansion.

### 3. **Apple Reportedly Expands Device Financing Strategy With Klarna-Backed Leasing Programme**

* **Source**: The Paypers · 2026-07-22
* **Summary**: Apple is reportedly working with Klarna on a new device leasing programme covering products including iPhones, iPads, Macs, and Apple Watches.
