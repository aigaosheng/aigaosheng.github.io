**VikingGPT: Viking Global Investors' In-House AI Strategy**

Viking Global Investors, a prominent hedge fund managing approximately $53 billion in assets, has developed its proprietary AI chatbot, VikingGPT 2.0, to enhance operational efficiency and support investment research.

---

### What Is VikingGPT?

VikingGPT 2.0 is an in-house artificial intelligence chatbot designed to assist Viking Global's teams across various functions. Since its deployment, the tool has seen a 400% increase in usage, with staff posing an average of three questions per minute. The AI supports tasks such as analyzing trade ideas, summarizing research, and streamlining routine operations. However, it does not make investment decisions independently; human judgment remains central to critical choices ([AInvest][1]).

---

### Why Build In-House?

Viking Global's decision to develop VikingGPT internally stems from several strategic considerations:

* **Customization:** Tailoring the AI to the firm's specific investment processes ensures better alignment with their needs.

* **Data Security:** Maintaining control over proprietary data mitigates risks associated with third-party platforms.

* **Competitive Advantage:** Developing unique tools can provide a technological edge in the competitive hedge fund industry.

Co-founder Andreas Halvorsen emphasizes that while AI can enhance productivity, it is not a substitute for human expertise, especially in decision-making processes ([E24][2]).

---

### Building and Evaluating VikingGPT

The development of VikingGPT involved:

* **Data Integration:** Incorporating internal research, market data, and historical performance metrics.

* **Model Training:** Utilizing advanced machine learning techniques to train the AI on relevant datasets.

* **User Feedback:** Iterative improvements based on feedback from Viking Global's teams.

Evaluation metrics for VikingGPT include user engagement levels, the accuracy of information provided, and the efficiency gains in research and operations. The 400% increase in usage since August 2025 indicates positive reception and utility among staff ([AInvest][1]).

---

### Return on Investment (ROI)

While specific financial metrics are not publicly disclosed, the ROI of VikingGPT can be inferred from:

* **Operational Efficiency:** Time saved on routine tasks allows staff to focus on more strategic activities.

* **Enhanced Research Capabilities:** Faster analysis and synthesis of information can lead to more informed investment decisions.

* **Scalability:** The AI's ability to handle increasing volumes of queries without proportional increases in cost.

Given the significant uptick in usage and the strategic importance of AI in modern finance, VikingGPT is positioned to deliver substantial long-term value to Viking Global Investors.

---

In conclusion, VikingGPT represents a strategic initiative by Viking Global Investors to integrate advanced AI capabilities into their operations. By developing the tool in-house, the firm ensures that it meets their specific needs while maintaining control over critical data. The positive reception and increased usage suggest that VikingGPT is a valuable asset in enhancing the firm's research and operational efficiency.
