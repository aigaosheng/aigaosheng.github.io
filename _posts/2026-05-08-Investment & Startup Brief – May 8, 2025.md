**Investment & Startup Brief – May 8, 2025**

Welcome to our daily roundup of the most impactful moves in venture capital and startup ecosystems. Yesterday saw significant activity, from new European defense tech unicorns to billion-dollar growth funds, signaling continued momentum in deep tech and enterprise software.

## Top Stories

- **NATO Innovation Fund-backed Tekever Becomes Europe‘s Newest Unicorn**  
  *NATO Innovation Fund* · May 7, 2025  
  TEKEVER, a European provider of AI-driven autonomous systems, completed a funding round confirming its valuation above £1 billion, with support from existing investors including the NATO Innovation Fund. The company also announced a £400 million five-year development program for the UK to boost sovereign AI-driven defense technology.  
  **Why It Matters:** This marks the NATO Innovation Fund’s first portfolio unicorn and underscores the accelerating convergence of defense and venture capital, with autonomous systems emerging as a critical priority for European security.

- **Mainsail Partners Raises $1.535 Billion Fund VII**  
  *Mainsail Partners* · May 7, 2025  
  Mainsail Partners closed its seventh flagship fund at the hard cap of $1.535 billion, the largest in the firm’s 22-year history, bringing total capital commitments to nearly $4 billion. The oversubscribed fund closed in under 100 days and will continue backing bootstrapped B2B software companies, with two initial growth equity investments already made.  
  **Why It Matters:** The swift, oversubscribed raise signals persistent LP appetite for growth-stage B2B software, particularly bootstrapped founders leveraging AI for scalable advantage.
