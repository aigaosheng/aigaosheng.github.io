### 1. **Singapore Passes New Scam Laws Giving Police Stronger Powers Over Banks and Digital Services**

* **Source**: Channel NewsAsia · 9 September 2026
* **Summary**: Singapore’s Parliament passed the Scams (Countermeasures) and Other Matters Bill, giving the police powers to order banks and telcos to disclose scam-related information and disable accounts suspected of facilitating scams.
