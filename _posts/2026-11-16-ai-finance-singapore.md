# AI Finance in Singapore Brief: GFTN Develops ASEAN Baseline for Bank AI Governance

**Today:** On the eve of Singapore FinTech Festival week, the news flow shows who answers for AI agents, how Singapore firms are productising them, and how fintechs are preparing for customers who delegate to software.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🏦 **GFTN Works With ASEAN Banks on a Common AI Governance Baseline**

*Fintech News Singapore · November 16, 2026*

**Bottom line:** The Global Finance & Technology Network is working with the ASEAN Bankers Association and other partners on a shared baseline for how Southeast Asian banks govern AI.

At the opening of its Insights2040 Annual Meetings in Singapore, GFTN said the two-day programme will examine who bears responsibility when AI agents act. The report also says GFTN's work with Financial Innovation for Impact looks at how regulators could use AI in their own supervision and what skills that would require. The same coverage notes GFTN's plan to launch a tokenised-markets sandbox in 2027, which sits outside this brief's AI scope.

**Why it matters:** A common regional baseline could ease compliance for banks operating across several ASEAN jurisdictions and give regulators a shared reference on agent accountability. Details of the baseline have not yet been published, so its content and enforceability remain open.

### 2. 🤖 **Linnovate Launches RAISE AI Agent for Alternative Investment Reporting**

*Fintech News Singapore · November 16, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Singapore-based Linnovate has launched an AI agent that generates fund reports and dashboards from natural-language requests inside its RAISE platform.

The company says the agent can produce performance metrics such as IRR, TVPI, DPI and NAV by fund, vintage or investor group. Users can adjust reporting periods, metrics and layouts through plain-language instructions and turn reports into dashboards. Linnovate describes the tool as aimed at fund managers, finance and operations teams, investor relations, compliance and risk teams, and investors. These are the company's own claims, and no customer results were reported.

**Why it matters:** Reporting is a labour-heavy task for private-capital administrators, and an agent that reads from the administrator's own data is a practical test of AI in fund operations. Buyers should still ask about output validation, audit trails and data controls before relying on generated investor reports.

### 3. 🤖 **Singapore Fintechs Aspire and Chocolate Finance Plan for Customers' AI Agents**

*Fintech News Singapore · November 16, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Executives at Singapore fintechs say they expect customers to send AI agents to transact on their behalf, and they are building access points and deciding where humans stay in the loop.

In an SAP-sponsored webinar, Aspire co-founder Joel Leong said some customers already use agents to interact with the company and that Aspire is rolling out connections for AI tools. Chocolate Finance's CTO described a future in which a customer's agent could schedule recurring deposits under customer-set rules. Leong also reported 70% weekly AI usage across Aspire teams, with 60% of engineers using AI to write code, and cited Crunchbase data that AI startups took 64% of global venture funding in Q3 2026.

**Why it matters:** This contrasts with the cautious stance reported from Singapore's large banks on third-party agents, and it shows fintechs treating agents as a new distribution channel. Investors are also reportedly expecting AI productivity gains to show up as lower operating costs.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://fintechnews.sg/139733/ai/ai-in-fintech-business-performance/)

## Key Takeaways

- Accountability for AI agents is the common thread: regional bodies such as GFTN are working on shared bank governance baselines while fintechs build agent-ready interfaces.
- Singapore firms are moving from AI assistants to agents that produce outputs or act on behalf of users, which raises validation and control requirements.
- Fintechs are positioning for agent-driven customers, with investors reportedly pressing for AI productivity to show up in cost structures.

## What to Watch

Singapore FinTech Festival runs from 18 to 20 November at Singapore Expo, where agentic AI and AI-enabled payments are headline themes. Watch for whether GFTN publishes the details of the ASEAN bank AI baseline and for any further agent-accountability announcements from MAS and the banks.
