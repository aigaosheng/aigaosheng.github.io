# AI Investment & Startup in China Brief: Kuaishou's Kling AI Round Reveals New Backers

**Today:** Fresh regulatory-style disclosures around Kuaishou's record video-AI raise show how China's AI capital stack is widening to include domestic chipmakers and enterprise software vendors, a signal worth watching for anyone tracking who actually bankrolls China's generative-AI buildout.

## Top Stories

### 1. 📊 **Kuaishou's Kling AI Financing Adds Chipmaker and Software Investors**

*Sina Finance · November 1, 2026*

**Bottom line:** New disclosures on Kuaishou's already-completed RMB 19.8 billion Kling AI capital raise reveal previously unnamed investors, including Hong Kong-listed Kingdee's domestic software unit and GPU maker MetaX, alongside the termination of two investors who failed to pay on schedule.

Kuaishou's Beijing-based video-generation unit, Kling AI, finalized a capital increase of approximately RMB 19.802 billion, slightly below an initial ceiling of RMB 20.447 billion, according to Sina Finance. The filing shows existing backers Dakang Lanwu, CPE Spruce and Hairui No. 1 transferred portions of their subscription rights to new entrants, including Kingdee Software (China), Metax Digital Shanghai (a unit of GPU designer MetaX), Wenzhou Ruida and others, while Dakang Lanwu and BFI MIDCO had their commitments terminated after missing payment deadlines. Kuaishou, through its Lucky Labs vehicle, ends up holding 66.68% of equity and 52.45% of voting rights.

**Why it matters:** The entry of a domestic GPU maker and an enterprise software firm as named investors points to tighter vertical alignment between China's AI compute, software and content-generation layers, a dynamic worth monitoring for Asian enterprise-AI buyers evaluating which vendors control their own supply chain versus relying on outside chip access. For Singapore and regional players assessing Chinese generative-video tools for enterprise or marketing use, the ownership reshuffle underscores how concentrated control remains with Kuaishou despite a broadening investor base.

## Key Takeaways

- China's largest AI funding rounds are increasingly structured with subscription-right transfers rather than simple new-money injections, making post-close disclosures as important as the initial headline figure.
- Domestic GPU makers like MetaX are moving beyond chip sales into strategic equity stakes in AI application companies, tightening China's compute-to-application supply chain.
- Kuaishou retains firm majority control (66.68% equity) of Kling AI despite the large capital raise, limiting outside investor influence over strategic direction.

## What to Watch

Watch for Kuaishou's next disclosures on how the newly raised capital is deployed across Kling AI's compute and model-training roadmap, and whether MetaX's equity stake signals a broader pattern of Chinese AI chipmakers taking positions in application-layer startups.
