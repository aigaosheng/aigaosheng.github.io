**AI-Powered Logistics: How C.H. Robinson Is Thriving Amid a Freight Downturn**

In the world of logistics, where every minute and dollar counts, C.H. Robinson is rewriting the playbook. Despite a prolonged freight slump that has sidelined many competitors, this 120-year-old freight broker is not just surviving—it's thriving. The secret? A bold blend of artificial intelligence (AI) and lean operational strategies.

---

### 🚚 From Skepticism to Success: The Bozeman Era

When Dave Bozeman took the helm of C.H. Robinson in June 2023, many were doubtful. Coming from Ford, Amazon, and Caterpillar, his background was far from traditional logistics. Yet, Bozeman saw an opportunity to modernize the company by integrating lean principles and cutting-edge AI technologies.

Under his leadership, the company has reported a $393 million operating profit for the first half of 2025, marking an $88 million increase from the previous year—even as revenue dipped 8% to $8.2 billion. This performance stands in stark contrast to many industry peers grappling with similar challenges.

---

### 🤖 AI at the Core: Automating the Future of Freight

C.H. Robinson's AI journey began with automating routine tasks like rate quotes, bookings, and appointment scheduling. Today, AI handles about half of the company's carrier bookings, enabling the firm to process more shipments with 20% fewer employees. This shift has not only improved efficiency but also enhanced customer responsiveness.

Looking ahead, the company is exploring "agentic AI"—advanced systems capable of making complex decisions autonomously. For instance, generating a quote for a shipment from China to Charlotte, N.C., which previously took days, can now be completed in about an hour.

---

### 📉 Lean and Mean: A Workforce Transformation

While AI has played a pivotal role, Bozeman emphasizes the importance of lean operations. By eliminating productivity bottlenecks and streamlining decision-making processes, C.H. Robinson has reduced its workforce by 20% over two years, from 16,000 to 12,858 employees. This lean approach allows the company to remain agile and cost-effective, even in a challenging market.

---

### 🌐 Positioned for the Future

With AI and lean principles at its core, C.H. Robinson is well-positioned to capitalize on future market rebounds. Bozeman remains confident, stating, "If it doesn't turn, if it's lower for longer, we're still going to win at that."

As the logistics industry continues to evolve, C.H. Robinson's innovative approach serves as a blueprint for others aiming to navigate downturns and emerge stronger.

---

### 📚 Glossary

* **Agentic AI**: Advanced artificial intelligence systems capable of making complex decisions autonomously, often used in scenarios requiring nuanced judgment.
* **Lean Operations**: A management philosophy focused on reducing waste and improving efficiency in all areas of production.
* **Freight Broker**: An intermediary between shippers and carriers, facilitating the transportation of goods.
