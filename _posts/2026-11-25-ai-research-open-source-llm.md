# AI Research & Open-Source LLM Brief: ShellBench Reveals Everyday Agent Reliability Gaps

**Today:** New benchmark results highlight the reliability and safety gap enterprises must weigh alongside model capability and local deployment options.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **ShellBench Reveals Everyday Agent Reliability Gaps**

*arXiv · November 25, 2026*

**Bottom line:** In ShellBench, the leading model completed roughly 63% of everyday work tasks, while the study found model choice mattered more than the agent harness.

The paper evaluates 12 models across five agent harnesses on 100 tasks based on patterns from more than 10 million user requests, with 53,100 graded runs. The top three models scored between 61.4% and 62.5% on single attempts; across all runs, 15.7% failed at least one safety check.

**Why it matters:** These results point to a gap between benchmark capability and dependable workflow completion. For fintech and payments teams, the inclusion of tasks involving suspicious payment requests makes end-to-end safety and verification relevant evaluation criteria, not just model accuracy.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32946)

### 2. 🤖 **Sakana AI Announces 30B Namazu Small Model**

*Sakana AI · November 25, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Sakana AI says its 30-billion-parameter Namazu Small outscored NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Nano and the roughly four-times-larger Nemotron 3 Super on the average of 25 benchmarks.

Built on Nemotron 3 Nano, the model received continued pretraining and post-training from Sakana AI. The company highlights web research and long-document tasks, and says it plans to share details on on-premises availability in early-to-mid December.

**Why it matters:** A model designed for local operation could give Asian organisations another option for handling document-heavy workloads on their own infrastructure. The reported benchmark results are Sakana AI’s own; teams should assess the model against their workloads when access and deployment details become available.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://sakana.ai/namazu-small/)

## Key Takeaways

- Agent evaluation is shifting toward complete, repeated task success, including evidence handling and safety checks.
- ShellBench’s results suggest model selection can matter more than harness selection, though both influence outcomes.
- Sakana AI’s announcement adds a Japan-developed model targeting on-premises use, with availability details still to come.

## What to Watch

Sakana AI plans to share on-premises availability details for Namazu Small in early-to-mid December. OpenClaw says it will update ShellBench as new results become available.
