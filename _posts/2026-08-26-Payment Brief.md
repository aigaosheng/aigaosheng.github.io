### 1. **Basware to Acquire Trustpair, Adding Payment Account Validation to Invoice Assurance**

* **Source**: FinTech Futures · August 26, 2026
* **Summary**: Accounts payable automation provider Basware has signed an agreement to acquire Trustpair, a specialist in supplier account validation and payment fraud prevention. Trustpair integrates with corporate ERP systems to verify vendor bank details, extending Basware's capabilities from invoice processing into payment-stage assurance.
* **Why It Matters**: The deal highlights the growing convergence of AP automation and payment fraud prevention. As business payment fraud increasingly exploits changes to supplier banking details, validation is becoming a strategic control point in enterprise payment workflows.
