### 1. UK Injects £60M into Fundamental AI Research to Boost Sovereignty
- **GOV.UK / UKRI** · 2026-06-23
- **Summary**: The UK government has announced up to £60 million in funding for two new AI research labs led by the University of Oxford and University College London (UCL).
