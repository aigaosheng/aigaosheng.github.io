# Payment Brief — 2026-08-05

## Top Stories

### 1. **India Moves Closer to Reintroducing MDR for UPI Merchant Payments**

* **Source**: The Economic Times · 2026-08-05
* **Summary**: India's government introduced amendments to the Payment and Settlement Systems Act that could restore Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) for certain Unified Payments Interface (UPI) merchant transactions. The proposal represents a significant policy shift from the zero-MDR framework that has governed UPI for several years.
* **Why It Matters**: A return of MDR could materially improve the economics of banks and payment providers while reshaping merchant acceptance costs across the world's largest real-time payment ecosystem.
* **URL**: [https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/economy/finance/government-moves-closer-to-restoring-mdr-on-upi-merchant-payments/articleshow/132871595.cms](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/economy/finance/government-moves-closer-to-restoring-mdr-on-upi-merchant-payments/articleshow/132871595.cms)
