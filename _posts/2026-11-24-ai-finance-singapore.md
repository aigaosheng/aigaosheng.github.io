# AI Finance in Singapore Brief: Banks Test AI Agent Safeguards

**Today:** A cross-industry proof of concept puts agent identity, customer authority and auditability at the centre of Singapore’s effort to make AI-led financial activity safer.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **Banks Test AI Agent Safeguards**

*PR Newswire (Terminal 3) · November 24, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Green Link Digital Bank, AXS, Terminal 3 and HSBC Singapore tested controls for AI agents interacting with financial institutions and payment providers.

The PathFin.ai proof of concept examined how institutions could verify an agent’s identity and authority, enforce a client mandate and record decisions. Its controlled scenarios covered bill payments and corporate treasury requests, with client confirmation required before a request could proceed. The participants said the exercise was not a customer product launch or an autonomous payment service.

**Why it matters:** The trial makes cross-institution verification and permission controls concrete design questions for Singapore’s emerging agentic finance ecosystem. For banks and payment providers across Asia, it also highlights the need to coordinate safeguards across existing payment rails before agents handle higher-value tasks.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/terminal-3-partners-with-banks-to-test-safeguards-for-agentic-finance-under-mass-pathfinai-initiative-302948091.html)

## Key Takeaways

- Agent identity and delegated authority were tested alongside transaction controls and auditability.
- The proof of concept involved both consumer bill payment and corporate treasury scenarios.
- The participants framed the work as controlled testing, with no customer product or autonomous service launched.

## What to Watch

Watch for further PathFin.ai or SAFR work that shows how these controls perform across more institutions and payment use cases, and whether the tests lead to defined operating standards or live pilots.
