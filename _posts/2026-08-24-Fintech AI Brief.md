# Fintech AI Brief — 2026-08-24

## Top Stories 

### 1. **Fintechs Report 86% Productivity Gains in Technology and Product Functions**

* **Source**: FinTech Global · 2026-08-24
* **Summary**: A new analysis of the University of Cambridge's Global AI in Financial Services research finds that fintechs report the strongest AI-driven productivity gains in technology, data and product functions, at 86%, compared with 68% for traditional financial institutions. The results are based on responses from 203 fintechs and 149 incumbent financial institutions across 151 countries and highlight an uneven distribution of AI benefits across business functions.
* **Why It Matters**: The data suggests AI is becoming a competitive operating advantage for digitally native financial firms, particularly in product and engineering. The next challenge is translating these gains into customer-facing revenue growth and enterprise-wide productivity.

### 2. **ADIB Appoints Its First Chief AI Officer**

* **Source**: Gulf Business · 2026-08-24
* **Summary**: Abu Dhabi Islamic Bank has appointed its first Chief AI Officer as part of its Vision 2035 strategy. The move formalizes AI leadership at the executive level and builds on the bank's broader digital transformation, with the institution positioning AI as a core capability rather than a standalone technology initiative.
* **Why It Matters**: Dedicated AI leadership is becoming an important organizational signal in banking. Financial institutions are increasingly moving from experimentation toward centralized ownership of AI strategy, governance and deployment.

### 3. **AI-Native Finance Platform Rillet's $100 Million Round Gains Further Attention**

* **Source**: The Paypers · 2026-08-24
* **Summary**: Rillet, an AI-native enterprise resource planning platform focused on finance teams, has raised $100 million in a Series C round at a $1 billion valuation. The company is building AI agents designed to automate increasingly complex accounting and finance workflows around a real-time general ledger.
* **Why It Matters**: The funding reinforces investor conviction that AI-native financial software can challenge established ERP and accounting platforms. The strategic shift is from AI-assisted workflows toward agentic systems embedded directly in systems of record.

### 4. **DESILO Expands European Presence for Privacy-Preserving Financial Crime Detection**

* **Source**: FF News · 2026-08-24
* **Summary**: DESILO has established a Luxembourg entity to support European pilots of HOLMES, its financial-crime detection platform based on fully homomorphic encryption. The approach is designed to enable cross-institutional analysis while keeping underlying data encrypted, addressing a major obstacle to collaborative fraud and AML intelligence.
* **Why It Matters**: AI and advanced analytics in financial crime increasingly depend on data sharing, but privacy and regulatory constraints limit centralized data pooling. Privacy-enhancing technologies could become an important infrastructure layer for cross-bank AI collaboration.
* **URL**: [https://ffnews.com/news/desilo-launches-european-entity-in-luxembourg-to-tackle-cross-institutional-fraud-via-fhe/](https://ffnews.com/news/desilo-launches-european-entity-in-luxembourg-to-tackle-cross-institutional-fraud-via-fhe/)
