## Market Signal

**The dominant Singapore-relevant theme today is not a new banking product but the changing economics of AI.** Financial institutions, investors and policymakers increasingly need to evaluate AI through three lenses: productivity gains, infrastructure capital intensity and operational risk. For Singapore's position as both a financial centre and an AI infrastructure hub, that makes AI investment discipline increasingly important.
