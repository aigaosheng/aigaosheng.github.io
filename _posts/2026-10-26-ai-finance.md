# AI Finance in Singapore Brief: Igloo Targets MENA With AI Insurance Platform

**Today:** A quiet news day, but useful if you buy or build AI for insurance, or follow where fintech capital in Singapore is going.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **Igloo Targets MENA Expansion for Its AI-Driven Insurance Software**
*Fintech News Singapore · October 26, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Singapore-based insurtech Igloo plans to grow its insurance software business fivefold in two years and take it into the Middle East and North Africa, with AI automation as its main selling point.

Igloo Tech Solutions launched in June 2025. It is a software-as-a-service platform that helps insurers, banks and other businesses build, distribute and manage insurance products. Clients now number more than 10 across Asia, including MSIG Vietnam, Great Eastern Indonesia, Tokio Marine Thailand and HSBC Hong Kong. Igloo plans to enter MENA through organic growth and acquisitions.

Igloo says its AI product builder has cut product setup time from two days to 20 minutes. It also says its AI claims system decided more than 50,000 cases in Q1 2026, with a self-reported accuracy rate of 94%. Revenue rose 45.9% in FY2025, and Igloo is targeting adjusted EBITDA breakeven by the end of 2026.

**Why it matters:** Singapore insurtechs are now selling AI claims and product automation as exportable software, not only using it in-house. Insurers and banks buying these tools should ask how the 94% accuracy is measured and where humans review decisions. Under MAS's AI risk expectations, financial institutions stay accountable for AI supplied by third parties.

### 2. 📊 **Singapore Takes Over Half of Asia's Fintech Funding in Q3 as AI Dominates Global VC**
*Fintech News Singapore · October 26, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Singapore fintechs raised a record US$1.7 billion in Q3 2026, about 53.1% of Asia's US$3.2 billion total, but most of it came from two mega-rounds.

The figures come from CB Insights. Asian fintech venture funding rose 28% quarter on quarter, while global fintech funding fell 18% to US$11 billion. Singapore's total was driven mainly by Ant International's US$1.2 billion Series A and a US$400 million strategic investment in Crypto.com.

Across all sectors, AI took US$103.3 billion, or 55.2%, of the US$187 billion in global venture funding in the quarter. Fintech's share was 5.9%. The article ties Singapore's momentum to MAS's S$220 million FSTI 4.0 scheme (MAS's three-year fintech funding programme). One of its tracks, AI Pathfinder, supports scaling market-ready AI solutions listed on PathFin.ai, MAS's shared platform for AI use cases in finance.

**Why it matters:** Singapore's record total rests on a very narrow base, so it says little about funding for early-stage AI fintechs. For AI vendors, the more practical lever may be the FSTI 4.0 AI Pathfinder track, which funds adoption by financial institutions rather than equity rounds.

## Key Takeaways
- AI in Singapore finance is increasingly sold as a built-in feature of vertical software, such as claims decisions and product setup, and the performance figures are usually self-reported.
- Singapore's headline fintech funding strength depends on a few large rounds. Global capital is flowing mainly to AI, with fintech taking a small share.
- Public funding through FSTI 4.0 and PathFin.ai is aimed at moving AI solutions into production, which favours vendors that can show governance-ready deployments.

## What to Watch
Watch whether Igloo announces a MENA acquisition or first client, and whether it reaches its end-2026 EBITDA breakeven target. Also watch whether Singapore's Q4 funding holds up without mega-rounds.
