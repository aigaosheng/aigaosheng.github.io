# Enterprise AI Brief: Anthropic and Hg Plan European AI Consultancy

**Today:** Anthropic is building out its enterprise delivery arm in Europe, and cloud-run agent work becomes the default for Claude Enterprise on November 16.

## Top Stories

### 1. 📊 **Anthropic and Hg Reportedly Plan a European AI Consultancy**

*The Next Web, citing the Financial Times · October 23, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Anthropic is reportedly backing an independent European consultancy with private equity firm Hg to place AI engineers inside client businesses.

According to the FT report as relayed by The Next Web, the venture is expected to launch by the end of 2026 with offices in London and Berlin. Anthropic would fund its share from its own balance sheet, while Hg would invest through funds it manages, including HgCapital Trust. It would be Anthropic's first standalone consultancy in Europe, and the report says it follows the model of Ode in the US. Neither company commented to the FT.

**Why it matters:** Frontier labs increasingly treat hands-on deployment services, not only model access, as a route to enterprise revenue, which puts them in closer competition with established IT consultancies and the Big Four. Asia-based buyers should note that the sources describe a European plan only, and no equivalent for the region has been announced.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://thenextweb.com/news/anthropic-hg-european-ai-consultancy)

### 2. 🤖 **Anthropic Makes Unified Chat and Cowork Generally Available for Claude Enterprise**

*Anthropic Claude Help Center · October 23, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Claude Enterprise customers can now let a chat hand off agentic work to a cloud sandbox in the same conversation, with admins controlling who gets it.

Every conversation starts as a chat. When a task needs code execution or file work, Claude continues in an isolated cloud sandbox managed by Anthropic and returns the finished work for review. The feature appears in admin settings as "Chat and Cowork unified." It is off by default in existing organisations and turns on by default on November 16, 2026. Network egress from the sandbox is off by default for Enterprise, and sandbox environments expire 30 days after creation.

**Why it matters:** Administrators now have a fixed date to complete security reviews and set role-based access, connector and spend policies. Organisations using zero data retention, the HIPAA-ready configuration or customer-managed encryption keys cannot use it yet, which matters for banks and payments firms with strict data-handling rules.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/17444462-set-up-and-manage-the-new-unified-claude-experience-for-your-enterprise-organization)

## Key Takeaways

- Model vendors are extending into the enterprise delivery layer, through embedded consultants in one case and vendor-hosted agent runtimes in the other.
- Governance is being built into rollout mechanics: opt-in now, default-on later, with role-level and spend-level controls.
- Regulated buyers face a gap: the most data-restrictive configurations are excluded from the new Claude Enterprise experience for now.

## What to Watch

November 16 is when the unified Claude experience switches on by default for existing Enterprise organisations. Also watch for any confirmation from Anthropic or Hg of the consultancy's structure and terms before the end-2026 launch target.
