# Enterprise AI Brief: Agentic AI Foundation Adds Four Open Projects

**Today:** A light edition with two qualifying stories: rival AI vendors move more agent infrastructure into neutral governance, and 1Password opens a credential layer for AI agents.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **Anthropic, OpenAI, Hugging Face and Alibaba Contribute Four Projects to the Agentic AI Foundation**

*Agentic AI Foundation · October 22, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Four competing AI companies placed agent building blocks under one neutral foundation, a sign that the agent stack is converging on shared, vendor-independent standards.

At AGNTCon + MCPCon North America in San Jose, the foundation took on Agent Skills (from Anthropic), Open Responses (OpenAI), OpenEnv (Hugging Face) and OpenSandbox (Alibaba). Together they cover how agents gain skills, how applications talk to models from different providers, how agents are trained and evaluated, and where they run safely. The foundation now hosts ten projects, up from three at its launch a year ago.

**Why it matters:** For enterprise buyers in Asia and elsewhere, neutral governance of the agent layer lowers the risk of lock-in to a single model or platform vendor. Our read: it is now reasonable to ask vendors which of these open specifications their agent products support. Alibaba's contribution of an execution-sandbox project also shows non-US vendors shaping the standards.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://aaif.io/news/agentic-ai-foundation-announces-four-new-projects)

---

### 2. 🔒 **1Password Launches Platform Letting AI Agents Sign In Without Seeing Credentials**

*1Password · October 22, 2026*

**Bottom line:** 1Password is positioning itself as a credential broker for AI agents, so that agents can complete logins without ever holding the passwords.

The new 1Password for Agents platform is aimed at personal AI agents. An agent that reaches a sign-in form asks for a credential, the user approves the request, and 1Password fills it into the destination site directly. The announcement names Grok Bot among the supported agents. 1Password also says its broader Unified Access platform covers humans, machines and AI agents.

**Why it matters:** Agent authentication is becoming a gating control for deployment, especially in regulated sectors such as banking and payments. The pattern here is a human approval per request with secrets kept outside the model. This launch targets personal agents rather than enterprise fleets, so organisations should not assume it covers enterprise-agent governance.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://1password.com/press/2026/october/1password-for-agents-trust-layer-personal-ai-agents)

## Key Takeaways

- Agent infrastructure is being standardised in neutral venues rather than inside single vendors, which favours buyers who want interoperability.
- Both stories concern the control and plumbing layer (shared specifications, credential access) rather than new models.
- Access control for agents remains unsettled; announced tools vary widely in scope, from personal to enterprise.

## What to Watch

AGNTCon + MCPCon North America runs through October 23 in San Jose, so further foundation and vendor announcements may follow. The next signal is whether major enterprise platforms publicly commit to supporting the newly hosted specifications.
