# AI Research & Open-Source LLM Brief: Z.ai Unveils GLM-5.4 Loop Transformer

**Today:** New model designs and downloadable weights are sharpening enterprise choices around performance, cost and deployment control.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **Z.ai Unveils GLM-5.4 Loop Transformer**

*Z.ai · November 23, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Z.ai’s new flagship uses recurrent depth to increase computation within a model, while initially limiting access to its Coding Plan.

Z.ai says GLM-5.4 loops a shared 28-layer block twice within its 750-billion-parameter base model. In a 30-billion-parameter comparison, the company reports that the looped version improved results with about 10% more training compute, while outputs were 10–14% shorter on several benchmarks. An API release is still pending, and Z.ai says it plans to publish weights after safety hardening.

**Why it matters:** Recurrent depth offers another way to improve model performance without proportionally increasing parameters, but enterprise buyers will need independent evaluations and clearer deployment options. The initial subscription-only access and pending weights leave open questions about model portability and operational control.

### 2. 🤖 **Ant Group Opens LingSante Medical Models**

*Business Wire · November 23, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Ant Group has released two open-weight medical models and introduced a platform for evaluating health AI.

The LingSante family’s first available models are a multimodal model with 124 billion total parameters and 5.5 billion active per token, and a reasoning model with 560 billion total parameters, about 25 billion active per token, and a context window of up to one million tokens. Ant says its Afu health app serves 150 million users and handles nearly 20 million health consultations daily; it also says LingSante-3.1-flash ranked first in InclusionMed’s initial open-model rankings.

**Why it matters:** The release gives healthcare developers and researchers new models to evaluate for localized and complex medical workflows. Ant’s rankings and usage figures are company-reported; independent assessment and fit with local clinical and data requirements will matter for adoption across Asia.

## Key Takeaways

- GLM-5.4 tests recurrent depth as a way to add computation, but its access and deployment paths remain limited.
- Ant Group’s medical model release expands open-weight options for healthcare AI and makes independent evaluation more accessible.
- Both launches underscore the need to validate vendor claims against enterprise workloads, operating costs and governance requirements.

## What to Watch

Watch for Z.ai’s API launch and safety-hardened GLM-5.4 weights, as well as independent evaluations of both GLM-5.4 and LingSante on practical enterprise and healthcare tasks.
