# Enterprise AI Brief: Ant Digital Launches Agent Factory and Trust Layer

**Today:** As enterprises push AI agents from pilots into production, today's stories show identity and security layers for agents becoming a product category of their own, with Asia's fintech sector moving especially fast.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **Ant Digital Unveils Agent Factory and Agent Identity Layer**

*PR Newswire (Ant Digital Technologies) · November 3, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Ant Digital Technologies launched an end-to-end agentic AI framework alongside a new identity-verification layer for AI agents, treating "who is this agent and what can it do" as a problem on the same scale as KYC.

At Hong Kong FinTech Week, Ant's enterprise AI arm unveiled "Agent Factory," which combines its Agentar agent-development tools, ZOLOZ security, LingDT vertical models and blockchain infrastructure into one framework for building and managing AI agents at scale. It also upgraded ZOLOZ with a "Know Your Agent" (KYA) module that extends existing KYC/KYB-style verification to AI agents throughout their lifecycle. The company said it ranks fourth overall, and first among non-cloud vendors, in China's private agent platform market, with 13.3% share of financial-sector large-model platforms and over 30,000 enterprise customers.

**Why it matters:** As banks and fintechs deploy growing numbers of AI agents to handle transactions and customer data, verifying an agent's identity and authority becomes a compliance necessity, not a nice-to-have — a trend MAS-regulated BaaS providers in Singapore and the wider region should watch as agent-identity standards start to take shape.

### 2. 🔒 **CrowdStrike Embeds AI Agent Security Into AWS Bedrock**

*CrowdStrike, via Yahoo Finance · November 3, 2026*

**Bottom line:** CrowdStrike moved AI agent protection directly into the guardrail layer of Amazon Bedrock, rather than requiring a separate security deployment.

Announced at Fal.Con Europe 2026 in Barcelona, the integration extends CrowdStrike's Falcon Guardian into Amazon Bedrock AgentCore Guardrails, defending Bedrock-built AI applications and agentic workflows against prompt injection, sensitive-data leakage and malicious activity with no extra deployment step. CrowdStrike also added automatic Falcon sensor deployment across Amazon ECS Managed Instances as environments scale.

**Why it matters:** Built-in runtime security lowers the operational bar for regulated industries, including banking and payments, to run AI agents on AWS without opening new attack surfaces — a prerequisite for broader agentic adoption in finance.

### 3. 🔒 **CrowdStrike Brings Agent Security to Anthropic's Claude Cowork**

*CrowdStrike, via Yahoo Finance · November 3, 2026*

**Bottom line:** CrowdStrike is building its Falcon Guardian detection engine natively into Anthropic's Claude Cowork, giving security teams visibility into what autonomous agents do once deployed.

The integration covers the agent lifecycle from creation through task execution on Windows and Mac, flagging malicious activity or potential data exfiltration as agents access enterprise systems. CrowdStrike said the move responds to enterprise demand for controls as agent use scales; Anthropic and an early customer, Mondelēz, both framed it around giving security teams visibility and control over agent behaviour.

**Why it matters:** As coding and knowledge-work agents move from pilots to production, enterprise security teams are increasingly likely to require audit-by-default agent behaviour before approving wider rollouts — a gating factor for how fast agentic AI scales inside large organisations.

### 4. 🤖 **Klook Launches K.ai Agent for Its Merchants**

*PR Newswire, via The Manila Times · November 3, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Travel platform Klook launched K.ai, an AI agent that executes back-office tasks rather than just answering questions, aimed at the small merchants who run most of its "experiences" listings.

K.ai updates inventory, pricing and bookings directly inside Klook's merchant portal and generates structured listings from merchants' existing materials. Klook said individual task updates complete 95% faster and batch checks 97% faster, with merchants using K.ai logging 3.4 times the monthly bookings of non-users; the agent is rolling out globally ahead of the year-end travel season.

**Why it matters:** It's a concrete example of agentic AI monetised through SME productivity gains rather than headcount cuts — a model other Asian platform businesses serving fragmented merchant bases are likely to replicate.

### 5. 🤝 **Glean and Emergys Partner on Enterprise Agent Adoption**

*PR Newswire (Emergys/Glean) · November 2, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Enterprise search and agent platform Glean partnered with IT services firm Emergys to help joint customers move agentic AI from pilots into production.

The partnership pairs Glean's multi-model agent platform and enterprise knowledge graph with Emergys' EMY cross-platform agents, aiming to unify AI search and workflows across ERP, ESM and CRM systems from vendors including SAP, BMC, ServiceNow, Freshworks and Salesforce, with a stated focus on improving token efficiency as agent usage scales.

**Why it matters:** It's a further sign that systems integrators are becoming the default distribution channel for enterprise AI platforms, since most large organisations lack the in-house capacity to connect agents to fragmented legacy systems on their own.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/emergys-forges-partnership-with-glean-to-accelerate-enterprise-ai-and-agentic-adoption-302926723.html)

## Key Takeaways

- Agent identity and security are consolidating into standalone product categories — Ant Digital's KYA and CrowdStrike's dual AWS/Anthropic integrations both treat "which agent did what" as core infrastructure, not an add-on.
- Platform vendors are bundling agentic AI into existing enterprise systems (Glean-Emergys) rather than selling it as a separate product, reflecting how little in-house integration capacity most large organisations have.
- Consumer-facing Asian platforms like Klook are building the ROI case for agentic AI around SME productivity gains rather than headcount reduction, a framing likely to spread across the region's platform economy.

## What to Watch

Watch whether Ant Digital's KYA framework becomes a reference model for AI agent identity verification at other Asian financial institutions, and whether CrowdStrike's Fal.Con Europe integrations extend beyond AWS and Anthropic to other major cloud and model providers.
