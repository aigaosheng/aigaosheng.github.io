# AI for Science Brief: GPT-6 Protein Designs Show Lab Evidence

**Today:** New lab results and funding announcements point to growing investment in the data, experiments and model capabilities needed to apply AI in scientific research.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🔬 **GPT-6 Protein Designs Show Lab Evidence**

*OpenAI · November 24, 2026*

**Bottom line:** OpenAI reports that GPT-6.2-designed proteins showed evidence of binding in tests across five targets, while specialized models still supported parts of the workflow.

OpenAI says its models generated protein structures using reasoning and code, then partnered with Onava to test selected designs. Of 322 minibinders tested, 35 showed binding to their intended target. OpenAI notes that specialized models helped propose sequences and select candidates, and that binding alone does not establish safety, efficacy or therapeutic value.

**Why it matters:** The work suggests general-purpose models may contribute to more stages of protein design, but the results remain early and depend on laboratory testing and specialist tools. For research teams in Asia, the practical signal is to assess the full workflow and experimental evidence, not model-generated structures alone.

### 2. 📊 **Leash Biosciences Raises $11M for Scientific AI**

*Business Wire · November 24, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Leash Biosciences raised $11 million to expand lab-measured data and training environments for scientific AI.

The company says it has tested 6.3 million molecules and generated more than 44 billion measurements across almost 1,000 protein targets. The new financing, co-led by Innovation Endeavors and Insight Partners, will support more data production and expand its environments beyond protein–molecule binding. Those scale figures are company-reported.

**Why it matters:** Leash is betting that reusable experimental outcomes can help train and evaluate scientific agents where historical datasets are limited. The approach highlights a potential infrastructure opportunity for research organizations and life-sciences companies in Asia seeking to connect laboratory capacity with AI development.

### 3. 🔬 **Peptone Advances AI-Developed Cancer Candidate**

*PR Newswire · November 24, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Peptone has nominated PEP-5089, an AI- and biophysics-developed prostate cancer candidate, for IND-enabling work.

The company says the small molecule targets the androgen receptor’s disordered N-terminal domain and showed activity in preclinical studies, including prostate cancer xenograft models. Peptone also appointed Deepak Sampath as chief scientific officer. These findings are preclinical and do not establish clinical benefit.

**Why it matters:** The program moves an AI-enabled discovery effort into the studies needed to support a potential clinical trial application. Its progress will offer a concrete test of how Peptone’s experimental biophysics and AI platform translate into development-stage evidence.

### 4. 🔬 **Amateur Astronomers Use AI to Flag Exoplanets**

*Interesting Engineering · November 24, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Two amateur astronomers used OpenAI tools to search NASA telescope data and identify potential exoplanet candidates awaiting further verification.

One search identified 49 candidates in observations covering about 19 million stars; another flagged more than 200 signals in Kepler data. An astronomer at the SETI Institute independently examined some of the Kepler signals, but none of the candidates described has been confirmed as a planet.

**Why it matters:** The report illustrates how AI-assisted coding and analysis can help researchers explore large public datasets, while also underscoring the need for independent scientific checks. For practitioners, access to capable tools can broaden participation, but candidate generation remains distinct from validated discovery.

## Key Takeaways

- Protein design results are beginning to include laboratory tests, but specialist tools and experimental validation remain essential.
- Scientific AI investment is extending to the data and evaluation infrastructure that connects models with measured outcomes.
- AI can help generate candidates across disciplines; independent verification remains the deciding step.

## What to Watch

Watch for Leash’s promised results on whether its environments improve model performance, and for updates on PEP-5089’s IND-enabling program. Follow-up observations will determine whether any of the reported exoplanet candidates are confirmed.
