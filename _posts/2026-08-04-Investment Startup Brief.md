### 1. **SoftBank’s AI Investment Strategy Faces Investor Scrutiny Ahead of Earnings**

## Market Insight

AI continues to dominate venture investment activity, but capital allocation is increasingly shifting from pure software models toward infrastructure, robotics, industrial automation, and enterprise applications. Investors are balancing strong AI growth expectations with greater scrutiny on valuations, founder commitment, and paths to sustainable revenue. ([Reuters][1])

---

## Investment Themes to Watch

* **AI Infrastructure**: Capital continues flowing into companies supporting large-scale AI deployment.
* **Physical AI**: Robotics and industrial automation are emerging as major venture categories.
* **Enterprise AI Applications**: Startups solving workflow automation and business productivity problems remain attractive.
* **Startup Governance**: Investors are paying closer attention to founder incentives and long-term commitment. ([The Economic Times][2])
