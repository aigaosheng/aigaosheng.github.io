# Enterprise AI Brief: Replit Shifts Most AI Spend to OpenAI

**Today:** A thin weekend window with one qualifying story: OpenAI undercutting Anthropic on price to win a major coding-platform customer.

## Top Stories

### 1. 📊 **Replit Moves Most of Its AI Spend From Anthropic to OpenAI After a 30%+ Discount**

*The Information · October 24, 2026*

**Bottom line:** OpenAI won most of Replit's AI spend by discounting by more than 30%, a sign that enterprise model contracts are now decided on price as much as performance.
The Information reports that Replit has moved the majority of its AI spend from Anthropic to OpenAI in a deal worth more than $100M a year. According to the report, OpenAI offered a discount of over 30% after Anthropic declined to extend Replit's earlier discount without a larger spending commitment. Replit's CEO said at TechCrunch Disrupt on October 16 that the company was no longer working with Anthropic. He later clarified that OpenAI is now the default for Replit Agent and that customers can still select Claude models.

**Why it matters:** Large AI contracts are now negotiable, and buyers have leverage when a rival is willing to discount. For Asia-based fintech and enterprise teams, this argues for keeping model routing flexible and renegotiating commitments. Procurement should also check data-processing terms before any default model provider changes. This last point is our own practical read, not a claim from the report.

## Key Takeaways

- Price is becoming a primary lever in enterprise model sales, especially for coding workloads with heavy token use.
- A default-model switch can move most of a customer's spend even when the incumbent stays available as an option.

## What to Watch

Anthropic's financial disclosures ahead of its reportedly planned mid-November listing, which should show how far enterprise discounting is pressuring margins. Also watch whether other large coding-tool customers press for similar terms.
