### 1. Temasek's Net Portfolio Value Reaches S$518 Billion

* **Source**: TechNode Global · **2026-07-08**
* **Summary**: Singapore investment company Temasek reported that its net portfolio value reached S$518 billion (about US$400 billion), doubling over the past decade. The increase was driven by strong performance from listed Singapore companies, realized investment gains, and updated valuation methods for private assets. ([TNGlobal][1])
* **Why It Matters**: The results highlight the resilience of Singapore's long-term investment strategy and reinforce Temasek's importance as a major global institutional investor.
