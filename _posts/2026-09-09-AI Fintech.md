### 8. **The Agentic Bank Faces a Less Glamorous Challenge: Fragmented Data**

* **Source**: FinTech Futures · 2026-09-09
* **Summary**: FinTech Futures highlights a fundamental infrastructure challenge behind agentic banking: financial institutions still store critical information across PDFs, emails, scanned documents, shared drives, legacy applications and structured systems. AI agents may be capable of reasoning and executing actions, but their usefulness depends on reliable access to fragmented institutional data. The analysis argues that the underlying data and integration plumbing remains essential to agentic transformation.
* **Why It Matters**: For banks, the bottleneck may not be the AI model itself but **enterprise data accessibility and workflow integration**. Institutions that solve document intelligence, system connectivity, permissions and auditability could extract more value from the same foundation models than competitors with better models but weaker infrastructure.
* **URL**: [https://www.fintechfutures.com/bankingtech/georgie-cog-the-agentic-bank](https://www.fintechfutures.com/bankingtech/georgie-cog-the-agentic-bank)
