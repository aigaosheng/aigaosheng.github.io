# 2. In-Depth Highlight

## Agentic AI Moves From Experimentation to Financial Infrastructure

The biggest strategic shift this week is the acceleration of **agentic AI adoption across financial services**. Banks and fintech companies are moving beyond traditional AI assistants toward autonomous systems capable of executing workflows, managing customer interactions, and supporting operational decisions. ([FinTech Futures][5])

Companies including Airwallex, ConnectOne, Rabobank, and other financial institutions are investing in AI-powered automation across payments, lending, compliance, and customer service. These developments indicate that AI is becoming part of core financial infrastructure rather than simply a productivity tool.

The impact is significant: banks may achieve lower operating costs, faster service delivery, and more personalised experiences. However, regulators are increasingly focused on AI governance, cyber risks, transparency, and accountability. ([finextra.com][6])

The next competitive advantage in fintech may come from companies that successfully combine proprietary financial data, AI agents, and trusted regulatory frameworks.

Financial institutions are shifting investment toward AI systems that directly improve revenue and operational efficiency. Areas gaining traction include:

The digital asset market is increasingly moving away from consumer speculation toward enterprise applications:
