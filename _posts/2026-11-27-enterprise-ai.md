# Enterprise AI Brief: IBM and Microsoft Launch Agent Platform

**Today:** IBM and Microsoft launched a joint enterprise agent platform targeting regulated industries, while new data showed Southeast Asian developers are moving rapidly from ad-hoc AI use to governed production deployments.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **IBM and Microsoft Launch Imagine Enterprise Agent Platform for Regulated Industries**

*Cloud Watch · Nov 27, 2026*

**Bottom line:** IBM and Microsoft have jointly launched Imagine Enterprise, an agentic platform designed to help enterprises build, operate, and govern AI agents within existing systems, with financial services and insurance as the first target industries.

The platform combines IBM Consulting's experience managing over 6,000 AI agents with Microsoft's AI, data, and security infrastructure, including Microsoft Foundry, Copilot Studio, and Azure . It is designed to integrate with existing enterprise data, workflows, and technology environments, allowing organizations to deploy agents incrementally without replacing legacy systems . Industry-specific frameworks will initially cover financial services, insurance, and retail, with plans to extend to manufacturing, energy, and the public sector .

**Why it matters:** For banks and insurers across Asia navigating MAS and regional regulators' expectations around AI governance, Imagine Enterprise offers a pre-built control layer—observability, compliance support, and governance—that addresses the biggest enterprise objection to agentic AI: auditability. The joint IBM-Microsoft backing signals that agent platforms are consolidating around hyperscaler infrastructure rather than standalone tools.

### 2. 📊 **Agoda Report Finds Four in Five Southeast Asian Developers Now Work Under AI Usage Limits**

*Yonhap / PRNewswire · Nov 27, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Agoda's AI Developer Report 2026 found that 80% of developers across Southeast Asia and India now operate under token quotas, usage caps, or budget restrictions, with limits scaling from 41% at small firms to over 91% at organizations with more than 1,000 employees.

The survey of over 800 developers and engineering leaders across seven markets found that formal AI guidelines correlate with stronger adoption: 56% of developers at organizations with established guidelines report production or broad agent use, versus 43% without them . Nearly a third of respondents (31%) identified managing AI risk as engineering leadership's biggest challenge over the next 12 months, ahead of workflow integration and upskilling . Agoda's own GenAI Proxy serves as a unified layer that authenticates usage, enforces limits, monitors costs, and supports compliance across multiple model providers .

**Why it matters:** This is the clearest data yet that Southeast Asian enterprises are treating AI as a governed business resource rather than an open tool. For fintech and enterprise leaders in Singapore, the finding that governance correlates with higher production adoption—not slower rollout—undercuts the argument that controls impede innovation.

### 3. 🤖 **Endava Achieves Anthropic Select Partner Status for Claude Enterprise Deployments**

*IT Brief UK · Nov 27, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Endava has been named an Anthropic Select Partner, recognizing its work deploying Claude in enterprise settings, with a focus on application modernization via Claude Code and AI agents with governance controls across AWS, Google Cloud, and Azure.

The partnership reflects growing demand from clients moving from AI experimentation to production systems within existing technology estates . Endava's Dava.Flow delivery framework manages software work with governance and traceability, and a recent project with Marinus Solutions built a conversational AI system for port and terminal operations in under two months, estimated to save around 200 hours per month in reporting and analysis . Endava employs over 10,000 people and covers payments, banking, and insurance among its sectors .

**Why it matters:** Anthropic's partner ecosystem is expanding beyond model access into implementation and governance, a signal that enterprises need help operationalizing Claude within regulated workflows rather than just API access. For Asia-based financial institutions evaluating Claude, partners like Endava offer a route to deployment that satisfies internal audit and compliance requirements.

### 4. 📊 **TP Wins Best AI-Powered CX Partner in Banking for Agentic Collections and Compliance**

*ChannelLife Australia · Nov 27, 2026*

**Bottom line:** TP (formerly Teleperformance) was named Best AI-Powered CX Partner in The Digital Banker's 2026 Global AI Innovation Awards, citing measurable results from agentic AI in banking collections, AML investigations, and dispute resolution.

TP's framework combines AI agents, human staff, and banking workflows in a single governed operating model, routing interactions based on intent and policy to AI, a combined process, or a specialist . Reported results include up to 35% reduction in collections costs, 40-60% time savings per AML alert, and 25-40% reduction in manual chargeback work . One international bank client increased collections revenue by 177% using conversation analysis to align outreach with payroll cycles .

**Why it matters:** TP's results provide concrete benchmarks for agentic AI in regulated banking operations, where abstract efficiency claims are insufficient for compliance buy-in. The Asia-Pacific adaptation of these systems across languages and regulations suggests the model is portable to Singapore and regional markets facing similar cost pressures.

## Key Takeaways

- **Governance is becoming the product:** IBM-Microsoft's Imagine Enterprise, Agoda's usage-limit findings, and Endava's governance-focused Claude deployments all point to the same pattern—enterprises are buying control and observability as much as raw model capability.
- **Banking operations are the proving ground:** TP's quantified results in collections and AML, combined with Imagine Enterprise's financial services focus, show that agentic AI is delivering measurable ROI in back-office workflows where human review remains the final gate.
- **Southeast Asia is governing faster than expected:** Agoda's finding that 80% of regional developers work under usage limits, with the highest adoption at large organizations, signals that governance infrastructure is maturing alongside deployment.

## What to Watch

Whether Imagine Enterprise announces its first named financial services customers in Asia, and whether Agoda's report prompts other regional enterprises to publish similar governance benchmarks for developer AI usage.
