**Tech Layoffs 2025: A Stark Reality Check for the Industry**

---

The tech industry is facing a sobering reality in 2025, with over 22,000 job cuts reported so far this year. This follows a staggering 150,000 layoffs across 549 companies in 2024, according to Layoffs.fyi. February alone accounted for more than 16,000 of these reductions, highlighting a significant trend in workforce downsizing.

**Key Highlights:**

* **February 2025:** Over 16,000 layoffs, marking the highest monthly total.
* **April 2025:** More than 24,500 employees affected, indicating a peak in job cuts.
* **July 2025:** Approximately 16,000 layoffs, continuing the downward trend.
* **September 2025:** Around 2,200 job cuts, suggesting a possible stabilization.
* **October 2025:** Notable layoffs include over 100 design roles at Google and more than 500 positions at Paycom.

**Glossary:**

* **Layoffs.fyi:** An independent tracker monitoring tech industry layoffs.
* **AI and Automation:** Technologies increasingly cited as reasons for workforce reductions.
