# AI Finance in Singapore Brief: Reinsurers Launch AI Risk Handbook

**Today:** Singapore's financial sector pushed AI governance further into practice this week, with reinsurers codifying a risk framework and a homegrown digital bank elevating a generative-AI leader into its top technology role.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🏦 **Singapore Reinsurers' Association Launches AI Risk Handbook**

*(Re)in Asia · November 2, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Singapore's reinsurance industry now has a sector-specific framework for assessing how cedants use AI, adding practical teeth to the city-state's broader AI governance push.

The Singapore Reinsurers' Association (SRA) launched its Reinsurers' Artificial Intelligence Handbook at the opening of the 22nd Singapore International Reinsurance Conference (SIRC) on November 2. Developed by a working group drawn from SRA's Technical and Legal & Compliance Subcommittees, the handbook sets out a five-stage assessment cycle covering data and model reliability, third-party AI dependencies, underwriting, claims and portfolio concentration risk, giving underwriting and claims teams a structured way to evaluate how much cedants rely on external AI vendors.

**Why it matters:** The handbook complements MAS-linked initiatives such as the Project MindForge AI Risk Management Toolkit, signalling that Singapore's AI governance expectations are now cascading from banks into reinsurance and underwriting workflows. Regional and global reinsurers operating through Singapore's SIRC hub will likely need to align cedant due diligence with this framework to keep placing business here.

### 2. 🤖 **GXS Bank Promotes Generative-AI Lead to Chief Technology Officer**

*Fintech Singapore · November 2, 2026*

**Bottom line:** GXS Bank, Singapore's Grab- and Singtel-backed digital bank, has handed its technology leadership to an internal executive with a generative-AI and enterprise-technology background.

GXS Bank named Harikrishnan Raguraman as Chief Technology Officer, promoting him from his prior role overseeing enterprise technology, generative AI and tech strategy at executive director level. The appointment comes as GXS Group moves toward closer integration of engineering, data and product teams with parent company Grab, following earlier departures of its Chief Data Officer and Group Head of Business Banking in February 2026.

**Why it matters:** Placing a generative-AI specialist atop GXS Bank's technology function underscores how Singapore's digital banks are treating AI fluency as a prerequisite for senior technical leadership, not just a product feature, as competition intensifies among the city-state's digital-only lenders.

## Key Takeaways

- Singapore's AI governance momentum is broadening beyond banks into insurance and reinsurance, with sector-specific frameworks now layering on top of economy-wide guidance.
- Leadership appointments at digital banks increasingly favour candidates with direct generative-AI and enterprise-AI delivery experience.
- Industry self-regulation (SRA's handbook) is moving in parallel with MAS's own AI Risk Management guidelines, suggesting convergence toward common assessment standards across financial sub-sectors.

## What to Watch

Watch for how reinsurers and cedants operationalise the SRA handbook ahead of MAS's AI Risk Management Guidelines taking effect on October 7, 2027, and for further leadership or structural moves at GXS Bank as its integration with Grab deepens.
