# Payment Brief — 2026-08-14

## Top Stories

### 1. **PSD3 Moves Closer to Reshaping Europe’s Payments Rulebook**

* **Source**: FinTech Global · August 14, 2026
* **Summary**: The EU’s PSD3 and Payment Services Regulation (PSR) are nearing publication, with entry into force expected in Q3 2026 and an approximately 21-month transition period. The reforms would change licensing structures, safeguarding requirements and fraud liability, while requiring real-time IBAN and account-name verification. Non-bank payment providers would also gain more direct access to European payment infrastructure.
* **Why It Matters**: PSD3 could materially change the economics and compliance burden of European payment institutions. The shift of authorised push-payment fraud liability toward PSPs makes fraud prevention, identity verification and transaction monitoring strategic priorities rather than purely compliance functions.

### 2. **Yuno Raises $45M to Scale Payment Orchestration and Agentic AI**

* **Source**: FinTech Futures · August 14, 2026
* **Summary**: Colombia-founded payments infrastructure company Yuno raised a $45 million Series B led by Global PayTech Ventures, taking total funding to $80 million. The company provides a single API connecting merchants to payment methods, processors and fraud services, and says it recovered more than $5 billion in otherwise-failed transaction volume over the past year. Yuno plans to use the funding for international expansion, technology investment and its agentic AI strategy.
* **Why It Matters**: Payment orchestration is evolving from routing infrastructure into an intelligent revenue-management layer. Yuno’s emphasis on AI agents that optimise routing and recover failed transactions also signals a convergence between payment optimisation and agentic commerce.
* **URL**: [https://www.fintechfutures.com/venture-capital-funding/fintech-yuno-45m-series-b](https://www.fintechfutures.com/venture-capital-funding/fintech-yuno-45m-series-b)

### 3. **Apple Pay Chief Jennifer Bailey to Retire in October**

* **Source**: FinTech Futures · August 14, 2026
* **Summary**: Jennifer Bailey, who has led Apple Pay since its launch in 2014, will retire from Apple in October. During her tenure, Apple expanded Apple Pay and Apple Wallet and launched Apple Card, while also developing payment-related fraud prevention and trust-and-safety capabilities. Apple has not yet announced her successor.
* **Why It Matters**: The leadership transition comes as digital wallets increasingly compete with traditional card interfaces for control of the consumer payment experience. The next leadership team will inherit a payments business that sits at the intersection of wallets, cards, identity, commerce and fraud prevention.
* **URL**: [https://www.fintechfutures.com/job-cuts-new-hires/apple-pay-chief-jennifer-bailey-to-retire](https://www.fintechfutures.com/job-cuts-new-hires/apple-pay-chief-jennifer-bailey-to-retire)

### 4. **AI and Stablecoins Converge Around Cross-Border Payments**

* **Source**: FinTech News Switzerland · August 14, 2026
* **Summary**: New research highlights growing overlap between AI and stablecoins in financial services. The cited Forrester research found that 70% of financial-institution professionals identify stablecoins as a key focus, with cross-border transactions the leading objective. The article also points to emerging agentic-payment initiatives from Visa and Mastercard, including stablecoin-enabled infrastructure for automated machine-to-machine payments.
* **Why It Matters**: Stablecoins are moving beyond a crypto-native use case toward payment infrastructure, particularly for cross-border settlement and treasury. Combined with AI agents, they could create payment systems designed for machine-initiated transactions rather than only human checkout.

### 5. **RHB Launches Device Combining DuitNow QR and Card Acceptance**

* **Source**: FinTech News Malaysia · August 14, 2026
* **Summary**: RHB has launched Sound Box Plus, allowing Malaysian merchants to accept DuitNow QR alongside debit and credit card payments through a single device. The device includes NFC, built-in SIM connectivity and real-time voice transaction confirmations. RHB positions it as a more cost-efficient alternative to conventional card terminals, particularly for smaller transactions.
* **Why It Matters**: Combining QR and card acceptance reflects the continued convergence of payment rails at the merchant edge. For SMEs, a single low-cost acceptance device can reduce operational complexity while increasing payment-method coverage.

### 6. **Payment Orchestration Becomes a Board-Level Issue**

* **Source**: The Fintech Times · August 14, 2026
* **Summary**: A new industry analysis argues that payment orchestration is moving beyond an engineering concern as merchants increasingly diversify across multiple processors. The article highlights failed-payment recovery, reconciliation, settlement visibility and fraud management as issues increasingly owned jointly by payments, finance and treasury teams. It also forecasts greater use of AI-powered routing and tighter integration between payment and fraud orchestration.
* **Why It Matters**: The strategic value of payment infrastructure is shifting from simply processing transactions to maximising successful revenue capture. This increases the importance of approval-rate optimisation, multi-PSP resilience and payment operations as enterprise-level capabilities.
* **URL**: [https://thefintechtimes.com/from-routing-to-revenue-why-payment-orchestration-has-become-a-boardroom-issue/](https://thefintechtimes.com/from-routing-to-revenue-why-payment-orchestration-has-become-a-boardroom-issue/)
