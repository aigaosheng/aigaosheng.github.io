# Payment Brief — 2026-08-20

## Top Stories 

### 1. **UnionPay Expands E-Commerce Acceptance Across Nine African Markets**

* **Source**: FinTech Futures · August 20, 2026
* **Summary**: Standard Bank and UnionPay International are expanding online payment acceptance into Botswana, Ghana, Kenya, Malawi, Namibia, Tanzania, Uganda, Zambia and Zimbabwe. Nearly 900 hospitality and tourism merchants will gain UnionPay e-commerce acceptance, with transactions supported in local currency, US dollars or both.
* **Why It Matters**: The rollout expands cross-border digital commerce infrastructure across Africa and strengthens UnionPay's merchant acceptance footprint in a region where international payments and tourism-related commerce are growing.
* **URL**: [https://www.fintechfutures.com/cross-border-payments/unionpay-rolls-out-e-commerce-acceptance-across-nine-african-markets-via-standard-bank-partnership](https://www.fintechfutures.com/cross-border-payments/unionpay-rolls-out-e-commerce-acceptance-across-nine-african-markets-via-standard-bank-partnership)

### 2. **ShopeePay and TenPay Global Launch QR Payments for Southeast Asian Travelers in China**

* **Source**: TNGlobal · August 20, 2026
* **Summary**: ShopeePay and Tencent's TenPay Global have launched a cross-border payment service allowing users in Singapore and Thailand to pay at Weixin Pay merchants in China through QR-code scanning in ShopeePay and Shopee apps. Malaysia, Vietnam and the Philippines are planned for later phases.
* **Why It Matters**: The partnership further connects Southeast Asian digital wallets with China's vast merchant ecosystem, reducing friction for regional travel and accelerating interoperability between previously separate payment networks.
* **URL**: [https://technode.global/2026/08/20/shopeepay-to-let-singapore-thailand-users-scan-and-pay-in-china-via-tencent-global/](https://technode.global/2026/08/20/shopeepay-to-let-singapore-thailand-users-scan-and-pay-in-china-via-tencent-global/)

### 3. **Nium Launches Domestic Card Issuance in the United States**

* **Source**: Fintech News Singapore · August 20, 2026
* **Summary**: Nium has launched domestic card issuance in the US, extending its multi-region issuing platform across North America, APAC, Europe and the Middle East. ### 4. **PayPal and Venmo Expand Into US Tuition Payments**

* **Source**: FinTech Global · August 20, 2026
* **Summary**: PayPal and Venmo are expanding into higher-education payments through integrations with Illumia, Nelnet Campus Commerce and TouchNet. Students and families at participating universities can use linked bank accounts, cards or PayPal and Venmo balances to pay tuition and fees.
* **Why It Matters**: Tuition represents a large, high-value payment category that remains relatively fragmented. The integrations extend PayPal and Venmo beyond retail and peer-to-peer transactions into recurring institutional payment infrastructure.
* **URL**: [https://fintech.global/2026/08/20/paypal-and-venmo-expand-into-us-tuition-payments/](https://fintech.global/2026/08/20/paypal-and-venmo-expand-into-us-tuition-payments/)

### 5. **TerraPay and Deutsche Bank Expand Cross-Border Payment Connectivity**

* **Source**: BeBeez International · August 20, 2026
* **Summary**: TerraPay is collaborating with Deutsche Bank to access the bank's correspondent banking network, payments capabilities and foreign-exchange services. The partnership is designed to strengthen TerraPay's settlement infrastructure and improve the efficiency, speed and reliability of cross-border payment flows, particularly for USD settlement.
* **Why It Matters**: The deal illustrates the increasing convergence of bank infrastructure and fintech payment networks. Access to established correspondent banking capabilities can help payment specialists scale internationally without rebuilding traditional settlement rails market by market.
* **URL**: [https://bebeez.eu/2026/08/20/terrapay-and-deutsche-bank-expand-global-payment-connectivity/](https://bebeez.eu/2026/08/20/terrapay-and-deutsche-bank-expand-global-payment-connectivity/)
