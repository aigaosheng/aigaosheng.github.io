### Pinterest Unveils the Next Generation of AI Personalized Boards

Pinterest is rolling out a wave of new features designed to make personalized discovery both smarter and more delightful for users in the U.S. and Canada. Their latest experiment harnesses AI to curate boards, suggest outfits, and tailor shopping recommendations, marking a major leap from simple organizational tools to dynamic, taste-driven platforms.

#### Main Insights and Features
- The headline innovation is the 'Styled for you' collage tool, which lets users mash up favorite fashion Pins to generate outfits suited to their unique style.
- Simply tap on an item in your collage, and Pinterest’s AI will instantly recommend complementary pieces, enabling engaging mix-and-match experiences.
- 'Boards made for you' harnesses a blend of editorial curation and AI-powered suggestions, populating users’ feeds and inboxes with trending styles, shoppable links, and weekly looks.
- The updates position Pinterest not only as a discovery and inspiration engine but also as an “AI-enabled shopping assistant,” in step with the company’s strategic shift announced by its CEO earlier this year.

#### User Experience Advances
- Users will see new tabs that sort and suggest Pins by category — ‘Make It Yours’ for fashion and decor, ‘More Ideas’ for variety, and ‘All Saves’ for easy access to previous Pins.
- Pinterest is simultaneously experimenting with global rollout, promising upcoming access beyond North America in the months ahead.

#### Balancing Innovation with Moderation
- While Pinterest leans into AI, it’s also drawing a line: the platform recently rolled out labels for AI-generated images and controls for limiting their appearance in feeds, affirming its commitment to transparency and content quality.

***

### Glossary
- **AI-powered Recommendations:** Suggestions generated by artificial intelligence algorithms that analyze user preferences and behavior.
- **Collage Tool:** A feature that lets users combine various Pins into a single, styled layout for visual inspiration.
- **Shoppable Content:** Online material featuring direct links to products, integrating shopping into the discovery process.
