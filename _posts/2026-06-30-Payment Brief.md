# Payment Brief — 2026-06-30

## Top Stories

### 1. UK Regulator Moves to Open Apple and Google App Stores to Alternative Payments
- **Source**: Reuters · 2026-06-30
- **Summary**: The UK's Competition and Markets Authority (CMA) proposed measures that would allow app developers to direct users toward alternative payment methods outside Apple's App Store and Google Play. The regulator also suggested that any remaining platform fees should be significantly lower and objectively justified. Additionally, Apple could be required to provide third-party access to NFC functionality on iPhones.
- **Why It Matters**: This is one of the most significant regulatory developments affecting digital payments and app commerce. Lower payment friction and reduced commissions could reshape mobile payment economics for developers, payment providers, and fintech platforms.
- **URL**: https://www.reuters.com/world/uk-regulator-proposes-easing-apple-google-app-store-payment-rules-2026-06-30/
