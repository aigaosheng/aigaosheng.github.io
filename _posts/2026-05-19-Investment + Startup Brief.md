### 4. Q1 2026 VC Funding Hits Record $297B, But "Barbell Problem" Emerges
- **SoftwareSeni / Tech Insider** · 2026-05-19
- **Summary**: Global venture capital funding reached a record $297 billion in Q1 2026, with AI capturing approximately $242 billion (81%) of all dollars. - **Why It Matters**: The "barbell problem" reveals a bifurcated market where frontier AI labs raise infrastructure-scale rounds while non-AI and application-layer startups struggle. Enterprise buyers must evaluate vendor dependency risks, as squeezed middle-stage companies face potential acquisition or shutdown .
