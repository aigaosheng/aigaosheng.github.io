* **Summary**: Injective's affiliate, Injective Institutional Services, has registered with the SEC as a transfer agent. The registration is intended to provide regulated ownership-record infrastructure for securities represented on blockchain networks and strengthen Injective's positioning in institutional RWA tokenization.
* **Why It Matters**: Transfer-agent infrastructure addresses one of the central barriers to institutional tokenization: maintaining legally recognized ownership records alongside on-chain representations. ## Executive Takeaway

The strongest signal today is that tokenization is moving from **asset representation toward financial-market utility**. Institutional attention is increasingly centered on collateral, settlement, ownership records and regulated infrastructure—areas where tokenized assets can potentially change how capital moves through traditional markets.

The implication for investors and financial institutions is significant: the next phase of tokenization may be determined less by the number of assets issued on-chain and more by whether those assets can become **productive financial primitives inside institutional workflows**.
