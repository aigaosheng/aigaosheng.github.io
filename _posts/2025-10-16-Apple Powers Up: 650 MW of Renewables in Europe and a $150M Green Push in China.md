---

**Apple's Green Surge: 650 MW in Europe and $150M for China**

Apple is accelerating its renewable energy strategy, announcing a significant expansion of 650 megawatts (MW) of clean power across Europe and a $150 million investment in China to support its suppliers' transition to renewable energy sources. This move aligns with Apple's commitment to achieving carbon neutrality by 2030.

**European Expansion: Solar and Wind Projects**

In Europe, Apple has secured contracts for renewable energy projects in several countries:

* **Greece and Latvia:** 110 MW solar farms in each country.
* **Spain:** 131 MW solar capacity.
* **Poland:** 40 MW solar project.
* **Romania:** 99 MW wind farm.
* **Italy:** A portion of a 129 MW solar-wind hybrid project.

These projects are expected to offset the energy consumption of Apple products used by European customers, which accounts for nearly one-third of the company's carbon footprint.

**China Investment: Supporting Supplier Transition**

In China, Apple plans to invest $150 million to help its suppliers transition to renewable energy. Currently, over 90% of Apple's manufacturing and production in China is powered by renewable sources. This investment aims to further reduce the carbon footprint of Apple's supply chain.

**Strategic Implications**

Apple's renewable energy initiatives are not just about sustainability; they also make economic sense. Solar and wind energy are among the most cost-effective forms of new generating capacity, often undercutting coal and natural gas. Additionally, these projects can be completed relatively quickly, providing a reliable power source for Apple's data centers and AI operations.

By investing in renewable energy, Apple is setting a benchmark for other tech companies, demonstrating that environmental responsibility can go hand-in-hand with business growth.

**Glossary**

* **Megawatt (MW):** A unit of power equal to one million watts.
* **Carbon Neutrality:** Achieving a net-zero carbon footprint by balancing emitted carbon with an equivalent amount offset or sequestered.
* **Power Purchase Agreement (PPA):** A contract between a power producer and a buyer to purchase electricity at a predetermined price.
