# Qwen Turns Research Into Ready-Made Webpages and Podcasts — One Click to Publish

> Imagine finishing a research brief and, with a single click, having it live as a slick webpage and a two-voice podcast episode ready to share. That’s the new reality Qwen just shipped.

Alibaba’s Qwen team rolled out a major upgrade to **Qwen Deep Research** that converts research outputs into interactive websites and multi-speaker podcasts — no dev ops or audio studio needed. The feature is available inside Qwen Chat and compresses the typical “research → edit → publish” workflow into a few clicks.

---

## What happened 

* Deep Research reports can now be exported as **fully hosted webpages** or **multi-speaker podcasts**.
* The system is powered across the stack by:

  * **Qwen3-Coder** for web generation
  * **Qwen-Image** for visuals/illustration
  * **Qwen3-TTS** for podcast audio
* The entire workflow — source aggregation, reasoning, formatting, page generation, and hosting — sits inside one product.
* The release positions Qwen as a *content production and distribution* layer, not just a language model.

## Why it matters

This release is not just about making research “better” — it’s about making research **instantly publishable**.

Instead of:

1. Write →
2. Proofread →
3. Export →
4. Build page →
5. Host →
6. Produce audio →
7. Distribute

Qwen collapses it into:
**Write → Click → Publish.**

For analysts, teachers, journalists, and content teams, that’s a shift from *artifact creation* to *audience delivery* — dramatically shorter time to public impact.

## Bigger implications

* **AI is moving from copilots to *publishers***
  Qwen is now functioning like an integrated CMS + narrator.
* **Model value is shifting upward into workflows**
  The moat isn’t the model; it’s *distribution* and *hosting*.
* **Frictionless “idea → media”**
  Soon, writing may no longer be the bottleneck — *distribution* won’t be either.
