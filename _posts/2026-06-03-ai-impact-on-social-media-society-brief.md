### 1. Hackers exploited Meta’s AI support flow to hijack Instagram accounts
- **TechCrunch** · 2026-06-01
- **Summary**: TechCrunch reported that Instagram fixed a security issue that let attackers use Meta’s AI-powered support assistant to add a new email address and reset a victim’s password. The reported victims included notable public-interest accounts, and the attack reportedly worked without first compromising the victim’s original email account. The episode shows how AI automation in platform support can expand attack surfaces when identity and recovery controls are weak.
- **Why It Matters**: This is a high-signal example of AI changing platform trust and safety risk in a very direct way: not through content, but through account security infrastructure. For social platforms, it underscores that AI-assisted support tooling can create system-level vulnerabilities with immediate reputational and civic consequences.
- **URL**: https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/01/hackers-hijacked-instagram-accounts-by-tricking-meta-ai-support-chatbot-into-granting-access/

### 2. India’s communications minister warned that AI and deepfakes are creating a journalism credibility crisis
- **MSN** · 2026-06-03
- **Summary**: Reporting on remarks by Jyotiraditya Scindia highlighted growing concern that AI-generated media and deepfakes are undermining trust in journalism. The comments frame synthetic media not just as a content-moderation problem but as a structural threat to the credibility layer that helps audiences interpret information online.
- **Why It Matters**: The most important societal effect of generative AI on social media may be erosion of epistemic trust, not just isolated false posts. When senior policymakers openly describe a credibility crisis, it suggests further pressure for provenance, labeling, and authentication systems across news and platforms.
