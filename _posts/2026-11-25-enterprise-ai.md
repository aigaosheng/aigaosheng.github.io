# Enterprise AI Brief: Automation Anywhere Closes Boost.ai Deal

**Today:** Enterprise AI announcements show vendors connecting agents to business systems and specialist data, while buyers look for evidence of governed, production-ready deployments.

## Top Stories

### 1. 📊 **Automation Anywhere Closes Boost.ai Deal**

*PR Newswire · November 24, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Automation Anywhere has completed its acquisition of conversational AI provider Boost.ai, combining customer-facing voice and chat tools with enterprise automation.

The company says Boost.ai supports more than 36 languages and handles about 50,000 calls monthly for nearly 100 financial institutions through Jack Henry. Automation Anywhere plans to connect those conversations to actions across enterprise systems, including identity checks, account updates and compliance processes.

**Why it matters:** The deal reflects a push to make agents complete multi-system work, rather than stop after answering a customer. For financial institutions, the proposed model could streamline service, but its value will depend on how reliably it handles controls and hand-offs across core systems.

### 2. 🤖 **Couchbase and AWS Expand Agent Data Infrastructure**

*PR Newswire · November 24, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Couchbase and AWS have signed a strategic collaboration agreement to expand access to Couchbase’s AI Data Plane on AWS.

The platform combines operational data, search and analytics over a single copy of JSON data, with integrations for Amazon Bedrock and agent frameworks including Strands, LangGraph, CrewAI and LlamaIndex. Couchbase says the arrangement is intended to help enterprises manage agent context, cost and governance as they move deployments beyond pilots.

**Why it matters:** A shared data foundation can reduce the integration work required to connect agents to current business information. For Asia-based enterprises, the agreement also highlights the importance of being able to deploy data services across cloud, private data centre and edge environments.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/couchbase-signs-strategic-collaboration-agreement-with-aws-to-expand-delivery-of-the-ai-data-plane-for-production-ai-agents-302948880.html)

### 3. 📊 **Infosys Reports CMMI AI Maturity Level 5**

*PR Newswire · November 25, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Infosys says it is the first organization assessed at Level 5 of the CMMI AI Maturity Model.

The assessment, conducted by a licensed CMMI partner, sampled more than 1,000 projects across Infosys service lines, geographies and functions. Infosys says it reviewed how AI is governed, deployed and measured, and that the assessment found statistically validated business performance gains.

**Why it matters:** The assessment offers a framework for discussing AI operating maturity beyond model choice, though the reported results are specific to Infosys and do not establish a universal benchmark. Its experience may also shape how the IT services firm supports clients building governance and measurement into large-scale AI programs.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/infosys-becomes-the-worlds-first-organization-to-achieve-cmmi-ai-maturity-aim-level-5-302950378.html)

### 4. 🤖 **Ansarada Links Data Rooms With Grata AI**

*PR Newswire · November 25, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Ansarada and Grata Intelligence have integrated permissioned data room content into an agentic workspace for M&A teams.

The integration lets users work with live Ansarada data alongside Grata’s market and transaction information, firm knowledge and Blueflame M&A agent. Ansarada says existing data room permissions determine what users can access, and the integration is available to Grata customers with active Ansarada rooms.

**Why it matters:** Bringing deal documents into an AI workflow could speed up diligence and preparation of investment memos while keeping access controls in place. The partnership is relevant to Asia-Pacific deal teams handling sensitive transaction information across advisers, investors and companies.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/ansarada-partners-with-grata-intelligence-to-accelerate-due-diligence-and-deal-execution-302949163.html)

### 5. 🤖 **Clarivate Develops IPOne With Corporate Partners**

*PR Newswire · November 25, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Clarivate is developing an AI-native intellectual property platform with corporate and law firm partners, including Mercari, Brndpwr and Sterne Kessler.

IPOne is intended to connect patent, trademark and litigation intelligence for research and other IP workflows. Clarivate says customers will be able to use the platform directly or integrate its data into enterprise AI tools such as Claude, Microsoft Copilot and ChatGPT; the product is still in development.

**Why it matters:** The announcement points to a market for domain-specific, licensed information that can be used inside general enterprise AI environments. Its eventual utility will depend on the quality of its source data, integration controls and fit with professional review processes.

### 6. 🤖 **Singapore’s GoVeda Launches IP Intelligence Agent**

*PR Newswire · November 25, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Singapore-based GoVeda has launched an AI agent for patent research and intellectual property workflows.

The company says the service searches more than 220 million patents, 350 million research papers and the live web, and supports tasks such as prior-art searches, patent landscapes and valuation. GoVeda also reports that its agent found 81.8% of references cited by examiners in its own patent-analysis tests; that result is a company-reported benchmark.

**Why it matters:** The launch brings a specialist research tool from a Singapore company to IP teams, startups and research groups. Its usefulness will turn on how well professionals can verify its findings and fit the output into existing review and filing processes.

### 7. 📊 **Driven Tech Completes ADEX and TekBay Acquisitions**

*PR Newswire · November 24, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Driven Tech has completed its acquisitions of ADEX and TekBay, adding engineering capacity to its enterprise AI and cloud services.

The company says the teams will join its Applied Intelligence unit and contribute expertise in AI, data engineering, agentic AI and cloud-native development. It also plans to use the expanded organization to develop and deliver enterprise solutions, including its Lasius orchestration and security platform.

**Why it matters:** The completed deals show a services firm adding technical talent to pursue enterprise AI delivery, rather than relying only on product development. The next signal will be whether the combined teams translate that capacity into customer deployments.

### 8. 🤖 **Altimetrik Earns AWS Agentic AI Specialization**

*Altimetrik · November 24, 2026*

**Bottom line:** AWS has recognized Altimetrik in its Agentic AI Specialization for consulting partners.

Altimetrik says its work includes building and scaling agents using Amazon Bedrock Agents and its ALTi AIOS platform, with multi-agent orchestration for business processes. The company describes the designation as recognition of its technical expertise and customer delivery experience.

**Why it matters:** Partner specializations can help enterprises shortlist implementation providers, but they are not a substitute for assessing performance in a buyer’s own environment. For organizations considering agent projects, the practical test remains whether a provider can meet production requirements for governance, monitoring and cost control.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.altimetrik.com/news/altimetrik-achieves-the-aws-agentic-ai-specialization/)

## Key Takeaways

- Enterprise AI vendors are connecting agents to data, systems and domain-specific information so they can support end-to-end workflows.
- Governance, permissions and measurable outcomes remain central to deployments in financial services and other sensitive sectors.
- Specialist platforms and implementation partners are extending enterprise AI into functions such as M&A, intellectual property and customer service.

## What to Watch

Watch for customer deployments and independently verifiable results from these platforms and partnerships, especially evidence on accuracy, control effectiveness, cost and time saved.
