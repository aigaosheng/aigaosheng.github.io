# Payment Brief — 2026-08-06

## Top Stories

### 1. **India's Parliament Passes Bill That Could Enable Charges on UPI Transactions**

* **Source**: The Economic Times · 2026-08-06
* **Summary**: India's Lok Sabha passed amendments to the Payment and Settlement Systems Act that would allow the central government to authorize fees, including Merchant Discount Rate (MDR), on specified digital payment methods such as UPI. While charges are not introduced immediately, the legislation creates the legal framework for future pricing decisions.
* **Why It Matters**: This represents one of the most significant policy shifts in India's digital payments ecosystem. If implemented, it could materially improve the economics of banks and payment providers while reshaping merchant adoption and transaction pricing.

### 2. **Mastercard Says UPI Expands Rather Than Threatens Digital Payments**

* **Source**: The Economic Times · 2026-08-06
* **Summary**: Mastercard's India leadership stated that the company does not view India's UPI network as a competitive threat. Instead, it believes UPI has accelerated overall digital payment adoption and expanded opportunities across the payments ecosystem.
* **Why It Matters**: The comments reflect how global card networks increasingly position themselves alongside domestic real-time payment rails rather than against them, emphasizing value-added services and cross-border capabilities.
* **URL**: [https://m.economictimes.com/industry/banking/finance/upi-no-challenge-to-mastercard-has-expanded-digital-payments-exec/articleshow/132994603.cms](https://m.economictimes.com/industry/banking/finance/upi-no-challenge-to-mastercard-has-expanded-digital-payments-exec/articleshow/132994603.cms)
