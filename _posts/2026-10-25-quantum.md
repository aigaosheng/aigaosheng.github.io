# Quantum Brief: Defence Quantum Funding Rises on Two DARPA Deals

**Today:** New data analysis shows US defence quantum funding jumped in 2026 mainly on announced DARPA deals rather than committed cash, which matters to anyone sizing quantum vendors or valuations.

## Top Stories

### 1. 📊 **Disclosed Defence Money for Quantum Computing Up Two- to Threefold in 2026**
*Quantum Zeitgeist · October 25, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Announced DARPA agreements, not obligated money, account for all of the 2026 rise in disclosed US defence funding for quantum computing.

Quantum Zeitgeist's tally of priced US Department of Defense quantum computing awards shows $80 million in 2023, $38 million in 2024 and $67 million in 2025. For 2026 through October 11 it shows $157 million or $188 million, depending on whether PsiQuantum's $125 million DARPA agreement includes an earlier $32 million deal. The increase comes from that agreement and Diraq's announced $51 million first tranche, while obligated money on the federal register fell from $35 million in 2025 to $12 million so far. The author cautions that part of the rise may reflect more disclosure rather than more money, and that the $300 million figures attached to DARPA's final benchmarking stage are caps, not payments.

**Why it matters:** Only obligations on the federal register are firm, so funding headlines overstate committed cash. In our view, enterprise buyers vetting quantum suppliers should treat dependence on government money as a supplier-risk factor.

### 2. 📊 **Verifiable Quantum Defence Spending Runs to Hundreds of Millions, Not Billions**
*Quantum Zeitgeist · October 25, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Headline figures in the billions mostly reflect contract ceilings or paid market reports, while the defence quantum spending that can be checked is far smaller.

The analysis puts Pentagon quantum research at about $320 million in FY2023, a figure read off a government chart to within roughly $3 million. It notes that the $151 billion SHIELD missile defence contract is a ceiling, and that IonQ, Infleqtion, Vector Atomic and QuSecure each hold a single $500 order with no task orders won by late October. Paid market reports put the 2024 quantum warfare market anywhere from about $0.2 billion to $1.9 billion. Rigetti drew 48% of its 2025 revenue from the US government and Infleqtion about 60%, though neither separates defence from civil agencies.

**Why it matters:** Anyone sizing the quantum market or a vendor's public-sector exposure should check whether a figure is a budget, an award, a ceiling or revenue before quoting it. The measures should not be added together.

## Key Takeaways
- The 2026 funding jump is announced money from two DARPA agreements, while obligated money on the register fell.
- Ceilings and unverifiable market reports inflate headline numbers, and the checkable defence figure is in the hundreds of millions of dollars.
- Several listed quantum firms lean heavily on government customers, but none discloses its defence share.

## What to Watch
Whether any of the four quantum firms holding SHIELD positions wins its first task order, since that is where real money would flow. Any DARPA budget detail on final-stage payments would also help separate disclosure effects from genuine growth.
