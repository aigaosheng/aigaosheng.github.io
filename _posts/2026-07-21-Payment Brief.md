### 1. **Ant International Raises $1.2 Billion to Accelerate Global Payments Expansion**
- **Source** · Reuters · 2026-07-21

### 2. **Brazil’s Pix Payment System Becomes a Global Payments Geopolitical Flashpoint**
- **Source** · Reuters · 2026-07-21
- **Summary**: Brazil’s instant payment network Pix is drawing international attention as governments debate the future of digital payment infrastructure. Pix now serves around 170 million users and has become a major alternative to traditional card-based payment systems. The system’s growth has triggered discussions about competition, sovereignty, and the role of central-bank-operated payment rails.
- **Why It Matters**: The Pix model is influencing global discussions around real-time payments, financial inclusion, and whether public payment infrastructure can compete with private card networks.
