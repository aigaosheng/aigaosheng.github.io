# AI Governance Brief: JPMorgan, GM And Pfizer Seek AI Policy Voice

**Today:** Major AI deployers are organising to influence US policy, while US-China discussions highlight the region’s interest in AI safety coordination.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🏦 **JPMorgan, GM And Pfizer Seek A Voice In AI Policy**

*Axios · November 19, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Seven large companies have launched a council to bring AI deployers’ concerns into US policy discussions.

The America’s AI Deployer Council includes FedEx, GE Aerospace, General Motors, JPMorgan Chase, Pfizer, John Deere and United Airlines. Its members argue that businesses adopting AI need a greater role in safety discussions, including better access to incident information and advance notice of risks that could affect systems they rely on.

**Why it matters:** More direct deployer input could shape expectations for how AI developers share safety information with business customers. For companies operating across Asia and the US, the council’s priorities may also signal which deployment risks are gaining attention among large enterprise buyers.

### 2. 🌐 **US-China AI Safety Coordination Draws Attention At APEC**

*Associated Press · November 20, 2026*

**Bottom line:** APEC discussions brought renewed attention to the possibility of US-China coordination on AI safety.

An Asia Society Policy Institute analyst told the Associated Press that China had affirmed concerns about AI safety and security and welcomed bilateral work. The report said it was unclear whether officials involved in the countries’ diplomatic channel on AI met in Shenzhen.

**Why it matters:** Even without a confirmed meeting or new agreement, bilateral safety discussions matter to organisations managing AI across borders. Enterprises in Singapore and the wider region should watch for concrete areas of coordination that could affect governance expectations or operating practices.

## Key Takeaways

- Large AI adopters are seeking a formal role in policy discussions about deployment risks and safety information.
- AI safety is also appearing in US-China diplomacy, though the reporting does not confirm a new agreement or official meeting.
- For regional businesses, policy engagement and cross-border coordination are signals to track as governance expectations develop.

## What to Watch

Watch for the AI Deployer Council’s policy agenda and any follow-up from the US-China diplomatic channel on AI. Concrete proposals or confirmed meetings would clarify whether either development is moving beyond discussion.
