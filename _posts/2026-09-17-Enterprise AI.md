## Executive Takeaway

Enterprise AI is increasingly becoming a **systems-integration and operational-governance problem rather than a model-selection problem**. Today's developments point toward a common architecture: enterprise data + models + agents + tools + identity/permissions + governance + human oversight.

The strategic opportunity is therefore moving up the stack—from deploying individual copilots to building an **enterprise AI operating layer capable of safely orchestrating real business work**.
