## Top Stories

### 1. Google Pushes “Agentic AI” as the New Enterprise Standard

**Source:** Reuters | **Published:** April 22, 2026
**Summary:** At Google Cloud Next 2026, Google unveiled its **Gemini Enterprise** platform and a new generation of AI agents designed to autonomously execute business workflows. The company emphasized that enterprise AI is moving beyond experimentation into production-scale deployment, with integrated governance and security controls. Google also highlighted heavy infrastructure investment and growing enterprise adoption. ([Reuters][1])
**Why It Matters:** The shift from copilots to **autonomous agents** signals a structural change in enterprise software—AI is no longer assisting workflows but increasingly running them.

### 2. Google Launches 8th-Gen TPUs and Agent Platform to Rival Nvidia

**Source:** Investors Business Daily | **Published:** April 22, 2026
**Summary:** Google introduced its latest **TPU 8-series chips** alongside a new enterprise agent platform, delivering significant performance gains for training and inference. The platform supports autonomous AI agents capable of executing complex enterprise tasks, with early adoption from major corporations like Home Depot and PepsiCo. ([Investors][2])
**Why It Matters:** Vertical integration of **AI hardware + agent platforms** is becoming a competitive moat in enterprise AI.

* **Agentic AI is now the dominant paradigm** replacing copilots
