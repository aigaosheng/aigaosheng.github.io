### AI+Fintech Brief — May 10, 2026

### Top Stories

- **SEC Signals Rulemaking for Onchain Markets and AI-Driven Finance**
    - **Source:** AInvest · May 9, 2026
    - **Summary:** The SEC is actively examining how federal securities laws apply to onchain trading systems and AI-driven finance, focusing on blockchain-based trading mechanisms, broker definitions, and automated settlement systems. Chair Paul Atkins indicated potential rulemaking in these areas and expressed support for the CLARITY Act, a proposed legislative effort to create a shared regulatory framework between the SEC and CFTC for crypto markets.
    - **Why It Matters:** This marks a significant step toward regulatory clarity for AI-powered financial products and blockchain-based trading platforms, reducing uncertainty for market participants and potentially accelerating institutional adoption of on-chain finance.
    - **URL:** [SEC Examines Onchain Trading, AI, and Blockchain Finance Regulations](https://www.ainvest.com/news/sec-examines-onchain-trading-ai-blockchain-finance-regulations-2605/)

- **IMF Warns AI Amplifies Cybersecurity Risks to Global Financial Stability**
    - **Source:** 163.com (环球网) · May 9, 2026
    - **Summary:** The IMF warned that advanced AI models can rapidly identify and exploit system vulnerabilities, amplifying cyber risks in financial systems that rely on shared digital infrastructure such as cloud services and payment networks. The organization urged regulators to strengthen cyber defenses and conduct regular stress tests to mitigate potential disruptions to payment systems and market confidence.
    - **Why It Matters:** As financial institutions accelerate AI adoption, this formal warning from the IMF underscores that AI-related cyber risk is now a systemic stability concern, not just an operational issue, likely prompting stricter regulatory scrutiny.
    - **URL:** [国际货币基金组织警示：人工智能对金融稳定构成潜在威胁](https://www.163.com/dy/article/KSG17P6T0514R9OJ.html?referFrom=)
