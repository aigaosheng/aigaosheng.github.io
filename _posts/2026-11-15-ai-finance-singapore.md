# AI Finance in Singapore Brief: MAS Previews Agentic AI At Fintech Festival

**Today:** With Singapore FinTech Festival opening this week, MAS has set agentic AI and its governance as a central theme, a signal executives should read as a preview of where supervisory attention is headed next.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🏦 **MAS Sets Agentic AI And Accountability As Fintech Festival Centrepiece**

*Monetary Authority of Singapore · November 15, 2026*

**Bottom line:** MAS and the Global Finance & Technology Network previewed the 11th Singapore FinTech Festival, confirming that agentic AI, tokenisation and accountability for autonomous systems will anchor this year's agenda.

The festival runs from November 18 to 20 at Singapore EXPO under the theme "The Coming Rewiring of the Financial System," organised around five forces: technology, geoeconomics, capital, talent and policy. MAS said the event is expected to draw more than 70,000 participants, 600-plus sponsors and exhibitors, and 20,000-plus pre-scheduled meetings, building on 2025's turnout of attendees from 142 countries. A separate invitation-only programme, the Insights2040 Annual Meetings on November 16 and 17, will bring senior public- and private-sector leaders together specifically to discuss establishing accountability and liability frameworks for agentic systems.

**Why it matters:** The preview confirms that Singapore's regulator is treating agentic AI governance, not just adoption, as the defining fintech issue of the year, which matters for any bank, insurer or payments firm operating in the city-state ahead of sessions on programmable money and agentic commerce. Asian financial institutions planning AI agent rollouts should expect the accountability and liability questions raised at Insights2040 to shape MAS's supervisory posture well beyond the festival week.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2026/singapore-fintech-festival-2026-explores-rewiring-of-global-finance)

## Key Takeaways

- Singapore is positioning agentic AI governance, rather than raw adoption metrics, as its signature fintech narrative for 2026.
- Accountability and liability for autonomous AI agents are moving from technical white papers into senior-leader policy discussions via Insights2040.
- The scale of SFF 2026 (70,000+ attendees, 600+ exhibitors) means agentic AI framing set this week will influence vendor and bank messaging through year-end.

## What to Watch

Watch for concrete outcomes from the Insights2040 Annual Meetings on November 16-17 and the festival's technology-track sessions from November 18-20, which should translate today's preview themes into specific MAS positions or industry commitments on agentic AI accountability.
