# AI Fintech Brief: Federal Bank Adds AI Interviews to Apprentice Hiring

**Today:** A thin news window brings one verifiable, in-scope item: an Indian lender putting AI into its recruitment pipeline, a reminder that bank AI adoption extends beyond customer-facing products.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **Federal Bank Brings AI Interviews to Graduate Apprentice Hiring**

*Elets BFSI · November 21, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Federal Bank has added an AI-conducted interview to the selection process for its graduate apprentice programme, using AI in talent acquisition rather than in customer-facing banking.

According to Elets BFSI, the Indian private-sector lender announced on November 20 that the second selection round now combines an online aptitude test with an AI interview in a single sitting. The programme is a one-year apprenticeship under India's Apprentices Act, with a stipend of Rs 19,650 a month listed on the bank's careers portal. Applications close at 11:59 pm on November 24, 2026, and the bank's chief human resources officer described the change as part of a digital-first talent strategy.

**Why it matters:** The story is small, but it shows banks in Asia applying AI to internal functions such as hiring, where the risks are fairness and explainability rather than customer-facing errors. Executives should treat candidate screening as another AI use case that needs governance. Coverage here rests on a single trade-press report of the bank's announcement, and the source gives no assessment criteria, vendor or outcome data.

## Key Takeaways

- Verified, in-window AI Fintech news was sparse in this edition, and most search results were dated earlier in November or before the cutoff.
- Banks are extending AI into back-office and HR workflows as well as customer service and lending.
- Hiring is a high-scrutiny AI use case, so how much detail banks disclose about assessment criteria and human oversight is worth tracking.

## What to Watch

Federal Bank's apprenticeship applications close on November 24, 2026, so any disclosure about applicant volumes or how the AI interview is scored would be a useful early signal. Also watch whether other Indian and Asian lenders announce similar AI-assisted screening.
