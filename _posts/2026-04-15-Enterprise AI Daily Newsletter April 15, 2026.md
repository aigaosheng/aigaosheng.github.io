## **Key Takeaways (Signal Summary)**

* **Platformization wins:** Enterprise AI competition is shifting toward full-stack ecosystems (OpenAI, HubSpot).
* **Data + context > models:** Data access and contextual reasoning are the real bottlenecks (Cloudera, Neo4j).
* **Agentic AI is inevitable:** Enterprises are moving from copilots to autonomous workflow execution.
* **Security becomes central:** AI introduces new cyber risks, requiring AI-native defense systems (IBM, Goldman Sachs).
* **From pilots to production:** The defining challenge is organizational integration—not experimentation.
