Several weekend sales end on Sunday 25 October, including cookware at Suntec, a SMEG showroom sale and Challenger's tech and home offers. Singapore Airlines and Scoot have also opened their yearly discounted-fare sale, and Shopee's 11.11 campaign is due to start next week.

## Top picks

**1. Le Creuset Last Family Sale: up to 80% off selected cookware**
Le Creuset is holding what it calls its last family sale in Singapore at Suntec Convention Centre, Hall 334–336, until Sunday 25 October. Discounts apply to selected items only, and the cookware range includes cast iron pots, stoneware, mugs and accessories.
*Why it's worth it:* It has the biggest stated saving of any live in-person sale we found, and it ends tomorrow. *Caveat:* "Up to 80%" is a ceiling, not the average. One site reports that all sales are final, so check before you pay.

**2. Singapore Airlines and Scoot "Time To Fly" fares**
SIA and Scoot are selling discounted tickets from Singapore to 125 destinations. SIA return fares in Economy, Premium Economy and Business go to 78 destinations for travel from 1 January to 30 September 2027. Scoot one-way fares go to 70 destinations for travel from 9 November 2026 to 15 October 2027. The online sale runs to 30 October, and a travel fair is on at Suntec until 25 October.
*Why it's worth it:* It is an official, large-scale sale and anyone can use it. *Caveat:* The announcement does not give route prices, so compare fares on the airline sites before you book.

**3. SMEG Craziest Mega Sale: up to 75% off at the Marina One showroom**
SMEG is running a two-day showroom sale today and tomorrow. Listed prices include a hand mixer at S$119.00 (usual S$258.00) and a frying pan at S$48.00 (usual S$208.00). A dryer is listed at S$760.00 (usual S$1,980.00).
*Why it's worth it:* The listed prices are concrete and the savings on small items are large. *Caveat:* The usual prices come from one roundup site. Ask the showroom to confirm the price and stock before you travel.
