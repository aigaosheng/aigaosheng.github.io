### 1. U.S. moves to block advanced AI chip sales to Chinese firms’ overseas units
- **Reuters** · 2026-06-01
- **Summary**: The U.S. Commerce Department issued weekend guidance clarifying that export-license requirements for advanced AI chips also apply to entities headquartered in China even when those entities operate outside mainland China. ### 2. Nvidia unveils a new PC AI chip aimed at running agents locally
- **Reuters** · 2026-06-01
- **Summary**: Nvidia introduced its RTX Spark PC chip at Computex, positioning it as a processor for running AI capabilities directly on laptops and desktops rather than relying only on cloud inference.
