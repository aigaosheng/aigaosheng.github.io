**Hong Kong Market Report – October 8, 2025**

---

### 📉 Market Overview

Hong Kong stocks experienced their sharpest decline in two weeks on Wednesday, mirroring Wall Street's retreat from record highs. This downturn was driven by mounting concerns over an artificial intelligence (AI) bubble and the potential for overvaluation in the tech sector. The Hang Seng Index (HSI) closed at 26,517.45, down from the previous close of 26,957.77 ([Bloomberg][1]).

---

### 📰 Top News (Last 24 Hours)

1. **AI Bubble Concerns Weigh on Tech Stocks**
   Hong Kong's stock market fell amid fears of an AI bubble, with investors wary of overvaluation in the tech sector ([South China Morning Post][2]).

2. **China's Central Bank Extends Gold Buying Streak**
   China's central bank has extended its gold buying streak to an 11th consecutive month, contributing to gold's record-setting run ([South China Morning Post][3]).

3. **US Lawmakers Push for Expanded Chip Export Curbs on China**
   A bipartisan panel of US lawmakers is urging the expansion of chip export curbs to China, amid concerns over national security and technological competition ([South China Morning Post][4]).
