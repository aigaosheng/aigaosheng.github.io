**Global Shockwaves Ahead? WEF Chief Flags Three Potential Bubbles to Watch**

---

When World Economic Forum (WEF) President Børge Brende warns of possible bubbles forming in the global economy, it’s a signal worth hearing. Speaking in São Paulo, Brende identified **three distinct risk zones**: crypto, artificial intelligence, and public-debt. ([Reuters][1])

---

## What’s the story?

Brende told reporters that as markets hit record highs and technology-stocks face sharp falls, we should stay alert. ([Reuters][1])

Here are his three bubbles of concern:

1. **Crypto bubble** – While crypto markets have already had dramatic swings, Brende suggests valuations could still be overstretched. ([Reuters][1])
2. **AI bubble** – The hype around AI is extensive, especially given high expectations for productivity gains and job impacts. Brende noted the risk of “a Rust Belt” of white-collar workers being displaced by AI. ([Reuters][1])
3. **Debt bubble** – Governments globally are more indebted than ever since 1945, and the combination of high debt, elevated interest rates and inflation could set the stage for trouble. ([Reuters][1])

---

## Why it matters

* Markets are operating on optimism: The hope that AI will drive a leap in productivity has helped propel equities higher despite risks. Brende’s comment signals that this optimism may be overlooking underlying vulnerabilities. ([Reuters][1])
* Job market implications: The mention of AI-driven displacement highlights a societal dimension — productivity gains don’t automatically guarantee prosperity unless managed properly. ([Reuters][1])
* Fiscal stress: With debt levels elevated globally, any shock (rate hike, inflation spike, geopolitical event) could trigger a broader economic weakening.
* Risk of complacency: The markets “shrugged off concerns” over inflation, high rates and trade turmoil, according to the piece. ([Reuters][1]) Brende’s remarks act as a caution flag against that complacency.

---

## What to watch moving forward

* **Valuations** in AI-related sectors and crypto assets: If valuations detach further from fundamentals, corrective pressures may rise.
* **Corporate and government debt levels**: Metrics such as debt-to-GDP and servicing costs will be key.
* **Labour market shifts**: Especially in sectors vulnerable to automation or AI uptake — purple-collar and white-collar alike.
* **Macro triggers**: Interest-rate changes, inflation surprises, or geopolitical shocks could all ignite one of these bubbles.
* **Market sentiment**: If optimism around AI begins to fade, it could impact broader markets given how interlinked these themes are.

## Glossary

* **Bubble**: A market condition where asset prices inflate well beyond what fundamentals can justify, eventually leading to a sharp correction.
* **Productivity gains**: Improvements in the amount of output per unit of input (e.g., labour or capital), often a driver of long-term economic growth.
* **Debt servicing**: The costs associated with repaying principal and interest on outstanding debt.
* **Valuation**: The process of determining the current worth of an asset or business, often relative to earnings, growth or other fundamentals.
