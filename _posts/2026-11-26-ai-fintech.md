# AI Fintech Brief: Intesa Sanpaolo Takes AI Into Dealmaking

**Today:** Intesa Sanpaolo’s investment banking division is positioning AI as a way to speed complex analysis, with human judgment remaining central to client advice and transaction decisions.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **Intesa Sanpaolo Takes AI Into Dealmaking**

*Intesa Sanpaolo · November 26, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Intesa Sanpaolo says AI could accelerate analysis in investment banking, while relationship management and human judgment remain essential.

In an article published November 26, the bank recapped comments its IMI Corporate & Investment Banking chief Mauro Micillo made at a conference on November 17. The division has established an AI Core Team to support adoption across the business. Micillo pointed to M&A scenario analysis and risk assessment in structured and infrastructure finance as areas where AI could help bankers work faster.

**Why it matters:** The bank’s account frames AI as a tool for augmenting high-value advisory work, not removing the need for experienced deal teams. For Asia-based banks and fintechs, the strategic signal is to connect AI investment to specific transaction workflows while maintaining clear human accountability.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://group.intesasanpaolo.com/en/newsroom/all-news/news/2026/mauro-micillo-ai-investment-banking)

## Key Takeaways

- Intesa Sanpaolo is targeting AI adoption across everyday corporate and investment banking work.
- M&A analysis and structured lending are among the use cases the bank identifies for faster AI-supported assessment.
- The bank presents client trust, human judgment and responsible governance as core parts of AI adoption.

## What to Watch

Watch for concrete use cases and adoption outcomes from Intesa Sanpaolo’s AI Core Team, particularly in M&A analysis and structured finance workflows.
