# Payment Brief — 2026-08-13

## Top Stories

### 1. **Adyen Raises 2026 Revenue Outlook as Payment Volumes Accelerate**

* **Source**: Reuters · 2026-08-13
* **Summary**: Dutch payments platform Adyen raised its 2026 net-revenue growth forecast to 21–23%, up from 20–22%, after reporting stronger-than-expected first-half performance. Net revenue rose 21% on a constant-currency basis to €1.30 billion, while processed payment volume increased 24% to €803.8 billion. The company attributed the stronger outlook partly to its acquisitions of Talon.One and Orb, while management indicated that further payments-company acquisitions are unlikely in the near term. Adyen also continues to expand its competitive position against major payment platforms in North America.
* **Why It Matters**: The results reinforce the shift toward scaled, vertically integrated payment platforms that combine processing with adjacent merchant capabilities. Adyen's acquisition strategy also signals a broader payments-industry trend toward adding software and commerce capabilities around the core transaction-processing layer.
* **URL**: https://www.reuters.com/technology/adyen-lifts-2026-revenue-outlook-after-strong-first-half-2026-08-13/

### 2. **Adyen Reports H1 2026 Results and Maintains Long-Term Margin Ambition**

* **Source**: Adyen Investor Relations · 2026-08-13
* **Summary**: Adyen published its H1 2026 results and held an investor call on August 13. The company reported first-half net revenue of approximately €1.3 billion and highlighted continued expansion of its payments platform, including the integration of recent acquisitions. The company continues to target an earnings margin above 55% by 2028.
* **Why It Matters**: Adyen's combination of high payment-volume growth, platform expansion and long-term margin discipline provides an important benchmark for the economics of global merchant acquiring and payment infrastructure. Its performance is particularly relevant as payment providers increasingly compete on software, data and orchestration rather than transaction processing alone.
* **URL**: https://investors.adyen.com/events/earnings-call-h1-2026/
