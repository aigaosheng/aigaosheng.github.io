# AI Fintech Brief: Finda Plans AI-Native Savings Bank

**Today:** Finda’s planned AI-led bank, Qfin’s shrinking loan volumes and Rabobank’s lending-platform move show how AI ambitions are meeting credit risk and execution realities.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **Finda Plans AI-Native Savings Bank**

*The Korea Times · November 25, 2026*

**Bottom line:** South Korean fintech Finda plans to reshape its acquired savings bank around AI-supported lending and routine banking work.

Finda plans to move Daewon Savings Bank’s headquarters to Chuncheon and rename it Finda Savings Bank, subject to a shareholder meeting next month. The company aims to launch a service early next year with AI agents handling routine tasks while employees retain decision-making and oversight; planned uses include loan assessment and credit management.

**Why it matters:** Finda is moving beyond AI as a tool for comparing loans and into operating a regulated lender. For Asian fintechs, the plan is a test of whether AI can support core banking workflows while preserving human control over credit decisions.

### 2. 📊 **Qfin Reports Sharp Loan Volume Contraction**

*GlobeNewswire · November 24, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Qfin’s third-quarter results show a steep decline in lending activity alongside lower revenue and net income.

The company reported loan facilitation and origination volume of RMB49.5 billion, down 40.6% year over year, and net income of RMB380.5 million, down from RMB1.43 billion a year earlier. Qfin attributed pressure to industry liquidity constraints, disrupted risk management and tighter regulation. Its release was timestamped 5 p.m. ET on November 24, equivalent to 6 a.m. SGT on November 25.

**Why it matters:** Qfin describes itself as an AI-powered credit technology platform, but the results underline that underwriting models operate within wider market and regulatory conditions. Credit volumes, delinquency and profitability will matter as much as AI capability when lenders assess platform resilience.

### 3. 🤖 **nCino Brings AI Lending Platform to Rabobank**

*GlobeNewswire · November 25, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Rabobank plans to consolidate business lending on nCino’s platform, which includes embedded AI and governance controls.

The platform is intended to provide automated and guided lending journeys matched to client needs, with routine work handled more efficiently and relationship managers able to focus on complex cases. nCino says AI will support work across the lending lifecycle while bank staff remain in control of decisions; the announcement gives no deployment timeline or quantified results.

**Why it matters:** The move puts AI within a major European bank’s business-lending operations, where cycle time and relationship coverage can affect service to entrepreneurs. For banks in Asia, it is a useful signal to watch for measurable outcomes and evidence that AI-enabled automation fits existing credit governance.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/25/3410008/0/en/ncino-offers-lending-platform-to-rabobank.html)

## Key Takeaways

- AI is moving into core lending workflows, while banks keep people responsible for consequential credit decisions.
- Credit-market conditions remain a major constraint: AI-enabled platforms do not insulate lenders from falling demand, liquidity pressure or regulatory change.
- Commercial value will depend on operational results, including faster lending journeys, sound risk performance and sustainable economics.

## What to Watch

Watch for Finda’s shareholder decision and progress towards its planned early-next-year service launch. Qfin’s next results and Rabobank’s implementation milestones may show whether their AI-linked lending strategies deliver measurable outcomes.
