### 1. US Government Explores Taking Equity Stakes in Major AI Firms
- **CNBC / Financial News** · 2026-06-07
- **Summary**: The Trump administration has held preliminary discussions with leading AI companies, including OpenAI, about the government taking equity stakes. OpenAI CEO Sam Altman has reportedly discussed the concept with White House officials for over a year, proposing a "Public Asset Fund" where government-owned shares would distribute returns directly to citizens. President Trump confirmed he intends to meet with AI executives as soon as next week to discuss the proposal .
- **Why It Matters**: Government equity ownership would represent a fundamental shift in US AI policy—moving from regulator to shareholder. This could reshape AI company valuations, governance structures, and IPO timelines, with OpenAI now valued above $850 billion. However, opposition from figures like former AI czar David Sacks warns of "AI nationalization" risks .

### 4. Scientists Solve AI Scaling Mystery, Enabling Smaller Models to Match Giants
- **AI Tech Suite** · 2026-06-07
- **Summary**: Researchers from Stanford, Harvard, MIT, and Anthropic have mathematically proven why large models acquire "emergent" capabilities that smaller models miss. The study identifies "gradient interference"—where frequent tasks overwrite rare skill learning—as the core bottleneck. By upsampling rare task data, small models can achieve capabilities previously requiring billions of parameters .
- **Why It Matters**: This shifts AI development from brute-force scaling to data-centric optimization, offering a cost-effective path for edge computing, on-device AI, and specialized enterprise models. Companies can now build highly capable small models without trillion-parameter clusters .
- **URL**: [Scientists Solve AI Scaling Mystery to Help Small Models Match Tech Giants](https://www.aitechsuite.com/ai-news/scientists-solve-ai-scaling-mystery-to-help-small-models-match-tech-giants)

### 7. UC Berkeley Mathematically Proves "Implicit Chain-of-Thought" Reasoning
- **36Kr** · 2026-06-08
- **Summary**: Researchers from UC Berkeley and Princeton, including AI pioneer Stuart Russell, have proven that transformers can internalize chain-of-thought reasoning without generating visible intermediate tokens. Their "Log-ICoT" method reduces training stages from linear to logarithmic scale (e.g., 15 stages to 4) and provides the first mathematical convergence guarantee for implicit reasoning .
- **Why It Matters**: This could slash inference costs for reasoning-heavy AI applications. Current "slow thinking" models generate hundreds or thousands of tokens per query; implicit reasoning would enable the same capabilities with latency equivalent to standard conversations .
- **URL**: [Can AI Truly Learn Mental Arithmetic? Implicit Chain of Thought Theoretically Proven](https://eu.36kr.com/en/p/3842559523850504)
