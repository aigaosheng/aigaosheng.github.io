*News from 2-8 November 2026. Draft for review. All "$" figures are USD as reported by the sources.*

## 1. Top Headlines

1. **Revolut hits $115bn valuation to cement status as Europe's most valuable private firm** ([FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/digital-banking/revolut-hits-115bn-valuation))
   Revolut closed its latest fundraising process at a $115bn valuation. The figure was first reported in July; this week's news is the [formal confirmation after the secondary share sale](https://tech.eu/2026/11/04/revolut-confirms-115bn-valuation-after-completing-secondary-sale/). It also [launched banking in New Zealand](https://www.fintechfutures.com/digital-banking/revolut-secures-new-zealand-banking-licence). *Takeaway:* scale and licences drive the premium, but this is a private-round price, not a public-market one.

2. **ECB reveals network service providers for proposed digital euro** ([FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/blockchain-crypto-digital-assets/european-central-bank-network-service-providers-digital-euro))
   Nexi Payments with Deutsche Telekom, and Telefónica with Indra, will build the connectivity layer ahead of a planned 2027 pilot. *Takeaway:* payment providers can connect through either group.

3. **German B2B fintech Pliant bags $80m Series C** ([FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/venture-capital-funding/german-b2b-fintech-pliant-bags-80m-series-c))
   The round was co-led by Seaya Nova and Speedinvest to support North American growth. *Takeaway:* AI-agent-ready B2B payments are attracting growth capital (see In-Depth).

4. **OPay postpones planned New York IPO** ([FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/stock-exchange-ipos/opay-postpones-planned-new-york-ipo))
   The Nigerian fintech pulled its listing on 30-31 Oct. [Reuters reports](https://www.dealstreetasia.com/stories/softbank-backed-opay-us-ipo-499768) it cited a regulatory inquiry in an unnamed foreign jurisdiction, after planning to raise $304m. *Takeaway:* regulatory overhang can derail an IPO at the last minute.

5. **eToro agrees deal for Argentina's LB Finanzas as 2026 M&A spree continues** ([FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/m-a/etoro-to-acquire-lb-finanzas))
   It is eToro's fourth acquisition of 2026, and Argentina becomes its Latin American hub. [Terms are undisclosed](https://www.financemagnates.com/fintech/etoro-agrees-to-acquire-argentine-fintech-lb-finanzas/) and the deal is subject to customary closing conditions. *Takeaway:* buying a licensed local player beats building from scratch.

6. **Thought Machine lands core banking deal with Thailand's CLICX** ([FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/core-banking-technology/clicx-thought-machine-thailand-core-banking))
   Vault Core will underpin the new bank, which will lend to freelancers, gig workers and small entrepreneurs. *Takeaway:* Southeast Asia's virtual-bank wave is reaching live operations.

7. **OCC unveils supervisory framework for bank AI deployment** ([FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/regulations-compliance/occ-unveils-supervisory-framework-for-bank-ai-deployment))
   Examiners are to assess AI use against 16 governance areas across six pillars. The OCC's own documents call AI "super intelligence". *Takeaway:* it is an [examiner resource, not new rules for banks](https://www.pymnts.com/news/banking/2026/occ-releases-supervisory-framework-for-bank-ai-use/).

8. **JP Morgan Payments and Mastercard invest in agentic commerce startup firmly** ([Finextra](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/48712/jp-morgan-payments-and-mastercard-invest-in-agentic-commerce-startup-firmly))
   Both joined a $15m Series A for firmly's no-code agentic commerce platform. *Takeaway:* incumbents are backing agent-ready checkout.

## 2. In-Depth Highlight: Pliant's $80m Bet on Agent-Run B2B Payments

Berlin-based Pliant raised an $80m Series C co-led by Seaya Nova and Speedinvest, taking total funding to around $200m. Illuminate Financial, Motive Partners and Molten Ventures also participated. Pliant says external AI agents such as Claude and ChatGPT can now [use its B2B payments infrastructure via the Model Context Protocol (MCP)](https://phemex.com/news/article/berlin-fintech-pliant-closes-80m-series-c-to-expand-aipowered-b2b-payments-infrastructure-102407). The round matters because Pliant has [passed $100m in annual recurring revenue](https://fintechmagazine.com/news/this-weeks-top-5-stories-in-fintech-6-11-2026), so this funds a scaling business rather than a prototype. Seaya Nova's reported logic is that [licensed, auditable firms must stay accountable when agents move money](https://thenextweb.com/news/pliant-80m-series-c-ai-agents-b2b-payments-seaya-speedinvest). Supervisors are moving the same way: the OCC framework directs examiners to consider how much authority an AI system has been given. *Our read (moderate confidence):* banks and BaaS partners will increasingly ask about agent permissions, audit trails and liability.

## 3. Market & Industry Insight

**Agentic AI is moving from demos to plumbing, but governance lags.** Capital this week went to infrastructure: firmly's $15m and Pliant's $80m. [Capgemini's new research](https://www.capgemini.com/news/press-releases/financial-institutions-accelerate-transition-to-multi-agent-ai-in-drive-for-bottom-line-value/) finds 59% of financial institutions are piloting or deploying multi-agent AI, yet only 10% have achieved enterprise-wide orchestration. About 90% use or plan intelligent model routing to manage token costs. The OCC framework is the regulatory mirror of that gap.

**Licensed, local and consolidating.** Revolut's New Zealand licence is [its seventh worldwide and fifth in 2026](https://www.mpamag.com/nz/news/general/revolut-becomes-nzs-27th-bank-launches-35-intro-savings-rate/595865). eToro is buying a regulated local player, and CLICX targets gig workers with alternative-data lending. Growth is going to firms that hold licences or buy them, including for financial inclusion.

**Exits remain fragile.** OPay's pulled IPO is a reminder that public-market windows for emerging-market fintech can close quickly.

## 4. Company & Startup Spotlight

- **firmly (Seattle):** A no-code platform that connects merchants to AI assistants, social and shoppable channels. Its Series A was [led by Squarepoint Capital, with J.P. Morgan Payments, Mastercard and Samsung NEXT participating](https://retailtechinnovationhub.com/home/2026/11/6/agentic-commerce-startup-firmly-announces-15-million-series-a-led-by-squarepoint-capital). Its Connect product reaches more than 11 million merchants representing over $900bn in GMV. *Why care:* merchants may plug into AI shopping channels without rebuilding integrations.
- **Thought Machine (London):** Cloud-native core banking vendor. Its CLICX win adds to its Southeast Asian footprint. *Why care:* modular cores let new banks reprice and launch products without vendor bottlenecks.

## 5. Regulatory & Policy Watch

*Needs [Compliance/Risk] sign-off before external use.*

- **US, OCC:** The AI examiner framework does not set supervisory expectations for banks, and the OCC plans regular updates. It appears to signal what examiners will look at, not a new rulebook.
- **EU, ECB:** The network contracts are [not an obligation to issue a digital euro](https://paymentexpert.com/2026/11/02/ecb-nexi-deutsche-telekom-euro/), which is a separate decision. The ECB announcement is dated 30 Oct.
- **New Zealand, RBNZ:** Revolut's registration as a bank came with deposits of up to NZD 100,000 protected under the Depositor Compensation Scheme.
