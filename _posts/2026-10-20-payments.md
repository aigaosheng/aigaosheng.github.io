# Payments Brief: Visa Moves Stablecoin Prefunding Into Commercial Use

**Today:** Stablecoins are moving closer to mainstream payment operations as Visa advances commercial payout infrastructure, while Southeast Asian fintech listings highlight the scale and capital needs of digital financial services.

## Top Stories

### 1. 💳 **Visa Advances Stablecoin Prefunding for Cross-Border Payouts**

*eCommerceNews Australia · October 20, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Visa Direct’s stablecoin-prefunding capability has moved from pilot programmes into commercial deployment, with Inyo Global identified as the first provider to put the option into production.

The service uses USDC to prefund eligible cross-border payouts, potentially allowing payment providers to fund transactions without relying exclusively on conventional prefunding arrangements. The rollout integrates stablecoin infrastructure into existing money-movement operations rather than requiring businesses to build a separate customer-facing payment system.

**Why it matters:** Payment providers serving Asia’s cross-border corridors should assess whether stablecoin settlement can improve liquidity management and payout speed. Commercial availability does not eliminate the need to evaluate licensing, custody, liquidity, redemption and operational risks in each market.

### 2. 📊 **Sea Plans IPO for Financial Services Arm Monee**

*Reuters · October 20, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Singapore-headquartered Sea plans to list Monee, its financial services business, to provide additional resources for expansion.

According to an internal memo reviewed by Reuters, Monee spans digital wallets, payment processing, credit and banking services across Southeast Asia and other markets. The proposed listing would give investors a more direct way to evaluate the financial services operation within Sea’s wider technology business.

**Why it matters:** A separate listing could sharpen market scrutiny of the economics of digital payments and embedded finance. Regional competitors should watch how Sea balances growth, funding costs, credit exposure and investment in payment infrastructure as it scales.

### 3. 📊 **GCash Parent Mynt Completes Record Philippine IPO**

*Reuters · October 20, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Mynt, the parent of Philippine mobile wallet GCash, finished its first trading day flat after raising approximately US$967 million in the country’s largest-ever initial public offering.

The listing valued Mynt at roughly 442 billion pesos, or US$7 billion. Reuters reported that GCash had 41.5 million active users and processed 9.84 trillion pesos in transactions during the first half of 2026; Mynt intends to use IPO proceeds to support digital-finance expansion and operations.

**Why it matters:** The listing provides a significant public-market reference point for the scale of Southeast Asian wallet businesses. For payment companies and investors, the next test is whether large user bases and high transaction volumes translate into durable profitability, disciplined credit growth and sustainable returns on capital.

### 4. 🌐 **Thunes and Coinbase Link Stablecoins to Fiat Payouts**

*The Paypers · October 20, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Thunes and Coinbase are combining stablecoin infrastructure with local fiat payout networks to support international transfers and business payments.

Under the partnership, Coinbase will integrate Thunes’ Direct Global Network to extend local payout reach, while Coinbase’s stablecoin products will support Thunes’ digital-asset infrastructure. The intended use cases include supplier payments, gig-worker payouts and international remittances.

**Why it matters:** The partnership addresses a core challenge for stablecoin adoption: connecting blockchain-based value to local bank accounts, mobile wallets and payment methods. For Asian payment providers, the opportunity lies in reducing friction between digital-asset settlement and local currency delivery while maintaining appropriate compliance controls.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://thepaypers.com/crypto-web3-and-cbdc/news/thunes-and-coinbase-partner-on-stablecoin-and-fiat-payouts)

### 5. 💳 **Fiserv Launches Commerce Hub Globally for Enterprise Merchants**

*The Paypers · October 20, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Fiserv has made Commerce Hub globally available, offering enterprise merchants a unified connection to payment acceptance, local payment methods, settlement and reporting.

The platform is designed to reduce the complexity of managing separate payment integrations across countries. Dell Technologies is already using Commerce Hub to introduce Brazil’s Pix payment method, illustrating how a common platform can accommodate local payment preferences.

**Why it matters:** Multinational merchants expanding across Asia face similar fragmentation, with domestic wallets, QR schemes, bank transfers and card networks requiring different integrations and operational processes. Consolidated payment infrastructure can simplify expansion, although merchants should compare local coverage, transaction costs, settlement terms and resilience before committing to a single provider.

## Key Takeaways

- **Stablecoins are becoming payment infrastructure.** Commercial prefunding and fiat payout partnerships indicate a shift towards integrating digital assets into existing cross-border payment operations.
- **Scale is attracting capital and strategic attention.** Sea’s planned Monee listing and Mynt’s Philippine IPO highlight investor interest in dedicated digital-finance businesses.
- **Payment orchestration remains a competitive differentiator.** Platforms that combine local payment methods, settlement and reporting can reduce integration burdens for international merchants.

## What to Watch

Monitor Visa’s stablecoin-prefunding rollout, the execution and timing of Sea’s proposed Monee IPO, and the practical expansion of Thunes–Coinbase payout coverage. For Singapore and other Asian markets, licensing, local-currency conversion, settlement reliability and interoperability will determine how quickly these developments translate into usable payment services.
