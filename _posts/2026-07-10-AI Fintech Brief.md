## Market Intelligence Summary

### Key Trend: AI Agents Become the Next Fintech Infrastructure Layer

The fintech industry is moving from AI-assisted automation toward **agentic financial systems** capable of executing workflows, making recommendations, and interacting with customers autonomously. The competitive bottleneck is shifting from model capability toward:

- AI governance and auditability
- Runtime controls for autonomous actions
- Fraud and cybersecurity resilience
- Regulatory compliance
- Human-in-the-loop decision frameworks

Financial institutions that successfully combine AI capability with strong governance infrastructure are likely to gain the strongest long-term advantage.
