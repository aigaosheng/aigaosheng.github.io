### 3. **AI Model Routing Emerges as a Strategic Enterprise Layer**

* **Source**: Axios · August 25, 2026
* **Summary**: Enterprises are increasingly using AI routing systems to direct workloads to different models based on cost, speed, performance, trust, and data-security requirements.
