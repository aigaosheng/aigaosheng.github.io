### Open Source AI Model Brief — 2026-06-15

## Top Stories 

### 1. Rio de Janeiro's Municipal Government Releases World-Class Open Model
- **India Today** · 2026-06-14
- **Summary**: The city government of Rio de Janeiro, through its IT company IplanRIO, has released "Rio 3.5 Open 397B," a 397-billion-parameter AI model built on Alibaba’s open-source Qwen architecture. The model, fine-tuned with a reasoning framework called SwiReasoning, is reportedly outperforming major open-source competitors like DeepSeek V4 and even some closed models like older versions of ChatGPT and Claude on benchmarks.
- **Why It Matters**: This development demonstrates that fine-tuning existing open-source models is a viable, low-cost path to frontier AI capabilities. It signals a future where municipal governments and smaller institutions can deploy sovereign AI tailored to local languages and regulations, potentially decentralizing power away from Big Tech in Silicon Valley.

### 3. Zhipu AI Confirms GLM-5.2 Open-Source Release with 1M Context
- **Pandaily** · 2026-06-14
- **Summary**: Zhipu AI officially announced that GLM-5.2, a model featuring a 1 million token context window, will be released as open-source software under the permissive MIT license. By prioritizing coding tasks and long-context retention, Zhipu is targeting enterprise developers worried about vendor lock-in or sudden loss of access to proprietary APIs like Claude.

### 4. Open Model "Rio 3.5" Features Massive 1M Context Window
- **KuCoin** · 2026-06-14
- **Summary**: Technical specifications for the Rio 3.5 Open model reveal a Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architecture with 397B total parameters (17B activated) and a context window of approximately 1 million tokens. It is licensed under the MIT License and integrates "SwiReasoning" to balance explicit and latent space reasoning.
- **Why It Matters**: The 1M context window puts Rio 3.5 in direct competition with the largest commercial models. The MIT license means this technology can be freely used commercially, even by competitors, accelerating the commoditization of base-model architecture.

### 7. Anthropic’s Loss is Open-Source’s Gain
- **India Today** · 2026-06-14
- **Summary**: The release of Rio 3.5 occurred exactly one day after the US government banned foreigners from using Anthropic’s most advanced Claude models (Mythos and Fable 5) due to national security concerns. Unlike Anthropic’s restricted access, Rio 3.5 is freely available to all.
