## Market Insight

Today's developments reveal three dominant trends shaping AI fintech:

1. **Regulatory oversight is accelerating**, with supervisors moving from AI guidance toward direct monitoring of AI governance inside financial institutions.
2. **Large financial institutions are industrializing AI**, establishing dedicated AI organizations and enterprise-wide governance frameworks.
3. **Operational AI is replacing experimental AI**, particularly across compliance, KYC, payments, and customer lifecycle management where measurable productivity gains are becoming commercially important.
