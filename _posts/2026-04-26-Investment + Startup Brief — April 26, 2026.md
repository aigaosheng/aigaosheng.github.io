## **Top Stories**

### 1. Space Tech Funding Hits Record High on IPO Momentum

**Source:** Reuters | **Published:** April 21, 2026
**Summary:** Global investment in space startups surged to **$7.95B in Q1 2026**, nearly doubling the previous quarter. Growth is driven by late-stage mega-rounds and strong anticipation of a potential SpaceX IPO. Deal volume also climbed, reflecting expanding investor interest beyond satellites into infrastructure like space stations and orbital data systems. ([Reuters][1])
**Why It Matters:** Space is transitioning from speculative frontier to institutional-grade asset class, with IPO expectations acting as a liquidity catalyst for venture investors.
**Citation URL:** [https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/space-investment-first-quarter-hits-record-spacex-ipo-buzz-lifts-sector-2026-04-21/](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/space-investment-first-quarter-hits-record-spacex-ipo-buzz-lifts-sector-2026-04-21/)

### 2. Bezos-Backed AI Startup Nears $38B Valuation

**Source:** Reuters / Business Insider | **Published:** April 21, 2026
**Summary:** Jeff Bezos’ AI venture **Project Prometheus** is raising ~$10B, targeting a **$38B valuation**. The company focuses on “physical AI” applications across manufacturing and industrial sectors, and is aggressively hiring top talent from leading AI labs. ([Reuters][2])
**Why It Matters:** Mega-rounds are concentrating capital into fewer, high-conviction AI bets, signaling a shift toward infrastructure-scale AI plays rather than consumer apps.

### 3. Sarvam AI Funding Signals Global Investor Appetite

**Source:** Economic Times | **Published:** April 20, 2026
**Summary:** Sarvam AI is reportedly raising **$320M–$350M**, potentially reaching a **$1.5B valuation**, with participation from global investors like Glade Brook Capital. The company is emerging as a key AI player in India’s fast-growing ecosystem. ([The Economic Times][3])
**Why It Matters:** AI funding is becoming globally distributed, with emerging markets like India producing unicorn-scale startups earlier in their lifecycle.
**Citation URL:** [https://m.economictimes.com/tech/funding/glade-brook-in-talks-to-join-sarvams-funding-ai-startup-may-hit-1-5-billion-valuation/articleshow/130376840.cms](https://m.economictimes.com/tech/funding/glade-brook-in-talks-to-join-sarvams-funding-ai-startup-may-hit-1-5-billion-valuation/articleshow/130376840.cms)

**Source:** Multiple (YourStory, Crunchbase, Reuters) | **Published:** April 2026
**Summary:** Across geographies and stages, AI remains the dominant investment theme—from seed infrastructure tools to multi-billion-dollar mega rounds. Capital is concentrating in fewer but larger deals, with increasing global participation. ([YourStory.com][7])
