# AI Governance Brief: APEC Leaders Call for Regional AI Cooperation

Today: Singapore and China are pushing regional AI cooperation higher on the policy agenda, with implications for responsible AI deployment, cross-border data governance and regulatory coordination across Asia-Pacific.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🏦 Singapore and China Push Regional AI Governance Cooperation at APEC

Prime Minister's Office Singapore · November 19, 2026

Bottom line: Singapore and China are calling for stronger regional cooperation on AI safety, responsible adoption and cross-border data governance as artificial intelligence becomes a driver of Asia-Pacific economic growth.

At the 33rd APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting in Shenzhen, Singapore Prime Minister Lawrence Wong highlighted opportunities for economies to cooperate on AI safety and responsible deployment. He also identified SME adoption, investment in AI infrastructure and workforce development as practical priorities.

Chinese President Xi Jinping called for improved regional data governance, stronger digital collaboration and efficient, secure cross-border data flows. China also announced plans to establish cooperation institutions covering digital technology and cross-border data flows.

Why it matters: For Singapore-based financial institutions and multinational enterprises, regional cooperation could influence future approaches to AI assurance, data transfers and regulatory interoperability. Businesses operating across Asia should prepare for increasingly important governance requirements around responsible AI deployment and cross-border data management.

## Key Takeaways

- Regional cooperation is gaining importance: AI governance is increasingly becoming a shared economic and policy priority across Asia-Pacific.
- Cross-border data governance deserves attention: Secure data flows and regulatory coordination will be important for regional AI infrastructure and financial services.
- Responsible adoption must translate into implementation: SME enablement, workforce development and AI safety need to accompany investments in AI infrastructure.

## What to Watch

Watch for concrete outcomes from APEC's discussions, particularly regional initiatives on AI safety, data governance and cross-border digital cooperation. Singapore's upcoming ASEAN Chairmanship in 2027 will also provide an important opportunity to advance regional digital and AI cooperation.

## MY TAKE

Asia-Pacific has an opportunity to develop a more practical approach to AI governance: one that enables innovation while maintaining trust, accountability and data protection.

For enterprises operating across multiple jurisdictions, the strategic challenge is not simply complying with individual AI regulations. It is building a consistent governance framework that can accommodate different regulatory environments, data residency requirements and AI risk-management expectations.
