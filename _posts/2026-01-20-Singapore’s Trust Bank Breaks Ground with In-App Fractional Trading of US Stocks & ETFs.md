# *Singapore’s Trust Bank Breaks Ground with In-App Fractional Trading of US Stocks & ETFs*

In a bold move that could reshape retail investing in Singapore, **Trust Bank** has officially launched a fully integrated **fractional trading platform** for U.S. stocks and exchange-traded funds (ETFs) — directly within its banking app. This strategic launch marks the first time a *banking app in Singapore* offers fractionalised access to global equities, bringing Wall Street within reach of everyday investors with unprecedented convenience. ([trustbank.sg][1])

## A Simpler Path to Global Markets

Traditionally, Singapore-based investors had to open separate brokerage accounts to buy U.S. stocks — and often grapple with minimum share sizes that make high-flying names like Tesla or Meta prohibitively expensive. Trust Bank’s new **TrustInvest** feature eliminates most of those hurdles:

* **Fractional trading**, allowing investors to buy *partial shares* starting from as little as **US$10**, democratizing access to expensive U.S. equities. ([trustbank.sg][1])
* **Over 7,000 U.S. stocks and ETFs** available, powered through a partnership with Saxo Singapore, a seasoned global brokerage. ([trustbank.sg][1])
* **Seamless integration** — no separate accounts, no awkward fund transfers between apps: everything happens inside Trust Bank’s mobile platform. ([trustbank.sg][1])

Until **30 June 2026**, Trust Bank is also offering **zero trading commissions**, with zero custody and settlement fees — a compelling incentive for investors to dip their toes into U.S. markets. First-time traders who place a minimum **US$1,000** order can even win up to **US$500 in free fractional stocks** via a digital reward mechanic. ([trustbank.sg][1])

## Meeting Investors Where They Are

According to early data, **10,000 customers** have already opened trading accounts during the platform’s pre-launch phase, with nearly half executing fractional trades — a clear sign that the **bite-sized investing model resonates**. ([The Business Times][2])

Trust Bank’s CEO, Dwaipayan Sadhu, frames the launch as part of a broader mission to make investing *“simple, transparent, and accessible to everyone”*. With a streamlined onboarding process that can be completed in *under one minute*, the bank is betting that combining digital banking with investing will appeal to both first-time investors and more seasoned market participants. ([trustbank.sg][1])

## Why It Matters

This launch arrives at a time when **fractional trading is gaining traction globally** — not just in Singapore. Fintech brokers and platforms are increasingly allowing partial share purchases to lower barriers to entry and help smaller investors construct diversified portfolios without hefty capital requirements. ([Syfe][3])

Trust Bank’s *bank-app first* approach could accelerate that trend locally by bringing investing directly into users’ everyday financial hub — their bank — rather than relegating it to a standalone brokerage app.

Moreover, the entry of a regulated bank into the fractional trading arena raises the competitive stakes for other platforms like Syfe, Moomoo, and Webull, which have already offered similar capabilities. ([The Business Times][2])

## Glossary

**Fractional Trading**
The ability to buy a *portion* of a stock or ETF share, rather than having to purchase a whole share. This makes expensive stocks more affordable for retail investors. ([trustbank.sg][1])

**ETF (Exchange-Traded Fund)**
A basket of securities — like stocks or bonds — that trades on an exchange like a single stock. ETFs offer diversification and often have lower fees compared to mutual funds. ([trustbank.sg][1])

**Custody Fees**
Charges levied by a brokerage or platform to hold an investor’s assets. Trust Bank currently waives these to reduce cost burdens on its customers. ([trustbank.sg][1])

**Zero Commission**
A promotional fee structure where no trading commission is charged for buying or selling securities — an attractive feature for cost-conscious investors. ([trustbank.sg][1])
