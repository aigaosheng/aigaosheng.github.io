## Executive Takeaway

Enterprise AI is entering a deployment-focused phase in 2026. The market is moving beyond standalone copilots toward **agentic systems integrated with business processes, governed data access, and enterprise-grade controls**. The winners are likely to be organisations that combine AI capability with operational discipline: secure data foundations, workflow integration, and measurable business outcomes.
