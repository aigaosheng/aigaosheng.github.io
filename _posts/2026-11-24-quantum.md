# Quantum Brief: Transmon Qutrits Advance Spin-1 Simulation

**Today:** New results in quantum simulation sit alongside a post-quantum security appointment, highlighting the technology and migration work enterprises should track.

## Top Stories

### 1. ⚛️ **Transmon Qutrits Advance Spin-1 Simulation**

*npj Quantum Information · November 24, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Researchers used transmon qutrits to simulate spin-1 states, demonstrating a route to shallower circuits for this class of problem.

The team calibrated microwave gates to operate transmon qutrits and prepared high-fidelity ground states of the AKLT model. It also used the qutrits to calculate a Berry phase and examine ground-state topology. The reported comparison with qubit circuits found an advantage under the study’s chosen compilation strategy and noise model.

**Why it matters:** Using three-level qutrits could reduce circuit depth for problems with a local dimension of three, which may help limit noise. The result is a research demonstration; its performance across other workloads and hardware settings remains to be established.

### 2. 🔒 **Qtonic Names Gen. Gary Brito Senior Advisor**

*PR Newswire · November 23, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Qtonic Quantum appointed retired U.S. Army General Gary Brito to lead its Army Readiness Council and advise the company’s founders.

The company said Brito will help the council address post-quantum migration as a readiness issue across Army and joint environments. The announcement was published at 4:21 p.m. Eastern Time on November 23, after the stated SGT cutoff.

**Why it matters:** The appointment adds military-readiness experience to a company focused on post-quantum security. Organizations serving national security systems can watch whether this focus translates into concrete migration assessments ahead of the January 1, 2027 CNSA 2.0 expected-compliance date for new deployments.
