### 1. **US Agencies Warn of AI-Assisted Attacks on Siemens Industrial Controllers**

* **Source**: Recorded Future News · August 20, 2026
* **Summary**: The NSA, FBI and other US agencies warned that attackers are targeting Siemens S7 programmable logic controllers used across critical infrastructure.
