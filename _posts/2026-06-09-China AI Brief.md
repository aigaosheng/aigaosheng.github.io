### China AI Brief — 2026-06-09

## Top Stories

### 1. Moonshot AI Seeks $30 Billion Valuation in New Fundraising Push
- **Bloomberg via The Edge Malaysia** · 2026-06-08
- **Summary**: Kimi chatbot developer Moonshot AI is in early talks to raise over $1 billion, aiming for a valuation of up to $30 billion—a seven-fold increase from its December 2025 valuation of just over $4 billion. This would be its third financing round in six months, following a separate round led by Meituan that values the firm at $20 billion post-investment.
- **Why It Matters**: The aggressive fundraising underscores intensifying competition among China’s elite AI labs. A $30 billion valuation would eclipse publicly traded peer MiniMax ($20 billion) and narrow the gap with Zhipu AI (~$80 billion) and DeepSeek (~$50 billion target).
- **URL**: [China’s Moonshot AI seeks US$30 bil value in new funding talks — Bloomberg](https://dev.theedgemalaysia.com/node/806162)

### 2. Huawei Cloud Unveils “Agentic Infra” Strategy, Pivots to Agentic AI
- **China Daily** · 2026-06-08
- **Summary**: At its INSPIRE summit, Huawei Cloud formally proposed a new “Agentic Infra” paradigm, declaring AI agents the new center stage with compute and models receding into the background. CEO Zhou Yuefeng announced a strategic repositioning as “silicon-based black soil,” emphasizing国产化 (domestically produced) compute power. New product launches include the AICS compute cluster and the “Zhi Guo Yuan” enterprise agent platform.
- **Why It Matters**: Huawei’s strategic pivot signals a major industry shift from model training to agent deployment. Its dual focus on public and private clouds directly addresses state-owned enterprise data security concerns, positioning Huawei as a critical infrastructure provider for China’s next AI wave.
- **URL**: [Huawei Cloud CEO:不在乎Token总量，做面向智能体的“硅基黑土地”](https://www.163.com/dy/article/KUTV2LO80514R9KE.html)

### 3. China Issues First National-Level Data Policy for AI Development
- **CCTV / 央广网** · 2026-06-08
- **Summary**: The National Data Administration released the “Implementation Plan for Promoting High-Quality Industry Dataset Construction,” marking the first systematic national-level deployment on data empowerment for AI. The plan outlines six major actions covering dataset supply, circulation, and application, while exploring data rights systems and rules for using copyrighted data in model training.
- **Why It Matters**: Data scarcity and copyright ambiguity are major bottlenecks for model development. This policy creates a compliant, enabling environment for training data usage, directly benefiting Chinese model providers and accelerating vertical industry applications.
- **URL**: [国家数据局：探索面向人工智能发展的数据相关制度](https://stcn.com/article/detail/3949581.html)

### 5. Changan Auto’s Tianshu LLM Receives National Generative AI Approval
- **证券时报 / 人民财讯** · 2026-06-08
- **Summary**: Changan Automobile announced that its fully self-developed Tianshu large model has passed the Cyberspace Administration of China’s generative AI service备案 (filing), making it the first automaker in Chongqing and one of the few nationwide to secure this资质 (qualification). The in-vehicle multimodal model can now legally enter public commercial service.
- **Why It Matters**: This marks a milestone for in-car AI commercialization. Regulatory clearance removes a major barrier to deploying LLMs in production vehicles, potentially accelerating the integration of advanced AI assistants and autonomous driving features across China’s automotive industry.
- **URL**: [长安汽车自研天枢大模型通过国家AI备案审批](https://finance.sina.com.cn/jjxw/2026-06-08/doc-iniasscn8704955.shtml)

### 6. Alibaba Restructures AI Units Into “Token Foundry” Under CEO Eddie Wu
- **CLS.cn via Moomoo** · 2026-06-08
- **Summary**: Alibaba announced it will merge the Tongyi Large Model Business Unit and the Future Life Lab to form the Token Foundry Business Unit, directly overseen by Group CEO Eddie Wu. Zhou Jingren becomes Alibaba’s Chief Scientist and will lead a new AI Future Institute for cutting-edge research. Bo Zheng’s video generation teams (including Happy Horse) will join the new unit.
- **Why It Matters**: This represents Alibaba’s third AI reorganization since March, signaling heightened strategic urgency. Centralizing model development, video generation, and applications under direct CEO oversight suggests Alibaba is consolidating resources to compete more aggressively against DeepSeek, Zhipu, and Moonshot.
- **URL**: [Alibaba Reorganizes AI Structure Again! Establishes New Token Foundry Business Unit, Directly Overseen by Eddie Wu](https://www.moomoo.com/news/post/71197661/alibaba-reorganizes-ai-structure-again-establishes-new-token-foundry-business)

### 7. Ten Government Departments Issue AI Ethics Review Regulations
- **Ministry of Industry and Information Technology** · 2026-06-08
- **Summary**: Ten central government departments—including MIIT, the Ministry of Science and Technology, and the Cyberspace Administration—jointly issued the “Artificial Intelligence Technology Ethics Review and Service Measures (Trial).” The regulations establish ethics review requirements for AI research, development, and deployment across sectors.
- **Why It Matters**: This multi-department framework signals an accelerating regulatory push to govern AI safety and ethics systematically. Companies developing or deploying AI in China must prepare for formal ethics review processes, potentially affecting product timelines and compliance costs.
- **URL**: [工业和信息化部等十部门关于印发《人工智能科技伦理审查与服务办法（试行）》的通知](https://credit.lanzhou.gov.cn/320/165889.html)
