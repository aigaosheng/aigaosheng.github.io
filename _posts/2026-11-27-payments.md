# Payments Brief: GoCardless Completes FCA Agentic Payment Test

**Today:** GoCardless completed live testing of agentic account-to-account payments under the FCA's AI programme and published consumer trust research, while ASEAN+3 finance deputies agreed to form a dedicated working group on cross-border digital payments and stablecoin regulation.

## Top Stories

### 1. 💳 **GoCardless Completes FCA AI Live Testing on Agentic Account-to-Account Payments**

*The Paypers · Nov 27, 2026*

**Bottom line:** GoCardless, a Mollie company, has completed the second cohort of the UK FCA's AI Live Testing programme, processing live agentic account-to-account payments in September and October 2026, and published research showing only 13% of UK adults are likely to let an AI agent pay for them in the next year.

The company processed an agentic account-to-account payment in September for the charity Trussell, then extended its checkout to one-off agentic payments in October . Its YouGov survey of 2,184 UK adults found that 44% believe the company providing the AI agent should be responsible for correcting payment errors, while 22% said the payer should be accountable . Fraud and theft of payment details was the top concern at 36%, and 32% said they would not know who to hold responsible if something went wrong .

**Why it matters:** GoCardless's live testing provides some of the first real-world evidence on how agentic payments perform outside sandboxes, while the trust data exposes a critical gap: consumers expect agent providers to bear liability, but regulatory frameworks in Asia and Europe have yet to assign that responsibility. For payment firms building agentic checkout, the liability question is as much a product design problem as a legal one.

### 2. 🌐 **ASEAN+3 Forms Working Group on Cross-Border Digital Payments and Stablecoin Regulation**

*Digital Today · Nov 27, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Finance and central bank deputies from South Korea, China, Japan, and the 11 ASEAN member states agreed in Kyoto to establish a dedicated working group under the AMRO Cross-Border Digital Payments Programme, covering retail payments, remittances, and digital asset regulation including stablecoins.

The working group will study how to connect payment systems built primarily for domestic use so they function across borders, with scope covering retail and wholesale payments, person-to-person remittances, merchant payments, tourism, e-commerce, and overseas remittances . The agreement builds on a May 2026 meeting in Samarkand where cross-border digital payments were identified as a new focus, with ministers directing deputies to produce a cooperation framework by year-end . The issue will be reviewed again at the ASEAN+3 finance ministers' meeting in Nagoya in May 2027 .

**Why it matters:** This is a formal coordination mechanism, not a finished system—no common stablecoin rules or integrated payment network have been announced . But for firms building cross-border payment or stablecoin infrastructure in Asia, a harmonised regulatory approach across China, Japan, South Korea, and ASEAN could reduce compliance complexity in one of the world's largest trading regions.

### 3. 💳 **Antom Launches PayNow Deep-Linking for Lazada, Cutting Checkout to One Step**

*The Paypers · Nov 25, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Ant International's merchant payments arm Antom has introduced live PayNow deep-linking for ecommerce under MAS's PayNow Generation 2.0 initiative, with Lazada reporting roughly 9% higher successful transactions after implementation.

Previously, paying by PayNow online could require users to screenshot a QR code, switch to their banking app, upload the image, and return to the merchant site—six or more manual steps. Antom's deep-linking sends users directly to their banking app with payment details pre-filled, then returns them to the confirmation page . Antom is also working with GovTech on a proof of concept for GovWallet deep-linking, and expects to be among the initial participants connecting to the SGQR+ switch for domestic and cross-border QR interoperability .

**Why it matters:** The 9% uplift in successful transactions at Lazada is a concrete data point for merchants weighing PayNow Gen2 integration, and it demonstrates that MAS's push to modernise Singapore's domestic payment rails is delivering measurable conversion improvements. For regional e-commerce and fintech players, the GovWallet proof of concept signals potential expansion into government disbursements and collections.

### 4. 🤖 **Visa Expands Agentic Commerce Push with OpenAI ChatGPT Wallet Integration**

*Impress Watch · Nov 27, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Visa has partnered with OpenAI to integrate ChatGPT Wallet with Visa Intelligent Commerce, with the company expecting full purchase and payment completion through AI agents by early 2027.

Visa's Abhijeet Ramesh said transaction volume on Visa's network from AI agents is already increasing, and the company is focused on ensuring purchases are safe and users retain control . The VIC flow requires biometric verification before an agent is authorized, explains what the agent will do, then re-verifies identity before the agent executes the transaction . Ramesh noted that regulators across Asia hold differing views on agentic payments, particularly around customer protection and fraud liability .

**Why it matters:** Visa's integration with ChatGPT Wallet moves agentic commerce from experimental pilots toward mainstream consumer flows, but the company's emphasis on biometric re-verification suggests that trust—not technology—remains the binding constraint. For payment firms in Singapore and Asia, Visa's approach offers a reference model for balancing agent autonomy with consumer protection.

### 5. 📊 **Solana Reports First Stablecoin Premium Settlement by Major Insurance Broker**

*ChainCatcher · Nov 27, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Solana has announced that one of the world's largest insurance brokers completed its first stablecoin premium settlement on the Solana network, according to a brief disclosure.

The announcement provided no further details on the broker's identity, the settlement amount, or the stablecoin used . The event marks a potential use case for stablecoins in institutional insurance payments, though the lack of disclosure limits assessment of its significance.

**Why it matters:** Insurance premium settlement is a high-value, recurring payment flow that has traditionally relied on bank transfers. If stablecoin settlement gains traction among brokers, it could open a new institutional demand channel for regulated stablecoins and tokenised deposit infrastructure—though a single undisclosed transaction is far from establishing a trend.

## Key Takeaways

- **Agentic payments are moving from sandbox to live testing, but liability remains unresolved:** GoCardless's FCA cohort completion and Visa's ChatGPT Wallet integration both show technical feasibility, yet consumer research and regulator divergence on error responsibility suggest the commercial model is still unsettled.
- **Asia is building cross-border payment coordination infrastructure:** The ASEAN+3 working group formalises a policy dialogue on linking domestic payment rails and aligning stablecoin oversight, a multi-year process that will shape compliance requirements for regional payment firms.
- **Singapore's PayNow Gen2 is delivering measurable conversion gains:** Antom's 9% uplift at Lazada provides concrete evidence that deep-linking reduces friction and improves success rates, strengthening the case for merchant adoption.

## What to Watch

The ASEAN+3 working group's first concrete deliverables ahead of the Nagoya meeting in May 2027, and whether Visa and OpenAI's ChatGPT Wallet integration launches full purchase completion in early 2027 as expected.
