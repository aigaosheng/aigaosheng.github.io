## **2. In‑Depth Highlight**

### *AI and Digital Wallets: How 2025 Set the Stage for 2026*

In 2025, fintech innovation intertwined *AI and wallet technology* more deeply than ever — from agentic AI initiatives to explorations of identity‑linked wallets that empower automated decision‑making and trust frameworks. Finextra and FinTech Futures review articles highlight how *AI‑driven automation* and *intelligent payments infrastructure* became core themes across the industry. ([Finextra Research][4])

**Why this matters:**

* **AI acceleration:** Agentic AI — systems capable of multi‑step planning and autonomous execution — transitioned from buzzword to practical roadmap consideration for banking and payments platforms. ([PYMNTS.com][5])
* **Digital wallet evolution:** Discussions on *trust frameworks and ID‑wallets* — digital wallets linked to verified credentials — opened new horizons for secure authentication and seamless digital identity in finance. ([Finextra Research][4])
* **Payments reimagined:** Wallets paired with AI agents could soon automate tasks like bill pay, fraud monitoring, and cross‑border settlement without direct user intervention.

**Market/Regulatory impact:**
These technological shifts are provoking a rethink of compliance, data governance, and consumer consent frameworks, signaling that 2026 will further advance the fusion of *AI‑led automation* and *secure digital money management*. Early movers could redefine customer expectations at the intersection of *UX, risk, and regulatory harmony*.

## **3. Market & Industry Insight**

**Digital Wallets & Identity — The Next Frontier**
Digital wallets are steadily evolving from purely *payment tools* to *secure identity platforms*. Opinions from industry experts suggest a future where wallets are linked to *verifiable credentials* that control not just money but access, identity, and trusted decisioning — opening doors to enterprise automation and AI agent integration. ([Finextra Research][4])

This shift matters because:

* Financial services will pivot toward *identity‑centric models* where wallets serve as *trusted credential stores* that enable highly personalised, secure workflows.
* Banks and fintechs gain new avenues for loyalty, compliance simplification, and cross‑platform interoperability.

**AI & Automation — Beyond Assisted Tasks**
While earlier AI rollouts focused on *chatbots and fraud detection*, 2025 has spotlighted *agentic AI* — machines capable of multi‑step autonomous work such as self‑optimising fraud defenses, tailored financial advice, and real‑time compliance decisions. ([PYMNTS.com][5])

Key insight: AI is no longer just a *tool* — it is rapidly becoming a *co‑pilot* for financial workflows, enhancing speed, precision, and contextual intelligence across payments, risk, and customer engagement.

## **4. Company & Startup Spotlight**

### **1) Lithic & Coinstar — Bridging Physical & Digital Funds**

**What they do:** Lithic provides card issuing and processing technology, now powering **Coinstar’s CINQ digital wallet and debit card**, allowing physical cash to be converted into digital spendable funds.
**Recent development:** This integration enables near‑real‑time cash‑to‑digital value flows for consumers.
**Why it matters:** It represents a *tangible bridge* between traditional cash habits and digital finance, particularly important in markets with mixed digital adoption. ([Finextra Research][6])

### **2) Usio & Genius Avenue — Payments for Regulated Businesses**

**What they do:** Usio provides payment technology and ACH processing.
**Recent development:** Partnership with Genius Avenue expands digital payment options (including prepaid cards and ACH) for the insurance and healthcare sectors — a niche poised for automation.
**Why it matters:** This demonstrates how *specialised vertical payment solutions* can drive adoption in enterprise sectors traditionally slower to digitise. ([FinTech Futures][7])
