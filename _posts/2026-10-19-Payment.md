# Payment Brief — 2026-10-19

**Today:** Money20/20 week opens with a bank-issued deposit token reaching Ethereum mainnet, fresh funding for recurring-payment infrastructure, and telco-grade fraud signals pitched to financial institutions.

## Top Stories

### 1. 💳 **JPMorgan Brings JPM Coin Deposit Token to Ethereum**

*The Block · Oct 19, 2026*

**Bottom line:** JPMorgan's tokenized dollar deposit now lives on Ethereum as well as Base, widening where institutional clients can move bank money onchain.

JPMorgan's Kinkexys unit said Ethereum's onchain liquidity and tokenized assets make it a natural next network for JPMD, which first launched on Base in November 2025. The token represents existing JPMorgan deposits and now supports peer-to-peer transfers across the Ethereum Virtual Machine ecosystem, including Base and Ethereum.

**Why it matters:** A bank-issued deposit token sitting beside tokenized assets such as money market funds on the same network is a building block for onchain settlement. It also keeps the stablecoin-versus-deposit-token contest firmly in play as banks and card networks make their Money20/20 announcements.

### 2. 📊 **ScribeUp Raises $13M Series A to Help Banks Manage Recurring Payments**

*Banking Dive · Oct 19, 2026*

**Bottom line:** ScribeUp closed a $13M Series A to give financial institutions tools that help consumers keep track of subscriptions and other recurring payments.

The round was led by Curql Collective and announced to coincide with Money20/20 in Las Vegas. ScribeUp says it serves more than 60 financial institutions and covers subscriptions, buy now pay later, rent and insurance payments.

**Why it matters:** Banks and credit unions are looking for ways to make digital banking more proactive. Recurring-payment management is a practical use case that sits close to the account relationship.

### 3. 🔒 **Comviva and Aduna Pitch Network APIs for Fraud and Onboarding at Money20/20**

*PR Newswire (via Newswire.ca) · Oct 19, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Comviva and Aduna are showing financial institutions how telecom network signals can support identity checks and fraud prevention.

At Money20/20 USA, the two companies are demonstrating network APIs such as number verification and SIM-swap detection. They say these can strengthen authentication and make customer onboarding and KYC faster and lower-friction. Their partnership dates to November 2025, and they plan to extend it to lending risk and payments intelligence.

**Why it matters:** SIM-swap and account-takeover fraud remain persistent problems for payment providers. Operator-sourced signals are becoming an additional layer in identity and risk stacks, though this is a product showcase and results have not been disclosed.
