# Quantum Brief: IonQ to Acquire Elisity for $1.3B to Boost Security

**Today:** Major consolidation and record capital deployments are reshaping the quantum computing industry as hardware providers integrate zero-trust security and secure massive funding for fault-tolerant architectures.

## Top Stories

### 1. ⚛️ **IonQ Acquires Cybersecurity Startup Elisity for $1.3 Billion**

*SiliconANGLE · October 12, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Quantum computing developer IonQ has agreed to acquire enterprise cybersecurity firm Elisity in a transaction valued at $1.3 billion.

The acquisition includes $150 million in performance-based earnouts to combine IonQ's trapped-ion quantum architectures with Elisity’s zero-trust microsegmentation and identity-based security software. IonQ plans to leverage Elisity's network policy enforcement tools to build quantum security solutions that address post-quantum encryption and sovereign communications requirements.

**Why it matters:** As commercial quantum systems advance toward practical deployment, integrating zero-trust networking directly into quantum platforms provides enterprise and government clients with immediate cybersecurity infrastructure ahead of full post-quantum migration.

### 2. 📊 **Oratomic Raises $775 Million to Build Fault-Tolerant Quantum Systems**

*Data Center Dynamics · October 12, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Quantum computing startup Oratomic has secured $775 million in total funding to accelerate the development of fault-tolerant quantum computers.

The Pasadena-based firm, which emerged from stealth earlier this year, expanded its funding base following a $300 million Series A round three months prior. The new capital will fund research and hardware development required to scale fault-tolerant systems capable of running error-corrected quantum algorithms.

**Why it matters:** Capital concentration in fault-tolerant architecture startups highlights growing investor commitment toward long-term quantum hardware scaling rather than near-term, noisy intermediate-scale quantum (NISQ) applications.

### 3. ⚛️ **IonQ Demonstrates Quantum Memory Interconnect via Photonic Link**

*eeNews Europe · October 12, 2026*

**Bottom line:** IonQ has successfully demonstrated a photonic interconnect achieving over 1,000 entanglement events per second between trapped-ion qubits and solid-state quantum memory.

The test results, published in a technical preprint, demonstrate high-speed entanglement generation across hybrid quantum hardware components. The demonstration addresses key interconnect bottlenecks critical to building modular quantum processing units (QPUs) and networked quantum data centers.

**Why it matters:** Quantum interconnects are essential for modular quantum computing systems, enabling scalable network architectures that financial institutions and data center operators will require to run complex optimization models.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.eenewseurope.com/en/ionq-demonstrates-world-first-quantum-memory-interconnect/)

### 4. ⚛️ **Infineon and ZuriQ Partner to Scale Trapped-Ion Quantum Chips**

*eeNews Europe · October 13, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Semiconductor giant Infineon Technologies and Swiss startup ZuriQ have expanded their collaboration to develop two-dimensional trapped-ion quantum processing units.

The partnership focuses on combining ZuriQ's ion-trap architecture with Infineon's industrial-scale semiconductor fabrication and photonics manufacturing technologies. The next phase aims to scale qubit counts and advance hardware compatibility toward fault-tolerant computing systems.

**Why it matters:** Leveraging existing semiconductor fabrication plants for quantum chip manufacturing accelerates the transition from lab-scale quantum prototypes to repeatable, commercial-grade hardware production.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.eenewseurope.com/en/infineon-and-zuriq-scale-trapped-ion-quantum-chips/)

### 5. 🔒 **WISeSat.Space Advances Post-Quantum Communications for Satellite Networks**

*Markets Insider · October 13, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Satellite operator WISeSat.Space is deploying SEALSQ post-quantum semiconductors across its space communications infrastructure.

The hardware integration utilizes SEALSQ's Quantum Shield QS7001 and QVault platforms, which feature NIST-standardized algorithms including ML-KEM (FIPS 203) and ML-DSA (FIPS 204). The initiative aims to secure satellite telemetry and orbital data transmissions against future quantum-assisted decryption attacks.

**Why it matters:** Post-quantum cryptography (PQC) integration is expanding beyond ground networks into satellite infrastructure, providing critical defense for cross-border communications and critical national infrastructure across the Asia-Pacific region.
