## **2. In-Depth Highlight**

### **📌 Finextra’s 2026 US Regulation Pulse Check: Fintech’s Compliance Compass**

Finextra’s latest **2026 US Regulation Pulse Check** captures evolving regulatory priorities for fintech and financial services firms as compliance demands shift in the post-pandemic era. ([Finextra Research][1]) The survey explores areas such as risk frameworks, automation for compliance and the prioritization of regulatory technology (RegTech). Unlike previous years, fintech firms face a complex blend of deregulation in some areas and tighter supervisory expectations in others, with compliance teams under pressure to modernise workflows and adopt automation. Importantly, the report highlights how AI-powered solutions are being used not only for efficiency but also for improving reporting accuracy and reducing false positives in financial crime detection. The insight provides executives with a practical roadmap for navigating regulatory risk in 2026 — from risk-based AI governance to cross-border policy alignment.

**Why it Matters:** This survey sets the tone for compliance strategy across the fintech ecosystem, reaffirming that regulatory readiness is a competitive advantage — especially as firms adopt automation and AI at scale.

**Key Players:** Finextra, US financial institutions, RegTech vendors.

**Market Impact:** Firms leveraging compliance automation and real-time monitoring tools will likely outperform peers in both risk management and operational resilience.

## **3. Market & Industry Insight**

### **📈 Payments, Digital Wallets, and AI — 2026’s Twin Engines**

The fintech landscape continues to evolve rapidly in 2026, driven by two core pillars: **digital payments growth** and **AI-enabled automation**. Digital wallets and cashless transaction volumes are projected to cross **2 trillion global transactions** this year, signalling profound consumer preference for instant, secure, and accessible payment experiences. ([Finextra Research][2])

Digital wallets — from PicPay’s blockbuster IPO success to infrastructure partnerships across borders — show that payments services remain a vertical of strategic importance. As fintechs expand into Asia, Latin America, and beyond, personalized and interoperable payment experiences are emerging as differentiators in an increasingly crowded market.

Meanwhile, AI is shifting from backend efficiency into value-added services — from fraud detection to intelligent customer journeys — reshaping how firms engage users. However, as industry voices point out, robust **trust mechanisms** and **reliable payment rails** remain the backbone of fintech’s credibility, even as automation accelerates. ([Finextra Research][3])

**Strategic Takeaway:** The winners in 2026 will be those who can blend secure, seamless payments with intelligent automation — all while maintaining trust, reliability, and regulatory compliance.
