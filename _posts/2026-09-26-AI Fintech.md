# AI Fintech Brief — 2026-09-26

**Today:** AI in finance is moving beyond pilots, with banks deploying conversational and agentic experiences while regulators emphasize governance, risk controls, and trust.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **NatWest to trial generative AI for personalised spending insights and fraud support**

*NatWest Group · September 26, 2026*

**Bottom line:** NatWest is combining generative AI-powered financial insights with an AI fraud-triage agent to move banking assistants from answering questions toward handling customer needs.

The bank plans to trial a fully generative audio-visual Spending Insights tool that lets customers explore transactions through natural voice and text conversations. It is powered by NatWest's proprietary Small Language Models and can respond to follow-up questions, interruptions and changes in topic.

NatWest is also introducing a Fraud Triage Agent within Cora to help customers reporting suspicious card transactions reach the appropriate support more quickly.

**Why it matters:** The combination points toward a broader banking model in which AI becomes an interface to financial data and operational workflows, rather than simply a chatbot layered onto existing services.

### 2. 🏦 **Indonesia's OJK calls for stronger AI governance and risk management in banking**

*ANTARA News · September 26, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Indonesia's financial regulator says AI adoption in banking must be accompanied by governance, supervision and risk management to protect financial stability and public trust.

OJK Deputy Commissioner Deden Firman Hendarsyah said AI can introduce risks extending beyond individual models into bank operations and the wider financial industry. The regulator highlighted the need to balance AI innovation with adequate oversight and risk controls.

**Why it matters:** As banks deploy AI across credit, customer service, fraud and operational processes, regulatory expectations are increasingly shifting from experimentation toward accountable deployment and enterprise-level risk management.

### 3. 🤖 **FITC TechNnovation FinTech Conference focuses on AI, blockchain and autonomous financial systems**

*The Guardian Nigeria · September 26, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Nigeria's financial-services ecosystem is focusing on AI, blockchain and autonomous systems as technologies for improving productivity and trust across financial services.

The sixth FITC TechNnovation FinTech Conference brought together regulators, financial institutions, fintech companies, technology firms, policymakers and academics under the theme of building intelligent financial ecosystems. Discussions examined how AI, blockchain and autonomous systems could reshape financial-sector productivity and trust.

**Why it matters:** The focus on autonomous systems alongside AI and blockchain reflects a broader fintech transition from digitising existing processes toward increasingly software-driven financial operations.
