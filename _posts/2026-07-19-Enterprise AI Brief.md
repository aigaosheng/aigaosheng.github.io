## Market Signal

Enterprise AI momentum continues to shift from **AI experimentation → operational deployment**. The biggest barriers are no longer only model quality, but also:

- Data readiness
- AI governance
- Security controls
- Workflow redesign
- Cost management

The enterprise winners of the next AI cycle are likely to be organizations that successfully transform AI from a productivity tool into a deeply integrated operating capability.
