# Payments Brief: OpenAI Launches ChatGPT Wallet With Card Networks

**Today:** Agentic commerce moved from pilot to live product as OpenAI switched on card-network-backed checkout inside ChatGPT, while four ASEAN central banks and two Southeast Asian regulators advanced cross-border payment and currency rails.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **OpenAI Launches ChatGPT Wallet With Visa, Mastercard and Amex**

*TechCrunch · November 13, 2026*

**Bottom line:** OpenAI's "dots" AI agents can now complete purchases on a user's behalf using tokenised card credentials from Visa, Mastercard and American Express, rather than raw card numbers.

OpenAI began rolling out ChatGPT Wallet to US-based ChatGPT Pro subscribers aged 18 and over with eligible US-issued cards. Users review items and totals before approving a purchase with a biometric check such as Face ID, after which the payment networks generate a token that stands in for the actual card so merchants never see full card details; OpenAI also published a Commerce Partner Playbook to help merchants support agent-led shopping.

**Why it matters:** This is one of the clearest live deployments of tokenised, agent-initiated payments at consumer scale, a model Visa and Mastercard have both flagged as essential to trust in agentic commerce. Asian card issuers, wallets and PSPs should expect merchant and compliance questions about agent checkout to arrive well before similar rollouts reach the region.

### 2. 🌐 **Four ASEAN Central Banks Sign Local Currency Transaction Pact**

*Xinhua · November 14, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Bank Negara Malaysia, Bank Indonesia, the Bank of Thailand and the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas signed a memorandum of understanding to expand the use of local currencies in settling bilateral trade and investment transactions.

The Local Currency Transaction (LCT) Framework, signed on November 13, supersedes earlier bilateral arrangements between BNM, BI and BOT dating back to 2023 and now brings the Philippines into the fold. The four central banks said the framework aims to cut costs from multiple currency conversions and improve cross-border settlement efficiency across the four markets.

**Why it matters:** Reducing reliance on USD intermediation for intra-ASEAN trade settlement is a direct, structural play on payment costs and FX risk for corporates operating across these markets, and it builds pressure on cross-border payment and treasury providers to support local-currency rails alongside dollar-based ones.

### 3. 💳 **BSP and Cambodia's Central Bank to Link QR Payment Systems**

*Manila Times · November 14, 2026*

**Bottom line:** The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas and the National Bank of Cambodia have committed to linking their national QR payment systems to enable cheaper, faster cross-border retail transactions.

Following bilateral talks on October 30–31 in Phnom Penh between BSP Governor Eli Remolona Jr. and NBC Governor Chea Serey, the two institutions reaffirmed plans to pursue QR interoperability, covering governance, regulatory requirements and the participation of financial institutions. The announcement also covered cybersecurity and fraud-prevention cooperation; no implementation timeline was disclosed.

**Why it matters:** This extends the growing web of ASEAN QR interlinkages beyond the more established Singapore-Thailand-Malaysia-Indonesia network, giving travellers, migrant workers and SMEs trading between the Philippines and Cambodia a lower-cost alternative to card rails and remittance operators.

### 4. 💳 **HKEX Launches Digital Payment Solution for After-Hours Margin Deposits**

*Caproasia · November 14, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Hong Kong Exchange will let derivatives clearing participants submit advance margin deposits digitally during its after-hours trading session from December 14, 2026.

Eligible participants will be able to submit margin requests between 5:00pm and 1:30am, building on real-value trials HKEX ran with settlement banks following a June 2026 initiative with the Hong Kong Monetary Authority to explore wholesale e-HKD, a 24/7 central bank digital currency, for this use case. HKEX said the move supports planned extensions to after-hours trading from March 2027.

**Why it matters:** It is a concrete, revenue-generating use case for CBDC-adjacent settlement rails in a major Asian market infrastructure, illustrating how wholesale digital currency projects are migrating from trials into operational finance rather than staying confined to pilots.

### 5. 💳 **Wealthsimple Opens Retail Beta for Canada's CADD Stablecoin**

*Fintech.ca · November 13, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Wealthsimple began offering a limited group of retail clients access to CADD, a Canadian-dollar stablecoin issued by Alberta-regulated Tetra Trust Company, with plans to open it to all clients by year-end.

Each CADD token is designed to redeem 1:1 for Canadian dollars, backed by reserves held at regulated Canadian institutions in cash, money market funds and short-term government debt. Wealthsimple is offering a promotional 3.25% annual reward on CADD balances, paid weekly in the token, and says it is the first regulated Canadian financial institution to distribute it to retail clients.

**Why it matters:** It shows regulated retail brokerages, not just crypto-native firms, becoming distribution channels for fiat-backed stablecoins — a pattern MatchMove and other Asian BaaS players should watch as a template for how regulated consumer stablecoin access could develop in SG, PH and ID once local frameworks mature.

## Key Takeaways

- Tokenisation is now the common thread across both agentic commerce (ChatGPT Wallet) and wholesale infrastructure (HKEX/e-HKD), reinforcing card networks' and market infrastructures' bet that token-based trust models, not raw credentials, will underpin the next wave of payments.
- ASEAN cross-border rail-building is accelerating on two fronts at once — wholesale currency settlement (the four-bank LCT framework) and retail QR interoperability (BSP-Cambodia) — reducing dependence on USD intermediation and card networks for intra-regional flows.
- Regulated, non-crypto-native institutions (HKEX, Wealthsimple) are increasingly the ones operationalising digital-asset-adjacent payment rails, signalling that mainstream financial infrastructure is absorbing these tools faster than headline stablecoin and CBDC pilots alone would suggest.

## What to Watch

Watch for ChatGPT Wallet's rollout beyond US Pro subscribers and whether any Asian card issuers or wallets announce comparable agentic-checkout integrations, plus any BSP-NBC or ASEAN LCT implementation milestones that move beyond the current memorandum stage.
