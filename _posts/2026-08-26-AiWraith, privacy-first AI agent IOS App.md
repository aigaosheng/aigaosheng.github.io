## AiWraith: Your AI. On your iPhone. Private by design.

Meet [**AiWraith**](/wraith-site/index.html), a private AI assistant that runs directly on your iPhone.

No account.
No cloud AI.
No tracking.
No subscription required.

AiWraith runs open-source AI models **on-device**, so your conversations, documents, and everyday tasks can stay on your phone.

### What can AiWraith do?

* 🤖 **Chat with local AI models** — no API key or cloud account required
* 📄 **Read your documents** — PDFs, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, CSV, images and more
* 🌐 **Research the web** — search, read sources, and get answers with citations
* 📅 **Manage your day** — create reminders, plans, briefs and tasks
* 📊 **Create things** — charts, diagrams, reports, spreadsheets and other files
* 🔒 **Control network access** — use the **No-Egress Lock** to keep the assistant completely offline

The idea is simple:

> **Your assistant should live where your data lives.**

AiWraith is built for people who want the convenience of an AI assistant without handing every conversation and document to a cloud service.

**Private AI, running in your pocket.**
