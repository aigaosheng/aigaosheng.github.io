# FintechNews.sg – Weekly Newsletter

**Period:** January 3 – January 10, 2026

---

## 📝 Executive Summary

This week’s FintechNews.sg coverage reflects ongoing expansion and strategic shifts across digital assets, payments and regional fintech leadership roles. A major theme is **infrastructure growth and interoperability**, illustrated by stablecoin‑powered payouts expanding into new currencies and broader adoption of mobile payments in tourism markets. ([Fintech Singapore][1])

In **crypto and digital assets**, institutional building blocks continue to be fortified: Fireblocks has moved deeper into financial controls with a strategic acquisition, while stablecoin‑based payout platforms are diversifying services that underpin global settlement infrastructure. ([Fintech Singapore][2])

On the **payments front**, countries beyond Singapore are driving adoption, especially in Southeast Asia. Thailand’s focus on cashless options for tourists highlights evolving payment behaviors and cross‑border use cases. ([Fintech Singapore][3])

Regionally, **leadership movement and sector enablement** are prominent. Salesforce’s appointment of a new ASEAN head underlines enterprise fintech growth priorities, particularly around AI‑led transformation. ([Fintech Singapore][4])

Lastly, **risk and compliance awareness** is gaining prominence with the announcement of a regional webinar addressing the Asia Pacific fraud crisis — signaling the sector’s need to reinforce safeguards amid rapid digitisation. ([Fintech Singapore][5])

---

## 📈 Key Trends

* **Stablecoin infrastructure expansion:** Platforms are broadening payout capabilities across currencies, signaling maturity in crypto‑linked settlement rails. ([Fintech Singapore][1])
* **Cross‑border payments in tourism growing:** QR and card‑based cashless options are gaining traction among tourists in Southeast Asia. ([Fintech Singapore][3])
* **Leadership moves spotlight regional strategy:** Enterprise tech firms are prioritising ASEAN growth with senior fintech leadership hires. ([Fintech Singapore][4])
* **Risk and compliance resurgence:** Fraud mitigation and regulatory response are rising to prominence as fintech activity scales. ([Fintech Singapore][5])
* **Events and educational formats proliferate:** Webinars and forums address core challenges like fraud and risk across APAC.
