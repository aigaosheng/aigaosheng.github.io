# Investment+Startup Brief — 2026-08-10

## Top Stories

### 1. **Global New Unicorn Creation Surges Past Full-Year 2025 Levels**

* **Source**: Crunchbase News · August 10, 2026
* **Summary**: A total of 195 companies joined the Crunchbase Unicorn Board in H1 2026, already exceeding the 193 companies that reached unicorn status during all of 2025. AI and robotics were the leading categories, while AI infrastructure, semiconductors, fintech, healthcare and aerospace also featured prominently. The US accounted for 110 new unicorns, while China accelerated sharply to 38 from just 10 in 2025.
* **Why It Matters**: The data points to a bifurcated venture market: capital is flowing aggressively into perceived technology leaders while remaining selective elsewhere. Rapid follow-on rounds and large valuation increases suggest that competition for frontier AI and infrastructure assets is intensifying.

### 2. **Aum Ventures Raises ₹225 Crore First Close for Deeptech Fund**

* **Source**: YourStory · August 10, 2026
* **Summary**: Aum Ventures has completed a ₹225 crore first close for its ₹750 crore India Innovation Fund II. The fund targets pre-seed and seed-stage Indian companies developing deep technologies, with more than 65% of the first-close commitments coming from international LPs. Aum expects to invest in roughly 25–30 companies, with initial cheques of approximately $750,000–$2 million and reserves for later rounds.
* **Why It Matters**: The fund signals growing institutional appetite for India's longer-duration technology opportunities, particularly semiconductors, AI, robotics, energy transition and advanced manufacturing. The international LP participation also suggests increasing confidence in India's ability to produce globally competitive deeptech companies.
* **URL**: [https://yourstory.com/2026/08/aum-ventures-raises-225-crore-first-close-for-deeptech-fund](https://yourstory.com/2026/08/aum-ventures-raises-225-crore-first-close-for-deeptech-fund)

### 3. **Discovered Materials Raises $9M to Use AI Agents for Semiconductor Materials Discovery**

* **Source**: TechCrunch · August 10, 2026
* **Summary**: YC-backed Discovered Materials raised a $9 million seed round led by Lightspeed India Partners, with participation from Peak XV Partners and prominent angels. The startup uses AI-agent workflows and physics models to search for materials that could improve semiconductor efficiency and thermal performance. Its system reportedly increases the number of material candidates explored from dozens per day to thousands.
* **Why It Matters**: The investment reflects a broader shift from AI software toward AI-enabled scientific discovery and industrial technology. Semiconductor thermal management is becoming increasingly important as AI compute density rises, creating a potentially valuable intersection between AI, materials science and chip manufacturing.

### 5. **Visoid Raises €2.16M to Scale AI-Powered Architectural Visualisation**

## Investor Takeaways

* **AI remains the dominant capital magnet**, but the investment opportunity is broadening beyond foundation-model companies into AI infrastructure, scientific discovery and vertical applications.
* **Deeptech is attracting larger pools of institutional capital**, particularly where AI intersects with semiconductors, materials science and industrial R&D.
* **The venture market is increasingly bifurcated**: top-performing AI and technology companies can raise rapidly at sharply higher valuations, while capital remains much more selective for companies without strong technical or commercial differentiation.
* **India is becoming increasingly important for early-stage deeptech capital**, with domestic VC funds attracting international LP participation and targeting globally relevant technology companies.
* **Vertical AI is moving toward workflow integration**, with investors increasingly backing startups that apply AI to specific professional or industrial processes rather than generic AI tooling.
