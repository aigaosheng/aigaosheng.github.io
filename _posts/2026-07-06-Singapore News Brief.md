## Top Stories

### 1. **Singapore and Indonesia deepen energy cooperation with cross-border electricity deal**

* **Reuters** · 2026-07-06
* **Summary**: Singapore and Indonesia signed a memorandum of understanding involving Singapore firms Keppel Electric, Sembcorp Industries, and Singapore Energy Interconnections alongside Indonesia’s sovereign wealth fund Danantara. The agreement focuses on developing a cross-border electricity project aimed at strengthening regional energy connectivity and sustainability.
* **Why It Matters**: This marks a major step toward an integrated Southeast Asian energy grid, improving energy security and enabling cleaner electricity imports for Singapore’s long-term transition goals.
* **URL**: [https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/indonesia-singapore-sign-agreement-cross-border-electricity-project-2026-07-06/](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/indonesia-singapore-sign-agreement-cross-border-electricity-project-2026-07-06/) ([Reuters][1])

### 2. **Singapore and Indonesia reaffirm maritime security cooperation over Strait of Malacca**

* **Times of India** · 2026-07-06
* **Summary**: Both countries pledged to keep the Strait of Malacca open, safe, and accessible, calling themselves “strategically aligned” on maritime stability. The strait remains one of the world’s most important shipping routes for global trade and energy transport.
* **Why It Matters**: The agreement reinforces geopolitical stability in a critical global chokepoint, supporting uninterrupted trade flows that Singapore’s economy heavily depends on.
* **URL**: [https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/rest-of-world/strategically-aligned-singapore-indonesia-pledge-to-keep-strait-of-malacca-open-and-free/articleshow/132215725.cms](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/world/rest-of-world/strategically-aligned-singapore-indonesia-pledge-to-keep-strait-of-malacca-open-and-free/articleshow/132215725.cms) ([The Times of India][2])

### 4. **Singapore expands Circle Line with new MRT stations in final network stage**

* **LTA (official)** · 2026-07-06 (related rollout updates)
* **Summary**: The final phase of Circle Line Stage 6 introduces three new stations—Keppel, Cantonment, and Prince Edward Road—linking HarbourFront to Marina Bay. Full passenger service is scheduled to begin mid-July after preview operations.
* **Why It Matters**: This completes the Circle Line loop, improving cross-island connectivity and reducing commute times in key business and residential districts.
* **URL**: [https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/newsroom/2026/5/news-releases/circle-line-stage-6-to-open-for-public-preview-on-4-july-2026.html](https://www.lta.gov.sg/content/ltagov/en/newsroom/2026/5/news-releases/circle-line-stage-6-to-open-for-public-preview-on-4-july-2026.html) ([Land Transport Authority][4])
