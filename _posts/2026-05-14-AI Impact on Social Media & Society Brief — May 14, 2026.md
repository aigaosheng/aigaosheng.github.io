- **Spain Advances Strict Social Media and AI Laws Defying Big Tech Lobbying**
- **Source** · Reuters via U.S. News · May 13, 2026
- **Summary**: Spain is pushing forward with aggressive legislation to regulate social media algorithms and high-risk AI systems, despite intense pushback from major technology firms. The proposed laws would ban social media for teenagers, force companies to disclose algorithmic workings, and hold executives personally liable for hate speech. This positions Spain alongside France, Greece, and Australia in a tightening regulatory environment.
- **Why It Matters**: These rules represent a major shift toward platform accountability, threatening business models built on engagement algorithms and anonymous speech. Tech companies may face operational fragmentation as they navigate stricter European regulations versus looser frameworks elsewhere.
