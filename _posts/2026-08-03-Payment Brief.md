### 1. **Worldline Finalizes Sale of Indian Payment Activities to BillDesk**
- **Source**: GlobeNewswire · August 3, 2026

The transaction includes a long-term technology and software partnership, ensuring operational continuity while allowing Worldline to focus on its North Star transformation plan. The company will retain its Indian Global Competence Centres as strategic innovation hubs for AI and automation.
- **Why It Matters**: This strategic exit strengthens Worldline’s financial profile, contributing to an estimated €590M–€640M in net cash proceeds from recent global disposals. This capital flexibility enables targeted redeployment toward core European payment operations and next-generation infrastructure.
