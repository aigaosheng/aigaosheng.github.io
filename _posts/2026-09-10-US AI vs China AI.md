## Strategic Takeaway

The **US-China AI race is becoming less about a single frontier-model leaderboard and more about control of the full AI stack**.

The US retains major advantages in frontier-model companies, advanced semiconductor design, cloud infrastructure and AI capital markets. China, however, is rapidly strengthening three complementary advantages: **low-cost inference, open-weight model distribution, and domestic AI hardware**.

The immediate bottleneck is compute economics. China's ability to substitute for Nvidia is being constrained by HBM availability and cost, while US restrictions are simultaneously accelerating China's incentives to develop indigenous chips, memory, manufacturing and software.

The most consequential development today is therefore the emerging **two-track AI relationship**: Washington and Beijing are simultaneously escalating technology-security restrictions while signaling willingness to establish formal AI dialogue. That combination is likely to define the next phase of the global AI race.
