### 1. **Together AI raises $800M at $8.3B valuation, fueling neocloud expansion**

* **Reuters / TechCrunch** · 2026-07-01
* **Summary**: AI infrastructure startup Together AI raised $800 million in a Series C round led by Aramco Ventures, pushing its valuation to $8.3 billion. The company provides GPU-based “neocloud” infrastructure for training and running open-source AI models. Demand has surged as enterprises shift from closed frontier models to lower-cost open-source alternatives, driving rapid usage growth and multi-billion-dollar infrastructure bets across the sector. ([Reuters][1])
* **Why It Matters**: This reinforces the accelerating shift toward AI infrastructure plays (“neoclouds”) as one of the most heavily funded startup categories in 2026, rivaling foundation model companies in capital intensity.

### 2. **OXMIQ raises $35M to build licensable AI chip architecture**

* **Reuters / Economic Times** · 2026-07-01
* **Summary**: Chip architecture startup OXMIQ raised $35 million to develop a unified AI chip design that integrates GPUs, CPUs, and tensor processors into a single IP block. The company aims to license its architecture to governments and enterprises seeking sovereign AI capabilities. ([Reuters][2])
* **Why It Matters**: The deal highlights rising demand for sovereign AI infrastructure and the push to reduce dependence on traditional semiconductor stacks dominated by major incumbents.
* **URL**: [https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/startup-oxmiq-raises-35-million-build-chip-architecture-lower-cost-ai-2026-07-01/](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/startup-oxmiq-raises-35-million-build-chip-architecture-lower-cost-ai-2026-07-01/)
