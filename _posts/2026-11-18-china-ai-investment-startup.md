# AI Investment & Startup in China Brief: NeuxMind.AI Raises Series B for Enterprise Agent OS

**Today:** Mid-sized Chinese AI rounds are concentrating on enterprise agent software, physical-AI data and life-science foundation models, which are the areas enterprise and fintech buyers in Asia will increasingly meet through partners.

## Top Stories

### 1. 📊 **NeuxMind.AI Closes Several Hundred Million Yuan Series B for Enterprise Agent Platform**

*Pedaily (投资界) · November 18, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Beijing-based NeuxMind.AI has raised a Series B of several hundred million yuan, led by Saize Capital, to build an operating system that governs fleets of AI agents inside large enterprises.

The company, founded in 2021, says its stack runs from agent infrastructure (compute, models, data) through an agent operating system to applications in finance, R&D, marketing and operations. It also says it is adapting the stack to domestic compute, models and chips. Both US-dollar and yuan-denominated existing shareholders joined the round, and the proceeds will go to its enterprise "AI brain", physical intelligence and global service capabilities. These descriptions are the company's own claims.

**Why it matters:** Orchestrating and governing large numbers of agents is becoming a distinct product category, and a Chinese vendor is pitching it with an explicit domestic-stack compatibility angle. Asia-based enterprises and banks evaluating agent platforms should watch whether such vendors can show measurable deployments beyond the funding announcement.

### 2. 📊 **OneX Intelligence Raises Several Hundred Million Yuan First Round Led by IDG Capital**

*Pedaily (投资界) · November 18, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Shanghai Jiao Tong University spin-out OneX Intelligence has secured an IDG Capital-led first round of several hundred million yuan to build a foundation model for life sciences.

CCV, Yunbai Capital, SJTU Hanyuan Assets and Yunqi Capital also took part, with Palm Capital as financial adviser. The money is earmarked for model development, a wet-lab platform and validation with industry partners. The company says it is in talks with pharmaceutical firms and contract development and manufacturing organisations. It was initiated by a professor at the university's AI school, and its CEO also serves as chief scientist.

**Why it matters:** A top-tier venture firm writing a large first cheque for a model-plus-wet-lab company shows investors still backing science-focused foundation models in China. Commercial proof will depend on the pharma partnerships the company says it is negotiating.

### 3. 📊 **ByteTree Raises 50 Million Yuan Pre-A for Physical-AI Training Data**

*Pedaily (投资界) · November 18, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Suzhou-based ByteTree, an autonomous-driving data-services provider, has raised a 50 million yuan Pre-A round to supply training data for embodied AI.

Investors include Yuanhe Houwang, Yifeng Capital and embodied-AI firm Xinghaitu. The company, founded in 2021, says it serves nearly 100 automakers and driver-assistance suppliers. It also reports monthly embodied-data capacity above 50,000 hours and datasets exceeding 10,000 hours across 12 real-world scenarios. These figures come from the company and have not been independently verified.

**Why it matters:** Investors are treating data infrastructure as a lower-risk way into physical AI than robot hardware or models, and one backer said as much. Suppliers with existing cash-generating data businesses in autonomous driving may be better placed to cross over.

### 4. 📊 **SenseTime's Guoxiang Capital Backs Flapping-Wing Robot Maker Yingkan Zhiyi**

*Pedaily (投资界) · November 18, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Shenzhen start-up Yingkan Zhiyi has closed a Pre-B round of tens of millions of yuan, funded exclusively by SenseTime's Guoxiang Capital, to commercialise bio-inspired flapping-wing robots.

Founded in March 2025, the company plans to use the funds for its next-generation robots, a fluid-simulation engine, supply chain and production lines, and overseas expansion. Its first consumer product, Eagle X, won an award at the IFA 2026 trade show in September. The company says the product is in production ramp-up, with a Kickstarter campaign running and a retail presence in Sweden. Earlier backers include MiraclePlus, Qigao Capital and Yuanhe Origin.

**Why it matters:** The round reflects strategic corporate venture money flowing into niche physical-AI hardware that is aimed at overseas consumers first. Execution on volume manufacturing and channel build-out, rather than the technology alone, will determine whether the model scales.

### 5. 📊 **Xellar Secures Strategic Funding from State and Pharma-Backed Investors for AI Drug Discovery**

*Pedaily (投资界) · November 18, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Beijing-based Xellar has raised tens of millions of yuan in a strategic round from Shanghai Guotou Xianfeng and CSPC's investment fund to expand its AI and organ-chip drug-discovery platform.

The company says it combines organ-on-chip models, three-dimensional cell imaging, computer vision and AI to generate human-relevant biological data. Proceeds will go towards building out that platform and accumulating data for AI models. The round was backed by a state-linked investor and an industry fund, and the financial terms beyond "tens of millions of yuan" were not disclosed.

**Why it matters:** The mix of government-guided and pharma-linked capital shows how Chinese biotech-AI start-ups are being financed outside the large venture rounds. Small, strategically anchored rounds like this are a signal of where industrial partners want data and model capability.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://news.pedaily.cn/202611/572051.shtml)

## Key Takeaways

- Mid-sized rounds (tens to hundreds of millions of yuan) dominated the day, with capital spread across enterprise agents, physical-AI data and hardware, and life-science models rather than general-purpose LLMs.
- Strategic and state-linked investors, including SenseTime's venture arm, CSPC's fund and Shanghai Guotou Xianfeng, are prominent, suggesting industrial alignment matters as much as financial return.
- Several companies emphasise overseas expansion or domestic-stack compatibility, a pair of themes relevant to Singapore and Southeast Asian buyers weighing Chinese vendors.

## What to Watch

Monitor whether NeuxMind.AI and OneX Intelligence disclose named customer deployments or pharma partnerships, and whether later rounds for physical-AI data providers disclose valuations. Also watch the pace of Hong Kong and mainland listing filings by larger Chinese AI companies.
