**Why 80% of AI Projects Fail — and How Salesforce Plans to Fix It**

Imagine pouring millions into an AI initiative, only to watch it stall due to fragmented data, poor governance, or security lapses. It's a nightmare scenario for enterprises—and it's happening more often than you'd think. According to Salesforce, over 80% of AI projects fail to deliver meaningful business value. But the company isn't just sitting back; it's launching a comprehensive solution to turn the tide.

---

### 🧱 Enter the Einstein GPT Trust Layer

Salesforce's latest innovation, the **Einstein GPT Trust Layer**, aims to provide a "trusted AI foundation" for enterprises grappling with AI deployment challenges. This suite of tools focuses on three core capabilities:

* **Unified Business Data Grounding**: Ensuring AI outputs are based on consistent and accurate business data.

* **Embedded Security and Compliance Controls**: Integrating robust security measures and compliance standards into every AI workflow.

* **Cross-Platform AI Agent Connectivity**: Facilitating seamless interaction between AI agents across different platforms and data sources.

Desiree Motamedi, Salesforce's Senior Vice President and Chief Marketing Officer, emphasizes the importance of these features, stating, "Customers still have fragmented data, weak governance, and poor security... they want a way to bring AI at scale that has accuracy, context, and control." ([Venturebeat][1])

---

### 🛠️ Tools to Tackle Data Chaos

Salesforce's new tools are designed to address specific aspects of the enterprise AI challenge:

* **Data Cloud Context Indexing**: Helps AI agents interpret complex documents like contracts and technical diagrams within their proper business context.

* **Data Cloud Clean Rooms**: Allows organizations to securely share and analyze data with partners without exposing sensitive information, using Salesforce's "zero copy" technology.

* **Tableau Semantics**: Ensures consistent definitions of business metrics across different systems and teams, translating raw data into standardized business language.

These tools aim to create a more cohesive and secure AI ecosystem within enterprises.

---

### 🔍 Looking Ahead

As Salesforce prepares for its annual Dreamforce conference, CEO Marc Benioff is expected to showcase the company's vision for what he calls the "agentic enterprise"—workplaces where AI agents work alongside humans across every business function. With the launch of the Einstein GPT Trust Layer, Salesforce is positioning itself to lead in this new era of AI-driven business operations.
