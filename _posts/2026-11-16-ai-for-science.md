# AI for science Brief: Yuyao Launches MatterSeek Materials Design Platform

**Today:** A single verified story qualified today: a China-linked materials-AI platform pairing a foundation model with an automated lab, relevant to practitioners tracking Asian deep-tech R&D.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🔬 **Yuyao Technology Launches MatterSeek AI Platform for Materials R&D**

*PR Newswire, via The Manila Times · November 16, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Yuyao Technology has announced a global launch of MatterSeek, which it describes as a general-purpose AI platform that proposes new materials together with a route to make them.

The company says the platform rests on an in-house physics-based foundation model rather than a language model. It supports forward design, which predicts performance from a composition, and inverse design, which proposes a material, crystal structure and processing route from target properties. Yuyao says promising candidates are checked with simulation and then tested in its own automated lab, with results fed back to retrain the model. It claims inverse design can optimise 190 metrics, that training data includes several hundred thousand real experimental records, and that it has served dozens of industrial customers.

**Why it matters:** If the closed-loop claims hold up, tying design outputs to synthesis routes and cost estimates targets a gap that has slowed AI materials tools in industry, notably in the semiconductor and new-energy sectors Yuyao names. All figures come from the company's own press release, no customers or partners are named, and no independent validation has been published, so treat the claims as unverified.
