# AI research & open-source LLM model Brief — 2026-09-21

## Top Stories

### 1. **StepFun Introduces Step 5 Preview, a 600B-Parameter Agentic Model**

* **Source**: StepFun · September 21, 2026
* **Summary**: StepFun introduced Step 5 Preview, a sparse Mixture-of-Experts model with 600B total parameters and 27B active parameters per token. The model supports a 1M-token context window, vision input, and is positioned for software engineering, professional knowledge work, research, and finance. StepFun says the model scores 44 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index and will be released with open weights on October 15.
* **Why It Matters**: The release reinforces the emerging model strategy of separating API availability from subsequent open-weight distribution. If the October release proceeds as announced, a frontier-scale Chinese model with open weights could materially expand the pool of models available for private deployment, research, and agent experimentation.
* **URL**: [https://www.stepfun.com/step-5-preview](https://www.stepfun.com/step-5-preview)

### 2. **Sovereign AI Is Increasingly Being Built on Adapted Open Models**

* **Source**: Counterpoint Research · September 21, 2026
* **Summary**: Counterpoint Research reports that 56% of tracked sovereign AI LLMs are adapted models rather than models trained entirely from scratch. Among adapted sovereign models, Meta's Llama accounts for 38% of the foreign base-model share, followed by Alibaba's Qwen and France's Mistral.
* **Why It Matters**: The finding highlights an important shift in the economics of AI sovereignty: national and regional AI initiatives can increasingly differentiate through fine-tuning, data, alignment, infrastructure, and deployment rather than rebuilding every layer of the foundation-model stack. Open-weight ecosystems therefore remain strategically important even when the resulting systems are branded as sovereign models.
* **URL**: [https://counterpointresearch.com/en/insights/56-percent-of-sovereign-ai-llms-are-adapted-models-with-metas-llama-the-dominant-base](https://counterpointresearch.com/en/insights/56-percent-of-sovereign-ai-llms-are-adapted-models-with-metas-llama-the-dominant-base)

### 3. **Experiential Launches an Open-Source Gateway for Cloud and Local AI Models**

* **Source**: GIGAZINE · September 21, 2026
* **Summary**: Experiential has launched an open-source AI gateway that provides a unified OpenAI-compatible API across cloud and locally deployed models. The system supports centralized API-key, usage, and cost management, while its routing layer can evaluate model quality, latency, and cost and use historical telemetry to optimize model selection. The platform lists more than 1,000 supported models, including DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi, GLM, Mistral, and Llama.
* **Why It Matters**: Model proliferation is creating a new infrastructure layer between applications and foundation models. Open gateways that combine local and cloud inference could make model routing, cost optimization, BYOK, and private deployment increasingly standard components of production AI stacks.
* **URL**: [https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20260921-experiential/](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20260921-experiential/)
