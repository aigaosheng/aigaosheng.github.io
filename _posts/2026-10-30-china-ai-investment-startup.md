# AI Investment & Startup in China Brief: DeepSeek Discloses Landmark $15.8 Billion Funding Round

**Today:** Fresh disclosures on DeepSeek's record funding round, state-backed capital flowing into Kai-Fu Lee's 01.AI, and a quiet Hong Kong listing filing from a humanoid-robot maker show how deeply Chinese state and corporate capital is now embedded in the country's AI champions.

## Top Stories

### 1. 📊 **DeepSeek's Second Funding Round Details Emerge: CNY105.5bn Raised, State Share Triples**

*East Money · October 30, 2026*

**Bottom line:** DeepSeek's second funding round totalled roughly CNY105.5 billion (about $15.8 billion) from nearly 30 investors, with state-linked capital's share of the round jumping to 27% from 2% in its first round.

Newly disclosed details show Tencent and CATL each committing CNY10 billion, alongside new participation from China Mobile, China Telecom, CITIC Securities, CICC and China Life. Corporate investors including Meituan, vivo, Geely, Midea, Luxshare, Trip.com and Xiaohongshu also joined, while the report attributes part of the round's expansion to rising Nvidia-based server costs. The article did not disclose a post-money valuation figure.

**Why it matters:** The sharp rise in state-linked ownership signals Beijing is treating frontier AI labs as strategic national assets, a dynamic enterprise and fintech leaders across Asia should watch when assessing data-sovereignty exposure, partnership risk and compute-cost pass-through from Chinese model providers.

### 2. 📊 **Kai-Fu Lee's 01.AI Secures Hangzhou State Backing**

*TechNode · October 30, 2026*

**Bottom line:** 01.AI has secured a strategic investment worth hundreds of millions of yuan from state-backed investors in Hangzhou and its Linping district, announced two days after the agreement was signed.

The funding will anchor 01.AI's new headquarters and an AI adoption base in Linping, reinforcing the company's pivot from consumer-facing model services toward enterprise and industry-specific AI deployment. 01.AI reportedly now customises open-weight Chinese models, including DeepSeek, Qwen and GLM, for business clients, with roughly half of revenue coming from outside China.

**Why it matters:** The deal illustrates how Chinese local governments are using targeted equity stakes to anchor AI talent and tax base locally, a funding pattern that enterprise AI vendors elsewhere should factor into competitive and pricing assumptions when facing Chinese rivals abroad.

### 3. 📊 **Noetix Robotics Confidentially Files for Hong Kong Listing**

*TechNode · October 30, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Humanoid-robot maker Noetix Robotics has confidentially submitted a Hong Kong listing application, according to media reports, potentially targeting a 2027 flotation, after building sales momentum with its RMB9,998 Bumi humanoid.

Founder Jiang Zheyuan has prioritised low-cost, reliable hardware over advanced AI features, using composite materials to cut costs, and is targeting over 10,000 Bumi unit sales in 2026 following exposure from a Spring Festival Gala performance. The company is backed by existing investors including CATL-linked Chendao Capital and is developing embodied-intelligence models under its Scalabot sub-brand.

**Why it matters:** A hardware-first, revenue-led path to a public listing is an alternative model to the capital-intensive frontier-model race, offering a template Southeast Asian hardware and robotics investors may look to replicate as embodied AI funding accelerates regionally.

### 4. 📊 **AI Inference Chip Startup Nano-core Chip Raises Over RMB 1 Billion**

*TechNode · October 30, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Hangzhou-based AI inference chip developer Nano-core Chip has raised more than RMB1 billion in a Series C2 round led by JianTou Investment, just over a month after a similarly sized Series C1 round.

The Peking University-incubated startup develops chips combining 3D near-memory computing, in-memory computing and RISC-V processing, with its first three chips having completed tape-out and customer sampling planned for early 2027. Hangzhou Capital and Kaitai Capital also joined the round.

**Why it matters:** Back-to-back billion-yuan rounds for a pre-revenue inference-chip startup underline how much domestic capital is being funnelled into chip alternatives as compute costs rise, a trend that could eventually widen the hardware options available to AI deployments across Asia.

## Key Takeaways

- State-linked capital's share of China's top AI rounds is rising sharply, as seen in DeepSeek's jump from 2% to 27% between funding rounds, reshaping investor cap tables and strategic alignment with Beijing.
- Local governments are using targeted equity, not just subsidies, to anchor AI companies, as shown by Hangzhou's stake in 01.AI.
- China's AI financing landscape is diversifying across models: mega-rounds for frontier labs, state-anchored strategic stakes for enterprise pivots, hardware-led listings for robotics, and repeat rounds for chip startups are all advancing in parallel.

## What to Watch

Watch for a formal regulatory filing or valuation confirmation on DeepSeek's round, and whether Noetix's confidential Hong Kong listing application becomes a public filing with the Hong Kong Stock Exchange.
