# US AI vs China AI Brief: Cambricon Profit Jumps 148% Despite Chip Supply Squeeze

**Today:** One story cleared the filters: China's leading Nvidia alternative grew fast but fell short of sales forecasts, showing how much memory and tooling limits still cap domestic AI compute.

## Top Stories

### 1. 📊 **Cambricon's Q3 profit jumps 148%, but sales miss as chip supply limits bite**
*The Next Web (citing Bloomberg) · October 23, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Cambricon doubled its revenue and lifted profit 148% in Q3, yet missed analyst sales forecasts by a wide margin, which suggests China's drive to replace Nvidia is now constrained by supply rather than demand.

Cambricon reported Q3 revenue of 3.53 billion yuan (about $529 million), double the prior year but well below the roughly 6.26 billion yuan analysts expected. Net profit rose 148% to 1.4 billion yuan, and the company kept its target of at least 13.5 billion yuan in 2026 revenue. The report attributes the shortfall to scarce high-bandwidth memory, which US-led sanctions stop Samsung, SK Hynix and Micron from selling to Chinese buyers, and to limited access to advanced chipmaking equipment. It also notes that Cambricon's inventory grew by about 3.4 billion yuan in the quarter and that its shares have lost roughly half their value since a June record.

**Why it matters:** Morgan Stanley expects Cambricon to hold about 14% of China's AI chip market this year, second only to Huawei, and its chips run models from labs such as DeepSeek and Moonshot. For Asia-based enterprise and fintech teams weighing US against Chinese model stacks, the takeaway is that Chinese compute capacity, not buyer appetite, looks like the near-term bottleneck. That puts the pressure points of US export policy on memory and tooling rather than on finished accelerators alone.

## Key Takeaways
- Chinese AI chipmakers have demand but not enough supply: strong profit growth sits alongside a large sales miss and swelling inventory.
- The tightest limits are upstream, in high-bandwidth memory and advanced manufacturing tools, which is where US and allied controls apply most directly.
- Profit growth alone is not winning over investors, as Cambricon's shares remain far below their June peak.

## What to Watch
Whether Cambricon can turn its larger inventory into shipments that meet its 13.5 billion yuan 2026 revenue target, and whether the US congressional bill to tighten chipmaking-equipment curbs on China advances.
