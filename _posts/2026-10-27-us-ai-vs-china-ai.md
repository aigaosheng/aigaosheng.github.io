# US AI vs China AI Brief: China AI Stocks Shed $480 Billion in Rout

**Today:** China's AI equity boom has unwound even as its models close in on US leaders. That matters if you price Chinese AI suppliers, buy their hardware, or plan around Chinese open-weight models.

## Top Stories

### 1. 📊 **China's AI Stock Bubble Bursts, Wiping Out About $480 Billion**
*Reuters (via The Business Standard) · October 27, 2026*

**Bottom line:** China's AI equity rally has collapsed far harder than the US one, and the threat of US bans on Chinese data-centre suppliers is part of the reason.

The STAR50 index of Chinese AI and semiconductor leaders has fallen about 40% in under four months, erasing roughly $480 billion in market value. Over the same period the Nasdaq is only about 6% below its record. Traders blamed stretched valuations and tax bills. They also cited the risk that the US or other countries ban Chinese equipment from data centres. Optical-module makers Zhongji Innolight and Eoptolink are down about 60% from their mid-year highs, partly as Washington moves towards barring them from supplying US data centres. Goldman Sachs estimates AI stocks make up 42% of China's market capitalisation and 57% of turnover.

**Why it matters:** The rout will likely slow IPOs and equity fundraising by Chinese AI and chip firms. Asia-based buyers of Chinese optical and server components should treat US supplier restrictions as a live sourcing risk, not a hypothetical one.

### 2. 🤖 **Moonshot's Kimi K3.1 Pro Narrows Gap With US Frontier Models**
*China Daily · October 27, 2026*

**Bottom line:** A new Chinese open-weight model now sits within a few points of top US systems on coding benchmarks, though China still lags in high-end chips.

Moonshot AI's Kimi K3.1 Pro debuted fourth on Artificial Analysis' Intelligence Index with 55 points. Full weights are due by November 4. On Moonshot's own Terminal-Bench 4.0 evaluation, the model scored 65.7%. That is ahead of OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra (58.2%) and just behind Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5 (66.4%). Moonshot says it removed cybersecurity data from parts of training to limit offensive capability. Bloomberg Intelligence puts the gap between top Chinese and US models at about 3%, its narrowest on record. Separately, Bloomberg reported that Moonshot raised money at roughly a $50 billion valuation and is targeting a Hong Kong listing in Q1 2027.

**Why it matters:** Enterprises weighing Chinese open-weight models face a smaller capability trade-off than before. Data-residency, procurement and US-restriction risks still need separate assessment. Note that the benchmark figures are self-reported by Moonshot.

### 3. 🌐 **CSIS Analyst: Taiwan's Chip Dominance Is Negotiating Leverage With Washington**
*Taipei Times · October 27, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Taiwan's near-monopoly on AI servers and advanced chips, plus its alignment with US export controls, gives it bargaining power as the US-China rivalry hardens. This is analyst opinion.

Aalok Mehta, director of the Wadhwani AI Center at CSIS, argued that most countries cannot build frontier models alone and must partner with either the US or China. In his view, Taiwan can use two advantages in talks with Washington: TSMC's large US investments and its export-control alignment. The article notes that Taiwan produces about 90% of the world's AI servers and advanced AI chips. It also notes Taipei is preparing to tighten AI chip export controls to match US rules.

**Why it matters:** Regional firms that source AI hardware through Taiwan should expect US-aligned controls to apply there as well. That raises end-use due diligence requirements across Asian supply chains.

## Key Takeaways
- Capability and capital are moving apart in China: its models are converging with US leaders while investors are pulling back from its AI stocks.
- US pressure is spreading from chips to supporting components such as optical transceivers, and markets are already pricing that in.
- Allies such as Taiwan are turning export-control alignment into bargaining power, which tightens compliance across Asian hardware supply chains.

## What to Watch
Moonshot's promised release of the full Kimi K3.1 Pro weights by November 4. Independent benchmark results will show whether the reported gap with US models holds.
