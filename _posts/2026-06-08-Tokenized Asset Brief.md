### Tokenized Asset Brief — 2026-06-08

## Top Stories

### 1. Securitize Clears Key SEC Hurdle for NYSE Listing via SPAC Merger
- **CoinMarketCap** · 2026-06-08
- **Summary**: The US SEC has approved the Form S-4 registration statement for the SPAC merger between Cantor Equity Partners II and Securitize, a leading real-world asset tokenization firm. A shareholder vote is scheduled for June 29th, and if approved, the combined entity will list on the NYSE as Securitize Corp (ticker: SECZ). Securitize currently manages $4 billion in assets and reported Q1 revenue of $19.5 million, up 39% YoY.
- **Why It Matters**: This marks a potential first-of-its-kind public listing for a pure-play tokenization infrastructure company, offering traditional investors direct exposure to the RWA sector's growth. It also signals increasing regulatory acceptance of compliant digital asset models.

### 2. Bybit Launches "IPO Express" Offering Tokenized SpaceX Shares at Primary Market Price
- **BlockBeats** · 2026-06-07
- **Summary**: Bybit has launched "IPO Express," a product allowing eligible VIP and Pro users to subscribe to tokenized IPO shares at the issuance price. The first offering is for SpaceX's highly anticipated IPO, with subscriptions running from June 7th to 11th. The tokenized shares, provided by xStocks, are backed 1:1 by real equity, with spot trading set to begin on June 12th.
- **Why It Matters**: This represents a major leap in democratizing access to pre-IPO primary markets, which have traditionally been reserved for large institutions. It demonstrates how tokenization can break down geographical and financial barriers in capital markets.
- **URL**: [Read more](https://en.theblockbeats.news/flash/349913)

### 3. Franklin Templeton: Tokenization is Rebuilding Financial Infrastructure, Not Just a Marketing Tool
- **Financial News** · 2026-06-08
- **Summary**: In an interview, Franklin Templeton's Director of Digital Asset Research, Christopher Jensen, argued that RWA tokenization represents a structural innovation that rebuilds financial infrastructure from the ground up. He highlighted that moving issuance, settlement, and compliance "entirely into code" creates a programmable economy that offers unmatched efficiency. The firm's OnChain MMF, FOBXX (Benji), has proven a regulated fund can operate on public blockchain infrastructure.
- **Why It Matters**: One of the world's largest asset managers is articulating a vision that goes beyond simple fund tokenization, focusing on automated compliance and settlement. This provides strong validation for institutional adoption of blockchain rails for traditional finance.
- **URL**: [Read more](https://en.fnnews.com/news/202606031237012400)

### 4. Total On-Chain RWA Value Hits Record $32 Billion as Tokenized Treasuries Dominate
- **CoinMarketCap** · 2026-06-08
- **Summary**: Data from RWA.xyz shows the total value of tokenized real-world assets (excluding stablecoins) reached a record $32 billion in May, up 220% over 12 months. Tokenized US Treasuries account for nearly half of the market, while tokenized commodities represent 16% and tokenized stocks make up a smaller 4.8% (approx. $1.5 billion).
- **Why It Matters**: The explosive growth, driven largely by yield-bearing assets like Treasuries, confirms a strong product-market fit for tokenized traditional financial instruments. The relatively small share of tokenized equities highlights a significant growth opportunity for platforms addressing that segment.
