# AI Finance in Singapore Brief: MAS Launches National AI Missions for Finance

**Today:** The Singapore FinTech Festival opened with MAS turning its AI-in-finance agenda into concrete programmes, which sets the near-term direction for banks, fintechs and vendors operating in Singapore.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🏦 **MAS and Future of Finance Institute Set Out National AI Missions for Financial Services**

*Monetary Authority of Singapore · November 18, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Singapore has defined three national AI missions for finance (financial crime prevention, business financing access and safe AI-agent transactions) and attached delivery vehicles to each.

MAS and the Future of Finance Institute (FFI) announced the initiatives at the Singapore FinTech Festival, with Deputy Prime Minister and MAS Chairman Gan Kim Yong setting them out. They include an AI Innovation Arena that gives developers synthetic data, starting with a cross-bank scam dataset built with GovTech, and an Agentic Money Sandbox for testing AI agents that transact with digital money. DBS, OCBC, Standard Chartered and UOB were named as FFI's financial institution launch partners, and FFI will also work with AI Singapore on talent programmes such as apprenticeships.

**Why it matters:** The state is now steering AI adoption in finance through shared infrastructure and named missions, not guidance alone. Vendors and fintechs should expect the Arena and the Sandbox to become the main route for testing and co-developing with Singapore's largest banks.

### 2. 💳 **Singapore Plans Sandbox to Test AI Agent Payments Before Live Use**

*Fintech News Singapore · November 18, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Singapore intends to let AI agents move money only inside tested limits, with trials covering agent identity, permitted actions and cross-system transactions.

According to the report, Gan outlined a model in which an agent releases a payment only once preset conditions are met, such as verified contract terms. Industry trials will examine how an agent's identity and authority can be verified and how agents could transact across different systems. FFI will run the Agentic Money Sandbox, with agent-initiated payments and treasury management as the first use cases. The article also reports Gan's position that institutions remain accountable for what agents do on their behalf.

**Why it matters:** The accountability stance signals that agentic payments in Singapore will be built around bank liability from the outset. Payment providers and treasury-technology vendors building agent features should plan for identity, mandate and limit controls as baseline requirements.

### 3. 🔒 **DPM Gan Details Scam Detection Utility Built on Five-Bank Data Pooling**

*Prime Minister's Office Singapore · November 18, 2026*

**Bottom line:** A five-bank proof-of-value on pooled transaction data is moving towards a design study for an industry-wide Scam Detection Utility.

In his festival speech, Gan said five banks are pooling transaction data to test whether AI can detect scam patterns that individual banks cannot see. MAS will begin a design study early next year covering the utility's blueprint, governance and operating model, initially focused on Singapore accounts and data. The speech also covers the SME-financing mission, where AI would draw on richer data such as bank accounts, invoices, payments and government records, tested first with synthetic data and SME feedback.

**Why it matters:** A shared utility would change how scam-fighting capability is built, from bank-by-bank models towards a common layer, with data-sharing governance as the central issue. Banks and anti-fraud vendors should watch how the design study treats data privacy, liability and access.

### 4. 🤖 **UOB and Standard Chartered Join A*STAR and FFI Effort to Stress-Test Finance AI**

*Fintech News Singapore · November 18, 2026*

**Bottom line:** A*STAR's AI institute and FFI have signed an MoU to set joint research priorities and test AI systems with participating financial firms, with UOB and Standard Chartered as the first to join.

Per the report, the partners will publish findings and build prototypes that turn research into tools for finance. One focus is MERaLiON, A*STAR's multilingual model, aimed at the languages, accents and code-switching common in Singapore and Southeast Asia. The second is testing AI systems with finance firms to find risks and gaps and improve reliability in real-world use.

**Why it matters:** Locally grounded language models and shared testing could help banks serving multilingual Asian customers, particularly in voice and compliance monitoring. The initiative also gives MAS-aligned evidence on AI reliability ahead of its AI risk guidelines taking effect in 2027.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://fintechnews.sg/141600/ai/mas-ai-research-collaboration-astar-ffi/)

## Key Takeaways

- MAS is moving from principles to programmes: missions, sandboxes and shared datasets now sit alongside its AI risk management guidelines.
- Agentic AI is the common thread, and Singapore's approach keeps institutions accountable while trialling agents in controlled settings.
- Pooled industry data, for scam detection and SME financing, is becoming a strategic asset, which makes governance of data sharing the key design question.

## What to Watch

MAS has said it will begin the Scam Detection Utility design study early in 2027, and has said it intends to consult the financial sector in 2027 on further agentic AI guidance. Also watch for the first participants and use cases in the Agentic Money Sandbox and the AI Innovation Arena.
