## Top Stories

### 1. Data Security Firm Cyera Raises $600M at $12B Valuation
- **Yahoo Finance / Reuters** · 2026-06-10
- **Summary**: Data security startup Cyera has raised $600 million in a late-stage funding round led by Evolution Equity Partners, quadrupling its valuation to $12 billion from just over a year ago. The round saw participation from Temasek, Cyberstarts, and existing investors including Accel, Blackstone, and Coatue. The company reports tripling its annual recurring revenue for three consecutive years and has grown to 1,500 employees across 18 countries.
- **Why It Matters**: This deal underscores surging investor demand for data security solutions amid rising cybersecurity threats and generative AI adoption. Cyera’s rapid growth and aggressive M&A strategy (five acquisitions in 18 months) position it as a major consolidator in the data security posture management space.
- **URL**: [Data security firm Cyera raises $600 million at $12 billion valuation](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/data-security-firm-cyera-raises-121814549.html)

### 2. Hark Launches with $700M Series A at $6B Valuation for AI Personalized Intelligence
- **Latham & Watkins LLP** · 2026-06-10
- **Summary**: New AI lab Hark has emerged from stealth with a $700 million Series A funding round at a $6 billion post-money valuation, one of the largest Series A rounds on record. The oversubscribed round was led by Parkway Venture Capital, with participation from NVIDIA, AMD, Intel Capital, Qualcomm Ventures, Salesforce Ventures, and ARK Invest among others. Hark is focused on building "advanced personalized intelligence" and is backed by a powerhouse consortium of semiconductor and venture capital firms.
- **Why It Matters**: The sheer size of this Series A illustrates the intense capital concentration in foundational AI. The involvement of nearly every major chipmaker (NVIDIA, AMD, Intel) signals a strategic land grab to shape and access the next generation of AI models and the hardware that powers them.
- **URL**: [Latham & Watkins Advises on Hark's US$700 Million Series A](https://www.lw.com/en/news/2026/06/latham-watkins-advises-on-hark-series-a)

### 3. Robinhood Receives Approval to Underwrite IPOs, Stock Rises
- **Nasdaq** · 2026-06-10
- **Summary**: Robinhood Markets announced it has received regulatory approval to underwrite initial public offerings, a significant expansion of its capital markets business. CEO Vlad Tenev's announcement came just days before the widely anticipated SpaceX IPO, sending Robinhood's stock up over 3% on a down day for major indexes. Gaining a foothold in IPO underwriting, especially for high-profile deals like SpaceX, could open a lucrative new revenue stream and deepen its ecosystem for active traders.

### 4. S2G Investments Closes $1B Solutions Fund for Growth-Stage Climate Tech
- **ImpactAlpha** · 2026-06-10
- **Summary**: S2G Investments, founded by Walmart heir Lukas Walton, has closed its $1 billion Solutions Fund I to target profitable, revenue-generating climate and sustainability companies that need capital to scale. The fund will write checks between $25 million and $100 million, taking minority stakes in areas like food & agriculture, energy, and oceans. This strategy targets the "missing middle" between early-stage venture capital and mature infrastructure buyouts.
- **Why It Matters**: The fund directly addresses a critical financing gap for "pre-profit" climate tech companies that have proven business models but are too mature for VC and too small for private equity. S2G's successful raise from outside institutional investors validates a "third way" of climate investing focused on scaling operational impact over breakthrough technology bets.
- **URL**: [G is for growth after $1 billion raise for Lukas Walton-backed S2G Investments](https://impactalpha.com/g-is-for-growth-after-1-billion-raise-for-lukas-walton-founded-s2g-investments/)

### 5. Rivvun AI Raises $7.55M Seed Round for Enterprise Spend Recovery Platform
- **The Hindu BusinessLine** · 2026-06-10
- **Summary**: Rivvun AI has raised $7.55 million in an oversubscribed seed round co-led by Sitara Capital and 3one4 Capital. The startup deploys autonomous AI agents to recover uncollected enterprise value from contracts, addressing an estimated $2 trillion in annual revenue and spend leakage. Founded by veterans from Icertis, the platform integrates with existing ERP and CRM systems to enforce supplier rebates and correct billing variances.
- **Why It Matters**: This seed round highlights investor appetite for applied AI with direct, measurable ROI for CFOs, rather than horizontal productivity tools. Rivvun's focus on "P&L impact" from day one represents a pragmatic shift in enterprise AI valuation metrics away from user adoption and toward bottom-line recovery.
- **URL**: [Rivvun AI raises $7.55 m Seed Round to recover enterprise spend, revenue leakage](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/companies/rivvun-ai-raises-755-m-seed-round-to-recover-enterprise-spend-revenue-leakage/article71085392.ece)
