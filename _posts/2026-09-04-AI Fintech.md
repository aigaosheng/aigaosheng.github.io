# AI Fintech Brief — 2026-09-04

## Top Stories 

### 1. **Nasdaq Acquires AI Due-Diligence Platform Dasseti to Expand eVestment**

* **Source**: FinTech Futures · September 4, 2026
* **Summary**: Nasdaq has completed its acquisition of Dasseti, an AI platform that automates investment due-diligence questionnaires and RFP processes for institutional investors. Dasseti's technology will be integrated into Nasdaq's eVestment institutional intelligence platform, bringing automated data collection, research management, investor relations and ESG analysis closer to the core investment workflow.
* **Why It Matters**: The deal illustrates a broader shift from generic AI assistants toward embedded, workflow-specific intelligence in financial infrastructure. For institutional finance, AI is increasingly becoming part of the data and decision workflow rather than a separate productivity layer.

### 2. **Singapore Fintechs Face a Growing Shortage of AI-and-Finance Talent**

* **Source**: The Business Times · September 4, 2026
* **Summary**: Singapore fintech companies are reporting difficulty finding experienced professionals who combine technology, finance and regulatory expertise. The shortage is becoming more pronounced as financial institutions adopt AI while operating under increasingly complex regulatory requirements. ### 5. **Apate.ai Raises $8.15M to Scale Adversarial AI for Fraud Prevention**

* **Source**: FinTech Futures · September 4, 2026
* **Summary**: Australia-based Apate.ai has raised $8.15 million in an oversubscribed seed round led by Lobby Capital. Its technology uses conversational AI agents to infiltrate fraud networks, engage with fraudsters and extract intelligence about scams. ### 7. **Warba Bank Expands Agentic AI Into Transactional Banking**

* **Source**: International Finance · September 4, 2026
* **Summary**: Kuwait's Warba Bank is highlighting Bdr AI, an agentic banking assistant integrated into its mobile banking application. Customers can communicate through natural-language voice or text in English and Arabic, including Kuwaiti Arabic, to initiate supported banking transactions rather than navigating multiple menus manually. ### 8. **Agentic AI Could Automate 80% of Routine Treasury Work, EY Says**

* **Source**: ETCIO · September 4, 2026
* **Summary**: An EY assessment says agentic AI could automate roughly 80% of routine coordination and processing work in treasury operations. Potential starting points include cash forecasting, reconciliation, reporting and KYC/AML exception handling, while the report recommends an "advisory before autonomous" approach and cautions against autonomous payment execution during early pilots.
* **Why It Matters**: Treasury may become one of the most practical enterprise-finance use cases for agents because much of the work involves repetitive coordination across ERP systems, banks and financial data sources. The limiting factor is likely to be data architecture and governance rather than model capability alone.
* **URL**: [https://cio.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/artificial-intelligence/agentic-ai-could-automate-80-of-routine-treasury-work-leave-strategic-decisions-to-humans/133752506](https://cio.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/artificial-intelligence/agentic-ai-could-automate-80-of-routine-treasury-work-leave-strategic-decisions-to-humans/133752506)
