# AI Finance in Singapore Brief: MAS Advances Know Your Agent Protocol for Financial Services

Today: Singapore is moving beyond AI experimentation towards shared financial-sector infrastructure, with MAS focusing on trusted AI agents, cross-bank fraud detection and AI-enabled SME financing.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🏦 MAS Advances Know Your Agent Protocol for Financial Services

The Business Times · November 19, 2026

Bottom line: MAS is developing a common protocol to verify AI agents before financial institutions allow them to access services or execute transactions.

Managing Director Chia Der Jiun announced the proposed Know Your Agent (KYA) protocol at the Singapore FinTech Festival. It is intended to help financial institutions establish an agent's identity, the customer it represents, its authority and its permitted mandate.

The initiative also envisages secure communication channels between financial institutions and authorised agents, providing a common foundation for agent-driven financial activity.

Why it matters: KYA could become an important trust layer for agentic banking, payments and wealth management. Financial institutions should start considering how agent identity, delegated authority, transaction limits and auditability will fit into their existing authentication and risk-control architectures.

### 2. 🤖 MAS Outlines Agentic Money Sandbox for Banking and Payments

Monetary Authority of Singapore · November 19, 2026

Bottom line: MAS plans to test AI agents and programmable money in controlled environments covering corporate treasury management and payments.

In his Singapore FinTech Festival keynote, MAS Managing Director Chia Der Jiun outlined an Agentic Money Sandbox developed with the Future of Finance Institute. Participating banks and payment providers will explore how agents can monitor liquidity, initiate payments and execute authorised treasury instructions using tokenised deposits and stablecoins.

The proposed framework places responsibility for defining mandates with corporate customers, while participating financial institutions establish execution controls and determine which agent instructions they accept.

Why it matters: This moves agentic finance towards practical institutional deployment rather than conversational banking alone. Banks will need to integrate agent orchestration with treasury systems, programmable money, transaction authorisation and operational risk controls.

## Key Takeaways

- Trust infrastructure is becoming essential: Agent identity, delegated authority and transaction mandates are emerging as foundational requirements for agentic financial services.
- Financial autonomy needs boundaries: Institutions must retain control over execution permissions, transaction limits and accountability.
- Singapore is building shared infrastructure: MAS is positioning industry collaboration and controlled experimentation as pathways towards practical AI adoption in financial services.

## What to Watch

Watch for MAS and the Future of Finance Institute to advance the Know Your Agent protocol and launch the Agentic Money Sandbox. The critical next signal will be participation by banks and payment providers, followed by evidence of successful live pilots and clearly defined liability arrangements.
