# Quantum Brief: UK FCA Sets 2028 Quantum Security Deadline

**Today:** Regulators and quantum hardware makers both moved from research-stage thinking to concrete deadlines and shipped products, a signal that enterprise and fintech leaders should start treating post-quantum readiness as a near-term planning item rather than a distant risk.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🏦 **UK FCA Sets 2028 Deadline for Quantum-Safe Migration Plans**

*Finadium · November 4, 2026*

**Bottom line:** The UK's financial regulator has told firms to complete post-quantum cryptography migration plans by 2028, with full system migration required by 2035.

The FCA, aligned with National Cyber Security Centre guidance, is asking firms to map where cryptography is used across their systems, set risk-based migration objectives, and test migration plans, with high-priority migration activities due by 2031. The regulator noted that failed change projects accounted for around a fifth of incidents reported to it in 2025 and said it is trialling a self-assessment tool to help firms gauge readiness.

**Why it matters:** For BaaS and card-issuing platforms handling long-lived customer data across Singapore, the Philippines and Indonesia, the same "harvest now, decrypt later" risk applies even without an equivalent local mandate yet; MAS-regulated entities should expect similar planning expectations to follow as UK and EU timelines firm up.

### 2. ⚛️ **Universal Quantum Unveils Foundry-Built Ion-Trap Chip**

*The Quantum Insider · November 3, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Universal Quantum's new iQPU processor is built on commercial 200mm semiconductor foundry lines, a step toward mass-manufacturable quantum hardware rather than lab-only devices.

The chip packs a compute surface of over 400 mm² using a Quantum Charge-Coupled Device architecture that shuttles ions for flexible qubit connections, paired with integrated ultra-low-noise control electronics and "UQConnect" modular links for joining multiple chips into larger systems. The UK-based company recently secured a €67 million contract with Germany's aerospace agency DLR for two scalable systems targeting climate and materials modelling.

**Why it matters:** Foundry-compatible manufacturing addresses one of quantum computing's biggest scaling bottlenecks; if the approach holds up, it shortens the runway for enterprise-grade machines that fintech and payments players would eventually need for post-quantum-safe infrastructure testing and optimisation workloads.

### 3. 🔒 **Why Banks Need Crypto-Agility Before Quantum Computers Arrive**

*The Quantum Insider · November 4, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Financial institutions should build the ability to swap cryptographic algorithms quickly, rather than wait for a fixed date when quantum computers can break current encryption.

A guest analysis from ZeroTier's CEO argues that adversaries are already harvesting encrypted financial data today to decrypt once quantum computers mature, and recommends building a cryptographic inventory, prioritising payment and identity systems, deploying quantum-safe overlays during the transition, and aligning vendors on migration timelines. It cites NIST's 2024 post-quantum standards and the US Treasury's Quantum-Readiness Task Force, launched in August 2026, as reference points.

**Why it matters:** Payment rails and card networks sit squarely in the "prioritise first" category the piece describes; Singapore and regional BaaS providers managing long-lived KYC and transaction data should treat vendor and processor crypto-agility as a procurement question now, not a 2030s one.

### 4. 🔬 **University of Chicago and Toyota Launch Quantum Mobility Research Pact**

*The Quantum Insider · November 3, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Toyota is committing up to $1 million annually to a multi-year quantum research partnership with the University of Chicago focused on transportation applications.

The collaboration spans quantum communication, sensing, materials discovery, theoretical modelling and electronic properties, aimed at developing quantum sensors for vehicles, secure vehicle-to-infrastructure data links, and novel materials for quantum devices and energy storage. Up to eight UChicago graduate students will be embedded in the effort, alongside Toyota researchers already working as visiting scientists at the university's labs.

**Why it matters:** The deal signals growing corporate appetite for quantum R&D tied to concrete industrial use cases rather than pure research, a pattern enterprise technology buyers in Asia's manufacturing and automotive supply chains should watch for signs of where quantum sensing and materials science become commercially relevant.

### 5. 🔬 **MIT Researchers Find New Path to Universal Quantum Control**

*The Quantum Insider · November 4, 2026*

**Bottom line:** MIT-led researchers showed that deliberately breaking a quantum system's mirror symmetry can unlock full control using only global, shared control fields rather than individually addressed qubits.

The team demonstrated the theory on an eight-atom Rydberg-atom chain, using optimised global laser pulses to create an effective three-atom interaction and observing topological edge-mode signatures beyond what the hardware's built-in pair interactions would normally produce. The findings are due to appear in Nature Physics.

**Why it matters:** Techniques that achieve fine-grained control with simpler, global hardware rather than per-qubit addressing could lower the engineering overhead of future quantum processors, a long-term input into the cost curve enterprise buyers eventually see.

## Key Takeaways

- Regulators are moving from guidance to deadlines: the FCA's 2028/2031/2035 timeline puts a concrete planning burden on financial firms, with payment and card infrastructure likely first in scope.
- Quantum hardware progress and post-quantum security planning are now running on parallel tracks, both demanding attention from the same risk and technology teams.
- Corporate-sponsored quantum research, such as the Toyota-UChicago pact, shows deep-tech investment broadening beyond pure-play quantum vendors into industrial R&D partnerships.
