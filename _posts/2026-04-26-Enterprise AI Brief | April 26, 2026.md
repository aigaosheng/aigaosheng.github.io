### **Google Doubles Down on AI Agents for Enterprise Monetization**

* **Source:** Reuters | April 22, 2026
* **Summary:** Google is restructuring its enterprise AI strategy by centering it on AI agents, rebranding Vertex AI under the Gemini Enterprise umbrella. The move emphasizes autonomous agents that can execute workflows across enterprise systems, signaling a shift from tools to “AI coworkers.” ([Reuters][1])
* **Why It Matters:** This marks a pivotal transition toward agent-based enterprise software, where value shifts from APIs to autonomous execution layers—reshaping SaaS economics.

## **Key Takeaways**

* **Agentic AI is the dominant paradigm** (Google, Adobe, ServiceNow)
* **Vertical AI is overtaking horizontal platforms**
* **Governance, control, and interoperability are now critical layers**

* **Enterprise AI is becoming sovereign, regulated, and mission-critical**
