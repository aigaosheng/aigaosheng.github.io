# Market Themes

## 1. Enterprise AI Moves From Pilots to Infrastructure

Banks, insurers, and payment companies are increasingly treating AI as foundational infrastructure rather than standalone experiments. The next competitive phase is likely to focus on proprietary data, governance systems, and integration into core workflows.

## 2. Fraud Prevention Remains the Strongest AI Fintech Use Case

Payment networks and financial institutions continue investing heavily in behavioural analytics, identity intelligence, and AI-driven risk systems as fraud becomes more automated and sophisticated.

## 3. Agentic Finance Emerges as a New Strategic Frontier

AI agents executing financial workflows represent a potential transformation of payments, procurement, and corporate finance. However, authorization models, compliance controls, and auditability remain critical challenges.
