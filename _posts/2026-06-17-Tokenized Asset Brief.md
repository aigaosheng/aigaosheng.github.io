### 1. US SEC Poised to Allow Stock Token Trading in Potential Market Shakeup
- **Channel NewsAsia (Reuters)** · 2026-06-17
- **Summary**: The U.S. SEC is preparing a new "innovation exemption" policy that would allow crypto companies to offer trading in blockchain-based tokenized stocks. This move, expected to be unveiled by SEC Chair Paul Atkins, could allow prominent players like Coinbase, Robinhood, and Kraken to launch these products in the U.S., potentially revolutionizing stock markets by enabling 24/7 trading and instant settlement. The combined global market value of tokenized stocks has already grown to over $6.4 billion .
- **Why It Matters**: This regulatory shift represents a major victory for the crypto industry and could fundamentally disrupt traditional brokerages by introducing blockchain efficiency to equity markets. The exemption could pave the way for crypto firms to perform exchange and clearing functions without full SEC registration, while also raising concerns about investor protections and market structure .
- **URL**: [US SEC poised to allow stock token trading in potential market shakeup](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/business/us-sec-poised-allow-stock-token-trading-in-potential-market-shakeup-6190061)

### 4. Kula's 'Title Tokenization' Aims to Solve RWA's $31B Gap
- **Forbes** · 2026-06-16
- **Summary**: Impact investment firm Kula is pioneering "title tokenization," where the token itself is the legal title to a real-world asset, rather than just a contractual claim. This model aims to address a core issue in the $31 billion RWA market, where investors often don't directly own the underlying asset. Kula has raised $25 million for projects in Africa and is running a proof-of-concept with Lionhart Capital to bring this regulated model to market .
- **Why It Matters**: This approach addresses a critical legal and structural gap in tokenization, potentially increasing investor protection and trust. If successful, Kula's model could set a new standard for how physical assets are represented on-chain, unlocking further institutional adoption by ensuring digital tokens carry full legal weight .
- **URL**: ['The Token Is The Asset'—Kula's Fix For RWA Tokenization's $31B Gap](https://www.forbes.com/sites/boazsobrado/2026/06/16/the-token-is-the-asset-kulas-fix-for-rwa-tokenizations-31b-gap/)
