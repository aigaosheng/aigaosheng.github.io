### 1. G7 Leaders to Discuss AI and Social Media Security Risks
- **The Hindu** · 2026-06-17
- **Summary**: On the final day of the G7 summit in France, leaders from Canada, France, Germany, Italy, Japan, Britain, and the United States are convening to discuss the security risks posed by AI and social media. - **Why It Matters**: The inclusion of top AI executives at a G7 working session signals a shift toward public-private collaboration on AI governance, focusing on cybersecurity, protecting democracies, and safeguarding children online. Outcomes from this meeting could establish new international norms for AI regulation .

### 2. Campaigners Urge G7 to Act on AI Risks for Children
- **Citizen Digital** · 2026-06-17
- **Summary**: A coalition of 95 young people from 19 countries has issued a manifesto calling on G7 leaders to protect children's education, mental health, and social well-being from the risks of generative AI. Campaigners warn that safeguards cannot be left solely to tech companies, citing risks like sexualized deepfakes and emotional dependency on AI chatbots .
- **Why It Matters**: As governments move to ban under-16s from social media, a regulatory gap remains for AI. France's AI ambassador noted the urgency, stating, "We can't wait 15 years again to take the right decision for our kids" as was done with social media .
