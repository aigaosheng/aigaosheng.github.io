# Payments Brief: StraitsX and Visa Partner for Global Card Issuing

**Today:** Major payment networks and fintech issuers are rapidly bridging stablecoins and card infrastructure globally, while regional digital payment fragmentation reshapes checkout strategies across Southeast Asia.

## Top Stories

### 1. 💳 **StraitsX and Visa Partner to Expand Stablecoin Card Issuing to 100 Markets**

*Fintech News Singapore · October 15, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Singapore-based digital asset infrastructure firm StraitsX has partnered with Visa to scale its stablecoin-backed card issuing program beyond Asia to over 100 markets globally.

The partnership enables consumers and businesses to spend digital settlement assets at any merchant accepting Visa worldwide. By integrating StraitsX's stablecoin infrastructure with Visa's network, the initiative aims to offer real-time currency conversion and transparent transaction settlement.

**Why it matters:** The expansion marks a major step in taking Asian stablecoin issuance global, allowing digital assets to function smoothly at point-of-sale checkout networks worldwide.

### 2. 💳 **Mastercard and BVNK Launch Integrated Global Money Platform for Stablecoins**

*GuruFocus · October 15, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Mastercard has launched its Global Money Platform in partnership with its subsidiary BVNK, offering a unified API for businesses to hold, convert, and pay out fiat and stablecoins seamlessly.

This rollout represents the first major technical integration following Mastercard's acquisition of BVNK. Early enterprise adopters including Nuvei and Marqeta will use the unified API layer to streamline multi-currency corporate payouts and treasury operations globally.

**Why it matters:** Mainstream payment networks are embedding stablecoin settlement directly into core B2B infrastructure, removing friction between traditional banking rails and digital currencies.

### 3. 🏦 **MAS Grants KPay Major Payment Institution License in Singapore**

*Technode Global · October 15, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Regional fintech platform KPay has secured a Major Payment Institution (MPI) license from the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS).

With the new regulatory approval, KPay will offer merchant acquiring and cross-border money transfer services in Singapore. The company plans to scale its all-in-one financial management and online payment solutions for small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) across the region.

**Why it matters:** The regulatory approval highlights Singapore's position as a launchpad for regional fintechs building integrated merchant acquiring and cross-border payment platforms for Asia-Pacific trade.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://technode.global/2026/10/15/fintech-kpay-gains-major-payment-institution-licence-in-singapore-adds-online-payments/)

### 4. 🌐 **Xendit and Cube Report Highlights Payment Fragmentation Across Southeast Asia**

*e27 · October 15, 2026*

**Bottom line:** A new report by Xendit and Cube reveals that while cash-on-delivery is declining sharply in Southeast Asia, digital payment adoption has fragmented across six distinct national rail preferences.

The report highlights that missing localized payment options causes cart abandonment rates as high as 51% in Indonesia. While cards dominate 60% of online transactions in Singapore, account-to-account bank transfers lead in Thailand via PromptPay, and e-wallets dominate in the Philippines and Indonesia.

**Why it matters:** Regional e-commerce merchants and payment orchestrators must maintain localized multi-rail checkout integrations to capture growth as Southeast Asia's digital economy approaches $400 billion by 2031.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://e27.co/cash-on-delivery-is-dying-in-southeast-asia-six-different-wallets-are-taking-its-place-20261015/)

### 5. 💳 **Payoneer Launches Direct Online Checkout Service in Singapore**

*The Asian Banker · October 15, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Global payment platform Payoneer has officially launched Payoneer Checkout for merchants and SMBs operating in Singapore.

The solution enables Singapore-based merchants to accept global online payments directly on their websites while consolidating foreign exchange management and payout workflows into a single account interface. The launch aims to reduce multi-currency conversion costs for cross-border merchants.

**Why it matters:** Streamlined cross-border checkout solutions give Asia-Pacific exporters direct access to international shoppers without needing complex multi-entity bank setups.

*The Asian Banker · October 14, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Global payment orchestration platform Yuno has partnered with SEON to embed real-time AI fraud detection and anti-money laundering (AML) verification directly into its checkout layer.

## Key Takeaways

* Payment giants Visa and Mastercard are accelerating the commercialization of stablecoin rails by connecting them directly with global merchant card networks and B2B payout APIs.
* Digital payments in Southeast Asia are fragmenting along national preferences (account-to-account, e-wallets, QR standards), forcing merchants to adopt orchestration layers.
* MAS continues to grant MPI licenses, bringing institutional governance and regulated merchant acquiring to growing Asia-Pacific fintechs.

## What to Watch

Watch for corporate adoption rates of unified fiat-stablecoin payment APIs among global enterprise ERP systems, alongside new regulatory guidelines for cross-border stablecoin card issuing across Asia.
