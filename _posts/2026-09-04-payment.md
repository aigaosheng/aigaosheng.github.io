# Payment Brief — 2026-09-04

## Top Stories 

### 1. **Handwave Expands Visa Partnership to Scale Palm-Based Biometric Payments**

* **Source**: The Paypers · 2026-09-04
* **Summary**: Handwave has expanded its partnership with Visa to integrate Visa tokenisation, authentication, and fraud-prevention capabilities into its palm-based payment platform. Payment cards can be linked to a user's biometric identity through tokens rather than exposing underlying card credentials. Visa will also support Handwave's expansion into European markets and potential integration with the Visa Acceptance Platform. ([The Paypers][1])
* **Why It Matters**: Biometric payments are moving from experimental checkout experiences toward network-integrated infrastructure. The combination of biometrics, tokenisation, and real-time risk controls could lower the security and interoperability barriers that have limited adoption.
* **URL**: [https://thepaypers.com/payments/news/handwave-expands-visa-agreement-for-biometric-payments](https://thepaypers.com/payments/news/handwave-expands-visa-agreement-for-biometric-payments)

### 2. **Citi Hong Kong to Launch Virtual Credit Card With Dynamic CVC**

* **Source**: Media OutReach Newswire · 2026-09-04
* **Summary**: Citi Hong Kong announced a virtual credit card developed with Mastercard that uses a dynamically generated, one-time-use CVC for online transactions. The capability will be available through the Citi Mobile app and is designed to reduce exposure of physical card credentials in card-not-present transactions. Citi says approximately 80% of its credit-card fraud attempts are related to CNP transactions. ([portal.sina.com.hk][2])
* **Why It Matters**: Dynamic credentials are becoming an increasingly important layer in card-not-present security. The model combines virtualisation, tokenisation, and transaction-specific authentication without requiring merchants to change the underlying checkout experience.
* **URL**: [https://portal.sina.com.hk/finance/finance-mediaoutreachnewswire/media-outreach/2026/09/04/1910948/citi-hong-kong-to-launch-market-first-virtual-credit-card-with-dynamic-card-validation-code-cvc-in-partnership-with-mastercard-setting-a-new-standard-for-digital-payment-security/](https://portal.sina.com.hk/finance/finance-mediaoutreachnewswire/media-outreach/2026/09/04/1910948/citi-hong-kong-to-launch-market-first-virtual-credit-card-with-dynamic-card-validation-code-cvc-in-partnership-with-mastercard-setting-a-new-standard-for-digital-payment-security/)

### 3. **ACI Worldwide to Acquire Cranium Ventures for Card Switching**

* **Source**: The Paypers · 2026-09-04
* **Summary**: ACI Worldwide has agreed to acquire Cranium Ventures, adding its microservices-based SYNAP card-switching technology to ACI Connetic for Cards. The acquisition is intended to accelerate ACI's cloud-native card-processing capabilities and strengthen its broader payments-orchestration platform. The deal remains subject to customary conditions and regulatory approvals. ([The Paypers][3])
* **Why It Matters**: Card switching remains a major modernisation bottleneck for banks and payment providers. ACI's move signals continued consolidation around cloud-native, modular payment infrastructure rather than isolated payment applications.
* **URL**: [https://thepaypers.com/mergers-aquisitions-and-investments/news/aci-worldwide-to-acquire-cranium-ventures-for-card-switching](https://thepaypers.com/mergers-aquisitions-and-investments/news/aci-worldwide-to-acquire-cranium-ventures-for-card-switching)

### 4. **Ant International Receives Brazil Payment Institution Licence**

* **Source**: FinTech News Singapore · 2026-09-04
* **Summary**: Brazil's central bank has granted Ant International a payment institution licence. Ant already operates in Brazil through Antom, its merchant payment and digitalisation platform. The licence strengthens Ant's ability to expand local and cross-border payment services in one of Latin America's largest digital-payment markets. ([Fintech Singapore][4])
* **Why It Matters**: Regulatory licensing is becoming a strategic asset for global payment companies seeking to combine domestic acquiring, merchant services, and cross-border flows. Brazil also provides a major test market for payment platforms operating alongside Pix and other instant-payment infrastructure.
* **URL**: [https://fintechnews.sg/136729/payments/ant-international-brazil-payment-licence/](https://fintechnews.sg/136729/payments/ant-international-brazil-payment-licence/)

### 5. **SumUp Secures Brazilian Licence to Expand SME Credit**

* **Source**: The Paypers · 2026-09-04
* **Summary**: SumUp has obtained an SCFI licence from Brazil's Central Bank, expanding its regulatory scope beyond its previous direct-credit structure. The licence enables SumUp to diversify funding sources, broaden financing products, and manage its balance sheet with greater flexibility. SumUp says payments remain its primary revenue source while credit and banking services now contribute around 30% of revenue. ([The Paypers][5])
* **Why It Matters**: Payment processors increasingly use transaction relationships with SMEs as the foundation for adjacent financial products. Regulatory control over funding and lending can make this embedded-finance model more scalable and less dependent on banking partners.
* **URL**: [https://thepaypers.com/fintech/news/sumup-obtains-scfi-licence-from-central-bank-of-brazil](https://thepaypers.com/fintech/news/sumup-obtains-scfi-licence-from-central-bank-of-brazil)

### 6. **Eftsure Acquires AI-Powered Invoice and Vendor Validation Fintech Relish**

* **Source**: FinTech Futures · 2026-09-04
* **Summary**: Australian fintech Eftsure has acquired US-based Relish, bringing its Data Assure vendor-validation platform and Invoice AI technology into the Eftsure ecosystem. The combined offering is designed to validate vendor identities, automate invoice processing, and verify payment files before funds are released. Eftsure says the combined platform will serve more than 4,000 corporate customers and provide bank-account verification coverage across 190 countries. ([FinTech Futures][6])
* **Why It Matters**: Payment security is expanding upstream into accounts payable, procurement, and treasury workflows. AI-assisted invoice verification combined with real-time beneficiary validation could become a critical control against business-payment fraud.
* **URL**: [https://www.fintechfutures.com/m-a/eftsure-acquires-relish](https://www.fintechfutures.com/m-a/eftsure-acquires-relish)

### 7. **AI Commerce Faces a Major Trust Gap, Alipay+ and S&P Global Find**

* **Source**: The Paypers · 2026-09-04
* **Summary**: A new Alipay+ and S&P Global study of 6,000 consumers across nine markets highlights continued fragmentation in cross-border payments and significant consumer caution toward autonomous AI purchasing. While 81.3% of respondents use AI for travel research, only 26% are interested in allowing AI to make autonomous payment decisions. Security and privacy remain major concerns. ([The Paypers][7])
* **Why It Matters**: Agentic commerce may face a trust bottleneck rather than a technology bottleneck. Payment providers will need strong identity, consent, tokenisation, dispute, and liability frameworks before consumers broadly allow AI agents to spend on their behalf.
* **URL**: [https://thepaypers.com/fraud-and-fincrime/news/alipay-and-sp-global-report-finds-trust-gap-in-ai-commerce](https://thepaypers.com/fraud-and-fincrime/news/alipay-and-sp-global-report-finds-trust-gap-in-ai-commerce)

### 8. **SEON Expands Fraud and AML Technology Into Southeast Asia**

* **Source**: The Paypers · 2026-09-04
* **Summary**: Fraud-prevention provider SEON has partnered with Exist Software Labs to deploy fraud detection, identity verification, transaction monitoring, and AML technology for clients in the Philippines. The platform combines risk scoring, payment screening, identity checks, case management, and compliance reporting. ([The Paypers][8])
* **Why It Matters**: The expansion reflects rising demand for integrated fraud controls as digital and cross-border payments scale. Combining payment risk, identity, and AML signals in a single workflow is increasingly important as fraud networks become more coordinated across markets.
* **URL**: [https://thepaypers.com/fraud-and-fincrime/news/seon-partners-with-exist-software-labs-in-southeast-asia](https://thepaypers.com/fraud-and-fincrime/news/seon-partners-with-exist-software-labs-in-southeast-asia)

### 9. **Cross-Border Mule-Account Fraud Emerges as a Growing Payment Threat**

* **Source**: The Paypers · 2026-09-04
* **Summary**: Incognia reported that 81% of financial institutions surveyed have seen an increase in mule-account handover fraud. The reported pattern involves legitimate accounts being opened by recruited money mules and subsequently transferred to operators in other countries, allowing criminal networks to bypass conventional identity-verification controls. ([The Paypers][9])
* **Why It Matters**: Strong KYC alone is insufficient when legitimate identities are deliberately recruited and subsequently handed over. Payment providers increasingly need behavioural, device, network, and account-linkage intelligence alongside traditional identity checks.
* **URL**: [https://thepaypers.com/fraud-and-fincrime/news/incognia-flags-rise-in-cross-border-mule-account-fraud](https://thepaypers.com/fraud-and-fincrime/news/incognia-flags-rise-in-cross-border-mule-account-fraud)

### 10. **Citi Processes Live US-Dollar Transactions on Swift's Blockchain Ledger**

* **Source**: PaySpace Magazine · 2026-09-04
* **Summary**: Citi has processed live US-dollar transactions on Swift's blockchain-based ledger with First Abu Dhabi Bank and OCBC, marking the first live transactions on the ledger in the Middle East and Southeast Asia regions. Citi expects DBS and UOB to participate in additional transactions later in September. ([PaySpace Magazine][10])
* **Why It Matters**: The development moves blockchain-based payment infrastructure closer to production-grade institutional use. Shared-ledger payment commitments could eventually reduce the constraints imposed by banking cut-off times while connecting traditional settlement infrastructure with tokenised assets and deposits.
* **URL**: [https://payspacemagazine.com/news/citi-processes-live-dollar-payments-on-swift-blockchain-ledger/](https://payspacemagazine.com/news/citi-processes-live-dollar-payments-on-swift-blockchain-ledger/)
