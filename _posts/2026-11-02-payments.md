# Payments Brief: HSBC RedCoin Stablecoin Tops 50,000 Users

**Today:** Hong Kong FinTech Week produced three concrete payments milestones in one day — a bank-issued stablecoin hitting real adoption numbers, a central bank pushing settlement infrastructure to 24/7, and a domestic QR scheme opening to 100 million-plus overseas merchants.

## Top Stories

### 1. 💳 **HSBC's RedCoin Stablecoin Passes 50,000 Users in Hong Kong**

*crypto.news · November 2, 2026*

**Bottom line:** HSBC's Hong Kong dollar-pegged RedCoin attracted 50,000 users within roughly a week of its October 23 launch, giving the bank an early real-world adoption data point for bank-issued stablecoins.

Group CEO Georges Elhedery disclosed the figure at a Hong Kong FinTech Week panel, saying the circulating supply grew by about HK$6.75 million in the preceding week. RedCoin activates through the PayMe wallet without a new bank account and already supports transfers and payments at more than 100,000 merchants via PayMe's existing network; HSBC plans to add it to its main mobile banking app and extend it to tokenised money-market fund subscriptions.

**Why it matters:** RedCoin is among the first HKMA-licensed bank stablecoins to publish live usage figures, giving Asian banks and regulators a concrete reference point as Singapore, Hong Kong and other MAS- and HKMA-supervised markets weigh how bank-issued stablecoins compete with e-wallets and card rails for everyday payments.

### 2. 🏦 **HKMA Pushes Project Ensemble to 24/7 Tokenised Settlement**

*Hong Kong Monetary Authority · November 2, 2026*

**Bottom line:** The HKMA advanced its EnsembleTX platform to round-the-clock settlement in tokenised central bank money, and will next bring regulated stablecoins into the same pilot alongside tokenised Exchange Fund Bills.

Announced as part of a wider "DART" set of initiatives at Hong Kong FinTech Week 2026, the upgrade lets approved dealers settle tokenised asset trades outside normal Real Time Gross Settlement hours, supporting round-the-clock treasury management and cross-border fund transfers. The HKMA also launched a tokenisation adoption programme, tConnect Launchpad, with Cyberport.

**Why it matters:** Moving wholesale settlement to a 24/7 cycle is a structural change for banks' treasury and liquidity operations, and folding regulated stablecoins into the same pilot signals Hong Kong is building one settlement layer for tokenised deposits and stablecoins rather than parallel systems — a model other Asian regulators, including Singapore, are likely to watch closely.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/news-and-media/press-releases/2026/11/20261102-3/)

### 3. 🌐 **Hong Kong's FPS to Link With UnionPay for QR Payments in 40+ Markets**

*The Standard · November 2, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Hong Kong's Faster Payment System will connect with UnionPay International by year-end, letting FPS users scan QR codes at an estimated 100 million-plus merchants across mainland China and over 40 overseas markets.

Financial Secretary Paul Chan announced the link at Hong Kong FinTech Week x StartmeupHK 2026, building on FPS's base of more than 22 million registrations in a city of 7.5 million people and average daily HKD payment volume of HK$16.2 billion in 2026. The integration extends FPS beyond its current domestic cross-bank and cross-wallet transfer use into cross-border retail acceptance.

**Why it matters:** Linking a domestic faster-payments rail directly to a major international card network's QR acceptance footprint is a practical template for interoperability that Singapore's PayNow and other ASEAN instant-payment schemes are pursuing through separate bilateral and Nexus-style arrangements.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/finance/article/347213/FPS-to-unlock-QR-code-payments-in-over-40-markets-through-UnionPay-collaboration-Paul-Chan)

## Key Takeaways

- Hong Kong is now running three parallel tokenisation and payments tracks at once — a bank-issued retail stablecoin, wholesale 24/7 tokenised settlement, and cross-border QR interoperability — all surfaced in a single policy framework.
- Regulators are treating stablecoins and tokenised deposits as complementary rather than competing, with HKMA folding both into the same EnsembleTX pilot rather than running separate regimes.
- Retail-rail interoperability (FPS-UnionPay) is advancing on a similar timeline to wholesale tokenisation work, suggesting Hong Kong wants visible consumer-facing wins alongside institutional infrastructure upgrades.

## What to Watch

Watch for HSBC's first independent reserve attestation for RedCoin, due after Q4 2026, and for confirmation of the FPS-UnionPay QR link's go-live date, which officials have only committed to "by the end of this year."
