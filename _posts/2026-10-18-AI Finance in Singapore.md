# AI Finance in Singapore Brief — 2026-10-18

**Today:** Enterprise AI platform RapidCanvas secured $29 million in Series B funding led by Singapore sovereign backer Vertex Ventures to accelerate enterprise automated operational modeling across finance and supply chains.

## Top Stories

### 1. 📊 **RapidCanvas Raises $29 Million Series B Led by Singapore’s Vertex Ventures**

*TNGlobal · October 18, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Enterprise artificial intelligence provider RapidCanvas closed a $29 million Series B financing round anchored by Vertex Ventures, the venture capital arm of Singapore’s Temasek Holdings.

The fresh capital will be deployed to expand the platform's autonomous data-to-insight capabilities across financial operations, forecasting, and supply chain management. RapidCanvas allows non-technical business teams and financial analysts to build, deploy, and monitor custom predictive AI models without complex code. Vertex Ventures' lead investment underscores growing institutional confidence in specialized enterprise AI middleware tailored for regulated regional markets.

**Why it matters:** Singaporean state-backed venture funds are aggressively backing enterprise AI infrastructure, reinforcing the city-state's strategy to anchor regional enterprise AI deployment and financial tech innovation.
