## Top Stories 

### 1. **Global Fintech Funding Falls 20% as AI-Enabled Digital Banking Remains a Bright Spot**

* **Source**: Fintech News Switzerland · August 19, 2026
* **Summary**: Global fintech funding declined 20%, with weaker crypto investment contributing to the downturn. Digital banking stood out as a comparatively resilient segment, supported by investment in neobanking platforms expanding internationally, diversifying products and incorporating AI-driven capabilities.
* **Why It Matters**: The funding environment is becoming more selective. AI is increasingly part of the differentiation strategy for fintechs seeking capital, while investors appear to be rewarding infrastructure, banking platforms and scalable operating models over speculative growth.

### 2. **Rentify Expands Its AI Workforce for Property and Financial Operations**

* **Source**: International Business Magazine · August 19, 2026
* **Summary**: Fintech and proptech company Rentify launched Renewal Command Center, its third specialised AI agent within the Earn AI operating system. The company says its AI-native platform supports property portfolios representing more than AED 22 billion in assets and over AED 1.3 billion in annual rental value.
* **Why It Matters**: The development illustrates the shift from AI copilots toward specialised agents that execute recurring operational workflows. Financial technology increasingly overlaps with AI-driven vertical operating systems capable of automating revenue, collections and asset-management processes.
* **URL**: [https://intlbm.com/2026/08/19/rentify-launches-ai-workforce-for-property-managers/](https://intlbm.com/2026/08/19/rentify-launches-ai-workforce-for-property-managers/)

### 4. **Account Takeover Risk Moves Beyond Onboarding in the AI Era**

* **Source**: Fintech News Malaysia · August 19, 2026
* **Summary**: A new industry webinar highlights how account takeover increasingly occurs after onboarding, targeting login, account recovery and high-risk transactions. The discussion focuses on fraudsters using stolen credentials, phishing and social engineering while banks attempt to strengthen detection without increasing friction for legitimate customers.
* **Why It Matters**: As AI makes fraud and social-engineering attacks more scalable, financial institutions must move toward continuous, behavioural and transaction-level risk assessment rather than relying primarily on identity checks performed at onboarding.

### 5. **Cross-Border Payments Remain a Major Fintech Infrastructure Battleground**

* **Source**: BFSI Network · August 19, 2026
* **Summary**: An industry analysis argues that cross-border payments remain slower, more fragmented and more expensive than domestic digital payment systems. The sector is increasingly being shaped by real-time payment infrastructure, APIs, embedded finance and new technology layers designed to improve interoperability and automation.
* **Why It Matters**: AI can become an important intelligence layer for cross-border payments, supporting routing, fraud detection, compliance and liquidity optimisation. The opportunity is not simply faster payments, but more autonomous financial infrastructure.
