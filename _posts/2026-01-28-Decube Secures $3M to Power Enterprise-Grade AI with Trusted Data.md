## **Decube Secures $3M to Power Enterprise-Grade AI with Trusted Data**

In a sign of mounting interest in practical AI infrastructure, Malaysian-headquartered **Decube** has raised **US $3 million** in fresh funding to accelerate its expansion across Asia Pacific and globally. The round, led by **Taiwania Hive Ventures** with participation from **Iterative** and **500 Global**, underscores investor confidence in solutions that help enterprises move beyond proof-of-concept AI pilots to production-ready deployments built on trustworthy data. ([DealStreetAsia][1])

Founded on the insight that **trusted data is the foundation of scalable AI**, Decube’s platform gives enterprises a unified layer of visibility into what their data means, where it came from, and whether it’s reliable — something many firms struggle with even if they have modern data warehouses and analytics engines. ([DealStreetAsia][1])

### **Why Data Trust Matters for AI Adoption**

Most large organizations already have data platforms and pipelines, but often lack a **context layer** that explains data lineage, quality, and governance. Without this context, AI models may be trained or fed with raw data that is inconsistent, outdated, or poorly defined — leading to unreliable insights or even costly errors. Decube’s platform fills that gap by centralizing metadata, lineage tracking, data quality monitoring, and governance into one operational system. ([TNGlobal][2])

In practical terms, Decube sits between raw data systems and AI or analytics workloads. It delivers a **single source of truth** for data meaning and reliability — essential for regulated industries like banking, telecom, and financial services, where visibility and auditability are as critical as performance. ([TNGlobal][2])

### **Where the Funding Will Be Deployed**

With the fresh capital, Decube plans to:

* Expand its **product capabilities**, particularly around context and trust layers that make data ready for AI use. ([TNGlobal][2])
* Grow its presence in the **APAC region**, where demand for AI built on secure, explainable data infrastructure is rising. ([TNGlobal][2])
* Forge deeper **regional partnerships** and enterprise deployments, emphasizing practical, scalable AI solutions. ([TNGlobal][2])

In the view of Taiwania Hive’s leadership, the funding validates Decube’s mission: to fix one of the most stubborn barriers to AI adoption — **data quality and trust**. As companies move AI beyond experimentation toward mission-critical use cases, founders and data leaders alike increasingly see context and trust as non-negotiable foundations. ([TNGlobal][2])

### **Glossary: Key Terms Explained**

* **Data Trust**: Confidence that data is accurate, current, reliable, and governed — enabling AI systems and business users to make decisions without second-guessing underlying inputs. ([decube.io][3])
* **Data Lineage**: The documented pathway of data from its origin through transformation stages to its final form, helping teams understand where data came from and how it changed. ([decube.io][3])
* **Metadata**: Descriptive information about data — such as its structure, meaning, usage, and ownership — that helps users interpret and govern datasets. ([decube.io][3])
* **Governance**: Policies, roles, and controls that ensure data is managed responsibly, securely, and in compliance with internal standards or regulations. ([decube.io][3])
* **APAC**: Asia Pacific — a diverse region spanning East Asia, Southeast Asia, South Asia, and Oceania where enterprise tech adoption is rapidly growing.
