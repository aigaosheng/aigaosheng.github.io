**How to choose an AI-enabled CPU for gaming laptops**

---
To choose an AI-enabled CPU for a gaming laptop in 2025, focus on balancing AI-centric capabilities (like the Neural Processing Unit, or NPU), raw gaming performance, power efficiency, and platform compatibility.[1][2][3]

### Key Considerations for AI-Enabled Gaming CPUs

- Check for an integrated NPU (Neural Processing Unit) or an "AI Engine," as this hardware accelerates AI gaming features, improves frame generation, and enhances real-time upscaling without taxing the CPU or GPU.[2][3]
- Look for high TOPS (trillions of operations per second) in the NPU specs—current top CPUs like AMD's Ryzen AI 9 365/395 and Intel's Core Ultra 9/7 series feature 40–50 TOPS, meeting or exceeding the Copilot+ PC requirements for Windows AI features.[3][2]
- For best traditional gaming performance, prioritize CPUs with high core counts and clocks, plus support for PCIe 4.0 or above and DDR5 memory. Intel Ultra 9/7 HX-series and AMD Ryzen AI HX 370/365 are leading chips.[1][2]
- Evaluate the laptop’s thermal and power profile. More efficient AI chips tend to deliver longer battery life and better sustained performance while gaming unplugged.[3]
- Confirm compatibility with your favorite games, since some AI-assisted features or optimizations (like AI upscaling, frame interpolation, or Copilot+ features) may vary by GPU vendor or Windows version.[4][2]
- Consider future-proofing. AI-assisted gaming is evolving rapidly, so a CPU with a current-gen NPU and firmware update support is ideal.
