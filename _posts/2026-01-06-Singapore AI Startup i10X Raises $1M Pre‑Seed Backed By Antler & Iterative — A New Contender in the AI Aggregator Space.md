# 🚀 Singapore AI Startup *i10X* Raises $1M Pre‑Seed Backed By Antler & Iterative — A New Contender in the AI Aggregator Space

In a funding landscape that’s tightening globally, a young Singapore‑based AI startup *i10X* is making noise. The company has just closed a **US$1 million pre‑seed funding round** led by early‑stage backers including **Iterative**, **Antler AI Disrupt**, and **PPR Ventures** — a strong signal of confidence in its vision to unify access to a fragmented AI ecosystem. ([asiabusinessoutlook.com][1])

The raise, completed in late 2025, gives *i10X* the runway to refine its product, scale infrastructure, and expand the team as it pushes toward broader adoption. ([asiabusinessoutlook.com][1])

## 🧠 Solving the AI Fragmentation Problem

At its core, *i10X* is built around a deceptively simple premise: **make it easy for users and developers to harness the power of multiple AI models in one place** — without juggling individual subscriptions or APIs.

Today’s AI scene is dominated by major models like **GPT**, **Claude**, and **Gemini**, alongside thousands of niche tools and specialised agents. *i10X* aggregates access to these disparate technologies in a unified platform, offering users one interface to tap into over **500 AI agents and models**. ([asiabusinessoutlook.com][1])

This combination of breadth and simplicity positions the startup as an “AI aggregator” — a hub where users can harness specialised agents *and* mainstream large language models without technical complexity.

## 🌍 Global Footprint from Singapore and Berlin

While *i10X* is headquartered in Singapore — one of Asia’s fastest‑growing AI and tech ecosystems — it also operates an office in Berlin, signaling early international ambitions. The team reports a growing user base — in the tens of thousands — and is focused on continually improving performance, reliability, and user experience. ([asiabusinessoutlook.com][1])

Alongside product development, the new capital will help *i10X* build out its underlying infrastructure and team — critical foundations for scaling in the highly competitive AI tooling space.

## 💡 Why Investors Are Paying Attention

The pre‑seed round was backed by **Iterative**, a Singapore‑based venture firm known for supporting ambitious tech startups across Southeast Asia, and **Antler AI Disrupt**, the AI‑focused arm of global VC Antler that has been aggressively backing early AI innovation. ([The SaaS News][2])

In recent years, Antler has emerged as one of the most active early‑stage investors in AI globally, deploying millions across startups in Southeast Asia and beyond — even as broader funding levels soften. ([antler.co][3]) Their involvement gives *i10X* not just capital but often access to mentorship, talent networks, and follow‑on opportunities.

## 🧩 What Comes Next

While *i10X* hasn’t disclosed enterprise customers, revenue figures, or compliance certifications yet — common early milestones for SaaS and platform companies — the startup is stacking early usage and user growth as key performance indicators. With a relatively affordable subscription model compared to multiple individual AI services, customer economics could become a competitive advantage in the long term. ([asiabusinessoutlook.com][1])

As AI tools proliferate and buyers demand simplicity, aggregators like *i10X* could find fertile ground — especially as businesses look to reduce tooling overhead and streamline workflows.

## 📘 Glossary

**AI Aggregator** — A platform that connects users to multiple AI tools or models from different providers, simplifying access and usage.

**Pre‑Seed Funding** — An early stage of startup investment used to validate ideas, build early products, and grow the initial team before larger seed or Series A rounds.

**AI Agent** — A software component powered by artificial intelligence that performs specific tasks, from answering questions to automating workflows.
