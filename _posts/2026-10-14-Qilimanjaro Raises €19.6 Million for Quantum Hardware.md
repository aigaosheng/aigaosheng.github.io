# Quantum Brief: Qilimanjaro Raises €19.6 Million for Quantum Hardware

**Today:** European quantum investment is increasingly tied to domestic hardware manufacturing and practical deployment, with new funding for Qilimanjaro's quantum processors and Infleqtion's quantum sensing technology.

## Top Stories

### 1. 📊 Qilimanjaro Raises €19.6 Million in Series A Funding

*Quantum Computing Report · October 14, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Qilimanjaro Quantum Tech has secured €19.6 million in the first close of its Series A round to scale quantum hardware manufacturing and commercial deployment.

The Spanish company received €10 million from Indra Group through its IndraMind unit and €9.6 million from Spain's Spanish Society for Technological Transformation (SETT). The funding will support chip fabrication, manufacturing and deployment of quantum computing systems built around fluxonium superconducting qubits. Qilimanjaro combines analog and digital quantum processing with classical high-performance computing infrastructure.

**Why it matters:** The financing illustrates how government-backed technology investment and defence-sector capital are supporting the industrialisation of quantum computing. For Asian technology companies and research institutions, the combination of quantum processors and conventional high-performance computing offers a potential partnership model for application development without requiring an entirely quantum-based computing environment.

### 2. 📊 Infleqtion Wins £9 Million for Quantum Sensing Deployment

*Quantum Computing Report · October 14, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Infleqtion has secured £9 million from Innovate UK to move its Rydberg-atom quantum radio-frequency sensing system towards operational deployment.

The award supports the second phase of the Quantum Direction Finding programme. Developed with defence and aerospace company Leonardo, the Quantum Spectrum-DF system uses atomic sensing and optical techniques to detect and locate radio signals, offering an alternative to conventional antenna arrays. Target applications include defence platforms, spectrum monitoring and infrastructure security.

**Why it matters:** Quantum technology's near-term commercial opportunities extend beyond general-purpose quantum computing to sensing, navigation and communications. For Asia-Pacific organisations, these applications warrant attention where conventional sensing systems face size, power or deployment constraints, although commercial readiness will depend on operational testing and integration.

## Key Takeaways

- **Hardware industrialisation is attracting strategic capital.** Qilimanjaro's funding links quantum processor manufacturing to national technology policy and defence-sector demand.
- **Quantum sensing offers a distinct commercial pathway.** Infleqtion's award supports deployment of a specialised technology rather than a general-purpose quantum computer.
- **Deployment remains the critical milestone.** Funding and technical progress are important, but enterprise adoption ultimately depends on measurable performance, reliability and integration with existing infrastructure.

## What to Watch

Watch for Qilimanjaro's next Series A closing and evidence of scaled hardware production, alongside Infleqtion's progress from prototype testing to operational quantum sensing. For Singapore and the wider Asia-Pacific region, relevant signals include local research partnerships, access to quantum hardware and demonstrations that establish practical advantages over conventional systems.
