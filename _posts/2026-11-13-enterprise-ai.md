# Enterprise AI Brief: Capgemini Finds AI Costs Exceeding Budgets

**Today:** Two independent research reports land the same message for enterprise and fintech leaders: AI deployment is outrunning cost control and data readiness, even as vendors keep shipping new agent platforms for banks, insurers and IT teams to adopt.

## Top Stories

### 1. 📊 **Capgemini Finds Nearly Six in Ten Firms Overspent on AI**

*Capgemini Research Institute · November 12, 2026*

**Bottom line:** A Capgemini survey of 500 senior executives found most organisations underestimated AI costs and lack the visibility to fix it.

Capgemini's AI Economics research found that close to 60% of executives saw significantly higher-than-budgeted AI spending over the past year, with overruns averaging 24%. Six in ten organisations still lack enterprise-wide visibility into AI costs, and only one in five calculate "AI Yield" — verified outcome value net of full delivery cost. More than 60% now plan to adopt multi-model strategies within 12 months to manage expense and risk.

**Why it matters:** For banks and fintechs under MAS and other regulators' scrutiny of AI spend and controls, the finding that CFO involvement in AI approvals jumped from 42% to 52% year-on-year signals that finance and compliance functions, not just technology teams, will increasingly own AI governance decisions.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.capgemini.com/insights/research-library/ai-economics/)

### 2. 🤖 **Fivetran Finds Data Fixes Eat a Quarter of AI Budgets**

*Fivetran · November 13, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Fivetran's survey of 500 IT and data leaders found 39% of firms lose at least a quarter of their AI budget to avoidable data-quality work.

The "AI tax" report, covering the US, Europe and Asia-Pacific, found 37% of Asia-Pacific firms lose at least a quarter of AI spend to data fixes, versus 45% in the US and 34% in EMEA. Firms relying on custom data integrations lose budget at higher rates than those on standardised platforms, and heavy losers are five times more likely to have AI projects stall.

**Why it matters:** The report reinforces Capgemini's findings from a different angle — enterprises in Singapore and the region that skip investment in data foundations risk the same stalled agentic AI rollouts regulators and boards are now asking banks to account for.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.comparethecloud.net/news/nearly-four-in-10-firms-lose-a-quarter-of-ai-spend-to-data-fixes-fivetran-finds)

### 3. 🌐 **Cohere Deepens Enterprise Push Into Japan**

*PR Newswire · November 13, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Cohere plans to set up a Japanese legal entity and expand sovereign AI infrastructure as local enterprises move from pilots to regulated, mission-critical deployments.

The company is expanding its Model Vault in-region inference platform so data and responses stay within Japan, continuing its Takane large language model partnership with Fujitsu, which was selected for Japan's Digital Agency GENAI evaluation programme. Cohere is also launching training and certification programmes for local partners.

**Why it matters:** Cohere's sovereign-AI bet mirrors a wider pattern across Asia, where regulators and enterprises increasingly tie AI vendor selection to data residency — a consideration Singapore and Indonesian financial institutions are weighing as they scale their own agentic AI deployments.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.prnewswire.com/in/news-releases/cohere-deepens-commitment-to-japan-with-sovereign-ai-investments-and-growing-enterprise-adoption-302937096.html)

### 4. 🔒 **Expedient Launches Unified AI Governance Platform**

*PR Newswire · November 12, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Expedient's next-generation AI CTRL Platform puts a single control plane between enterprise users and every AI model, system and agent they use.

The platform combines governed multi-model chat, retrieval-augmented knowledge grounded in company data, and an agentic workflow engine, with centralised admin controls over model access, spending caps and audit-ready request logs. It runs on Expedient-owned infrastructure with SOC 2 compliance and HIPAA-eligible models, aimed at enterprises still managing AI tools separately.

**Why it matters:** It is the latest example of vendors packaging cost and governance controls as a single product, directly addressing the budget-visibility gaps Capgemini and Fivetran quantified today.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/expedient-expands-next-generation-ai-ctrl-platform-to-unify-enterprise-ai-governance-across-models-data-and-agents-302937156.html)

### 5. 💳 **Insight Launches Global AI Agent Store**

*Business Wire via CIOInfluence · November 12, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Insight Enterprises opened a global online Agent Store offering a catalogue of 15 pre-built AI agents, aiming to move clients from pilots to production in days rather than months.

New additions include Quote Advisor, an NDA Review Agent and Parts Order Assist, alongside its earlier Flight Academy training tool. The store is live across 14 countries in North America, Asia-Pacific and EMEA, with all agents client-tested or proven internally before launch.

**Why it matters:** Pre-packaged agent catalogues lower the technical bar for mid-sized financial and corporate customers to adopt agentic AI without building in-house, accelerating the kind of broad deployment Genpact's research this week found most enterprises have not yet achieved.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://cioinfluence.com/machine-learning/insight-launches-agent-store-to-accelerate-enterprise-ai-adoption/)

### 6. 🤖 **RightRev Launches AI Agents for Revenue Recognition**

*PR Newswire · November 12, 2026*

**Bottom line:** RightRev introduced RightAgents, a suite of AI agents built to automate revenue recognition and keep audit trails for finance teams running variable-pricing models.

The agents handle contract term extraction, reconciliation checks, anomaly flagging and period-to-period variance explanations, while finance staff retain approval authority. RightRev, which processes $44 billion in annual customer revenue for clients, made the agents accessible via Model Context Protocol inside tools like Claude and ChatGPT.

**Why it matters:** It is a concrete example of narrow, audit-ready agentic AI reaching office-of-the-CFO workflows — the kind of bounded, reviewable deployment regulators have signalled they prefer over open-ended autonomous agents.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/rightrev-sets-the-vision-for-next-gen-finance-launching-ai-agents-that-keep-revenue-right-around-the-clock-302938035.html)

## Key Takeaways

- AI cost overruns and data-quality drag, not model capability, are now the dominant constraint on enterprise AI scaling, according to two independent surveys published within a day of each other.
- Vendors are responding by bundling governance, cost control and pre-built agents into single platforms (Expedient, Insight) rather than leaving enterprises to assemble point solutions.
- Sovereign and in-region AI infrastructure, illustrated by Cohere's Japan expansion, is becoming a competitive differentiator as regulated industries in Asia weigh data residency alongside capability.

## What to Watch

Watch whether more enterprises begin reporting an "AI Yield" or equivalent outcome-based metric in upcoming quarterly updates, and whether CFO sign-off requirements for AI spending — already up sharply per Capgemini — show further increases in financial-services disclosures.
