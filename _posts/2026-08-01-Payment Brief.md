### 3. **BSP Clarifies Digital Bank Transfers Are Not Required to Be Free**

* **Source**: Yugatech · 2026-08-01

* **Why It Matters**: The clarification provides regulatory certainty while encouraging more transparent pricing in the Philippine digital payments ecosystem.
