# Singapore’s Dyna.Ai Secures Eight-Figure Series A to Scale Enterprise-Grade AI Globally

Singapore’s AI startup **Dyna.Ai** has closed an undisclosed **eight-figure Series A funding round**, signaling a leap forward in the maturation of enterprise artificial intelligence (AI) in Asia Pacific and beyond. The round was led by Singapore-based venture capital firm *Lion X Ventures*, advised by OCBC Bank’s mezzanine capital unit, with participation from Taiwan’s ADATA, a Korean financial institution, and a group of seasoned finance veterans. ([CFOtech Asia][1])

## From Pilots to Production: The New Era of Enterprise AI

Dyna.Ai was founded in 2024 with a clear mission: help large organisations overcome the common barrier of moving AI systems from experimental proof-of-concepts into fully operational, revenue-generating deployments. The company’s software focuses on “agentic AI” — AI systems that act autonomously within structured workflows to complete discrete business tasks with compliance, controls, and governance baked in. ([CFOtech Asia][1])

This funding infusion will help accelerate the rollout of Dyna.Ai’s portfolio of products — including AI agent builders, task-ready agents, and workflow-embedded applications — into regulated industries such as banking, financial services, and insurance where operational rigor is critical. The company already counts deployments across global and regional banks and enterprises spanning Asia, the Middle East, and the Americas. ([TNGlobal][2])

## Why This Matters: Measurable Results Over Hype

A key theme of the raise is the shift in enterprise AI sentiment. Investors and business leaders alike are increasingly prioritising **measurable business outcomes** — such as revenue uplift, efficiency gains, and workflow automation — over broad experimentation with AI capabilities.

That’s where Dyna.Ai positions itself distinctively: its “Results-as-a-Service” approach aligns AI investments directly with business KPIs, enabling enterprises to confidently scale beyond test beds and into mission-critical operations. ([CFOtech Asia][1])

Dyna.Ai’s leadership emphasises that while many AI tools promise broad potential, enterprises are demanding solutions that fit existing regulatory, operational, and governance frameworks — especially in financial services and other heavily regulated sectors. ([CFOtech Asia][1])

## Singapore’s AI Momentum

Dyna.Ai’s funding news comes amid a broader surge in AI investment and adoption in Singapore and Southeast Asia. Government initiatives, strategic fintech festivals, and a vibrant startup ecosystem are drawing capital and talent to the region while fostering responsible innovation that meets regulatory requirements. Singapore has pledged more than **S$1 billion** (around US $778.8 million) in public AI research funding over the coming years — underscoring the city-state’s role as a regional AI hub. ([TNGlobal][2])

## Glossary

* **Series A Funding** — An early stage of venture capital investment that helps startups scale their products, business models, and teams after initial validation.
* **Agentic AI** — AI systems designed not only to generate outputs but to execute tasks autonomously within defined business processes.
* **Results-as-a-Service** — A commercial model where technology is sold based on measurable business outcomes rather than just usage or access.
* **Regulated Industries** — Sectors such as finance, insurance, and healthcare that require strict adherence to compliance, governance, and operational rules.

## Final Thoughts

Dyna.Ai’s Series A round underscores a pivotal phase in enterprise AI: leaving the realm of pilots and prototypes behind, and prioritising **scalable, operational, and outcome-focused deployments**. As companies seek real ROI from AI, solutions that blend advanced autonomy with governance, compliance, and measurable value will likely lead the next wave of transformation.
