# Quantum Brief: PostQat Sets Aviation PQC Roadmap

**Today:** A European aviation research project is mapping how long-lived communications systems can move toward post-quantum protection.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🔒 **PostQat Maps Quantum-Safe Aviation Communications**

*Fraunhofer AISEC · November 23, 2026*

**Bottom line:** The PostQat project is developing post-quantum cryptography guidance and a migration roadmap for Europe’s air traffic management systems.

The project is assessing vulnerabilities in existing infrastructure and plans to publish technical blueprints for quantum-resistant encryption, secure communications and digital identity management. Researchers are evaluating ML-KEM, ML-DSA and SLH-DSA, including hybrid approaches, and are working on cryptographic agility so algorithms can be replaced over the long lifecycles of avionics systems. The consortium, coordinated by Delft University of Technology, runs through February 2029 with approximately €1 million in EU funding.

**Why it matters:** Long-lived aviation systems need a migration path that accommodates changing cryptographic standards without requiring full system recertification. For Asia-Pacific operators and financial institutions with similarly long-lived infrastructure, the project’s planned open guidance may offer a reference point; its applicability will depend on local standards and implementation requirements.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.aisec.fraunhofer.de/en/media/press-releases/2026/postqat-makes-europes-air-traffic-management-quantum-secure.html)

## Key Takeaways

- PostQat combines technical vulnerability analysis with a practical transition roadmap for aviation systems.
- The project is evaluating both standardized post-quantum algorithms and hybrid cryptographic approaches.
- Crypto-agility is a central design concern for infrastructure that must remain supportable over decades.

## What to Watch

Monitor PostQat’s forthcoming technical blueprints and roadmap, as well as its contributions to aviation standards bodies including EUROCAE, ICAO and ETSI.
