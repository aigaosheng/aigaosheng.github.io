## Executive Takeaways

* AI governance is rapidly becoming a strategic investment area as regulators tighten oversight of AI deployed in financial services.
* Financial institutions continue prioritizing AI for AML, fraud detection, compliance automation, and operational efficiency rather than purely customer-facing applications.
* Venture capital remains concentrated in AI-native fintech infrastructure, while investors are increasingly scrutinizing the commercial returns of large-scale AI investments.
* Cross-border payments, trade finance, and SME financing continue emerging as high-impact domains for AI-driven innovation.
