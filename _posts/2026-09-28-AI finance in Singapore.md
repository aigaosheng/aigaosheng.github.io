# AI finance in Singapore Brief — 2026-09-28

**Today:** Singapore's financial ecosystem is connecting AI-driven risk management with digital banking, embedded finance and interoperable cross-border infrastructure.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **AI-driven risk models emerge as a key component of ASEAN's next-generation financial infrastructure**

*French Chamber of Commerce in Singapore · 28 September 2026*

**Bottom line:** Finance leaders discussing ASEAN's next-generation financial system identified AI-driven risk models, digital banking and embedded finance as practical areas of transformation.

The French Chamber of Commerce in Singapore and the Franco-Thai Chamber of Commerce reported on their recent “Next-Gen Finance: The ASEAN Innovation Wave” webinar, bringing together corporate treasurers, fintech leaders and financial executives. The discussion covered AI-driven risk models, digital banking, embedded finance, cross-border payment linkages and real-time network interoperability. ([FCC Singapore][1])

**Why it matters:** The combination of AI risk capabilities with interoperable payment infrastructure points toward a financial model in which intelligence and connectivity increasingly operate together across ASEAN markets.

### 2. 🤖 **Singapore AI festival puts an AI finance “teammate” in the spotlight**

*Singapore AI Association · 28 September 2026*

**Bottom line:** Singapore's AI ecosystem is showcasing AI finance employees and agentic workflows as practical applications for SME finance operations.

The Singapore AI Association's AI Festival on 28 September includes a dedicated session titled “The AI Finance Teammate,” focused on working alongside an AI finance digital employee for SME finance. The wider programme also covers AI agents, sovereign AI infrastructure and practical enterprise adoption. ([SAIA A.I. Festival 2026][2])

**Why it matters:** The focus on an AI finance “teammate” reflects the industry's movement from generic chatbots toward domain-specific AI systems that can support recurring finance workflows and operational decisions.

### 3. 🏦 **Singapore finance ecosystem focuses on practical AI adoption and workforce readiness**

*Accenture / Discover Singapore · 28 September 2026*

**Bottom line:** A Singapore AI workforce event is bringing implementation experience from banking and other sectors into discussions about how organisations can operationalise AI.

Accenture's Singapore programme on 28 September focuses on real-world AI implementation and how organisations are changing their operations as AI adoption accelerates. The programme specifically identifies banking among the sectors represented by Accenture's Singapore practice. ([Discover NYC][3])

**Why it matters:** For financial institutions, the next stage of AI adoption depends not only on models and infrastructure but also on organisational capability, implementation experience and workforce adaptation.

### 4. 🤖 **UOB FinLab programme advances applied generative and agentic AI for business workflows**

*UOB FinLab · 28 September 2026*

**Bottom line:** UOB FinLab is training Singapore-based professionals to identify workflows suitable for generative and agentic AI and turn them into validated business solutions.

The two-day GROW programme begins on 28 September and covers generative AI, agentic AI, workflow analysis, AI-enabled automation, prototyping and validation of AI-driven solutions. The programme is hosted at Ngee Ann Polytechnic in Singapore. ([thefinlab.com][4])

**Why it matters:** For financial institutions and SMEs, workflow-level AI adoption is increasingly about redesigning processes around agents rather than simply adding AI features to existing software.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://thefinlab.com/events/gain-real-outcomes-for-work/)
