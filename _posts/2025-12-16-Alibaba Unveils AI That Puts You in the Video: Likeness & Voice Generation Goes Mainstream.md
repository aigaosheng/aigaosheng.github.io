**Alibaba Unveils AI That Puts *You* in the Video: Likeness & Voice Generation Goes Mainstream**

In a bold leap toward next-generation content creation, Alibaba has rolled out a new suite of generative AI models capable of producing fully lifelike videos using *a person’s own likeness and voice*. The move pushes China’s AI capabilities further into territory once dominated by Western tech — and raises fresh questions about creativity, identity, and the future of digital media. ([Tech in Asia][1])

At the heart of Alibaba’s announcement is **Wan2.6-R2V**, the first model in China designed to blend visual likeness and vocal replication in AI-generated video. This means users can upload a photo and voice sample and have the system create dynamic, multi-shot video content starring *their own digital avatar*. Developers say the capability opens doors for advertising, entertainment, virtual avatars, and other immersive digital experiences that feel distinctly personal. ([Tech in Asia][1])

Unlike previous tools that generated static images or generic clips, the latest offering focuses on *consistent identity fidelity*. That’s a big deal for creative professionals and social media creators who want to produce content that looks and sounds like real people — even when entirely synthesized. ([Tech in Asia][1])

This announcement continues a broader trend at Alibaba and across China’s tech sector: massive investment in AI models that generate audio, visuals, and integrated multimodal content. Over the past year, Alibaba Cloud has open-sourced a series of high-performance models — from **Wan2.1** to **Wan2.2-S2V** — that can turn images and audio into expressive, film-quality videos, and continue to be shared on platforms like Hugging Face and GitHub. ([Alibaba Cloud][2])

**Why this matters:** Generative AI with realistic likeness and voice has broad implications — from *revolutionizing content creation workflows* to *raising ethical concerns about authenticity and consent*. As these tools proliferate, creators and regulators alike will grapple with how to balance innovation with responsibility. ([Tech in Asia][1])

## **Glossary: Key Terms Explained**

**Generative AI**
A type of artificial intelligence that creates new content — whether text, images, audio, or video — based on patterns learned from data.

**Likeness Replication**
The ability of a model to generate visual representations that resemble a specific individual’s appearance.

**Voice Synthesis / Voice Cloning**
AI techniques that generate an audio voice matching a person’s unique speaking style, tone, and inflection.

**Multimodal Model**
An AI model that can process and generate multiple types of data — e.g., text *and* images *and* audio *and* video.

**Open Source**
Software whose code is publicly available to use, modify, and distribute freely; Alibaba has frequently open-sourced its models to drive innovation. ([Alibaba Cloud][2])
