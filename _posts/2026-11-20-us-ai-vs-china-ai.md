# US AI vs China AI Brief: US Chip Curbs Threaten Alibaba Cloud Plans

**Today:** New US limits on remote access to advanced chips are putting pressure on Chinese AI training and cloud expansion, while reinforcing incentives to build domestic compute capacity.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **US Chip Curbs Threaten Alibaba Cloud Plans**

*Associated Press · November 19, 2026*

**Bottom line:** New US restrictions on remote access to advanced chips could constrain Alibaba’s overseas data centre plans and slow Tencent’s next-generation model work.

Alibaba said it was assessing how the rules could affect some global data centre expansion plans. Tencent said the restrictions would likely affect the training timelines for its next AI models, while Nvidia said it had stopped shipping some chips, including the H200, to China.

**Why it matters:** The rules reach beyond physical chip exports to compute accessed through data centres in third countries. For Asian cloud and infrastructure operators, that raises the importance of assessing licensing exposure and customer access controls; the pressure may also accelerate Chinese firms’ shift toward domestic chips.

## Key Takeaways

- US-China AI competition is increasingly shaped by access to compute through overseas data centres, as well as direct chip exports.
- Alibaba’s expansion plans and Tencent’s model development illustrate how restrictions can affect both infrastructure investment and frontier training.
- Tighter access may strengthen China’s push to develop domestic chips and supply chains, though the pace and capacity of that transition remain uncertain.

## What to Watch

Watch for updates from Alibaba and Tencent on the operational effects of the restrictions, and for signs that Chinese firms are shifting more AI workloads to domestic chips.
