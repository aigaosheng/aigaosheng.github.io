### 1. China Launches First Homegrown 100,000-Card AI Supercluster "Dawning 8000" - **Source**: CCTV · 2026-07-10
- **Summary**: China's first domestically produced 100,000-card AI supercluster, Dawning 8000 (Dengfeng), was officially launched in Zhengzhou, Henan province, and connected to the national supercomputing internet. The system features end-to-end homegrown infrastructure from chips to applications and has already completed optimization for over 300 applications across 20+ fields.
- **Why It Matters**: This milestone marks China's transition from 10,000-card to 100,000-card AI infrastructure, establishing a new baseline for next-generation AI capabilities. It demonstrates China's ability to build world-class AI infrastructure without relying on foreign technology.
