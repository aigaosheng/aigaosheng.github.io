### **AI adoption rises in wealth management, but execution gap persists**

*Source: FinTech Global | Apr 17, 2026*

**Why It Matters:**
The gap between intent and execution is creating a competitive advantage for AI-native fintechs and digitally mature firms.

### **Bank of England tests AI-related systemic risks in finance**

*Source: Reuters | Apr 17, 2026*
The Bank of England is conducting scenario testing to assess risks posed by AI in financial markets, including synchronized trading behaviors and cyber vulnerabilities. While current risks are limited, regulators warn of rapid scaling effects.

**Why It Matters:**
Regulators are proactively stress-testing AI risks, indicating a shift toward stricter oversight and governance requirements for AI in finance.
