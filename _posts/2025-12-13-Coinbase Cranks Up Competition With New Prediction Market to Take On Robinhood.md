**Coinbase Cranks Up Competition With New Prediction Market to Take On Robinhood**

Coinbase is gearing up to launch its own **prediction markets platform**, signalling a major strategic shift as the crypto exchange pushes beyond traditional digital assets and squarely into new territory shared by rivals like **Robinhood** and others. This move comes amid a cooling crypto market and reflects Coinbase’s broader ambition to become a **one-stop “everything” financial app** that blends crypto, real-world assets, and event-based trading. ([Investing.com India][1])

The news first broke via *Tech in Asia* reporting that Coinbase plans to unveil its prediction markets product, which could put it in direct competition with platforms such as Robinhood — already active in the space — as well as other players like Gemini, Kraken, and Polymarket. ([Startup News][2])

### **Why It Matters**

Prediction markets let users trade contracts that pay out based on real-world event outcomes — from elections and economic data to sporting results. They’re increasingly seen as **financial tools for gauging collective expectations**, and have drawn interest from both retail and institutional players. Coinbase’s entry signals that this niche is rapidly moving toward the mainstream. ([AInvest][3])

According to reports, Coinbase’s offering is expected to be powered in part through a partnership with **Kalshi**, a U.S.-regulated prediction platform. The official reveal is anticipated at Coinbase’s *“Coinbase System Update”* event on **December 17, 2025** — where the company may also introduce other products like **tokenized stocks**. ([Investing.com India][1])

### **Competitive Context**

Robinhood isn’t sitting still — its own prediction market arm has become one of the company’s fastest-growing products this year, with strong trading participation from retail investors. Coinbase’s entry raises the stakes in a market where **trading volume and user engagement have grown significantly**, making prediction markets an increasingly contested turf in fintech. ([The Block][4])

### **What to Watch Next**

* **Regulatory landscape** — Prediction markets operate under stringent oversight (e.g., by the U.S. CFTC), and industry firms are working together to advocate for clearer rules. ([FinanceFeeds][5])
* **User adoption** — Whether Coinbase can attract users away from incumbents like Robinhood and specialized platforms such as Polymarket.
* **Product experience** — How Coinbase tailors its markets — including fee structure, supported events, and integration with its broader ecosystem.

---

## 🔑 Glossary

**Prediction Markets** — Trading platforms where users buy and sell contracts that pay out if a particular real-world event (e.g., election results, economic stats) occurs. Payout values typically reflect the market’s aggregated probability of an event happening.

**Tokenized Stocks** — Blockchain-based representations of traditional equities that mimic the price movement of a real stock but can be traded on crypto platforms.

**Kalshi** — A U.S.-based, federally regulated prediction market operator that provides compliant event-based trading infrastructure.
