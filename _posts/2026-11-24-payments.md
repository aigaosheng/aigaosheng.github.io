# Payments Brief: South Korea Proposes Bank-Led Won Stablecoins

**Today:** South Korea’s proposed stablecoin framework and new business payment products show how rules, treasury operations and card distribution are shaping digital payments.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🏦 **South Korea Proposes Bank-Led Won Stablecoins**

*CHOSUNBIZ · November 24, 2026*

**Bottom line:** South Korea’s Financial Services Commission (FSC) has proposed that banks hold a collective majority in consortia issuing won stablecoins.

The FSC’s proposed framework would require issuers to obtain authorisation, maintain at least ₩5 billion in capital and back tokens with reserves equal to their outstanding issuance. It would also allow qualifying foreign issuers to enter the Korean market through a local branch and FSC registration. The proposal is part of planned legislation, not an enacted law.

**Why it matters:** The proposal sets a potentially influential model for bank participation and non-bank roles in stablecoin issuance. For Asian banks and payment firms, reserve, redemption and governance requirements will shape consortium structures and the scope for fintech-led distribution.

### 2. 💳 **Finmo Adds USDC And USDT To Business Accounts**

*PR Newswire · November 24, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Finmo says eligible businesses can hold, receive and pay USDC and USDT through the same business account used for fiat currencies.

Stablecoin balances appear alongside connected bank accounts in Finmo Treasury, with shared permissions and approvals. Customers can convert between stablecoins and AUD, USD, EUR and GBP. Finmo says the service is available in supported markets, but it is not available to customers in Singapore.

**Why it matters:** Bringing stablecoin balances into familiar treasury controls may make them easier for finance teams to oversee and reconcile. Singapore-based businesses will need to check eligibility and availability before treating the service as a local option.

### 3. 🌐 **Worldline Repositions Global Collect For Cross-Border Commerce**

*Worldline · November 24, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Worldline has relaunched Global Collect as a dedicated brand for cross-border payments in travel and digital commerce.

Worldline says the platform processes more than €23 billion in annual cross-border payment volume for over 600 major brands and offers access to more than 150 payment methods across Asia-Pacific and other regions. The company says its technology has been modernised and integrated into Worldline, with the rebrand intended to give the business greater visibility.

**Why it matters:** The move highlights the value payment providers place on combining global acquiring with sector expertise and local payment-method coverage. For merchants expanding across Asia-Pacific, the scale claims are relevant, but the practical test will be platform performance and local-market support.

### 4. 💳 **Deel Adds Stablecoin Spending With Visa Card**

*Deel · November 24, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Deel has launched a Visa card that lets eligible contractors spend their DLUSD balance through the Deel platform.

The company says the card converts DLUSD at purchase so merchants receive local currency, and supports virtual cards through Apple Pay and Google Pay. Deel reports that users had held more than $45 million in DLUSD since the wallet launched in June. The card is initially available in 26 markets across Latin America, the Caribbean and Africa; physical cards are expected in the coming weeks.

**Why it matters:** The launch links contractor payouts, stablecoin balances and everyday card spending within one platform. Its initial market footprint does not include Asia, so regional relevance will depend on whether Deel expands availability.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.deel.com/blog/introducing-new-deel-card/)

### 5. 🤖 **NEOPAY Adds ACI’s AI Fraud Management**

*ACI Worldwide · November 24, 2026*

**Bottom line:** UAE acquirer NEOPAY will extend its ACI acquiring services and deploy ACI’s AI-powered Fraud Management solution.

The expanded relationship covers NEOPAY’s point-of-sale and e-commerce acquiring. ACI says its fraud system combines machine learning, behavioural analytics, risk scoring and real-time decisions; implementation is the immediate focus of the expansion.

**Why it matters:** The deployment pairs merchant acquiring with automated fraud screening as NEOPAY grows across retail, e-commerce, hospitality and public-sector clients. Payment providers in the Middle East will be watching how the system performs against fraud losses and customer friction once deployed.

### 6. 💳 **Clapp Launches Stablecoin-Linked Visa Card**

*PR Newswire · November 24, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Clapp has launched a Visa card that can draw directly on USDT and USDC balances and can be linked to its crypto-backed credit lines.

Clapp says card purchases convert stablecoins to local currency at checkout and that it charges no fee for crypto-to-fiat conversion, although network and processor fees may apply. Users can also borrow stablecoins against crypto collateral and spend them without selling that collateral.

**Why it matters:** The product combines card acceptance with access to secured digital-asset credit. For payments providers, the model broadens stablecoin spending options while tying card use to the availability and risk controls of collateralised borrowing.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/clapp-finance-launches-crypto-card-with-0-transaction-fees-and-credit-line-spendings-302948473.html)

### 7. 💳 **Klarna Expands Visa Acceptance Platform Access**

*Business Wire · November 23, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Merchants using Visa Acceptance Platform can now offer Klarna at checkout through their existing setup in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and Europe.

Klarna and Visa said merchants can provide pay-in-full, interest-free instalment and longer-term financing options without building a separate integration. The announcement was published at 8:30 a.m. Eastern Time on November 23, or 9:30 p.m. Singapore time, after the stated cutoff.

**Why it matters:** Embedding an alternative payment method in an existing acceptance platform can extend merchant reach without requiring a separate integration project. The expansion shows how distribution through payment infrastructure can help payment providers add checkout options across multiple markets.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261123561809/en/)

## Key Takeaways

- Stablecoin policy and payment products are converging around clear rules for issuance, reserve management and redemption.
- Treasury workflows and card-linked spending are bringing stablecoins closer to routine business and consumer payment activity.
- Payment providers are extending reach through platform distribution, while using AI fraud tools to support merchant growth.

## What to Watch

Watch for South Korea’s legislative next steps and how banks and fintechs respond to the proposed issuance framework. For new business and card products, availability by market, merchant acceptance and operating performance will show where stablecoin payment use is gaining traction.
