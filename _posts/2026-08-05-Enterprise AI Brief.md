# Key Takeaway

Enterprise AI is entering a deployment-focused phase where competitive advantage is shifting away from simply owning powerful models. The winners are likely to be organizations that can integrate AI into workflows, manage data effectively, govern autonomous systems, and measure business impact at scale.
