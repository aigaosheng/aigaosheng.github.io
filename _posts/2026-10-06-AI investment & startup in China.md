# AI investment & startup in China Brief — 2026-10-06

**Today:** China’s leading AI startups are attracting unprecedented late-stage capital, with DeepSeek and Moonshot AI moving toward major 2027 IPOs.

## Top Stories

### 1. 📊 **DeepSeek set to raise at least $12 billion in Tencent- and CATL-backed funding**

*Reuters · October 6, 2026*

**Bottom line:** DeepSeek is close to securing at least 80 billion yuan ($11.93 billion) in new funding, potentially reaching 100 billion yuan, as it builds capital ahead of a possible domestic IPO.

The round has already exceeded DeepSeek’s original 50 billion yuan target, with Tencent and CATL among the largest committed investors. The company is reportedly targeting a valuation of about 500 billion yuan ($74 billion), while the fundraising is expected to conclude this month.

**Why it matters:** The scale of the financing signals that Chinese investors are increasingly treating frontier AI as strategic infrastructure rather than a conventional software category. It also gives DeepSeek substantial resources for compute, model development and a potential Shanghai STAR Market listing while reinforcing the role of domestic capital in China’s effort to reduce dependence on Nvidia’s AI ecosystem.

### 2. 📊 **Moonshot AI completes final private funding round at roughly $50 billion valuation**

*The Business Times · October 6, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Moonshot AI has reportedly completed its final private fundraising at a valuation of about $50 billion and is preparing for a Hong Kong IPO targeted for the first quarter of 2027.

The Kimi developer is expected to begin early investor meetings in October as it gauges demand for a potential offering that could raise as much as $5 billion. The company’s rapid ascent has been driven by Kimi K3, strengthening its position among China’s most closely watched foundation-model startups.

**Why it matters:** Moonshot’s transition from repeated private fundraising to a large public-market debut illustrates how quickly China’s leading AI companies are moving toward monetization and institutional ownership. Alongside DeepSeek, it suggests that investors increasingly see frontier AI companies as potential public-market leaders rather than long-duration venture bets.
