# AI Investment & Startup in China Brief: INFevo Raises Record Virtual-Cell Pre-A Round

**Today:** Only one qualifying China AI funding story could be verified after the cutoff, a sign that capital is still reaching specialised AI-for-science startups.

## Top Stories

### 1. 📊 **INFevo Raises Over RMB 100 Million Pre-A for AI Biology Platform**

*Sina Finance · November 16, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Virtual-cell startup INFevo (无界进化) closed a Pre-A round it describes as a record single financing for a Chinese company in its field, five months after its angel round.

According to Sina Finance, the round exceeds RMB 100 million, though the exact amount and valuation were not disclosed. New investors include Xinglian Capital, Prosperity7, Huagai Capital, Huakong Fund and Detong Capital. Existing backers Shunwei Capital, Sequoia China and Songhe Capital added further funds. The company, incubated within XtalPi, plans to use the money to speed up AI-for-science infrastructure and to build a platform covering target discovery, virtual screening and clinical protocol optimisation. The record claim comes from the company's announcement and is not independently verified.

**Why it matters:** Follow-on money from existing investors and a second round within five months suggest continued appetite for AI-for-science startups in China. For Singapore and Asia-based pharma and enterprise AI buyers, it signals that Chinese biology foundation-model platforms are maturing as potential partners and competitors.

## Key Takeaways

- Funding is flowing to vertical AI niches such as virtual cells, not only to general-purpose model developers.
- Existing investors topping up quickly after an angel round points to conviction in specialised AI-for-science platforms.
- Undisclosed round sizes and valuations remain common, so company-reported records should be treated cautiously.

## What to Watch

Watch for INFevo disclosing the exact round size or valuation, and for further China AI-for-science rounds in the coming weeks. Also track whether large pending Hong Kong and STAR Market AI listings advance.
