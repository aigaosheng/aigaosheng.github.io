## The End of Traditional Web Search? How Nimble’s Agentic Platform Is Powering AI’s Next Frontier

In a dramatic signal of how artificial intelligence is transforming the internet, Israeli-founded startup **Nimble** has unveiled its *Agentic Search Platform*—a system designed not just to fetch information, but to *act like a digital researcher* on behalf of enterprise AI systems. Backed by a fresh **$47 million Series B fundraising round led by Norwest and Databricks Ventures**, this launch could mark the beginning of a new paradigm where machines, not humans, are the primary users of the web. ([MLQ][1])

### From Humans to Machines: Why This Matters

For decades, web search has been built around humans typing keywords and clicking links. But AI is changing that model. Large language models (LLMs)—such as those used in generative AI assistants—rely on external information to answer questions, yet struggle when the data they access is incomplete, unstructured, or unverifiable. Nimble’s platform aims to fix that by giving AI *real-time, accurate, and structured web data* that can be directly consumed by business workflows. ([MLQ][1])

Rather than pulling a simple list of links or a high-level summary, Nimble’s agents can interact with live websites just like a human researcher: navigating pages, handling dynamic content, and aggregating information into structured tables. This isn’t scraping static APIs or preindexed results—it’s *browser-level exploration* guided by AI models. ([The Outpost][2])

### How Agentic Search Works

At its core, the platform uses coordinated AI “agents”—software entities empowered to search, verify, and structure data from the live web. Here’s a simplified look at how it differs from traditional approaches:

* **Real Browser Navigation:** Agents control full web browsers, enabling access to dynamic and interactive websites that traditional scrapers struggle with. ([The Outpost][2])
* **Data Cleaning & Structuring:** After retrieving raw content, the platform applies governance processes—cleaning, deduplication, aggregation— turning scattered bits into structured, queryable datasets. ([MLQ][1])
* **High Accuracy:** Nimble claims over **99 % accuracy**, a critical metric for enterprise decision-making where unreliable data can cost millions. ([MLQ][1])
* **No-Code and Developer Access:** Business teams can build automated search workflows without coding, while developers can use SDKs and APIs to integrate capabilities into existing systems. ([The Outpost][2])

This approach allows AI systems to confidently reason about sources, compare facts, and build deeper insights because the data isn’t just found—it’s verified and structured.

### Enterprise Adoption and Use Cases

Nimble’s platform is already catching on in sectors that depend on timely, accurate data. Companies across financial services, consulting, retail analytics, and competitive intelligence are tapping into agentic search for:

* **Real-Time Pricing and Market Analysis:** Monitoring competitor movements or price changes minute-by-minute. ([Israel.com][3])
* **Risk and Compliance Workflows:** Pulling verified evidence from public sources for due diligence or regulatory checks. ([MLQ][1])
* **AI-Driven Decision Systems:** Feeding structured external data into machine learning models or business analytics stacks. ([DayTraders][4])

By streamlining discovery and external fact-gathering, Nimble positions itself as a critical layer of the modern AI stack—one where the accuracy of information is as important as the intelligence built on top of it.

### What This Means for Web Search and AI

Nimble’s launch signals a broader trend in which *agentic systems*—AI agents designed to act autonomously—are reshaping how we get and use information. Traditional search engines may remain vital for human users, but behind the scenes, machines will increasingly demand data in formats they can *consume directly*, free of ambiguity and noise.

As companies build more autonomous AI workflows—whether for forecasting, research, or operational automation—the infrastructure that supplies accurate external data becomes a fundamental bottleneck. Nimble’s approach of combining autonomous browsing, validation, and structured data delivery aims squarely at this challenge. ([MLQ][1])

## Glossary

**Agentic AI:** AI systems that can perform tasks autonomously—taking actions, making decisions, and gathering data with limited human oversight.
**Structured Data:** Information organized in a consistent format (e.g., tables) that machines can easily query or integrate.
**Browser Automation:** The use of software to simulate human interaction with web content, including navigating pages and filling forms.
**Series B Funding:** A funding round that helps startups scale products and enter broader markets after initial product-market fit.
