# RWA Brief: Victory Park Capital Launches Tokenized Private Credit Fund

**Today:** Institutional tokenization is moving further into private credit and traditional banking infrastructure, with new products highlighting the importance of investor protections, liquidity design and connections to established financial markets.

## Top Stories

### 1. 📊 **Victory Park Capital and Tradable Launch Tokenized Private Credit Fund**

*Business Wire · October 15, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Victory Park Capital (VPC), an affiliate of Janus Henderson, has launched a tokenized feeder fund providing access to an existing asset-backed private-credit strategy.

The AltsABF Tokenized Feeder Fund (VALTS) provides tokenized exposure to the Privacore VPC Asset Backed Credit Fund. VPC partnered with private-asset marketplace and tokenization platform Tradable, with the fund initially available on the Stellar blockchain. The structure extends blockchain-based access to an underlying investment fund rather than directly tokenizing each individual loan.

**Why it matters:** Tokenization is expanding beyond government securities and money-market products into private credit. For Asian wealth platforms and institutional distributors, the opportunity is to improve investment distribution and administration while retaining rigorous controls over investor eligibility, underlying assets, valuation and redemption terms.

### 2. 🌐 **Deutsche Bank Plans Digital-Asset Expansion Into Asia**

*Reuters · October 16, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Deutsche Bank plans to enter digital-asset custody and subsequently develop services involving tokenized deposits and blockchain-based securities issuance.

The bank expects to launch its initial digital-asset service in Germany before expanding into key Asian financial centres in 2027. Its longer-term plans include tokenized deposits and digital bonds, alongside custody and other digital-asset services. These plans indicate a gradual extension from digital-asset safekeeping toward broader institutional financial infrastructure.

**Why it matters:** Asian financial institutions should watch how established banks connect custody, issuance and settlement rather than treating tokenization as an isolated technology project. For Singapore, the commercial opportunity will depend on interoperability, legal certainty, operational resilience and access to institutional counterparties.

## Key Takeaways

- **Private-credit tokenization is broadening the asset mix.** The VALTS launch illustrates how blockchain infrastructure can distribute exposure to traditional alternative-investment strategies.
- **Institutional adoption requires more than token issuance.** Custody, valuation, investor eligibility, redemption arrangements and reliable settlement remain central to making tokenized assets commercially viable.
- **Asia is becoming a strategic expansion target.** Deutsche Bank's planned regional rollout underscores the importance of connecting digital-asset infrastructure with established banking relationships and financial-market operations.

## What to Watch

Watch for further details on Deutsche Bank's Asian rollout in 2027, including the markets and services it will support, and for disclosures on VALTS investor eligibility, liquidity arrangements and adoption. These details will help distinguish announced tokenization capabilities from products achieving sustained institutional use.
