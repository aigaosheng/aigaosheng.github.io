# AI Research & Open-Source LLM Brief: Baidu Opens ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview

**Today:** Baidu's staged rollout of a 280-billion-parameter open-weight model underscores how Chinese labs continue to out-pace Western rivals on permissive licensing, a factor enterprise teams should weigh when comparing self-hosting against proprietary API vendors.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **Baidu Opens API Access to ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview**

*TechNode · November 12, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Baidu has opened API access to ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview, a 280-billion-parameter model it plans to open-source under the permissive Apache 2.0 licence within two weeks.

Baidu released ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview, a mixture-of-experts model with 280 billion total parameters and roughly 14 billion active per token, supporting a 1-million-token context window, as the first post-trained version of its earlier ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base model. The company opened access through its Qianfan developer platform and described gains across coding, agentic tasks and deep research versus the base version, while flagging that the release is an intermediate checkpoint that may still carry some capability gaps. Baidu committed to publishing the model weights under Apache 2.0 before November 25.

**Why it matters:** For enterprise and fintech teams weighing open-weight alternatives to US frontier labs, another large Chinese MoE model with a liberal licence adds to a fast-growing roster that already includes DeepSeek, Qwen and GLM, sharpening pricing and deployment-choice pressure on proprietary API vendors operating in Asia.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://technode.com/2026/11/12/baidu-opens-api-access-to-ernie-5-5-flash-preview/)

## Key Takeaways

- Chinese labs continue to default to Apache 2.0 or similarly permissive licences, lowering the barrier for enterprises to self-host rather than depend solely on API vendors.
- Baidu's own caveat that ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview is an "intermediate" checkpoint is a reminder to treat early benchmark claims on preview-stage models as provisional.
- Staged rollouts — API preview first, open weights weeks later — are becoming a standard release pattern among large open-weight model makers.

## What to Watch

Baidu's committed November 25 deadline to publish ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview's weights under Apache 2.0, which will allow independent verification of its coding, agentic and deep-research benchmark claims.
