# AI Finance in Singapore Brief: OCBC Deploys Defensive AI Wrapper

**Today:** OCBC's technology chief disclosed that the bank is building a defensive AI layer to counter agentic attacks, while MAS moved forward with a Know-Your-Agent framework and Agentic Money Sandbox for AI-initiated transactions.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🔒 **OCBC Builds Defensive AI Wrapper as Tech Chief Warns Breaches Are a Matter of When**

*Fortune · Nov 27, 2026*

**Bottom line:** OCBC is deploying AI for cyber defense and preparing for inevitable breaches, with its technology head stating that human teams cannot match the speed of agentic AI attacks.

Praveen Raina, OCBC's head of group operations and technology, told Fortune that the bank is "building a defensive AI wrapper around the bank" and regularly conducts red-teaming exercises with industry peers . The bank also runs a bug bounty program for white-hat hackers. Researchers traced September attacks on financial institutions to Artex, an AI-powered tool that automated intrusions.

**Why it matters:** For Singapore banks and their enterprise clients, the shift from prevention to resilience as the primary security posture has direct implications for vendor risk assessments and incident response planning. The acknowledgment that AI-driven attacks outpace human defenders signals that AI-for-defense spending is becoming non-negotiable.

### 2. 🏦 **MAS Advances Agentic Finance with KYA Protocol and Agentic Money Sandbox**

*Monetary Authority of Singapore / Industry Sources · Nov 26-27, 2026*

**Bottom line:** MAS is developing a Know-Your-Agent framework requiring financial institutions to verify an AI agent's identity, authority, and limits before executing transactions, alongside a sandbox for agent-initiated payments and treasury management.

Chia Der Jiun, MAS Managing Director, outlined the approach at the Singapore FinTech Festival, emphasizing that agents require bounded autonomy, verified identity, clear mandates, and proportionate controls . The Agentic Money Sandbox, established with the Future of Finance Institute, will test use cases including agent-initiated payments and treasury management, with DBS, OCBC, Standard Chartered, and UOB as launch partners . A KYA protocol is planned to verify agent identity, represented customer, permitted scope, operational limits, and trusted credentials before transaction approval .

**Why it matters:** Singapore is moving to formalize the accountability layer for agentic commerce before widespread commercial deployment, giving financial institutions a regulatory template for how AI agents can safely transact. For fintechs building agentic products, early engagement with the sandbox could shape the standards that govern the region.

### 3. 🤖 **Papaya Insurtech Launches Agentic Claims AI for Singapore, Thailand, and Indonesia**

*Beinsure · Nov 25, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Papaya Insurtech has launched a claims adjudication platform using over 55 AI agents, including 28 dedicated to fraud, waste, and abuse detection, now available in Singapore with an eight-week implementation timeline.

The platform converts claims documents into ICD-10 coded data with 98% accuracy for printed and handwritten documents, including local languages . Papaya claims the system reduces claims costs by 8-15% and has been tested with a multinational life insurer in Thailand and one of Indonesia's five largest life insurers. Human medical assessors retain final decision authority.

**Why it matters:** Health insurers across Southeast Asia face projected medical cost increases of 16.9% in Singapore and 15.1% in Indonesia for 2026, with FWA identified as a leading cost driver by 51% of Asia Pacific insurers . Papaya's production deployment—rather than pilot—signals that agentic AI in insurance operations is moving from experimentation to operational reality.

## Key Takeaways

- **Security and agency are converging:** OCBC's defensive AI and MAS's KYA framework both address the same underlying challenge—how to govern AI agents that operate at machine speed, whether as attackers or as authorized transactors.
- **Singapore is building the plumbing for agentic finance:** The Agentic Money Sandbox and KYA protocol give financial institutions a structured environment to test AI-initiated transactions before regulators formalize rules, a pattern that could influence standards across Asia.
- **Insurance operations are a proving ground:** Papaya's 55-agent platform in live production across three markets demonstrates that agentic AI can handle complex, document-heavy workflows in regulated industries with human oversight intact.
