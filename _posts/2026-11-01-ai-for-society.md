# AI for Society Brief: AI Chatbots Spread Election Misinformation Ahead of Midterms

**Today:** As conversational AI becomes a default information channel for millions of people, fresh evidence that leading chatbots routinely get high-stakes facts wrong is a warning enterprises and fintechs cannot ignore as they push AI into customer-facing and compliance roles.

## Top Stories

### 1. 👥 **Study Finds AI Chatbots Riddled With Errors on US Election Questions**

*Arab News (AP) · November 1, 2026*

**Bottom line:** A new analysis found that roughly 40% of chatbot responses to US election-related questions contained at least one factual error, just days before the November 3 midterms.

Forum AI tested major chatbots, including Anthropic's Claude, Google's Gemini 3.6 Flash and AI Overview, and Microsoft's Copilot, and found recurring failures: Gemini told users the Pennsylvania mail-in ballot deadline was November 3, a week after the real cutoff, while Claude confused North Carolina Representative Don Davis with another person entirely. Several chatbots also repeated a debunked, Russian-style disinformation claim against Texas Senate candidate James Talarico, and models frequently cited low-quality sources such as Reddit threads or misquoted candidates. Separate Brennan Center research cited in the same report found the chatbots did resist outright conspiracy theories when directly challenged.

**Why it matters:** This is the first US federal election where millions of voters are actively relying on AI tools for political information, and the error rate shows frontier labs have not solved factual reliability for time-sensitive, high-stakes queries. For enterprises and fintechs building customer-facing assistants or compliance tools on the same model families, including firms operating under MAS and other Asian regulators' scrutiny of AI governance, the findings strengthen the case for mandatory human review and verified sourcing before LLM outputs reach customers on anything factual or regulated.

## Key Takeaways

- Even top-tier AI chatbots from major labs remain unreliable for authoritative, time-sensitive factual information.
- Enterprises embedding conversational AI in customer-facing or compliance workflows should treat outputs as unverified without human review.
- Scrutiny of AI accuracy is intensifying as everyday reliance on chatbots grows, a dynamic relevant well beyond elections to financial services and public-sector AI deployments in Singapore and the wider region.

## What to Watch

Watch how Anthropic, Google and Microsoft respond to the Forum AI findings before the November 3 US midterms, and whether similar accuracy audits extend to AI tools deployed in Singapore and regional financial services and government digital services.
