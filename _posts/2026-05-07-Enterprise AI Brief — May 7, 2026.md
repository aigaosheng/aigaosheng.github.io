### Enterprise AI Brief — May 7, 2026

### Top Stories

- **Anthropic and OpenAI Move into Enterprise Services, Testing India’s IT Leaders**
    - **Source:** The Economic Times · May 6, 2026
    - **Summary:** Anthropic announced a $1.5 billion joint venture to embed engineers directly with mid-sized clients for hands-on AI deployment, while OpenAI is reportedly building a standalone services arm backed by $4 billion. This shift moves both companies beyond pure model providers into end-to-end transformation partners, directly challenging traditional IT services giants like TCS and Infosys.
    - **Why It Matters:** The lines between AI software and services are blurring. Enterprises now have alternative paths to deployment that promise speed and deep technical integration, forcing incumbent consultancies to accelerate their own AI-native offerings or risk disintermediation.

)
- **SAP Buys Prior Labs to Boost Enterprise AI Research**
    - **Source:** IT Brief · May 6, 2026
    - **Summary:** SAP has agreed to acquire Prior Labs, a German AI startup specializing in Tabular Foundation Models (TFMs) designed for structured business data. The deal includes a €1 billion+ investment to expand Prior Labs into a dedicated European AI research lab, integrating its TabPFN models with SAP’s AI Core, Business Data Cloud, and Joule assistant.
    - **Why It Matters:** While most AI focus is on LLMs for text, SAP is betting that TFMs unlock greater value for numerical tasks like supply chain risk and financial forecasting. This move signals a major shift toward domain-specific AI that understands the structured data running core enterprise systems.
    - [URL](https://itbrief.com.au/story/sap-buys-prior-labs-to-boost-enterprise-ai-research)

- **ServiceNow Launches Real-Time Data Foundation for Autonomous AI**
    - **Source:** MarketScreener (Business Wire) · May 6, 2026
    - **Summary:** At its Knowledge 2026 event, ServiceNow unveiled new data capabilities—including Context Engine and Autonomous Data Analytics—designed to provide the live, governed intelligence required for agentic AI. The launch includes the ServiceNow MCP Registry for enterprise control over AI agents and expands RaptorDB Pro to handle real-time operational and analytical workloads simultaneously.
    - **Why It Matters:** ServiceNow is tackling the root cause of AI failure: fragmented, ungoverned data. By embedding real-time context directly into workflows, the platform aims to move AI from passive recommendations to autonomous execution, setting a new standard for how agents operate across siloed enterprise systems.
    - [URL](https://sa.marketscreener.com/news/servicenow-launches-the-real-time-data-foundation-that-puts-autonomous-ai-to-work-across-the-enterpr-ce7f58d2dd88f524)

- **Enterprise AI Ambitions Outpace Readiness, Survey Finds**
    - **Source:** IT Brief · May 6, 2026
    - **Summary:** Research from Harvard Business Review Analytic Services, published by Hyland, reveals that while 94% of organizations see connected data as essential to AI success, only 27% have those foundations in place. Unstructured data (emails, PDFs, images) lags significantly behind structured data in readiness (39% vs. 65%), and only 12% say AI is truly embedded into their daily workflows.
    - **Why It Matters:** The gap between ambition and operational reality remains wide. As enterprises move toward agentic AI, the inability to govern and connect unstructured content—not a lack of data—is emerging as the primary bottleneck, suggesting that AI success now depends more on information architecture than model selection.
    - [URL](https://itbrief.com.au/story/enterprise-ai-ambitions-outpace-readiness-survey-finds)
