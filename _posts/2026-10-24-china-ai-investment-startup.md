# AI Investment & Startup in China Brief: DeepSeek Reportedly Raises $15 Billion Ahead of IPO

**Today:** Relevant if you track where Chinese AI capital is going. A record-scale model-lab round and a city-fund-led embodied AI chip deal mark the two ends of the market.

## Top Stories

### 1. 📊 **DeepSeek Reportedly Closes $15 Billion Round, Speeds Up Shanghai IPO Plans**
*TechNode · October 23, 2026*

**Bottom line:** DeepSeek has reportedly raised USD 15 billion in private funding, almost twice its original target, and is moving faster towards a Shanghai listing.

According to The Wall Street Journal's report on October 22, which cited people familiar with the matter, the Chinese AI developer finished the round after strong investor demand pushed it well past its initial goal. The report also says DeepSeek is speeding up preparations for a possible Shanghai listing early next year. The new money is expected to support model development and expansion of its computing infrastructure.

**Why it matters:** A private round of this size gives a Chinese model lab capital on a scale more often seen at US frontier labs. A Shanghai listing would also give onshore public markets a direct way to invest in a leading Chinese foundation-model company. For Asia-based enterprises choosing model vendors, a better-funded DeepSeek is more likely to remain a durable, lower-cost option. The deal is still based on sources and has not been confirmed by the company.

### 2. 🤖 **Beijing AI Fund Leads Robot Chipmaker Huixi's Series B**
*TechNode · October 23, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Huixi Technology has closed Pre-Series B+ and Series B financing to build next-generation chips for embodied AI. The company says its equity funding this year now exceeds RMB 2.5 billion.

The company announced the financing on October 22. The Beijing Artificial Intelligence Industry Investment Fund led the round, joined by the Beijing Robotics Industry Development Investment Fund and Hidden Hill Capital. Existing shareholders Shunwei Capital and Cathay Capital also put in money. The round size was not disclosed. Huixi makes the R1 AI system-on-chip (a single chip that combines processor and AI functions) and the Huixi Embodied computing platforms. Its platform already runs in AgiBot's Genie G2 robots, and the new funds will go towards new chips and faster commercial deliveries.

**Why it matters:** City-level industry funds are leading deals in the robot computing layer, not only in robot hardware. This points to a deliberate push to build a domestic embodied AI supply chain. Investors watching China's robotics boom should follow component suppliers that already have named customer deployments.
