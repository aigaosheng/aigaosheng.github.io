## 1. Top Headlines

- **[Visa and Tencent ink agentic commerce and cross-border payments MoU](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/48672/visa-and-tencent-ink-agentic-commerce-and-cross-border-payments-mou)** (Finextra). The two firms have signed a memorandum covering cross-border payments, agentic commerce, enterprise AI and help for businesses expanding globally. AI shopping agents are moving from pilots into the strategy of two of the world's largest payment ecosystems.

- **[Firms object to shares being part of OKX-ICE tokenised stock trading venture](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/48663/firms-object-to-shares-being-part-of-okx-ice-tokenised-stock-trading-venture)** (Finextra). The OKX and ICE venture first listed 62 companies, and 33 have now objected. Issuers clearly want a say before their shares move onto blockchain rails.

- **[Monzo launches in Spain](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/48665/monzo-launches-in-spain)** (Finextra). The UK digital bank has gone live in Spain, its first market in continental Europe. This tests whether a UK neobank playbook can win in a market already crowded with digital challengers.

- **[Coinbase says customer contact data exposed in third-party breach](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/48662/coinbase-says-customer-contact-data-exposed-in-third-party-breach)** (Finextra). Coinbase says its own systems were not breached. A small set of customer data was caught up in an incident at analytics vendor Amplitude. Vendor risk is now a front-line security issue for every exchange and fintech.

- **[UK Government takes on till fraud with POS software standards](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/48664/uk-government-takes-on-till-fraud-with-pos-software-standards)** (Finextra). The UK will make software standards mandatory for point-of-sale systems to fight till fraud. POS vendors and payment firms should expect new certification work.

- **[Canada opens stablecoin consultation](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/48651/canada-opens-stablecoin-consultation)** (Finextra). Ottawa has opened a 60-day public consultation on draft stablecoin rules that keep the ban on paying yield. Canada is siding with banks that worry about deposits leaving for stablecoins.

- **[Ripple secures final OCC authorisation to establish Ripple National Trust Bank](https://www.fintechfutures.com/regulatory-actions/ripple-secures-final-occ-authorisation-to-establish-ripple-national-trust-bank)** (FinTech Futures). The trust bank will provide digital asset custody, manage the segregated reserves behind Ripple USD, and act as collateral trustee for those reserves. Another crypto-native firm now has direct US federal supervision.

- **[Barclays appoints Anita Tanna as first chief AI officer](https://www.fintechfutures.com/job-cuts-new-hires/anita-tanna-appointed-as-first-chief-ai-officer-at-barclays)** (FinTech Futures). Barclays has named its group chief of staff, Anita Tanna, as its first chief AI officer. Bank of Ireland, HSBC and ANZ are among the banks that named their first AI chiefs earlier this year.

- **[Wealthsimple raises around $300m at new $9.8bn valuation](https://www.fintechfutures.com/venture-capital-funding/wealthsimple-raises-around-300m-at-new-9-8bn-valuation)** (FinTech Futures). The Canadian money manager raised up to CAD 425 million (just over USD 300 million) at a post-money valuation of CAD 13.8 billion (USD 9.8 billion). Investors are still paying up for fintechs at scale.

## 2. In-Depth Highlight

**Visa and Tencent bet on AI agents and two-way China commerce.** The MoU builds on an existing partnership for international visitors to China. It aims to make cross-border payments easier for travellers in both directions through TenPay Global and Visa Pay. The tie-up now also covers Tencent's overseas AI businesses, with work on AI payment experiences and agentic payments. The partners will also look at how Visa Intelligent Commerce could let AI agents help eligible users find, choose and buy goods or services. The key players are Tencent Financial Technology, led by corporate VP Forest Lin, and Visa's Greater China business under Elaine Chang. For payments firms in Asia, the deal points to cross-border wallets, card networks and AI agents coming together in one stack. That raises open questions on liability, consent and data governance that regulators have not yet fully answered. Link: [Finextra](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/48672/visa-and-tencent-ink-agentic-commerce-and-cross-border-payments-mou).

## 3. Market & Industry Insight

**Digital assets are being pulled into mainstream regulation, with friction along the way.** This week showed both sides of that process. In Canada, non-bank stablecoin issuers would need to back tokens 1:1 with reserves at qualified Canadian institutions, settle redemptions within three business days, and cap fees at 1% ([Finextra](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/48651/canada-opens-stablecoin-consultation)). Canada's banks have their own alternative in progress: in September the largest banks began exploring a Canadian-dollar tokenised deposit system.

In the US, Ripple's new national trust charter gives a stablecoin issuer federal oversight of its reserves ([FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/regulatory-actions/ripple-secures-final-occ-authorisation-to-establish-ripple-national-trust-bank)). Tokenised equities are meeting resistance from issuers, though. The SEC's new framework gives qualified providers a five-year exemption to test the technology, but listed companies can block their shares. Nearly half of the companies on the original OKXICE list have opted out. ([Finextra](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/48663/firms-object-to-shares-being-part-of-okx-ice-tokenised-stock-trading-venture))

Security remains the weak point. Last month Bitget had to halt withdrawals after hackers it suspects are linked to North Korea stole more than USD 380 million. Coinbase's vendor-related exposure followed this week. The takeaway for operators is that regulatory clarity is improving, but third-party and custody risk now decide who earns trust.

## 4. Company & Startup Spotlight

**Monzo.** Its move into Spain follows its launch in Ireland under a European banking licence, and its exit from the US to focus on markets closer to home. Spanish customers can open free personal, joint, under-18, business and instant-access savings accounts. Each comes with a Spanish IBAN and connects to Bizum. Local rails, not just a translated app, are now the price of entry for neobanks in Europe. [Finextra](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/48665/monzo-launches-in-spain)

**Wealthsimple.** Over the past year it launched more than 50 products, including commission-free options and futures trading, IPO access, accounts for kids and teens, and business chequing. Its last valuation was USD 7.2 billion, after a USD 538 million round 12 months ago. Launching many products quickly is still being rewarded with higher valuations. [FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/venture-capital-funding/wealthsimple-raises-around-300m-at-new-9-8bn-valuation)

## 5. Regulatory & Policy Watch

- **UK, POS software standards:** Announced in the budget, the plan directs HMRC to work with stakeholders and publish a technical consultation next summer with detailed proposals. [Finextra](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/48664/uk-government-takes-on-till-fraud-with-pos-software-standards)

- **Canada, stablecoin rules:** Stablecoins are currently overseen by provincial and territorial securities regulators. The new Stablecoin Act targets private issuers operating across jurisdictions, and the draft rules keep the yield ban that banks lobbied for. [Finextra](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/48651/canada-opens-stablecoin-consultation)

- **US, OCC trust charters:** Ripple's final approval adds to the crypto firms now supervised as national trust banks. [FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/regulatory-actions/ripple-secures-final-occ-authorisation-to-establish-ripple-national-trust-bank)

## 7. What's Next

- **Canada's stablecoin consultation** runs for 60 days from its launch this week. Watch for pushback from crypto firms on the yield ban and the reserve rules.
- **HMRC's technical consultation** on POS software standards is due next summer. POS and payment vendors should start mapping how tamper-proof transaction logging would affect them.
- **OKXICE's issuer list** may shrink further, or recover, as companies decide how to use their right to opt out under the SEC framework.
- **Visa and Tencent** have signed an MoU, not a launch. Look for the first agentic payment pilots and how they handle consumer consent.
- **Coinbase/Amplitude:** watch for follow-up disclosures on how many customers were affected, and for regulator responses.
