# AI for society Brief: Pakistan And Philippines Push AI Reskilling

**Today:** Governments across Asia and Europe moved to address AI's workforce and education fallout, even as new research complicates the narrative that AI data centres are driving up household electricity bills.

## Top Stories

### 1. 👥 **Philippines Leans on Reskilling as Unemployment Climbs and AI Risk Grows**

*Daily Tribune · November 6, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Manila is betting on upskilling programmes to cushion an AI-exposed workforce just as joblessness hits its highest level in over a year.

Presidential Communications Office Undersecretary Claire Castro said the government is expanding reskilling and upskilling collaboration with the private sector, pointing to the Philippine Semiconductor and Electronics Industry Roadmap and newly approved economic zones projected to generate roughly 189,000 jobs. The push comes as the Philippine Statistics Authority reported unemployment rising to 5.4% in October, nearly 900,000 more jobless than a year earlier, with consultant Rey Lugtu warning that clerical and collections roles are especially exposed to automation.

**Why it matters:** The Philippines' BPO and shared-services sector is deeply tied to global fintech and payments back-office work; how quickly it retrains clerical and voice staff will shape outsourcing cost and quality calculations for financial institutions sourcing from the region, including in Singapore.

### 2. 👥 **Pakistan Sets Goal of 1 Million AI Professionals by 2030**

*The Nation (Pakistan) · November 6, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Pakistan's planning minister tied a national AI skills target to a broader push to shift the economy from IT services export toward higher-value products.

Minister for Planning, Development and Special Initiatives Ahsan Iqbal said Pakistan produces over 75,000 IT graduates annually and will open seven new AI centres linking research, skills training, startups and industry under its National AI Policy. The announcement was paired with a goal of a trillion-dollar economy by 2035 and cited RAAST, Pakistan's instant payment rail, processing 646 million transactions in a single quarter.

**Why it matters:** A credible AI talent pipeline in Pakistan would expand the pool of offshore technical and fintech engineering talent available to BaaS and payments firms, though execution risk remains high given the scale of the 2030 target.

### 3. 👥 **EU Launches Education Package With AI Literacy Certificate**

*Via europeansting.com (European Commission) · November 6, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Brussels is responding to declining basic skills and teacher shortages with a package that includes a planned AI Literacy Certificate for schools.

The European Commission's Education and Skills Package sets up a European Digital Infrastructure Consortium on Education, pilots an AI Literacy Certificate from the 2027/28 school year, and launches "Teacher Academies 2.0" alongside a Council Recommendation to reverse slipping literacy, maths and science scores — currently affecting around a third of 15-year-olds across the bloc. A European Education and Skills Summit is scheduled for November 20.

**Why it matters:** The package signals that AI literacy is becoming a baseline education requirement in major markets, raising the bar for digital and financial literacy expectations that multinational employers, including banks and fintechs, will eventually face when hiring European graduates.

### 4. 🔬 **Study Finds AI Data Centres Have Only Marginal Effect on Home Electricity Bills**

*The Washington Post · November 6, 2026*

**Bottom line:** New academic research complicates the popular narrative that AI data centres are the main driver of rising household power costs.

Researchers from Harvard University and the University of Pennsylvania found data centres likely have only a small effect on residential electricity prices, consistent with separate MIT findings that data centres lifted average residential prices by just 2.1% between 2010 and 2024. The same research found a starker effect on industrial customers, who absorbed about 15% of price increases between 2020 and 2025 tied to data centre growth.

**Why it matters:** The finding arrives just days after US voters rejected data centre expansion in nearly every midterm ballot measure, and complicates the political and public pressure campaign AI infrastructure operators are facing over energy costs, including in Asian markets weighing similar data centre build-outs.

### 5. 👥 **OpenAI Expands Australian University Partnerships as Student AI Use Surges**

*OpenAI / smbtech.au · November 6, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Four more Australian universities are embedding OpenAI tools campus-wide as survey data shows AI use among students has become near-universal.

UNSW Sydney, the University of Sydney, La Trobe University and Adelaide University are rolling out ChatGPT Edu, with UNSW alone extending access to more than 80,000 students and staff. A Pureprofile survey of 1,018 Australian students commissioned by OpenAI found 81% use AI several times a week and 28% have already built their own AI agent or assistant, with La Trobe launching what it calls Australia's first Bachelor of AI Engineering.

**Why it matters:** The scale of embedded, vendor-provided AI access raises equity and dependency questions for students without equivalent exposure elsewhere in the region, reinforcing the AI literacy gap that Asian education systems, including Singapore's SkillsFuture push, are racing to close.

## Key Takeaways

- Governments in the Philippines, Pakistan and the EU are converging on the same playbook: name an AI skills target, launch a certificate or training programme, and hope execution keeps pace with job displacement that is already showing up in unemployment data.
- The data centre backlash from this week's US midterms is colliding with academic research that undercuts the direct link between AI infrastructure and consumer electricity prices, muddying the policy debate just as it intensifies.
- Vendor-embedded AI access at scale, as in Australia's university rollout, is quietly setting the baseline for what "AI literacy" means for the next generation of hires entering fintech and other digital-first sectors.

## What to Watch

Watch for the European Education and Skills Summit on November 20 for firmer detail and timelines on the EU's AI Literacy Certificate, and for follow-up Philippine Statistics Authority releases to see whether November unemployment data shows further deterioration tied to AI-exposed clerical roles.
