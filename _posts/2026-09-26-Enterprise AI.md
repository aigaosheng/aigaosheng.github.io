# Enterprise AI Brief — 2026-09-26

**Today:** Enterprise AI is moving beyond chat toward production-grade agents, with real-time voice workflows highlighting the need for orchestration, observability, security, and infrastructure control.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **Oracle details enterprise architecture for real-time AI voice agents**

*Oracle · September 26, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Oracle is positioning open-source LiveKit Agents together with OCI AI services as a practical architecture for enterprises building real-time voice agents they can operate and control.

Oracle describes an architecture combining LiveKit's open-source Agents framework with Oracle Cloud Infrastructure AI services and infrastructure. The design connects speech recognition, LLM reasoning, tool calls, text-to-speech, WebRTC/SIP audio transport, and session management into a coordinated agent workflow. ([Oracle Blogs][1])

**Why it matters:** Enterprise voice AI is increasingly an infrastructure problem rather than simply an LLM problem: production deployments need reliable orchestration across models, telephony, enterprise data, security, monitoring, and business systems. The emphasis on self-hosting and operational control also reflects growing enterprise demand for architectures that reduce dependency on opaque managed AI stacks.
