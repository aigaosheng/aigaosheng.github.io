## Investment Signals

- **AI infrastructure remains the dominant investment theme**: Recent large rounds continue to favor chips, autonomous systems, robotics, and AI platforms rather than only consumer-facing applications.
- **Defense technology is becoming a strategic venture category**: Geopolitical uncertainty is driving more capital toward dual-use technologies combining AI, autonomy, and security applications. 
- **Private market liquidity is evolving**: Companies such as Wayve are testing alternative paths between venture-backed growth and public-market access.
