# AI Investment & Startup in China Brief: MicroLED AI Interconnect Startup Raises Two Rounds

**Today:** New funding for an AI interconnect startup and an AI audio venture shows capital flowing into both compute infrastructure and products for real-world work.

## Top Stories

### 1. 📊 **Shanke Guanglian Raises Two MicroLED Rounds**

*Investment界 · November 26, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Shanke Guanglian closed seed and angel rounds totaling tens of millions of yuan for its MicroLED optical interconnect business.

Investment界 reports that the Shanghai startup, founded in September, is developing optical interconnect chips and engines for AI clusters and data centres. Lishi Ventures backed the seed round; SenseTime Guoxiang, Changshi Capital and Houxue Capital co-led the angel round, with other funds also participating. The company says it will use the funding for chip R&D and a packaging sample line, and has started preparing for another round.

**Why it matters:** The financing points to investor interest in components aimed at easing connectivity constraints as AI systems scale. For Singapore and other Asian data-centre operators, the practical signal will be whether Shanke can validate its technology and move toward production.

### 2. 📊 **HearWell Raises Tens of Millions of Yuan**

*36Kr · November 26, 2026*

**Bottom line:** HearWell raised tens of millions of yuan across two rounds to build an AI acoustic-sensing platform and related audio products.

36Kr reports that Boyu Ventures, Canlan Capital, Meihua Ventures and Xiuyuan Capital participated. The company’s first products include Palmo Meet, an AI speakerphone for meetings, and Earmind, a personal listening device; Palmo Meet has entered small-batch production, with crowdfunding promotion planned soon.

**Why it matters:** HearWell is pursuing a product-led route for AI that interprets sound and context, with an initial use case in mobile and hybrid meetings. Its crowdfunding launch and early sales will indicate whether users see enough value in acoustic understanding beyond transcription and meeting summaries.

## Key Takeaways

- Funding reached both AI infrastructure components and AI-enabled audio products.
- The announcements disclosed round totals only in broad terms, limiting comparisons of valuations and capital efficiency.
- Engineering validation and early product demand will matter more than funding announcements for judging commercial progress.

## What to Watch

Watch for Shanke Guanglian’s engineering validation and production progress, and for HearWell’s crowdfunding launch and early demand for Palmo Meet. The companies have not announced specific dates for these milestones.
