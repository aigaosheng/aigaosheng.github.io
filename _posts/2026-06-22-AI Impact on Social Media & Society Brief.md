### 1. Microsoft CEO Nadella Warns Against AI Power Concentration
- **The Wall Street Journal via IT之家** · 2026-06-22
- **Summary**: In a recent interview, Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella issued a stark warning against the concentration of AI power in the hands of a few companies like OpenAI and Anthropic. ### 2. AI Training Workers Admit Using Chatbots to "Cheat," Risking Model "Collapse"
- **New Scientist** · 2026-06-22
- **Summary**: Whistleblowers have told New Scientist that workers hired by third-party firms to train new AI models are widely cheating by using chatbots like ChatGPT to complete their tasks. This practice, done to meet quotas and avoid penalties for low-paid, insecure work, can lead to "AI inbreeding" or "model collapse," where the power and usefulness of future AI models are significantly diminished. Experts warn that while not catastrophic, this will degrade AI performance on human-like tasks.
- **Why It Matters**: This reveals a critical vulnerability in the AI development pipeline, where the very data used to train next-generation models may be polluted. It undermines the quality of future AI systems and highlights ethical and labor issues in the gig economy that fuels AI development.
- **URL**: [People training new AI models admit they just get chatbots to do it](https://www.newscientist.com/article/2531050-people-training-new-ai-models-admit-they-just-get-chatbots-to-do-it/)
