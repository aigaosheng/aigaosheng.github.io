### 1. IBM launches sovereignty risk profile to boost hybrid cloud compliance
- **SDxCentral** · 2026-06-01
- **Summary**: IBM introduced a Cloud Sovereignty Risk Profile integrated into its Security and Compliance Center Workload Protection platform. The tool is positioned to give enterprises continuous visibility into controls tied to data residency, encryption, resilience, concentration risk, and operational independence across hybrid and multi-cloud environments as AI workloads expand. The announcement frames sovereignty not just as a cloud issue but as a governance and assurance requirement for AI deployments.
- **Why It Matters**: As AI systems move into regulated and cross-border environments, sovereignty and control evidence are becoming board-level compliance issues. Tools that map operational controls to residency and independence requirements could become part of enterprise AI audit readiness.
