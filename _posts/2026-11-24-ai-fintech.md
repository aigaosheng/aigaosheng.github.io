# AI Fintech Brief: Danamon Taps AIGL for Banking AI

**Today:** Two banks in Asia outlined phased approaches to banking AI, with Danamon exploring customer-facing agents and Dukhan Bank planning an enterprise rollout.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **Danamon and AIGL Explore Banking AI Agents**

*Danamon · November 24, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Danamon and Advance Intelligence Group (AIGL) signed an MOU to explore AI agents for collections and telesales at the Indonesian bank.

The proposed agents would support customer interactions through voice and text, with governance and oversight from Danamon teams. The bank says customer data will be handled under Indonesia’s data protection and localisation requirements.

**Why it matters:** Collections and telesales give the partnership specific operational areas to assess, while keeping the initiative at the exploration stage. For regional financial institutions, the agreement also puts local data requirements alongside the use of AI agents in customer interactions.

### 2. 🤖 **Dukhan Bank Plans Phased Claude Adoption**

*Dukhan Bank · November 24, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Dukhan Bank signed an agreement with Anthropic to introduce Claude in stages, starting with selected employees and internal use cases.

The bank identified research and analysis, knowledge synthesis, employee productivity and technology development as initial priorities. It says any expansion will depend on business value, operational readiness and governance and risk assessments.

**Why it matters:** The phased plan makes governance and operational readiness part of the bank’s path to broader adoption. For financial institutions assessing enterprise AI, Dukhan’s stated expansion criteria offer concrete signals to watch as the rollout develops.

## Key Takeaways

- The announcements show two different entry points for banking AI: targeted customer operations at Danamon and internal employee use at Dukhan Bank.
- Both initiatives are described as phased, with oversight and risk assessment built into their planned approaches.
- Danamon’s MOU remains exploratory, while Dukhan has announced an agreement to introduce Claude.

## What to Watch

Watch whether Danamon moves from exploring the collections and telesales agents to controlled pilots, and what results it reports. For Dukhan Bank, track which functions follow the initial internal use cases and how it assesses readiness to expand Claude’s use.
