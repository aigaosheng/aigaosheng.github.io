## Key Takeaway

AI’s societal impact is moving into a new phase: the debate is no longer only about what AI can do, but how societies manage workforce transitions, creative ownership, public-sector adoption, and human values. The next competitive advantage will likely come from organizations and individuals that combine AI capability with strong governance and human judgment.
