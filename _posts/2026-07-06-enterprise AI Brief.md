### 1. **Enterprise AI moves from pilots to full-scale transformation programs**

* **Source** · The Australian (Adobe Summit coverage) · 2026-07-06
* **Summary**: Enterprises such as RACQ and RMIT are shifting from isolated AI pilots to full-scale transformation initiatives built on unified data foundations and generative AI. ### 2. **Enterprises tighten AI ROI controls as spending efficiency becomes critical**

* **Source** · Business Insider · 2026-07-06
* **Summary**: Despite broad adoption of AI tools, enterprises are increasingly struggling to extract measurable value. Research shows only high-intensity AI adopters achieve meaningful productivity gains, while many firms lack clear strategies for converting AI usage into business outcomes. ([Business Insider][2])
* **Why It Matters**: AI investment is shifting from “adoption race” to **execution discipline**, forcing companies to link AI usage directly to productivity and revenue impact.
