# Enterprise AI Brief: Zoho Launches Blended AI Agents for Zoho Desk

**Today:** Only one qualifying enterprise AI story cleared source and timestamp checks since the last edition, and it shows vendors building human oversight into customer-facing AI agents.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **Zoho Launches Blended AI Agents for Zoho Desk Customer Service**

*ANI, via The Tribune · November 16, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Zoho has put AI agents that act across connected apps into Zoho Desk, with human review and handoff built in, available on all paid plans.

Zoho introduced Blended Agents on its customer service platform, built on its Zia Agents platform. They can retrieve order details, check stock and update customer accounts, and hand a conversation to a human rep with its history when judgement is needed. Employees can also pause an agent's execution to review or approve an action before it proceeds. Zoho launched Automation Studio, a no-code workflow builder, and Workstation, a customisable interface for support reps, alongside the agents.

Per the report, the agents carry no extra charge on paid Zoho Desk plans, but customers pay for LLM usage. Zoho-hosted models come with a monthly token allowance that varies by plan, or customers can bring their own API keys. Zoho says it does not train its models on customer data and hosts Zoho Desk in local data centres in India. The launch coincides with the product's tenth anniversary, and Zoho says it serves more than 125,000 businesses in 155 countries.

**Why it matters:** Bundling agents into existing paid tiers and allowing bring-your-own-model keys puts pricing pressure on rivals that charge per resolution. Pause-and-approve controls and local hosting also address two common Asian enterprise concerns: oversight and data residency.

## Key Takeaways

- Vendors are packaging agents as a feature of existing suites rather than a separately priced product, shifting the cost conversation to model usage.
- Human approval and handoff controls are becoming a standard part of customer-facing agent design.
- Data residency and bring-your-own-model options are increasingly part of the pitch to buyers in India and wider Asia.

## What to Watch

Watch whether Zoho's rivals respond with similar bundled-agent pricing, and what customer results Zoho publishes for Blended Agents after launch.
