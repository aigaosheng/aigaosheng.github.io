# AI Impact on Social Media & Society Brief — May 11, 2026

## Top Stories

### 1. Delhi High Court Protects Aman Gupta’s Personality Rights, Orders AI Deepfake Takedowns

**Sources ·** Bar and Bench · Live Law · May 10, 2026

The Delhi High Court granted an interim injunction protecting entrepreneur and “Shark Tank India” judge Aman Gupta from AI-generated deepfakes, fake endorsements, obscene content, and unauthorised commercial use of his identity. The court reportedly directed major platforms and search engines to remove infringing material and disclose uploader identities.

**Why It Matters ·** Courts are increasingly recognising personality and publicity rights as enforceable against AI-driven impersonation, potentially reshaping platform accountability around synthetic likeness usage.
