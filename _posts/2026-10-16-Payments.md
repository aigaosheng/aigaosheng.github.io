# Payments Brief: Visa Launches 24/7 Commercial USDC Prefunding via Visa Direct

**Today:** Global card networks and fintech infrastructures are rapidly integrating stablecoins and digital asset rails to overhaul liquidity management, cross-border treasury clearance, and international account capabilities.

## Top Stories

### 1. 💳 **Visa Introduces 24/7 Commercial USDC Prefunding for Global Visa Direct Payments**

*Pluang · October 16, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Visa has officially rolled out commercial USDC stablecoin prefunding capabilities for cross-border transactions via its Visa Direct platform.

The new operational setup allows payment service providers and financial institutions to prefund cross-border accounts in USDC around the clock. Financial infrastructure platform Inyo Global has signed on as the inaugural deployment partner. By eliminating dependence on traditional banking hours for treasury prefunding, Visa enables seamless 24/7 liquidity management and faster end-to-end clearing for international money transfers.

**Why it matters:** For enterprise treasury and fintech operators in Asia and globally, 24/7 stablecoin prefunding reduces capital drag, eliminates weekend settlement bottlenecks, and accelerates instant payouts across high-volume trade routes.

### 2. 🏦 **Ant International Obtains Full CBUAE Licences for Antom and WorldFirst**

*Fintechly · October 15, 2026*

**Bottom line:** The Central Bank of the UAE has granted two full payment operating licenses to Ant International for its Antom and WorldFirst business units.

Antom secured a Retail Payment Services and Card Schemes license to launch merchant acquiring and digital payment services in the UAE, while WorldFirst obtained a Stored Value Facilities license to offer cross-border account services for business entities. The dual regulatory approvals strengthen Ant International's presence across Middle East and Asia-Pacific commerce corridors, providing localized acquiring and multi-currency accounts.

**Why it matters:** As trade flows between East Asia and the Middle East continue to accelerate, localized acquiring and cross-border payment infrastructure help enterprise platforms reduce payment friction and optimize foreign exchange processing.

### 3. 🌐 **PPRO Partners with Damisa for Stablecoin-Powered Treasury and Cross-Border Settlement**

*Damisa · October 15, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Global digital payments infrastructure provider PPRO has partnered with fintech firm Damisa to adopt stablecoin rails for cross-border treasury settlement.

Damisa's platform converts transaction funds into regulated stablecoins to execute same-day international clearing, replacing traditional multi-hop correspondent banking chains. After an initial rollout in Latin America where Damisa processed over $70 million in settlements, PPRO is expanding the integration globally across additional corridors to improve cash visibility and cut liquidity costs.

**Why it matters:** Enterprise payment aggregators are increasingly turning to regulated stablecoins as a backend treasury vehicle, proving that digital assets are establishing themselves as core enterprise liquidity rails rather than just consumer payment methods.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.damisa.xyz/announcements/ppro-partners-with-damisa-for-stablecoin-powered-treasury-and-cross-border-settlement)

### 4. 💳 **Unit Launches SWIFT-Based International Payouts Across 190 Countries**

*The Paypers · October 15, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Banking-as-a-service platform Unit has expanded its embedded finance suite by launching native SWIFT international payments.

The update enables platforms built on Unit to deliver cross-border payouts directly through their existing banking setup to financial institutions across more than 190 countries and territories. The service sits alongside Unit's existing ACH, real-time payments, wires, and card issuing features, sharing a single ledger, dashboard, and unified automated return logic.

**Why it matters:** B2B platforms and software vendors can now embed international vendor and contractor payouts without needing to manage separate multi-bank APIs or standalone remittance providers.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://thepaypers.com/payments/news/unit-launches-international-payments-across-190-countries)

### 5. 🏦 **Nuvei Receives PASA Authorisation for Local Acquiring in South Africa**

*The Paypers · October 15, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Payments technology provider Nuvei has obtained official authorization from the Payment Association of South Africa (PASA) for local acquiring operations.

The license allows Nuvei to directly process domestic card and alternative payment transactions for international and regional enterprise merchants operating in South Africa. Direct local acquiring connectivity increases transaction approval rates, lowers card scheme processing fees, and optimizes payout speeds for global merchants expanding in the market.

**Why it matters:** For enterprise merchants executing cross-border growth strategies, direct local acquiring licenses reduce payment drop-offs and minimize cross-border processing surcharges.

## Key Takeaways

* **Stablecoins are moving deeper into backend liquidity:** Network giants like Visa and orchestrators like PPRO are utilizing stablecoins for 24/7 treasury prefunding and same-day settlement.
* **B2B platforms demand unified payout rails:** BaaS providers are integrating SWIFT alongside local real-time rails, consolidating international B2B payments into a single ledger experience.
* **Emerging corridor licensing accelerates:** FinTech giants are securing full local payments and stored-value licenses across key trade gateways like the UAE and South Africa to control the full acquiring stack.

## What to Watch

Keep an eye on further institutional announcements from card networks regarding additional stablecoin integration options (such as EURC or local-currency tokenised deposits) within global payout networks like Visa Direct and Mastercard Send.
