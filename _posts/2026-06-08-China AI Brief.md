### 1. Moonshot AI Seeks $30 Billion Valuation in New Funding Round
- **Bloomberg via The Edge Singapore** · 2026-06-08
- **Summary**: Moonshot AI, the developer of the Kimi chatbot, is in early talks to raise up to $2 billion in a new funding round that would value the startup at $30 billion. This marks the company's third financing round in six months and comes as it nears the close of another round led by Meituan that values it at $20 billion. The aggressive fundraising highlights the immense capital required to compete in the foundational LLM space, driven by the shift towards high-Token-consumption AI Agents.

### 2. Changan Auto's Tianshu LLM Receives National AI备案 Approval
- **Securities Times via Sina Finance** · 2026-06-08

It signals a future where sophisticated on-device AI becomes a standard and regulated feature in passenger vehicles.

### 3. World's First Prefabricated 'AI-Power Island' Unveiled in Qingdao
- **Xinhua** · 2026-06-08
- **Summary**: Chinese power equipment manufacturer TGOOD has launched the world's first prefabricated power supply station exclusively designed for AI data centers in Qingdao. The modular "AI-Power Island" cuts construction time by nearly 70%, reduces land use by over 30%, and lowers civil engineering costs by almost 80%. It also reduces the electricity cost per AI token by about 30% via AI-driven power coordination and direct green energy integration.
- **Why It Matters**: As AI computing demand soars, power supply and energy costs have become critical bottlenecks. This innovative infrastructure solution directly tackles these challenges, potentially lowering the barrier to entry for AI model deployment and operation. It represents a major hardware innovation to support China's "AI Plus" initiative and make AI more affordable and scalable.
