### 1. UK ICO lays out next-year plan for “safe AI-powered innovation”
- **ICO** · 2026-05-29
- **Summary**: The UK Information Commissioner’s Office published its response to the government on enabling safe AI-powered innovation, outlining priorities for 2026/27. The regulator said its focus will include an AI code of practice, dedicated guidance on agentic AI, and consumer-facing work to address increasingly personalized AI systems.
- **Why It Matters**: The update gives organizations an early view of where UK data protection oversight of AI is heading, especially around agentic systems and practical compliance expectations. Firms building or deploying AI in the UK should expect more operational guidance rather than only high-level principles.
