## 1. Top Headlines

- [PE firm CD&R targets buyout of $40bn Global Payments - FT](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/48681/pe-firm-cdr-targets-buyout-of-40bn-global-payments---ft) (**Finextra**): Private equity firm CD&R is reportedly working with GTCR, Global Payments' largest shareholder, on a bid to take the processor private. Talks are ongoing and may not produce a deal, but the report sent the shares up more than 11% in afternoon trading. Acquiring scale is still attracting large pools of private capital.

- [Visa and Tencent ink agentic commerce and cross-border payments MoU](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/48672/visa-and-tencent-ink-agentic-commerce-and-cross-border-payments-mou) (**Finextra**): The memorandum sets out a framework for working together on cross-border payments, agentic commerce, enterprise AI and global business expansion. It builds on an existing partnership aimed at international visitors to China, and it targets both inbound and outbound travellers through TenPay Global and Visa Pay. A global card network and a super-app wallet are moving towards interoperability in China corridors.

- [Ripple secures final OCC authorisation to establish Ripple National Trust Bank](https://www.fintechfutures.com/regulatory-actions/ripple-secures-final-occ-authorisation-to-establish-ripple-national-trust-bank) (**FinTech Futures**): Ripple received final approval from the US Office of the Comptroller of the Currency to charter Ripple National Trust Bank. The trust bank will provide digital asset custody, manage the segregated reserves backing Ripple USD, and act as collateral trustee. More stablecoin infrastructure is now operating inside the federal supervisory perimeter.

- [European payments players unite to launch the Local Payments Council for Agentic Commerce](https://fintechfutures.com/ai-in-fintech/local-payments-council-for-agentic-commerce-launches) (**FinTech Futures**): A group of European payments organisations launched the council to build an industry standard for agentic commerce that works across established payment systems. It will study Europe's local payment systems and how they can connect with AI agents responsibly. The aim is to keep local schemes from being sidelined as AI agents start to make purchases.

- [PhotonPay raises $40m Series C to scale its stablecoin-centric Financial OS](https://www.fintechfutures.com/paytech/photonpay-raises-40m-series-c-to-scale-its-stablecoin-centric-financial-os) (**FinTech Futures**, sponsored content): Blockchain Capital led the round, with SBI Group joining as a new investor alongside returning backers IDG Capital, GL Ventures and Lightspeed Faction. Investors are backing platforms that run stablecoin and fiat flows through a single API.

- [Liquidity Services deploys Flywire tech for domestic and cross-border B2B payments](https://www.finextra.com/pressarticle/111512/liquidity-services-deploys-flywire-tech-for-domestic-and-cross-border-b2b-payments) (**Finextra**, company press release): The e-commerce marketplace now uses Flywire for both domestic and cross-border B2B payments, replacing a reconciliation process that had been manual across bank transfers, cards and multiple vendors. Business-to-business (B2B) collections remain one of the clearest use cases for payment orchestration, meaning one layer that routes payments across many methods and providers.

## 2. In-Depth Highlight

The week's most consequential deal story is the reported interest in taking Global Payments private. According to the Financial Times, as reported by Finextra, CD&R views the processor, valued at roughly $40 billion in enterprise value, as an acquisition target and is working with GTCR on a bid. The timing is notable. The interest comes a year after Global Payments agreed to buy Worldpay from GTCR and FIS for $22.7 billion, while selling its Issuer Solutions business to FIS for $13.5 billion. One reading is that private ownership would let management integrate Worldpay without quarterly market pressure, though this is our interpretation, not a reported motive. A Mizuho analyst said the bid news was not a surprise. If a deal goes ahead, merchants and partner banks should watch for changes in pricing, platform consolidation and regulatory review of the transaction.

## 3. Market & Industry Insight

**Agentic commerce** means AI agents shopping and paying on behalf of people. This week it moved from conference talk to formal partnerships and governance. Visa and Tencent named it as a pillar of their new MoU. In Europe, payment schemes formed a council so that the rules for how local payments work with agentic protocols are set from the start, not added later. The open question is who controls the payment credential and how much choice of payment method the consumer keeps when an agent pays. Global card networks and domestic schemes are now competing on that question.

At the same time, **stablecoins are becoming part of corporate payment infrastructure**. PhotonPay's platform combines multi-asset wallets, global money movement, foreign exchange, local clearing and card issuing through an API-first design. The new funding is meant to connect stablecoin liquidity with fiat networks for day-to-day corporate operations and cross-border settlement. In the US, BNY will support RLUSD and XRP on its digital asset custody platform following Ripple's charter approval.

For issuers, acquirers and BaaS providers, the practical result is a mixed stack. Agent-initiated payments, card rails, local account-to-account schemes and stablecoin settlement will increasingly sit side by side. Firms that can orchestrate across these rails, and manage fraud and compliance across them, are best placed. Flywire's network, for example, spans more than 240 countries, 140 currencies and over 1,200 local payment methods.

## 4. Company & Startup Spotlight

**PhotonPay** is a Hong Kong-based payments infrastructure firm building what it calls a stablecoin-centric "Financial OS" for businesses. It closed a $40 million Series C led by Blockchain Capital. The funding will expand a platform that connects stablecoin liquidity with global fiat networks. Card issuers and cross-border operators in APAC should watch it as a regional competitor and a possible partner. ([FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/paytech/photonpay-raises-40m-series-c-to-scale-its-stablecoin-centric-financial-os), sponsored content)

**Flywire** provides payments enablement and software for complex, cross-border receivables. Its integration with Liquidity Services runs via API inside the marketplace's customer portal, so buyers can pay in their own currency and preferred method. This deal is a reference case for embedded B2B collections at marketplace scale. ([Finextra](https://www.finextra.com/pressarticle/111512/liquidity-services-deploys-flywire-tech-for-domestic-and-cross-border-b2b-payments))

## 5. Regulatory & Policy Watch

- **US: stablecoin issuers inside the federal perimeter.** With the OCC's final approval, Ripple National Trust Bank will manage RLUSD reserves. RLUSD will still be issued by Ripple's NYDFS-regulated Standard Custody & Trust Company, with BNY as primary custodian. ([FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/regulatory-actions/ripple-secures-final-occ-authorisation-to-establish-ripple-national-trust-bank))

- **China: foreign networks deepen local ties after approval.** Visa's MoU with Tencent comes weeks after Visa won regulatory clearance to expand its operations in China. This shows how licensing decisions quickly turn into ecosystem partnerships. ([Finextra](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/48672/visa-and-tencent-ink-agentic-commerce-and-cross-border-payments-mou))
