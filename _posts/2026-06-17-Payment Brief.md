### 1. Nuvei to Acquire Payoneer in $2.75 Billion Cross-Border Payments Megadeal
- **CFOtech** · 2026-06-17
- **Summary**: Nuvei has agreed to acquire Payoneer for $2.75 billion in cash. The deal, approved by both boards, combines Nuvei's payment acceptance with Payoneer's cross-border payout network, creating a combined entity with over $3 billion in annual revenue and processing more than $500 billion in annual payment volume for 2.4 million customers .
- **Why It Matters**: This represents a major consolidation in the fintech space, creating a comprehensive platform for businesses to handle acceptance, payouts, treasury, and FX in over 190 countries. It signals a push toward providing a one-stop shop for global financial infrastructure, directly competing with legacy banks and other fintech aggregators .
- **URL**: [Nuvei agrees to buy Payoneer for USD $2.75 billion](https://cfotech.co.nz/story/nuvei-agrees-to-buy-payoneer-for-usd-2-75-billion)

### 4. dtcpay and BitGo Singapore Forge Regulated Digital Asset Payment Partnership
- **KuCoin** · 2026-06-17
- **Summary**: Singapore-licensed dtcpay has partnered with BitGo Singapore to build regulated infrastructure for digital asset payments across Asia-Pacific. The collaboration pairs dtcpay's merchant network with BitGo's institutional-grade custody and settlement tools, operating under Singapore's Major Payment Institution framework .
- **Why It Matters**: This establishes a fully regulated fiat-to-digital asset transaction channel from wallet to merchant settlement. By combining their MPI licenses, the partnership aims to bridge the gap between crypto wallets and everyday merchant transactions, though transaction volume in the next 12-18 months will be the key test .
