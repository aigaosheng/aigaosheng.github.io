**🛍️ Google Supercharges AI Shopping: From Conversational Search to “Agentic Checkout” and Store-Calling AI**

Google just raised the bar for online shopping — again. The tech giant announced a new wave of **AI-powered shopping features** that promise to make buying things online (and even offline) feel as natural as chatting with a friend. From conversational search to autonomous checkout and an AI that literally calls stores for you, Google is reimagining how we shop in 2025.

👉 [Read the full TechCrunch report here](https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/13/google-expands-ai-shopping-with-conversational-search-agentic-checkout-and-an-ai-that-calls-stores-for-you).

---

## 🧠 The Next Generation of AI Shopping

### 1. Conversational Shopping via Search

Google’s new **AI Mode** lets users ask natural-language questions like *“Show me cozy autumn sweaters under $50”* — and instantly get visual, text, and product results that feel more like a chat than a query.

### 2. “Agentic Checkout”

This new feature allows Google to **buy products on your behalf** once certain conditions are met — say, when a price drops below your set budget. The AI confirms details and permissions before completing the purchase via the merchant site and Google Pay.

### 3. AI That Calls Stores for You

Powered by **Google Duplex**, the AI can now **call physical stores** to check whether an item is in stock, confirm prices, or ask about promotions — all on your behalf. Retailers can opt out, but for users, this bridges the gap between online and offline shopping.

---

## 💡 Why It Matters

* **End-to-End Shopping Ecosystem**: Google is evolving from being a search tool to a full-fledged shopping assistant — guiding you from *“I want it”* to *“I’ve bought it”*.
* **Frictionless Experience**: Natural language, automatic price tracking, and autonomous checkout drastically cut clicks and browsing time.
* **Retail Implications**: Merchants gain new exposure — but may grow more dependent on Google’s ecosystem and face tighter control over pricing visibility. Sponsored listings are part of AI Mode from day one.
* **Offline-Online Bridge**: With store-calling, Google’s AI starts interacting directly with the real world — signaling a new era for digital assistants and local retail.

---

## 📊 Under the Hood

* Google’s **Shopping Graph** powers everything — boasting **50 billion product listings** with **2 billion updates per hour**, ensuring fresh, real-time data.
* **Agentic checkout** launches first in the U.S. with partners like Wayfair, Chewy, Quince, and select Shopify merchants.
* The **AI calling feature** is limited to certain U.S. categories (toys, beauty, electronics) with merchant opt-in to prevent call overload.
* **Privacy and transparency** remain open questions — especially around disclosure of sponsored listings and data collection in AI-assisted transactions.

---

## 📘 Glossary

* **Shopping Graph** — Google’s massive, constantly updating dataset that maps products, prices, merchants, and inventory across the web.
* **Agentic Checkout** — An AI-powered system that automatically completes purchases once predefined conditions are met (e.g., price drop).
* **Google Duplex** — Conversational AI tech that can make phone calls to businesses on a user’s behalf, now extended to retail inquiries.
* **Conversational Search** — A natural-language search experience where users talk to Google like a chatbot instead of typing keywords.
* **Sponsored Listings** — Paid placements within AI Mode results that promote certain products or merchants.

---

## 🧩 The Bigger Picture

Google’s latest upgrade cements its ambition to dominate **AI-driven commerce** — not just helping users *find* what they want, but *acting* on their behalf. It’s a glimpse into the future of retail: one where the line between assistant, buyer, and store is increasingly blurred.

For consumers, it means effortless discovery and purchase.
For retailers, it’s a wake-up call — to ensure their data, pricing, and visibility are ready for this new “AI-first” marketplace.
