### How AI Boosted Naver to Dominate Korea’s Search Market with Over 62% Share**

In a striking shift in South Korea’s internet landscape, **Naver — long considered the “Google of Korea” — reclaimed a dominant position in the local search market**, capturing roughly **62.86% of searches** as of 2025. This marks the first time since 2022 that the company has crossed the **60% threshold**, widening the gap with global rival Google, whose share dipped to about **29.55%**. ([Korea Times][1])

Industry analysts point to **Naver’s strategic rollout of AI-powered search tools** as a central factor behind this resurgence — a trend that illustrates how artificial intelligence is reshaping user expectations and competitive dynamics in search. ([Korea Times][1])

---

### **AI Features Driving the Surge**

Naver’s renewed momentum isn’t just a numbers game — it’s rooted in strategic feature development. Over the past year, the company has pushed an **AI-centric enhancement to its core search engine**, most notably through:

* **AI Briefing** — An AI-powered search feature that delivers up-to-date, summarized answers for complex queries, such as health or stock information, directly in search results. ([Korea Times][1])
* Expansion into **topic-specific AI results**, adding AI Briefings tailored to sectors like healthcare and financial data — an approach that users have responded positively to. ([Pulse][2])

By prioritizing not just speed but **reliability**, Naver aims to make the search experience more intuitive for users — particularly where accurate information matters most. ([Korea Times][1])

---

### **Market Impact: Widening the Gap with Google**

The latest figures reveal that Google’s search share in Korea continued to fall, while **smaller players like Microsoft’s Bing and Daum remain distant**, each holding just a few percentage points. ([Korea Times][1])

This trend suggests that **localized AI tools tailored to Korean user needs** — including language context, content preferences, and trusted data sources — can significantly shift loyalty away from global search giants.

---

### **What This Means for Users and Businesses**

Naver’s AI evolution signals more than just changing market shares:

* **Users benefit from more direct, context-aware answers** to their queries — moving beyond traditional link lists to curated insights that feel conversational and informative. ([Korea Times][1])
* **Brands and content creators may need to rethink search optimization**, as AI summaries and briefings could reduce the number of clicks to traditional results. ([Asiance, Corporate website][3])
* The growth underscores how **regionally focused AI strategies** can outperform global incumbents in localized markets.

As AI continues to expand its footprint in search, the competition may soon extend beyond Korea’s borders — especially if Naver’s localized AI innovations prove adaptable to broader audiences. ([LinkedIn][4])

---

### **Glossary**

* **Search Market Share:** The percentage of all search queries in a region handled by a particular search engine.
* **AI Briefing:** A feature that uses artificial intelligence to generate concise, context-aware summaries of search results for users.
* **Generative AI:** A type of AI that can produce content, summaries, and insights (like text or answers to questions) based on user inputs.
