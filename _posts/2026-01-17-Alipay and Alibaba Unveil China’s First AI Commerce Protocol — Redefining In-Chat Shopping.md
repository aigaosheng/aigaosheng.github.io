# **Alipay and Alibaba Unveil China’s First AI Commerce Protocol — Redefining In-Chat Shopping**

In a bold leap toward the future of digital commerce, **Alipay has launched China’s first open *Agentic Commerce Trust Protocol*** — a framework that empowers AI assistants to act on users’ behalf and complete transactions directly **inside conversational interfaces** like Alibaba’s Qwen AI app. This marks a fundamental shift in how users shop, pay, and interact with services online — moving from browsing and clicking to *talking and doing*. ([PaySpace Magazine][1])

---

## **AI Moves From Chatting to Transacting**

Imagine telling an AI “*Order me a matcha latte from a nearby café*” — and it not only finds the drink but compares prices, applies promotions, and checks out with your payment — all without leaving the chat. That is exactly what **Alipay’s new protocol enables**. Under this framework:

* **Qwen App** has become the **first platform** to adopt the protocol, integrating with **Taobao Instant Commerce** and **Alipay AI-enabled payments** to complete purchases within chatbot dialogs. ([PaySpace Magazine][1])
* Smart agents can connect to **multiple services** — from food delivery and travel booking to commerce platforms — **using a common technical language**, eliminating the need for multiple API integrations. ([PaySpace Magazine][1])
* Two payment modes are supported: instant checkout for quick purchases and delegated authorization, where predefined criteria allow AI to act autonomously on your behalf. ([PaySpace Magazine][1])

This innovation goes beyond traditional e-commerce plugins — it’s an **open standard** designed to be adopted across platforms, devices, and third-party AI agents. ([PaySpace Magazine][1])

---

## **Qwen App: The AI That Gets Things Done**

Alibaba has been aggressively expanding **Qwen App** from a conversational assistant into a **task-execution engine**. Instead of merely answering questions, the app can now:

* Order food and apply discounts
* Complete in-chat payments
* Book travel and compare options
* Handle multi-step tasks like planning itineraries — all from a single prompt. ([PYMNTS.com][2])

This reflects a broader trend across tech giants — from **OpenAI’s Instant Checkout** to agentic AI integrations in search and ecommerce — toward tools that **act on behalf of users** rather than just inform. ([Barron's][3])

Alibaba’s ecosystem — including **Taobao**, **Fliggy**, and **Amap** — is now tightly interconnected through this agentic AI strategy, allowing users to traverse commerce, payments, and services without traditional app switching. ([Yicai Global][4])

---

## **Why This Matters: A New Commerce Paradigm**

Here’s why the launch of this protocol is significant:

* **Consumer Convenience:** AI can handle complex tasks with simple language — ordering food, booking flights, or shopping without clicking through multiple screens. ([PYMNTS.com][2])
* **Developer Efficiency:** A unified protocol reduces integration complexity for merchants and service providers. ([PaySpace Magazine][1])
* **Security Standards:** Built-in trust and security mechanisms help maintain safe transactions across services. ([PaySpace Magazine][1])
* **Competitive Edge:** Alibaba and Alipay are reinforcing their positions in the global AI race, especially against rivals like Tencent and ByteDance, and in line with innovations from U.S. tech firms. ([Digital Commerce 360][5])

With AI now able to  *comprehend, decide, and act*, we’re witnessing **a fundamental transformation in how digital services are consumed and executed**.

---

## **Glossary: Key Terms Explained**

**Agentic Commerce:**
AI systems capable of performing actions on behalf of humans — not just generating responses, but executing tasks end-to-end.

**Protocol:**
A set of rules and standards that enable different software systems and platforms to communicate and work together efficiently.

**Instant Commerce:**
A service model that enables fast, real-time purchases — often with streamlined checkout and delivery.

**Delegated Authorization:**
A mode where users set preferences and spending criteria, allowing an AI to complete approved tasks autonomously.
