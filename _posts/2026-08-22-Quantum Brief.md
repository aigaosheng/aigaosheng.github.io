# Quantum Brief — 2026-08-22

## Top Stories 

### 1. **U.S. Researchers Demonstrate a 13-Mile Free-Space Quantum Network Link**

* **Source**: Brookhaven National Laboratory · August 21, 2026
* **Summary**: Researchers at Brookhaven National Laboratory and Stony Brook University demonstrated a free-space optical link transmitting quantum information across 13 miles (21 km). The system sends individual quantum states of light between rooftop facilities and is now moving toward experiments involving entangled photons.
* **Why It Matters**: The demonstration expands the architecture of quantum networks beyond fiber, opening a potential path toward hybrid fiber/free-space quantum infrastructure and longer-distance quantum communication.
* **URL**: [https://www.newswise.com/articles/brookhaven-and-stony-brook-researchers-demonstrate-wireless-capability-for-quantum-network2](https://www.newswise.com/articles/brookhaven-and-stony-brook-researchers-demonstrate-wireless-capability-for-quantum-network2)

### 2. **Frozen Optical Fiber Boosts Light–Sound Interaction by More Than 1,000×**

* **Source**: ScienceDaily / Max Planck Institute for the Science of Light · August 22, 2026
* **Summary**: Researchers found that freezing the liquid core of a specialized optical fiber at cryogenic temperatures dramatically strengthens interactions between light and hypersonic sound. The resulting platform enabled an optoacoustic memory demonstration and could support photonic neuromorphic computing, quantum information processing and precision sensing.
* **Why It Matters**: Extremely strong light–sound coupling could provide a new hardware primitive for low-energy photonic computing and quantum information systems, particularly where optical storage and signal processing need to coexist.
* **URL**: [https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/08/260821012228.htm](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/08/260821012228.htm)

### 3. **Quantum Business Leaders Need Literacy, Not “Quantum Washing”**

* **Source**: The Quantum Insider · August 22, 2026
* **Summary**: EDHEC Quantum Institute founding director Lionel Martellini argues that executives should understand quantum technology without overstating its near-term commercial capabilities. The discussion highlights the gap between proposed quantum-finance applications and practical industry problems, particularly during the current noisy intermediate-scale quantum era.
* **Why It Matters**: The message is increasingly relevant for corporate strategy and investment committees: quantum readiness should focus on credible use cases, workforce literacy and technology monitoring rather than premature claims of quantum advantage.
* **URL**: [https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/08/22/lionel-martellini-edhec-quantum-institute-why-business-leaders-need-quantum-awareness-not-quantum-washing/](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/08/22/lionel-martellini-edhec-quantum-institute-why-business-leaders-need-quantum-awareness-not-quantum-washing/)

### 4. **Philippines Builds National Quantum Capability Through Education and Supply Chains**

* **Source**: The Manila Times · August 22, 2026
* **Summary**: A five-day Quantum Roadshow Philippines concluded at the University of the Philippines Diliman, bringing together government, universities, researchers and industry around quantum engineering, education, supply chains and quantum communications. Organizers plan to use an education-gap analysis to inform a national white paper ahead of the country's second Quantum Information, Science and Technology Conference.
* **Why It Matters**: Quantum competition is increasingly becoming an ecosystem-building exercise rather than a race solely between processor manufacturers. Education, engineering talent, supply chains and communications infrastructure will determine which countries can participate meaningfully in the emerging quantum economy.

## Executive Takeaway

**Quantum momentum is increasingly shifting from isolated processor breakthroughs toward infrastructure, networking, photonics and ecosystem development.** Today's developments point to three strategic themes: connecting quantum systems over real-world networks, developing new photonic primitives for scalable hardware, and building the talent and organizational literacy required to distinguish credible quantum opportunities from hype.
