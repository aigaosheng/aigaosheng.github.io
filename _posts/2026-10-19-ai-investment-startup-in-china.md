# AI Investment & Startup in China Brief — 2026-10-19

> **Executive summary:** China is accelerating commercial adoption of embodied AI while tightening scrutiny of AI technology exports. Robotics and semiconductor firms attracted substantial funding, while DeepSeek expanded its developer ecosystem with model and retrieval tools.

## Top Stories

### 1. Meituan Leads Series D Financing for Collaborative Robot Maker FAIRINO

<small>TechNode · October 19, 2026</small>

**Bottom line:** Embodied AI and collaborative robotics developer FAIRINO secured nearly RMB 1 billion across Series C+ and D funding rounds led by Meituan to expand manufacturing and scale its AIRLab intelligence platform.

Chinese collaborative robot manufacturer FAIRINO completed its Series D round led by Meituan, following a Series C+ round led by Meituan's Long-Z fund, bringing total capital raised across both rounds to nearly RMB 1 billion. The startup plans to deploy the capital into expanding manufacturing capacity for core components, including its ART force-controlled robotic arms and AIRLab embodied-intelligence architecture. Co-investors in the financing included Yongxin Capital and Shishan Capital, supporting FAIRINO's global go-to-market strategy and ongoing industrial deployments.

**Why it matters:** Meituan's escalating direct investment into robotics underlines how China's tech giants are channeling venture capital into physical AI and automation to lock down supply chains across commercial services and industrial manufacturing.

### 6. DeepSeek Launches Web Search and Page-Fetching APIs for Autonomous AI Agents

<small>TechNode · October 19, 2026</small>

**Bottom line:** DeepSeek opened its proprietary search engine and web extraction infrastructure to third-party developers via low-cost Search and Fetch APIs designed for autonomous AI agents.

The new Search API delivers structured web results directly to agent workflows, while the Fetch API parses and formats arbitrary webpage contents into clean Markdown ready for LLM context processing. Both developer endpoints are competitively priced at RMB 5 ($0.70) per 1,000 successful requests, with zero charges for failed queries, empty responses, or timeouts. The service utilizes existing DeepSeek account credentials, allowing agent developers to seamlessly ground reasoning in live web data.

**Why it matters:** By offering inexpensive native retrieval and parsing tools, DeepSeek is building out an end-to-end developer ecosystem to capture the fast-growing market for agentic AI applications.

### 7. China Proposes Tighter Export Controls Covering 23 Advanced AI Technologies

<small>DIGITIMES · October 19, 2026</small>

**Bottom line:** Chinese commerce regulators proposed strict export licensing requirements and technology transfer bans covering 23 strategic artificial intelligence and algorithmic systems.

The draft policy aims to regulate the outbound flow of intellectual property and high-end algorithms as domestic tech corporations expand into international markets. Exporters of covered AI technologies—alongside select photovoltaic and biotechnology processes—will be mandated to obtain state approval and undergo comprehensive national security assessments before transferring technical assets or establishing overseas joint ventures. The proposed measures seek to safeguard domestic technical advantages while standardizing global expansion protocols.

**Why it matters:** Chinese AI startups pursuing international expansion will face elevated regulatory compliance thresholds, compelling founders to carefully ring-fence domestic core algorithms from overseas operational entities.
