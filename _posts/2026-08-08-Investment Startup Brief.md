### 2. **Sam Altman Highlights the Investment Value of Unpopular Ideas**

* **Source**: The Times of India · August 8, 2026
* **Summary**: Sam Altman described an investment philosophy shaped by Peter Thiel and Paul Graham: the strongest opportunities are often companies or ideas that are not yet widely accepted. Altman said his investment portfolio spans hundreds of startups, including early investments in companies such as Airbnb, Stripe and Reddit.
* **Why It Matters**: The comments reinforce a central venture-capital principle: differentiated insight and early conviction can matter more than following already-established market narratives. For founders, this suggests that defensible technology or unconventional market theses may attract capital even before mainstream validation.
* **URL**: [https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/technology/tech-news/sam-altman-who-has-invested-in-several-companies-including-stripe-and-airbnb-on-not-to-miss-investing-advice-billionaire-peter-thiel-gave-him/amp_articleshow/133045670.cms](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/technology/tech-news/sam-altman-who-has-invested-in-several-companies-including-stripe-and-airbnb-on-not-to-miss-investing-advice-billionaire-peter-thiel-gave-him/amp_articleshow/133045670.cms)
