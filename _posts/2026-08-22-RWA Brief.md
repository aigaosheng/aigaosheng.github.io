# RWA Brief — 2026-08-22

## Top Stories 

### 1. **Shinhan Asset Management Signs Solana MOU to Pilot a Won-Denominated Tokenized Fund**

* **Source**: Generation Infinity · August 22, 2026
* **Summary**: Shinhan Asset Management signed a four-party memorandum of understanding with the Solana Foundation, Etherfuse and Orca to pilot a Korean won-denominated tokenized fund. The proposed proof of concept would explore issuing and distributing the fund on Solana, bringing a major South Korean asset manager into a public-blockchain tokenization experiment.
* **Why It Matters**: The initiative is notable because it shifts RWA experimentation beyond crypto-native issuers toward a large conventional asset manager. If successful, it could become an important test of whether public blockchain infrastructure can support regulated fund products in Korea.
* **URL**: [https://genfinity.io/2026/08/22/shinhan-asset-management-solana-krw-tokenized-fund-mou/](https://genfinity.io/2026/08/22/shinhan-asset-management-solana-krw-tokenized-fund-mou/)

### 2. **Vietnam Explores How to Build a Competitive Tokenized Real-World Asset Market**

* **Source**: Vietnam Economic Times · August 22, 2026
* **Summary**: Industry participants outlined how Vietnam could develop a competitive market for tokenized real-world assets, emphasizing digitization, standardization, broader investor access, improved liquidity and lower transaction costs. The discussion points toward the need for market infrastructure and regulatory frameworks that connect tokenized assets to enforceable real-world rights.
* **Why It Matters**: Vietnam is emerging as a potentially important Southeast Asian RWA market. The strategic opportunity is not simply token issuance, but building legal, custody and market infrastructure capable of supporting assets from the country's real economy.
* **URL**: [https://en.vneconomy.vn/how-to-build-a-competitive-market-for-tokenized-real-world-assets-in-vietnam.htm](https://en.vneconomy.vn/how-to-build-a-competitive-market-for-tokenized-real-world-assets-in-vietnam.htm)

### 3. **Vietnam's Real-Economy Assets Highlight a New RWA Opportunity**

* **Source**: Vietnam Economic Times · August 22, 2026
* **Summary**: An industry interview highlighted Vietnam's potential to tokenize assets linked to manufacturing, agriculture, energy, real estate and carbon markets. The argument is that Vietnam could build digitally native tokenization infrastructure while its regulatory and market frameworks are still evolving, rather than retrofitting legacy financial systems.
* **Why It Matters**: The story underscores an important shift in the RWA market: growth opportunities are increasingly tied to country-specific asset pools and regulatory strategies, rather than simply launching another generic tokenization platform.
* **URL**: [https://en.vneconomy.vn/vietnams-potential-in-tokenized-real-world-assets.htm](https://en.vneconomy.vn/vietnams-potential-in-tokenized-real-world-assets.htm)

### 4. **Bitget Highlights Demand for Tokenized Access to Private Markets**

* **Source**: CoinGape · August 22, 2026
* **Summary**: A case study on Bitget's IPO Prime platform examines the use of tokenized structures to broaden access to selected pre-IPO opportunities and enable secondary trading after allocation. According to the report, the platform's first offerings attracted significant user participation and demonstrated demand for digital distribution of private-market exposure.
* **Why It Matters**: Private-market tokenization remains one of the more commercially compelling RWA use cases because it targets traditionally illiquid assets. The key question is whether token-based distribution can improve liquidity and accessibility without weakening investor protections or regulatory compliance.
* **URL**: [https://coingape.com/block-of-fame/case-studies/bitget-case-study-how-bitget-ipo-prime-is-bridging-private-markets-and-digital-assets/](https://coingape.com/block-of-fame/case-studies/bitget-case-study-how-bitget-ipo-prime-is-bridging-private-markets-and-digital-assets/)
