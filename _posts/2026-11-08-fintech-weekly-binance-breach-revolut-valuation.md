*Covering 2-8 November 2026. Sources: Finextra and FinTech Futures, plus linked primary sources where noted.*

## 1. Top Headlines

- **[Binance says hackers used fake job interviews and AI tools in $6.3bn hack](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/48694/binance-says-hackers-used-fake-job-interviews-and-ai-tools-in-63bn-hack)** (Finextra): Binance says the attack was a multi-stage campaign using fake job interviews, malicious software downloads and compromised AI tooling. **Takeaway:** attackers are now targeting engineers and internal AI tools, not just code.
- **[Revolut hits $115bn valuation to cement status as Europe's most valuable private firm](https://www.fintechfutures.com/digital-banking/revolut-hits-115bn-valuation)** (FinTech Futures): The valuation follows a secondary share sale, 12 months after the previous $75bn mark. **Takeaway:** investors are pricing Revolut as a full bank, not just a payments app.
- **[Goldman bans traders and bankers from using AI coding tools](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/48690/goldman-bans-traders-and-bankers-from-using-ai-coding-tools)** (Finextra): Goldman has barred traders and bankers from advanced AI coding tools over productivity and risk concerns. **Takeaway:** large banks are rationing AI access by role, a sign that controls are lagging adoption.
- **[OCC unveils supervisory framework for bank AI deployment](https://www.fintechfutures.com/regulations-compliance/occ-unveils-supervisory-framework-for-bank-ai-deployment)** (FinTech Futures): The US regulator issued a framework to help its examiners assess banks' use of AI and agentic AI. **Takeaway:** supervisors are now writing down how they will examine autonomous AI.
- **[Capgemini: Multi-agent AI moves into banking and insurance, but orchestration remains limited](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/48708/capgemini-multi-agent-ai-moves-into-banking-and-insurance-but-orchestration-remains-limited)** (Finextra): Only 10% of banks and insurers have enterprise-wide orchestration, though 59% are piloting or deploying multi-agent AI. **Takeaway:** the gap between pilots and scale is the industry's main AI problem.
- **[Canada and Europe to link real-time payment systems under new alliance](https://www.fintechfutures.com/instant-real-time-payments/canada-european-union-real-time-payments-alliance)** (FinTech Futures): Negotiations to connect the two instant payment systems will start over the coming months. **Takeaway:** a transatlantic instant-payment corridor could cut reliance on legacy cross-border routes.
- **[Gen Z agentic money app Frank exits stealth ahead of 2027 launch](https://www.fintechfutures.com/fintech-start-ups/gen-z-agentic-money-app-frank-exits-stealth-ahead-of-2027-launch)** (FinTech Futures): The London start-up is building an account, wallets, debit card, savings pots and an AI chat assistant for Gen Z. **Takeaway:** "agentic" is becoming the default pitch for new consumer finance apps.

## 2. In-Depth Highlight: The $6.3bn Binance Breach

Binance has published its first account of the attack. According to [Finextra](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/48694/binance-says-hackers-used-fake-job-interviews-and-ai-tools-in-63bn-hack), the hackers, presumed to be linked to North Korea, began in June with a supply chain attack. A Wallet DevOps engineer searching for an AI coding tool downloaded a tampered Homebrew installation. A second engineer was later lured through a fake recruiter and a coding exercise containing hidden malware, and the attackers then used an internal AI tool to gain control of two developers' systems, per [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/markets/binance-hack-fake-job-interview-heist-rcna607238). The theft is described as the biggest crypto theft on record, exceeding the $1.5bn taken from Bybit in February 2025 ([The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/money/2026/11/03/binance-says-fake-job-interviews-and-internal-ai-tool-used-in-63bn-heist/)). Binance says it will absorb the losses, has begun an independent cybersecurity review, and is setting up a security research fund. The key lesson is that the weak point was people and AI tooling, not the blockchain itself. No regulatory response appeared in this week's coverage, so that remains an open question.

## 3. Market & Industry Insight

**Agentic AI is moving faster than the controls around it.** Capgemini's research shows nearly half of financial firms cannot consistently demonstrate a return on AI, and 51% lack a framework for judging whether AI spending is justified. It also finds that executives prioritising outcomes over efficiency as their main AI funding criterion are expected to grow 141% by 2028. This week's other stories fit the same pattern: Goldman narrowed access, the OCC wrote an examination framework, and Binance showed how an internal AI tool can become an attack path.

**Tokenisation is moving into core market plumbing.** Eurex Clearing says it will integrate native digital instruments and become the first "hybrid CCP" ([Eurex press release](https://www.eurex.com/ec-en/find/news/Eurex-Clearing-to-become-the-world-s-first-Hybrid-CCP-5678036)). Broadridge plans on-chain governance and digital transfer agency services for the NYSE's tokenised securities platform, which launches only after regulatory approval ([Broadridge release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/broadridge-to-bring-onchain-governance-and-digital-transfer-agency-to-new-york-stock-exchange-tokenized-securities-platform-302925895.html)).

## 4. Company & Startup Spotlight

**Revolut** is a UK-founded digital bank offering payments, accounts, trading and crypto. It confirmed a $115bn valuation after completing a secondary share sale, following several banking licence wins including in the UK and Europe ([tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2026/11/04/revolut-confirms-115bn-valuation-after-completing-secondary-sale/)). A private valuation this size resets expectations for what a digital bank can be worth. Note that it is a private secondary-sale price, not a public-market valuation.

**Frank** is a London start-up founded in 2025 by former Mayne Capital managing director Derek Mayne. It is preparing a hard launch in 2027 and running a pilot first. It is worth watching as an early test of whether Gen Z will accept an AI-first money app.

## 5. Regulatory & Policy Watch

- **US OCC:** The framework came with OCC Bulletin 2026-54 and a 22-page document, and it sets no new supervisory expectations for banks. It groups sixteen components into six pillars: purpose, risk, control, oversight, resilience and assurance. The OCC's own wording calls AI "super intelligence" ([OCC release](https://occ.treas.gov/news-issuances/news-releases/2026/nr-occ-2026-97.html)).
- **Canada-EU:** Canada is set to adopt the Real-Time Rail, while the Eurosystem uses the ECB's TARGET Instant Payment Settlement (TIPS) system. No timeline has been disclosed.
