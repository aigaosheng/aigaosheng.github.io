# AI Research & Open-Source LLM Brief: OpenAI Launches GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol

**Today:** Only one qualifying release cleared this edition's verification, but it is a frontier-model launch that resets API pricing and shows how far vendor benchmark claims diverge from user experience.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **OpenAI Releases GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol, Cutting Astra's API Price 20%**

*VentureBeat · November 17, 2026 (2:33 pm PT, which is November 18 in Singapore)*

**Bottom line:** OpenAI's new GPT-6.2 pair posts higher scores on its own benchmark charts and lowers flagship pricing, but early independent results and power-user feedback are mixed.

OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol. Both are available in ChatGPT Work and Codex for Plus, Pro, Enterprise and Edu users, and through the API. VentureBeat reports that Astra is priced at $8 per million uncached input tokens and $40 per million output tokens, down from $10 and $50 for the previous Astra. Sol stays at $2 and $10. Both models offer a context window of about 1.05 million tokens and up to 128,000 output tokens.

Using OpenAI's launch charts, VentureBeat says Astra beat GPT-6 Astra on all nine head-to-head comparisons, for example 78.3% versus 73.1% on Agents' Last Exam. Sol beat its predecessor on eight of nine, with the exception of Vals Science Index. Artificial Analysis reportedly found Astra flat on its Codex coding-agent index and Sol three points lower. Several AI power users were critical, with some describing the models as uneven or hard to steer. Others praised them for tasks such as code review and motion graphics.

**Why it matters:** The scores come from OpenAI's own charts, so enterprise buyers should test on their own workloads before switching. The price cut also puts Astra level with Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.2, which VentureBeat says launched eight days earlier at $8 and $40. That increases pressure on regional and open-weight providers across Asia to compete on cost.

## Key Takeaways

- Frontier labs are competing on price as well as capability, and the new Astra pricing matches Claude Fable 5.2 at $8 and $40 per million tokens.
- Vendor-published benchmark gains do not always show up in independent or user testing, which makes in-house evaluation on real workloads important.
- Other launches this week were excluded from this edition. They were either published before the cut-off, already covered, or lacked a verifiable publication time.

## What to Watch

Watch for further independent evaluations, such as Artificial Analysis and similar leaderboards, to see whether Astra's coding-agent results move or the gains on OpenAI's own charts are confirmed. Also track whether OpenAI adjusts the models in response to the early user feedback.
