### 1. Binance adds U.S. stocks and signals a new tokenized shares push
- **Fortune** · 2026-06-01
- **Summary**: Binance launched access to more than 7,000 U.S. stocks and ETFs for eligible users outside the United States as part of its broader “super app” strategy. Fortune reported that Binance also plans to roll out tokenized shares through its forthcoming bStocks product, using Nest Trading for brokerage functions and Alpaca for custody, dividends, and corporate actions.
- **Why It Matters**: This is one of the largest crypto-exchange moves yet to merge traditional equities access with onchain distribution. If execution and regulation hold, it could materially expand global distribution for tokenized public-market assets.

### 2. Citi projects tokenized securities could reach $5.5 trillion by 2030
- **CoinDesk** · 2026-06-01
- **Summary**: Citi’s new “Tokenization 2030: Wall Street On-Chain” report projects a base-case $5.5 trillion tokenized securities market by 2030, with a range of $2.7 trillion to $8.2 trillion depending on adoption. The report highlights tokenized U.S. Treasuries and equities as the main growth engines and argues that clearer regulation, market-infrastructure adoption, and stablecoin settlement rails are moving tokenization from pilot stage into mainstream capital markets.
- **Why It Matters**: Large-bank forecasts help shape institutional budgets, product roadmaps, and board-level prioritization. Citi’s framing reinforces that tokenized assets are increasingly being viewed as core market infrastructure rather than a niche crypto vertical.
