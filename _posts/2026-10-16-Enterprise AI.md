# Enterprise AI Brief: ServiceNow Introduces AI Passport for Agent Governance

**Today:** Enterprise AI is moving beyond deploying agents to controlling their permissions, monitoring their actions and measuring their costs, making governance a core requirement for scaling adoption.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **ServiceNow Introduces AI Passport for Enterprise Agent Governance**

*Express Computer · October 16, 2026*

**Bottom line:** ServiceNow has introduced AI Passport, a system designed to verify agent permissions, constrain unauthorised actions and maintain auditable records of agent activity.

The product is designed to provide cryptographically signed credentials that validate which actions AI agents are authorised to perform. It is intended to work across first-party and third-party agents, with integrations planned for NVIDIA's Open Agent Safety Platform. ServiceNow also announced AI Cost Management capabilities and highlighted LTM Limited as an early adopter, reportedly deploying 1,500 AI agents.

**Why it matters:** Enterprises need controls that extend across agent frameworks rather than relying exclusively on individual model safeguards. For banks, insurers and other regulated businesses in Asia, identity, authorisation, revocation and auditability are important building blocks for expanding agent use into operational workflows. The report indicates that AI Passport is expected to launch in the fourth quarter of 2026.

## Key Takeaways

- **Agent governance is becoming an enterprise platform requirement.** Identity, least-privilege permissions, runtime enforcement and audit trails are essential considerations when scaling autonomous systems.
- **Controls must extend beyond individual models.** Organisations need consistent oversight across internally developed agents, third-party tools and enterprise applications.
- **AI economics require operational visibility.** Tracking agent activity and expenditure alongside business outcomes helps enterprises evaluate whether deployments deliver sustainable value.

## What to Watch

Watch for ServiceNow's expected fourth-quarter 2026 rollout of AI Passport and AI Cost Management, including evidence of cross-platform enforcement, adoption by regulated enterprises and measurable improvements in operational control.
