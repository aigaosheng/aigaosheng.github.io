# AI finance in Singapore Brief — 2026-10-05

**Today:** Singapore’s AI-finance landscape is increasingly about capital allocation and financial infrastructure, with AI build-out driving credit demand while digital assets and tokenisation push settlement and cash management toward 24/7 rails.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **GIC CEO says AI build-out is becoming a credit-market story**

*GIC · 5 October 2026*

**Bottom line:** GIC says AI infrastructure investment is increasingly being financed through debt, making technology exposure an increasingly important consideration for credit investors.

GIC CEO Lim Chow Kiat argued that government borrowing, AI infrastructure and energy-system investment are competing for the same pool of long-duration capital. He highlighted the growing role of debt in financing AI infrastructure and estimated that AI-related investment-grade issuance could add significant duration supply to credit markets.

**Why it matters:** The investment implications extend beyond AI equities: banks, bond investors and asset managers need to assess how AI capex affects credit quality, financing costs and long-duration capital allocation.

### 2. 💳 **Payward and Singapore Gulf Bank enable 24/7 institutional digital-asset settlement**

*Singapore Gulf Bank · 5 October 2026*

**Bottom line:** Payward has integrated Singapore Gulf Bank’s real-time clearing network to enable qualifying institutional digital-asset transactions to settle around the clock.

The partnership connects Payward’s institutional platform with SGB Net, initially supporting US-dollar settlement for selected clients in Asia and the Gulf region. Singapore Gulf Bank will also onboard Kraken Prime as an additional source of digital-asset liquidity.

**Why it matters:** Always-on settlement reduces the friction created by conventional banking cut-off times and points toward a financial infrastructure model where digital-asset markets operate more like continuous institutional markets.

### 3. 🌐 **Maybank expands distribution of a tokenised Singapore-dollar money-market fund**

*Asia Asset Management · 5 October 2026*

**Bottom line:** Maybank Asset Management Singapore is expanding access to its tokenised Singapore-dollar money-market fund through Singapore-based Synthesys Network.

The tokenised share class is now accessible through more than 80 distribution channels globally, including banks, payment providers, fintech firms and other financial institutions. The product is designed to let Singapore-dollar payment platforms and brokers put otherwise idle balances to work while retaining exposure to a money-market fund.

**Why it matters:** Tokenised cash-management products are moving closer to practical financial infrastructure, potentially connecting payments, treasury and investment markets through programmable distribution rails.

### 4. 🤖 **Singapore’s AI ecosystem enters a new phase of finance-focused capital allocation**

*GIC · 5 October 2026*

**Bottom line:** Singapore’s sovereign investor is framing AI not simply as a technology theme but as a cross-asset capital-allocation problem spanning equities, credit, infrastructure and energy.

GIC’s latest investment outlook argues that the AI build-out requires trillions of dollars of spending across compute, power and grid infrastructure, while outcomes are becoming increasingly uneven across the technology value chain. The firm says it is therefore assessing individual assets rather than treating AI as a single broad investment theme.

**Why it matters:** For Singapore’s investment ecosystem, the shift signals a more mature AI-finance thesis: returns increasingly depend on identifying infrastructure bottlenecks, financing structures and durable economic moats rather than simply owning headline AI companies.
