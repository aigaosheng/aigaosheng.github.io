# Quantum Brief: KT And LG Test Quantum Data Links

**Today:** A South Korean trial moves quantum communications beyond key exchange to transmitting data itself, while practical network use remains a development goal.

## Top Stories

### 1. ⚛️ **KT And LG Demonstrate Quantum Direct Communication**

*Yonhap News Agency · November 20, 2026*

**Bottom line:** KT and LG Electronics report transmitting and restoring image and text data using a quantum direct communication system.

The companies encoded 1,024 bits of image data into quantum signals and reconstructed the image at the receiver; they also demonstrated text transmission. Unlike quantum key distribution, which shares encryption keys, quantum secure direct communication (QSDC) carries the information itself in quantum states. The companies plan further development and testing for use over fibre networks.

**Why it matters:** The trial is a step toward a broader range of quantum network applications in Asia, but it does not establish that QSDC is ready for commercial deployment. For financial institutions and payment providers, network testing and system performance will be important signals of whether the approach can support practical use.

## Key Takeaways

- QSDC extends quantum communication research from distributing encryption keys to transmitting data in quantum states.
- KT and LG’s demonstration is a laboratory milestone; the companies still plan to validate the technology in fibre networks.
- Enterprise interest should track performance and deployment evidence as the technology moves toward network trials.

## What to Watch

Watch for KT and LG’s planned fibre network validation and updates on transmission speed, stability and system architecture.
