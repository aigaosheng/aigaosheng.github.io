## Market Insight

### AI Infrastructure and Enterprise Security Remain Core Venture Themes

Investment activity on 2026-08-03 continues to show a clear concentration around AI infrastructure, cybersecurity, and enterprise technology. Large funding rounds are increasingly targeting companies that provide foundational capabilities — including AI chips, autonomous security systems, and enterprise AI platforms — rather than consumer-facing AI applications. ([The Wall Street Journal][1])

Institutional investors are also showing renewed willingness to allocate capital into venture funds, particularly in regions such as South Korea and Southeast Asia where governments and pension institutions are supporting startup ecosystems. ([KED Global][4])

## Investor Takeaways

* AI infrastructure remains the strongest capital magnet across venture markets.
* Cybersecurity is becoming a strategic AI investment category.
* Founder quality and long-term commitment are becoming more important diligence factors.
* Institutional investors are gradually returning to venture allocation after a period of caution.
* Emerging markets, especially Asia and Latin America, are seeing renewed startup investment momentum.
