# US AI vs China AI Brief — 2026-09-25

**Today:** The Trump-Xi summit put AI cooperation and safety dialogue on the table, but Washington and Beijing remain divided over guardrails, strategic competition and technology controls.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **Xi calls for deeper US-China cooperation on AI**

*Xinhua / State Council of China · September 25, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Xi Jinping said China and the US have more reasons to cooperate than compete on AI and should jointly address its risks and misuse.

Xi said both countries should leverage their respective AI strengths rather than guard against one another. He called for continued dialogue on AI risks and benefits, cooperation against misuse and abuse, and maintaining human control over AI technologies.

**Why it matters:** The statement establishes AI governance and safety as an explicit area for bilateral engagement even as the two countries remain strategic competitors in advanced technology.

### 2. 🏦 **Trump and Xi show different approaches to AI guardrails**

*Yonhap News Agency · September 25, 2026*

**Bottom line:** The US and China agreed on the need for AI dialogue while presenting materially different positions on how much regulatory control advanced AI should face.

Xi stressed that AI development should remain under human control, while Trump has rejected broader international AI restrictions and argued that existing US institutions provide sufficient safeguards. The contrast emerged during their White House summit amid growing debate over frontier-model safety.

**Why it matters:** The AI race is becoming a competition not only over models and compute, but also over competing governance philosophies that could shape how frontier AI is developed and deployed.

### 3. 🤖 **US and Chinese leaders keep AI dialogue open despite strategic rivalry**

*Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the People's Republic of China · September 25, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Trump and Xi agreed that the US and China should maintain dialogue and strengthen cooperation on AI despite continuing competition.

The official readout says Trump described AI as important to humanity's future and supported continued dialogue and cooperation. Xi separately proposed exchanges on AI risks and benefits and joint efforts to prevent misuse.

**Why it matters:** Maintaining a direct government-to-government channel could provide a mechanism for discussing AI incidents and safety risks even when broader technology competition remains intense.

### 5. 🔒 **US-China AI dialogue faces unresolved differences over safety**

*MLex · September 25, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Xi's proposal for AI cooperation contrasts with the Trump administration's preference for continued development with limited additional international constraints.

Xi called for dialogue on AI risks and benefits and joint action against misuse, while Trump has framed AI primarily through competition and development. The two positions leave substantial questions around common standards, incident reporting and frontier-model safeguards.

**Why it matters:** A bilateral dialogue can create channels for crisis communication, but meaningful technical or regulatory alignment will depend on whether the two sides can reconcile fundamentally different approaches to AI governance.
