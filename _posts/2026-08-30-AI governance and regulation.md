# AI governance and regulation Brief — 2026-08-30

## Top Stories 

### 1. **China’s AI Policy Approach Seeks Earlier Guardrails Without Repeating Past Tech Crackdowns**

* **Source**: Mint · 2026-08-30
* **Summary**: China is developing AI policies earlier in the technology lifecycle, seeking to manage potential social and economic risks before they become systemic problems. The approach reflects lessons from Beijing’s earlier technology-sector crackdowns, while attempting to preserve AI investment and development momentum. The emerging model illustrates how China is combining proactive regulation with industrial-policy objectives rather than waiting for market failures to trigger intervention.
* **Why It Matters**: China is increasingly establishing a distinct AI governance model that combines regulatory intervention, strategic industrial development and state oversight. For multinational AI companies, the divergence between Chinese, European and US approaches increases the need for jurisdiction-specific governance, deployment controls and regulatory intelligence.
