# AI Fintech Brief: BMO Deploys Agentic AI for Credit Card Support

**Today:** Two fresh deployments show where banks and insurers are actually putting agentic AI to work right now: not in customer-facing chat, but in the unglamorous back office of call-centre triage and policy renewal tracking.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **BMO Rolls Out Agentic AI Tool for Credit Card Contact Centre**

*BMO Newsroom · November 6, 2026*

**Bottom line:** BMO is giving employees at its Virtual Connect contact centre an agentic AI tool that automatically surfaces transaction data and policy guidance, targeting 20-30% faster resolution of credit card inquiries.

BMO's Virtual Connect centre fields more than 500,000 Canadian client calls a year about credit card transactions, each currently averaging 12-14 minutes to resolve. The new "AI Agent Assist" tool performs AI-enabled transaction matching, policy retrieval and case preparation for staff to review, and BMO expects it to automatically process around 40% of eligible refund requests, with qualifying clients receiving credit the same day. The bank describes the design as "human-in-the-loop," with employees retaining accountability for final servicing decisions.

**Why it matters:** BMO's approach reflects a broader pattern among North American banks of augmenting staff rather than exposing customers directly to autonomous agents, a cautious model that banks in Hong Kong, Australia and other Asian markets weighing similar contact-centre AI are likely to study closely given regulators' continued emphasis on human oversight of high-volume consumer decisions.

### 2. 💳 **InsurGrid Launches AI Policy Change Monitoring for P&C Agencies**

*PR Newswire · November 6, 2026*

**Bottom line:** InsurGrid's new AI tool re-reads client policy documents roughly every 15 days to automatically flag cancellations, non-renewals and material coverage changes for property-and-casualty insurance agencies.

The feature uses an AI extraction engine to convert refreshed declaration pages into structured, review-ready data, alerting agencies when a premium rises, a coverage limit changes, or a policy lapses before a client shops elsewhere. It is rolling out to agencies on InsurGrid's existing subscription plan, aimed at helping independent P&C producers catch retention risks earlier.

**Why it matters:** The launch shows agentic AI spreading beyond headline underwriting and claims use cases into the more routine mechanics of policy retention, a segment relevant to insurers and brokers across Asia's fragmented agency-led distribution channels looking for low-cost efficiency gains.

## Key Takeaways

- Both deployments keep humans in the approval loop rather than granting AI agents direct authority over money movement or policy decisions, consistent with the industry's continued caution on autonomous agents.
- Vendors are attaching concrete efficiency targets to AI launches (20-30% faster call resolution, automated refunds, 15-day policy refresh cycles), signalling that ROI measurement is now a design requirement, not an afterthought.
- AI adoption is broadening past high-profile bank chatbots into narrower, operational back-office workflows such as contact-centre support and insurance renewal tracking.

## What to Watch

Watch for BMO to report actual progress against its 20-30% resolution-time and 40% refund-automation targets in future disclosures, and for whether other P&C agencies adopt similar AI-driven renewal-monitoring tools as InsurGrid scales its rollout.
