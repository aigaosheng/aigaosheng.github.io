## Strategic Takeaways

* **The AI race is moving from model performance to ecosystem dominance**: chips, open-source communities, robotics, cloud infrastructure, and deployment networks are becoming equally important.
* **China’s strategy increasingly emphasizes efficiency and open models**, while the U.S. maintains advantages in frontier research, compute infrastructure, and leading AI companies.
* **Geopolitical fragmentation is accelerating**: AI companies may increasingly need separate strategies for U.S., Chinese, and global markets.
* **Open-weight AI has become a strategic issue**: it is simultaneously viewed as a driver of innovation and a potential national security challenge.
