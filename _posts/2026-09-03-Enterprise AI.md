## Executive Takeaway

**Enterprise AI is moving from "AI adoption" to "AI operations."** Today's developments point to a common architecture: agents need access to real enterprise data, the ability to execute through existing systems, and centralized controls for identity, governance, security, and cost.

The competitive advantage is therefore shifting beyond foundation models toward the **enterprise control plane**—the infrastructure that makes autonomous AI reliable, governable, observable, and economically viable at scale.
