## This Week's Deals

### Tenstorrent — AI Chips and Systems

- **Region:** US
- **Round:** Series E, $1.5B
- **Investors:** Trace Capital and Innovation X (leads); Cadence Ventures, Eclipse, Maverick Silicon, Sandisk and Tether Investments participating
- **Valuation:** $6.2B (post-money)
- **Source:** [Tenstorrent release via Kyodo News PR Wire](https://kyodonewsprwire.jp/release/202610218369)
- **Notes:** Announced October 19 by the Jim Keller-led chip company. The valuation is more than double the roughly $2.6B set at its December 2024 Series D, and proceeds will fund volume manufacturing and Galaxy-system deployment.

### Flapping Airplanes — Foundational AI

- **Region:** US
- **Round:** Series A, $1B
- **Investors:** Index Ventures and Lux Capital (co-leads); Sequoia, GV, Kleiner Perkins, Valor, Atreides, A* and Nvidia
- **Valuation:** About $6B (reported by Bloomberg and TechCrunch)
- **Source:** [Index Ventures](https://www.indexventures.com/perspectives/toward-more-efficient-ai-leading-flapping-airplanes-1b-series-a/)
- **Notes:** Announced October 21, nine months after a $180M seed at a $1.5B valuation. The year-old San Francisco lab is working on models that need less data and compute, and press coverage says it has no product yet. Index partner Mark Xu joins the board.

### ASI — AI for Critical Infrastructure

- **Region:** US
- **Round:** Series C, $325M
- **Investors:** Spark Capital (lead); Andreessen Horowitz, Craft Ventures, Meritech Capital and affiliates of Citadel Securities
- **Valuation:** $3B
- **Source:** [The Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/10/20/business/asi-unicorn-ai-funding-transportation/)
- **Notes:** Announced October 20 by the Boston company, formerly Air Space Intelligence. It builds software that models and operates air traffic, energy grids and defense operations.

### Xona Space Systems — Satellite Navigation

- **Region:** US
- **Round:** Series D, $250M
- **Investors:** Future Ventures (lead); new and existing investors
- **Valuation:** $2.45B
- **Source:** [SpaceNews](https://spacenews.com/navigation-startup-xona-raises-250-million-as-it-begins-satellite-deployment/)
- **Notes:** Announced October 22, after the company launched six satellites this month. It is building a commercial low-Earth-orbit alternative to GPS and plans to start beta service in early 2027. The round follows a $170M Series C announced in March.

### Pigment — Enterprise Planning Software

- **Region:** Europe
- **Round:** Series E, $250M
- **Investors:** Scaleup Europe Fund, managed by EQT (lead); World Innovation Lab, Greenoaks, ICONIQ, IVP and Meritech; Nvidia plans to invest
- **Valuation:** $2.3B
- **Source:** [Sifted](https://sifted.eu/articles/pigment-series-e-nvidia-scaleup-europe-fund)
- **Notes:** The Paris-based planning platform announced the round on October 21. The company says it has passed $100M in ARR, growing 2x year over year.

### Arda — AI for Manufacturing

- **Region:** US
- **Round:** Series A, $160M (founder's figure; $232M total funding including a $72M seed)
- **Investors:** Altimeter Capital, Accel and Khosla Ventures (co-leads); XYZ Venture Capital, 8VC, SV Angel and Felicis
- **Valuation:** $1.5B
- **Source:** [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/arda-bob-mcgrew-232m-1-5bn-ai-manufacturing)
- **Notes:** Founded by former OpenAI research chief Bob McGrew, Arda came out of stealth on October 20 with software that turns product designs into working assembly lines. Anduril is its design partner. CNBC reported the raise as $230M, so the figure depends on whether the seed is counted.

### Eka Robotics — Robotics

- **Region:** US
- **Round:** Series A, $150M
- **Investors:** a16z and F-Prime (co-leads); Obvious Ventures and E14 Fund (returning seed leads)
- **Valuation:** $1.45B (post-money, as reported by TechCrunch)
- **Source:** [a16z portfolio page](https://jobs.a16z.com/company/eka-robotics)
- **Notes:** The Cambridge, Massachusetts startup pairs a force-sensitive gripper with a new deep-learning model for dexterous manipulation. a16z's own write-up says sustained performance across full production shifts has yet to be shown.

### Neolix — Autonomous Delivery

- **Region:** Asia
- **Round:** Series E, nearly $400M
- **Investors:** Boyu Capital (lead, alongside a national-level fund per Chinese reports); Alibaba's AEF NextGen Fund, GGV Capital, Vertex Growth and GAC Capital
- **Valuation:** Undisclosed
- **Source:** [TechNode](https://technode.com/2026/10/20/autonomous-delivery-vehicle-maker-neolix-raises-nearly-400-million-in-series-e-funding/)
- **Notes:** The Beijing-based driverless delivery-van maker announced the round on October 20 to fund overseas expansion. It follows a Series D of more than $600M about a year earlier and takes total funding past $1.4B.

## Trends & Analysis

AI compute and efficiency took the largest checks. Tenstorrent and Flapping Airplanes together raised $2.5B, more than half of the roughly $4.3B that Crunchbase's U.S. top-10 list adds up to for October 17-23. The two bets differ: Tenstorrent sells chips and systems and has begun shipping servers, while Flapping Airplanes is a research lab pricing an efficiency thesis. Flapping's valuation quadrupled in nine months with no product on the market, and Tenstorrent's more than doubled in under two years. That makes execution against deployment milestones the key risk for both.

Physical and industrial AI was the second theme. ASI, Arda, Eka, Xona and Neolix all build systems that operate in the real world, whether airspace, factory lines, robotic hands, orbit or city streets. Crunchbase's list also included Warhound, a $200M seed round for a startup breeding working dogs for the military. Defense and government demand shows up as a customer base in several of these deals, and the commercial proof points are still ahead. Xona's consumer service is slated for 2027, and Eka's production reliability is unproven.

The investor landscape also moved. On October 22 a16z renamed its crypto unit a16z Frontier and widened its mandate to AI and robotics. The same firm co-led Eka and joined ASI this week. In Europe, the EU-backed Scaleup Europe Fund led Pigment's round, and Seaya said it raised €600M for a new fund, per Bloomberg. Bloomberg also reported that DeepSeek raised about $15B led by CATL and Tencent. We excluded that from the deal list because the company has not confirmed it and the terms may still change.

## Actionable Insights

- **Founders in AI infrastructure:** Expect investors to pay for a clear efficiency or cost-per-inference thesis, but also to ask for measurable benchmarks as rounds step up quickly.
- **Founders in physical AI:** Lead with named design partners and deployed units. Arda's Anduril relationship and ASI's government-facing work are what support their prices.
- **Investors:** Track milestone delivery over valuation headlines, such as Tenstorrent's Galaxy deployments and Xona's early-2027 beta. These show whether the large step-ups hold.
- **Everyone:** Watch strategic and sovereign capital. Nvidia appears in both Flapping Airplanes and Pigment, and state-backed or industrial investors are anchoring large rounds in Europe and China.
- **Analysts:** Reconcile figures before publishing. This week sources disagreed on Arda ($160M versus $230M) and Whop ($200M versus $205M), so check against the company announcement.
