## Top Stories

### 1. Q2 launches Q2 Assistant to embed AI agents across banking operations
- **Q2 Holdings** · 2026-06-02
- **Summary**: Q2 unveiled Q2 Assistant, a unified AI layer embedded across its banking software stack for banks, credit unions, alternative finance providers, and fintechs. The product connects staff to specialized agents inside existing workflows and emphasizes regulated-institution controls including data isolation, audit logging, encryption, and human oversight for consequential actions. Initial deployment starts with a customer-care agent aimed at speeding issue resolution in digital banking support.
- **Why It Matters**: This is a concrete example of AI moving from pilot mode into core banking operations. For financial institutions, the differentiator is not just automation, but workflow-native AI with governance controls that can satisfy compliance and operational risk requirements.

### 2. Aveni raises £12m to monitor AI-agent conduct risk in regulated financial services
- **Prolific North** · 2026-06-04
- **Summary**: Edinburgh-based Aveni secured £12 million in fresh funding to accelerate its Unified Assurance Platform and launch Agent Assure and Agent Approve, products built to monitor and approve AI agents used in regulated customer interactions. Backers include PXN Ventures, Puma Growth Partners, Lloyds Banking Group, Nationwide, and Scottish Enterprise. The company positions the platform as a response to the widening governance gap as firms push agentic AI into live financial-services environments.
- **Why It Matters**: As banks and wealth firms move toward customer-facing AI agents, assurance tooling is becoming a strategic control layer rather than a nice-to-have. Funding for Aveni signals growing investor conviction that AI governance and conduct monitoring will be a meaningful fintech category.
