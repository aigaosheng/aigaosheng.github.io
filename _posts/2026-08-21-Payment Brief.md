### 1. **Liquid Group and PagBrasil Launch RoamQR-Pix Cross-Border QR Payments in Brazil**

* **Source**: The Manila Times / PR Newswire · 2026-08-21
* **Summary**: Liquid Group and PagBrasil announced the launch of RoamQR-Pix, linking participating RoamQR-enabled wallets in Asia with Brazil's Pix payment infrastructure. The initial rollout enables cross-border QR payments, allowing users to pay Brazilian merchants through participating digital wallets.
* **Why It Matters**: The development expands the interoperability of national real-time payment systems and highlights the growing strategic importance of QR-based cross-border payments, particularly between Asia and Latin America.

### 2. **Natural Secures $100 Million Facility to Scale Agentic Payments**

* **Source**: FinTech Futures · 2026-08-21
* **Summary**: Agentic payments start-up Natural has arranged a credit facility of up to $100 million with Upper90 Capital Management. The financing is intended to support technology that enables AI agents to hold and transact with real money, following the company's earlier $30 million Series A.
* **Why It Matters**: The transaction shows that agentic commerce is beginning to attract dedicated financial infrastructure and capital. As AI agents move from recommendation and workflow automation into purchasing and transaction execution, payments infrastructure will become a critical competitive layer.

### 4. **Thailand Links PromptPay and Alipay for Chinese Tourist Payments**

* **Source**: Thai Times · 2026-08-21
* **Summary**: Thailand has launched a cross-border QR payment linkage allowing Chinese visitors to use Alipay-compatible wallets to pay merchants through Thailand's PromptPay QR network. The initiative involves the Bank of Thailand, National ITMX, Ant International and Krungthai Bank.
* **Why It Matters**: The linkage demonstrates how domestic instant-payment rails are increasingly being connected internationally rather than replaced by global card networks. Tourism is becoming an important proving ground for interoperable national payment systems.
* **URL**: [https://thaitimes.com/thailand-links-promptpay-with-china-s-alipay-for-seamless-qr-payments-from-chinese-tourists](https://thaitimes.com/thailand-links-promptpay-with-china-s-alipay-for-seamless-qr-payments-from-chinese-tourists)

### 5. **Danal Joins Open USD Stablecoin Ecosystem**
