### **SAP accelerates enterprise AI strategy with multiple acquisitions**

**Source:** PR Newswire — May 4, 2026
**Summary:** SAP announced plans to acquire Dremio to unify SAP and non-SAP data for real-time analytics and agentic AI workloads. In parallel, SAP is also acquiring Prior Labs to strengthen causal AI capabilities and establish a frontier AI lab in Europe. **Why It Matters:** Data integration remains the bottleneck for enterprise AI. SAP’s strategy signals a shift toward **data-centric AI platforms** that enable agentic and real-time decision systems at scale.
