### 1. Cohere acquires Aleph Alpha in major European AI consolidation move

**Source + Date:** Reuters — April 24, 2026
**Summary:** Cohere has acquired German AI startup Aleph Alpha to expand its enterprise and government AI footprint in Europe. The deal is supported by a $600M investment from Schwarz Group into Cohere’s upcoming funding round. The combined entity focuses on “sovereign AI” infrastructure for regulated industries like finance, defense, and healthcare.
**Why it matters:** This marks a major **AI consolidation wave in Europe**, signaling that sovereign AI infrastructure is becoming a strategic investment category.
