## Investor Takeaway

The strongest signal today is **capital moving deeper into infrastructure and specialized verticals rather than simply chasing generic consumer apps**. AI-related investment remains prominent, but today's transactions span data-center networking, autonomous transportation, enterprise semantic infrastructure and healthcare.

At the ecosystem level, **South Korea's record first-half venture investment** stands out as a broader market signal: Asian venture capital is showing signs of renewed capacity, while India continues to attract substantial private-equity and startup capital. For investors, the emerging opportunity set is increasingly defined by infrastructure bottlenecks, vertical AI, healthcare delivery and platforms built around existing distribution networks.
