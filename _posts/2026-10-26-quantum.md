# Quantum Brief: Quantum Stocks Slide on Dilution and Rate Fears

**Today:** Read this if you track quantum vendors' funding health, quantum-safe migration, or Southeast Asia's quantum build-out. Pure-play stocks are under pressure, and a Vietnamese bank is backing the country's first machine.

## Top Stories

### 1. 📊 **Quantum Pure-Plays Fall Again as Dilution and Yield Worries Weigh**
*TipRanks · October 26, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Investors are pricing quantum pure-plays on funding risk rather than technical milestones, and November earnings are the next test.

In US pre-market trading on Monday, IonQ fell about 1.95%, Rigetti 1.48%, Quantinuum 1.18%, D-Wave 1.03% and Quantum Computing Inc. 0.91%. Year to date, D-Wave is down 55.41%, Rigetti 47.99%, Quantinuum 39.55%, QCi 36.06% and IonQ 30.31%. TipRanks points to IonQ's USD 2 billion equity offering, announced October 14, which followed its planned all-stock acquisition of cybersecurity firm Elisity worth up to USD 1.3 billion. It also cites a 10-year US Treasury yield near 5%, which weighs on stocks whose payoff is years away.

**Why it matters:** Vendor balance sheets are now a procurement factor. Enterprises and banks buying quantum access or quantum-safe tooling should weigh supplier dilution and runway alongside their roadmaps.

### 2. ⚛️ **Quandela and Quanova to Deploy Vietnam's First Quantum Computer, Backed by Nam A Bank**
*DCD · October 26, 2026*

**Bottom line:** A Vietnamese commercial bank is funding national quantum capacity, an early sign of Southeast Asian financial institutions investing in quantum skills.

French photonic quantum firm Quandela has partnered with local startup Quanova to install a MosaiQ photonic system at Quang Trung University in Quy Nhơn, Gia Lai, with support from Nam A Bank. The machine will anchor the university's quantum research, innovation, service and entrepreneurship ("quantum-RISE") programme. The programme also includes faculty training, curriculum development and access to Quandela's software tools. Quanova focuses on quantum education, readiness consulting and ecosystem building.

**Why it matters:** For fintechs operating in Vietnam, this points to a growing local quantum talent pool. It also suggests banks there may start treating quantum readiness as a strategic investment rather than only an IT cost.

### 3. 🔒 **Fortinet Executive Urges Cryptographic Audits Now, Ahead of Any "Q-Day"**
*TechRadar · October 26, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Opinion: the "harvest now, decrypt later" threat means long-lived sensitive data is already at risk, so migration planning cannot wait for a firm Q-Day date.

In this opinion piece, Dave Spillane, a Systems and Engineering Director at Fortinet, argues that the starting point is a cryptographic audit. That means finding which data needs long-term confidentiality and which algorithms currently protect it. He positions NIST's post-quantum standards as the baseline for migration. He sees quantum key distribution as a niche option for high-value links, because it needs specialised infrastructure. He recommends that organisations map exposure, test alternatives and set migration priorities now.

**Why it matters:** For payments firms holding card, KYC and transaction data with long retention periods, the inventory-first approach is a practical first step. This is vendor commentary, not regulatory guidance, so any migration timelines should be checked against your regulator's actual requirements.

### 4. ⚛️ **QClairvoyance to Build Quantum Software and QKD Labs at India's Ramdeobaba University**
*The Quantum Insider · October 26, 2026*

**Bottom line:** India's quantum push is reaching second-tier campuses, with hands-on quantum-safe communication hardware as well as software.

Ramdeobaba University in Nagpur and Secunderabad-based startup QClairvoyance Quantum Labs signed an MoU to set up two labs. One is a quantum software lab using QClairvoyance's browser-based QClair-Edu platform. The other is a communication hardware lab with quantum key distribution (QKD) and quantum random number generator (QRNG) kits. QClairvoyance will place two PhD- or postdoc-qualified professionals at the university for 12 months. It will also run a one-week faculty development programme and offer internships.

**Why it matters:** Talent, not hardware, is the binding constraint on quantum-safe migration across Asia. Programmes like this widen the pipeline of engineers who understand QKD and quantum randomness, skills that regional banks and payment networks will need.

## Key Takeaways
- Capital markets are punishing dilution across the whole quantum sector, so one firm's raise moves every pure-play's stock.
- Asia's quantum build-out is moving through partnerships with universities and banks rather than large national machines.
- Quantum-safe messaging is shifting from "when is Q-Day" to "what have you inventoried", which favours early, audit-led programmes.

## What to Watch
The US Federal Reserve rate decision on October 28 is a near-term driver for rate-sensitive quantum stocks. Q3 results from the quantum pure-plays, due in early November, will show whether bookings justify recent capital raises.
