## Key Themes & Insights

* **Proactive governance is accelerating:** Pre-deployment testing and risk validation are becoming standard practice.
* **Regulatory enforcement is tightening:** Authorities are moving beyond guidelines to active supervision.
* **Enterprise readiness gap persists:** Governance, security, and compliance lag behind AI adoption.
* **Agentic AI introduces new risks:** Non-human identities require new IAM and audit frameworks.
* **Global fragmentation is rising:** Diverging regulations increase compliance complexity for multinational firms.
