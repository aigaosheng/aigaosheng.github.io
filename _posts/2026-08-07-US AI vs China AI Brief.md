## Executive Takeaway

The U.S.–China AI competition continues to shift from model performance alone toward control of the entire AI value chain. Today's developments highlight four converging fronts:

* **Semiconductor enforcement**, as Washington investigates potential export-control circumvention.
* **Industrial competitiveness**, with AI hardware increasingly driving China's exports and economic growth.
* **Capital markets**, where investors reward companies positioned to benefit from AI infrastructure demand.
* **Embodied AI**, where humanoid robotics is emerging as the next strategic arena after large language models.

Together, these developments reinforce that the AI race is increasingly defined by infrastructure, manufacturing, supply chains, and national industrial policy—not solely by advances in foundation models.
