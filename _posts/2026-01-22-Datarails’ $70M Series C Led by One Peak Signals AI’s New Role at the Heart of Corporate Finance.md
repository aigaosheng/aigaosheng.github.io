**Datarails’ $70M Series C Led by One Peak Signals AI’s New Role at the Heart of Corporate Finance**

In a funding landscape crowded with flashy AI startups and infrastructure plays, today’s big news comes from a corner of the enterprise world where **innovation has long been overdue — corporate finance.** Datarails, an **AI-native financial platform designed to modernize the CFO’s office**, has announced a **$70 million Series C funding round** led by London‑based growth equity firm **One Peak**, with participation from Singapore’s **Vertex Growth** and other seasoned investors. ([Datarails][1])

This round brings the company’s total capital raised to **$175 million to date**, highlighting strong investor confidence in the idea that finance teams — even those still reliant on Excel — can benefit significantly from generative AI and automation. ([The SaaS News][2])

### **From Spreadsheets to Smart Finance**

Datarails has carved out a unique niche. Rather than replacing Excel — the tool that nearly every finance professional knows and loves — the platform **augments it with AI and unified data infrastructure.** The result: finance teams can keep working in familiar spreadsheets while eliminating manual tasks like data consolidation, repetitive reporting, and error‑prone reconciliations. ([Datarails][1])

At its core is **FinanceOS**, an operating system‑like suite that integrates financial and operational data from ERP, CRM, HRIS systems and Excel. This consolidated “single source of truth” enables CFOs and analysts to run forecasting, budgeting, month‑end close processes, and cash management without contextswitching across tools. ([The SaaS News][2])

But the latest round isn’t just about growth — it’s about **positioning AI as a strategic foundation of financial operations.** Datarails has launched a set of **AI Finance Agents** that can be queried conversationally (“**What’s driving profitability this quarter?**”) and deliver **board‑ready reports**, visualizations, or downloadable outputs like Excel and PowerPoint. These capabilities are designed to replace hours of manual work with intelligent insights. ([Datarails][1])

### **Growth Momentum and Market Timing**

2025 was a breakout year for Datarails. The company reported **70% year‑over‑year revenue growth** and **nearly doubled its workforce to over 400 employees globally** — a clear signal of both demand and operational scale. ([Datarails][1])

Investors see this as part of a broader shift: CFOs and finance teams increasingly recognize that traditional spreadsheet workflows don’t cut it in an era of rapid market shifts and data complexity. With the consolidation of AI into core workflows, firms can make faster, data‑backed decisions — and avoid the long hours of manual spreadsheet manipulation that have historically bogged teams down. ([ctech][3])

### **Implications for the Industry**

This round sends several key signals:

* **AI is no longer a “nice‑to‑have” add‑on** in enterprise software — it’s becoming **foundational infrastructure** for strategic business functions. ([The AI Journal][4])
* **CFOs are waking up to AI’s business value** beyond analytics dashboards and basic automation, demanding tools that handle real workflows end‑to‑end. ([Datarails][1])
* **Excel remains relevant** — but only when augmented effectively with centralized data, automation, and generative intelligence. ([The SaaS News][2])

For investors like Vertex Growth, this represents a bet on **enterprise AI adoption at scale** — a segment seen as ripe for disruption as companies move past point solutions to integrated platforms that fuel strategic decision‑making.

## **Glossary**

**Series C Funding** — A later stage of startup fundraising where the company has proven product‑market fit and is scaling its business.

**AI‑Native Platform** — Software designed from the ground up with artificial intelligence deeply embedded into its core workflows.

**FinanceOS** — Datarails’ term for its finance operating system — a unified platform that consolidates financial and operational data for CFO teams.

**ERP/CRM/HRIS** — Key business systems that manage core data: Enterprise Resource Planning, Customer Relationship Management, and Human Resource Information Systems, respectively.
