### 12. **Agentic AI's Disruption of Retail and SME Banking**

**Published:** Septemper 12, 2025
**Summary:** Successful adoption of agentic AI goes beyond deploying AI agents—it requires redesigning workflows, integrating agents into existing systems, and ensuring human oversight.
