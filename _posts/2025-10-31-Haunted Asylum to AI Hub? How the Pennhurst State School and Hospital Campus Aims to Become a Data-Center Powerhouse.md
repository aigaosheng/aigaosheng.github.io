**Haunted Asylum to AI Hub? How the Pennhurst State School and Hospital Campus Aims to Become a Data-Center Powerhouse**

The sprawling grounds of the former Pennhurst State Hospital in rural Pennsylvania—once an institution for the disabled, later a famed Halloween “haunted asylum” attraction—may soon metamorphose into something completely different: a giant data-centre campus catering to the artificial-intelligence boom.

### From ghost stories to gigabytes

The site, located in East Vincent Township (Chester County, PA), is under consideration for redevelopment into a large-scale data-centre facility. ([aterio.io][1]) While the haunted-house attraction put Pennhurst on the pop-culture map, the change in usage would reflect a radical shift—from historic institution to digital infrastructure node.

### Why the relocation to Pennhurst?

Companies building the next generation of AI systems are hungry for space, power and connectivity. Rural and under-zoned properties like this one offer an appealing canvas. The article from Bloomberg highlights how even a haunted-house owner is “jumping in” on the AI infrastructure craze because the opportunity has become so large. ([bloomberg.com][2])
In essence: old buildings + large land plots + fewer urban constraints = attractive data-campus site.

### What’s on the table

Though still in early stages, the plan reportedly envisions:

* Several major data-centre buildings (roughly 1.3 million sq ft across five two-storey structures) on roughly 125 acres of the Pennhurst site. ([aterio.io][1])
* An industrial support building and a dedicated electrical substation covering several acres. ([aterio.io][1])
* Zoning and planning work underway in East Vincent Township to regulate large-scale developments like this to account for impacts. ([DELCO.Today][3])

### Local and historical complications

The Pennhurst campus is not just any parcel of land. It opened in 1908 as a state institution for the “feeble-minded and epileptic,” and it was eventually closed in 1987 after a landmark court ruling found constitutional rights violations. ([Wikipedia][4])
Local residents are upset. A petition has garnered more than 10,000 signatures calling to preserve the site’s heritage and to ensure the development respects the environment and rural character. ([aterio.io][1])
Township officials are proactively drafting zoning rules to handle big-tech infrastructure proposals like this. ([DELCO.Today][3])

### Broader implications

* **AI infrastructure build-out is supporting unlikely conversions.** The Pennhurst case shows how even haunted-house franchises and former institutional campuses are being eyed by tech infrastructure developers because of the sheer scale of demand.
* **Regional shifts in data-centre geography.** As cloud and AI workloads expand, so does the pressure for rural, power-rich, large-land-plots. Sites like Pennhurst sit between historical uses and future digital utility.
* **Social and environmental trade-offs.** The project raises questions of how communities negotiate big infrastructure in historically burdened or rural zones—balancing jobs and investment against local character, heritage and resource strain (water, energy, noise, traffic).
* **Historical re-use narrative.** The shift from institutional asylum to Halloween attraction to AI campus highlights changing land-use paradigms: spectacle → memory → infrastructure.

### What happens next

The proposal has not yet been approved. It is under review as a conditional-use application in the township, with public hearings slated. Local activists say they want to ensure that any redevelopment “honors the history” while protecting the environment and neighbourhood. ([aterio.io][1])
If approved, the timeline and developer partners have not been fully disclosed. The proposal nonetheless signals how aggressive the infrastructure needs of the AI era have become.

### Glossary

* **Data Centre**: A facility housing computer systems and associated components (e.g., servers, storage, networking) for large-scale processing and data storage.
* **Conditional Use**: A zoning classification where a land-use permit is granted subject to conditions and public review rather than being automatically allowed.
* **AI Infrastructure Build-out**: The large-scale expansion of hardware, facilities, power and network systems required to support artificial-intelligence workloads (training, inference, storage).
* **Redevelopment**: The process of converting or repurposing an existing site, often with new use, infrastructure, or zoning.
