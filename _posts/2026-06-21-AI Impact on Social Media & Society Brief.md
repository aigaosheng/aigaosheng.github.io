### 2. Tech Workers Shift from 'Tokenmaxxing' to 'Tokenminning' as AI Costs Soar
- **The Indian Express** · 2026-06-21
- **Summary**: Tech giants like Meta, Uber, and Amazon are scaling back their employees' unrestricted use of AI after facing unexpectedly high bills for tokens from providers like OpenAI and Anthropic. This marks a shift from a culture of "tokenmaxxing" (maximizing AI use) to "tokenminning" (minimizing costs), with companies now imposing limits and tracking output rather than volume .
- **Why It Matters**: This is a critical signal for the enterprise AI market. The "gold rush" mentality around AI is giving way to a more strategic approach focused on ROI, which will pressure AI vendors to justify their costs and push companies to optimize their internal AI operations.
