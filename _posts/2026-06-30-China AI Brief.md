### China AI Brief — 2026-06-30

## Top Stories

### 1. **Meituan Launches LongCat-2.0, Claiming First Trillion-Parameter AI Model Trained Fully on Chinese Chips**
- **Source** · Reuters · 2026-06-30
- **Summary**: Meituan announced the release and open-sourcing of LongCat-2.0, a next-generation large language model that it claims was trained entirely on a 50,000-chip cluster powered by domestically produced Chinese processors. The company positions the model as a major step in China's push for AI self-reliance amid continued restrictions on advanced foreign AI hardware access. LongCat-2.0 targets agentic coding, long-context processing, and commercial AI applications.
- **Why It Matters**: The announcement highlights China's accelerating effort to build an independent AI stack covering chips, infrastructure, and foundation models. If validated, large-scale domestic-chip training capability could reshape the global AI hardware competition.
- **URL**: https://www.reuters.com/world/china/chinas-meituan-says-new-ai-model-trained-domestic-chips-2026-06-30/
