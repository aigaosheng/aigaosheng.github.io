# AI Investment & Startup in China Brief: DeepSeek Leads $400 Million Round for Robotics Startup Winverse AI

**Today:** Chinese foundation model provider DeepSeek expands into physical AI by leading a $400 million seed round into stealth embodied robotics startup Winverse AI.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **DeepSeek leads $400M round for robotics AI startup Winverse AI**

*Quartz · October 12, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Hangzhou-based AI lab DeepSeek is leading a $400 million seed round for stealth robotics AI startup Winverse AI.

Chinese AI developer DeepSeek is pivoting into embodied intelligence by anchoring a $400 million financing round for Winverse AI. The Hangzhou-based stealth startup was founded by a former senior scientist from Tencent. The deal comes shortly after DeepSeek completed its own major capital raise and signals an effort to couple frontier foundation models directly with physical robotics systems.

**Why it matters:** The move demonstrates that top Chinese LLM developers are reallocating fresh capital into embodied AI, accelerating the convergence of foundation software models and advanced robotics hardware across China's manufacturing and consumer hubs.

### 2. 💳 **Ola Dimensions secures fourth funding round in seven months for home robots**

*Tech Insider · October 13, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Consumer embodied-AI startup Ola Dimensions closed its fourth investment round in seven months, securing hundreds of millions of RMB.

Shenzhen-based robotics startup Ola Dimensions has completed an "Angel-plus" funding round worth hundreds of millions of RMB, backed by Chengyuanxiang Fund, Xinneng Venture Capital, Hony Capital, and Topband. Founded in March 2026, the 70-person team is developing household embodied intelligence designed to continuously adapt to family environments.

**Why it matters:** Rapid capital deployment into pre-product robotics startups highlights how aggressively domestic venture funds, strategic component manufacturers, and municipal funds are competing for early position in China's consumer robotics market.

### 3. 🤖 **Yisheng raises $14.9M for diamond composite AI chip cooling**

*Dealroom · October 13, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Advanced materials startup Yisheng raised $14.9 million to commercialize diamond composite thermal management solutions for high-density AI chips.

Chinese thermal materials developer Yisheng finalized a $14.9 million fundraising round to ramp up production of its diamond composite cooling architectures. The company's hardware targets high-performance AI accelerators and data center racks that face thermal throttling under intense LLM training and inference workloads.

**Why it matters:** Specialized hardware supply chain startups in China are capturing targeted early-stage capital as local data center operators work to optimize efficiency and thermal constraints on domestic AI computing infrastructure.
