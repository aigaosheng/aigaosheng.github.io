### 1. Experian Launches AI Fraud Detection Platform with 200% Detection Boost

**Source:** Fintech Magazine | **Date:** April 27, 2026
**Summary:** Experian introduced “Transaction Forensics,” an AI-powered fraud detection platform developed with Resistant AI. It reflects a broader shift toward layered, intelligence-driven fraud prevention architectures.
**Why It Matters:** Fraud remains the highest-ROI AI use case in fintech. A step-change improvement in detection signals rapid maturation of real-time AI risk engines and sets new benchmarks for banks globally.
