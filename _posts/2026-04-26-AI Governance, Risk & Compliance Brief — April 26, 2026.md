### 1. DOJ Backs Challenge to Colorado AI Law, Escalating Federal–State Tensions

The law mandates mitigation of algorithmic bias in sectors like hiring and healthcare, but critics argue it violates constitutional protections and imposes ideological constraints on AI design. The case now represents a broader clash between federal authority and state-level AI governance initiatives.
**Why It Matters:** This is a landmark legal test for AI regulation in the U.S., with implications for whether AI governance will be centralized federally or fragmented across states—directly impacting compliance strategies for enterprises.
