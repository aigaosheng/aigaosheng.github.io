## Strategic Takeaways

* **Competition is becoming full-stack.** The contest now spans frontier models, accelerators, networking, cloud infrastructure, manufacturing and capital.
* **China's strategy is increasingly vertical.** Domestic models, chips, data centers and industrial applications are being developed as mutually reinforcing parts of one ecosystem.
* **US advantages remain concentrated in frontier compute and capital.** China is responding with domestic substitution, engineering scale and tighter integration between AI and manufacturing.
* **Safety cooperation is emerging alongside strategic rivalry.** The prospective US-China incident-communication channel suggests both governments recognize risks that cannot be managed entirely through unilateral controls.
* **The key question is shifting from model leadership to ecosystem resilience.** The long-term competitive balance will depend not only on benchmark performance but also on compute availability, energy, semiconductor supply chains, developer ecosystems, deployment scale and ability to commercialize AI.
