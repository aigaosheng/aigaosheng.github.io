# AI Finance in Singapore Brief: MAS Official Joins SFF Panel on AI Agents

**Today:** A light news window: MAS's agent-safeguards work heads to the Singapore FinTech Festival stage, and two vendor stories show AI becoming a product pitch for fintechs and card issuers.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🏦 **Singapore FinTech Festival Panel to Examine Controls for AI Agents**
*TokenPost · October 21, 2026*

**Bottom line:** MAS is taking its AI-agent safeguards to a public festival panel, with a UK regulator and a card network in the room.

The Nov. 19 session at the Singapore FinTech Festival (Nov. 18–20) will cover who is accountable when an AI agent acts and how a failing agent can be contained. Speakers include Alan Lim, head of MAS's Financial Infrastructure & Artificial Intelligence Office, Colin Payne of the UK FCA, and Casey Klyszeiko of American Express. The report adds that SAFR version 1.1 was published in October, that MAS released an open-source reference implementation by Oct. 9, and that MAS plans a 2027 consultation on whether extra guidance for AI agents is needed.

**Why it matters:** SAFR's proposed checkpoints test an agent's action against its mandate and record the decision before execution, a pattern payments and treasury teams piloting agents can design toward early. With MAS still weighing formal agent guidance, the festival panel is a near-term place to gauge supervisory thinking.

### 2. 🤖 **Airwallex Becomes McLaren F1 Team's Official AI Finance Partner**
*Marketing-Interactive · October 22, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Airwallex is using its McLaren extension to position itself as an AI-native finance platform.

From January 2027 Airwallex becomes the McLaren Mastercard Formula 1 Team's first official AI finance partner, in a multi-year extension of a tie-up that began in 2024. It will keep supplying the technology behind McLaren's digital partner merchandise platform. CFO Pranav Sood tied the deal to the company's move into what it calls the agentic banking era, with agentic business accounts at the centre of its stack. The announcement follows a US$320 million Series H round in June at a US$11 billion valuation, which Airwallex is using to build AI-native financial software.

**Why it matters:** Cross-border payments platforms are now marketing AI-driven finance operations as a headline capability, not a back-office feature. The June raise at that valuation shows the capital behind that positioning.

### 3. 💳 **Mastercard Launches AI Assistant for Card Benefits; Citi Explores Singapore Use**
*Fintech News Singapore · October 22, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Mastercard is offering issuers a ready-made AI benefits assistant, and Citi is exploring it for Singapore and Hong Kong.

Mastercard Max helps cardholders find and use the benefits already attached to their cards, starting with Brazil's C6 Bank. Banks can embed it in their mobile apps or existing AI assistants, while Mastercard manages the underlying technology and content. SNB Group, Nedbank, I&M Bank and Attijariwafa Bank are also working to introduce it. It is available globally, but customer access depends on each bank rolling it out.

**Why it matters:** For Singapore card issuers, this puts a buy-versus-build choice on customer-facing AI, with the network supplying the content layer. Citi's interest is an early signal, but only exploratory; no Singapore launch date has been reported.
