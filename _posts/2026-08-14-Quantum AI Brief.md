## Executive Takeaway

The strongest signal today is **industrialization**. Quantum computing is increasingly being treated less as a standalone physics program and more as an emerging computing layer that requires manufacturing scale, enterprise software, regional ecosystems and integration with conventional AI infrastructure.

For Quantum+AI specifically, the near-term opportunity remains **hybrid computing**: classical AI and HPC systems handle most workloads while quantum processors are introduced selectively for optimization, simulation, chemistry and other problems where a measurable advantage can eventually be demonstrated.
