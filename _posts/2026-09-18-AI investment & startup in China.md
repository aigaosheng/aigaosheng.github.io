# AI investment & startup in China Brief — 2026-09-18

## Top Stories

### 1. **D-Robotics Raises $400M Series C to Scale China’s Embodied-AI Infrastructure**

* **Source**: D-Robotics / PR Newswire · 2026-09-17
* **Summary**: Chinese robotics infrastructure company D-Robotics closed a $400 million Series C. The capital will expand its Sunrise AI-chip portfolio across compute tiers and fund a software platform covering the robot-development chain, from development and training through deployment. The company said Sunrise chip shipments exceeded 8 million units in the first half of 2026 and its embodied-AI business has entered mass production.
* **Why It Matters**: Capital is increasingly flowing toward the infrastructure layer beneath China’s humanoid-robot boom, not only robot manufacturers. D-Robotics’ strategy also shows how domestic chip, software and developer ecosystems are becoming investable platforms for physical AI.

### 2. **ByteDance Spin-Off Anew Labs Raises $290M at $1.5B Valuation**

* **Source**: South China Morning Post · 2026-09-18
* **Summary**: Shanghai-based Anew Labs, spun out of ByteDance as an AI drug-discovery company, raised $290 million at a $1.5 billion valuation. Investors reportedly include HSG, IDG Capital and 5Y Capital, while ByteDance retains a 56% stake. Anew develops AI models for biomolecular structure prediction and drug design, including its AnewFold foundation model and the open-source Protenix model.
* **Why It Matters**: The round highlights growing investor appetite for AI-native biotechnology in China, where specialized scientific AI companies are attracting capital beyond the foundation-model market. The ByteDance spin-off structure also illustrates how large Chinese technology groups are separating capital-intensive AI ventures into focused businesses.
* **URL**: [https://www.scmp.com/business/china-business/article/3367926/bytedance-spin-anew-labs-raises-us290m-ai-drug-developers-enjoy-fundraising-boom](https://www.scmp.com/business/china-business/article/3367926/bytedance-spin-anew-labs-raises-us290m-ai-drug-developers-enjoy-fundraising-boom)

### 3. **Hello Robotaxi Raises About $100M at Nearly $3B Valuation**

* **Source**: MarkLines · 2026-09-18
* **Summary**: Shanghai Zaofu Intelligent Technology, which operates Hello Robotaxi, completed a financing round of approximately $100 million led by Shanghai State-owned Capital Investment. The company plans to use the capital for a 10,000-GPU computing cluster, end-to-end autonomous-driving models and a high-quality training-data supply chain. Its first mass-produced Level-4 vehicle, the HR1, entered production in July.
* **Why It Matters**: The financing demonstrates that Chinese autonomous-driving startups increasingly require AI-infrastructure-scale capital, rather than conventional automotive R&D budgets. Compute, proprietary data and vehicle manufacturing are converging into a single capital-intensive AI stack.
* **URL**: [https://www.marklines.com/en/news/350953?sitesearchKey=Model%2C%20Y](https://www.marklines.com/en/news/350953?sitesearchKey=Model%2C%20Y)

### 4. **PaXini Secures Another B++ Round as Embodied-AI Capital Concentrates**

* **Source**: Securities Times · 2026-09-18
* **Summary**: Shenzhen-based embodied-intelligence company PaXini completed a new B++ financing round worth several hundred million yuan. Investors include Guangxin Fund, Sichuan Science and Technology Innovation Investment Group, Shandong Guohui Fund and CITIC Capital, alongside follow-on investment from existing shareholders. The company said its self-developed tactile-sensing chips have shipped more than one million units over the past year.
* **Why It Matters**: PaXini's financing emphasizes tactile sensing as a strategic layer in embodied AI, complementing vision-based perception with physical interaction data. The repeated financing activity also shows investors are increasingly funding the data-and-sensor infrastructure needed to move robots from visual demonstrations toward reliable physical execution.
* **URL**: [https://stcn.com/article/detail/4191020.html](https://stcn.com/article/detail/4191020.html)

### 5. **Shutu Technology Raises Nearly RMB100M for Embodied-AI Data and Tactile Infrastructure**

* **Source**: Gasgoo · 2026-09-18
* **Summary**: Shenzhen-based Shutu Technology completed a nearly RMB100 million Pre-A round led by CDH VGC and Kunpeng Fund, with Guangzhou Industrial Investment and YiHe Capital participating and Trend Investment increasing its position. The company is developing SynaData for converting human behavior into training data and SynaTac for tactile intelligence and robot contact strategies. The financing is the company's third funding round within a month.
* **Why It Matters**: Investors are backing the data bottleneck around embodied AI alongside models and hardware. Shutu's combination of human-behavior datasets and tactile data points toward a broader infrastructure market for training and evaluating robots in real-world tasks.

### 6. **Qianjue Technology Raises Nine-Figure RMB Series A+ for Practical Robotics AI**

* **Source**: InforCapital · 2026-09-18
* **Summary**: Robotics-intelligence company Qianjue Technology completed a nine-figure RMB Series A+ round backed by Oriza Rivertown, SSC Fund, KHK Fund, Innoangel Fund, Jingming Capital, Future Marginal Ventures and Maple Pledge. The company is developing predictive world models designed to anticipate environmental changes and the consequences of robotic actions. Its commercial strategy focuses on service robots for hospitality, cleaning, retail and home services.
* **Why It Matters**: The investment reflects a shift in robotics financing toward measurable deployment economics rather than demonstrations alone. World models are being positioned as components of an operational robotics stack where task success, uptime, reliability and cost determine commercial value.
