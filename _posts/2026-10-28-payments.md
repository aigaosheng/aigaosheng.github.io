# Payments Brief: Visa Moves Stablecoin Payouts Into Commercial Production

**Today:** Card networks, wallets and regulators all moved stablecoins and account-to-account rails closer to mainstream payments, and Singapore named the leader of its new national payment scheme body.

## Top Stories

### 1. 📊 **Visa Puts Stablecoin Payouts Into Production as Agentic Partners Grow**
*PYMNTS · October 27, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Visa's stablecoin business has moved from pilots to commercial scale, with settlement running at a USD 25 billion annualised pace.

On its fiscal Q4 call, Visa said its stablecoin prefunding and cross-border service under Visa Direct is now in commercial production. The initial offering uses USDC, with more stablecoins to follow. Stablecoin settlement passed a USD 25 billion annualised run rate, ten times higher than a year earlier, across five stablecoins on 10 blockchains. Visa expects its roughly 50 AI partners in production to double in the coming months. Quarterly net revenue rose 13% to USD 12.1 billion, and full-year revenue rose 15% to USD 46 billion.

**Why it matters:** Payout and remittance providers in Asia can now fund Visa Direct flows with stablecoins on a production service. That makes on-chain treasury a working option for cross-border operations, not just a pilot.

### 2. 🏦 **OCC Gives Ripple Final Approval for a National Trust Bank**
*PYMNTS · October 27, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Ripple now has a federally supervised bank to support its RLUSD stablecoin and offer enterprise custody, and it is widening distribution through BNY and Robinhood.

The Office of the Comptroller of the Currency gave final approval for Ripple National Trust Bank, following a conditional approval in December. Separately, BNY now supports XRP and RLUSD on its digital asset custody platform, and the two firms will explore tokenised trade finance. RLUSD is live on Robinhood Chain and Bitstamp and is expected in the Robinhood app later this year. The approval comes while community bankers are suing the OCC over how it handles crypto charter applications.

**Why it matters:** Stablecoin issuers backed by a bank charter will be stronger candidates for bank partnerships and institutional settlement. This includes corridors into Asia.

### 3. 📊 **PayPal's BNPL Volume Jumps 25% While Branded Checkout Barely Grows**
*PYMNTS · October 27, 2026*

**Bottom line:** PayPal is growing through its financial services products while its core online checkout stays flat.

Third-quarter revenue rose 2% to USD 8.58 billion. Total payment volume rose 6% to USD 487.1 billion. Global online branded checkout grew just 1% on a currency-neutral basis. Buy now, pay later (BNPL) volume rose 25%, and Pay with Venmo volume rose 43%. PayPal has cut more than 2,000 jobs and is targeting at least USD 1.5 billion in gross annualised savings over two to three years. The CEO said PayPal is working with Meta and other AI firms to build PayPal into personal shopping agents.

**Why it matters:** PayPal's numbers show that for large wallets, growth now comes from credit, debit and agent features rather than checkout alone. Regional wallets competing for merchants can expect the same pressure.

### 4. 💳 **Stripe Launches "stripe pay" for Free Instant B2B Transfers**
*PYMNTS · October 27, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Stripe businesses can now send money to each other instantly and for free, using a Stripe profile handle.

The feature works from the Stripe Dashboard and the command line. The sender needs a funded Stripe financial account, and the recipient needs a financial account linked to its Stripe profile. CEO Patrick Collison said he expects account-to-account (A2A) payments to matter more than person-to-person (P2P) payments.

**Why it matters:** A free transfer network inside Stripe gives B2B platforms another option besides cards and bank wires. Fintechs that earn fees on B2B payouts should watch whether it spreads beyond the US.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.pymnts.com/news/b2b-payments/2026/stripe-pay-targets-instant-b2b-payments/)

### 5. 🏦 **Singapore Payments Network Names Derek Chan as Inaugural CEO**
*Fintech News Singapore · October 28, 2026*

**Bottom line:** SPaN, the new body that will govern Singapore's national payment schemes, now has a CEO and a board chaired by a MAS deputy managing director.

MAS and the Association of Banks in Singapore set up SPaN as a not-for-profit. It will start operating in early 2027. Chan has close to 20 years in payment schemes and was previously Chief Business Officer at Banking Computer Services. The 11-member board is chaired by Jacqueline Loh of MAS. SPaN will first focus on a stable handover of the national schemes, then on resilience, infrastructure upgrades and regional connectivity.

**Why it matters:** Payment institutions and banks in Singapore will deal with a new scheme governor from 2027. Rules, access and connectivity decisions may change during the handover.

### 6. 🌐 **OCBC Adds Alipay+ Travel Deals as Overseas QR Use Doubles**
*Fintech News Singapore · October 28, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Overseas QR payments from a Singapore bank app are growing fast, and OCBC is now adding merchant offers on top.

OCBC launched Alipay+ Travel Treat, with more than 200 merchant deals across seven destinations, including mainland China, Japan, South Korea and Malaysia. In the first eight months of 2026, both the number of OCBC customers paying by overseas Alipay+ QR and the value of those payments more than doubled from a year earlier. Customers can pay by QR directly from their Singapore bank accounts in more than 55 destinations.

**Why it matters:** Banks are using cross-border QR links to keep travel spending that would otherwise go to cards or multi-currency wallets. This shapes outbound acceptance strategy across Southeast Asia.

### 7. 💳 **Diebold Nixdorf and Euronet Explore Global ATM Outsourcing Tie-Up**
*Fintech News Singapore · October 28, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Two major ATM players plan to offer banks a combined outsourced ATM package, though the deal is not yet binding.

The two firms signed a non-binding letter of intent. The package would combine Diebold Nixdorf's cash-recycling ATMs, software and field services with Euronet's Ren processing platform and back-office systems. They are targeting lower costs for banks and more services at the ATM, and banks could keep their existing provider relationships.

**Why it matters:** Banks are still under pressure to cut the cost of their cash networks. Bundled outsourcing could change how regional lenders budget for ATM upgrades.

## Key Takeaways
- Stablecoins are being added to existing rails, not built as separate systems: Visa uses them for prefunding, Ripple is getting a bank charter, and BNY is adding custody.
- Large wallets and processors are pushing account-to-account and agent-led payments to grow beyond flat card-based checkout.
- In Asia, payment infrastructure is changing on two fronts: Singapore is reshaping how its national schemes are governed, and banks like OCBC are building out cross-border QR.

## What to Watch
Watch for RLUSD's arrival in the Robinhood app, expected later this year, and PayPal's detailed 2027 guidance, due in February.
