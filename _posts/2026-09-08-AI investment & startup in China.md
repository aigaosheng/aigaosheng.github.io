# AI investment & startup in China Brief — 2026-09-08

## Top Stories 

### 1. **DeepCtrls Raises Hundreds of Millions of RMB in Series B+, Led by CATL**

* **Source**: 投中网 / Chinaventure · 2026-09-08
* **Summary**: Physical-AI company DeepCtrls completed a new Series B+ round worth hundreds of millions of RMB. CATL led the round, with Aramco Ventures, Taiping Innovation Investment, Guangfa Xinde and Fosun Fortune participating, while existing investors Source Code Capital and Guangyuan Capital continued to invest. The company is applying its physical-AI technology to energy management, industrial control and AI-compute infrastructure.
* **Why It Matters**: The round is a strong signal that industrial customers are increasingly becoming strategic AI investors. Capital is moving beyond foundation models toward AI systems that directly optimize electricity, cooling and physical infrastructure supporting large-scale compute.
* **URL**: [https://www.chinaventure.com.cn/news/112.html](https://www.chinaventure.com.cn/news/112.html)

### 2. **Qianjue Technology Raises Hundreds of Millions of RMB to Build Robot Intelligence Infrastructure**

* **Source**: 投资界 · 2026-09-08
* **Summary**: Qianjue Technology completed a Series A+ financing worth hundreds of millions of RMB, backed by Yuanhe Houwang, Xiaoguang Capital, Xineng Venture Capital, Inno Angel Fund, Jingming Capital and Future Frontier Ventures, among others. The Beijing startup develops predictive world models and polynomial representation architectures designed to provide deployable intelligence across different robot forms.
* **Why It Matters**: Investors are increasingly funding the intelligence layer rather than individual robot bodies. A reusable world-model and intelligence infrastructure could become a horizontal platform across humanoids, drones and industrial robots.
* **URL**: [https://news.pedaily.cn/202609/568660.shtml](https://news.pedaily.cn/202609/568660.shtml)

### 3. **Xiaoyu Zhizao Completes Several-Hundred-Million-RMB B3 Financing for Embodied AI**

* **Source**: 36氪 · 2026-09-08
* **Summary**: Xiaoyu Zhizao announced completion of a new B3 financing round worth hundreds of millions of RMB, backed by Blooming Star, Singapore-based Kamet Capital and Guoke Yingfeng Fund. The company says this is its third financing round in 2026, following its B and B+ rounds earlier in the year. Its "one brain, multiple forms" architecture targets welding, grinding, precision assembly and inspection robots.
* **Why It Matters**: Three financing rounds in one year show how quickly capital is concentrating around embodied AI companies with an industrial commercialization path. The investment thesis is shifting from robot hardware differentiation toward generalizable manipulation intelligence.

### 4. **Deep Space Matrix Raises 100-Million-RMB-Level Angel Round for Space AI Computing**

* **Source**: 新华网 · 2026-09-08
* **Summary**: Beijing-based Deep Space Matrix completed an angel round worth more than RMB100 million, jointly led by Dinghui VGC and Linkage Capital, with additional participation from other investment institutions. The funding will support core space-AI computing technology, engineering validation of its "Star Ring" constellation and development of the space-AI supply chain. The company's initial plan calls for roughly 210 satellites integrating remote sensing, communications and in-orbit computing.
* **Why It Matters**: Space-based AI computing is emerging as a new frontier at the intersection of commercial aerospace and AI infrastructure. The investment reflects a willingness to fund unconventional compute architectures before the commercial model is fully proven.
* **URL**: [https://www.news.cn/tech/20260908/fd0cd6129ad2484b960286ac5cb16423/c.html](https://www.news.cn/tech/20260908/fd0cd6129ad2484b960286ac5cb16423/c.html)

### 5. **Jialiang Brain Science Raises Several Hundred Million RMB Across Series C and D**

* **Source**: 证券时报网 · 2026-09-08
* **Summary**: Hangzhou-based Jialiang Brain Science completed consecutive Series C and Series D financing rounds, with total funding reaching several hundred million RMB. Qiming Venture Partners led Series C, while Series D included Saizhibole, Kuanqiao Hengsong and Ningbo state-backed investors. The company is developing an integrated brain-computer interface platform combining hardware, clinical data and AI, with plans to accelerate product commercialization and overseas regulatory approvals.
* **Why It Matters**: The financing indicates that China's BCI investment market is moving toward clinical validation and commercialization rather than pure research-stage experimentation. The combination of AI, neural interfaces and medical-device capabilities creates a potentially high-value but highly regulated technology category.
* **URL**: [https://stcn.com/article/detail/4176178.html](https://stcn.com/article/detail/4176178.html)

### 6. **China Launches a Three-Year Support Program Targeting 10,000 AI SMEs**

* **Source**: 新浪财经 / 中华工商时报 · 2026-09-08
* **Summary**: A new three-year government support program for AI-focused SMEs is being rolled out, targeting more than 10,000 new technology and innovation-oriented SMEs and more than 2,000 specialized "little giant" companies. The program focuses on improving access to computing power, high-quality data, application scenarios, financing, talent and compliance services. It also encourages AI incubators, open-source ecosystems and support for emerging one-person and small-scale AI businesses.
* **Why It Matters**: The policy broadens China's AI investment funnel from a small number of foundation-model champions toward a much larger startup ecosystem. For investors, the most important implication is likely to be increased deal flow in vertical AI applications, data services, AI infrastructure and specialized industrial solutions.

## Investor Takeaways

* **Physical AI is attracting strategic capital.** DeepCtrls and Qianjue show that investors are increasingly targeting AI systems capable of controlling physical processes, not just generating digital content.
* **Embodied AI is becoming an infrastructure play.** The latest rounds emphasize world models, reusable intelligence and cross-platform robot capabilities rather than single-purpose robot hardware.
* **AI infrastructure is expanding beyond conventional data centers.** Deep Space Matrix's space-compute thesis illustrates how investors are exploring new locations and architectures for AI computing.
* **Commercialization is becoming the key valuation test.** Jialiang Brain Science's latest financing is tied closely to registration, clinical adoption and international expansion, highlighting the transition from technical validation to revenue and regulatory execution.
* **Policy is widening the startup pipeline.** China's new AI SME program could materially increase the number of investable AI companies, particularly in industry applications, data and compute services.
* **Capital is increasingly strategic and industrial.** CATL, state-backed funds and other industry-linked investors are appearing alongside traditional VC/PE firms, suggesting that access to customers, manufacturing capacity, energy and infrastructure is becoming as important as financial capital.
