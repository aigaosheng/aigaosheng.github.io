# AI for science Brief: Isomorphic Labs Raises $5 Billion Series C

**Today:** One verified qualifying story this edition: a $5 billion raise that shows how much capital is now flowing to AI-native drug design, with Singapore's Temasek among the backers.

## Top Stories

### 1. 📊 **Isomorphic Labs Announces $5 Billion Series C to Scale AI Drug Design**

*Isomorphic Labs · November 17, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Isomorphic Labs, the AI drug design company led by Demis Hassabis, has closed a $5 billion Series C to scale its drug design engine and move its first candidates toward clinical trials.

Greenoaks and Sequoia Capital co-led the round, joined by Andreessen Horowitz and other new investors, with continued backing from Alphabet. All existing investors took part: Thrive Capital, MGX, GV, CapitalG, Temasek and the UK Sovereign AI Fund. The company says it will use the proceeds to develop its IsoDDE drug design engine, expand its pipeline, build partnerships, and set up translational and clinical development capabilities. It reports positive preclinical results in oncology and immunology, both internally and with partners, but says it has yet to enter the clinic. The release does not disclose a valuation.

**Why it matters:** The round follows a $2.1 billion Series B announced in May 2026, and it tests whether simulation-led drug design can turn into clinical evidence. Temasek's participation is a signal for Singapore and Asia-Pacific capital allocators, though the performance and preclinical claims are the company's own and not independently verified.

## Key Takeaways

- Capital for AI-native drug discovery is concentrating in a few platform companies, with large rounds arriving months apart rather than years.
- The next test is clinical rather than computational: investors are funding a pipeline that, on the company's own account, has not yet entered human trials.
- Sovereign and Asian investors such as Temasek and MGX remain repeat backers of frontier science-AI platforms, which matters for regional deep-tech strategies.

## What to Watch

Look for Isomorphic Labs to disclose its first clinical-trial entries and any valuation or new pharma partnerships, plus independent validation of IsoDDE's reported benchmark performance. Other science-AI releases this week, such as NVIDIA's Nemotron for Science, were published before this edition's window and are not covered here.
