### 1. **Zhipu AI’s GLM-5.2 Highlights China’s Rise in Open-weight Frontier Models**

* **The Wall Street Journal** · 2026-06-28
* **Summary**: Zhipu AI has released GLM-5.2, an open-weight model reported to compete with leading proprietary systems in cybersecurity-related tasks. The model’s open-weight availability allows developers and organizations to run, adapt, and deploy it without relying exclusively on commercial AI APIs. ([The Wall Street Journal][1])
