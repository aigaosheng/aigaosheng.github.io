# Payments Brief: MAS and ABS Set PayNow Gen 2 Roadmap

**Today:** Singapore Fintech Festival week produced a national payments roadmap from MAS, plus a cluster of stablecoin and agent-payment moves from Visa and regional players that show where Asian payment rails are heading.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🏦 **MAS and ABS Publish PayNow Generation 2 Implementation Roadmap**

*Monetary Authority of Singapore · November 18, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Singapore has put dates on the next upgrade of PayNow, with a QR interoperability switch, shared deep-linking standards and clearer merchant dispute rules all scheduled for 2027–2028.

MAS and the Association of Banks in Singapore released the Phase 2 report of the PayNow Gen2 study, which sets out five enhancements. A limited rollout of the SGQR+ switch is planned for 2027 and would let residents and tourists pay at any merchant with their preferred app. Common deep-linking standards and a central facility are due by end-2027, and the scheme's person-to-merchant rules on disputes, refunds, fraud and scams are to be introduced in stages over 2028. MAS also plans a "Future Payment Rails Blueprint" in 2027 and will convene partners on capabilities for new payment types, including AI-agent payments.

**Why it matters:** A dated roadmap gives banks, acquirers and wallet providers a planning horizon, and the dispute and fraud rules will shape liability for merchant payments over PayNow. QR interoperability that includes inbound tourists also matters for regional acceptance networks.

### 2. 🌐 **Visa Extends Stablecoin Prefunding for Cross-Border Payouts to Asia Pacific**

*Fintech News Singapore · November 18, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Visa is bringing stablecoin prefunding for Visa Direct payouts to Asia Pacific, moving the capability from a US commercial launch into the region's cross-border corridors.

According to the report, eligible Asia Pacific clients will be able to fund cross-border payouts with supported stablecoins while recipients still receive fiat. The first pilot is with dtcpay. The report says the capability entered commercial production in the US in October 2026 with Inyo Global, and cites Visa's figure that Visa Direct reaches more than 18 billion endpoints.

**Why it matters:** Prefunding with stablecoins targets the working-capital cost of pre-positioned currency in remittance and payout flows, a pain point for Asian payment firms. It also shows card networks absorbing stablecoins into existing rails rather than competing with them.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://fintechnews.sg/141598/digitalassets/visa-stablecoin-prefunding-asia-pacific/)

### 3. 💳 **FOMO Pay Taps Anchorage Digital for US Dollar Stablecoin Payments**

*Fintech News Singapore · November 18, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Singapore-based FOMO Pay will build regulated US dollar stablecoin payments and settlement on Anchorage Digital's infrastructure, targeting cross-border business flows in Asia and the Middle East.

The report says FOMO Pay will use Anchorage's minting, redemption and trading services and is the first Singapore institution to join Anchorage Digital's US platform. The tie-up could later extend to digital asset custody, subject to technical, regulatory and compliance requirements. The article does not disclose commercial terms or volumes.

**Why it matters:** Regional payment firms are sourcing regulated stablecoin issuance and redemption from US-regulated infrastructure providers instead of building it themselves. How such arrangements are treated under Singapore's stablecoin framework will be worth following.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://fintechnews.sg/141664/payments/fomo-pay-anchorage-digital/)

### 4. 🤖 **Visa Opens Agentic Ready Acceptance Phase Across Asia Pacific**

*Fintech News Singapore · November 18, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Visa is extending its Agentic Ready programme from issuers to the acceptance side, enlisting acquirers, gateways and merchants to prepare for agent-initiated transactions.

The acceptance phase starts with more than 50 participants, and Visa expects more to join through 2027. Named partners include Adyen, Airwallex, Checkout.com, DBS, Fiserv, Stripe and UOB. The report says over 100 issuers across 10 markets have joined since the first phase launched in April 2026.

**Why it matters:** Agentic commerce needs both card issuers and merchant-side systems to recognise agent-initiated payments. Moving to acceptance is the step that tests whether merchants and acquirers will handle those transactions at scale.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://fintechnews.sg/141550/ai/visa-agentic-ready-merchants/)

### 5. 🌐 **MetaComp Expands StableX Agentic Network Across Six ASEAN Markets**

*Fintech News Singapore · November 18, 2026*

**Bottom line:** MetaComp is widening its stablecoin-based network for local-currency payouts in Southeast Asia and tying agent payments to verified human or corporate owners.

The report says the network passed 1,000 members and 100,000 users in its first year. Institutions can send local currency to Singapore, Indonesia, Malaysia, Vietnam, the Philippines and Thailand, and can collect in Singapore, the Philippines and Indonesia, with Vietnam to follow. Under its Know Your Agent framework, each AI agent must have a verified owner. The milestone was marked at a forum on November 17, ahead of the festival.

**Why it matters:** Know-your-agent checks are an early attempt to solve accountability for agent payments in a region with fragmented rails. Reported member and user counts come from the company and have not been independently verified.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://fintechnews.sg/141635/singapore-fintech-festival-2026/metacomp-agentic-payment-network-asean/)

### 6. 🌐 **Ravi Menon Cites Project Nexus as Model for AI Agent Payment Standards**

*Fintech News Singapore · November 18, 2026*

**Bottom line:** GFTN Chairman Ravi Menon reiterated a 2027 launch target for Project Nexus and argued that agent payments need the same kind of shared standards.

Speaking at the festival's opening, Menon described Nexus as a shared gateway linking instant payment systems in five Southeast Asian countries and India. He pointed to MAS's Project Guardian and cited a Deloitte estimate that tokenised payments could cut corporate cross-border transaction costs by 12.5 percent and save businesses more than US$50 billion in 2030. He argued that agents need interoperable identity and authorisation standards.

**Why it matters:** Nexus remains the main multilateral effort to link Asian fast-payment systems. The 2027 target and the Deloitte figure are statements from the speaker and the cited estimate, not verified outcomes.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://fintechnews.sg/141521/payments/ravi-menon-project-nexus-ai-agent-payments/)

### 7. 💳 **NETS Plans "Tap QR to Pay" Trial in Toa Payoh**

*Fintech News Singapore · November 18, 2026*

**Bottom line:** NETS will test tapping an NFC phone at an SGQR payment point instead of scanning a code, with a nationwide rollout planned if the trial succeeds.

The two-month trial starts in December in Toa Payoh, and shoppers will not need to open the NETS App first. NETS expects other banking apps and wallets to support the feature later. The company also announced a family wallet that lets the main holder set spending limits and receive alerts for linked wallets. NETS says its app is accepted at more than 50,000 payment points.

**Why it matters:** Cutting a step from QR checkout targets a common friction point and fits the SGQR+ interoperability push. Adoption will depend on other apps and wallets adding support.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://fintechnews.sg/141599/payments/nets-tap-qr-to-pay/)

### 8. 💳 **Grab and Weixin Pay Plan Pre-Arrival Vouchers for Chinese Travellers**

*Fintech News Singapore · November 18, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Grab and Weixin Pay are building on their May 2026 payment link to bring inbound Chinese travellers into Grab's merchant network before they arrive.

Weixin Pay became a payment option in the Grab app in Singapore, Indonesia, Malaysia and Cambodia in May 2026. The companies say Singapore transaction volume, payment value and active users rose each month through October, with all three at record highs. They plan to let travellers claim vouchers and find local recommendations before arrival and to extend the service to more Southeast Asian markets.

**Why it matters:** Super-apps are using wallet partnerships to capture inbound tourism spend. The growth figures are company-reported and no absolute numbers were given.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://fintechnews.sg/141608/payments/grab-weixin-pay-travel-vouchers/)

### 9. 💳 **Google Wallet Adds Visa-Backed Alipay+ QR Payments for Visitors to Singapore**

*Fintech News Singapore · November 18, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Visitors to Singapore can now pay at Alipay+ merchant QR codes using a Visa card saved in Google Wallet, extending Visa's scan-to-pay rollout beyond Samsung Wallet.

The report says Google calls the feature "Traveler QR." Visitors from supported markets scan merchant QR codes without installing a local payment app. Visa and Alipay+ plan to bring it to more wallets and destinations in Asia Pacific outside mainland China. This follows the previously reported Samsung Wallet launch in Singapore and Australia.

**Why it matters:** Big-tech wallets are becoming a route for card-network credentials to reach local QR acceptance. That lowers the barrier for tourists and strengthens the case for QR interoperability in Singapore.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://fintechnews.sg/141567/payments/google-wallet-visa-qr-payments-singapore/)

## Key Takeaways

- Stablecoins are moving into mainstream payment plumbing: Visa is extending stablecoin prefunding into Asia Pacific, while FOMO Pay and MetaComp build regulated stablecoin settlement for regional corridors.
- Interoperability is the main theme in Singapore and ASEAN, spanning PayNow and SGQR+, NETS tap-to-pay, Alipay+ on Google Wallet and Project Nexus.
- Agentic commerce is shifting from issuer readiness to acceptance, accountability and standards, with Visa's acceptance phase, MetaComp's Know Your Agent checks and MAS's plans for AI-agent payments.

## What to Watch

Look for the first SGQR+ switch rollout, which MAS has scheduled for a limited launch in 2027, and the results of NETS's Toa Payoh tap-to-pay trial that starts in December. Also follow whether Project Nexus holds its 2027 target and how many acquirers and merchants join Visa's Agentic Ready acceptance phase.
