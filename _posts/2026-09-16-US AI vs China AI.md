### Strategic Takeaways

* **The AI competition is expanding beyond model benchmarks.** Governance, open-source distribution, semiconductor supply chains, infrastructure, and international standards are becoming equally important strategic arenas.
* **Open-weight models are becoming a geopolitical variable.** Chinese models can reach global developers without requiring the same physical infrastructure or semiconductor access as frontier training.
* **AI safety is becoming intertwined with national competition.** Both Washington and Beijing increasingly discuss AI risk, but their differing strategic incentives make common constraints difficult.
* **The next phase is likely to be ecosystem competition.** Compute, models, developers, enterprise adoption, energy, capital, and regulatory influence are increasingly interconnected components of national AI capability.
