### 1. Worldline First in Europe to Bring Click to Pay to Recurring Payments
- **Euronext Live Markets** · 2026-06-24
- **Summary**: Worldline has become the first payment provider in Europe to enable Click to Pay for recurring payments on its Global Collect platform. This innovation extends the one-click checkout experience to the entire subscription lifecycle, using tokenisation to automatically update card details and reduce involuntary churn.
- **Why It Matters**: Subscription businesses lose up to 40% of customers to involuntary churn from payment failures. By increasing conversion and protecting recurring revenue, Worldline addresses a critical pain point for SaaS, streaming, and digital membership merchants.
- **URL**: [Worldline: first in Europe to bring Click to Pay to recurring payments](https://live.euronext.com/en/products/equities/company-news/2026-06-24-worldline-first-europe-bring-click-pay-recurring-payments)

### 2. Global Payments Executive Highlights Stablecoins' Role in Enterprise Infrastructure
- **FinTech Magazine** · 2026-06-24
- **Summary**: Nabil Manji of Global Payments discussed how stablecoins are transitioning from experimental assets to core financial infrastructure. He noted that global stablecoin circulation increased 59% to $316bn between 2024 and 2025, with enterprises increasingly using them for cross-border settlement and liquidity management.
- **Why It Matters**: This signals a major shift as major payment processors integrate stablecoins for practical use cases like payouts and treasury movement. Global Payments' work to enable stablecoin payouts demonstrates the growing demand for faster, more transparent payment rails.
- **URL**: [Global Payments: How Stablecoins Are Reshaping Payments](https://fintechmagazine.com/news/global-payments-how-stablecoins-are-reshaping-payments)

### 7. Singapore Government to Assist 400 Unpaid Migrant Workers
- **Daily Excelsior** · 2026-06-24
- **Summary**: Around 400 migrant workers in Singapore, mostly from India and Bangladesh, will receive SGD 200 each in cash and vouchers after not being paid wages for three to four months. The National Trade Union Congress and Migrant Workers' Centre are providing assistance while working with the Ministry of Manpower to recover salaries.
- **Why It Matters**: This incident highlights the vulnerability of migrant workers to wage non-payment and the importance of labor protections. The government's intervention demonstrates regulatory commitment to worker welfare, with NTUC finding 150 new job placements for affected workers.
