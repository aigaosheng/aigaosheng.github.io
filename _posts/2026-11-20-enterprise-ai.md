# Enterprise AI Brief: Terminal 3 and Affinidi Launch Agent Trust Network

**Today:** Singapore’s TENON network and Mirae Asset’s production search service show how trust controls and grounded answers are shaping financial-sector AI deployment.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🤖 **Terminal 3 and Affinidi Launch Agent Trust Network**

*PR Newswire · November 20, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Terminal 3 and Singapore-founded Affinidi announced TENON, a network designed to verify and govern AI agents working across organisations, starting with financial services.

The companies say TENON checks agent identity and authority, applies organisational policies, and records actions in an immutable audit trail. Its first application will be Terminal 3’s Agent Command platform; the companies also demonstrated a banking assistant handling overseas transfers and disputed card charges while keeping account details from the underlying agent.

**Why it matters:** Cross-company agent workflows raise questions about identity, permissions and sensitive data that single-platform controls may not address. A Singapore-built network could offer regional financial institutions a shared approach, though adoption will depend on additional partners and deployments.

### 2. 🤖 **Mirae Asset Rolls Out AWS-Powered Financial Search**

*Digital Today · November 20, 2026*

**Bottom line:** Mirae Asset Securities is operating an AWS-based AI search service for employees, connecting financial product information with company data, disclosures and research.

Digital Today reports that a knowledge graph links about 13,000 documents to product records, while answers include sources and reference dates. More than 450 employees had used the service for over 2,000 complex queries by October 20; Mirae Asset plans to expand access to customers in the first half of 2027 and introduce a pension-focused AI agent during 2027.

**Why it matters:** The deployment illustrates how financial firms can ground AI answers in traceable internal material and keep model changes within a security review process. Customer expansion will test whether these controls and employee results carry over to external use.

🔗 [Read the full story](https://www.digitaltoday.co.kr/en/view/128591/mirae-asset-securities-launches-aws-based-ai-search-for-financial-product-knowledge)

## Key Takeaways

- Financial institutions are pairing AI workflows with identity, access and audit controls before extending agents to sensitive tasks.
- Grounded answers with source records and reference dates can make enterprise search more usable in regulated settings.
- The next test is whether early employee deployments expand into customer-facing and cross-organisation workflows.

## What to Watch

Watch for TENON’s additional network operators and production deployments, which the companies say will be announced in the coming months. Mirae Asset’s planned customer search expansion in the first half of 2027 and its pension AI agent rollout during 2027 will indicate how quickly its employee service moves outward.
