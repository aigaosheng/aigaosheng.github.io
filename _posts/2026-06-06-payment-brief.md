### Payment Brief — 2026-06-06

*Covering developments published in the 48h to 2026-06-06 20:53:59 (+0800).*

## Top Stories

### 1. Visa explores private stablecoin settlement with Brale on Canton
- **Visa** · 2026-06-04
- **Summary**: Visa said it is collaborating with Brale on a proof of concept for stablecoin-based institutional settlement using SBC, a U.S. dollar-backed stablecoin, on the Canton Network. The work focuses on whether privacy-enabled blockchain infrastructure can support programmable settlement while preserving confidentiality around sensitive transaction data for financial institutions and payment companies.
- **Why It Matters**: The announcement shows Visa pushing beyond public-chain pilots toward privacy-preserving institutional payment flows, a key requirement if stablecoin settlement is to scale inside regulated banking and network environments.
- **URL**: https://investor.visa.com/news/news-details/2026/Visa-and-Brale-Explore-Private-Stablecoin-Settlement-for-Institutional-Payments/default.aspx

### 2. Bank of America unveils cross-border real-time payments plan
- **Bank of America** · 2026-06-04
- **Summary**: Bank of America announced plans to launch a cross-border real-time payments service next quarter for corporate, commercial, and financial-institution clients via Swift or CashPro. The offering will connect to domestic real-time networks including Mexico’s SPEI, the U.K.’s Faster Payments Service, and India’s UPI, with features including real-time tracking, pre-validation, and full-principal delivery.
- **Why It Matters**: This is a significant signal that large incumbent banks are productizing cross-border instant payments, not just domestic real-time rails, which could raise competitive pressure on correspondent banking and specialist remittance providers.
- **URL**: https://newsroom.bankofamerica.com/content/newsroom/press-releases/2026/06/bank-of-america-to-launch-cross-border-real-time-payments---expa.html

### 3. Thunes launches real-time USD payment capabilities into the U.S.
- **The Paypers** · 2026-06-05
- **Summary**: Thunes launched real-time payment capabilities into the U.S. for members of its Direct Global Network, connecting cross-border senders to ACH, Same-Day ACH, and real-time payment rails through a single API. The company said the expansion is backed by its U.S. money transmission licensing footprint and direct institutional connectivity rather than intermediary-heavy correspondent models.
- **Why It Matters**: The move strengthens infrastructure for inbound U.S. payouts and reflects a broader industry shift toward direct, API-led access to local clearing systems as payment firms seek lower-cost and lower-failure cross-border delivery.
- **URL**: https://thepaypers.com/payments/news/thunes-launches-real-time-usd-payment-capabilities-into-the-us

### 4. Vietnam spotlights digital payments as core economic infrastructure
- **SGGP English Edition** · 2026-06-06
- **Summary**: Vietnam’s deputy prime minister described digital payments as a backbone of the digital economy during Digital Finance Day events in Ho Chi Minh City. The remarks underscored official support for further payments modernization as part of the country’s wider financial digitization agenda.
- **Why It Matters**: Government backing matters in payments because regulation, public infrastructure, and merchant adoption often move together; strong policy signaling can accelerate deployment across wallets, QR payments, and digital financial services.
