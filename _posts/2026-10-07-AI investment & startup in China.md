# AI investment & startup in China Brief — 2026-10-07

**Today:** China’s AI funding market is scaling rapidly, with DeepSeek reportedly pursuing a multibillion-dollar round while Xiongan targets a new generation of high-value AI companies.

## Top Stories

### 1. 📊 **DeepSeek reportedly set to raise more than US$12 billion ahead of potential IPO**

*DIGITIMES · 2026-10-07*

**Bottom line:** DeepSeek is reportedly targeting more than CNY80 billion (about US$11.9 billion) in fresh funding, potentially creating one of China’s largest private AI financing rounds ahead of a possible domestic IPO.

The Chinese AI startup is said to be raising at least CNY80 billion, with the round potentially reaching CNY100 billion. The fundraising is reportedly being driven by strong investor demand and is aimed at strengthening the company’s capital base ahead of a potential listing.

**Why it matters:** The scale of the round signals that Chinese investors are willing to commit unprecedented amounts of capital to frontier AI, while a potential IPO would give DeepSeek another major source of funding for compute, models and talent.

### 2. 🤖 **Xiongan seeks high-value technology firms across AI, robotics and satellite internet**

*South China Morning Post · 2026-10-07*

**Bottom line:** Xiongan New Area is pursuing a three-year plan to cultivate five companies valued at at least CNY10 billion, with AI among the core sectors targeted for development.

Officials are seeking technology companies with the potential to reach billion-dollar valuations and eventually list, including in Hong Kong. The strategy spans artificial intelligence, robotics and satellite internet as Xiongan attempts to build a self-sustaining technology and startup ecosystem.

**Why it matters:** The initiative illustrates how Chinese regional governments are increasingly competing for AI startups and attempting to shape local capital formation, talent concentration and eventual IPO pipelines around strategic technology sectors.
