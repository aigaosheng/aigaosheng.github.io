# AI Governance Brief — 2026-10-20

**Today:** A light day with two qualifying stories: FX market standard-setters debate reviewing their rules more often because of AI, and NAB's planned job cuts raise AI disclosure and oversight questions in Australia.

## Top Stories

### 1. 🏦 **FX Market Standards Body Weighs More Frequent Reviews as AI Advances**

*Bloomberg · 2026-10-20*

**Bottom line:** The group that sets best-practice principles for the $9.5 trillion-a-day currency market is debating whether its three-year review cycle is too slow for AI-related risks.

The Global Foreign Exchange Committee currently updates its principles once every three years. Bloomberg reports, citing people familiar with the matter, that representatives of the group and other market participants are discussing more frequent reviews of technology-related risks. No decision has been reported.

**Why it matters:** Wholesale-market codes are voluntary, but firms follow them as the market standard. If the committee shortens its review cycle, AI expectations could reach FX participants through market codes as well as through regulators. Treat this as a discussion, not an adopted change.

### 2. 🤖 **NAB Job Cuts Prompt Calls for Clarity on AI's Role and Oversight**

*ABC News (Australia), live business coverage · 2026-10-20*

**Bottom line:** After NAB announced plans to cut 2,500 jobs by mid-2027, an Australian governance expert said the bank should be open about whether AI is involved and how it is overseen.

ABC's live coverage reports that NAB shares fell on the announcement. The bank's statement did not mention AI or artificial intelligence. Governance expert Helen Bird argued that NAB should explain AI's role in the cuts, including the governance protocols around its use and where human oversight sits in these changes.

**Why it matters:** Whether a company attributes workforce decisions to AI, or leaves AI out of the explanation, is becoming a governance and disclosure question. Boards should be able to show how AI factors into major people decisions and where human accountability lies.
