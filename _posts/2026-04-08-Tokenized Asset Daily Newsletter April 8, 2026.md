## Top Stories

### 1. Securitize Brings Nasdaq-Listed Shares On-Chain

*Source: PR Newswire | Publish Date: April 8, 2026*
*Summary:* Securitize announced the tokenization of Currenc Group Inc. shares on Ethereum and Solana, enabling public equities to exist natively on blockchain infrastructure. The model supports fractional ownership, 24/7 trading, and integration with DeFi protocols. This represents a major step toward merging traditional public markets with blockchain-based financial systems. ([PR Newswire][1])
*Why It Matters:* This is a landmark in equity tokenization—moving beyond private assets into regulated public equities, signaling institutional-grade adoption.

### 2. South Korea Moves to Institutionalize Tokenized RWAs

*Source: The Block | Publish Date: April 8, 2026*
*Summary:* South Korea’s ruling party is advancing legislation to bring tokenized real-world assets (RWAs) and stablecoins under formal regulatory frameworks. The move aims to integrate tokenization into the existing financial system while ensuring compliance and investor protection. ([The Block][2])
*Why It Matters:* Regulatory clarity in a major Asian economy accelerates institutional adoption and sets a benchmark for global RWA frameworks.

### 6. IMF Warns Tokenization Could Amplify Financial Instability

*Source: CoinDesk | Publish Date: April 6, 2026*
*Summary:* The IMF cautioned that tokenization may increase systemic risk by accelerating market cycles and embedding volatility through automated smart contracts. It emphasized the need for central bank oversight. ([CoinDesk][6])
*Why It Matters:* Highlights the growing tension between innovation and financial stability—key for regulators and institutional investors.

Citation URL: [https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/04/06/imf-warns-tokenization-could-bring-crypto-risks-into-global-financial-markets](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/04/06/imf-warns-tokenization-could-bring-crypto-risks-into-global-financial-markets)
