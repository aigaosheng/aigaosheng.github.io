Frequently asked

Do I need an account to use Wraith?

No. Wraith works immediately on launch with no sign-up, login, or personal information required.

Does Wraith send my data to the cloud?

No. Wraith runs AI models on your device. Data leaves your device only when you explicitly ask it to search the web, and you can disable even that with the built-in No-Egress setting.

Why does the first launch download a file?

Wraith downloads the open-source AI model it runs on-device. After that, core features work offline.

What is Mac pairing?

An optional feature: you can pair Wraith with your own Mac on the same Wi-Fi network to run larger models. It is not required — the app is fully useful on its own.

How do I delete my data?

All data is stored locally. Deleting the app removes it. In-app settings also let you manage and delete saved memories and backups.
