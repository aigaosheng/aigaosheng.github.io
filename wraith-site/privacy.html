Privacy Policy

Wraith · Effective August 14, 2026

Wraith is built to keep your data on your device. We do not operate servers that receive your content, we do not require an account, and we do not collect, sell, or share personal information. This policy explains what that means in practice.

Information we collect

None. Wraith has no user accounts and no analytics or tracking SDKs. Your conversations, documents, tasks, notes, and any data you create stay in local storage on your device. We have no ability to access them.

How your data is processed

Wraith runs open-source AI models directly on your device. Chat, document reading, transcription, and other core features work entirely offline once the necessary model files are downloaded. Wraith does not use any third-party AI service — there is no cloud model, no API key, and no server of ours that receives your prompts or your content.

When data leaves your device, and to whom

Data leaves your device only for the features listed below, only when you ask for them, and only after you have granted permission on first launch. Until you grant it, Wraith's No-Egress lock is engaged and no network request is made at all; you can re-engage it at any time in Settings → Privacy, and review or change your choice under Settings → Privacy → Data sharing.

Because Wraith has no accounts, nothing sent below is associated with your identity. Each recipient handles what it receives under its own privacy policy.

Optional Mac pairing. You may pair Wraith with your own Mac on your local Wi-Fi network to run larger models. Your requests then travel only between your two devices over the local network; they are not sent to us or to any cloud service.

What never leaves. Your conversations, documents, notes, tasks and memories; your activity, calendar, reminders, contacts and photos; and your precise location. Place-based suggestions send only a coarse area name, never coordinates.

Wraith includes an on-device privacy ledger that shows you exactly what has left your device and where.

Health and activity data

Wraith does not use Apple HealthKit and cannot read your Health records. If you grant Motion & Fitness access, it reads your step count on your device only, to answer questions about your day. This is never transmitted off the device.

Device permissions

Wraith may ask for access to the microphone, camera, location, calendar, reminders, contacts, motion, and notifications. Each is used only for the feature you invoke, is processed on-device, and none of it is transmitted to us.

Children's privacy

Wraith is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. Because we collect no information at all, no such data is gathered.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
