BANK ACCOUNT VERIFICATION METHODS
🔬
Micro-Deposit Verification
Two small random deposits (<$1) sent to the account. Payee confirms the exact amounts. Proves ownership. Adds 1-3 days friction. Used by ACH-based systems.
⚡
Instant Account Verification (IAV)
Payee authenticates via OAuth with their bank. Platform gets read-only access to confirm ownership and check balance. Plaid, MX, TrueLayer.
🔔
Prenote (ACH Pre-Notification)
Zero-dollar test entry. Returns NOC (Notification of Change) if account details have changed. No funds moved.
💳
Zero-Dollar Card Auth
For Visa Direct / Mastercard Send. A $0 authorization validates the card number and confirms it can receive push payments.
PAYOUT FRAUD VECTORS
ACCOUNT TAKEOVER
Bank Account Update Fraud
Attacker compromises payee account and changes bank details to their own. Defense: No payout within 24h of bank account change. Out-of-band confirmation.
FIRST-PARTY FRAUD
Fraudulent Earnings Claims
Payee claims gig earnings fraudulently (e.g., fake deliveries). Defense: Behavioral analytics, GPS verification, graph analysis of payout networks.
MONEY MULE
Unwitting Fund Transfer
Payee's account receives and forwards fraudulent funds. Defense: Detect "receive large credit → immediately wire out" pattern via TMS.

FX MECHANICS
Spread vs. Transparent Fee
Traditional MTOs embed cost in the exchange rate spread (2-5% markup on mid-market). Wise model: publishes mid-market rate + explicit transparent fee. Disrupted industry cost expectations.
Rate Locking & Hedging
MTOs lock the rate shown to the customer and hedge FX exposure via forward contracts with banks. For illiquid pairs (USD/XOF), pre-funded local balances required — creating balance sheet FX risk.
Herstatt Risk (FX Settlement Risk)
When one leg of a currency exchange settles but the other does not (due to time zones or default). CLS Bank eliminates this via PVP (Payment vs. Payment) simultaneous settlement across 18 currencies.
