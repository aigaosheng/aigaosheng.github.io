This policy covers aisengtech.com: the daily briefs, the series pages, and the pages describing consulting and development services. Other products whose pages are hosted on this domain publish their own policies, linked from their own pages.

What this site asks you for

Nothing. There is no account, no newsletter signup, no comment box and no contact form. The site never asks you to type anything except a search term, and search runs entirely in your own browser — the index is a static file your browser downloads, and what you type into it is never sent anywhere.

What is collected automatically

Google Analytics 4

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which briefs get read. It sets cookies in your browser and reports to Google: the page you are on, the page that referred you, your approximate location (derived from your IP address, which Google truncates before storing), your device type, browser and screen size, and how far down the page you scroll. IP addresses are not stored in a form that identifies you, and the data is not used to build an advertising profile of you here.

Google AdSense

This site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve those ads, to measure whether they were seen, and — where you have consented, or where local law permits it — to personalise them based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies is described in Google's advertising policy, and its handling of data from sites that use its services in the Google Privacy & Terms partner-sites notice.

Third-party vendors and ad networks other than Google may also serve ads here. Where they do, they may set their own cookies.

Hosting

The site is served as static files by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs requests, including IP addresses, as part of operating the service; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. Some page assets — icon fonts, the search library — are loaded from public CDNs (cdnjs and jsDelivr), which see your IP address as part of delivering the file.

Your choices

  • Turn off personalised advertising across the web at Google My Ad Center. You will still see ads here; they just will not be based on your browsing history.
  • Opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised ads at aboutads.info or Your Online Choices (EU/UK).
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Nothing on this site depends on them.
  • Install Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on to stop Google Analytics on every site, including this one.

Children

This site is written for professional readers and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If what the site collects changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top is updated.
